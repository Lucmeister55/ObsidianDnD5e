# DS:SotDQ

What to buy, print and build for each chapter. Figures and terrain carry over between chapters. **Need** is the most you'll have on the table at once by that point, and the costs count only what's **new** in that chapter. Shopping candidates live in [[Wishlist]].

**Prices** are rough, in euros:
- **Maps:** printed free on A0, so they cost nothing.
- **Figures:** unpainted 3D-printed resin from Etsy. Small or medium €3–4, large €10, huge €25, a named character €6–15.
- **Pawns:** the Pathfinder Battle Box standees (monsters and war units) cover anything marked **Pawn**: generic monsters, soldiers, guards and beasts. Buy minis only for the Dragonlance-specific figures, the named characters and the set pieces.
- **Terrain:** cheap scatter or card. Foam and DIY versions are cheaper still. **Terrain is the real spend**, so see the [[#Terrain Plan]].

The map and terrain for every encounter are listed under it in the chapter notes. Figures follow from the encounter's monster list.

**Cheaper still:**
- **Soulblight proxies.** Use the Soulblight Gravelords minis for every undead, if you'd rather not use pawns for them.
- **Skirmish units** need no new figures: use the war-unit pawns, or 3–5 minis on a movement tray.
- **Terrain bought as sets** (below) comes to about €425 instead of €516, because one set serves many encounters.

**Buy first:** sets 1–4 cover the whole prologue and most of Storm Clouds. Then set 6 before the catacombs, set 7 before the Wastes, and the rest of set 5 before the siege.

**Cheap routes:**
- **Forest:** aquarium plants glued to washers.
- **Rocks:** cork bark and offcuts of slate.
- **Walls and buildings:** foam board, or free printable card terrain.
- **Dressing:** 3D prints if you have access to a printer, like the pawn insert.
