# Materials

Everything we have at our disposal for D&D, whatever the campaign, and whose it is. A campaign's own shopping and needs stay in that campaign's folder. For Dragonlance, that's the [[Need List]], [[Picture Board]] and [[Wishlist]].

**Owner** is who to ask. **Count** is how many, and **Painted** is how many of those are painted, so it starts at 0 and can never be more than Count. Counts assume one standard box of each kit, so raise them if you have more.
