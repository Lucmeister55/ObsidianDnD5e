# The War in Numbers

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. Sized to the module's "hundreds" at Steel Springs and "thousands" at the siege. Who's who: [[Forces of Kalaman]] and [[Dragon Armies]].


**How to use it.** This is a lookup, so there's nothing to keep track of. When someone asks how the war stands, find the last thing that happened and read out the number for how it went. Each row assumes everything before it went as expected, so don't add rows together. If the party has had a very good or bad run, nudge the number and move on.

Kalaman's numbers are its regulars. The enemy's are its Kalaman front, until the expedition sails.

**In between:** each downtime, Kalaman gains about **100** from Darrett's recruits, and the enemy about **200** from mercenaries heading north. Those are already in the numbers above.

**The enemy at the siege**, about 7,000:
- the Kalaman front (about 2,800);
- what's left of the Wastes army (about 2,700);
- 1,000 fresh troops from Taman Busuk;
- 500 on the flying citadel;
- Soth's 30 skeletal knights, 3 lesser death dragons, Ignia, 40 dragonnels and 12 war engines.

It's three to one against a wall that has never fallen, until the citadel arrives.

The Wastes battles (Scenarios 7 and 8) hit the **Wastes army**, not the Kalaman front, and they're already in the siege total.

**Militia** lose **twice** what the regulars lose in the siege, from the same column. **Siege Ledger** allies are extra, on top of these numbers.
