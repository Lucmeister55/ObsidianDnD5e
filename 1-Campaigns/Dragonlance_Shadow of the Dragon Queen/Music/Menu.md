# Music

Your soundboard, by what's happening at the table. Tracks by place are in [[Locations]], by character in [[NPCs]], and all combat music is in [[Battles]]. To add a cue to any note, run the `Music Cue` template at the cursor: it lists every track in Soundbar.

Most Fire Emblem tracks come in pairs: a calm version, and an **Ablaze** version of the same tune. Start a scene on the calm one here and switch to its Ablaze version in [[Battles]] when swords come out.
