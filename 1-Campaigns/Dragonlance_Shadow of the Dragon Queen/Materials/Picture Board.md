# Picture Board

Which figure, terrain piece or map you'll use for each entry on the [[Need List]]. Paste or drag pictures under an entry: the first one shows up in the Need List, and on that NPC's own note. Named PCs and NPCs always get a real 3D mini, never a pawn. **Uses** suggests what you own: figures from the master [[Materials]] list and pawns from your [[Monster Core Pawn Box]], [[Battlecry Pawn Box]] and [[Beginner Box]]. **Buy** links to the [[Wishlist]].
