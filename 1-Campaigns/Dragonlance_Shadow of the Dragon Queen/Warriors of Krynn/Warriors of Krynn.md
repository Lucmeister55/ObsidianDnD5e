## Three Kinds of Battle

| Kind | Scale | Played on | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Combat** | The party and a few enemies | Battlemap, minis | Everything that isn't a battle |
| **[[Skirmish Battles\|Skirmish]]** | A handful of units, with the heroes among them | Battlemap, Battlecry units ([[Need List]]) | A battle too small for WoK, or one WoK scenario zoomed in, with the party in the fray |
| **WoK** | Whole armies | The board game | The big battles below. The party command, fight and decide where the battle turns |

### House Rules

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.

1. **The DM places the Dragon Army's things.** Where the rules let the players choose where the enemy's effects go, such as where fire spreads, the DM chooses instead. The same goes for where the Dragon Army puts its units, hazards and reinforcements. The DM doesn't have to do it every time: sometimes leave it to the rules or to the players, and sometimes place it where it hurts. The enemy has a mind of its own, and the players shouldn't get to set it up where it helps them most.
