# Warriors of Krynn

> [!wok] Source: **WoK**, the *Dragonlance: Warriors of Krynn* board game, which the module offers as its optional mass-combat system ([[01-war-comes-to-krynn#The War of the Lance|War Comes to Krynn]]). Our notes on it, and pictures of the books to read at the table.

## Three Kinds of Battle

| Kind | Scale | Played on | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Combat** | The party and a few enemies | Battlemap, minis | Everything that isn't a battle |
| **[[Skirmish Battles\|Skirmish]]** | A handful of units, with the heroes among them | Battlemap, Battlecry units ([[Need List]]) | A battle too small for WoK, or one WoK scenario zoomed in, with the party in the fray |
| **WoK** | Whole armies | The board game | The big battles below. The party command, fight and decide where the battle turns |

## How We Use It

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.

1. **We never run Scenario 0, Training Ground.** The party learns the game by playing the first scenario.
2. **Scenario 1, Battle of Vogler, is always the first game**, and it is the only WoK battle the story requires ([[2.02 Preparing for the Worst]]). Give it its own session, teach the rules as you go with the [[Rulebook]] open, and write down what you learn in Our Notes below.
3. **Everything after it is optional.** Use that first game to judge whether WoK is worth keeping. If it is, each later scenario is a **bonus battle** you can throw in at the slot marked in its chapter. If it isn't, run the chapter's normal version (the module's, or a [[Skirmish Battles|skirmish]]) and nothing is lost.
4. **A bonus battle only gives.** A win gives the scenario's bonus. A hold or a loss gives nothing and costs nothing, and the story goes on as written. Nothing in the chapters depends on a WoK result.
5. **Titles.** Every scenario names its win after a reputation card (the title in the table). Say it aloud once if the party wins. It is flavour and has no mechanics unless the table lists a bonus.
6. **The War in Numbers.** If a battle is skipped, read its **Hold** column. If it's played, read the column for its result ([[The War in Numbers]]).
7. **Where the slots come from.** The module's own sidebars decide where scenarios 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 11 and 12 go. The other four (5, 6, 9, 10) are not in the module, so they follow the **book's own order** and the situation each one opens with. Anything that is a bonus (a unit, a faction point) is **Homebrew** and marked so in the chapter.

### House Rules

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.

1. **The DM places the Dragon Army's things.** Where the rules let the players choose where the enemy's effects go, such as where fire spreads, the DM chooses instead. The same goes for where the Dragon Army puts its units, hazards and reinforcements. The DM doesn't have to do it every time: sometimes leave it to the rules or to the players, and sometimes place it where it hurts. The enemy has a mind of its own, and the players shouldn't get to set it up where it helps them most.
2. **Dead commanders are replaced, not played.** Raven Uth Vogler is dead for the rest of the campaign (Kansaldi killed her), so she never appears in WoK. The book uses her in these places:
   - **Her commander card, C2** (Scenario 0's setup shows it as hers), which is in the setup of scenarios **2, 3 and 11**. In 2 and 3, put **Darrett Highwater's card (C1)** on C2's tile, in the same state (ready, unless the setup shows the card shaken). In 11, Darrett is already on the field, so use **Cudgel Ironsmile's card** if she's alive, and **Tatina Rookledust's** if not.
   - **Scenario 3's opening text**, which names her among the attacking commanders. Read "Darrett" instead.
   - **Scenario 9, entries 20 and 91**, where her friendly voice carries through the mist. Read **Darrett Highwater's** voice instead.
   - **Scenario 12's list of commanders a hero can place.** Leave her off the list.

   The same goes for **Becklin Uth Viharin** and anyone else who has died. Becklin is dead after a hold or a loss in Scenario 1 ([[2.02 Preparing for the Worst#Scenario 1: Battle of Vogler]]), and she may be dead after a win too. If her card turns up in a later scenario, replace it the same way.
