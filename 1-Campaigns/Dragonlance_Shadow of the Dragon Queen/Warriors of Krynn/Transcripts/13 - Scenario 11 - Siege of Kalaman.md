### RUNNING THIS SCENARIO

#### Starting the Scenario

After setting up, read the following aloud:

*(red)* *Kalaman is under siege. Marshal Vendri addresses her commanders. "The Dragon Army is at our gates, but the greater threat is the flying citadel."*

*Her eyes fall upon you. "Dragonnels are coming to carry you to the citadel. From there, we trust you'll find a way to bring it down. We'll cover your ascent with an attack, then fall back. But we lost touch with the scouts who know where to meet the dragonnels. Find the scouts to learn the location of the rendezvous point."*

#### Scenario Rules

This scenario has the following rules:

**Flanks.** Only battle tiles in the current defensive zone, the zone in which battles occur, have flank tokens. Only battle tiles with a flank token can become the active flank. See the "Retreating" section for details on how the defensive zone shifts.

**Rendezvous Point.** Objective tokens mark tiles where you gain information about where to meet the dragonnels. During play, objective tokens will be added to some tiles in the table below. When you examine an objective, remove its token and read the scenario entry for that tile.

##### Retreating

Scenario entries will ask if you want Alliance troops to retreat. You may retreat if you are in Zone 1 or Zone 2 (see the table). You can't retreat from Kalaman.

| Zone | Name | Battle Tiles |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Outer Defense | Crossroads, Forest Road, Scrublands |
| 2 | Field Fortifications | Farmstead, Fields |
| 3 | City of Kalaman | City Gate, City Wall |

If you call a retreat, do the following:

- Move up to 5 Alliance units from each tile in the current zone that has a ready commander to a battle tile in the next zone.
- Remove Alliance commanders, remaining Alliance units, vile champions, barricade tokens, and fire tokens from the current zone.
- Move all Dragon Army units from the current zone to the next zone (remove any extras). All are placed ready.
- If retreating to Zone 2, remove the Center Flank token and Bakaris the Traitor.
- Move the Left Flank token and Kansaldi Fire-Eyes to the Farmstead (if retreating to Zone 2) or the City Gate tile (if retreating to Zone 3).
- Move the Right Flank token and the Red Ruin to the Fields battle tile (if retreating to Zone 2) or the City Wall battle tile (if retreating to Zone 3).
- Move the Flying Citadel to the current *[one word is hidden at the bottom edge of the photo]* zone.
- Choose which flank is the active flank.
- When retreating to Zone 3, after performing the previous steps and if the Gatehouse adventure tile is in play, remove 1 unit from each Dragon Army line on both flanks and Shake both Dragon Army commanders.

#### Command Tent Choice

Vendri offers aid for your mission. Choose 1 option; it occurs immediately:

**Rebuild the Gatehouse.** Flip the Ruins tile, and add a wilderness token to it.

**Send Knights to Fight the Dragon.** Remove the Red Dragon vile champion from the Village adventure tile.

#### Dragon Army Control

After the Dragon Army takes control of a battle tile, follow these instructions:

**First Tile.** Read scenario entry 17.

**Second Tile.** Read scenario entry 138.

**Third Tile.** Read scenario entry 75. The scenario immediately ends in a loss.

#### Additional Endings

This scenario has the following special ending conditions:

- The heroes leave on the dragonnels.
- The Dragon Army takes control of the City Gate or City Wall battle tile.

#### Aftermath

Determine the scenario's outcome:

**Win.** The heroes reached the dragonnels while the battle was in Zone 1 or Zone 2.

**Hold.** The heroes reached the dragonnels while the battle was in Zone 3.

**Loss.** The Dragon Army took control of the City Gate or City Wall battle tile, or another loss condition occurred.

##### Win

*(red)* *You mount the dragonnels and fly to the citadel above.*

Take the Winged Riders of Kalaman reputation card and an available medal card.

##### Hold

*(red)* *You narrowly escape atop the dragonnels and fly up to sabotage the flying citadel.*

Take the Winged Riders of Kalaman reputation card.

##### Loss

*(red)* *You spot the allied dragonnels landing outside the city walls. You're barely able to make it to them and ascend to the flying citadel, leaving your allies to an uncertain fate.*

At the beginning of the next scenario, the heroes will get extra equipment and tactic tokens (the loss bonus).
