### SCENARIO 9: MARCH OF THE BLACK ROSE

#### Objective

Take control of 3 battle tiles. Search the battlefield to gain aid from mystical forces nearby.

#### Your Valor Is Known

- If the heroes have 9+ honor cards, Shake 3 Alliance units on the Barrows.
- If the heroes have 11+ honor cards, add the Your Reputation Precedes You event card (40) to the event deck.

### RUNNING THIS SCENARIO

#### Starting the Scenario

After setting up this scenario, read the following text aloud:

*(red)* *You're roused from a sound sleep by a flare of magic within your tent. The image of the red-robed wizard Commander Thea appears in your midst. "Heroes," she begins, her voice wavering. "There's a problem."*

*She goes on to explain that the forces of the undead knight Lord Soth have ambushed your contingent. Unnatural mist has surrounded the camp. Worse, it slays soldiers with weak constitutions and reanimates them as undead foes.*

*"If we can't drive away the mists, this battle is lost. I'll do what I can here. There are other mystic forces in this region. See if you can find any that are friendly to our cause. Ultimately, it will come down to you whether our soldiers fight for the living or the dead."*

#### Scenario Rules

This scenario has the following special rules:

**Scouting.** Most of the battlefield is shrouded in mist. As you play, you'll set up more tiles, as well as units and commanders for battle tiles.

**Revealing Tiles.** When a hero examines an objective, remove the objective token and read the scenario entry for that tile, as noted on the table here.

#### Command Tent Choice

Before you leave, Thea offers aid for your mission. Choose 1 option, which occurs immediately:

**Part the Mist.** Thea temporarily suppresses the mist. Find the Necrotic Mist event card (36) in the event deck, put it in the event discard pile, then shuffle the event deck. Make sure the Key Moment event card (1) is still the bottom card of the event deck.

**Provide Insights.** Thea relates what she knows about the terrain. Read scenario entry 70.

#### Dragon Army Control

After the Dragon Army takes control of a battle tile, follow these instructions:

**First Tile.** Read scenario entry 48.

**Second Tile.** Read scenario entry 99. The scenario immediately ends in a loss.

#### Additional Endings

This scenario has the following special ending condition:

- The Alliance takes control of 3 battle tiles.

#### Aftermath

*(red)* *You notice Thea's red robes are stained with a darker substance as she makes her way toward you.*

Determine the scenario's outcome:

**Win.** The Alliance took control of 3 battle tiles, and the Dragon Army took control of none.

**Hold.** The Alliance took control of 3 battle tiles, and the Dragon Army took control of 1.

**Loss.** The Dragon Army took control of 2 battle tiles, or another loss condition occurred.

#### Sidebar: Lord Soth

*(Black box.)* Hundreds of years ago, Lord Loren Soth was a proud Knight of Solamnia. Cursed for betraying the gods, he is now doomed to spend an eternity as an undead servant of evil, a death knight. Clad in charred Solamnic armor, Lord Soth is said to leave few survivors in his encounters with mortals.

##### Win

*(red)* *Thea says, "The next time I speak at the Tower of High Sorcery, I'll relate how we faced down the dead and lived!"*

Take the Survivors reputation card and an available medal card.

##### Hold

*(red)* *Thea says, "We held our own against a terror most thought was just a legend. What more could be asked of us?"*

Take the Survivors reputation card.

##### Loss

*(red)* *Thea says, "No one could have held against that abomination. We're lucky to be alive."*

At the beginning of the next scenario, the heroes will get extra equipment and tactic tokens (the loss bonus).
