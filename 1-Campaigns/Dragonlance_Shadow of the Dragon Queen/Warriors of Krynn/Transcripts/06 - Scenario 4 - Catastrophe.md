### RUNNING THIS SCENARIO

#### Starting the Scenario

After setting up this scenario, read the following text aloud:

*(red)* *Bakaris the Elder, an arrogant noble who convinced Kalaman's leaders to launch an risky attack against the Dragon Army, bursts into the command tent. Following him is his son, Bakaris the Younger, who wears a new suit of armor and sneers at the battle-stained company.*

*"Ruined! Clearly my cunning strategy wasn't properly implemented," Bakaris the Elder blusters. "Now the enemy is headed our way. What should have been my moment of glory is ruined thanks to you lot!"*

*Darrett Highwater raises his voice over Bakaris's tirade. "Stay strong, everyone. We've sent our riders for reinforcements, but help won't be here until dawn."*

*"That's your plan, we're holding out for supposed reinforcements? That's a ... a fine plan," Bakaris the Elder says, slinking toward the tent's exit. The next moment, the noble and his son are gone.*

*Darrett turns to you. "I'll manage the defense here as best I can, but I need your help. Locals say a witch makes her home in the woods nearby. They say she aids those who leave offerings for the mysterious god of magic, Lunitari." Darrett shakes his head. "These are the straits we find ourselves in, my friends. See what offerings you can find, and deliver them to the witch's hut. I'll indulge any scheme if it helps us hold out till morning."*

#### Scenario Rules

This scenario has the following special rules:

**Offerings for the Witch.** Objective tokens represent offerings for the witch. When a hero examines an objective, place the objective token on the Command Post or Command Tent tile.

When 4 objective tokens are on the Command Post or Command Tent tile, read scenario entry 87.

#### Command Tent Choice

Bakaris the Elder isn't interested in assisting you. There is no command tent choice in this scenario.

#### Dragon Army Control

After the Dragon Army takes control of a battle tile, follow these instructions:

**First Tile.** Read scenario entry 31.

**Second Tile.** Read scenario entry 69.

**Third Tile.** Read scenario entry 135. The scenario immediately ends in a loss.

#### Additional Endings

This scenario has the following special ending condition:

- The heroes survive until dawn.

#### Aftermath

*(red)* *The war camp is a mess. Emerging from the chaos is the inexperienced commander Willem, his jaw set. He bears the news that Bakaris the Younger is missing and is presumed dead. His cowardly father, Bakaris the Elder, does not take the news well.*

Determine the scenario's outcome:

**Win.** The heroes survived until dawn with 1 or 0 battle tiles controlled by the Dragon Army.

**Hold.** The heroes survived until dawn with 2 battle tiles controlled by the Dragon Army.

**Loss.** The Dragon Army took control of 3 battle tiles, or another loss condition occurred.

#### Sidebar: Mages of High Sorcery

*(Black box.)* The reclusive Mages of High Sorcery divide their ranks into orders distinguished by the color of their robes: black, red, and white. From the mysterious Towers of High Sorcery, the heads of each order loosely unite their followers along the principles of good for the white robes, neutrality for the red robes, and evil for the black robes.

##### Win

*(red)* *Willem says, "That was not a battle we should have survived. I'll make sure everyone knows that we did thanks to your quick thinking."*

Take the Diehards reputation card and an available medal card.

##### Hold

*(red)* *Willem says, "We survived, if just barely, no thanks to Lord Bakaris. That any of us still live is a gift. Thank you."*

Take the Diehards reputation card.

##### Loss

*(red)* *Willem says, "At this point, we can only mourn our dead. Next time we'll be ready."*

At the beginning of the next scenario, the heroes will get extra equipment and tactic tokens (the loss bonus).
