## Scenario 6, Dark Moon Rises, Running This Scenario (printed page 15)

### RUNNING THIS SCENARIO

#### Starting the Scenario

After setting up this scenario, read the following text aloud:

*(red)* *The camp is in chaos when you return from a mission. A sergeant tells you the story: after you left, a Dragon Army ritual caused the Alliance commanders to join the enemy!*

*You also receive a message from Commander Thea, one of the Mages of High Sorcery. The Dragon Army is using the moon Nuitari's magic to create portals that will allow the Dragon Army to summon reinforcements.*

#### Scenario Rules

This scenario has the following special rules:

**Magic.** Objective tokens represent magic effects. Vile champions with objective tokens control portals, while commanders with objective tokens are magically controlled (see below for details on both).

##### Portals

Vile champions guard portals that transport Dragon Army reinforcements. If a vile champion with 1 or more objective tokens is defeated, its portal closes. Vile champions with objective tokens on them can't be affected by class powers that defeat vile champions. Each objective token on a vile champion raises their difficulty by 1.

##### Enchanted Commanders

Alliance commanders are being controlled by the Dragon Queen's magic. These commanders are on the adventure tiles their cards have been placed under.

***Aiding the Dragon Army.*** Enchanted commanders act as a second Dragon Army commander on a battle tile. When commander powers are activated for that tile, activate the power of the nearest ready enchanted Alliance commander as well (in either order). Enchanted commanders' powers have the following effects:

**Graystone.** Add barricades to Dragon Army units on the right flank.

**Lord Deveral.** Add Dragon Army units to the far right flank.

**Shadowbolt.** Add 2 tactic tokens to a Dragon Army line on the center flank.

**Thea.** Add 2 tactic tokens to a Dragon Army line on the left flank.

***Battle Tiles without Commanders.*** If a unit card checks if the Alliance commander is shaken, resolve that card as if a shaken Alliance commander were on the battle tile.

***Shaken Commanders.*** Powers that Shake a Dragon Army commander may be used to Shake an enchanted Alliance commander. Powers that Refresh an Alliance commander have no effect.

##### Freeing Commanders

To free an enchanted Alliance commander, a hero must be on the same tile as the commander's card, and there must be no vile champions there. That hero must initiate a conflict (not an encounter) with the commander. The conflict's ability is the ability listed on the commander's shaken side. The conflict's difficulty is equal to the listed action cost to Refresh the commander plus 1 for every objective token on their card. Follow these instructions, depending on the outcome:

**Success.** Remove all objective tokens from the commander, and move them to the nearest battle tile. They are now the Alliance commander there. If that battle tile is controlled, remove the commander instead. Shaken commanders remain shaken.

**Failure.** The commander remains enchanted. If they have more than 1 objective token, remove 1 token.

#### Command Tent Choice

Before you leave, the sergeant offers aid for your mission. Choose 1 option, which occurs immediately:

**Clear the Way to a Portal.** Kalaman's soldiers clear you a path to a portal. Remove the Auruk Draconian vile champion on the Wooded Crossroads adventure tile.

**Rally Leaderless Soldiers.** Experienced soldiers step up to rally their units. Refresh 6 Alliance units on the right flank.

#### Dragon Army Control

After the Dragon Army takes control of a battle tile, follow these instructions:

**First Tile.** Read scenario entry 132.

**Second Tile.** Read scenario entry 145. The scenario immediately ends in a loss.

#### Additional Endings

This scenario has the following special ending condition:

- The heroes remove all objective tokens.

#### Aftermath

*(red)* *A commander approaches wearily, pausing before speaking.*

Determine the scenario's outcome:

**Win.** All enchanted Alliance commanders were freed, and all portals were closed.

**Hold.** All enchanted commanders were freed, but not all portals were closed.

**Loss.** Some enchanted commanders weren't freed, or another loss condition occurred.

##### Win

*(red)* *Graystone salutes and says, "I'm amazed you did it. Thank you, from all of us."*

Take the Marauders reputation card and an available medal card.

##### Hold

*(red)* *Graystone says, "It's hard to believe we held, but here we stand. Good work."*

Take the Marauders reputation card.

##### Loss

*(red)* *The commander says, "Their magic was too much for us. This can't happen again."*

At the beginning of the next scenario, the heroes will get extra equipment and tactic tokens (the loss bonus).
