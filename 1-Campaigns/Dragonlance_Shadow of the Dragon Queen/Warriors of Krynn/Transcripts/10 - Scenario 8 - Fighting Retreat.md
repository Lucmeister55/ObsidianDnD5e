### RUNNING THIS SCENARIO

#### Starting the Scenario

After setting up this scenario, read the following text aloud:

*(red)* *Boulder-sized debris slams into the ground. A look to the heavens reveals its source: a massive fortress now rising into the sky. The rituals conducted in the City of Lost Names raised this flying fortress, granting the Dragon Army a terrible new weapon.*

*"Make for the river!" yells Commander Willem, signaling a full retreat. He turns to you. "The enemy is trying to cut us off. I've sent scouts ahead. Command them as you see fit. Go! Before that thing's upon us!"*

#### Scenario Rules

This scenario has the following special rules:

**Scouts.** Objective tokens represent scouts aiding the retreat. See the "Scouts" section below for details.

**Active Flank.** The battle tile next to the Rising City token is always the active flank. Ignore anything that would move the flank. If the Alliance takes control of the active flank, don't follow the standard rules; instead, Refresh all Alliance units on the active flank and then follow the Running Battle rules. If the Dragon Army takes control of the active flank, the scenario immediately ends in a loss.

**Skirmishes.** The Skirmishers event card (44) causes a skirmish to occur on the battle tile neighboring the active flank. There are no commanders. If a unit card checks to see if a commander is shaken, resolve the unit card as if a shaken commander were present. If a side takes control of a tile during a skirmish, nothing happens (don't read a scenario entry).

##### Running Battle

Units and commanders move toward the River Crossing battle tile when noted in key moments and if the Alliance takes control of the active flank. When this happens, do the following:

- Remove all shaken Alliance units on the active flank.
- Remove all tactic tokens and barricades from the active flank.
- Move the remaining Alliance units on the active flank to the neighboring battle tile. This moves the active flank 1 tile closer to (or onto) the River Crossing.
- Move all Dragon Army units to the same neighboring battle tile. If shaken, Dragon Army units remain shaken. Dragon Army units that can't be moved to the next battle tile are moved to the battle tile beyond that. Dragon Army units that can't be placed on either tile are removed, and 1 Alliance unit is removed from the active flank for each Dragon Army unit removed.
- Move both commanders to the neighboring tile.
- Advance the key moment tracker by 1, and read the corresponding scenario entry on the table in the "Key Moments" section.

The key moments provide further instructions.

#### Dragon Army Control

If the Dragon Army takes control of the active flank, read scenario entry 68. The scenario immediately ends in a loss.

#### Additional Endings

This scenario has the following special ending conditions:

- The Dragon Army takes control of the active flank.
- An all-out battle occurs on the River Crossing tile.

#### Aftermath

*(red)* *You find Willem alone beside a campfire at the rendezvous point. He nods to you.*

Determine the scenario's outcome:

**Win.** The Alliance was the attacker on all 3 lines on the River Crossing tile during the final battle.

**Hold.** The Alliance was the attacker on 2 lines on the River Crossing tile during the final battle.

**Loss.** The Alliance was the attacker on 1 or 0 lines on the River Crossing tile during the final battle, or another loss condition occurred.

#### Sidebar: Flying Citadel

*(Black box.)* Drawing upon ancient magic and the power of the Dragon Queen, the Dragon Army has created a flying citadel from the ruins of the City of Lost Names. The Dragon Army seeks to use it as a mobile base in its military campaign. No place on Krynn is safe from this flying menace.

##### Win

*(red)* *Willem says, "We made it thanks to you."*

Take the Life Guards reputation card and an available medal card.

##### Hold

*(red)* *Willem says, "We didn't all make it, but none of us would have if it weren't for you, friends."*

Take the Life Guards reputation card.

##### Loss

*(red)* *Willem silently stares into the fire.*

At the beginning of the next scenario, the heroes will get extra equipment and tactic tokens (the loss bonus).
