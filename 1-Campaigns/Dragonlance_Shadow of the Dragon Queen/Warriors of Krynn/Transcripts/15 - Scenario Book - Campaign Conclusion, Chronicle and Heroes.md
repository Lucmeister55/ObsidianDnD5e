### CAMPAIGN CONCLUSION

After you've completed scenario 12, read or paraphrase the following text:

The great hall of Castle Kalaman is festooned with glorious tapestries. The people of Kalaman have gathered in numbers, filling the hall with laughter and good cheer. Everyone, from the youngest child to the oldest veteran, has lost someone in this war. But today is a day to celebrate life, and those who remain. Marshal Vendri stands to speak, and you settle in with the commanders at the front of the hall.

### HEROES

#### Andir Valmakos

Andir completed his magical training and chose not to immediately seek out other Mages of High Sorcery. Instead, he took to the life of an adventuring wizard, wanting to see the world. Although his travels have just begun, Andir knows that one day his road will lead him to the Tower of High Sorcery at Wayreth, where he hopes to learn what his future holds.

#### Hrigg Roundrook

Hrigg lives to laugh heartily, feast well, and fight hard. He is one of five siblings, each of whom was chosen by Kiri-Jolith, the god of honor and war, to receive divine power. From their homeland of Kayolin, the dwarf siblings each set out to use their god's might to directly confound the Dragon Army.

#### Levna Drakehorn

Brave and decisive, Levna is a Solamnic knight of the Order of the Rose. After an encounter with the Dragon Army, Levna was left with a distinctive branching scar. She believes it came from the breath of a blue dragon, but she can't bring herself to share such an impossible tale.

#### Iriad

Iriad is a Kagonesti elf from the woodlands of Southern Ergoth, where she learned to move through the wilderness undetected. When Silvanesti elves arrived in Ergoth fleeing the Dragon Army, she set out to learn more about that threat. Iriad is a talented scout, and she isn't afraid to use her blades against the enemies of her people.

#### Ayik Ur

The youthful archer Ayik is a consummate survivor. As the Dragon Army swarmed over his homeland of Khur, he suffered a terrible leg injury beneath the hooves of a charging warhorse. Even as he fled, he swore that his bow would hasten the Dragon Army's defeat. Ayik is defiant and cocky to the point of recklessness, challenging his enemies to finish what the Dragon Army started back in Khur.

#### Tem Temble

Tem has the soul of a wanderer, though her feet most often take her deep into the wild and forgotten places of the world. Early in her travels, she befriended a winged lizard she named Melon, and the two have become inseparable. Tem's mannerisms are similar to a cat's, as she disregards personal space and forever seeks to curl up in warm spots.
