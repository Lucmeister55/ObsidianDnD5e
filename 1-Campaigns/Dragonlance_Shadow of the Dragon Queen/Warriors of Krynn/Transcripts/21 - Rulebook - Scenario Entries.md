### SCENARIO ENTRIES

This section contains scenario entries, descriptions of how the situation evolves as a scenario progresses. As you're playing through a scenario, whenever the key moment tracker advances or a situation specified in the scenario occurs, check the scenario book to see which scenario entry to read. Some scenario entries also send you directly to other entries. When a scenario entry tells you to do something, if you can't do everything, do as much as you can.

#### 1: The Crypt Horn Sounds

*Wersten Kern, a death knight in the service of Lord Soth, sounds his warhorn, and the Dragon Army charges.*

Conduct a skirmish on each battle tile from the left flank to the far right flank. Regardless of what's rolled on the skirmish die, activate the commander powers of ready commanders before the skirmish.

#### 2: Interdiction

*The voice of Fewmaster Roake booms out, and vile champions spring into action at his command.*

Choose 2 adventure tiles with a road. Add 1 random Recruit vile champion to each. Roll 3 battle dice for each portal. For each hit, add 1 Dragon Army unit to that portal's adjacent battle tile. Add units in this order until all have been added: 1 Foot unit, 1 Missile unit, 1 Mounted unit (repeat that order if necessary).

#### 3: Forest Edge

*At the edge of a dead forest, a once-bustling village stands empty. The sounds of battle surround you.*

Set up the tiles as shown below. Add objective tokens to the Village and Mine Entrance tiles.

*(Diagram: adventure tiles 23B (with an objective token) at the top, 30A beside it (labelled "This tile is already in play"), 22B in the middle and 17A (with an objective token) at the bottom.)*

#### 4: Reinforced from Within

*The Dragon Army forces use rubble from the City of Lost Names to build crude barricades for their lines, under the watchful eye of Lohezet of the Black Robes.*

Add a barricade to 1 unit on each Dragon Army line on the far left flank.

If 2 or more objective tokens are on the Command Tent or Command Post tile, add the Far Left Flank event card (7) to the bottom of the event discard pile.

#### 5: Get Those Heroes

*The battle reaches a fever pitch as the enemy commander orders their troops to target you.*

Refresh the Dragon Army commander on the active flank, and immediately activate their commander power.

**Minor War Engines Fire.** For each battle tile adjacent to a tile that has an objective token, roll the skirmish die. Apply 1 hit to the Alliance line matching the die result.

#### 6: Hunters on the Wing

*Jorl Morg exhorts the wyvern-riders to hunt you down. The beasts leap into the air and dive toward you.*

Move each Wyvern vile champion in play to an adventure tile that contains at least 1 hero, if possible. Each hero on that tile rolls 3 battle dice and takes 1 stress for each Woe result.

Remove 1 Alliance unit from each battle tile adjacent to an adventure tile that has a Wyvern vile champion. If no Wyverns are in play, add 1 Wyvern to the Trail tile.

#### 7: Witch's Hut

*Light beams from the witch's eyes as she intones, "Solinari, hear my prayer. Drive the dead back into the ground, and watch over these heroes in the journey to come."*

Add an objective token to the Command Tent or Command Post tile.

#### 8: Old Bridge

*Working together, you pry up boulders and send them tumbling down onto the enemy ranks below.*

Shake 3 Dragon Army Foot units on the left flank.

#### 9: Coordinated Reinforcements

*Message flags fly in the camps of Grikan Ghar and Belephaion. As one, their foot soldiers advance.*

Add a Dragon Army Foot unit to both the right and far right flanks.

**Minor War Engines Fire.** For each battle tile adjacent to a tile that has an objective token, roll the skirmish die. Apply 1 hit to the Alliance line matching the die result.

#### 10: Dragons from the East

*Red dragons burn a swathe from the steppes to the crossroads.*

Add 1 fire token to an Alliance line on the Steppes and Crossroads battle tiles.

#### 11: Defeat

The scenario immediately ends in a loss. Feel free to reset it and try again.

#### 12: On Leather Wings

*Snarls overhead herald the arrival of wyvern-riders from the wastes. The wyverns' huge wings stir the mists as the beasts soar low over the field of battle.*

Add a Wyvern vile champion to each of the following tiles (if in play): Mine Entrance, Mystic Path, Tomb, and Village.

#### 13: Gone to Ground

*As the battlefield shifts, the spy hides in the deep bracken.*

Roll 3 battle dice. If any Woe results are rolled, the spy is captured and the scenario immediately ends in a loss.

#### 14: Quest End: Show Your Prowess with Cavalry

*The people of the steppes are impressed and pledge their aid. Vulkak Warhoof rides with the news to the rest of his clan: they go to war against the Dragon Army.*

Add an objective token to the Command Post tile.

#### 15: Rally to the Banner

*The banners of the Dragon Army once again rise above the fray as the enemy soldiers rally.*

Add 1 unit to each Dragon Army line that's on a battle tile without a flank token. Remove 1 unit from each Alliance line that's on a battle tile without a flank token.

Move the Flying Citadel token next to the Forest Road battle tile.

#### 16: Terrible Toll

*Lohezet the Black must have located the spy. His forces move to cover the high road, which leads to safety.*

Add a vile champion to the High Road tile. Add a vile champion to 1 Woods tile without a vile champion.

#### 17: Terror in the Ranks

*Despite the efforts of Alliance commanders, dragons' terrifying roars send Alliance soldiers scrambling for cover.*

If the current defensive zone is the City of Kalaman (Zone 3), the scenario immediately ends in a loss. Otherwise, use the Retreating rules in the scenario, with the following changes:

- Remove all shaken Alliance units from the current defensive zone.
- Place only 3 Alliance units from each battle tile (not 5).
- The active flank is the flank with the fewest Alliance troops.

#### 18: Woodland Spirits

*When you ask the spirits of the haunted woods for aid, they ignore your plea.*

*(No further text.)*

#### 19: Assault on the Camp

*Fewmaster Roake sends his most loyal forces to cut off reinforcements trying to reach the Alliance camp.*

Add a vile champion to the Camp tile. If the tile is already flipped, add the vile champion to the Rocky Outcrop tile. Add a vile champion to each Woods tile without a vile champion. If no vile champions were added to Woods tiles, immediately activate the war engine's power.

#### 20: The Tomb, Continued

*Raven Uth Vogler's friendly voice carries through the mist as you emerge into a stunted forest. Across the field, Morphir the Gold sneers down from atop a throne.*

Set up the tiles as shown below, including units and commanders. Add the Center Flank token to the Stunted Forest tile, and make it the active flank. Add the Center Flank event card (4) to the bottom of the event discard pile. Add an objective token to the Haunted Woods tile.

*(Diagram: battle tile 9B with Dragon Army units, commander card C23 and the Center flank token above it, commander card C2 below it, adventure tiles 14B (with an objective token), 12A, 22B and 17B. Labelled: "These tiles are already in play" for 22B and 17B.)*

#### 21: The True Assault

*A direful prayer from Belephaion reverberates across the field of battle, making your blood run cold.*

For each portal, add Dragon Army units to the line on the adjacent battle tile with the fewest units until that line is full.

If all portals are closed, instead immediately activate the commander powers of ready Dragon Army commanders.

#### 22: Ride of the Allies

*The people of the five lands ride to the aid of Kalaman.*

For every objective token on the Command Post tile, add 1 Alliance unit of any type to any battle tile.

#### 23: Dangerous in Defeat

*The Dragon Army lines snap back into place. Kansaldi Fire-Eyes has prevented a rout, and the enemy forces are still a danger to be reckoned with.*

Add 1 unit to each Dragon Army line that's on a battle tile without a flank token. Remove 1 unit from each Alliance line that's on a battle tile without a flank token.

Move the Flying Citadel token next to the Crossroads battle tile.

#### 24: Quest End: Clear the Mines

*The Dragon Army is routed from the mines, and the captured miners cheer as they emerge into the light. The mayor keeps her word, and the fortified town readies for war.*

Move the objective token on the Mine Entrance tile to the Command Post. The mayor also sends along a warning: read scenario entry 104.

#### 25: Foothills Holdfast

*Abandoned huts and ancient ruins perch atop the foothills near the city. In the distance, you see the river cutting across the valley floor. You're almost there!*

Remove Alliance units from the Foothills battle tile equal to the total difficulty of vile champions on adventure tiles between the Forest Road and the Foothills. If the Dragon Army Skirmishers tile is still in play, remove 2 additional Alliance units.

For each Dragon Army unit that can't be placed on the Foothills or River Crossing tile, remove the unit and add a tactic token to the Dragon Army line of the unit's type on the Foothills tile.

Remove the Forest Road, Dragon Army Skirmishers (or Forest Edge), Abandoned Outpost, and Woods tiles. Place any heroes who were on those tiles on the Village tile. Move the Rising City token next to the Foothills.

#### 26: The Aurak's Brand

*An enormous illusory sigil rises from the war camp of Morphir the Gold. At her signal, war drums and horns resound, sending the draconian troops into a frenzy.*

Activate the powers of all war engines in any order. Add 1 tactic token to each Dragon Army line on the active flank.

#### 27: Mine Entrance

*You take out a small enemy force protecting a signal beacon. You light the beacon, and as smoke billows from it, chaos erupts in the Dragon Army's ranks.*

Remove 2 Dragon Army units from each battle tile.

#### 28: Abandoned Outpost

*Yesterday, this was a bustling outpost. Now the soldiers are gone, and they clearly left in a hurry. Through the mist, you hear a familiar, arrogant voice shouting orders.*

Read scenario entry 40.

#### 29: Hunted and Harried

*Enemy troops catch sight of the spy. The spy hides in the shadow of a boulder, but death is close at hand.*

Roll 4 battle dice. If any Woe is rolled, the spy is captured, and the scenario immediately ends in a loss.

#### 30: Library of Kalaman

*You find no information about where the riders will land. You do, however, find something of use.*

Draw 1 equipment card.

#### 31: Korav's Fire

*Korav the Shadow Weaver raises his hand to the sky. Fiery boulders rain down onto Alliance forces, and screams of panic fill the night air.*

Add 2 fire tokens to Alliance lines on 2 different battle tiles that have Alliance units.

#### 32: The Courier

*The courier relays a curious message: the riders marked a clue atop of the Watchtower of Kalaman.*

Add an objective token to the Watchtower.

#### 33: Quest End: Traitor's Demise

*Longfang's personal guard flees, and blades loyal to his niece seize him. Ashi Silvertongue is leader of the bandits once more. She says, "You helped me reclaim control. Now you shall see what my gratitude is worth."*

Move the objective token on the Forest Edge tile to the Command Post tile. If an objective token is on the Ancient Monolith tile, remove that token. 1 hero on the Forest Edge tile draws 1 equipment card.

#### 34: Fairy Pool

*Across the field, you spot the fairy pool you saw in the scrying pool. Eerie music calls to you through the mist.*

Flip the Pond tile to the Fairy Pool side, and add an objective token to it.

Apply 1 hit to an Alliance unit on each battle tile adjacent to a tile with a Wyvern vile champion (for tiles adjacent to 2 tiles with Wyverns, instead remove 2 units).

#### 40: Treacherous Attack

*As you step from the mist, you catch sight of Marshal Vendri behind the Alliance lines. From across the battlefield, the reedy voice of Bakaris the Traitor calls out, "Let's see what you're made of, swine!"*

If the Scrying Pool tile has been discovered and does not have an objective token, read scenario entry 34.

*(Diagram: battle tile 3A with the Far Left flank token and commander cards C25 and C6, adventure tile 24A at the top right, and adventure tiles 19B and 27A at the bottom, 27A labelled "This tile is already in play".)*

#### 41: The Horn of Takhisis

*A warhorn sounds from the right flank, as Belephaion invokes his goddess. Vile energy ripples across the battlefield, bolstering the Dragon Queen's commanders.*

Immediately activate the commander power of each ready Dragon Army commander. Refresh shaken Dragon Army commanders.

**Minor War Engines Fire.** For each battle tile adjacent to a tile that has an objective token, roll the skirmish die. Apply 1 hit to the Alliance line matching the die result.

#### 42: Into the Canyon

*Fighting free of the city, you see a vast tract of desert before you. You lead the charge into the canyon.*

Remove the City Wall and Command Post tiles. Place any heroes who were on those tiles on the Canyon. Move the Rising City token next to the Canyon.

#### 43: Night Falls on the North

*As the sun sets, any hope of securing allies sets with it. Alliances must be cemented now, or not at all.*

The scenario is over at the end of this turn.

#### 45: March of the Draconians

*A war cry echoes forth from reptilian throats: the draconians are on the march.*

Refresh all Dragon Army commanders. Immediately activate their commander powers.

The scenario is over at the end of this turn.

#### 46: The Final Stand

*Willem and the remaining soldiers dig in for one last battle. With the river at your backs, there is nowhere to go: you must stand and live, or fall and perish.*

Add a tactic token to each Dragon Army line. This turn's battle is an all-out battle instead of a skirmish.

#### 47: Battle on the Cactus Trail

*Massive cacti rise on both sides of the road. You rally the troops and press forward through the inhospitable terrain.*

Remove Alliance units from the Forest Road battle tile equal to the total difficulty of vile champions on all adventure tiles between the Canyon and Forest Road tiles.

Remove the Canyon, Bluff, Mine Entrance, Rocky Path, Forest Clearing (or Camp), and Thieves' Corner tiles (and any tokens on them). Place heroes who were on those tiles on the Forest Road. Move the Rising City token next to the Forest Road.

#### 48: Consumed by the Mists

*Your forces try to fall back before the oncoming undead, but find themselves lost in the mist. The enemy commanders rally, taking advantage of the break in the lines.*

Refresh all Dragon Army commanders, and immediately activate their commander powers.

#### 49: The Roar of Engines

*The deafening roar of war engines calls more vile champions to join the fray.*

Activate the powers of all war engines in any order. Add a vile champion to each uncrewed war engine.

If the Far Left Flank event card (7) is not in play, add it to the bottom of the event deck. If 4 or more objective tokens are on the Command Tent or Command Post tile, add the Far Right Flank event card (8) to the bottom of the event discard pile.

#### 50: Explosions or Reinforcements

*Scouts have rallied two units. They believe they can rig and explosion to blow up the gatehouse. They could instead lead the units to join the battle in the foothills. What is your command?*

**Blow Up the Gatehouse.** Roll 4 battle dice. If the result is 3 or more hits, remove any vile champion from the Gatehouse tile and flip the tile.

**Lead Units to the Foothills.** Add 2 Alliance Mounted units to the Foothills tile.

#### 51: Quest End: Deliver Equipment

*Ghaldric Longfang laughs as his troops take your equipment. From behind a monolith comes a draconian mage. Their magics stun you, giving Longfang time to flee.*

All heroes on the adventure tile take 2 stress and lose their equipment. Remove the objective token from this tile.

#### 52: Vogler Has Fallen

*Enemy troops muster behind their front lines, far more than your force can withstand. The last villagers must evacuate immediately.*

For each vile champion on the battlefield, remove 2 objective tokens from the vile champion's tile or an adjacent tile.

The scenario is over at the end of this turn.

#### 53: Gatehouse

*The gatehouse stands empty, its defenders all dead or risen as undead in the service of Lord Soth. The road beyond is desolate, eerie in the mist.*

Set up the tiles as shown below. Add an objective token to the Alliance Catapult tile.

*(Diagram: adventure tiles 23B (labelled "This tile is already in play"), 15B and 31A (with an objective token).)*
