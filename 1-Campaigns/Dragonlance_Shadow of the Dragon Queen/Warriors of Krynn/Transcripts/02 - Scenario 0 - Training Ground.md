### SCENARIO 0: TRAINING GROUND

#### Objective

Move 3 flags and 1 equipment card to the Command Tent or Command Post adventure tile (26), and defeat all the vile champions on the battlefield.

#### Scenario Setup

This page shows which game components are used in the scenario. Set up the game components as depicted here before the scenario begins.

Pay attention to how many units start on each battle tile and whether they begin the game ready or shaken. Shaken units are represented as colored outlines.

Commanders are also shown alongside the battle tile they affect. In this scenario, all commanders begin ready except Raven Uth Vogler, who is shaken, as represented by her card, which shows an outline with a diagonal line across it.

The event cards used in the scenario are detailed in the "Event Deck" section of the table to the right. Build the event deck using only these cards.

The scenario's objective is under the scenario's title, and the game components used are listed in the table on the right side of the page.

#### Diagram call-outs

- **OBJECTIVE TOKEN.** Objective tokens represent different things depending on the scenario. In this scenario, the tokens show the locations of 3 flags you need to retrieve.
- **FLAG.** Before you can spend an action to claim a flag, you need to defeat any vile champion on the adventure tile.
- **SHAKEN.** Some units start a scenario shaken, like this one.
- **SHAKEN.** Some commanders start a scenario shaken, like this one.
- **VILE CHAMPION.** This icon indicates to add a vile champion to this tile at the beginning of the scenario. Add a random Recruit vile champion unless the diagram says otherwise. Then turn it face up.

### RUNNING THIS SCENARIO

In this initial scenario, your mission features challenges that will help you learn how to play *Dragonlance: Warriors of Krynn*. Please review the rulebook before starting the scenario.

This scenario explains the organization of scenarios in this book, with new game concepts explained in the text. Use what you learn here to guide your campaign across the battlefields of Krynn.

#### Starting the Scenario

This section provides a narrative introduction to the scenario. It provides story context and hints of what might unfold as you play.

*(In red:)* Text that appears in red like this is meant to be read aloud or paraphrased. Choose a player to read this text aloud to the whole group.

#### Scenario Rules

This section details the scenario's special rules. These rules reference and build on those presented in the rulebook.

This scenario has the following special rules:

**Flags.** Objective tokens represent flags you need to gather. When you examine an objective token, place the token on the Command Tent or Command Post adventure tile. These are different sides of the same tile, and objective tokens remain if the tile is flipped.

#### Command Tent Choice

At this point, review the scenario's command tent choices, options presented by your allies that help you in different ways. As a group, choose 1 option. Follow the instructions for your choice before starting the game.

In this scenario, you have the following options:

**Fight Fires.** Fires are raging on the right flank. If you want to help get the fires under control, remove 1 fire token from the right flank.

**Send Reinforcements.** Alliance forces face staunch opposition on the left flank. If you want to reinforce that flank, add 1 Alliance Foot unit to it.

#### Key Moments

The Key Moment event card (event card 1) is in every scenario's event deck. When this card is revealed, advance the key moment tracker by 1 and read the corresponding scenario entry on the table below. A scenario ends when the last key moment is read.

| Key Moment | Scenario Entry |

#### Dragon Army Control

After the Dragon Army takes control of a battle tile, the scenario refers you to a scenario entry. In this scenario, follow these instructions each time the Dragon Army takes control of a battle tile:

**First Tile.** Read scenario entry 56.

**Second Tile.** Read scenario entry 11. The scenario immediately ends in a loss.

#### Additional Endings

This section explains special ways the scenario might end. These ending conditions are in addition to those that apply to every scenario, which are repeated below:

- The final key moment is read.
- The Dragon Army takes control of a number of battle tiles set by the scenario's "Dragon Army Control" section.
- A player has no ability cards left when their hero's stress would increase beyond the maximum.
- All the battle tiles become controlled.

In addition to the usual ending conditions, this scenario ends when all 3 objective tokens and a hero with an equipment card are on the Command Tent or Command Post adventure tile and no vile champions remain on the battlefield.

#### Aftermath

Check the conditions below to determine whether the scenario ended in a win, hold, or loss, then go to the appropriate section. In other scenarios, you'll gain rewards depending on the outcome.

**Win.** You returned all 3 flags, you defeated all the vile champions, and a hero with an equipment card is on the Command Tent or Command Post tile.

**Hold.** The scenario ends before you completed all tasks, but the Dragon Army hasn't taken control of 2 battle tiles.

**Loss.** The Dragon Army took control of 2 battle tiles, or a hero had no cards in their hand when taking stress beyond the maximum.

After you complete this scenario, you can either try it again or begin the campaign.

##### Win

You performed above and beyond the call of duty! Your training is complete. Feel free to proceed with the following scenarios in this book.

##### Hold

You did just fine. A hold result is itself an achievement!

##### Loss

The Alliance loses.
