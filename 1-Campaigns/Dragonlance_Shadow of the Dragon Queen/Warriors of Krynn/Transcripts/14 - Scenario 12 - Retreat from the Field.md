### SCENARIO 12: RETREAT FROM THE FIELD

#### Objective

Crush the remnants of the Dragon Army. Remove as many Dragon Army units as possible.

#### Your Valor Is Known

- If the heroes have 12+ honor cards, each hero removes 1 of their ability cards.
- If the heroes have 14+ honor cards, also add the Your Reputation Precedes You event card (40) to the event deck.

### RUNNING THIS SCENARIO

#### Starting the Scenario

After setting up this scenario, read the following text aloud:

*(red)* *Darrett Highwater and Marshal Vendri stand atop the battlements of Kalaman, watching the chaos surrounding the city. Even as Kalaman's forces and Dragon Army troops continue to clash, the flying citadel is crumbling, and stony shards fall onto the battlefield below.*

*Marshal Vendri turns to you. Relief shows on her face as she says, "This mess, as bad as it looks, is better than how this day could have turned out. All that's left now is to turn this chaos into a true victory."*

*"We're headed to defend the gates," Darrett says. "We need your help rallying our troops beyond. Together, we'll drive the enemy from the field. This is the day, finally, where the tide turns in our favor!"*

#### Scenario Rules

This scenario has the following special rules:

**Idle Troops.** At the beginning of the scenario, some battle tiles don't have flank tokens. If there isn't an active flank when a battle would take place, instead remove 3 Alliance units on each battle tile without a flank token.

**Placing Commanders.** A hero may spend 1 action on a battle tile without a flank token to add a flank token and 1 of the following Alliance commanders not in play: Hana Shadowbolt, Raven Uth Vogler, Thea of the Red Robes, Tatina Rookledust, or Willem the Steady. Commanders enter play ready.

**Adding Event Cards.** When you place a commander on a battle tile, find the event card associated with that flank (event cards 5-8) and add it to the bottom of the event discard pile. Then add 2 Alliance units on that battle tile and make it the active flank.

##### Alliance Takes Control

When the Alliance takes control of a battle tile, follow these steps instead of the standard rules:

- Remove all tokens from that battle tile.
- Remove both commanders on that battle tile.
- 1 Alliance unit of each type on that tile may be placed onto 1 other battle tile connected by road tiles. Alliance units can't be moved along roads across tiles with vile champions or through Dragon Army-controlled battle tiles. Any units that can't be moved remain where they are.
- Flip the battle tile's flank token to the inactive side. Do not place it on a new battle tile. The next time that flank's event card is revealed, remove it and reveal another event card.
- Choose another flank to become the active flank.

##### Dragon Army Takes Control

When the Dragon Army takes control of a battle tile, follow these steps instead of the standard rules:

- Remove both commanders on that battle tile.
- Flip the flank token to its inactive side. Choose another flank to become the active flank.
- Add a stop token to that battle tile.
- The next time that flank's event card is revealed, remove it and reveal another event card.

#### Command Tent Choice

Before you leave, Vendri offers aid for your mission. Choose 1 option, which occurs immediately:

**Deploy Reserves.** Vendri sends troops to reinforce a contested battlefront. Add 2 Alliance units to a battle tile.

**Dispatch Dragonslayers.** Vendri sends knights to deal with a dragon attacking a nearby village. Remove 1 Mounted unit from either the Farmstead or the Fields battle tile, and remove the Red Dragon vile champion on the Village adventure tile.

#### Dragon Army Seizes Control

After the Dragon Army takes control of a battle tile, follow these instructions:

**First Tile.** Read scenario entry 102.

**Second Tile.** Read scenario entry 136. The scenario immediately ends in a loss.

#### Additional Endings

This scenario has the following special ending condition:

- 5 or fewer Dragon Army units are in play.

#### Aftermath

*(red)* *Kalaman's commanders try their best to recover from a day none will soon forget. Marshal Vendri calls for quiet.*

Determine the scenario's outcome:

**Win.** 5 or fewer Dragon Army units were in play at the end.

**Hold.** 6 to 29 Dragon Army units were in play at the end.

**Loss.** 30 or more Dragon Army units were in play at the end, or another loss condition occurred.

##### Win

*(red)* *Vendri says, "What we've done here today will be remembered. You, the defenders of Kalaman, have earned our place in the history of this great city!"*

Take the Saviors of Kalaman reputation card and an available medal card.

##### Hold

*(red)* *Vendri says, "The Dragon Army has been dealt a crushing blow. Our foes will not return soon, and for that you should be proud."*

Take the Saviors of Kalaman reputation card.

##### Loss

*(red)* *Vendri says, "I fear the Dragon Army will return soon. We must remain vigilant, but thanks to you, Kalaman still stands."*
