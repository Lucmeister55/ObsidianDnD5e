### TIERS OF PLAY

The campaign has 3 tiers of play: Recruit, Veteran, and Legendary. The first 4 scenarios of the campaign are the Recruit tier, with the Veteran and Legendary components removed. When the heroes reach the latter tiers, those components are added into the game, and other aspects of game play change as well.

#### Veteran Tier (Scenarios 5-8)

At Veteran tier, add the Veteran class cards, unit cards, war engine cards, and commander cards to the game.

At the start of each Veteran-tier scenario, you get your class's Veteran class card in addition to your Recruit class card. You can use the class powers of both. Some class powers at the Veteran tier require you to remove ability cards rather than just discarding them.

Your maximum stress increases to 5, and you start with 6 ability cards: the 4 shown on your Recruit class card and the 2 on your Veteran class card.

##### Veteran Vile Champions

Two vile champions are introduced at the Veteran tier: the Aurak Draconian and the Wyvern. Veteran vile champions are added only by effects that identify them by name, never by effects that add a randomly chosen vile champion, and they are never shuffled into the pile of Recruit vile champions. For each Veteran vile champion on a tile that a hero moves off, the hero takes 2 stress.

##### Your Valor Is Known

At the start of each Veteran- or Legendary-tier scenario, count the honor cards earned during the campaign. Each scenario specifies a number of honor cards that represents drawing the Dragon Army's attention. If the heroes have earned at least that many honor cards, the Dragon Army's strength increases for that scenario. Each scenario also specifies an even greater number of honor cards to signify that the Dragon Army considers the heroes a serious threat. If the heroes have earned at least that many honor cards, add the Your Reputation Precedes You event card (40) to the event deck at the start of the scenario.

#### Legendary Tier (Scenarios 9-12)

At the Legendary tier, add the Legendary class cards, unit cards, war engine cards, and commander cards.

At the start of each Legendary-tier scenario, you get your class's Legendary class card in addition to your class's Recruit and Veteran cards. You can use the class powers of any of your class cards.

Your maximum stress increases to 6, and you start with 8 ability cards: the 4 shown on your Recruit class card, the 2 on your Veteran class card, and the 2 on your Legendary class card.

##### Legendary Vile Champions

Two vile champions are introduced at the Legendary tier: the Death Dragon and the Red Dragon. Legendary vile champions work similarly to Veteran vile champions, but they also make their tile a stop tile, and they have event cards that add them to the battlefield.

### D&D COMPANION ADVENTURE

This game is designed to work with the D&D adventure *Dragonlance: Shadow of the Dragon Queen*. Dungeon Masters can use this game to play through that adventure's battles on a grand scale. The adventure explores how results from this game carry over into D&D. Players are encouraged to narrate their turns as they would in D&D and can use their characters' minis instead of the hero figures provided in this game.

#### Role of the Dungeon Master

If you are a Dungeon Master including this game in your D&D adventure, you can participate in playing this game's scenarios in two ways: you can play a hero (as long as the total number of players is 5 or fewer), or you can bring the role of Dungeon Master to this game by reading narrative text and scenario entries and by rolling dice and revealing cards for the Dragon Army.

#### Portraying Player Characters

The details of D&D characters are abstracted in this game as ability cards and class powers; this keeps the focus on the epic battles being played out. To approximate D&D characters, encourage players to choose class cards that align with their D&D character's class features.

At a DM's discretion, characters might further customize their hero by swapping their ability cards. Record whatever adjustments players make to their class cards to keep them consistent between scenarios.
