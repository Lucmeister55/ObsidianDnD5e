### RUNNING THIS SCENARIO

#### Starting the Scenario

After setting up this scenario, read the following text aloud:

*(red)* *Following Lord Bakaris's catastrophic attack, the conflict between Kalaman's defenders and the Dragon Army has grown dire. Forced to seek every strategic advantage, you find yourself amid the Daargard Mountains southwest of Kalaman. Commander Graystone and his aides, a Solamnic knight named Lord Deveral and the clever gnome inventor Tatina Rookledust, sought to find a swift route through the rugged lands. Any hope of the troops' movement remaining a secret evaporated when Dragon Army forces appeared.*

*"The Dragon Army has blocked all the usual passes through the mountains. I need options," Commander Graystone says in the wind-whipped command tent. He turns to you. "Scout the wilderness and any ruins you find. The people who dwelled in this region used to keep passages circumventing the mountains. At least one of them must have survived the Cataclysm."*

*The commander reaches for his helm. "Get moving. And watch out for wyverns. The Dragon Army has been capturing wyverns in the region, and that's a whole other problem we don't need."*

#### Scenario Rules

This scenario has the following special rule:

**Scouting.** Objective tokens represent locations to scout. When a hero examines an objective, remove the objective token and read the corresponding scenario entry.

#### Command Tent Choice

Before you leave, Deveral offers aid for your mission. Choose 1 option, which occurs immediately:

**Attack the War Engine.** Deveral orders an assault on the Gnomish Flamethrower. Remove 1 Alliance Mounted unit from the left and center flanks, and remove the Gnomish Flamethrower card and its war engine token.

**Send Scouts to Survey the Terrain.** Deveral sends scouts up the mountain pass to survey the terrain for easier passage. Remove 2 Alliance Missile units from the left flank, and add a wilderness token to the Mountains battle tile.

#### Dragon Army Control

After the Dragon Army takes control of a battle tile, follow these instructions:

**First Tile.** Read scenario entry 114.

**Second Tile.** Read scenario entry 144. The scenario immediately ends in a loss.

#### Additional Endings

This scenario has the following special ending condition:

- The heroes remove all 7 objective tokens.

#### Aftermath

*(red)* *Tatina Rookledust threads her way between the exhausted soldiers marching through the mountains. A few sharp words move aside those who block the inventor's path as she approaches you.*

Determine the scenario's outcome:

**Win.** The heroes scouted 7 locations.

**Hold.** The heroes scouted 5 to 6 locations.

**Loss.** The heroes scouted 4 or fewer locations, or another loss condition occurred.

#### Sidebar: Draconians

*(Black box.)* Created by evil mages and clerics of the Dragon Queen, draconians are draconic monstrosities that serve as the spies, shock troops, and elite agents of the Dragon Army. Vicious and remorseless, draconians obey their commanders without question until death.

##### Win

*(red)* *Tatina says, "Thanks to you, our passage through the valley was smooth like a greased piston. Well done, wayfinders!"*

Take the Far Scouts reputation card and an available medal card.

##### Hold

*(red)* *Tatina says, "We took some losses getting through, but that's to be expected, just a bit of grit in the oil. Get some rest. You've earned it."*

Take the Far Scouts reputation card.

##### Loss

*(red)* *Tatina says, "That went as smoothly as a novice inventor's first test flight. I've got some gadgets in the works that might be helpful next time."*

At the beginning of the next scenario, the heroes will get extra equipment and tactic tokens (the loss bonus).
