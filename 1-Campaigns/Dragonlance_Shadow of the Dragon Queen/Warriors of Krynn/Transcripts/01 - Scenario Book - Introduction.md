### INTRODUCTION

This book presents scenarios for *Dragonlance: Warriors of Krynn*, tales of fantasy adventure and warfare. Use the rules presented in the rulebook to run these scenarios as a complete campaign, or choose your favorite scenarios to run as single-session battles. This section details what you'll find in this book and how to use it in your *Warriors of Krynn* games.

#### Using This Book

This scenario book provides dramatic battlefield en[counters] that use [and build on the rulebook. Make sure] you['ve] read the rulebook before using this book. Guid[ance t]hroughout this book supplements the game's rules and, unless a scenario states otherwise, does not replace those rules.

#### Training Ground

The first scenario in this book, "Training Ground," provides additional guidance to help you learn how to play *Dragonlance: Warriors of Krynn* and explains how the scenarios in this book are organized. This scenario isn't related to the story told through the other scenarios in this book and can be skipped if you already know how to play.

#### Campaign Play

The scenarios in this book tell a tale of the wicked Dragon Army's invasion of Solamnia. The introduction on the facing page establishes the origins of this conflict, while red text throughout this book develops the heroes' place in the war against the Dragon Army. Throughout the campaign, the heroes' role in the war grows, and their missions become more difficult. As they progress from scenario to scenario, the heroes will earn rewards based on their successes throughout the course of the story.

#### Tracking Campaign Progress

The Campaign Chronicle in the back of this book provides a place to track developments from your campaign. While playing a campaign, track your achievements using this sheet.

#### Skipping Scenarios

If you skip a scenario, give the heroes the reputation card for that scenario (as if they had achieved a result of Hold).

#### Ending the Campaign

After completing all the scenarios, read the "Campaign Conclusion" section on page 28 of this book. This section explains how the campaign resolves based on the number of honor cards you've gained throughout the campaign.

#### Single-Scenario Play

If you want to play scenarios individually without tracking campaign progress, you can play each scenario as a stand-alone challenge without adjustment.

#### Playing D&D Characters

While this game provides heroes for your use, whose backstories you can find on the inside back cover, the rulebook also presents guidance for using your own Dungeons & Dragons characters as the game's heroes. Consult with the Dungeon Master of your D&D roleplaying game to determine how the events of this game affect your roleplaying adventures.
