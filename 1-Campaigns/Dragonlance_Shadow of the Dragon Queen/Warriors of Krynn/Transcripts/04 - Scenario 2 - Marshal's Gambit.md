### RUNNING THIS SCENARIO

#### Starting the Scenario

After setting up this scenario, read the following text aloud:

*(red)* *After the retreat from Vogler, the villagers fled to the nearby city of Kalaman. There, the city's leaders requested aid from you and others who faced the Dragon Army and survived.*

*"Adventurers. Thank you for coming," says Marshal Vendri, leader of Kalaman's military, as you enter the command tent. "Darrett Highwater speaks highly of your valor."*

*The marshal goes on to explain the situation, both to you and to others you know from Vogler who now find themselves among Vendri's commanders. Several days ago, the marshal dispatched a spy to gather intelligence about these invaders. Now this spy is ready to make a break for safety. It is your task to locate the spy and escort them to the command tent while Kalaman's forces engage the Dragon Army in a mass distraction. If you also find defenders left behind during the retreat, all the better.*

*"I cannot overstate your importance to the battle ahead," Marshal Vendri says. "Find our spy before the Dragon Army does, and bring the spy safely back to the camp. Then we might have a better sense of what we're up against."*

#### Scenario Rules

This scenario has the following special rules:

**Spy.** The objective token on the right flank represents the spy. The spy isn't a unit, and it can't take hits or be affected by cards.

**Extracting the Spy.** To extract the spy, a hero needs to win a Dexterity conflict while on the right flank. The difficulty of the conflict equals the number of Dragon Army units on the right flank. If the hero is successful, place the spy on their hero board.

**Escorting the Spy.** The spy moves with the hero whose hero board they're on. The spy may be moved between heroes using the same rules as for transferring equipment between heroes.

**Capturing the Spy.** If the hero with the spy leaves a tile with a vile champion, roll 3 battle dice for each vile champion on that tile. Any Woe results cause the spy to be captured and the scenario to end in a loss. The roll's results can't be modified.

#### Command Tent Choice

Before you leave, Vendri offers aid for your mission. Choose 1 option, which occurs immediately:

**Contribute Funds.** Vendri provides additional funds. Take the Bag of Iron Coins card from the equipment deck, give it to a hero, then shuffle the equipment deck.

**Create a Diversion.** Vendri sends soldiers to create a diversion. Shake 2 Dragon Army units on the right flank.

#### Dragon Army Control

After the Dragon Army takes control of a battle tile, follow these instructions:

**Right Flank.** The scenario immediately ends in a loss.

**First Tile.** Read scenario entry 12.

**Second Tile.** Read scenario entry 29.

**Third Tile.** Read scenario entry 141. The scenario immediately ends in a loss.

#### Additional Endings

This scenario has the following special ending conditions:

- The Dragon Army captures the spy.
- The spy is escorted to the Command Tent or Command Post tile.
- The Dragon Army takes control of the right flank.

#### Aftermath

*(red)* *Dusty from the battlefield, Darrett finds you just as the moons Solinari and Lunitari rise. He passes around a few waterskins and lifts his hand in salute.*

Determine the scenario's outcome:

**Win.** The spy returned to the Command Tent or Command Post tile before the third key moment.

**Hold.** The spy returned to the Command Tent or Command Post tile after the third key moment.

**Loss.** The spy did not return to the Command Tent or Command Post tile, or another loss condition occurred.

#### Sidebar: The Dragon Army

*(Black box.)* The Dragon Army unites legions of wicked humans, draconians, dragon-like dragonnels, and other monsters under the banner of the evil god Takhisis the Dragon Queen. Few lands can stand against this vast army's devastating might. When the Dragon Army meets resistance to its invasions, its leaders unleash overwhelming forces and foul magic to crush their foes.

##### Win

*(red)* *Darrett says, "Outstanding work today. Thanks to the spy's warnings, we now have a better sense of what we're up against."*

Take the Invisible Agents reputation card and an available medal card.

##### Hold

*(red)* *Darrett says, "Vendri's spy made it back safely. Hopefully they learned something."*

Take the Invisible Agents reputation card.

##### Loss

*(red)* *Darrett says, "We lost a good one out there. Now I suspect we'll learn about our foe the hard way."*

At the beginning of the next scenario, the heroes will get extra equipment and tactic tokens (the loss bonus).
