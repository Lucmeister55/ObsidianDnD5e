## Rulebook p. 6, Building Your Hero

*Photo: [[07 - Rulebook p6 - Building Your Hero.jpg]]. Text inside the small card illustrations is partly too small to read and is only summarised.*

### BUILDING YOUR HERO

At the start of a scenario, you choose a hero figure, a hero board and stress marker, and a Recruit class card. One side of each hero board has the name and an illustration of a hero from the world of Krynn. Backstories for these heroes appear in the scenario book. Alternatively, you may use the nameless side of a hero board and your class card choice to represent any hero you imagine.

Whenever you start a new scenario, you may keep the same hero or build a new one.

#### Hero Board and Stress Marker

Hero boards include a reminder of actions you can take on your turn, as well as a stress tracker. As your stress changes, move the stress marker along the stress tracker. For Recruit-tier scenarios, your maximum stress is 4; for Veteran-tier scenarios, it's 5; and for Legendary-tier scenarios, it's 6. See "Stress" on page 8 for more details.

*(Pictured: Levna Drakehorn's hero board, labelled "Hero Board" and "Stress Marker". Options during your action phase: activate a class power, initiate a conflict, initiate an encounter, move your hero, play free action cards, put out a fire, transfer equipment. The stress track runs 0 (skull), 1, 2, 3, 4, then 5 (Veteran) and 6 (Legendary).)*

#### Class Cards

At the Recruit tier, you get your class's Recruit class card, which determines your hero's 2 mastered abilities, the 4 ability cards you will have in your hand, and the 3 class powers you will have access to.

At Veteran tier, you'll also get your class's Veteran class card, and at Legendary tier, you'll also get your class's Veteran and Legendary class cards. These cards will grant you additional starting ability cards and access to more class powers (see "Tiers of Play" on page 12).

*(Pictured: the Fighter Recruit class card, with its back showing the ability cards STR, STR, CON, DEX, and its front showing the mastered abilities STR and CON and three class powers. Labels: Class Card, Ability Cards, Mastered Abilities, Class Powers.)*

#### Mastered Abilities

Your hero has 2 mastered abilities set by their class. Mastered abilities help you during encounters and conflicts you face in scenarios.

#### Class Powers

Class powers represent your hero's class-specific abilities. You may activate one of your hero's class powers by discarding an ability card. For example, a barbarian may discard a Constitution ability card to activate one of their Constitution-based powers or a Strength ability card to activate their Strength-based power.

*(Pictured: the Fighter card's three powers. STR Action, Mighty Strike: "Defeat a vile champion. Take stress equal to their difficulty." STR Response, Surprise Attack: "At the start of battle. Shake 2 Dragon Army units on your tile. They don't add to combat strength this battle." CON Response, Unbreakable: "At the start of battle. If the Alliance commander is ready, Alliance wins ties in combat strength this battle.")*

Class powers can be used only at specific times. Those labeled "Action," like the barbarian's Awake power, can be activated during the action phase on your turn, at the cost of 1 action and the associated ability card. Those labeled "Response," like the Rage power, can be activated by discarding the associated ability card in the circumstance listed.

You can use each class power only once per turn, but you may use multiple different powers during the same turn. Unless a class power specifically states otherwise, it affects only the tile your hero is on when you use the power.

#### Ability Cards

Ability cards represent your hero's range of abilities. Some ability cards must be played during the action phase on your turn, and some can be played as a response on any player's turn if their requirements are met. An ability card's power affects the tile your hero is on. Ability cards can also be discarded to activate class powers.

*(Pictured: one card of each ability type with the sample effect printed on it.)*

| Ability | Colour | Sample card shown |
|---|---|---|
| Strength (STR) | Red | Response, after battle dice are rolled: roll 1 additional die. |
| Dexterity (DEX) | Green | Free action: move exactly 1 tile. |
| Constitution (CON) | Yellow | Free action: reduce the stress of a hero on your tile by 2. |
| Intelligence (INT) | Teal | Free action: add 1 tactic token to your tile. |
| Wisdom (WIS) | Blue | Response, after battle dice are rolled: reroll up to 2 dice. |
| Charisma (CHA) | Orange | Free action: refresh 2 units on your tile. |

Throughout the game, abilities use the colors shown above and the following abbreviations:

**STR:** Strength. **DEX:** Dexterity. **CON:** Constitution. **INT:** Intelligence. **WIS:** Wisdom. **CHA:** Charisma.

## Rulebook p. 7, Key Terms

*Photo: [[08 - Rulebook p7 - Key Terms.jpg]]. *

### KEY TERMS

#### Action Timing

A card or power with action timing can be played during your turn's action phase. See "2: Action Phase" on page 8 for more details.

#### Active Flank

The active flank is the battle tile where units are presently locked in combat. A battle tile can become the active flank due to an event card or another effect. When a battle tile becomes the active flank, flip its flank token to the green Active Flank side. That tile remains the active flank until another event card or effect makes another tile the active flank. Only 1 tile at a time is the active flank. When a new tile becomes the active flank, flip the previous tile's flank token to the red side. The active flank can change many times over the course of a scenario.

#### Adjacent/Neighboring Tiles

Tiles are adjacent to each other if they touch. Battle tiles are neighboring if they are either adjacent to each other or separated from each other only by adventure tiles.

#### Battle Line

A battle line, or just line, is a row of units on a battle tile. The Alliance and the Dragon Army each have a line for each unit type: Foot, Missile, and Mounted.

#### Discard Pile

Each player has their own discard pile for ability cards they discard or play. Additionally, the event deck and each unit card deck have their own discard piles. A card in a discard pile is still in play, it hasn't been removed.

#### Free Action Timing

Some cards and powers have the words "Free Action" printed on them, which means that you can play them as free actions during your turn's action phase (see "Play Free Action Cards" on page 10).

#### Place a Hero

Some cards and powers let you place a hero on a tile instead of moving there. When placing a hero, ignore all the tiles between the hero's origin and the destination; any vile champions or stop tiles between those tiles don't affect the hero.

#### Ready/Shaken

Commanders and units on the battlefield are either ready or shaken. They show whether they are ready or shaken in the following ways:

**Commanders.** Each commander card has a ready side and a shaken side.

**Units.** When a unit is ready, the solid side of its marker faces up. When a ready unit takes a hit, it becomes shaken, and its marker is flipped over so the hollow side is up. If a shaken unit is hit, it is removed.

#### Refresh/Shake

Refreshing a shaken commander or unit makes it ready. When text says to Shake a commander or unit, make that commander or unit shaken.

#### Remove

When a card or an object is removed, it is no longer considered to be in play or on the battlefield. When units are removed, they return to the pool of available unit markers that can be added to the battlefield. When non-event cards are removed, they return to play at the beginning of the next scenario unless an effect returns them to play sooner.

#### Response Timing

Some cards and powers have the word "Response" printed on them, which means that they may be used only in response to certain conditions. If a card or power must be played as a response, it states the condition that allows it to be played. For example, several cards and powers can be played only "At the start of battle" or "After battle dice are rolled."

Many cards and powers have the response timing "After battle dice are rolled." A hero can use them after battle dice are rolled in a conflict, in a battle, or due to a commander power, as long as the hero is on the affected tile. A hero can play these cards and powers after dice are rolled but before the results are resolved.

#### Reveal

To reveal a card, turn it over so its text is visible to all players, resolve its effects, and place it on top of the discard pile.
