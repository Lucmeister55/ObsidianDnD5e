### RUNNING THIS SCENARIO

#### Starting the Scenario

After setting up this scenario, read the following text aloud:

*(red)* *An unknown army has mustered, and it marches on the peaceful village of Vogler. In a command tent hastily assembled at the community's edge, the stalwart Knight of Solamnia Becklin Uth Viharin explains the situation to you and the local militia.*

*"This is not a battle that can be won," Becklin says. "Rather, the goal is to buy time for the people of Vogler to escape. The panicked villagers must be found and escorted to ships waiting along the coast. It won't be easy, though. Enemy forces are hunting for evacuees. Worse, a fire-throwing war machine has been spotted behind enemy lines, preparing to sow chaos from out of reach."*

*Becklin gives her orders to her attentive squire, Darrett Highwater, and to a nervous-looking militia member named Willem, who returns her salute with shaking hands. She then turns to you and says, "We'll focus on the enemy. You see the people to safety." She draws her blade. "Today we answer the calling of true Solamnic knights: Est solarus oth mithas," Becklin says, quoting the motto of her knighthood. "My honor is my life."*

#### Scenario Rules

This scenario has the following special rules:

**Villagers.** Objective tokens represent villagers trying to escape the enemy.

**Moving Villagers.** When a hero examines an objective, move that objective token to an adjacent tile. Objective tokens may not be moved onto an adventure tile with a vile champion.

#### Command Tent Choice

Before you leave, Darrett offers aid for your mission. Choose 1 option, which occurs immediately:

**Direct Villagers to the Evacuation Point.** Darrett orders militia members to direct a group of villagers to the evacuation point. Move 2 objective tokens on the Command Tent tile to the far right flank.

**Steady Willem's Nerves.** Darrett helps the nervous militia member Willem steel his resolve before the battle. Refresh Willem the Guard.

#### Dragon Army Control

After the Dragon Army takes control of a battle tile, follow these instructions:

**First Tile.** Read scenario entry 113.

**Second Tile.** Read scenario entry 89. The scenario immediately ends in a loss.

#### Additional Endings

This scenario has the following special ending conditions:

- All objective tokens in play are on the Coast tile with at least 1 hero.
- 3 or fewer objective tokens are in play.

#### Aftermath

*(red)* *You managed to escape alongside survivors from Vogler. The young knight Darrett Highwater approaches you. "I confirmed it, Commander Becklin fell in battle," he says. "She was an incredible leader. May her god, Paladine the Valiant Warrior, keep her soul."*

Determine the scenario's outcome:

**Win.** 8 to 12 villagers were saved.

**Hold.** 4 to 7 villagers were saved.

**Loss.** 3 or fewer villagers were saved, or another loss condition occurred.

#### Sidebar: Knights of Solamnia

*(Black box.)* The Knights of Solamnia are a chivalrous order known for their bravery and pride. Sworn protectors of the lands of Solamnia, they adhere to a strict code of honor, and dozens of legendary heroes have risen from their ranks. To a Solamnic knight, honor is more important than life.

##### Win

*(red)* *Darrett also says, "If Commander Becklin were here, she'd be proud. Because of you, the heart of Vogler survives."*

Take the Saviors of Vogler reputation card and an available medal card.

##### Hold

*(red)* *Darrett goes on to say, "Though many were lost, you did all you could. That will count for something in the days ahead."*

Take the Saviors of Vogler reputation card.

##### Loss

*(red)* *Darrett also says, "The loss of life at Vogler is heartbreaking. You did all you could. Don't blame yourselves."*

At the beginning of the next scenario, you'll draw 2 equipment cards, give them to any hero or heroes, and add 2 tactic tokens to any Alliance lines. (Applying this loss bonus is step 11 of "Setting Up a Scenario" in the rulebook.)
