### RUNNING THIS SCENARIO

#### Starting the Scenario

After setting up this scenario, read the following text aloud:

*(red)* *The ruins known as the City of Lost Names lie ahead, hidden by towering, natural canyon walls. Leading into the city is a single route, choked long ago by a solid wall constructed between the canyon's walls. Here the Dragon Army made its camp, and here the Alliance planned to attack.*

*The siege went well at first. Your allies had caught the Dragon Army off guard. But then Dragon Army reinforcements appeared. Now you find yourself caught between the enemy within the ruined city ahead and foes closing in from the wasteland behind you.*

*The roar of the Dragon Army troops shakes the earth as you struggle to hear the voice of Commander Willem. "Things are bad. Someone has to get into the city and put an end to whatever foulness they're plotting in those ruins.*

*"Scouts found what they think is a tunnel into the ruins, but we were cut off from them before they could send details. I need you to gather intelligence from around the battlefield and find this passage. Slip inside and do what you can to end whatever they're doing in those ruins. It's the only way any of us are going to survive."*

#### Scenario Rules

This scenario has the following special rules:

**Lost Information.** Objective tokens represent information about the secret passage.

**Gaining Information.** When a hero examines an objective, move the objective token to the Command Tent or Command Post tile.

**Secret Passage.** To find the secret passage, a hero must succeed at a Wisdom conflict while on the Watchtower adventure tile. The difficulty is the combined number of Dragon Army units on both the far left and far right flanks. Each objective token on the Command Tent or Command Post adds 1 hit to the roll. If a hero succeeds at this conflict, they find the secret entrance, and the scenario ends in a win.

#### Command Tent Choice

Before you leave, Willem offers aid for your mission. Choose 1 option, which occurs immediately:

**Deploy Reserve Troops.** Willem readies reserves for action. Flip the Command Tent adventure tile, and add the Reinforcements event card (11) to the bottom of the event deck.

**Prepare Other Commanders for Battle.** Willem detaches troops to assist other commanders. Remove 1 unit from the center flank, and Refresh any Alliance commander. Repeat this up to 3 times.

After the Dragon Army takes control of a battle tile, follow these instructions:

**First Tile.** Read scenario entry 110.

**Second Tile.** Read scenario entry 112. The scenario immediately ends in a loss.

#### Additional Endings

This scenario has the following special ending condition:

- The heroes find the secret passage.

#### Aftermath

*(red)* *As the battle continues, the Dragon Army closes in on the outnumbered Kalaman troops. Across the battlefield, the blue and gold of Kalaman's troops are nearly lost in a sea of crimson. Your hunt for the secret passage into the City of Lost Names comes to an end.*

Determine the scenario's outcome:

**Win.** The secret passage was found before the fourth key moment occurred.

**Hold.** The secret passage was found after the fourth key moment occurred.

**Loss.** The secret passage wasn't found before the scenario ended, or another loss condition occurred.

#### Sidebar: Dragons

*(Black box.)* Once dragons were creatures of legend, stories told to children. Now they have returned to the world of Krynn. Droves of evil chromatic dragons serve the Dragon Army, spreading ruin and furthering the Dragon Queen's conquests.

##### Win

*(red)* *The heroes head into the passage and onto the City of Lost Names.*

Take the Infiltrators reputation card and an available medal card.

##### Hold

*(red)* *The heroes follow the passage to the City of Lost Names.*

Take the Infiltrators reputation card.

##### Loss

*(red)* *The secret passage remains hidden. Desperate, you rejoin your allies on the battlefield and retreat with them. You'll have to find another way into the City of Lost Names.*

At the beginning of the next scenario, the heroes will get extra equipment and tactic tokens (the loss bonus).
