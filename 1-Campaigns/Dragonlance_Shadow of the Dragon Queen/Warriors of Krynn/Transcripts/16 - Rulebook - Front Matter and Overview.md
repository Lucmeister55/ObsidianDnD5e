### OVERVIEW

*Dragonlance: Warriors of Krynn* is a cooperative battle game in which 3 to 5 players join the War of the Lance, with each person playing a hero who aids the valiant Alliance against the Dragon Queen's invading army. The war unfolds in a multiple-scenario campaign, with play progressing through 3 tiers (Recruit, Veteran, and Legendary) as the heroes take on greater challenges. In each of the scenarios, the heroes bolster troops, harry enemy units, and carry out side missions, all with the aim of overcoming the terrifying might of the Dragon Army.

#### The First Time You Play

Before playing for the first time, punch out the game's components and open the Recruit cards. Set aside the Veteran and Legendary cards and tokens for later use. You'll also want to read the rest of this rulebook.

Once you've set up the game, start playing with scenario 0, "Training Grounds," in the scenario book.

#### Playing through Scenarios

After scenario 0, the campaign's scenarios should be played in order. Each scenario tells you how it ends and provides you any special rules it requires. Depending on how well the heroes meet the scenario's objective, they can earn rewards that increase their capabilities the next time you play. At the end of each scenario, record the results in the Campaign Chronicle on the back of the scenario book, and take any honor cards earned for use in subsequent scenarios.

For scenarios 1-4, use only Recruit cards and tokens. For scenarios 5-8, add in Veteran cards and tokens. For scenarios 9-12, add in Legendary cards and tokens.

#### Turn Sequence

In every scenario, the heroes each take turns as they try to meet the scenario's objectives. When it's your hero's turn, you play through the following phases in order: (1) event phase, (2) action phase, (3) respite phase, and (4) battle phase. Those phases are detailed in the "On Your Turn" section (page 8). After your turn, play passes to the next player in clockwise order, and that player goes through the same phases.

The group keeps playing until you reach one of the scenario's end conditions. For more information on how to finish a scenario, see "Ending a Scenario" on page 11.

#### Sidebar: Combine with D&D

You can enhance *Warriors of Krynn* by playing it in concert with the Dungeons & Dragons roleplaying game adventure *Dragonlance: Shadow of the Dragon Queen*. "D&D Companion Adventure" on page 12 explains how to combine these experiences.
