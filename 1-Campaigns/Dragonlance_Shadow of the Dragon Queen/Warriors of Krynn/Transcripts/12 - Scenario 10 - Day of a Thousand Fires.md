### SCENARIO 10: DAY OF A THOUSAND FIRES

#### Objective

Gain support from the leaders of 5 far-flung lands.

#### Your Valor Is Known

- If the heroes have 10+ honor cards, activate the commander powers of Red Ruin and Grikan Ghar.
- If the heroes have 12+ honor cards, also add the Your Reputation Precedes You event card (40) to the event deck.

#### About this scenario *(text beside the diagram)*

This scenario spans 5 different lands, each represented by a lone battle tile with connected adventure tiles. These lands each have a leader with a quest. It's up to the heroes to win these leaders' aid by speaking with them and completing their quests.

At the start of the scenario, the heroes' allies have already contacted 2 leaders who have made requests (see the "Initial Quests" section), but the heroes can contact the other leaders and learn about their quests as the "Scenario Rules" section details.

### RUNNING THIS SCENARIO

#### Starting the Scenario

After setting up this scenario, read the following text aloud:

*(red)* *Darrett says, "The Dragon Army has taken the fight to us on all sides, but Rookledust's device can transport you beyond their blockade. You need to engage the enemy and also enlist local leaders for the battles to come. I've established communication with five local leaders, who are expecting you. Two have offered support in exchange for aid. Bransad Wheathair's land is beset by a vile beast. And the Bandit King, Ghaldric Longfang, he wants supplies, but he's fickle in his loyalties, so be careful.*

*"Find out what the other leaders want, and do your best to win them over. Only united do we have a chance."*

#### Scenario Rules

This scenario has the following special rules:

**Quests.** Objective tokens represent quests to gain support from the ruler of a land. Objective tokens on battle tiles show where you can meet with leaders, while objective tokens anywhere else show where quests can be completed. Objective tokens on the Command Tent or Command Post tile represent having leaders' support.

**Mystic Path.** Tatina Rookledust has created a device that transports you between lands. You may spend 1 action to activate Rookledust's Traversal Rig, take 1 stress, and place yourself on the Mystic Path adventure tile.

##### Distant Lands

Any power dealing with tiles that are adjacent, neighboring, or a number of tiles away works only for tiles in the same land. If a land's battle tile becomes controlled, follow these steps instead of the standard rules:

- Remove any objective tokens from the battle tile.
- Remove both commanders. Do not move any units to another tile.
- Remove the battle tile's flank token. Next time that flank's event card is revealed, remove that card and reveal another event card.

##### Initial Quests

At the start of the scenario, these quests are available:

- Bransad Wheathair's land is being attacked by a monster. Aid him by defeating the Death Dragon vile champion on the Coast adventure tile.
- Ghaldric Longfang needs supplies. Aid him by bringing a piece of equipment to the Ancient Monolith adventure tile.

##### Additional Quests

A hero on a battle tile with an objective token may examine the objective to speak with that land's ruler and start a new quest detailed in the listed scenario entry.

#### Command Tent Choice

Darrett offers you aid. Choose 1 option:

**Harry a Commander.** Raiders harry the enemy. Remove an Alliance unit from 1 flank to Shake the Dragon Army commander on that flank.

**Tell Us about Ghaldric.** Darrett tells you more about the leader Ghaldric Longfang. Read scenario entry 82.

#### Dragon Army Control

After the Dragon Army takes control of a battle tile, follow these instructions:

**First Tile.** Read scenario entry 101.

**Second Tile.** Read scenario entry 73. The scenario immediately ends in a loss.

#### Additional Endings

This scenario has the following special ending condition:

- The Command Tent or Command Post tile has 5 objective tokens on it.

#### Aftermath

Determine the scenario's outcome:

**Win.** The heroes gained the support of 5 leaders.

**Hold.** The heroes gained the support of 4 leaders.

**Loss.** The heroes gained the support of 3 or fewer leaders, or another loss condition occurred.

##### Win

*(red)* *Darrett approaches and says, "I don't know how you kept your heads, but we're still here because you did. Thank you."*

Take the Heralds of the West reputation card and an available medal.

##### Hold

*(red)* *Darrett approaches and says, "The alliances you formed will make all the difference in the battles to come."*

Take the Heralds of the West reputation card.

##### Loss

*(red)* *Darrett approaches and says, "It was a long shot. You did the best you could."*

At the beginning of the next scenario, the heroes will get extra equipment and tactic tokens (the loss bonus).
