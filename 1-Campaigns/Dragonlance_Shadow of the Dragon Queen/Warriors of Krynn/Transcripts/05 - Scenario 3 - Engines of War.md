### RUNNING THIS SCENARIO

#### Starting the Scenario

After setting up this scenario, read the following text aloud:

*(red)* *"The upcoming battle was meant to be an ambush," Marshal Vendri says amid the shadows of the command tent. "Unfortunately, the Dragon Army managed to swing around and reinforce its lines. Still, our intelligence was good, and we've caught its war engines out in the open. Now's our best chance to destroy them."*

*She goes on to explain that larger war engines are deployed behind the enemy lines, with smaller engines among the Dragon Army troops. While Raven Uth Vogler, Vendri, and commanders Graystone and Hana Shad[...] conduct the attack on the Dragon Army, the marshal wants you to focus on destroying the war engines and capturing their crews.*

*With her orders given, Vendri dismisses you. Time is of the essence.*

#### Scenario Rules

This scenario has the following special rules:

**Minor War Engines.** Objective tokens represent minor war engines. Minor war engines are activated by scenario entries rather than by vile champions. When a hero examines an objective, remove the objective token.

**War Engine Crew.** Defeated vile champions are placed face up by the Command Tent or Command Post tile. They do not return to play if a card or scenario entry calls for more vile champions.

#### Command Tent Choice

Before you leave, Vendri offers aid for your mission. Choose 1 option, which occurs immediately:

**Build Barricades.** Vendri has a support crew build barricades to help in the early battle. Add 1 barricade each to any 4 Alliance units.

**Build a Watchtower.** Vendri has a crew build a watchtower, which makes it easier to move between flanks. Flip the Woods adventure tile to show the Watchtower.

After the Dragon Army takes control of a battle tile, follow these instructions:

**First Tile.** Read scenario entry 66.

#### Additional Endings

This scenario has the following special ending condition:

- No vile champions are in play.

#### Aftermath

*(red)* *The acrid smell of the war engines lingers long after the battle has ended. Darrett approaches, his face streaked with ash.*

Determine the scenario's outcome:

**Win.** 12 or more vile champions were defeated.

**Hold.** 9 to 11 vile champions were defeated.

**Loss.** Fewer than 9 vile champions were defeated, or another loss condition occurred.

#### Sidebar: Gnomish War Machines

*(Black box.)* Tinker gnomes are renowned for their ingenious mechanical inventions and explosive failures. The Dragon Army has pressed several of these inventors into service creating war machines for their forces. These overwrought instruments of destruction are effective against the enemy, but tend to backfire dramatically.

##### Win

*(red)* *Darrett says, "You made it look like you've done this before. Even if the Dragon Army manages to salvage some of those war engines, the loss of so many trained crews will be a significant blow."*

Take the Saboteurs reputation card and an available medal card.

##### Hold

*(red)* *Darrett says, "The Dragon Army managed to retreat with a number of engine crews intact, but this battle cost them dearly."*

Take the Saboteurs reputation card.

##### Loss

*(red)* *Darrett says, "The Dragon Army was able to escape with far too many engine crews intact. Next time will be different. I swear it."*

At the beginning of the next scenario, the heroes will get extra equipment and tactic tokens (the loss bonus).
