# Siege Engines

> [!info] Source: **2024 DMG** ("Siege Equipment") and **Module** (appendix A, the boilerdrak). Who has what, and Tatina's builds, are **Homebrew**.

**How to run them:**
- **A single engine** the heroes can reach, fire, crew or wreck: use its stat block as written. Loading takes a Utilize action, aiming takes another, and then a crew member fires. With a crew of three it fires every round, and with one creature every third turn.
- **A battery firing from far off** is a Trekiros **Artillery** battalion ("Siege Crew", "War Engine" in [[Skirmish Battles#Our Roster]]). Never use both for the same engines.
- **In a skirmish,** an engine firing at a battalion or a commander rolls as normal. A battalion attacking an engine deals its usual 25 damage.
- **Use them sparingly:** where the story already has one, or where one gives the fight an objective.
