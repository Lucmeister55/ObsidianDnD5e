# Harvesting and Crafting

> [!companion] Source: **Monster Loot: Dragonlance** by Anne Gregersen ([[1315435-Monster_Loot_-_Dragonlance.pdf]], DMs Guild), with **Homebrew** campaign hooks.

**The point:** monsters leave something behind worth taking, and someone in the party knows how to take it. It's most interesting for [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn)|Thorn]] (a hunter who wastes nothing) and [[Milo Geartickle|Milo]] (a tinkerer who can't leave a strange part alone), and it gives the Wastes a second source of food. Every encounter in the chapter notes lists what it yields.

## Harvesting

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** (Monster Loot, "How to Use This Book").

- **Check.** Survival or Nature for beasts, dragons, giants, monstrosities and plants. Religion for undead, fiends and celestials. Arcana for aberrations, constructs, elementals, fey and oozes.
- **DC** = 10 + the creature's CR (minimum 10, maximum 30). Success: everything listed. Failure: half. Fail by 5 or more: a mishap (a punctured acid gland, a rockfall), and it's all lost. Another character can help, for advantage.
- **Time:** Tiny ½ hour or less, Small ½ hour, Medium 1 hour, Large 2 hours, Huge 4 hours, Gargantuan 8+ hours. Interrupted means partial loot.
- **Tools.** Hide and organs need **harvester's tools** (30 gp: bonesaw, thin knife, whetstone, scraper, mincing blade). Liquids and dust need **vials** (4 ounces each).
- **People.** Humanoids give up only what they carry: weapons, armour (often **broken**: repair for half the item's price) and pocket items. Nobody butchers people.
- **Freshness.** Fresh kills only. Meat goes bad in 1–2 days unless cooked or cured.

## Using What You Harvest

- **Consumables** (glands, essences, flesh) must be treated first: **1 hour** with alchemist's supplies (potions), cook's utensils (food) or brewer's supplies (drinks), DC 5 + CR. Eaten raw, they do nothing, and the eater makes a DC 15 Constitution save or spends a turn retching.
- **Spell components** (arcane dust, wight hearts, flameskull eyes) are declared before the roll and used up.
- **Ammunition tips** (teeth, spikes): anyone proficient with the weapon can fit one in **10 minutes**. Each works once.
- **Gear** (hides, claws, fangs, wings) has to go to an **artisan** who can craft magic items. The listed cost covers materials and the artisan's time, and the time is how long they take. Crafted gear counts as magical.

## In This Campaign

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.

- **Rations count as Supply** ([[4. Duty - Survival]]). One ration is 1 Supply. A dragonnel is 2d6 Supply. A purple worm feeds an army.
- **The draconian problem.** Draconians turn to stone, acid or fire when they die, so there's nothing to harvest from their bodies, only the gear they carried. A draconian knocked out with a club ([[Weapon Triangle#Where Each Weapon Shines]]) can be questioned instead.
- **Who crafts:**
  - **In Kalaman:** the **Hammerstrike Forges** (Kadmos for armour, Tiria for weapons). A crafting job runs during downtime, while the party does other things. Guilds at Trusted: 10% off.
  - **Tatina Rookledust** for anything mechanical, and anything from an Istarian drone.
  - **In the Wastes:** **Grasha Migan** at Bluemaw Cave and **End Pike** at Heart's Hollow. Neither has the gear for months-long jobs, but either can turn a hide into armour or a claw into a dagger.
- **Thorn** harvests with advantage, and he always buries what's left (his rite). He's the obvious one to make **ammunition**: anhkolox spikes and dragon teeth make arrows his bow will remember.
- **Milo** gets the strange parts. An **Istarian drone eye** is the best thing in this list for them: crafted into an *eye of enhanced seeing* (30 ft blindsight), or, homebrew, fitted into their own drone as a free upgrade.
- **Wurm** wants nothing from a dragon. Let him say so the first time someone cuts one open.

## Things to Watch

- Does anyone actually stop to harvest, or does it slow the game down? If it drags, make it a single roll after the fight, with the time spent noted.
- Is the Wastes' food supply now too easy? If hunting predators solves Supply, halve the rations.
- Do the expensive items (60-day armour) feel like rewards, or just a list? Keep them for after the siege.
