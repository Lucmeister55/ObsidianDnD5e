# Bloodied and Bruised

> [!companion] Source: **Bloodied & Bruised: Dragonlance** by Anne Gregersen ([[1315435-Bloodied__Bruised_-_Dragonlance_v1.pdf]], DMs Guild). Each chapter encounter that uses it links here.

The back half of a fight should feel worse than the front. Monsters change when they're **Bloodied**: at or below **half their hit point maximum**. The book's HP numbers are for the 2014 stat blocks, so use half of whatever block you run.

## Rules

- **Tell the players** when a creature is Bloodied. It's how they know to press or to run. Enemies can see when a PC is Bloodied too, and go for them.
- **"While Bloodied"** lasts as long as it stays at half HP or below. **"When first Bloodied"** triggers once per encounter, even if it's healed and Bloodied again.
- A trigger that needs a **reaction** fails if the reaction is already spent, and an incapacitated creature can't use one.
- **Death Throes:** a final effect when it dies. Skip it if the creature is disintegrated, dies from a fall, or is killed outright.
- **10 HP or less:** no bloodied features. Goblins, bandits and guards just die.

**The common ones:**
- **Cornered:** when first Bloodied, one melee attack as a reaction. Most soldiers, beasts and dragons have it.
- **Recharge:** when first Bloodied, a breath weapon or signature action recharges, and legendary creatures regain all their legendary actions.
- **Made It Angry:** advantage on saves against charmed, frightened, prone and stunned.
- **Worn Out:** speed -10 feet.

## Things to Watch

- Two triggers in one round (Cornered plus a recharged breath) can drop a PC from full. Telegraph it the first time: "it rears back, bleeding, and you see the light build in its throat."
- Being of Death makes death dragons lethal for downed PCs. Get them up or out of the 60 feet fast, and offer a [[Last Stands|Last Stand]] first.
- In a big fight, use it for the boss and one or two elites only. Twelve Cornered reactions in a round is bookkeeping, not drama.
