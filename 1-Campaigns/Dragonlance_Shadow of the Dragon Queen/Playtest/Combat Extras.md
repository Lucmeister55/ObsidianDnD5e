# Combat Extras

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. The weapon triangle builds on the 2024 Weapon Mastery rules. Combos are the round-by-round version of the one-turn combos in [[Session 1.5]].

Four small rules: [[#Weapon Triangle]], [[#Combo Attacks]], [[#Slopes and High Ground]], and [[#Scrolls and Quick Items]].

## Weapon Triangle

A reason to carry more than one weapon. The 2024 rules make swapping cheap, and variant encumbrance makes carrying many expensive.

### The Rule

**Swords beat axes, axes beat spears, spears beat swords.** When your weapon's family beats the family of the target's weapon, you have **+2 to attack rolls** against it. The same applies to the enemy against you.

| Family | Weapons | Beats | Because |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blades** | dagger, shortsword, scimitar, rapier, sickle, longsword, greatsword | **Hafted** | Quick enough to strike inside a heavy swing. |
| **Hafted** | club, greatclub, handaxe, battleaxe, greataxe, light hammer, warhammer, maul, mace, morningstar, flail, war pick | **Polearms** | Smashes past the point and breaks the shaft. |
| **Polearms** | spear, javelin (melee), quarterstaff, trident, glaive, halberd, lance, pike | **Blades** | Reach keeps the swordsman out. |

- Wielding two families: use the one you attacked with last.
- Outside the triangle: natural weapons, unarmed strikes, ranged weapons, spells and whips.
- Heroes only. Battalions in [[Skirmish Battles]] don't use it.

### The Enemy

The Dragon Army is mostly blades, so polearms have the edge.

| Enemy | Weapon | Beat it with |
|---|---|---|
| Baaz, kapak, sivak | shortsword, dagger, serrated sword (blades) | **Polearms** (and see Death Throes) |
| Bozak | trident (polearm) | **Blades** |
| Dragon Army soldier | longsword (blade) | **Polearms** |
| Dragon Army officer | vicious lance (polearm) | **Blades** |
| Hobgoblin, goblin | longsword, scimitar (blades) | **Polearms** |
| Kalaman soldiers and knights (allies) | longsword, greatsword (blades) | They beat ogres' clubs and lose to spears. |

**Draconian Death Throes.** A **blade or polearm** that kills a baaz is stuck in the stone until someone uses an action for a DC 13 Strength check. Hafted and ranged weapons never stick. Against baaz, that's a choice between the +2 and a weapon you might lose. Kapak acid and bozak blasts punish killing up close, so reach and ranged weapons pay off.

### Underwater and Mounted

> [!info] Source: **2024 PHB** ("Underwater Combat", "Mounted Combat"), as written.

- **Underwater:** without a swim speed, melee weapon attacks have **disadvantage** unless the weapon deals Piercing damage. Ranged weapon attacks have disadvantage, and miss beyond normal range. Everything underwater resists fire.
- **Mounted:** the PHB's mounting and control rules. Nothing extra.
- **Taking prisoners:** any melee attack that drops a creature to 0 can knock it out instead of killing it (PHB). Knocked-out draconians don't trigger Death Throes, and the Military pays a bounty for live ones.

### The Peace-Tie

**Inside Kalaman's walls, in castles, temples and noble houses, and anywhere the Military is Disliked or worse,** martial weapons are peace-tied. Drawing one takes an action and draws the guards. Simple weapons that are also tools (club, quarterstaff, sickle, dagger, light hammer, handaxe, sling) never are. When the sivaks strike in the castle at night, the one with the club is armed.

## Combo Attacks

Two or more characters spend their turns on one move that's better than the sum of its parts. It's the most improvised rule here: say yes to anything that makes sense.

**The rule.** On your turn, pitch a combo to allies within 30 feet who can see you and still have their reaction. Each ally spends their **reaction**, you spend your **action**, and the DM picks the effect:

| Size | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Small** | Sensible | Advantage, +1d6 damage, or the target can't take reactions |
| **Big** | Clever, uses the terrain, or three or more people | Small, plus +2d8 damage or a condition (prone, pushed 15 feet, grappled, disarmed) |
| **Wild** | Once a session at most | Big, plus a scene effect: the bridge collapses, the dragonnel throws its rider, a [[Skirmish Battles|battalion]] breaks and falls back |

- **A stretch:** whoever does the hard part rolls (DC 12–16). On a failure it still works as Small.
- **One combo per character per round.** If anyone can't do their part, nothing happens and nothing is spent.
- **Examples:** Launch (boost an ally into a leaping strike), Hammer and Anvil (shove the target, then strike it), Covering Fire (shoot so an ally can close in). Write the players' best ones down.
- **NPCs** join if it fits them: Cudgel holds a line, Darrett follows Edric's lead, and anything with Tatina is Big, then a d6 (on a 1, it goes the wrong way).

## Slopes and High Ground

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.

A **slope** is ground the scene would call a hill (about 10 feet of rise over 30 feet). Say "that's a slope, up is that way", and mark it on the map.

| Situation | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Downhill** | Movement costs half, so you cover **double** the distance. |
| **Uphill** | Movement costs double, so you cover **half** the distance. |
| **Downhill charge** | After **20 feet downhill in a straight line** toward the target, your **first melee weapon hit** deals **one extra weapon die**. |
| **High ground, ranged** | **+2 to ranged weapon attacks** from at least **10 feet higher**, against a target you can see. |

- Works for enemies too, so the first question on a map with a hill is who holds the top.
- **Difficult terrain** and uphill don't stack. Use whichever costs more.
- **Battalions** use [[Skirmish Battles#The Battlefield]] instead (Archers gain range from height).
- **Spells** are unchanged.

**Where:** [[1.04 The Battle of High Hill]] (villagers start on the hill), [[2.01 Back in Vogler#Encounter: Soldiers on the Cliffs]], and any map with a hill.

## Scrolls and Quick Items

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, in the style of *Baldur's Gate 3*.

### Scrolls for Everyone

**Anyone who can read a spell scroll can cast it**, at the spell's normal casting time, and the scroll is used up.
- Use the scroll's DC and attack bonus (below), or the caster's own if better.
- No Arcana check, no failure roll. Concentration still applies, at the scroll's level.

| Spell level | Save DC | Spell attack |
|---|---|---|
| Cantrip to 2nd | 13 | +5 |
| 3rd to 4th | 15 | +7 |
| 5th to 6th | 17 | +9 |
| 7th to 8th | 18 | +10 |
| 9th | 19 | +11 |

Scrolls are rare and single-use, so a fighter can throw an emergency fireball without becoming a caster.

### Quick Items

A **bonus action**, one per turn:
- Drinking a potion, or giving one to an adjacent creature.
- Applying an oil, coating or antitoxin ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]).
- Lighting a torch or lantern, or using a smoke pellet or similar.

Throwing a flask, stabilising and tending wounds are still actions.

## Things to Watch

- **Scrolls:** a gift, or a free fireball? If the party hoards them, give a fight they can't win without one. If a fighter plans around them, make them rarer.
- **Quick items:** if bonus-action potions make fights too easy, keep giving one to someone else as an action.
- **Weapon Triangle:**
  - Do fighters carry and switch a second weapon?
  - Is +2 noticeable but not mandatory? If nobody notices, make it advantage on the first attack each turn. If it feels mandatory, make it +1.
  - Mark each enemy's family on the initiative tracker ("Baaz [B]").
  - Unarmed strikes sit outside on purpose, so monks and rogues have reliability.
- **Combos:** if they don't happen without reminders, put the examples on a card in the middle of the table. If they take more than a minute to agree, call it Small and move on.
- **Slopes:** do players use a hill on purpose? If not, point at it in round one. If the party refuses to fight uphill, make the charge die need a DC 10 Athletics check, or a flat +1d6.
