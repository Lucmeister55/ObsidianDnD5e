# Combat Extras

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. The weapon triangle builds on the 2024 Weapon Mastery rules. Combos are the round-by-round version of the one-turn combos in [[Session 1.5]].

Four small rules: [[#Weapon Triangle]], [[#Combo Attacks]], [[#Slopes and High Ground]], and [[#Scrolls and Quick Items]].

## Weapon Triangle

A reason to carry more than one weapon. The 2024 rules make swapping cheap, and variant encumbrance makes carrying many expensive.

### The Rule

**Swords beat axes, axes beat spears, spears beat swords.** When your weapon's family beats the family of the target's weapon, you have **+2 to attack rolls** against it. The same applies to the enemy against you.

| Family | Weapons | Beats | Because |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blades** | dagger, shortsword, scimitar, rapier, sickle, longsword, greatsword | **Hafted** | Quick enough to strike inside a heavy swing. |
| **Hafted** | club, greatclub, handaxe, battleaxe, greataxe, light hammer, warhammer, maul, mace, morningstar, flail, war pick | **Polearms** | Smashes past the point and breaks the shaft. |
| **Polearms** | spear, javelin (melee), quarterstaff, trident, glaive, halberd, lance, pike | **Blades** | Reach keeps the swordsman out. |

- Wielding two families: use the one you attacked with last.
- Outside the triangle: natural weapons, unarmed strikes, ranged weapons, spells and whips.
- Heroes only. [[Skirmish Battles]] units use troop types.

### The Enemy

The Dragon Army is mostly blades, so polearms have the edge.

| Enemy | Weapon | Beat it with |
|---|---|---|
| Baaz, kapak, sivak | shortsword, dagger, serrated sword (blades) | **Polearms** (and see Death Throes) |
| Bozak | trident (polearm) | **Blades** |
| Dragon Army soldier | longsword (blade) | **Polearms** |
| Dragon Army officer | vicious lance (polearm) | **Blades** |
| Hobgoblin, goblin | longsword, scimitar (blades) | **Polearms** |
| Kalaman soldiers and knights (allies) | longsword, greatsword (blades) | They beat ogres' clubs and lose to spears. |

**Draconian Death Throes.** A **blade or polearm** that kills a baaz is stuck in the stone until someone uses an action for a DC 13 Strength check. Hafted and ranged weapons never stick. Against baaz, that's a choice between the +2 and a weapon you might lose. Kapak acid and bozak blasts punish killing up close, so reach and ranged weapons pay off.

### Where Each Weapon Shines

The scene decides, so there's nothing to track. Name the situation when the party enters it ("You're chest-deep in the Wash: trident rules").

| Situation | Shines | Effect | Suffers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Underwater** (the Wash, the Blue Phoenix Shrine, a sunk hold) | Trident, spear, javelin, dagger, crossbow | No penalty. A trident hit can **pull the target 10 feet** (Strength save against the damage). | Other weapons have **disadvantage**. Bludgeoning deals half. |
| **Tight spaces** (5-foot tunnels, crypts, holds, the citadel's foundations) | Dagger, shortsword, club, handaxe, light hammer, sickle | **+2 to attack rolls.** | Two-handed and reach weapons have **disadvantage**. |
| **On the walls** (ladders, ramparts, cliff edges) | Glaive, halberd, pike, spear, quarterstaff | A hit can **shove the target off** (Strength save against the damage, minimum DC 10). | |
| **Against fliers** (dragonnels, gliding kapaks, death dragons) | Bows, crossbows, javelin, pike, lance | Bows and crossbows deal **+1d6** to a flying or gliding target. Javelin, pike or lance: on a hit, **DC 13 Strength save** or drop 10 feet, or be **unseated**. | |
| **Mounted** (Edric's mare, borrowed dragonnels) | Lance, spear, longsword, flail | **+1d6 damage** if the mount moved 20 feet this turn. | Two-handed weapons other than the lance have **disadvantage**. |
| **Bone and stone** (skeletons, death dragons, Soth's knights, baaz statues) | Club, greatclub, mace, maul, warhammer, morningstar | **+1d4 bludgeoning.** | Piercing deals half. |
| **Taking prisoners** | Club, quarterstaff, unarmed strike | Dropped to 0, a creature is **knocked out, never killed**. Knocked-out draconians don't trigger Death Throes, and the Military pays a bounty for live ones. | |
| **Breaking things** (doors, barricades, ballistae, drones, golems) | Greatclub, maul, war pick, greataxe | **Double damage** to objects and constructs. | Blades deal half to objects. |
| **Bad footing** (ice, Wash flats, floating rocks, a storm-tossed deck) | Quarterstaff, spear | **Advantage** against falling prone or losing your footing. | |
| **Boarding and rigging** | Scimitar, handaxe, dagger, sickle | **Swing on a rope** as part of your move (30 feet, no check). Handaxes and sickles cut lines and grapples as a bonus action. | Reach weapons have disadvantage in the rigging. |
| **In the city** (see the Peace-Tie) | Club, quarterstaff, sickle, dagger, light hammer, handaxe, sling | **Never peace-tied.** | Martial weapons take an action to free. |
| **In the Wastes** | Sickle, handaxe | **+2 to foraging checks**. A handaxe makes shelter without a check. | |

#### The Peace-Tie

**Inside Kalaman's walls, in castles, temples and noble houses, and anywhere the Military is Wary or worse,** martial weapons are peace-tied. Drawing one takes an action and draws the guards. Simple weapons that are also tools (club, quarterstaff, sickle, dagger, light hammer, handaxe, sling) never are. When the sivaks strike in the castle at night, the one with the club is armed.

### Optional Layer: Armour

*Only once the triangle feels natural.*

| Damage type | Against heavy armour | Against light or no armour |
|---|---|---|
| **Bludgeoning** | +2 damage | normal |
| **Piercing** | normal | normal |
| **Slashing** | -2 damage (minimum 1) | +2 damage |

Heavy armour is plate, splint, chain mail or ring mail (Dragon Army and Kalaman soldiers, officers, knights). Draconian scales count as light.

## Combo Attacks

Two or more characters spend their actions on one move that's better than the sum of its parts.

### The Rule

On your turn, pitch a combo to one or more allies within 30 feet who can see you and still have their reactions. Say what each of you does. If it makes sense:
1. Each ally spends their **reaction** on their part.
2. You take your **action** for yours.
3. The DM picks the **effect** from the scale below.

#### The Effect

| Size | When | Pick one or two |
|---|---|---|
| **Small** | Sensible, and it works. | Advantage on the attack · +1d6 damage · the target can't take reactions · move 10 feet free |
| **Big** | Clever, uses the terrain or both characters' strengths, or makes the table cheer. | Small, plus one of: +2d8 damage · prone, pushed 15 feet, grappled or disarmed · ignores cover and shields · an ally gets a free attack |
| **Wild** | Once a session at most. | Big, plus a scene effect: the bridge collapses under the ogre, the dragonnel throws its rider, the enemy line breaks (a Morale check in [[Skirmish Battles]]). |

- **A stretch:** one check from whoever does the hard part (DC 12–16). On a failure, it still works as **Small**.
- **Three or more people:** at least **Big**. The whole party is a candidate for **Wild**.
- **One combo per character per round**, starting or joining.
- The ally has no reaction until their next turn (no opportunity attacks or reaction spells).
- If anyone can't do their part (grappled, incapacitated, out of reach), nothing happens and nothing is spent.

### Examples

Show these to the players. Each is roughly **Big**. The list is inspiration, not a menu.

| Combo | The ally's part (reaction) | Your part (action) | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Launch** | Boost you up (Medium or larger, within 5 feet). | Leap up to 20 feet as part of the action, then make one melee attack. | **Advantage**, ignores half cover and shields, **+1d8** damage. |
| **Hammer and Anvil** | Shove the target 5 feet toward you or knock it prone (Athletics against its Athletics or Acrobatics). | Attack the target. | If the shove worked, your first attack this turn **crits on 19–20**. |
| **Pin and Strike** | Grapple the target, or attack to apply Topple, Push or Slow. | Attack the same target. | If it worked, the target **can't take reactions** until its next turn, and your attack deals **+1d6**. |
| **Spell-Strike** | Cast a damaging cantrip onto your weapon. | Make a weapon attack. | On a hit, add the cantrip's damage. Any rebound hits both of you (Davos's wild magic loves this). |
| **Covering Fire** | One ranged attack at the target. | Move up to your speed without provoking from that target, then attack, Dash, Disengage or Help. | If the ranged attack hit, the target has **disadvantage** on its next attack. |
| **Distraction** | Charisma (Performance or Deception) against the target's passive Insight. | Attack the target. | If it worked, your attack has advantage, and the target can't target the distracter until its next turn. |
| **Shield Wall** | Raise a shield beside you (holding one, within 5 feet). | Dodge, Ready, or attack. | Until your next turn, both of you have **+2 AC** and three-quarters cover against ranged attacks from the front. |

Add the players' good inventions to this table, with the effect you gave them.

### NPC Partners

An allied NPC on the battlefield can join a combo if it fits them.
- **Cudgel** holds a door, a line or an ogre while someone else hits.
- **Darrett** takes any knightly manoeuvre Edric suggests. If Wurm taught him to fight, they have their own.
- **Tatina:** anything with her is automatically **Big**, then roll a d6. On a 1, it goes off in the wrong direction.
- **Clystran:** dragonnels, ropes and climbing.
- **Allied units** in a [[Skirmish Battles|skirmish]]: the unit shoves, volleys or charges, and the PC's attack gets the effect.

## Slopes and High Ground

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.

A **slope** is ground the scene would call a hill (about 10 feet of rise over 30 feet). Say "that's a slope, up is that way", and mark it on the map.

| Situation | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Downhill** | Movement costs half, so you cover **double** the distance. |
| **Uphill** | Movement costs double, so you cover **half** the distance. |
| **Downhill charge** | After **20 feet downhill in a straight line** toward the target, your **first melee weapon hit** deals **one extra weapon die**. |
| **High ground, ranged** | **+2 to ranged weapon attacks** from at least **10 feet higher**, against a target you can see. |

- Works for enemies too, so the first question on a map with a hill is who holds the top.
- **Mounted:** the charge die and the Mounted +1d6 don't stack. Take the bigger.
- **Difficult terrain** and uphill don't stack. Use whichever costs more.
- **Units** charge downhill for double damage and take half while holding high ground ([[Skirmish Battles#Slopes and High Ground]]).
- **Spells** are unchanged.

**Where:** [[1.04 The Battle of High Hill]] (villagers start on the hill), [[2.01 Back in Vogler#Encounter: Soldiers on the Cliffs]], and any map with a hill.

## Scrolls and Quick Items

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, in the style of *Baldur's Gate 3*.

### Scrolls for Everyone

**Anyone who can read a spell scroll can cast it**, at the spell's normal casting time, and the scroll is used up.
- Use the scroll's DC and attack bonus (below), or the caster's own if better.
- No Arcana check, no failure roll. Concentration still applies, at the scroll's level.

| Spell level | Save DC | Spell attack |
|---|---|---|
| Cantrip to 2nd | 13 | +5 |
| 3rd to 4th | 15 | +7 |
| 5th to 6th | 17 | +9 |
| 7th to 8th | 18 | +10 |
| 9th | 19 | +11 |

Scrolls are rare and single-use, so a fighter can throw an emergency fireball without becoming a caster.

### Quick Items

A **bonus action**, one per turn:
- Drinking a potion, or giving one to an adjacent creature.
- Applying an oil, coating or antitoxin ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]).
- Lighting a torch or lantern, or using a smoke pellet or similar.

Throwing a flask, stabilising and tending wounds are still actions.

## Things to Watch

- **Scrolls:** a gift, or a free fireball? If the party hoards them, give a fight they can't win without one. If a fighter plans around them, make them rarer.
- **Quick items:** if bonus-action potions make fights too easy, keep giving one to someone else as an action.
- **Weapon Triangle:**
  - Do fighters carry and switch a second weapon?
  - Is +2 noticeable but not mandatory? If nobody notices, make it advantage on the first attack each turn. If it feels mandatory, make it +1.
  - Mark each enemy's family on the initiative tracker ("Baaz [B]").
  - Do players pack for the situation ("tunnels, bring the short stuff")? If not, foreshadow the next location's situation a session early.
  - Unarmed strikes sit outside on purpose, so monks and rogues have reliability.
- **Combos:**
  - Do they happen without reminders? If not, put the examples on a card in the middle of the table.
  - If everyone always combos, the ally also loses 10 feet of movement next turn.
  - Spell-Strike and Launch are the strongest. Watch them first.
  - Each should take under a minute to agree. Otherwise, call it Small and move on.
- **Slopes:** do players use a hill on purpose? If not, point at it in round one. If the party refuses to fight uphill, make the charge die need a DC 10 Athletics check, or a flat +1d6.
