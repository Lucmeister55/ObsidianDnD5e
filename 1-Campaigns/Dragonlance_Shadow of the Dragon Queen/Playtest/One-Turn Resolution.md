# One-Turn Resolution

*Source: **Homebrew**. This is the DM-side procedure for the "one-turn" described to the players in [[Session 1.5]].*

**The point:** skip padding fights without skipping the fun. When the party is clearly going to win, everyone gets one cinematic turn, and the description decides the cost.

## When to Call It

Call a one-turn when **all** of these are true:
- The party will obviously win: the enemies are outclassed, outnumbered or already broken.
- Nobody important is in the fight, on either side.
- Playing it out would take more than one round of real time and teach nobody anything.

Say it out loud: "This is a one-turn." The players then know the fight is won, and the question is only how cleanly.

## Procedure

1. **Set the scene** in two or three lines: who's where, and what's dangerous (the archer on the roof, the ogre's reach, the cliff edge).
2. **Go round the table once,** in any order. Each player describes their one turn. Combos ([[Combo Attacks]]) are encouraged, and players can build on each other's descriptions.
3. **Rate each description** (quietly, or out loud if the table likes it):

| Rating | What it looks like | Cost to that character |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic** | "I attack the nearest one." | Takes **2 hits** from the enemies (roll their damage twice). |
| **Solid** | Uses the terrain, their abilities or a teammate. | Takes **1 hit**. |
| **Stylish** | Answers a danger in the scene, sets up an ally, or makes the table laugh. | Takes **no damage**, and gets a small bonus: an extra piece of loot, a prisoner, information, or Heroic Inspiration. |

4. **Resources are spent as described.** A spell slot named in the description is spent. A potion thrown is gone.
5. **Narrate the ending** in one paragraph, using everyone's turn in the order it makes the best story.

**Roll only for the implausible.** If an idea is a stretch ("I kick the ogre off the cliff"), call for one check at a fair DC. On a failure, it still works, but they take the Basic cost.

## Things to Watch

- Do the players feel cheated of the fight? If so, call one-turns less often, or only after at least one real round.
- Is "Stylish" too easy? It should take a sentence of thought, not a paragraph.
- Does it speed up sessions meaningfully? Time a few.
