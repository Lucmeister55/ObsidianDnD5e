# Playtest Ideas

*Source: **Homebrew**. These are the experimental systems promised to the players in [[Session 1.5]], fleshed out for the table.*

Everything here is a test. Run each one for two or three sessions, then ask the players the questions in its "Things to Watch" section, and keep, change or cut it.

| Idea | What it's for | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weapon Triangle** | Fighters choosing weapons by the job and the enemy. Fire Emblem style, plus a niche for every weapon. | Not yet tested | [[Weapon Triangle]] |
| **Combo Attacks** | Rewarding teamwork in normal rounds. | Not yet tested | [[Combo Attacks]] |
| **Skirmish Battles** | Small mass combat on the battlemap, with units of soldiers. | Not yet tested | [[Skirmish Battles]] |
| **One-Turn Resolution** | Skipping padding fights cinematically. | Not yet tested | [[One-Turn Resolution]] |
| **Last Stands** | Blaze-of-glory deaths. | Not yet tested | [[Last Stands]] |
| **Variant encumbrance** | Making travel and supplies a real choice. | Running all campaign | [[4. Duty - Survival#Encumbrance]] |
| **Faction Standing** | Social consequences with minimal tracking. | Starts in Vogler | [[Faction Rules]] |
| **Warriors of Krynn** | Big battles on the board game, with custom outcomes. | Scenario 1 in Vogler | [[2. Broken Silence]] |

## Suggested Order

Don't introduce everything at once.
1. **Session 1–2 (Vogler):** encumbrance, the first Faction Standing moves, and One-Turn Resolution in a minor fight.
2. **Battle of Vogler:** *Warriors of Krynn* scenario 1, then a **skirmish** on the docks for the evacuation, to compare the two.
3. **Kalaman, first missions:** the **Weapon Triangle**. The Rookledust goblins and the Blockade draconians are all blades, so a hafted weapon has the edge. Let a player notice.
4. **Mid Storm Clouds:** **Combo Attacks**, once the party knows each other's tricks.
5. **Last Stands** are offered whenever they come up, from day one.
