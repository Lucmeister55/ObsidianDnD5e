# Dwarves

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Dwarves|ch. 1, "Dwarves"]]).

- **Mountain dwarves** live in [[Thorbardin]] and in **Kayolin**, which the New Sea cut off from its allies. They're proud, reserved, and fine engineers.
- **Hill dwarves**, once the **Neidar**, were shut out of Thorbardin after the Cataclysm ([[Thorbardin#The Dwarfgate War|the Dwarfgate War]]). They've lived among surface folk ever since, as traders, townsfolk, or hermits in old ruins. They're common around Kalaman, the Hammerstrike cousins among them. Most would rather not be reminded of their mountain cousins.

## History

> [!info] Source: **Canon** ([Dragonlance Timeline](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Dragonlance_Timeline), [Dwarf](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Dwarf), [Graygem of Gargath](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Graygem_of_Gargath)). Fan wikis, so dates can vary. Who knows what is **Homebrew**.

- **Thorbardin.** Dwarves from Thorin reached the southern Kharolis Mountains and delved the kingdom, finishing by about 2000 PC.
- **Allies of the elves.** They fought beside the elves in the Kinslayer War, and helped raise Pax Tharkas ([[Elves#The Kinslayer War]]).
- **The Graygem.** Gnomes tell a story that the Graygem of Gargath turned greedy gnomes into dwarves. Dwarves vehemently deny this, and insist gnomes and kender are the Graygem's creatures.

## Who Knows What

- **Everyone:** hill dwarves live among us, mountain dwarves don't come out.
- **Any dwarf:** the Dwarfgate War, and which side their family was on. Don't ask lightly.
- **Gnomes and sages:** the Graygem story. Tell it to a dwarf at your own risk.
