# Elves

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Elves|ch. 1, "Elves"]]).

Ansalon's elves keep to themselves, and half-elves are rare.

| People | Where | In short |
|---|---|---|
| **Silvanesti** | [[Silvanesti]] | The first kingdom. Old, strict, hierarchical, and proud of it. |
| **Qualinesti** | [[Qualinesti]] | Left Silvanesti for a freer life. They were friendlier to outsiders, once. |
| **Kagonesti** | Southern [[Ergoth]] | Wood elves who never settled in cities. They live in nomadic tribes. |
| **Dargonesti** | The deep sea | Sea elves, almost never seen. Zol Windswell of Wanderer's Rest is one ([[Kalaman]]). |
| **Dimernesti** | The shallows | Shoal elves, the Dargonesti's cousins. |

**The Kinslayer War**, over two thousand years ago, split the high elves into Silvanesti and Qualinesti. Neither side has tried to make peace since.
