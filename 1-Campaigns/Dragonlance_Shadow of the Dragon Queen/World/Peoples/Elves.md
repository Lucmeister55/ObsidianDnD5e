## The Kinslayer War

> [!info] Source: **Canon** ([The Kinslayer Wars](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/The_Kinslayer_Wars), [Dragonlance Timeline](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Dragonlance_Timeline), [Kith-Kanan](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Kith-Kanan_Silvanos), [Sithas](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Sithas_Silvanos)). Fan wikis, so dates can vary. Who knows what is **Homebrew**.

The war that made the elves two peoples, told in full in the Elven Nations novels (*Firstborn*, *The Kinslayer Wars*, *The Qualinesti*).

- **The twins.** Sithel Silvanos, Speaker of the Stars, had twin sons. **Sithas**, the elder, was raised to rule. **Kith-Kanan** was the warrior.
- **Ergoth moves east.** Humans from the Empire of Ergoth pushed towards elven land, and the two powers came to the edge of war.
- **The Speaker dies (about 2192 PC).** Sithel was killed, and Sithas became Speaker of the Stars. He threw out every peace plan, and the war began.
- **Brothers on one side.** Sithas ruled and raised the armies, and Kith-Kanan led them in the field. The elves fought Ergoth for about fifty years, and the dwarves ended up fighting beside them.
- **Elf against elf.** The western elves, loyal to Kith-Kanan, would not go back to Silvanesti's rigid order. The elves had turned on their own, which is the wound the name remembers.
- **The Swordsheath Scroll.** The war ended about 2140 PC, with combined elven and dwarven forces victorious (some timelines date the Scroll itself to 2073 PC). Sithas granted the western lands to Kith-Kanan and his followers, and Kith-Kanan named them **Qualinesti**. The new land also served as a buffer between Ergoth and [[Thorbardin]].
- **Pax Tharkas.** Elves, dwarves and humans raised the fortress in the pass as a monument to the peace ([[Abanasinia]]).

**Why it still matters.** Silvanesti sees a family that walked out on the true way of the elves. Qualinesti sees an exile that was forced on it. Two thousand years on, neither has tried to mend it, so when Silvanesti's survivors needed shelter they went to the Kagonesti in Southern Ergoth.

## Who Knows What

- **Everyone:** there are two elf kingdoms, and they don't speak.
- **Any high elf:** the whole story, told very differently depending on which side tells it. Say "Kinslayer" to one and watch their face.
- **Scholars and the Great Library's staff:** the Swordsheath Scroll and the alliance with the dwarves.
- **Thorbardin's dwarves and Hylar nobles:** that their ancestors fought beside the elves, and built Pax Tharkas with them.
- **Silvanesti exiles:** that their kingdom has fallen. Almost nobody else in the north knows.
