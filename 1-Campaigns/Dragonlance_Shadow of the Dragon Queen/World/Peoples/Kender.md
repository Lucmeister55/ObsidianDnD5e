# Kender

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Meulara's Oddities|ch. 4, "Meulara's Oddities"]], [[01-war-comes-to-krynn#People of Kalaman|"People of Kalaman"]]), with wider Dragonlance lore.

**What everyone knows.** Small, quick, endlessly curious folk who are never afraid and never still. Things find their way into their pouches. They call it "borrowing", and they mean it. They carry the **hoopak**, a staff that's also a sling, and they have a gift for insults that can drive anyone mad.

**Where.** [[Kendermore]] in [[Goodlund]], **Hylo** in Northern [[Ergoth]], and every road in Ansalon. They're common in Kalaman, where Meulara of Meulara's Oddities knows them all.
