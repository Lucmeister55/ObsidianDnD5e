# Draconians

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[01-war-comes-to-krynn#The Dragon Queen's Return|"The Dragon Queen's Return"]], appendix B), with wider Dragonlance lore.

**What people know.** Almost nothing: scaled, winged soldiers of the Dragon Army that turn to stone, acid or fire when they die. "Draconian" is the word for anything scaled, a dragonborn PC included ([[4.01 The Trial#The Trial|the Trial]]).

**The truth.** The chromatic dragons stole the metallic dragons' eggs, and promised to keep them safe if the metallics stayed out of the war. Takhisis taught her followers to corrupt the eggs into draconians. The metallic dragons don't know yet.

| Kind | Soldiers say | Egg | When it dies |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Baaz** | Brass | Brass | Turns to stone, and can trap the weapon that killed it |
| **Kapak** | Copper | Copper | Dissolves into acid |
| **Bozak** | Bronze | Bronze | Its bones explode |
| **Sivak** | Silver | Silver | Takes on its killer's shape |
| **Aurak** | Gold | Gold | Bursts into green fire |

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew** ([[3.02 Downtime 1#Briefing 1, War Room, After the Council|Briefing 1]]).
> **The codewords.** Baaz, kapak and bozak are the Mages of High Sorcery's code names, sent to Kalaman through Wyhan, and Vendri can't stand them. The mages guessed from the pattern that silver and gold would follow, without saying why. **Sivak** and **aurak** come after the first sightings. Kalaman's soldiers go by colour instead: "brass on the wall", "two coppers in the trees".

**Ties.** A dragonborn PC is Takhisis's first creation, and something else entirely ([[Wurm (DM)]]).

## Who Knows What

- **Everyone:** the Dragon Army has scaled soldiers now.
- **Veterans and Kalaman's soldiers:** that they turn to stone, acid or fire when they die, that you should let go of your sword, and which colour does what.
- **Mages, Vendri's staff and the party:** the code names, and that the mages predicted silver and gold before anyone had seen them.
- **Wyhan and the Order:** that brass, copper and bronze are dragons' metals. They suspect more than they say.
- **Black-robed mages and Dragon Army zealots:** where they come from. Almost nobody else does.
- **Metallic dragons:** only that their eggs were taken. They don't know what's been done with them.
