# Gnomes

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Gnomes|ch. 1, "Gnomes"]]), with wider Dragonlance lore.

- **Tinker gnomes** of [[Mount Nevermind]] are inventors. Each one has a **Life Quest**, a single invention their whole family works towards. Every device does six things, and none of them reliably. Tinker gnomes talk very fast, and a full gnome name takes hours to say.
- **Forest gnomes** are rarer, and live quietly in the woods of Sancrist and Kendermore.

**In Kalaman:** Tatina Rookledust, and later her assistant Than.

## The Graygem

> [!info] Source: **Canon** ([Graygem of Gargath](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Graygem_of_Gargath)). Fan wikis, so dates can vary. Who knows what is **Homebrew**.

Reorx made the **Graygem** and set it in the red moon, Lunitari. Gnomes besieged the tower of Gargath to get it, and when it broke free, its chaos changed those nearby. By the gnomes' telling, the curious became kender and the greedy became dwarves. Accounts conflict, and every people tells it differently.

## Who Knows What

- **Everyone:** gnomes invent things, and the things explode.
- **Tinker gnomes:** the Graygem story, and every detail of their clan's Life Quest, at length.
- **Mages and sages:** that the Graygem may be a real artefact, and not just a story.
