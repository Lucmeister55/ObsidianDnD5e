## Current Date

**13 Brookgreen, 351 AC** (start of [[1.00 An Ordinary Day]])

## Reckoning Years

Solamnics and most of Ansalon count years from the Cataclysm:
- **AC** (After Cataclysm): 351 AC is the campaign's present.
- **PC** (Pre-Cataclysm): years before it, counted backwards. The Cataclysm itself is year 0.

## Months

## The Three Moons

Krynn has three moons, one for each Order of High Sorcery. A wizard's power rises and falls with their moon.

| Moon | Colour | Order | Cycle | Each phase lasts | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Solinari** | Silver-white | White Robes (good) | 36 days | 9 days | Everyone |
| **Lunitari** | Red | Red Robes (neutral) | 28 days | 7 days | Everyone |
| **Nuitari** | Black | Black Robes (evil) | 8 days | 2 days | Only those who serve Nuitari; to everyone else it is a gap in the stars |

Each moon goes through four phases:

1. **Low Sanction** (new moon): the order's magic is at its weakest.
2. **Waxing**
3. **High Sanction** (full moon): the order's magic is at its strongest.
4. **Waning**

Lunitari's cycle matches the 28-day month, so it is full on the same day each month. That makes it the easiest moon for common folk to keep time by.

### Night of the Eye

When all three moons are at High Sanction together, full on the same night, they line up in the sky like a great eye. This happens once every **504 days**. Every wizard on Krynn is at the height of their power, and great workings of magic are attempted (and go wrong) on that night.

- **The last Night of the Eye:** **14 Brookgreen 350 AC**, a year before the campaign. Davos's trial on the Barb ([[Davos Bloodbane]]).
- **The next:** **14 Reapember 351 AC**, the night the Dragon Army means to raise the dead of Onyari ([[4.04 The Deadline#The Deadline]]).
- **After that:** 14 Brookgreen 353 AC.

## Tracking the Moons

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. The cycles are canon (36, 28 and 8 days). The starting points are fixed so that both Nights of the Eye fall on the right dates.

Every moon is **full** on a fixed rhythm, which makes it easy to track from day to day:
- **Lunitari** (red) is full on the **14th of every month**. High Sanction is the 11th to the 17th, waning the 18th to the 24th, Low Sanction from the 25th to the 3rd, and waxing the 4th to the 10th.
- **Solinari** (white) is full every **36 days**. High Sanction is the 9 days around each full moon (4 days either side). Low Sanction is the 9 days around the new moon, 18 days after each full moon.
- **Nuitari** (black) is full every **8 days**, and High Sanction is that day and the next. Only those who serve Nuitari can see it. To everyone else, it's a moving gap in the stars.

**Quick check for any day:** count the day of the year (the Nth day of month M is day (M − 1) × 28 + N). Solinari is full when (day − 22) is divisible by 36. Nuitari is full when (day − 6) is divisible by 8.

### At the Table

- The moons are tracked day by day in this campaign (table above). Wizards time rituals and journeys around them, and they make good omens.
- [[Davos Bloodbane]] answers to all three moons unevenly, and his temperament and control shift with their phases. Note the phases at the start of each session.
- The Lunar Sorcery subclass lets the player pick a phase (Full, New, Crescent) after each long rest. That choice doesn't need to match the actual sky, but it's a nice touch when it does.
