# Icereach

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Icereach|ch. 1, "Icereach"]], [[01-war-comes-to-krynn#Dragon Armies Regroup|"Dragon Armies Regroup"]]), with wider Dragonlance lore.

**What everyone knows.** The frozen far south, around the **Icewall Glacier**. Its humans compete for hunting grounds with the **thanoi**, the walrus-folk.

> [!warning] DM only
> The small **White Dragon Army** has gone south to conquer it, since white dragons fight best on ice. Its highlord, **Feal-Thas**, is a Silvanesti elf and a Black Robe ([[Dragon Armies]]).

## Who Knows What

- **Everyone:** nothing lives in the far south.
- **Sailors and whalers:** the thanoi, and the Ice Folk who hunt beside them.
- **Dragon Army officers:** that the White Army has gone south, and why.
