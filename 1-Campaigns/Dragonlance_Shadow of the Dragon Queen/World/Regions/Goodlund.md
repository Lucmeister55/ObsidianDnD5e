# Goodlund and Balifor

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[01-war-comes-to-krynn#Balifor and Goodlund Absorbed|"Balifor and Goodlund Absorbed"]], [[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Goodlund|ch. 1, "Goodlund"]]).

**What everyone knows.** The **Goodlund Peninsula** is kender country, across the eastern seas on the shore of the [[Blood Sea of Istar|Blood Sea]]. Human tribes wander the **Dairly Plains** and the coasts. Some live in Istarian ruins, poking at magic and machines they only half understand. **Balifor** is the coast and port beside it.

**The news.** Both fell in **349 AC** with hardly a fight, and most people there put up with the occupation. The kender don't: [[Kendermore]] leads a guerrilla war against the occupiers.

> [!warning] DM only
> The Black and White Dragon Armies took it. The Black Army holds it now ([[Dragon Armies]]).
