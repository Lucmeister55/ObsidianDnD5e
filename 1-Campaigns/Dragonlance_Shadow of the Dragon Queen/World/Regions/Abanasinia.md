# Abanasinia

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Abanasinia|ch. 1, "Abanasinia"]]), with wider Dragonlance lore.

**What everyone knows.** A peaceful land of small towns in the far southwest.
- [[Solace]], the village in the trees.
- **Haven**, ruled by the **Seekers**, a power-hungry new religion of vague, fickle gods that condemns magic.
- **The northern plains**, home of the **Plainsfolk** tribes (the Que-Shu among them): warriors, traders, and keepers of stories older than the Cataclysm.
- **Pax Tharkas**, an old fortress in the mountain pass between [[Qualinesti]] and [[Thorbardin]].
- **Xak Tsaroth**, a city that fell in the Cataclysm and still lies in ruins.

> [!warning] DM only
> **Verminaard** has taken most of the Red Dragon Army into Abanasinia, following whispers from the Dragon Queen. It's the other half of the war, and the reason Kansaldi is spearheading the Solamnia attack ([[Dragon Armies]]).
