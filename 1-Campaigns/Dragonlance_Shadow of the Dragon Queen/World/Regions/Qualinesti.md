# Qualinesti

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Qualinesti High Elves|ch. 1, "Qualinesti High Elves"]]), with wider Dragonlance lore.

**What everyone knows.** The western elven kingdom, in the forests of the far southwest. It was founded long ago by elves who left Silvanesti for a less rigid life, and it's ruled by the **Speaker of the Sun** from **Qualinost**. The Qualinesti once traded with humans and with the dwarves of [[Thorbardin]], but they've kept their borders shut since the Cataclysm. See [[Elves]].

> [!warning] DM only
> Verminaard's Red Dragon Army is heading into [[Abanasinia]], next door, and Qualinesti is in its path.
