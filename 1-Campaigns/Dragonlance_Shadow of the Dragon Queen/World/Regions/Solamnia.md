# Solamnia

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Solamnia|ch. 1, "Solamnia"]] and "Knights of Solamnia", [[01-war-comes-to-krynn#Kalaman Region|"Kalaman Region"]]), with wider Dragonlance lore.

**What everyone knows.** Once a mighty empire, still one of the largest and richest nations in Ansalon. Its provinces look after themselves and squabble constantly. Vinas Solamnus founded the Knights of Solamnia here. After the Cataclysm people blamed the knights for Istar, and they are fewer and less loved than they were.

**The land.** The **Plains of Solamnia** are grain country, crossed by the **Vingaard River**, the nation's great trade road down to the sea at [[Kalaman]]. North of the eastern provinces lie the [[Kalaman & Northern Wastes|Northern Wastes]].

**Provinces and cities you might hear of.**
- [[Nightlund]]: Kalaman, Dargaard and the mountains.
- **Hinterlund**, west of Nightlund, with river villages like [[Vogler]].
- **Vingaard**, whose capital is [[Relgoth]].
- [[Palanthas]], the greatest city in the north, on its own bay.
- [[Maelgoth]], the knights' city in the west.

> [!warning] DM only
> The Red and Blue Dragon Armies are gathering in [[Taman Busuk]] to invade ([[Dragon Armies]]). Kalaman is the first target, and the Blue Army is already fighting Maelgoth.

## History

> [!info] Source: **Canon** ([Solamnia](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Solamnia), [Dragonlance Timeline](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Dragonlance_Timeline), [Knights of Solamnia](https://dlsaga.com/knights-of-solamnia/)). Fan wikis, so dates can vary. Who knows what is **Homebrew**.

- **The Rose Rebellion (about 1793 PC).** Solamnia's eastern lands were provinces of the Empire of Ergoth. **Vinas Solamnus**, an Ergothian commander sent to crush their revolt, sided with the rebels and led them against the capital at Daltigoth. The rebels won, and the provinces became the nation of Solamnia.
- **The Knights (1775 PC).** On a Quest of Honour, Vinas had a vision from Paladine and the gods of good. He came back and founded the Knights of Solamnia, and became their first Grand Master.
- **Huma Dragonbane (1018 PC).** The Third Dragon War ended when Huma banished Takhisis and her dragons with the first dragonlance. He died doing it. No knight has ever matched him.
- **Istar's ally.** The Knights marched with Istar for centuries, and the kingpriests used them. That's what people have never forgiven.
- **The Time of Darkness.** After the Cataclysm, the Knights were persecuted across the continent, and even in Solamnia.

## Who Knows What

- **Everyone:** Huma's name, and that the Knights were somehow to blame for the Cataclysm.
- **Knights and squires:** the Oath and the Measure, and how few of them are left.
- **Scholars:** that Solamnia began as a rebellion, led by a traitor to Ergoth.
- **Nobles of the eastern provinces:** how little the Knights in the west can actually do for them.
