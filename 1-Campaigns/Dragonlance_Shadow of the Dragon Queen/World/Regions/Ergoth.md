# Northern and Southern Ergoth

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Northern and Southern Ergoth|ch. 1, "Northern and Southern Ergoth"]] and "Kagonesti Wood Elves").

**What everyone knows.** Two big islands off the west coast, all that's left of the old **Empire of Ergoth**, which the Cataclysm split in two.
- **Northern Ergoth.** Towns that claim to be the empire's heirs, living among its old cities and forts. They share the island peacefully with the kender of **Hylo** and the goblins of the dry south.
- **Southern Ergoth.** Forests where the **Kagonesti** wood elves live in small tribes. There are a few humans on the coast, and ogres and giants inland.

**Nearby.** The island of Sancrist, home of [[Mount Nevermind]].

> [!warning] DM only
> Thousands of Silvanesti refugees have landed in Southern Ergoth. The Kagonesti are helping them, but they won't be swamped by them.
