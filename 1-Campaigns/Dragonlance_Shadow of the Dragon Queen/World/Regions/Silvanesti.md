# Silvanesti

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Silvanesti High Elves|ch. 1, "Silvanesti High Elves"]], [[01-war-comes-to-krynn#Silvanesti Campaign|"Silvanesti Campaign"]]), with wider Dragonlance lore.

**What everyone knows.** The first elven kingdom, deep in the forests of southern Ansalon, and closed to outsiders for thousands of years. It's ruled by the **Speaker of the Stars** from **Silvanost**, around the **Tower of the Stars**. Few humans have ever seen it. See [[Elves]].

> [!warning] DM only
> - **It's gone.** The Red and Blue Armies attacked in late 349 AC. After a year of fighting, Speaker **Lorac Caladon** sent his people over the sea to Southern [[Ergoth]]. Then he tried to save the land with an Orb of Dragonkind, and it turned Silvanesti into a nightmare that's useless to both sides.
> - Almost nobody in Kalaman knows. A Silvanesti elf would.
> - Lorac's daughter is **Alhana Starbreeze**.

**Ties.** A Silvanesti PC's lost home, Zhelsuel's exiles and the Orb shard ([[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]]).

## History

> [!info] Source: **Canon** ([Silvanesti](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Silvanesti), [The Silvanesti Campaign](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/The_Silvanesti_Campaign), [Dragonlance Timeline](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Dragonlance_Timeline)). Fan wikis, so dates can vary. Who knows what is **Homebrew**.

- **Silvanos Goldeneye.** After the First Dragon War, about 3550 PC, the great elven leader Silvanos led his people from their old homeland to this forest and founded the kingdom. Every Speaker of the Stars since has come from his House Royal.
- **A closed, ordered people.** Silvanesti society is stratified, and it keeps its bloodlines pure. It has stayed that way for thousands of years.
- **The Kinslayer War.** The war with Ergoth, and the loss of the western elves ([[Elves#The Kinslayer War]]).
- **The Cataclysm.** The borders closed, and stayed closed.

> [!warning] DM only
> **Lorac's bargain.** In 348 AC Lorac made a truce with Ariakas: Silvanesti would stay out of the war, and his armies would stay out of Silvanesti. Ariakas invaded anyway in 349 AC. So the Speaker who saved his people had first tried to buy peace from the Dragon Queen's champion.

## Who Knows What

- **Everyone:** a hidden elf kingdom far to the south. Few humans have ever seen it.
- **Sailors and merchants on the southern routes:** that elf refugee ships have been seen, and that something is wrong.
- **Silvanesti exiles and Kagonesti:** the land is lost, and that Lorac stayed behind.
- **Dragon Army high command:** what Lorac's Orb did, and that the forest is useless to them. Very few know about the truce.
