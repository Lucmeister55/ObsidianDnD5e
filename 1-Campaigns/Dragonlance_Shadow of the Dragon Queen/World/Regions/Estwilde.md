# Estwilde

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[01-war-comes-to-krynn#Surrounding Regions|"Surrounding Regions"]], [[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Estwilde|ch. 1, "Estwilde"]]).

**What everyone knows.** Hard country east of [[Kalaman]], whose people have been unfriendly to Solamnia since the days of Istar. Its fiercely independent human tribes roam from the Turbidus Ocean to the New Sea. They allow no trespassing, by Solamnics or by the hobgoblin raiders from the east.

**Places.**
- **The steppes of Qlettaar**, south and east of Kalaman, rolling up to the peaks of [[Taman Busuk]].
- **The swamps of Qwalmish**, across the bay from Kalaman in northern Estwilde.

**In Kalaman**, "Estwilder" means raider. Half the bandit weapons on the Steady Beacon's walls came from over the border.

## Who Knows What

- **Everyone in Kalaman:** Estwilders are raiders.
- **Border scouts and caravan masters:** which tribes will trade, and which won't.
- **The tribes themselves:** that hobgoblin bands from the east have been passing through in numbers.
