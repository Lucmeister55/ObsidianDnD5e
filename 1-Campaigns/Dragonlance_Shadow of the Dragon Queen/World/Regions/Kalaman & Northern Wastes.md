## The Northern Wastes

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[06-chapter-5-the-northern-wastes#Learning about the Wastes|ch. 5, "Learning about the Wastes"]]) and **Companion** ([[4-The_Northern_Wastes.pdf]], "Wastes Research").

**Before the Cataclysm.** This was a green part of the Hinterlands, dotted with small communities that worshipped the gods of nature, above all **Habbakuk** and **Sirrion**.

**Now.** A barren land of canyons and sheer plateaus, where grand ruins lie unexplored and few who look for them come back.

**The Wash.** The tides of Krynn's three moons drive the Turbidus Ocean through the canyons. These flash floods can come weeks apart or several times a day, and they bring sea creatures with them.

**What lives here.** Giant desert beasts such as scorpions, spiders, vultures and dragonnels, and things the floods have left behind.

## Who Knows What

- **Everyone (or an hour in Castle Kalaman's library):** after the Cataclysm it became barren and deadly, with flash floods.
- **Readers (DC 12 to 14 History):** grand ruins litter the region, and few explorers survive them.
- **Readers (DC 12 Religion):** the old communities worshipped Habbakuk and Sirrion.
- **Very thorough readers (DC 16):** a tough little community lives west of the Eastern Maze, and two temples were found recently. No research team came back.
- **The most obscure texts (DC 20):** an ancient bronze dragon once protected the region, and some of the ruins may have fallen from the heavens.
- **Heart's Hollow's scavengers:** how to read the Wash, and when to climb.
