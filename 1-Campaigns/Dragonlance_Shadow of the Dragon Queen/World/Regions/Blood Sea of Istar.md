# The Blood Sea of Istar

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[01-war-comes-to-krynn#Before the Cataclysm|"Before the Cataclysm"]] and "The Cataclysm"), with wider Dragonlance lore.

**What everyone knows.** This is where Istar used to be. It grew from a city into an empire, allied with the Knights of Solamnia, and its kingpriests ruled in the name of the gods of good. The last Kingpriest tried to make himself a god. The gods sent Thirteen Warnings, then dropped a mountain of fire on the city. The sea that swallowed the empire is red. Sailors say there's a great whirlpool at its heart, the **Maelstrom**.

**Around it.** The minotaur islands of **Mithas** and **Kothas** lie to the east, and minotaur pirates sail these waters. [[Goodlund]] lies on the western shore.

**In Kalaman** you can still see Istar's work: it built the walls and the beacons.

**Ties.** Ispin claimed minotaur pirates once chased him across the Blood Sea for three days ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge]]). Onyari, the City of Lost Names, was Istarian ([[5.00 Destiny]]).
