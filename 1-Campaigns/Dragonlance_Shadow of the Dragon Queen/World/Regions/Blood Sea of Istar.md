# The Blood Sea of Istar

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[01-war-comes-to-krynn#Before the Cataclysm|"Before the Cataclysm"]] and "The Cataclysm"), with wider Dragonlance lore.

**What everyone knows.** This is where Istar used to be. It grew from a city into an empire, allied with the Knights of Solamnia, and its kingpriests ruled in the name of the gods of good. The last Kingpriest tried to make himself a god. The gods sent Thirteen Warnings, then dropped a mountain of fire on the city. The sea that swallowed the empire is red. Sailors say there's a great whirlpool at its heart, the **Maelstrom**.

**Around it.** The minotaur islands of **Mithas** and **Kothas** lie to the east, and minotaur pirates sail these waters. [[Goodlund]] lies on the western shore.

**In Kalaman** you can still see Istar's work: it built the walls and the beacons.

**Ties.** Ispin claimed minotaur pirates once chased him across the Blood Sea for three days ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge]]). Onyari, the City of Lost Names, was Istarian ([[5.00 Destiny]]).

## History

> [!info] Source: **Canon** ([Dragonlance Timeline](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Dragonlance_Timeline), [Lost Battles](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Battles), [Divine Hammer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divine_Hammer)). Fan wikis, so dates can vary. Who knows what is **Homebrew**.

- **The Proclamation of Manifest Virtue (118 PC).** Kingpriest Vasari II declared what was evil and how it should be punished. From then on, Istar was at war with actions, peoples and thoughts.
- **The Knights of the Divine Hammer.** The Kingpriest's own holy order, who carried that war out.
- **The Lost Battles (19 PC).** Istar, Solamnia and Ergoth moved against the Wizards of High Sorcery. Nobody really won ([[Tower of Wayreth]]).
- **Beldinas.** The last Kingpriest, who tried to become a god.

## Who Knows What

- **Everyone:** Istar was punished, and sailors avoid the middle of the Blood Sea.
- **Minotaur crews and old sailors:** the Maelstrom's currents, and how far in you can go.
- **Priests of the old ways and scholars:** the Proclamation, and how a holy empire turned into what it became.
- **Lohezet's circle and very few others:** that Istar's secrets didn't all sink ([[5.00 Destiny]]).
