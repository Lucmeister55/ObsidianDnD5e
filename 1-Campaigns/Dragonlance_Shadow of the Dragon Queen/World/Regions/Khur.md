# Khur

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[01-war-comes-to-krynn#Khur Joins|"Khur Joins"]], [[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Khur|ch. 1, "Khur"]]).

**What everyone knows.** A harsh, rocky land of nomad tribes, east beyond [[Estwilde]], and a slave market for anyone the raiders take.

**The news.** In 349 AC the chief **Salah-Khan** forced the tribes together and joined the invaders. Many Khur call him a traitor. A stubborn resistance holds out around the trade town of **Khuri-Khan**.

> [!warning] DM only
> Salah-Khan beat the Green Dragon Army's highlord in battle, and Ariakas made him highlord in the man's place ([[Dragon Armies]]).

**Ties.** Khur raiders burned a knight PC's village. The slavers were taking him to Khur when Ispin rode them down ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge]]).
