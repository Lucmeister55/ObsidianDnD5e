# Nordmaar

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[01-war-comes-to-krynn#Nordmaar Falls|"Nordmaar Falls"]], [[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Nordmaar|ch. 1, "Nordmaar"]]).

**What everyone knows.** A land across the sea to the northeast: rugged coastal towns in the north, and nomad tribes in the south. Both are proud, and both have strong old ties with Solamnia.

**The news.** Nordmaar fell in **348 AC**, the first country the "invaders from the east" took. Refugees have come through Kalaman's harbour ever since, but most people in the city still think of it as a faraway war.

> [!warning] DM only
> The Red and Green Dragon Armies took it, and the victory brought many hobgoblin and ogre bands over to the Dragon Queen. The Black Army now holds it ([[Dragon Armies]]).

## Who Knows What

- **Everyone:** there's a war somewhere over the sea.
- **Nordmaar refugees and Kalaman's dockhands:** how fast it fell, and what dragons do to a city.
- **Knights and Solamnic nobles:** the old ties, and that no help was sent.
