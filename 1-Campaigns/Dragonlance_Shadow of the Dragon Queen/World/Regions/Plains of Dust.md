# Plains of Dust

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Tarsis|ch. 1, "Tarsis"]]).

**What everyone knows.** Before the Cataclysm these were green plains in southern Ansalon. Now they're dust and scrub, crossed by nomad bands and dotted with small settlements that trade at [[Tarsis]].

## Who Knows What

- **Everyone:** dust, nomads and not much else.
- **Plains nomads:** that long lines of elves crossed the plains towards Southern Ergoth (Canon).
