# Dargaard Keep

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[01-war-comes-to-krynn|"War Comes to Krynn"]], [[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns|ch. 3]] on Leedara, and [[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war|ch. 4]] on Soth and Caradoc).

**What everyone knows.** A black ruin in the Dargaard Mountains, south of [[Kalaman]], always under cloud. Before the Cataclysm it was the seat of the Soths, a family of Knights of Solamnia who ruled the province then called Knightlund. Now it's a cursed, haunted ruin, and nobody goes there.

**What really happened.** [[Lord Soth]], a Knight of the Rose, rescued the Silvanesti priest Isolde, then broke his vows for her. The gods gave him a chance to stop the Cataclysm. He turned back, out of jealousy stirred by Isolde's attendants, and the fire took Dargaard. He rose as the Dread Knight.

**Who's there.**
- **Soth**, until the Dragon Queen calls him away. After that the keep is empty of its master for the first time in three and a half centuries.
- **Leedara**, the banshee, once an elven priest and Isolde's companion. For centuries she has tormented Soth with the memory of what he did.
- **Caradoc**, Soth's seneschal, now a spirit. He can't leave the keep's grounds except inside a body he has possessed.
- **Soth's knights**, risen as skeletal warriors.

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew** ([[Soth's Host]], [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]]).
> - **The Greenshield gate.** As the fire took Dargaard and the valley's people came begging for shelter, Soth ordered the gates barred. The first Greenshield opened them and held them with Ispin's shield. The shield came through the flames, and Soth never forgave it.
> - **The shadow spreads.** Since Soth woke, the dead of Nightlund's barrows don't rest. Undead are always worse closer to the mountains, and black roses grow on the opened graves.
> - **The vampire lords.** The blood knights who bent the knee to Soth keep their courts in the foothills.

## In This Campaign

**Seen, not visited.** Dargaard stays on the skyline: a black shape the party sees from the castle's windows and the Esker Brook road. Mention it often and explain it never. Ispin's campfire stories ([[1.00 An Ordinary Day]]) are the first time the players hear of it.

**After Kalaman.** Soth falls into the dark when the citadel breaks, but he isn't destroyed. If the players want more, the sequel is a **showdown at Dargaard Keep**: the barrows, the vampire lords, Leedara's song, and the end of the Greenshield story. It isn't written yet. See [[Soth's Host#After Kalaman]].
