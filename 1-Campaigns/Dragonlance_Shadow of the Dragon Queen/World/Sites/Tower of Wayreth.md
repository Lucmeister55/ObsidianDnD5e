# Tower of Wayreth

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Towers of High Sorcery|ch. 1, "Towers of High Sorcery"]] and "The Test of High Sorcery"), with wider Dragonlance lore.

**What everyone knows.** There were five Towers of High Sorcery. Three fell in the Cataclysm, and the one in [[Palanthas]] is cursed. **Wayreth** is the last one still working: the home of the Mages of High Sorcery and their Conclave, and the store of their greatest secrets. It stands in a magic forest that moves. You don't find Wayreth. It finds you, and only if the mages want it to.

**The Test.** Every mage who wants robes takes the Test of High Sorcery. It's different for everyone, and it can kill.

**Ties.** Wyhan is the Mages' voice in Kalaman ([[Mages of High Sorcery]]). A spellcaster PC's Test is coming ([[5.00 Destiny]]).

## History

> [!info] Source: **Canon** ([Lost Battles](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Battles), [Towers of High Sorcery](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Towers_of_High_Sorcery), [Dragonlance Timeline](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Dragonlance_Timeline)). Fan wikis, so dates can vary. Who knows what is **Homebrew**.

- **The five Towers.** Losarcum, Daltigoth, Istar, Palanthas and Wayreth.
- **The Lost Battles (19 PC).** The Kingpriest's war on the mages began at Losarcum, with the Knights of the Divine Hammer. The mages destroyed their own Towers at Losarcum and Daltigoth, and the blast at Daltigoth took a quarter of the city with it.
- **The bargain.** Horrified, the Kingpriest let the mages go in peace. They gave up Istar and Palanthas, and carried everything they could through magical portals to Wayreth. It has been their sanctuary ever since.
- **Palanthas.** As it was handed over, the Tower was cursed, so that no one may enter until the "master of the past and present" comes to claim it.
