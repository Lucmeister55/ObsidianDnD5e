# Thorbardin

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Thorbardin and the Dwarfgate War|ch. 1, "Thorbardin and the Dwarfgate War"]]).

**What everyone knows.** The great dwarf kingdom under the **Kharolis Mountains** in the southwest, thousands of years old. Its gates have been shut since the Cataclysm. The **Hylar** clan rules it, and its mountain dwarves want nothing to do with outsiders, other dwarves included. See [[Dwarves]].

**The Dwarfgate War.** After the Cataclysm, the High King sealed the gates against everyone, even the surface clan, the **Neidar**. The Neidar and other starving refugees tried to break in, and failed. Their descendants are the hill dwarves, and they haven't forgiven it.

## History

> [!info] Source: **Canon** ([Dragonlance Timeline](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Dragonlance_Timeline), [Dwarf](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Dwarf), [Lost Battles](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Battles)). Fan wikis, so dates can vary. Who knows what is **Homebrew**.

- **The delving.** Dwarves from Thorin reached the Kharolis Mountains, and finished the kingdom by about 2000 PC.
- **Friends once.** They fought beside the elves in the Kinslayer War and helped raise Pax Tharkas ([[Elves#The Kinslayer War]]).
- **The Dwarfgate War (39 AC).** The war at the gates drew in the wizard Fistandantilus.

## Who Knows What

- **Everyone:** the gates are shut.
- **Hill dwarves:** their grandparents' version of the Dwarfgate War.
- **Mountain dwarves:** the clans' feuds, and how few outsiders have seen the halls.
