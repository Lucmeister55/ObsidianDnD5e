# Mount Nevermind

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Gnomes|ch. 1, "Gnomes"]], [[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#City Market|ch. 4, "City Market"]]).

**What everyone knows.** A hollow, dormant volcano on the island of **Sancrist** in the far west, full of tinker gnomes. They invent constantly, and things explode constantly. Their curios turn up in Kalaman's market now and then. See [[Gnomes]].

**Ties.** Tatina Rookledust ([[3.00 Storm Clouds]]) and her gnomeflinger ([[Kalaman]]). A tinkerer PC will find gnome engineering familiar, and horrifying.
