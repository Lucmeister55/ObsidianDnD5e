# Relgoth

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, from [[Davos Bloodbane]] and [[Davos Bloodbane (DM)]]. Relgoth itself is a Solamnic city from wider Dragonlance lore.

**The city.** The capital of the province of Vingaard: an old Solamnic city in a dry, bowl-shaped valley near the Vingaard River, on the Knight's High Road between Delgaard and Ryn. It has been fought over for centuries, and it still hasn't recovered from the last occupation. It lies upriver from [[Kalaman]], on the same water.

**Inside the old walls** live the families with money and names. **Outside them**, in the lower districts, the river labourers, trade workers and small craftsmen live on top of one another.

**Places a sorcerer PC knows.**
- **The lower districts**, where the Bloodbanes live now: a mother, a younger brother and a younger sister.
- **The city prison**, where the father died. Nobody in the family talks about why.
- **The bathhouse** where the white robes were left on a bench, and where a guard died for them. Antimodes was watching ([[Davos Bloodbane (DM)]]).

**In the war.** The Red and Blue Dragon Armies are massing on Solamnia, and Relgoth is upriver from Kalaman. If Kalaman falls, nothing stands between the armies and the river towns. Letters from home ([[3.00 Storm Clouds]]) bring the war closer to the family one rumour at a time.
