# Tarsis

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Tarsis|ch. 1, "Tarsis"]]).

**What everyone knows.** A seaport without a sea. When the Cataclysm moved the coast, Tarsis was stranded on the [[Plains of Dust]], and the wrecks of its famous ships still lean in the dry harbour on waves of sand. It lives as a trading town for the plains. Its people turned their sailing ways to hunting, scavenging and caravans.

## History

> [!info] Source: **Canon** ([List of Dragonlance locations](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Dragonlance_locations)). Fan wikis, so dates can vary. Who knows what is **Homebrew**.

Tarsis was a major port before the Cataclysm. When the Empire of Ergoth built a navy of its own, it invaded and forced Tarsis to surrender.
