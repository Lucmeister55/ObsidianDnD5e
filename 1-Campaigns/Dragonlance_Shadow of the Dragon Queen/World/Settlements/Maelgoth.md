# Maelgoth

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[01-war-comes-to-krynn|"War Comes to Krynn"]], [[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war|ch. 4]] and [[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman|ch. 7]]) and **Companion** ([[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]], Part 3, "Mission 3").

**A knights' city.** A Solamnic city about **150 miles west** of [[Kalaman]], and the nearest place where the Knights of Solamnia still have real strength. The book's opening letter is written to the Council of Maelgoth, by knights asking how to mend things with Kalaman. Vogler lives under Maelgoth's distant protection ([[1.00 An Ordinary Day]]).

**In the war.**
- The Dragon Army tries to cut Kalaman off from Maelgoth and Palanthas in the west.
- Vendri sends to Maelgoth for help without the council's say-so. Clearing the road is the Draconian Blockade mission ([[3.00 Storm Clouds]]).
- The Maelgoth scouts' scroll reveals a **second Dragon Army**, the Blue, already at war with Maelgoth ([[3.07 Mission 3 - Draconian Blockade#Briefing 7, War Room, The Road to Maelgoth|Briefing 7]]). Maelgoth has its own war, and no help is coming soon.

**Where a knight PC was knighted.** Ispin sent his squire here to seek his knighthood. He spent the winter in the knights' yards, then swore the Oath and took the Measure before the Council of Maelgoth, a few weeks before Vogler ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge]]). He knows the city's knights, and they know him, a little.

**The relief column.** *(Homebrew.)* Maelgoth answers Vendri's secret letter with what it can spare: a few dozen knights and wagons of arrows, hunted down the western road. Vendri rides out herself to bring them in, which is why she's away when Bakaris takes the army to Steel Springs ([[3.10 Downtime 5]]). The column fights in the siege with the Knights of Solamnia.

**Afterwards.** If he lives, **Darrett** goes to Maelgoth to become a true knight. Vendri sends agents there to court the Knights ([[6.00 Conquest]]).

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew** ([[Davos Bloodbane]]).
> **The Barb**, a ruined spire near Maelgoth, only wakes with magic while the moons are aligned. A sorcerer PC sat the Trial of the Barb there on the Night of the Eye, 14 Brookgreen 350 AC.

## Who Knows What

- **Everyone:** a knights' city somewhere west.
- **Knights and squires:** that the Order's strength in the east is mostly here, and how thin it is.
- **Vendri and the Council of Maelgoth:** what passed between them in her secret letters.
