# Solace

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Abanasinia|ch. 1, "Abanasinia"]]), with wider Dragonlance lore.

**What everyone knows.** A village in [[Abanasinia]] built high in the branches of giant **vallenwood** trees, with rope bridges between the houses and a lake nearby. Travellers know it for the **Inn of the Last Home**, up in the biggest tree, and for its spiced potatoes.

> [!warning] DM only
> It lies in the Red Dragon Army's path ([[Dragon Armies]]).
