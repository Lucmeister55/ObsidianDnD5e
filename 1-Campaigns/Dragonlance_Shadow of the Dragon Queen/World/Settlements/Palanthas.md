# Palanthas

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[01-war-comes-to-krynn#The Cataclysm|"War Comes to Krynn"]], [[02-chapter-1-character-creation#Towers of High Sorcery|"Towers of High Sorcery"]]), with wider Dragonlance lore.

**What everyone knows.** The greatest city of northern [[Solamnia]], on a sheltered bay. It's rich, beautiful and proud, and it came through the Cataclysm untouched. Its own lord, **Amothus**, rules it.

**Places.**
- **The Great Library.** The largest collection of records in Ansalon, kept by the chronicler **Astinus**. The book's opening letter was written by one of its scholars.
- **The Tower of High Sorcery.** Once the finest of the five towers, now cursed and empty. Nobody goes near it. The dark wood around it, the **Shoikan Grove**, kills anyone who tries.
- **The High Clerist's Tower.** The knights' great fortress, guarding the mountain pass south of the city.

**Trade.** Even near Palanthas, it's often cheaper to send goods down the Vingaard to [[Kalaman]].
