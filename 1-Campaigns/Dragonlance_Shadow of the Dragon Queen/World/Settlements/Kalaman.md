## The City

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[01-war-comes-to-krynn#City of Kalaman|"City of Kalaman"]], [[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Kalaman Gazetteer|ch. 4, "Kalaman Gazetteer"]]) and **Companion** ([[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]], Appendix B).

**The Beacon of the East.** Kalaman is the capital of Nightlund and one of the largest cities in Solamnia, a deepwater port on the Turbidus Ocean near the mouth of the Vingaard, about thirty miles downriver from [[Vogler]]. It's one of the nation's great gateways to the outside world. The Vingaard is Solamnia's greatest trade artery, and even goods from near Palanthas often travel cheaper by river to Kalaman. Crops and crafted goods come down from the Plains of Solamnia. Its Istarian walls have never been breached.

**Who lives here.** Solamnic humans and families descended from the Istarian founders, with traders who settled here from Abanasinia, Nordmaar, Ergoth, Estwilde and further afield. Kender, hill dwarves and gnomes are common, and a few elves and mountain dwarves live here too. The city is cosmopolitan, while the country around it fishes and farms. Anyone is welcome who comes peaceably with goods to trade.

**Who rules.** Three powers, who rarely agree:
- **The Governor,** Calof Miat, who speaks for the people in council. The five great guilds appoint him (Companion).
- **The Guild Council,** which oversees the city's business and runs its public services. Its leaders are human and hill dwarf nobles, always bickering: **Alexandra Nightvane** (Cartographers), **Oflyn Vinhollow** (Dockhands), **Reynold Costwyn** (Fishers), **Uri Stonegrey** (Masons) and **Sigmund Pross** (Shipwrights). See [[The Guild Council]] and [[Kalaman Guilds]].
- **The Marshal,** [[Nestra Vendri]]. The city keeps a strong army of its own because the Knights are headquartered far to the west, and it can't send for them every time raiders cross from Estwilde or monsters come down from the Dargaard Mountains. See [[Kalaman Military]].

**The mood.** Proud, rich and stubborn. Because of the Dread Knight, the city doesn't trust the Knights of Solamnia. The weather is grey and drizzly, and the Dargaard Mountains sit on the southern skyline under their own dark clouds ([[Dargaard Keep]]). People blame the storms on the old gods' disfavour.

## Who Knows What

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Knowledge of Kalaman|ch. 4, "Knowledge of Kalaman"]]) for what everyone knows. The rest is **Homebrew**, sorted from canon facts on this page.

**Everyone** (locals, or DC 12 History). It's Nightlund's capital. Its walls, castle and beacons all predate the Cataclysm. A governor, a guild council and a marshal run it. The army is there for Estwilde's raiders and the Dargaard Mountains' monsters. It sits at the mouth of the Vingaard, about thirty miles below Vogler.

**Some people:**
- **Soldiers, and Jesen Thold's regulars,** know how thin the army is stretched, and that Vendri has to obey the council to keep her post.
- **Castle staff and old guards** know the catacombs are magically sealed, the dungeons are ruined, and the fourth floor is falling apart.
- **Priests of the old ways and anyone who reads the stonework** recognise Kiri-Jolith all through the castle (DC 12 Religion), and know whose castle this once was.
- **Dockhands** know to pay the Firefishers. Very few know who pays the Firefishers.
- **Scholars, and Istarian families,** remember the blockade and the Age of Might wars as more than a boast.
- **Spellcasters, and anyone who knows what to ask,** know what's in Wyhan's back room.

## History

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[01-war-comes-to-krynn#Kalaman Region|"Kalaman Region"]], [[01-war-comes-to-krynn#Nightlund|"Nightlund"]]) and **Companion** ([[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]], Appendix B).

- **A fishing village.** Kalaman was a sleepy village on the coast until an Istarian trading mission arrived, centuries before the Cataclysm. Istar saw a deep harbour close to a great river's mouth, and invested heavily.
- **Istar and Solamnia.** The port boomed, and Solamnia found Istar's grip on it intolerable. The two wrestled for the city through the Age of Might, until a climactic battle on the plains west of the city, when Istar ceded it. Kalaman is proud of having survived a war with the most powerful empire in the world.
- **Knightlund.** For generations, the Soths of Dargaard Keep ruled this province. Before the Cataclysm, the gods of good warned Lord Loren Soth of the coming destruction and gave him a chance to stop it, and he failed.
- **The Cataclysm.** Kalaman was one of the few large communities to come through with little damage, and the region went on flourishing. The province's name did not: people have called it **Nightlund** ever since.

> [!info] Source: **Canon** ([Dragonlance Wiki](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Kalaman), [ForgottenLance](https://www.forgottenlance.com/atlas/kalaman.htm)).
> Before the Cataclysm, Kalaman and Istar became bitter rivals in the sea trade, and at one point the Istarian navy blockaded the city completely.

## Locations

### Castle Hill

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Castle Kalaman|ch. 4, "Castle Kalaman"]] and "Within the Castle").

- **Castle Kalaman.** An old Solamnic fortress on hundred-foot cliffs at the city's heart, visible for miles. Giant statues line the path up the cliff. The Knights used it for centuries, and its halls still show knights and the gods and animals they honoured. Its defences include some no one can build any more, and its barracks hold hundreds. Inside:
  - **First floor:** the council chamber, government offices, a small library and the armoury.
  - **Second floor:** military command and officers' lodgings.
  - **Third floor:** the castle guard.
  - **Fourth floor:** mostly empty and falling apart. The party sleeps in an old ballroom with knight-carved pillars and tall windows facing the mountains.
  - **Basement:** storage, and the sealed doors to the old dungeons.
  - **Who:** Governor Miat, Vendri and Darrett. As the war gets closer, worried citizens crowd the castle gates asking for news (Companion).
- **Solamnic statuary.** All through the castle, the stonework shows **Kiri-Jolith**, patron of the Knights of the Sword (DC 12 Religion). It's a quiet reminder that this was a knights' castle before it was the city's.
- **The catacombs.** Long passages under the castle, sealed by magic for centuries. Soth wants what's inside them ([[3.13 The Raided Catacombs#The Lord's Arrival and the Raided Catacombs|Storm Clouds]]).

### The Walls and Gates

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Harbor Beacons|ch. 4]] and [[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Day of Dread|ch. 7, "Day of Dread"]] to "Over Enemy Lines").

- **The walls.** Tall, Istarian-built, lined with giant statues, and never breached. Ballista crews man them in the siege.
- **The Trade Gate (west).** Where caravans and refugees come in. The trade camps sprawl outside it. In the siege it's Vendri's command post, and **Captain Oklid Narnhelm** lowers its portcullis on the refugees.
- **The Warrior's Gate (east).** A smaller gate with a guardroom and a portcullis winch beside it. It's where Lord Bakaris tries to let the enemy in.
- **The southwest wall.** Tatina posts herself here in the siege, with a defunct catapult she and Than have turned into a **gnomeflinger**. So far no commander will use it.
- **The Harbour Beacons.** The walls run several hundred feet out into the harbour. A tower stands at each end, with a pre-Cataclysm flame behind rings of lenses whose light cuts through any fog.

### Harbour and Market

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Kalaman Locations|ch. 4, "Kalaman Locations"]]) and **Companion** (Appendix B and Part 3).

- **Kalaman Harbour.** Dozens of ships at the docks, from fishing boats to ocean-going merchantmen. Each day there's a **10% chance** a ship from a distant land puts in, with strange wares and stranger stories.
- **City Market.** The great open bazaar, run by the city. Anything in the Player's Handbook, plus curios from places like Ergoth and Mount Nevermind. Once the war starts, everything costs 50% more (Companion).
- **Hammerstrike Forges.** Two forges facing each other across one street, run by the dwarf cousins **Kadmos** (armour) and **Tiria** (weapons). By the Day of Dread there's a sign on a smithy door: "Weapons out of stock. Sorry and good luck."
- **Meulara's Oddities.** A trinket shop run by **Meulara**, a silver-haired kender who claims to know every kender in the city. New kender in town get sent to her.
- **Wyhan's Apothecary.** Good-luck charms at the front. Esoteric spell components in the back, for those who know what to ask. Run by **Wyhan** of the Black Robes, supposedly retired, who has been the Mages' representative here for over a decade ([[Mages of High Sorcery]]). She thinks the city is sheep in need of a shepherd, and hopes the Mages will quietly take it over one day (Companion).
- **The Steady Beacon.** A big tavern with a fish menu and rooms upstairs, its walls hung with the broken weapons of defeated bandits. It's the soldiers' favourite. Run by **Jesen Thold**, Vendri's old friend. Councillor **Reynold Costwyn** drinks here, and he loves to mock anyone who prays (Companion).
- **Wanderer's Rest.** The dockworkers' tavern, with a sand floor and sea creatures painted on the walls. Run by **Zol Windswell**, a one-legged Dargonesti elf. The Firefishers lean on him (Companion).
- **Hetch Warehouse.** A dockside warehouse, and the secret base of the **Firefishers**, the gang councillor **Oflyn Vinhollow** pays for ([[Kalaman Guilds]]).

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.
> - **The Knights' Chapterhouse.** A cold hall with a handful of knights, a great many old banners and a great deal of pride ([[Knights of Solamnia]]).
> - **The Cinque Teste.** A private casino in the old Istarian cisterns, reached through Hetch Warehouse, run by the Firefishers. Few people know it's there, and the chips have five little heads on them ([[Peppino Fettuccini#The Five Heads]]).
> - **The Bastion.** Six empty properties the city can spare, from a beacon house on the harbour wall to a house over an Istarian cistern ([[3.03 Mission 1 - Rookledust Rescue#Choosing the Bastion|Choosing the Bastion]]).

### Outside the Walls

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Trade Camps|ch. 4]] and [[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Assault on Hawker's Grove|ch. 7]]).

- **The Trade Camps.** East and west of the city, rows of shacks on muddy streets. They started as caravan camps. Now hundreds who can't find room inside live there, and the [[Vogler Refugees]] join them.
- **Esker Brook.** The road south through fields, hills and scattered woods. **[[Tatina Rookledust|Tatina Rookledust's]]** workshop is twelve miles down it, until the goblins come.
- **Hawker's Grove.** A wood southwest of the city, where Clystran brings his dragonnels in the siege. It burns.
- **The Dargaard Mountains.** On the southern skyline, always under cloud. Nightlund is said to be a land where the sun never shines. Really it's just gloomy, and it drizzles a lot ([[Dargaard Keep]]).

## Further Afield

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[01-war-comes-to-krynn#Surrounding Regions|"Surrounding Regions"]]).

- **West:** Hinterlund, then the Plains of Solamnia. [[Vogler]] is thirty miles upriver, [[Relgoth]] is further up the Vingaard, and [[Maelgoth]] is the knights' city.
- **North:** grassland gives way to the broken steppes and canyons of the Northern Wastes, where beasts, cliffs and sudden floods mean nothing is said to survive for long.
- **East:** Estwilde, a hard land whose people have been unfriendly to Solamnia since the days of Istar. The swamps of Qwalmish lie across the bay.
- **South and east:** the rolling steppes of Qlettaar, and beyond them the peaks of Taman Busuk.
- **South:** [[Dargaard Keep]], in the mountains.

## After the Campaign

> [!info] Source: **Canon** ([Dragonlance Wiki](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Kalaman), [*Dragons of Spring Dawning*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragons_of_Spring_Dawning)). **DM only.**
> The novels and setting books don't include this campaign's events, so treat these as where the wider story can go.
> - **War of the Lance.** The Blue Wing takes Kalaman in spring 351 AC, and a Flying Citadel assault partly destroys it. Laurana, the Golden General, frees it in the Vingaard Campaign of 352 AC. Kalaman honours her at its **Spring Dawning Festival**, and Soth carries her off to [[Dargaard Keep]].
> - **Afterwards.** The people ask Gilthanas Kanan to be governor, and he accepts. The Knights of Takhisis later take the city, until a militia drives them out in the Age of Mortals. After the War of Souls, people return in large numbers.
>
> In this campaign, the siege is in Reapember 351 AC and the walls are expected to hold. If you want canon's war to follow, the Blue Wing's spring capture needs moving to after your ending.
