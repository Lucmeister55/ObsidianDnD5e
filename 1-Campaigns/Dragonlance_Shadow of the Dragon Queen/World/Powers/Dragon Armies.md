# The Dragon Armies

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[01-war-comes-to-krynn#The Dragon Armies|"The Dragon Armies"]] and "The War of the Lance").

Takhisis's forces, five armies of draconians ([[Draconians]]), humans, goblins, hobgoblins and ogres, with chromatic dragons, dragonnels and wyverns overhead. Every highlord answers to her champion, **Ariakas**, in [[Taman Busuk]]. Treachery between and within the armies is encouraged.

| Army | Highlord | In 351 AC |
|---|---|---|
| **Red** | Verminaard, with **[[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]** leading the attack on Solamnia | The largest. Most of it has gone into [[Abanasinia]]. Kansaldi's force is coming for [[Kalaman]]. |
| **Blue** | Kitiara Uth Matar | The second strongest. Preparing to invade Solamnia, and already at war with [[Maelgoth]]. |
| **Green** | Salah-Khan | Holds [[Khur]]. |
| **Black** | Lucien of Takar, a half-ogre | Occupies [[Nordmaar]] and [[Goodlund]]. |
| **White** | Feal-Thas, a Silvanesti Black Robe | Small. Conquering [[Icereach]]. |

**What people in Kalaman know.** Rumours of "invaders from the east", "lizard-men", and Nordmaar and Goodlund falling. Nobody in the city has seen a dragon yet, and most don't believe in them.

## Faces of the Army

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, built on the module's note that the armies include "humans, goblins, hobgoblins, ogres, and others".

The Dragon Army isn't all draconians. Mix these in, so every fight doesn't look the same. Most reuse a stat block you already have.

| Who | What they're like | Stat blocks | Use them for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Draconians** | The Queen's own. Disciplined, terrifying, and even dying they're dangerous | The draconians ([[Draconians]]) | The core of any real fight |
| **Dragon Army regulars** | Human soldiers in red and black. Some are believers, most are paid | Dragon Army Soldier, Officer | Columns, garrisons, the officer in charge of everyone else |
| **Khurish mercenaries** | Desert raiders who came north for the pay, like Ardlic Vanse. Fast, cruel, and quick to run | Bandit, Bandit Captain, Berserker | Raids, Wheelwatch's garrison, farm burnings, and anything near a knight PC's past |
| **Hobgoblin legions** | Taman Busuk's hobgoblins: drilled, ugly and proud of it | Hobgoblin Warrior, Captain, Warlord | Camps, sieges, Camp Carrionclay |
| **Goblin rabble** | Cheap, numerous, and nobody's favourite | Goblin Warrior, Boss | Stealing, scouting, Rookledust's workshop |
| **Ogres** | Hired muscle from the mountains | Ogre, Ogrillon | Gate-smashing, the ballista at the blockade |
| **Minotaur corsairs** | Hired ships out of Mithas. Honour among themselves, none for anyone else | Minotaur of Baphomet (no demon lord), Pirate, Pirate Captain | The sea: the crossing, the bay, anything with a sail |
| **Black Robes and zealots** | Takhisis's priests and the Army's mages | Cultist, Mage, Priest | The citadel, Lohezet's people, the night before a battle |

**A rule of thumb:** any fight that isn't a set piece gets at most **one kind** of draconian, plus one other face from this list.

## How Big, and Where

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. Numbers sized to the module's "hundreds" and "thousands", and to [[The War in Numbers]].

### What a Force Looks Like

Use this whenever the party sees the enemy on the move, finds a camp, or hears a scout's report.

| What | How many | Where you'll see one |
|---|---|---|
| **Patrol** | 6 to 12 | Roads around Kalaman, random encounters in the Wastes |
| **Raiding party** | 20 to 60 | A burning farm, the farmstead, a village that wasn't reached in time |
| **Outpost or blockade** | 50 to 150 | The draconian blockade on the Maelgoth road, Camp Carrionclay |
| **Fort garrison** | 200 to 300 | Wheelwatch, after it falls |
| **Column** | 300 to 800 | The column in the Marshal's Gambit, a force on the march between camps |
| **Field army** | 1,500 to 2,500 | Kansaldi's army at Steel Springs |
| **Host** | 5,000 and more | The siege of Kalaman |

**Telling them apart.** A camp of 100 is a few dozen tents and one cookfire smoke. A camp of 2,000 is a town of canvas with its own latrine stink, visible for miles, and its night fires look like a second sky. A column of 500 takes an hour to pass a point on the road. Draconians don't need tents, which makes their camps look smaller than they are.

**What it's made of:**

| Ogres | 30 | Ogres |
| Dragonnels (Red Ruin's wing) | 20 | Dragonnel Riders |
| Engines and boilerdraks | 6 | War Engine |
| **Ignia**, Kansaldi's red dragon | 1 | Monster |

**Where it is:**

| Where | How many | What they're doing |

### At the Siege

About **7,000**: the Kalaman front, what's left of the Wastes army, 1,000 fresh troops from Taman Busuk, and 500 on the flying citadel. Add Soth's 30 skeletal knights, 3 lesser death dragons, Ignia, 40 dragonnels and 12 war engines.
