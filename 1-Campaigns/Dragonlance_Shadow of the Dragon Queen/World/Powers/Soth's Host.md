# Soth's Host

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, built on the **Module**'s skeletal knights, death dragons and the Chemosh cult in the flying citadel ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#The Flying Citadel|ch. 7]]). Minis: Soulblight Gravelords.

[[Lord Soth]] doesn't only have his skeletal knights. Since the Dragon Queen woke him, the dead of Nightlund have been getting up. Soth's Host gives him a way to **reach into the story between his big scenes**, so the party feels the Dread Knight's shadow long before they learn his name. It's also a darker, creepier texture next to the Dragon Army's draconians.

**Don't overdo it.** Each act gets **one or two** undead encounters at most. They should be rare, cold and memorable, and the party should never be sure who sent them.

For [[Skirmish Battles]], use **Undead Knights** for barrow knights and blood knights. A Deadwalker horde is a Foot unit (AC 8, HP 90, Damage 15, never Shaken, speed 20). A fell bat swarm is a Fliers unit (AC 13, HP 40, Damage 10, Morale +2).

## Where Do They Come From?

This is an open question. Let the party find clues, and decide the answer only when you need it. Each answer points somewhere different.

1. **The Shadow of Dargaard.** Soth's curse didn't stay in his keep. As his power grows, the shadow spreads across Nightlund, and the dead in its barrows don't rest anymore. *Clues:* undead are always worse the closer you get to the mountains, and black roses grow on the opened barrows.
2. **The Cataclysm's fire.** The violet flame Soth takes from the catacombs ([[3.00 Storm Clouds]]) raises the dead, which is how it raises the dragons in Onyari. Soth has been feeding it to Nightlund's graves. *Clues:* violet light in a barrow, and corpses that burn cold.
3. **Chemosh's bargain.** The vampires belong to the cult of Chemosh, the god of undeath, the same cult that rules the Bastion of Takhisis in the module ([[6.06 The Flying Citadel#The Flying Citadel]], Alstare Bellis). They serve Soth by pact, for a share of the dead of Kalaman. *Clues:* the black skull of Chemosh on the blood knights' armour, and a vargheist with a priest's ring.

Answers 1 and 3 both lead to Dargaard Keep, and that's the open end of the campaign (see below).

## After Kalaman

The open end. Soth falls into the dark when the citadel breaks ([[6.00 Conquest]]), but he isn't destroyed. If the players want more, the obvious sequel is a **showdown at Dargaard Keep**: the shadow, the barrows, the vampire lords, and the end of the Greenshield story. It isn't written yet. Just keep the threads loose.
