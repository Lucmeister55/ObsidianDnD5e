# Soth's Host

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, built on the **Module**'s skeletal knights, death dragons and the Chemosh cult in the flying citadel ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#The Flying Citadel|ch. 7]]). Minis: Soulblight Gravelords.

Since the Dragon Queen woke [[Lord Soth]], Nightlund's dead have been getting up. They let him reach into the story between his big scenes, long before the party learns his name. **One or two encounters per act at most**, and the party should never be sure who sent them.

## The Host

| Mini | What it is | Stat block | Use |
|---|---|---|---|

| **Fell Bats** | Bloated, hairless, dog-sized, corpse-light eyes | [[giant-bat-xmm\|Giant Bat]] as Undead: immune to poison and fear, Bite +1d4 necrotic | Soth's eyes, watching from rooftops long before anything attacks. |

| **Vargheists** | Starved vampires turned flying beasts | [[vampire-spawn-xmm\|Vampire Spawn]], fly 40 ft, no Spider Climb | Soth's hounds, sent to take something. Never alone. |
| **Blood Knights** | Vampire nobles of Nightlund in black plate, on undead destriers | [[vampire-nightbringer-xmm\|Vampire Nightbringer]] on a [[warhorse-skeleton-xmm\|Warhorse Skeleton]] | Courteous and terrifying. They talk before they kill. |
| **Terrorgheist / Zombie Dragon** | | [[lesser-death-dragon-dsotdq\|Lesser Death Dragon]] | The module's death dragons. |

For [[Skirmish Battles]], use **Undead Knights** for barrow knights and blood knights. A Deadwalker horde is an **Infantry** battalion with **Undead Fortitude** (speed 20). A fell bat swarm is **Flying Cavalry**.

## Where Do They Come From?

Leave it open until you need it. Let the party find clues.
1. **The Shadow of Dargaard.** Soth's curse is spreading across Nightlund. *Clues:* worse near the mountains, black roses on opened barrows.
2. **The Cataclysm's fire.** Soth feeds the violet flame from the catacombs ([[3.13 The Raided Catacombs]]) to Nightlund's graves, as it raises Onyari's dragons. *Clues:* violet light in a barrow, corpses that burn cold.
3. **Chemosh's bargain.** The vampires are the Chemosh cult that rules the Bastion of Takhisis ([[6.06 The Flying Citadel]], Alstare Bellis), serving Soth for a share of Kalaman's dead. *Clues:* Chemosh's black skull on blood knights' armour, a vargheist with a priest's ring.

Answers 1 and 3 lead to Dargaard Keep.

## After Kalaman

Soth falls into the dark when the citadel breaks, but isn't destroyed. The sequel, if wanted, is **Dargaard Keep**: the barrows, the vampire lords and the end of the Greenshield story. Keep the threads loose.

## Who Knows What

- **Everyone:** the Dread Knight stories.
- **Nightlund's farmers:** the barrows don't rest as they used to.
- **Scouts and Maelgoth patrols:** they've seen undead soldiers, and nobody believes them.
- **Very few:** that Soth serves the Dragon Queen, and why ([[Lord Soth#Why He Serves the Queen]]).
