# Kalaman Smallfolk

See [[Faction Rules]].
**Horizon: mixed.** A little every downtime, and a lot in the siege.

The ordinary people of Kalaman: dock labourers, fishwives, carters, washerwomen, farmers from the villages outside the walls, the survivors of Vogler in the trade camps, and children underfoot everywhere. They don't sit on any council, but there are more of them than anyone else, and they're the ones who will suffer first if the city falls. They're tough and suspicious, gossip travels fast among them, and they never forget who helped them and who didn't.

**Who speaks for them:** no one officially. **Jesen Thold** at the Steady Beacon hears everything. **Meulara**, the kender who runs Meulara's Oddities, knows every family in the poor quarters (and every kender in town). In the trade camps, Vogler's **Spokesperson** does ([[Vogler Refugees]]).

**They want:** bread, safety, their families protected, honest dealing, and to be noticed by those in charge.
**They hate:** nobles and guilds who profit from their misery, broken promises, and anything that brings the war to their door.
**Rivals:** [[The Firefishers]] (who take a cut of everything they earn), and the [[Knights of Solamnia]] (whom many blame for the Cataclysm).

## By Band

| Band | How they treat you | Each downtime | In the siege |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Honoured** (+6 to +10) | Your names are sung in the taverns. | Two useful rumours, **5 Supply** of dried fish, and the street children are your eyes everywhere. | The whole lower city fights: fire brigades and barricades in every street, Vogler's militia at the Trade Gate, and fishing boats running supplies along the shore. |
| **Trusted** (+3 to +5) | People bring you their problems. | One useful rumour, **3 Supply** of dried fish, and free food and lodging in the lower city. | Fire brigades and runners. Fires never spread, messages always get through, and Vogler's militia holds the Trade Gate beside the soldiers. |
| **Neutral** (0 to +2) | Smiles and directions. | One useful rumour. | People stay calm and out of the defenders' way, and the camps keep the gate clear. |
| **Wary** (-1, -2) | Doors close as you pass. | Nothing. | Nothing. |
| **Hostile** (-3 to -5) | Jeered in the street. | Nobody sells to you in the poor quarters. | Panic, with riots at the granaries and crowds blocking the streets. The camps take their boats and flee, and the Trade Gate goes undefended. |

**Mission perk (Trusted+):** carters and farmers who've seen the enemy tell you exactly where, so the party can't be surprised on arrival. Near Vogler, a guide or a fishing boat saves a day's travel.
**Mission complication (Hostile):** someone gossips about the party's plans in a tavern, and the enemy is waiting.

**When leaving for the Wastes:** whatever the poor can spare, given with pride. That's 2 Supply at Neutral, and 5 at Trusted or better.

**Signature reward (+10):** the lower city hides a secret store for the siege, enough food and arrows to let Kalaman hold for one extra day when it matters most. After the war, Vogler's militia and fishing fleet sail to take their village back.
**Signature disaster (-5):** a mob comes for the party, and anyone they protect, in the middle of the night.

## Favours (Trusted or better)

- Hide someone, or something, where no official will find it.
- Find anyone in the city within a day.
- Vogler's fishermen and their boats, for anything on the river.
- A dozen militia volunteers for one mission.
- A crowd to cause a distraction, or to stand somewhere and refuse to move.
- A rumour spread, or one quietly killed.
