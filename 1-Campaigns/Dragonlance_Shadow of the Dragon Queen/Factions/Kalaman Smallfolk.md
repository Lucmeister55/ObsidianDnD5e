# Kalaman Smallfolk

See [[Faction Rules]].
**Horizon: short.** Goodwill every downtime.

Kalaman's ordinary people: dockers, fishwives, carters, farmers from outside the walls, Vogler's survivors in the camps. No council seat, but the most numerous, and the first to suffer if the city falls. Tough, suspicious, gossiping, and they never forget who helped.

**Who speaks for them:** no one officially. **Jesen Thold** at the Steady Beacon hears everything, **Meulara** knows every poor family (and every kender), and the camps have Vogler's **Spokesperson** ([[Vogler Refugees]]).

**They want:** bread, safety, their families protected, honest dealing, and to be noticed by those in charge.
**They hate:** nobles and guilds who profit from their misery, broken promises, and anything that brings the war to their door.
**Rivals:** [[The Firefishers]] (who take a cut of everything they earn), and the [[Knights of Solamnia]] (whom many blame for the Cataclysm).

## By Band

| Band | How they treat you | Each downtime |
|---|---|---|
| **Honoured** (+6 to +10) | Your names are sung in the taverns. | **2 Goodwill**, one useful rumour, and no lifestyle costs anywhere in the lower city |
| **Trusted** (+3 to +5) | People bring you their problems. | **1 Goodwill**, one useful rumour, and no lifestyle costs in the lower city |
| **Neutral** (0 to +2) | Smiles and directions. | One useful rumour. |
| **Wary** (-1, -2) | Doors close as you pass. | Nothing. |
| **Hostile** (-3 to -5) | Jeered in the street. | Lifestyle costs **double**: nobody in the poor quarters sells to you at a fair price. |

The downtime column pays at the start of each downtime ([[Faction Rules#Dividends|Paydays]]).

**In the siege,** the Smallfolk matter only at the extremes. At **Trusted or better**, Vogler's **1 Militia** unit holds the Trade Gate beside the soldiers. At **Hostile**, the Trade Gate's stretch of wall starts empty, and the enemy starts on top of it.

## Goodwill

A **token** for a family who owes you one. The party keeps them as a pool, and they never expire or get taken back. This replaces the Smallfolk's favours. Spend one at any time to:
- **A bed for the night:** a long rest anywhere in or around Kalaman, hidden from anyone looking, with a hot meal.
- **A way out:** a back door, a cart of cabbages, a crowd that closes behind you. The party escapes a chase, an arrest or a bad fight in the city. In an [[Infiltrations|infiltration]], the Alarm drops by 2.
- **Wagons and horses:** a farmer's cart or the miller's horses for a mission, saving a day's travel.
- **Boats:** Vogler's fishermen carry the party anywhere on the river or the bay.
- **Tip-off:** before a mission or fight, carters and fishwives say where the enemy is. The party can't be surprised and has advantage on initiative.
- **Ask around:** one useful rumour, or anyone in the city found within a day.
- **Hide it:** someone or something kept where no official will find it.
- **Volunteers:** a dozen militia for one mission.
- **Word of mouth:** a rumour spread, or one quietly killed (it stops the spy rumours about a dragonborn PC escalating this downtime).

**Mission perk (Trusted+):** a local guide who knows the back ways comes along.
**Mission complication (Hostile):** someone gossips about the party's plans in a tavern, and the enemy is waiting.

**When leaving for the Wastes:** whatever the poor can spare, given with pride. That's 2 Supply at Neutral, and 5 at Trusted or better.

**Signature reward (+10):** the lower city watches the walls for the party. In the Night of Terror, the sivaks in Kalaman uniforms are caught in the streets before they reach the wall: skip that fight. After the war, Vogler's militia and fishing fleet sail to take their village back.
**Signature disaster (-5):** a mob comes for the party, and anyone they protect, in the middle of the night.
