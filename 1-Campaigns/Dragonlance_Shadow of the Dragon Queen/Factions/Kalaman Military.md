# Kalaman Military

See [[Faction Rules]].
**Horizon: long.** Banks everything for the siege.

Kalaman's own army ([[Forces of Kalaman]]): practical soldiers used to raiders and ogres, who've never seen anything like the Dragon Army. The party serves it as esquires.

**Who speaks for them:** [[Nestra Vendri|Marshal Nestra Vendri]]. [[Darrett Highwater]] is the party's liaison. **Jesen Thold** of the Steady Beacon, Vendri's old friend, hears every soldier's gossip.

**They want:** Kalaman defended, good intelligence, soldiers who come home alive, and orders that are followed.
**They hate:** cowardice, wasting lives on glory, disobeying orders, and nobles playing at generals (Lord Bakaris above all).
**Rivals:** the [[Kalaman Guilds]], who hold the council's purse.

**The war room.** The party is summoned by default. At a payday at **Hostile**, Vendri stops calling them, and the war is decided without them until a payday back at Wary or better ([[3.00 Storm Clouds#How a Briefing Works]]).

Kalaman's soldiers always fight their hardest. Standing decides how far they trust the people beside them ([[Skirmish Battles#Morale]]). Everyone gets soldier's issue: spear, shield, chain shirt, cloak.

**When it's checked:** the downtime column pays at the **start of each downtime**. The battle column is read at the **mission check-in** before every skirmish where Kalaman's army fights: Steel Springs, the Wastes, the retreat, the black rose and the siege ([[Faction Rules#Dividends|Paydays]], [[6.02 Return to Kalaman#The Siege Ledger]]). What's been paid out stays, whatever happens to Standing afterwards.

## Titles

The company is promoted together, at a payday, in front of the garrison: Vendri reads the patent, and a sword touches each shoulder. A title is **never taken back** for losing Standing. Only the signature disaster strips it.

| Title | Earned | Rank and pay | Influence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Esquires of Kalaman** | Sworn in ([[3.01 Arrival in Kalaman]]) | Lieutenant's rank, **5 gp a week** | Briefed, never asked. |
| **Knights Bachelor** | First payday at Trusted | Captain's rank, **7 gp a week**, a knight's spurs | Once per downtime, one soldier reassigned, protected or punished on their word. |
| **Knights Banneret** | First payday at Honoured | Commander's rank, **10 gp a week**, their own banner over the company | One Kalaman unit always serves under their banner, whatever their Standing ([[Skirmish Battles#Who Commands What]]). Before the siege, they choose where Kalaman's reserve stands. |

A knight PC's own ranks run ahead of these ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)#Arc: Rising Through the Ranks]]).

## Commendations

A **token** (a chip or coin on the table) for a soldier's word in the right ear. The party keeps them as a pool, and they never expire or get taken back. This replaces the Military's favours. Spend one at any time to:
- **Rally:** a Kalaman unit passes a Morale check automatically.
- **Cover:** soldiers pull a fallen hero clear: stabilised and moved 30 feet to safety, however the fight is going.
- **Detail:** six guards for one mission.
- **Requisition:** fast horses, or a river boat and crew.
- **Pull a string:** a prisoner released into the party's custody.

**The Vogler file** is never burned on Standing. It goes only if they help save Kalaman ([[DM Guide#Why They Stay Together]]).

**Vendri's protection** over Wurm, as a member of her company, never lifts on Standing alone. Only Wurm's own acts end it ([[Wurm (DM)]]). Below Trusted, the custody is all she gives: at the trial she states it on the record and makes no special effort for any of them ([[4.02 The Trial - Testimony#Defence Witnesses]]). Saving them from the noose after Vogler was effort enough.

**Command:** Military Standing decides how many soldiers the party can lead in skirmishes and battles, from none at Hostile to the whole field at Honoured ([[Skirmish Battles#Who Commands What]]).

**Mission perk (Trusted+):** horses and two veteran warriors come along (four at Honoured), or a fresh report gives advantage on the first check to scout or ambush the enemy.
**Mission complication (Hostile):** no horses, and nobody comes looking if you're late.

**For the Wastes:** at Trusted or better, the quartermaster quietly loads **10 Supply** and **10 Water** into the party's own packs, so they aren't on the supply ship when it goes down.

**Signature reward (+10):** forty of Vendri's veterans ask to serve under the company's banner, and she lets them. They follow the party into the siege and into anything after it.
**Signature disaster (-5):** the party is stripped of every title, arrested for desertion or treason, and has to talk, fight or break its way out of Castle Kalaman.
