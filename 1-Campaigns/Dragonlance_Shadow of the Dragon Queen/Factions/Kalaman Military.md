# Kalaman Military

See [[Faction Rules]].
**Horizon: long.** Banks everything for the siege.

Kalaman's own army ([[Forces of Kalaman]]): practical soldiers used to raiders and ogres, who've never seen anything like the Dragon Army. The party serves it as esquires.

**Who speaks for them:** [[Nestra Vendri|Marshal Nestra Vendri]]. [[Darrett Highwater]] is the party's liaison. **Jesen Thold** of the Steady Beacon, Vendri's old friend, hears every soldier's gossip.

**They want:** Kalaman defended, good intelligence, soldiers who come home alive, and orders that are followed.
**They hate:** cowardice, wasting lives on glory, disobeying orders, and nobles playing at generals (Lord Bakaris above all).
**Other factions** (tendencies, for rival costs nobody planned):

| Faction | Tends to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| [[Knights of Solamnia]] | Like | Fellow soldiers, and the best horse in the city. Vendri has no patience for their pride. |
| [[Mages of High Sorcery]] | Neutral | Useful, but Wyhan keeps secrets and wants every prisoner first. |
| [[Kalaman Guilds]] | Dislike | They hold the council's purse and haggle over every soldier's bread. |
| [[Kalaman Smallfolk]] | Neutral | Their sons fill the ranks, but the army comes before the farms. |
| [[The Firefishers]] | Dislike | Criminals Vendri would hang if she could. |

**The war room.** The party is summoned by default. While **Hated**, Vendri stops calling them, and the war is decided without them ([[3.00 Storm Clouds#How a Briefing Works]]).

Kalaman's soldiers always fight their hardest. Standing decides how far they trust the people beside them ([[Skirmish Battles#Battalions|Group Morale]]). Everyone gets soldier's issue: spear, shield, chain shirt, cloak.

**When it's checked:** the downtime column at the start of each downtime, the battle column at the mission check-in before each skirmish where Kalaman's army fights ([[6.02 Return to Kalaman#The Siege Ledger]]).

## Favours (Liked or better)

One per downtime ([[Faction Rules#Favours]]):
- **Rally:** a Kalaman battalion acts as if above half HP (Group Morale) for the rest of the battle.
- **Cover:** soldiers pull a fallen hero clear: stabilised and moved 30 feet to safety, however the fight is going.
- **Detail:** six guards for one mission.
- **Requisition:** fast horses, or a river boat and crew.
- **Pull a string:** a prisoner released into the party's custody.

**The Vogler file** is never burned on Standing. It goes only if they help save Kalaman ([[DM Guide#Why They Stay Together]]).

**Vendri's protection** over Wurm, as a member of her company, never lifts on Standing alone. Only Wurm's own acts end it ([[Wurm (DM)]]). Below Liked, the custody is all she gives: at the trial she states it on the record and makes no special effort for any of them ([[4.02 The Trial - Testimony#Defence Witnesses]]). Saving them from the noose after Vogler was effort enough.

**Command:** Military Standing decides how many soldiers the party can lead in skirmishes and battles, from none at Hated to the whole field at Admired ([[Skirmish Battles#Who Commands What]]).

**Mission perk (Liked+):** horses and two veteran warriors come along (four at Admired), or a fresh report gives advantage on the first check to scout or ambush the enemy.
**Mission complication (Hated):** no horses, and nobody comes looking if you're late.

**For the Wastes:** at Liked or better, the quartermaster quietly loads **10 Supply** and **10 Water** into the party's own packs, so they aren't on the supply ship when it goes down.

**Signature reward (first time Admired):** forty of Vendri's veterans ask to serve under the company's banner, and she lets them. They follow the party into the siege and into anything after it.
**Signature disaster (first time Hated):** the party is stripped of its esquire rank, arrested for desertion or treason, and has to talk, fight or break its way out of Castle Kalaman.
