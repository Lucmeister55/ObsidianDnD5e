# The Firefishers

See [[Faction Rules]].
**Horizon: short.** They pay out now, in cash, and they collect later.

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]]) for the gang, Vinhollow and Hetch Warehouse. As a faction, it's **Homebrew**.

Kalaman's dockside underworld: smugglers, loan sharks, card rooms and protection, run out of Hetch Warehouse and the Cinque Teste in the cisterns below it. They're violent and well organised, and in a city going hungry, they're often the only people who can get you what you need. Everyone knows they exist. Nobody admits to dealing with them. Most of the city does.

**Who speaks for them:** **Marta Dace** runs the street, and she's the one the party deals with. Councillor **Oflyn Vinhollow** is the local boss, in secret ([[The Guild Council]]). Above him, there are only stories. After Soth's massacre, **[[Peppino Fettuccini|Mister Fettuccini]]** comes to run things himself.

**They want:** money, quiet, their cut of everything, and debts paid.
**They hate:** informers above all, the watch, anyone who breaks a deal, and being laughed at.
**Rivals:** the [[Kalaman Military]], who'd hang them if they could, and the [[Kalaman Guilds]], who use them and deny it. The [[Kalaman Smallfolk]] pay their fees, and resent it.

| **Wary** (-1, -2) | Prices double, and you're followed. | Nothing. | The sabotage runs as written, and they loot what they can. |
| **Hostile** (-3 to -5) | You're a problem. | Someone goes through your rooms. | They sell a gate's watch rota to whoever's buying. |

**Mission perk (Trusted+):** a smuggler's route that avoids the checkpoints, or a forged pass that gets the party through one gate or patrol without a check.
**Mission complication (Hostile):** someone told the enemy which road you're taking, and got paid for it.

**Signature reward (+10):** you're family. Once, they do something impossible and don't ask why: make someone vanish, empty a guarded warehouse, or get a person out of Castle Kalaman.
**Signature disaster (-5): the Firefishers come calling.** It happens in three steps, one per session, so the party can see it coming:
1. **The courtesy call.** A polite young man in a good coat visits the Bastion and admires the place. "Shame if anything happened to it, with a war on." He suggests a monthly "insurance" fee.
2. **The message.** If they don't pay, they find a gutted fish nailed to the Bastion door one morning. A hireling stops turning up for work. A friendly merchant suddenly won't serve them.
3. **The hit.** One night, the Firefishers come for the Bastion with knives, clubs and lamp oil, and with someone inside who let them in.

They can buy peace (a lot of gold, or a very large favour), go to war with the Firefishers, or bring proof of Vinhollow's hand to Vendri, which turns a street war into a city scandal.

## Moving Standing

- **Doing business** with them: see [[Peppino Fettuccini#Doing Business]]. Each deal is **+1 Firefishers**, and **-1 Military** if Vendri finds out.
- **A sorcerer PC's favours:** each one is **+1 Firefishers** ([[Davos Bloodbane (DM)#Downtime: The Underground]]).
- **Informing on them:** **-2**, always.

## Favours (Trusted or better)

- Smuggle someone, or something, in or out of the city.
- A forged pass, or a false name on a ship's list.
- Find out who owes money to whom.
- A warning the night before something happens.
