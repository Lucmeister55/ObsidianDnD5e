# Vogler Refugees

**Standing:** `$= dv.current().standing` (`$= (s => s >= 4 ? "Honoured" : s >= 2 ? "Trusted" : s >= 0 ? "Neutral" : s == -1 ? "Wary" : "Hostile")(dv.current().standing)`). See [[Faction Rules]].
**Horizon: mixed.** A little every downtime, and a lot in the siege.

The survivors of Vogler, housed in rough wooden buildings outside Kalaman's Trade Gate. They lost their homes, their festival and many of their people in a single night. They're the **first faction the party meets**: their Standing starts at 0 and moves in Vogler itself, during [[1. An Ordinary Day]] and [[2. Broken Silence]], as a gentle introduction to the system. They're proud, grieving and determined to pull their weight, and they're always one bad week away from despair.

**Who speaks for them:** nobody, at first. Their mayor, [[Raven Uth Vogler|Raven]], died holding the docks against Kansaldi so they could escape. In Kalaman the refugees are leaderless and grieving. Whoever rises to speak for them is shaped by the party (see "A new voice" in [[3. Storm Clouds - Timeline]]): the captain of Vogler's surviving militia, an old ferryman everyone trusts, or **Cudgel Ironsmile** when she returns. Call this leader **the Spokesperson** below.

**They want:** their people fed, housed and safe, to be useful instead of pitied, Vogler taken back one day, and justice for the dead.
**They hate:** being abandoned, being used as cheap labour, being blamed for the war, and Lord Bakaris.
**Rivals:** the [[Kalaman Guilds]] (who see them as cheap labour), and the [[Kalaman Smallfolk]] (when bread runs short).

## By Band

| Band | How they treat you | Each downtime | In the siege |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Honoured** (+4, +5) | Vogler's people are your people. | A free Bastion hireling, and **5 Supply** of dried fish. | Vogler's militia, fighting under Raven's whistle, holds a section of the Trade Gate, and the fishing boats run supplies along the shore under the enemy's nose. |
| **Trusted** (+2, +3) | The Spokesperson asks your advice on everything. | A free Bastion hireling, and **3 Supply** of dried fish. | Vogler's militia holds the Trade Gate beside the soldiers. |
| **Neutral** (0, +1) | Grateful, warm, a little guarded. | A free Bastion hireling. | The refugees shelter their own and keep the gate clear. |
| **Wary** (-1) | They feel used, and say so. | Nothing. | Nothing. |
| **Hostile** (-2, -3) | The Spokesperson publicly blames you for Raven's death. | Nothing. | The refugees take their boats and flee, and the Trade Gate goes undefended. |

**Mission perk (Trusted+):** a Vogler guide who knows the land, or a fishing boat to the mission, saving a day's travel. On missions near Vogler, also advantage on Survival.
**Mission complication (Hostile):** no guides, and the refugees warn others not to help you.

**Signature reward (+5):** when the time comes to retake Vogler, every survivor fights. The village's whole militia and its fishing fleet become one unit under the party's command.
**Signature disaster (-3):** the refugees leave Kalaman for good, and tell everyone downriver what the party did to them.

## Favours (Trusted or better)

- Vogler's fishermen and their boats, for anything on the river.
- Guides who know the land around Vogler and the Vingaard.
- Extra hands for any labour.
- A dozen militia volunteers for one mission.
