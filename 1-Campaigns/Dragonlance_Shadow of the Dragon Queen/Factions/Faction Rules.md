# Faction Rules

*Source: **Homebrew**. The factions themselves come from the **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Power in Kalaman|ch. 4, "Power in Kalaman"]]).*

Kalaman is a city under strain, and six groups in it all want something from the party: the [[Kalaman Military]], the [[Knights of Solamnia]], the [[Mages of High Sorcery]], the [[Kalaman Guilds]], the [[Kalaman Smallfolk]] and the [[Vogler Refugees]]. They rarely want the same thing. Helping one often costs you with another, and that's the point.

The system runs during [[3. Storm Clouds]], with every downtime event mapped out in [[3. Storm Clouds - Timeline]]. It's where the party stocks up for the two ordeals still ahead: the survival trek through the **Northern Wastes**, and the **Siege of Kalaman**.

## The Only Thing You Track

**One number per faction: Standing, from -3 to +5.** It's shared by the whole party and stored in the `standing` property of each faction's note. That's it.

**Every faction starts at 0.** The one exception is the [[Vogler Refugees]], the first faction the party meets. Their Standing can already move in Vogler, before anyone reaches Kalaman, as a light introduction to the system (see "In Vogler" below).

Standing falls into five **bands**, and every faction uses the same ones:

| Band | Standing | What it feels like |
|---|---|---|
| **Honoured** | +4, +5 | They'd follow you anywhere, and they pay out big. |
| **Trusted** | +2, +3 | Doors open, real rewards, and you can **call in favours**. |
| **Neutral** | 0, +1 | A small, basic reward. Helping the war is in everyone's interest. |
| **Wary** | -1 | Nothing. They watch you. |
| **Hostile** | -2, -3 | They cost you something. |

Each faction's note has one table showing what each band means for that faction.

## Moving Standing

- **±1** for a meaningful choice that faction would notice.
- **±2** for a big one, like saving their people, a public betrayal, or a choice made in front of the whole city.
- Many choices are **+1 to one faction and -1 to its rival**.

Change each faction **at most once per event**, and always tell the players what changed and why. Seeing the cost is half the fun.

The changes listed in the timeline and chapters are **defaults**, not a complete list. When the party does something nobody planned for, ask two questions: which faction would notice, and would it be small (±1) or big (±2)? Then decide whether a rival would mind.

## The Big Moments

Two one-off moments make the ends of the track matter:
- **The first time a faction reaches +5**, it gives the party its **signature reward**, something big and memorable, once (listed in each note).
- **The first time a faction drops to -3**, it triggers its **signature disaster** at once (listed in each note).

## In Vogler

Before the party ever reaches Kalaman, only the **Vogler Refugees** track is in play, and only as the villagers of Vogler. Introduce the system here. Mention it once, show the players the number moving, and let them see that the village remembers. Keep it small, with **at most +3 or -2** in total before Kalaman. The chances are in [[1. An Ordinary Day]] and [[2. Broken Silence]].

## Working a Faction (downtime)

The players don't have to wait for events. Any character can spend **one workweek of their downtime** on their own project to win over a faction, instead of their usual activity. It might be teaching the refugee children to read, guarding a guild warehouse, carrying messages for Vendri, or helping Wyhan with an experiment. They pitch it, you pick a fitting check, and:
- **DC 15:** +1 with that faction.
- **Failed by 5 or more:** it backfires, -1.

Each faction can gain **at most +1 per downtime** from projects like this, however many characters work on it. It's a steady, reliable way to climb, but it costs the downtime they'd otherwise spend on their own activity or Bastion.

## Mission Check-ins

Even the long-term factions should pay off now and then during Storm Clouds, in small ways. **At the start of every major mission or scenario,** glance down the six tracks:
- Every faction at **Trusted or better** gives the party its **mission perk**: one small, concrete help for that mission.
- Every faction at **Hostile** throws in its **mission complication** instead.

Each faction's note lists its perk and complication. It takes ten seconds, and it means Standing is always visibly doing *something*. The big payoffs still come later.

## Favours

At **Trusted or better**, the party can call in **one favour per faction per downtime**. That's anything reasonable within the faction's power, with examples in each note. Asking for something the faction hates costs 1 Standing, even if they agree.

## Dividends

Every faction pays out according to its band, but on a different **horizon**:

| Faction | Horizon | Pays out |
|---|---|---|
| [[Kalaman Guilds]] | **Short** | At the end of every downtime, until the council massacre |
| [[Vogler Refugees]] | **Mixed** | A little every downtime, and in the siege |
| [[Kalaman Smallfolk]] | **Mixed** | A little every downtime, and in the siege |
| [[Mages of High Sorcery]] | **Mixed** | A little every downtime, and when leaving for the Wastes |
| [[Kalaman Military]] | **Long** | In the siege |
| [[Knights of Solamnia]] | **Long** | In the siege |

**The Guilds' clock.** The Guilds pay every downtime, but their leaders die in the council massacre at the end of Storm Clouds. Everything stockpiled by then stays with the party, and the new leaders give **one final dividend** when the party leaves for the Wastes.

## Supply

Provisions for the Wastes come in two kinds, both counted per character per day:
- **Supply** is food. **1 Supply is one ration**, one day of food for one character, and it weighs **2 pounds**.
- **Water** is fresh water. **1 Water** is a gallon, one day's drinking for one character, and it weighs **8 pounds**. It's the hard part in a land flooded with salt.

See [[4. Duty - Survival]] for how they're used.
