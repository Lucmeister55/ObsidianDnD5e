# Kalaman Guilds

**Standing:** `$= dv.current().standing` (`$= (s => s >= 4 ? "Honoured" : s >= 2 ? "Trusted" : s >= 0 ? "Neutral" : s == -1 ? "Wary" : "Hostile")(dv.current().standing)`). See [[Faction Rules]].
**Horizon: short.** Pays every downtime, until the council massacre.

Kalaman runs on trade, and the five great guilds run the trade. Their leaders sit on the city council beside the governor, and between them they control the food supply, the docks, the ships, the walls and the maps. They present a united front when the city is threatened. Behind closed doors, they compete for every advantage, and a war is the biggest opportunity any of them has ever seen.

**Who speaks for them:**
- **Alexandra Nightvane** (Cartographers): knows every road and sells maps to whoever pays.
- **Reynold "Rey" Costwyn** (Fishers): feeds the city, and knows it.
- **Uri Stonegrey** (Masons): keeps the walls standing, at a price.
- **Sigmund Pross** (Shipwrights): owns the ships everyone will need if Kalaman falls.
- **Oflyn Vinhollow** (Dockhands): controls who and what moves through the harbour.

**They want:** profit, stable trade, contracts honoured, and power on the council protected.
**They hate:** broken deals, disrupted trade, theft, price controls, and having their warehouses searched.
**Friendly with:** the old nobility, and above all [[Bakaris Uth Estide|Lord Bakaris]]. The guilds admire his Solamnic name and his money, and they're the only reason he has a voice on the council at all. Humiliating the Bakarises usually costs a little Guilds Standing, and [[Bakaris the Younger]] uses the guilds against the party (see "Running Threads" in [[3. Storm Clouds - Timeline]]).
**Rivals:** the [[Kalaman Military]] (over money and council power), and the [[Vogler Refugees]] (cheap labour and unwelcome competition).

> [!warning] The council massacre
> All five guild leaders die with the governor in the Lord's Arrival. Their successors keep the party's Standing, and pay **one final dividend** when the party leaves for the Wastes.

## By Band

| Band | How they treat you | Each downtime |
|---|---|---|
| **Honoured** (+4, +5) | Guild leaders ask your opinion on council business. | **20 Supply**, **20 Water** and **two** expedition items. 25% off everything in Kalaman. |
| **Trusted** (+2, +3) | Guild halls and warehouses are open to you. | **12 Supply**, **12 Water** and **one** expedition item. 10% off everything. |
| **Neutral** (0, +1) | Fair prices and a polite word. | **5 Supply** and **5 Water** "for the war effort". |
| **Wary** (-1) | "Out of stock." | Nothing. Prices are up 25%. |
| **Hostile** (-2, -3) | Blacklisted. | Your Bastion's suppliers stop delivering, and when the Wastes begin, **1d4 × 5** of your Supply is spoiled and the same of your Water casks leak. |

**Expedition items:** tents and bedrolls, a mule or pack sled (carries 300 pounds), a gnomish still that turns salt water into 2 Water a day, climbing gear, the Cartographers' best map of the Wastes (advantage on navigation), or ship passage up the coast toward the Wastes.

**Mission perk (Trusted+):** a Cartographers' map of the mission area (advantage on navigation there), or a fast boat or wagon to get there.
**Mission complication (Hostile):** the map they sold you is wrong, and you arrive late or lost.

**Signature reward (+5):** a fully provisioned expedition. **50 Supply**, **50 Water**, three pack mules and a guild guide who has been to the edge of the Wastes and back.
**Signature disaster (-3):** the guilds hire someone to make the party disappear, and a night attack hits their Bastion.

## Favours (Trusted or better)

- A ship and crew, or a wagon train, for one journey.
- Masons to fortify or repair something (your Bastion, a wall, a bridge).
- Maps of anywhere in the region.
- A vote on the council swung the party's way.
