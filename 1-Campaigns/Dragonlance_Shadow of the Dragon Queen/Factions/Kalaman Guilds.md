# Kalaman Guilds

See [[Faction Rules]].
**Horizon: short.** Pays every downtime, until the council massacre.

The five great guilds run Kalaman's trade, and their leaders sit on the council: food, docks, ships, walls and maps. United when the city is threatened, at each other's throats behind closed doors, and the war is their biggest opportunity yet.

**Who speaks for them:** ([[The Guild Council]] has a profile of each.)
- **Alexandra Nightvane** (Cartographers): knows every road and sells maps to whoever pays.
- **Reynold "Rey" Costwyn** (Fishers): feeds the city, and knows it.
- **Uri Stonegrey** (Masons): keeps the walls standing, at a price.
- **Sigmund Pross** (Shipwrights): owns the ships everyone will need if Kalaman falls.
- **Oflyn Vinhollow** (Dockhands): controls who and what moves through the harbour.

**They want:** profit, stable trade, contracts honoured, and power on the council protected.
**They hate:** broken deals, disrupted trade, theft, price controls, and having their warehouses searched.
**Friendly with:** the old nobility, and above all [[Bakaris Uth Estide|Lord Bakaris]]., for his Solamnic name and money: they're his only voice on the council. Humiliating the Bakarises costs a little Guilds Standing, and [[Bakaris the Younger]] turns the guilds on the party ([[3.00 Storm Clouds#Running Threads]]).
**Rivals:** the [[Kalaman Military]] (over money and council power), [[The Firefishers]] (whom they use, and deny knowing), and the [[Kalaman Smallfolk]] (cheap labour).

## Old Money

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.

**The slander,** known to every dockhand: the great houses are "fishmongers who got rich on wreck-plunder".

**The truth:** it's half true, and they've spent three centuries paying to forget it.
- **The wrecks.** Istarian ships in the bay sank in the Cataclysm's storms, and a few fishing families netted up their holds for generations: coin, glass, and now and then something that glowed.
- **The Wash.** The Wash has flushed pieces of fallen Onyari out of the Wastes into the bay for three hundred years. Some of the oldest heirlooms are from the City of Lost Names, and nobody knows it.
- **What they did with it.** Sold the small things, kept the best, married into old Solamnic names. The piety, the breeding and Bakaris's friendship are part of the cover.
- **Who knows.** The oldest families, and [[Wyhan]], who has bought from them for years. [[Lohezet]] will want it once he learns.

**Why it matters:** they're the only people in Kalaman with lasting magic to give (see Heirlooms). The Mages want the vaults emptied into the Towers.

> [!warning] The council massacre
> All five guild leaders die with the governor in the Lord's Arrival. Their successors keep the party's Standing and honour the old leaders' loans, so the heirlooms and expedition gear still come for the Wastes.

**The council.** The party sits in on council meetings by default. At a payday at **Hostile**, they're dropped from the list, and the council votes without them until a payday back at Wary or better ([[3.00 Storm Clouds#How a Briefing Works]]).

Prices apply to anything bought commercially anywhere in Kalaman: the market, the forges, the inns, a ship's passage.

**Patronage.** The guild families like to be seen with the city's heroes. The gold comes from dinners, toasts, a commission for a portrait, a "loan" nobody expects back. It's a good place for a hidden mark ([[Faction Rules#Hidden Consequences]]): every dinner is also someone asking for something.

## Heirlooms

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, from the old wreck-money ([[#Old Money]]). Items from the 2024 *Dungeon Master's Guide*.

The guild families lend Istarian heirlooms out of their vaults, and they're what makes the Wastes survivable: water, carrying weight, and getting there. Each is a loan and a favour owed back. **-1 Mages** if Wyhan learns one was kept from the Order. Never a bag of holding ([[DM Guide#House Rules]]).

| Heirloom | Whose vault | What it does for the party |
|---|---|---|
| [[alchemy-jug-xdmg\|Alchemy jug]] | Shipwrights (Pross) | **8 gallons of fresh water a day**: more than the whole party drinks. |
| [[gauntlets-of-ogre-power-xdmg\|Gauntlets of ogre power]] | Masons (Stonegrey) | Strength 19: carry **95 lb** before Encumbered, **190** before Heavily. |
| [[boots-of-striding-and-springing-xdmg\|Boots of striding and springing]] | Cartographers (Nightvane) | Speed never drops for carried weight, so the pack mule of the party isn't slow. |
| [[cloak-of-the-manta-ray-xdmg\|Cloak of the manta ray]] | Fishers (Costwyn) | Breathe and swim in the Wash, the shrine and the bay. |
| [[rope-of-climbing-xdmg\|Rope of climbing]] | Dockhands (Vinhollow) | Canyon walls, cliffs, and every Wash flood the party doesn't want to be in. |
| [[eyes-of-the-eagle-xdmg\|Eyes of the eagle]] | Cartographers (Nightvane) | Scouting the canyons, and the Dragon Army's patrols, from far off. |

**Rare, at +10** (signature reward), the party picks one:
- [[daerns-instant-fortress-xdmg|Daern's instant fortress]]: a sheltered long rest anywhere in the Wastes.
- [[figurine-of-wondrous-power-ivory-goats-xdmg|Figurine of wondrous power (ivory goats)]]: the goat of travelling carries a rider through the canyons.

**In Storm Clouds,** an heirloom can also be lent for a single mission (mission perk below).

**Expedition items** (mundane, never food; the full kit is all six):
- cold-weather tents, bedrolls and furs (no exhaustion from cold nights)
- a pack mule or sled (300 lb)
- a gnomish still (2 Water a day while the Wash is high)
- climbing gear: rope, pitons, grappling hooks, crampons
- the Cartographers' map of the Wastes (advantage on navigation)
- passage up the coast, cutting the first days of the march

**Mission perk (Trusted+):** one heirloom on loan for the mission, a Cartographers' map of the area, or a fast boat or wagon.
**Mission complication (Hostile):** the map they sold you is wrong, and you arrive late or lost.

**Signature reward (+10):** a fully outfitted expedition: three pack mules, a guide who has been to the edge of the Wastes, and a **rare heirloom**.
**Signature disaster (-5): the ledger closes.** No guild deals with the party: no ship, no merchant, and the Bastion's lease is called in. The council docks the army's budget for "keeping such company", and Vendri has to answer for it. It lifts with a public apology, a huge payment, or the guild leaders' funerals.

## Favours (Trusted or better)

- A ship and crew, or a wagon train, for one journey.
- Masons to fortify or repair something (your Bastion, a wall, a bridge).
- Maps of anywhere in the region.
- A vote on the council swung the party's way.
