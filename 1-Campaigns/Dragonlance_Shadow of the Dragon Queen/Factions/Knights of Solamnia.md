# Knights of Solamnia

See [[Faction Rules]].
**Horizon: long.** Banks everything for the siege, and for Edric.

In Kalaman, a cold chapterhouse with a handful of knights, old banners and a great deal of pride. The city still blames them for the Cataclysm. They live by the Oath and the Measure, remember every slight, and badly need someone to make them look good.

**Who speaks for them:** the knight-commander of the Kalaman chapterhouse, an old, proud Knight of the Rose (name them yourself), [[Becklin Uth Viharin]] if she survived Vogler, and [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge|Edric]] himself.

**They want:** honour restored, the Measure upheld, innocents protected, and the Order's name cleared in Kalaman.
**They hate:** oathbreakers, cowardice, dishonourable tactics, sorcery they don't control, and mockery.
**Other factions** (tendencies, for rival costs nobody planned):

| Faction | Tends to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| [[Kalaman Military]] | Like | Soldiers who follow orders, if a little common. |
| [[Mages of High Sorcery]] | Dislike | Sorcery the Order doesn't control. |
| [[Kalaman Guilds]] | Neutral | Merchants, but they honour the old Solamnic names. |
| [[Kalaman Smallfolk]] | Like | Sworn to protect them, however little they're thanked for it. |
| [[The Firefishers]] | Dislike | Everything the Measure forbids. |

> [!warning] The false knights
> After Caradoc's undead "knights" murder the council, the city blames the Order until [[4.01 The Trial|the Trial]] clears it. Until then, openly siding with the Knights also costs **-1 Smallfolk**.

**Mission perk (Liked+):** a knight's blessing before the mission. Each character gains **Heroic Inspiration**, and Edric rides out on a chapterhouse warhorse.
**Mission complication (Hated):** a knight is sent along "to observe", and reports every dishonourable thing the party does.

## Favours (Liked or better)

One per downtime ([[Faction Rules#Favours]]):
- **Vouch:** a knight speaks for the party before the council, the military, or at the Trial (a defence witness for one charge, whatever the Standing).
- **Records:** a night in the chapterhouse records: old Solamnic history, family lineages, *the Greenshield line*.
- **Escort:** a knight guards someone important for a mission or a downtime.
- **Hearing:** a formal hearing, or a trial by combat, called on the party's behalf.

**Signature reward (first time Admired):** the chapterhouse names Edric a Knight of the Sword or Rose, his choice, on the spot, and gives him the chapterhouse's finest warhorse. The Order also gives the company its **war banner**: once per battle, every ally within 60 feet of it automatically succeeds on saves against fear until the end of the fight, even against [[Lord Soth|Soth]].
**Signature disaster (first time Hated):** the Order declares the party dishonourable, takes back every relic, and calls Edric before a tribunal that could take his spurs.
