# Knights of Solamnia

See [[Faction Rules]].
**Horizon: long.** Banks everything for the siege, and for Edric.

The Knights are headquartered far to the west, and in Kalaman they are a small presence: a cold chapterhouse with a handful of knights, a lot of old banners and a great deal of pride. Many in Kalaman still blame the Order for the Cataclysm, and the Knights know it. They live by the Oath and the Measure, they remember every slight, and they badly need someone to make them look good again.

**Who speaks for them:** the knight-commander of the Kalaman chapterhouse, an old, proud Knight of the Rose (name them yourself), [[Becklin Uth Viharin]] if she survived Vogler, and [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge|Edric]] himself.

**They want:** honour restored, the Measure upheld, innocents protected, and the Order's name cleared in Kalaman.
**They hate:** oathbreakers, cowardice, dishonourable tactics, sorcery they don't control, and mockery.
**Rivals:** the [[Mages of High Sorcery]], and, uneasily, the [[Kalaman Smallfolk]], who don't trust them.

> [!warning] The false knights
> When Caradoc and Soth's undead "Knights of Solamnia" murder the council, the city blames the Order. The case is decided at **the Trial** right after the catacombs ([[4.01 The Trial#The Trial]]). Until it's cleared there, or later, any choice that sides openly with the Knights also costs **-1 Smallfolk**.

## By Band

| Band | How they treat you | In the siege |
|---|---|---|
| **Honoured** (+6 to +10) | Your deeds go into the Order's records. | The chapterhouse knights ride under Edric, and their charge can **break one enemy assault** outright. |
| **Trusted** (+3 to +5) | Training, shelter, fine horses, and the chapterhouse records. | **One mounted charge** at a decisive moment. The chapterhouse also backs Edric's rise through the ranks. |
| **Neutral** (0 to +2) | Polite, and faintly disapproving. | A few knights hold the chapterhouse street. |
| **Wary** (-1, -2) | Edric gets asked about the company he keeps. | Nothing. |
| **Hostile** (-3 to -5) | Turned away from the chapterhouse. | The Knights stay behind their own walls, and Edric faces a formal inquiry. |

**Mission perk (Trusted+):** a knight's blessing and advice before the mission. Each character gains **inspiration**, and Edric rides out on a chapterhouse warhorse.
**Mission complication (Hostile):** a knight is sent along "to observe", and reports every dishonourable thing the party does.

**Signature reward (+10):** the chapterhouse names Edric a Knight of the Sword or Rose, his choice, on the spot, and gives him the chapterhouse's finest warhorse. The Order also gives the company its **war banner**: once per battle, every ally within 60 feet of it automatically succeeds on saves against fear until the end of the fight, even against [[Lord Soth|Soth]].
**Signature disaster (-5):** the Order declares the party dishonourable, and Edric is called before a tribunal that could take his spurs.

## Favours (Trusted or better)

- A knight's word vouching for the party in front of the council or the military.
- A night in the chapterhouse records (old Solamnic history, family lineages, *the Greenshield line*).
- A knight to escort or protect someone important.
- A formal hearing, or a trial by combat, called on the party's behalf.
