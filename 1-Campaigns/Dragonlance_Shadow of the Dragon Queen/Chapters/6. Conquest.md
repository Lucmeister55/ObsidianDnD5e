## Overview

The flying citadel is coming for Kalaman, with the Red Dragon Army beneath it and Soth on its throne. Everything the party built in Storm Clouds pays out here: every faction's Standing turns into soldiers, gates, spells and food. Then the party flies up into the citadel to bring it down from inside, and faces Kansaldi in the ruins. It's the end of the campaign, and the end of most of the origin characters' arcs.

**Sources:**
- [[5-City_of_Lost_Names.pdf]] (the companions stop at the City of Lost Names)
- [[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman|Chapter 7: Siege of Kalaman]]

> [!abstract] Running this chapter
> - **Characters:** "a knight PC", "a Silvanesti PC" and so on name the kind of character a thread suits best, not a person. See [[DM Guide#Roles, Not Names]] for who carries each one right now.
> - **Levels:** the party starts at **10th level** and reaches **11th** on landing at the flying citadel (module).
> - **Factions pay out** in "The Siege Ledger" below. Do the sums before the battle, not during it.
> - **Mass combat:** WoK scenarios 8–12 for the big battles, and [[Skirmish Battles]] for anything you want to zoom in on.
> - **The deadline** from [[4. Duty]] decides how many death dragons are waiting (see [[5. Destiny#The Deadline Lands]]).

## Return to Kalaman

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Return to Kalaman|ch. 7, "Return to Kalaman"]]), with **Homebrew** changes.

Run the grim report to the new council: Governor Fuline Thren, Marshal Vendri, the new guild leaders, and Lord Bakaris blaming everyone. In this campaign:
- **The guilds' new leaders** know the party, and remember how the old ones were treated ([[Kalaman Guilds]]).
- **The Vogler Refugees' Spokesperson** has a seat at the table now, if the Refugees are Trusted or better ([[Vogler Refugees]]).
- **The verdicts.** Whatever the Trial decided ([[4. Duty#The Trial]]) is where the council starts. If the Knights' charge stood, Thren won't let them guard the castle, unless the party brings new evidence from the Wastes to the grim report.
- **If they escaped from the cells** ([[4. Duty#If They Are Arrested]]), they come home as fugitives. The ship docks to guards waiting on the quay, and the grim report starts as a hearing, with the party under arrest and Bakaris delighted. They have to talk their way out: the flying citadel on the horizon, Darrett's word, any evidence from the Wastes. Vendri wants them free, but she needs a reason the council can't argue with. Whoever helped them escape may be in trouble too.

### The Last Downtime

The module gives the party "at least a week" before the Dragon Army arrives. Make it **one last downtime of 1 week**: one workweek each.

- **Working a Faction** works as usual, and it's the last chance to push a track over a band.
- **Personal projects** get one last push: a knight PC's company, a ranger PC's grove, a sorcerer PC's charms, a tinkerer PC's drone, a dragonborn PC's letters.
- **Loose threads** to tie off now:
  - **A dragonborn PC's contract.** If it's still in the Bakarises' hands, it's in Lord Bakaris's strongbox in his lodgings.
  - **Jeyev**, if he's still loose in the city.
  - **Becklin**, if she's alive.
  - **Letters home.**
- **The Fizban question.** If the old man has been visiting a dragonborn PC, he turns up one last time this week, on a bench, telling children the story of Huma (the module's own "Dread in the Streets" table has him there). He tells the dragonborn PC, gently, that he'll be needed soon.

## Who Falls in the Siege

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. It ties into [[Darrett Highwater]], [[Nestra Vendri]] and a knight PC's ranks ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]]).

This is a war, and the people the party loves can die in it. Decide before the siege, based on how it's gone, rather than rolling for it.

**Darrett** may die, and if he does, make it the best scene he has ever had.
- **Where:** holding the Trade Gate (or the Warrior's Gate) while the party flies to the citadel, so they're gone when it happens. They hear it on the fargab, or find out when they get back.
- **When it's likely:** if the Military is below Trusted (he's holding the gate with too few soldiers), if Jeyev is still loose and close to him, or if Becklin is dead and he's trying to be her.
- **When he lives:** if a knight PC's Company or the Knights hold the gate beside him, or if someone stayed behind with him. If Becklin survived, she drags him out.
- **If he falls:** he's found with Becklin's helmet beside him, and the gate held. A knight PC takes up whatever he was carrying.

**Vendri** may die too, on the walls, when a death dragon comes over the Trade Gate.
- **When it's likely:** if the Mages and Military are both low (no ward, no reinforcements), or if the party left the walls for the citadel early.
- **If she falls:** Kalaman needs a new Grand Marshal, and if a knight PC is Honoured with the Military and the city held, it's him.
- **If both fall,** the end of the campaign is a city with no commanders, and a party it has to look to.

> [!tip] Don't overdo it
> At most **one** of them should die in a normal run. Two is for a siege that went badly.

### Flight of the Dragonnels

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Flight of the Dragonnels|ch. 7, "Flight of the Dragonnels"]]). Red Ruin's fate is **Homebrew** ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]], [[Wurm (DM)]] and [[Red Ruin]]).

Clystran's dragonnels carry the party up through the battle to the citadel, where **Red Ruin** and **Bakaris the Younger** come for them. This is the moment a knight PC's and a dragonborn PC's stories have been heading for.

- **Red Ruin** fights to knock riders off their mounts, as the module says. She's the one who killed Ispin, and she's a child the Dragon Army raised into a weapon, the same as a dragonborn PC.
- **The choice.** A knight PC wants her dead. A dragonborn PC wants to save her. If someone shouts that Soth gave the order and she only carried it out, she hesitates for one round. Nobody has ever told her she could stop.
  - **Kill her:** the module's version. A knight PC has his revenge, and it's hollow, because Ispin's voice told him who really did it.
  - **Spare her:** she has nowhere to go. If a dragonborn PC or a knight PC offers her a place, she breaks off and flies away. Whether she ever comes back is a sequel.
- **Bakaris the Younger** takes his helmet off to taunt them, and flees at 20 hit points. If he falls, he vanishes into the battle below. If the party never got a dragonborn PC's contract, it's in his saddlebag.

## The Flying Citadel

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#The Flying Citadel|ch. 7, "The Flying Citadel"]], S1–S25), with **Homebrew** ties.

Run the citadel as written: the tunnels, the crypts of Chemosh's cult, the priests' quarters and the Bastion above. There are three ways to win: unseat Soth, find the mirror of reflected pasts, and put out the Cataclysm's fire. **Tight spaces** apply in the foundations, and **bone and stone** everywhere.

- **Tenadria's Tomb (S9).** Tenadria was Jentida's sister, the ghost in Wakenreth who asked after her ([[4. Duty]], location E). Telling her spirit that Jentida is at peace, if the party closed Wakenreth's portal, is worth a moment.
- **The mirror of reflected pasts.** It paralyses Soth: he whispers "Isolde" and stares. If the old man gave **A dragonborn PC** a small silver mirror "for when you want to see who's really looking", it works exactly the same way. It always was this.
- **Caradoc (S22)**, Soth's cowardly seneschal, sells out his master for his own unlife. He's the one who murdered the council, so the Knights would want him alive.

### The Mourning Sanctum

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#The Flying Citadel|ch. 7, "S23: Mourning Sanctum"]]). A knight PC and the fire are **Homebrew** ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]]).

## Clash of Fallen Flames

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Clash of Fallen Flames|ch. 7, "Clash of Fallen Flames"]]). Kansaldi's demand and a dragonborn PC's answer are **Homebrew** ([[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]] and [[Wurm (DM)]]).

On the battlefield under the falling citadel, Kansaldi comes down on her red dragon, Ignia. She's beside herself with joy that the party lived, because it means the Queen saved them for her.

**Her demand,** from the module, rewritten for her: one of them must kneel and burn, and the others go free. She knows which one she wants. She turns to **A dragonborn PC**, weeping, and calls him "brother". She knows what he is. The Queen told her, last night, in the dark.

**A dragonborn PC's moment.** This is where his arc lands (see [[Wurm (DM)#Arc]]). The voice in him rises to meet her, and offers him everything.
- **If he rejects Takhisis,** helped by everything the old man taught him, the grey in his eyes turns silver. Paladine wakes in him, and his weapon becomes a radiant greatsword. If the old man is anywhere near, this is the moment someone finally learns his name: Fizban.
- **If he gives in,** Kansaldi wins her true believer, and the party has to fight their friend as well as her.

Then Kansaldi and Ignia attack, and fight to the death. As she dies, Kansaldi is laughing: her death is the sacrifice the Queen wanted, and victory is assured. Her eye-gem is a **sentient gem of seeing**, and it whispers to whoever picks it up in Kansaldi's voice.

## Kalaman Victorious

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Kalaman Victorious|ch. 7, "Kalaman Victorious"]]), with **Homebrew** endings.

Run the celebration, the medals and the mysterious letter in blue wax as written. Change the allies' plans for this campaign:
- **Vogler's people** go home under the **Spokesperson**, not Raven ([[Raven Uth Vogler]]). If the Refugees reached +10, the whole militia and fishing fleet sail to retake Vogler, and they ask the party to lead them.
