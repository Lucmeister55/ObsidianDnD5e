## Overview

The flying citadel is coming for Kalaman, with the Red Dragon Army beneath it and Soth on its throne. Everything the party built in Storm Clouds pays out here: every faction's Standing turns into soldiers, gates, spells and food. Then the party flies up into the citadel to bring it down from inside, and faces Kansaldi in the ruins. It's the end of the campaign, and the end of most of the origin characters' arcs.

> - **Levels:** the party starts at **10th level** and reaches **11th** on landing at the flying citadel (module).
> - **Factions pay out** in "The Siege Ledger" below. Do the sums before the battle, not during it.

## Scenario 8: Fighting Retreat

*Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Escaping the Enemy|ch. 6, "Escaping the Enemy"]]) and **WoK** scenario 8.*

The party rejoins Darrett as the Dragon Army regroups beneath its new citadel, and the army runs for the coast. With WoK, play scenario 8 (on a hold, the module's potion reward applies). Without it, or straight after it, zoom in on the rearguard:

> [!example] Skirmish: the canyon mouth
> *Source: **Homebrew** ([[Skirmish Battles]]).*
> - **Objective:** get the army clear of the canyon in **6 rounds**. After that, every allied unit still in the canyon is cut off.
> - **Allies:** 2 **Infantry** and Cudgel's **Mercenaries**, the last of Darrett's army, plus any heroes' own units that came to the Wastes.
> - **Enemies:** 1 **Baaz** per round from the canyon floor, and 1 **Dragonnel Riders** from round 3.
> - **Round 4:** a lesser death dragon flies over, out of the citadel's shadow (as a monster). Terrifying, so every allied unit makes a Morale check.

**Fed or starving** ([[4. Duty#The Starving Camp]]) still applies. A starving army starts every unit Bloodied.

## A Hasty Retreat

*Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#A Hasty Retreat|ch. 7, "A Hasty Retreat"]]). The ships are **Homebrew** ([[4. Duty#The Starving Camp]]).*

Darrett sends the party ahead to warn Kalaman and stays to bring the troops home himself. In this campaign, the ships are where the party left them: the engineers at **Wrecker's Edge** have finished the repairs, and they're waiting. If the camp ran out of food, the engineers are fewer, and the ships are short-crewed but sailing.

- **Dragonnel Down** (module) on the way to the coast. If they save it, it's another friendly wasteland dragonnel for Clystran later.
- **Clystran** guides them, if he's with them, then says goodbye at the coast and goes home to Heart's Hollow. He promises to send help if he finds any.

## Return to Kalaman

*Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Return to Kalaman|ch. 7, "Return to Kalaman"]]), with **Homebrew** changes.*

Run the grim report to the new council: Governor Fuline Thren, Marshal Vendri, the new guild leaders, and Lord Bakaris blaming everyone. In this campaign:
- **The guilds' new leaders** know the party, and remember how the old ones were treated ([[Kalaman Guilds]]).
- **The Vogler Refugees' Spokesperson** has a seat at the table now, if the Refugees are Trusted or better ([[Vogler Refugees]]).
- **The false knights.** If the party never cleared the Order's name after the council massacre, Thren doesn't trust the Knights, and won't let them guard the castle ([[Knights of Solamnia]]).

### The Last Downtime

*Source: **Homebrew** ([[Faction Rules]]).*

The module gives the party "at least a week" before the Dragon Army arrives. Make it **one last downtime of 1 week**: one workweek each.

- **Working a Faction** works as usual, and it's the last chance to push a track over a band.
- **Personal projects** get one last push: Edric's company, Thorn's grove, Davos's charms, Milo's drone, Wurm's letters.
- **Loose threads** to tie off now:
  - **Wurm's contract.** If it's still in the Bakarises' hands, it's in Lord Bakaris's strongbox in his lodgings.
  - **Jeyev**, if he's still loose in the city.
  - **Becklin**, if she's alive.
  - **Letters home.**
- **The Fizban question.** If the old man has been visiting Wurm, he turns up one last time this week, on a bench, telling children the story of Huma (the module's own "Dread in the Streets" table has him there). He tells Wurm, gently, that he'll be needed soon.

## The Siege Ledger

*Source: **Homebrew** ([[Faction Rules]] and each faction's note).*

Before the Day of Dread, go down the six tracks and write down what each faction brings to the siege. These are the "In the siege" entries in each note, and they're what the party gets to command in the skirmishes, and what WoK gets as bonuses.

| Faction | Check its note for | Also |
|---|---|---|
| [[Kalaman Military]] | Units on the walls, Vendri's trust | **+5:** a company of forty veterans under the party. |
| [[Knights of Solamnia]] | Knights at the gates, Edric's command | **+5:** Edric is knighted on the spot, before the battle. |
| [[Vogler Refugees]] | The Trade Gate militia, the fishing boats | **Hostile:** the Trade Gate goes undefended. |
| [[Kalaman Smallfolk]] | Food, arrows and hiding places | **+5:** one extra day of holding, when it matters most. |
| [[Mages of High Sorcery]] | One great working at the decisive moment | Davos's robes decide which Order answers. |
| [[Kalaman Guilds]] | Whatever's left of the stockpile | Barricades and siege engines, through the new leaders. |

**Personal additions:**
- **Edric's Company** (his downtime at progress 8) is an extra allied unit.
- **Thorn's grove** at progress 10 sends a treant for one decisive scene.
- **The Wastes:** Heart's Hollow scouts, Zhelsuel's exiles (if Thorn revealed himself) and Clystran's dragonnels all count.

## Scenario 9: March of the Black Rose

*Source: **WoK** scenario 9. The module doesn't reference it, so where it goes is **Homebrew**: Soth's skeletal knights, and the hollow Ispin ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]]).*

Run it on the road outside Kalaman as the Dragon Army's vanguard arrives, before the Day of Dread. Ahead of the army march Soth's own knights, skeletal riders under the black rose. At their head rides the **hollow Ispin**, wearing Ispin's face, the death knight Soth raised from his stolen body.

- With WoK, play scenario 9. Then zoom in for **one scene**: Edric and the hollow Ispin.
- Without WoK, run it as a skirmish:

> [!example] Skirmish: the black rose on the road
> *Source: **Homebrew** ([[Skirmish Battles]]).*
> - **Objective:** break the vanguard before it reaches the trade camps outside the walls (**6 rounds**), or the camps burn (-1 Smallfolk).
> - **Allies:** 2 **Infantry**, 1 **Knights**, Edric's Company if he has it, and anything from the Siege Ledger.
> - **Enemies:** 2 **Undead Knights**, 1 **Baaz** following behind, and the hollow Ispin as a monster at their head.
> - **Bone and stone** applies, so maces and clubs shine.

- **The hollow Ispin.** Use the full [[death-knight-xmm|death knight]] at this level, or a weakened version if the party is struggling. Ispin's shield is restored now, and its **Through the Flames** makes Edric immune to the death knight's hellfire. When the thing falls, the shield is warm again, and Edric hears Ispin one last time: "There. Now let me go, lad." This is Ispin's real funeral.

```encounter
name: The Hollow Ispin
creatures:
  - "1": Death Knight (XMM)
  - "4": Skeletal Knight (DSotDQ)
```

## Day of Dread

*Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Day of Dread|ch. 7, "Day of Dread"]]), with **Homebrew** faction effects.*

The citadel is sighted, and the city waits. Run the "Dread in the Streets" table, then:
- **Trouble at the Gates.** Captain Narnhelm has shut the Trade Gate on the refugees from the trade camps. Opening it gives **+1 Vogler Refugees** and **+1 Smallfolk**. Leaving it shut gives **+1 Military** and **-1 Refugees**. This is the last Standing change before the siege, and it lands straight in the ledger.
- **Strange Riders.** Clystran and Darrett arrive on the coppery wasteland dragonnel: the one from the mansion, if the party saved it. Plan the counterattack: the tunnels in the citadel's base, and Clystran's dragonnels.

## Scenario 10: Day of a Thousand Fires

*Source: **WoK** scenario 10, which the module doesn't reference. It's placed here as the night bombardment, and the encounters are the **Module**'s "Night of Terror" ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Night of Terror|ch. 7]]).*

The Dragon Army camps outside the walls and spends the night setting the city on fire. With WoK, play scenario 10 for the walls. Between rounds, or instead of it, run the module's night encounters:

- **Kapaks on the walls**, and **sivaks in Kalaman uniforms.** If Jeyev is still loose, he's the one who let them in (see [[Jeyev Veldrews]]).
- **Testing the Defenses.** The ballistae against dragonnels carrying bozaks. **Against fliers** applies, so hand out the javelins.
- **Highmaster's Message.** A lesser death dragon crashes into the street with a note pinned to its ribs. Kansaldi is a fanatic now ([[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]), so replace the module's note with hers:

  > *Children! I can see you! She lets me see you, little lights, all of you, running about on your walls. I prayed for you tonight. I prayed she lets me be the one. Sleep well. Tomorrow I bring the fire, and you will thank me for it. It is the Dragon Queen's will!!*

- **Bakaris Defects.** Lord Bakaris opens the Warrior's Gate to six Dragon Army officers, with six Kalaman knights he talked into it. His son's letter is on him. In this campaign, also:
  - **Wurm's contract** is in his coat if it wasn't taken earlier. If the party takes it, it's Wurm's to burn.
  - The six knights who follow Bakaris **clear the Order's name**, if it still needs clearing. They were never the Order's. The real knights hunt them down. (The false-knights warning in [[Knights of Solamnia]] ends.)

## Scenario 11: Siege of Kalaman

*Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Battle of Kalaman|ch. 7, "Battle of Kalaman"]]) and **WoK** scenario 11.*

The assault on the walls, and the defenders' sortie that gives the party their chance to reach the citadel. With WoK, play scenario 11 using the **Siege Ledger** for bonuses. Without it, run the module's "Defending the Wall" encounters, and zoom in on the gate:

> [!example] Skirmish: the Trade Gate
> *Source: **Homebrew** ([[Skirmish Battles]]).*
> - **Objective:** hold the gate for **5 rounds**, until Clystran's dragonnels arrive to carry the party to the citadel.
> - **Allies:** everything in the **Siege Ledger**, and every hero's own unit. This is the battle all of that was for.
> - **Enemies:** 2 **Dragon Army Soldiers** with a ram, 1 **Kapaks** on the walls, 1 **War Engine** out of reach, 1 **Ogres** on round 2, and 1 **Dragonnel Riders** on round 3.
> - **On the walls** applies: polearms shove climbers off ladders. Units on the walls are **behind cover**.
> - **If the Refugees are Hostile,** their section of the wall is empty, and the enemy starts on top of it.

### Flight of the Dragonnels

*Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Flight of the Dragonnels|ch. 7, "Flight of the Dragonnels"]]). Red Ruin's fate is **Homebrew** ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]], [[Wurm (DM)]] and [[Red Ruin]]).*

Clystran's dragonnels carry the party up through the battle to the citadel, where **Red Ruin** and **Bakaris the Younger** come for them. This is the moment Edric's and Wurm's stories have been heading for.

- **Red Ruin** fights to knock riders off their mounts, as the module says. She's the one who killed Ispin, and she's a child the Dragon Army raised into a weapon, the same as Wurm.
- **The choice.** Edric wants her dead. Wurm wants to save her. If someone shouts that Soth gave the order and she only carried it out, she hesitates for one round. Nobody has ever told her she could stop.
  - **Kill her:** the module's version. Edric has his revenge, and it's hollow, because Ispin's voice told him who really did it.
  - **Spare her:** she has nowhere to go. If Wurm or Edric offers her a place, she breaks off and flies away. Whether she ever comes back is a sequel.
- **Bakaris the Younger** takes his helmet off to taunt them, and flees at 20 hit points. If he falls, he vanishes into the battle below. If the party never got Wurm's contract, it's in his saddlebag.

```encounter
name: Red Ruin
creatures:
  - "1": Red Ruin (DSotDQ)
  - "1": Bakaris the Younger (DSotDQ)
  - "2": Dragon Army Dragonnel (DSotDQ)
```

The characters reach **11th level** when they land on the citadel.

## The Flying Citadel

*Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#The Flying Citadel|ch. 7, "The Flying Citadel"]], S1–S25), with **Homebrew** ties.*

Run the citadel as written: the tunnels, the crypts of Chemosh's cult, the priests' quarters and the Bastion above. There are three ways to win: unseat Soth, find the mirror of reflected pasts, and put out the Cataclysm's fire. **Tight spaces** apply in the foundations, and **bone and stone** everywhere.

- **Tenadria's Tomb (S9).** Tenadria was Jentida's sister, the ghost in Wakenreth who asked after her ([[4. Duty]], location E). Telling her spirit that Jentida is at peace, if the party closed Wakenreth's portal, is worth a moment.
- **The mirror of reflected pasts.** It paralyses Soth: he whispers "Isolde" and stares. If the old man gave **Wurm** a small silver mirror "for when you want to see who's really looking", it works exactly the same way. It always was this.
- **Caradoc (S22)**, Soth's cowardly seneschal, sells out his master for his own unlife. He's the one who murdered the council, so the Knights would want him alive.

### The Mourning Sanctum

*Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#The Flying Citadel|ch. 7, "S23: Mourning Sanctum"]]). Edric and the fire are **Homebrew** ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]]).*

Wersten Kern, Soth's standard-bearer, demands single combat on the scaffold above the brazier of Cataclysm fire. Soth arrives in 3 rounds unless he's paralysed.

**Putting out the fire.** The module says a holy relic of a good god bathed in the flames puts them out: the dragonlance. In this campaign there's a second relic: **Ispin's shield**. It held back the Cataclysm's fire at Dargaard's gate once, and **Through the Flames** means it can't be harmed by it now. If Edric climbs into the brazier with the shield raised, the same way his ancestor stood in the burning gateway, the fire goes out around him, and he walks out unburnt.

**Soth and Edric.** Soth speaks little, but he speaks to "Greenshield's boy". He tells the lie about the first Greenshield, that he was a coward who opened the gates in a panic. If Edric knows the truth by now, this is where he says it to Soth's face. Then the ground opens under Soth, as the module describes, and he falls into the dark. He'll be back, at Dargaard, in some other story.

```encounter
name: Mourning Sanctum
creatures:
  - "1": Wersten Kern (DSotDQ)
```

```encounter
name: Lord Soth (don't fight him straight)
creatures:
  - "1": Lord Soth (DSotDQ)
```

## Clash of Fallen Flames

*Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Clash of Fallen Flames|ch. 7, "Clash of Fallen Flames"]]). Kansaldi's demand and Wurm's answer are **Homebrew** ([[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]] and [[Wurm (DM)]]).*

On the battlefield under the falling citadel, Kansaldi comes down on her red dragon, Ignia. She's beside herself with joy that the party lived, because it means the Queen saved them for her.

**Her demand,** from the module, rewritten for her: one of them must kneel and burn, and the others go free. She knows which one she wants. She turns to **Wurm**, weeping, and calls him "brother". She knows what he is. The Queen told her, last night, in the dark.

**Wurm's moment.** This is where his arc lands (see [[Wurm (DM)#Arc]]). The voice in him rises to meet her, and offers him everything.
- **If he rejects Takhisis,** helped by everything the old man taught him, the grey in his eyes turns silver. Paladine wakes in him, and his weapon becomes a radiant greatsword. If the old man is anywhere near, this is the moment someone finally learns his name: Fizban.
- **If he gives in,** Kansaldi wins her true believer, and the party has to fight their friend as well as her.

Then Kansaldi and Ignia attack, and fight to the death. As she dies, Kansaldi is laughing: her death is the sacrifice the Queen wanted, and victory is assured. Her eye-gem is a **sentient gem of seeing**, and it whispers to whoever picks it up in Kansaldi's voice.

```encounter
name: Clash of Fallen Flames
creatures:
  - "1": Kansaldi Fire-Eyes (DSotDQ)
  - "1": Young Red Dragon (XMM)
```

## Kalaman Victorious

*Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Kalaman Victorious|ch. 7, "Kalaman Victorious"]]), with **Homebrew** endings.*

Run the celebration, the medals and the mysterious letter in blue wax as written. Change the allies' plans for this campaign:
- **Vogler's people** go home under the **Spokesperson**, not Raven ([[Raven Uth Vogler]]). If the Refugees reached +5, the whole militia and fishing fleet sail to retake Vogler, and they ask the party to lead them. Raven's whistle starts the first Kingfisher Festival after the war, blown by whoever kept it.
- **Darrett** goes to Maelgoth to become a true knight, as the module says. If Becklin lived, she goes with him.
- **Cudgel** buries Jeyev's name, whatever happened to him.

**The origin characters' endings.** Give each one a closing scene drawn from their DM notes:
- **Edric:** knighted, or not, and the shield's names. Does he carve his own?
- **Davos:** his robes, his family upriver, and the Night of the Eye.
- **Milo:** the ring, unmade or not, and who paid for it. The chronicle's first volume is finished.
- **Thorn:** the grove, Zhelsuel, and whether Alhana hears that a Caladon prince is alive.
- **Wurm:** his own name, if he chose one, and whose god he is now.
