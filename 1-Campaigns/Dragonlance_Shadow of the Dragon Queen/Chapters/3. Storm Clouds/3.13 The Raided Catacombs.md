## Briefing 12, The Grim Room

*After Steel Springs, before the Lord's Arrival.*

*Darrett, filthy, exhausted, in command of what's left. Lord Bakaris sits in the corner and doesn't speak.* (Companion, "Battle Conclusion".)

**The summons:** No summons. Everyone who can still walk ends up in the first room with a door, and Darrett sits down on the floor.

**The numbers** (expected): Bakaris marched out with **1,400**. About **800** came back. Kalaman has about **1,600** regulars left, against about **2,800**.

**The news:**
- Half the army is dead or missing. Bakaris the Younger is "believed dead". (The party may know better.)
- Kansaldi was there, on a red dragon. The soldiers can't stop talking about it.
- There are **five** Dragon Armies, if the party read the Wheelwatch letters. (Companion.)
- **Your doing:** the units and allies they saved at the Inkwater are alive. Name them.

**Darrett, quietly:** "She had us. That dragon could have burned the whole column. She sent fire straight up into the sky and let us go. Why would she do that?" *(DM: because she wants the survivors inside the walls. See [[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes#Why She Lets Them Go]].)*

**The question: Lord Bakaris.** The army wants him hanged.
- **Hand him to the soldiers' justice:** **+1 Military, +1 Smallfolk, -2 Guilds**. Ugly.
- **Protect him**, as Darrett and Becklin want: **+1 Knights, -1 Military**. He'll remember who saved him, and it won't help (he still opens the gate in the siege).
- **Send him to the council to be judged:** **+1 Guilds, -1 Smallfolk**. They let him off.

## The Lord's Arrival and the Raided Catacombs

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#The Lord's Arrival|ch. 4, "The Lord's Arrival", "Raided Catacombs"]]) and **Companion** ([[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]], Part 6), with **Homebrew** changes. The characters reach level 6 after the catacombs.

- **Something's wrong.** Tatina is arguing with the guards outside: they won't let her back in. **DC 12 Charisma:** a unit of Knights of Solamnia arrived this morning, and the council sealed the castle to speak to them.
- **The knights at the door** are **wights** in Solamnic armour (passive Perception 15 at 10 feet: a soft violet glow under the visors). The false knights ([[Knights of Solamnia]]).
- **Leedara** appears beside a servant killed by one sword-thrust and urges the party on. **DC 20 Charisma** to bring her along.
- **The council massacre.** The council is dead, and **Caradoc** waits (in Durstan's body, if that thread ran), with a sneer for each character's hypocrisy. If Leedara is there his sarcasm cracks: "Your master still has much to answer for. The same can be said for you." He jumps to a new host whenever his current one drops below half HP, preferring melee fighters with weak Charisma saves.
- **The catacombs.** A tomb for fallen knights, built so the living remember that no oath makes a knight incapable of evil. The spirit of **Knight Jandin** (R6) fled once and left her comrades to die, then spent her life helping others redeem themselves. She warns the party that the last room is beyond them.
- **Soth is there** (R7). He holds the rusted, flame-wrapped dragonlance and doesn't bother to fight: attacks barely touch him. He drives the lance into a corpse, which rises as a **skeletal knight**, and the fire spreads to the coffins: in **10 turns** the whole tomb walks on Kalaman. The ritual ends when the lance is pulled out (**DC 25 Strength** while the knight stands, an action once it's down). Then Soth leaves by the circle (below).
  - *Homebrew:* the **hollow Ispin** stands at his side and holds the room as rearguard. **Sarlamir's** story stays: the lance is his, and Karavarix remembers him in [[6.00 Conquest]].
- **Leedara's goodbye.** She gives the name everyone forgot: **Soth**. She is bound to him and feels him go north to the Wastes, where he has been for months. The fire is for something worse. They should take the lance. If asked, she admits she was one of Isolde's attendants, whose lie drove Soth to murder, cursed to haunt him.

- **Terrain:** coffins around the walls (each a future skeletal knight: mark the 10-turn countdown), violet fire, a teleportation circle at the foot of the south wall.

**The hollow Ispin (homebrew).** Ispin's body with none of his humour or kindness, helm on. Use the **death knight aspirant** ([[death-knight-aspirant-xmm|XMM]]) at **half hit points** (it's tough for a 5th-level party). He goes for whoever carries the shield: it goes **cold**, then **burns hot**, and a knight PC hears Ispin for the first time: "That isn't me, lad." If a blow knocks his helm back, the face is Ispin's. **He dies here, for good**: this is Ispin's real funeral, and the kill belongs to the party (ideally a knight PC). When he falls the shield is warm again and the knight PC hears: "There. Now let me go, lad."

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): the skeletal knight's ***enervating blade*** (attunement, necrotic greatsword with charges) and broken plate. Wights: a **heart** each.

> [!check] Full fight. **Objective:** pull the lance out of the risen knight before 10 turns pass, or the catacombs empty into Kalaman. Killing the knight makes it easy.

**Soth's escape (homebrew).** The module's ride out of the city and the companion's step off a ledge both leave a trail, and the framing needs none.
- **Arrival.** The wights come up from the harbour at noon and Holt lets them in. **Soth walks in with them under a hood and plain grey cloak**, a very tall seventh figure who never speaks. Witnesses remember a hooded knight at the back, nothing more.
- **The circle.** [[Lohezet]] is the army's teleporter (he teleported away from Ispin's murder, [[1.00 An Ordinary Day]]). A *teleportation circle* in his camp in the Wastes links to a circle inlaid in R7's floor, **laid by Caradoc while wearing Durstan Rial** (weeks to walk the catacombs unnoticed). So Soth arrived in a sealed tomb unseen. Once the ritual is under way he steps onto the circle with the scepter, leaving Ispin as rearguard, and it burns out when the last of them goes. The wall is **not broken**: no tunnel, hole or cliff (the module's "recent tunnel" is just a collapse inside the catacombs).
- **Stopping it.** **DC 16 Arcana** spots the circle. Scuffing its silver inlay before Soth reaches it strands him until he walks out through the castle, where he can be seen. Lohezet can be found in the Wastes.

**Homebrew beats:**

- **The deadline.** As he takes the fire, Soth lets the date slip out of contempt ([[4.00 Duty]]).
- **The Guilds' clock.** The massacre kills the guild leaders. The [[Kalaman Guilds]] pay one final dividend before the Wastes ([[Faction Rules#Dividends]]).

### Held, Not Hanged

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. How the party is framed, and why they're held instead of killed.

The Watch finds the party standing among the dead (the governor and five guild leaders), **nobody else alive**. To a frightened captain it adds up:
- **Only survivors.** Nobody saw anyone else leave.
- **Sealed from inside.** The council barred the doors, so no forced entry. The esquires can open any door in the castle.
- **The killers left nothing.** The wights crumbled to ash, the skeletal knight fell with the lance, Soth left by circle unseen, and Caradoc left his host. The party's story (a nameless dread knight, a ghost, a hollow dead man) sounds like madness. Leedara and Jandin can't testify, and Soth is a nursery tale.
- **Blood and armour.** They're covered in it and carry Solamnic pieces and a flame-wrapped lance. A knight PC is a Knight's squire, and the council had just been told the Order wanted Kalaman back.
- **Bakaris's file** (desertion, theft, conspiracy) is already on Vendri's desk and in every tavern ([[3.02 Downtime 1]]).
- **Witnesses.** Sergeant Holt let six "knights" in at noon, with a tall hooded one at the back. Mistress Oddny, behind a tapestry, heard boots right behind them.

**Why they aren't hanged.** The captain wants the cells and a rope, but the acting head of the council, **Fuline Thren**, wants an open inquiry to calm a rioting city. **Vendri** steps in: they're her sworn esquires, so the case is hers. She confines them to the **castle ballroom** under Darrett's soldiers and gives the city its **inquiry in two days**. If it goes badly, the cells wait ([[4.03 The Trial - Verdicts#If They Are Arrested]]).

**In the two days:** collect evidence, talk to Vendri, Darrett and Wyhan, read the room. Hidden marks from Storm Clouds start to show ([[Faction Rules#Hidden Consequences]]).
