## Briefing 12, The Grim Room

*After Steel Springs, before the Lord's Arrival.*

*Darrett, filthy, exhausted, in command of what's left. Lord Bakaris sits in the corner and doesn't speak.* (Companion, "Battle Conclusion".)

**The summons:** No summons. Everyone who can still walk ends up in the first room with a door, and Darrett sits down on the floor.

**The numbers** (expected): Bakaris marched out with **1,400**. About **800** came back. Kalaman has about **1,600** regulars left, against about **2,800**.

**The news:**
- Half the army is dead or missing. Bakaris the Younger is "believed dead". (The party may know better.)
- Kansaldi was there, on a red dragon. The soldiers can't stop talking about it.
- There are **five** Dragon Armies, if the party read the Wheelwatch letters. (Companion.)
- **Your doing:** the units and allies they saved at the Inkwater are alive. Name them.

**Darrett, quietly:** "She had us. That dragon could have burned the whole column. She sent fire straight up into the sky and let us go. Why would she do that?" *(DM: because she wants the survivors inside the walls. See [[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes#Why She Lets Them Go]].)*

**The question: Lord Bakaris.** The army wants him hanged.
- **Hand him to the soldiers' justice:** **+1 Military, +1 Smallfolk, -2 Guilds**. Ugly.
- **Protect him**, as Darrett and Becklin want: **+1 Knights, -1 Military**. He'll remember who saved him, and it won't help (he still opens the gate in the siege).
- **Send him to the council to be judged:** **+1 Guilds, -1 Smallfolk**. They let him off.

## The Lord's Arrival and the Raided Catacombs

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#The Lord's Arrival|ch. 4, "The Lord's Arrival"]] and "Raided Catacombs"). The bullets below are **Homebrew**.

The council massacre and the catacombs. The characters reach level 6 after the catacombs.

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]], Part 6, "Raided Catacombs"), with **Homebrew** changes marked.

- **Something's wrong.** Outside the castle, Tatina is arguing with the guards: she went out for supplies and they won't let her back in. DC 12 Charisma: a unit of Knights of Solamnia arrived this morning, and the council has sealed the castle until it can speak to them.
- **The knights at the door** are **wights** in Solamnic armour (passive Perception 15 at 10 feet: a soft violet glow under their visors). These are the false knights ([[Knights of Solamnia]]).
- **Leedara** appears beside a servant's body on the stairs, killed by a single sword-thrust, and urges the party on. DC 20 Charisma to bring her along.
- **The council massacre.** The council is dead, and **Caradoc** is waiting, still in Durstan's body if that thread ran. He has a sneer ready for each character's hypocrisy. If Leedara is there, his sarcasm cracks: "Your master still has much to answer for. The same can be said for you." In the fight, he jumps to a new host whenever his current one drops below half hit points, and prefers melee fighters with weak Charisma saves.
- **The catacombs.** A tomb for fallen knights, built so the living would remember that no oath makes a knight incapable of evil. The Cataclysm fire found its way here because of what the dead had done. The spirit of **Knight Jandin** (R6, "Shrine of the Redeemed") is a knight who once fled and left her comrades to die, then spent her life helping others find redemption. She warns the party what's in the last room, and that it's beyond them.
- **Soth is there.** When the party opens R7, **Soth** is finishing. He holds the rusted, flame-wrapped dragonlance. Attacks barely touch him, and he doesn't bother to fight. He drives the lance into a corpse, which rises as a **skeletal knight**, and the fire spreads to the coffins: in **10 turns**, the whole tomb rises and walks on Kalaman. The ritual ends when the lance is pulled out (DC 25 Strength while the knight stands, an action once it's down). Then Soth walks to the south wall, steps onto a glowing teleportation circle set into the floor, and is gone without looking back (homebrew: see "Soth's Escape" below). The wall stays whole.
  - *Homebrew:* the **hollow Ispin** stands at his side ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]]). When Soth goes to the circle, Ispin stays as his **rearguard**, and the party fights him (below). Soth's name and the deadline come out here too.
  - *Homebrew:* **Sarlamir's** story stays: the lance is his, and Karavarix remembers him in [[6.00 Conquest]].
- **Leedara's goodbye.** She tells them the knight's name (in this campaign, the name everyone forgot: **Soth**), that she's bound to him and can feel he's gone north into the Wastes, where he's been for months, and that the fire is for something worse. They should take the lance. If asked, she admits what she is: one of Isolde's elven attendants, whose lie drove Soth to murder, cursed to haunt him.

**The hollow Ispin (homebrew).** When Soth steps onto the circle, the hollow Ispin turns to face the party, helm on, and holds the room while the ritual runs. He is Ispin's body with none of his humour or kindness, and he fights as his master's rearguard.
- **Stat block.** The **death knight aspirant** ([[death-knight-aspirant-xmm|XMM]]). It is a tough fight for a 5th-level party, so use **half its hit points**, or drop it a tier.
- **Who he fights.** He goes for whoever carries the shield. If a knight PC has it, the shield goes **cold** when he enters, then **burns hot**, and the knight PC hears Ispin's voice for the first time: "That isn't me, lad."
- **He dies here, for good.** This is Ispin's real funeral, and the kill belongs to the party (ideally a knight PC). When he falls, the shield is warm again and the knight PC hears Ispin one last time: "There. Now let me go, lad." The circle burns out when the last of them has gone.
- **The helm.** If a character takes a blow that knocks it back, the face under it is Ispin's.

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): The skeletal knight carries an ***enervating blade*** (attunement, necrotic greatsword with charges) and broken plate. Wights: a **heart** each.

> [!check] Full fight. **Objective:** pull the lance out of the risen knight before 10 turns pass, or the catacombs empty into Kalaman. Killing the knight makes it easy.

**Soth's Escape (homebrew).** The module has him ride out of the city and the companion has him step off a ledge, and both leave a trail. The framing needs none, so he is never seen outside the tomb:
- **The false knights arrive as written** (the wights come up from the harbour at noon, and Holt lets them in). **Soth walks in with them under a hood and a plain grey cloak**, a very tall seventh figure who never speaks, because otherwise he stands out. Witnesses (Holt, the gate guards, the crowd) remember a hooded knight at the back, and nothing more. Nobody sees his face, his armour or his eyes.
- **Why he can vanish: Lohezet's teleportation circle.** [[Lohezet]], the black-robed mage with the Dragon Army in the Wastes, is the army's teleporter (he *teleported away* from the murder of Ispin, [[1.00 An Ordinary Day]]). Soth has been in the Wastes for months. A *teleportation circle* in Lohezet's camp links to a circle inlaid in the floor of the tomb's last room, which **Caradoc laid while he wore Durstan Rial in Kalaman** (he had weeks to walk the catacombs unnoticed). That is how Soth arrived inside a sealed tomb unseen, and how he leaves. Nothing about it needs fire.
- **How he leaves.** Once the ritual is under way, Soth walks to the circle at the foot of the south wall, the sigils light, and he steps through with the scepter in his hand, leaving the hollow Ispin as rearguard. The circle burns out behind him. The wall is **not broken**: there is no tunnel, hole or cliff. The module's tunnel "created only a short while ago" is a collapse inside the catacombs, nothing more.
- **Where it leads.** To Lohezet's circle in the Dragon Army's camp in the Northern Wastes, which is where Leedara feels him go. Nobody in Kalaman can follow, or knows to.
- **What the party can do.** A character who studies the floor (DC 16 Arcana) sees the circle. Scuffing or breaking its silver inlay before Soth reaches it (a secondary goal that suits the companion's reworked fight) strands him until he walks out through the castle, where he can be seen. If they miss it, Soth steps through, and the circle burns out at once, leaving a scorched ring. Lohezet is also a thread to pull in the Wastes: whoever opens the gate can be found.
- **Nothing is left behind.** When the last of them goes, the wights crumble to empty armour and violet ash, the skeletal knight falls when the lance is pulled, and Caradoc has already left the host he wore. No body or rider is ever seen leaving Kalaman, which is what makes the framing in "Held, Not Hanged" hold.
- **Leedara** feels him go north to the Wastes, where he has been for months, and says so.

The homebrew beats for this scene:
- **The name.** Soth's name is rediscovered here (see [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]] and [[Lord Soth]]).
- **The hollow Ispin.** See [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]].
- **The deadline.** When Soth takes the Cataclysm's fire, he lets the date slip, out of contempt for the people he's talking to. See "The Deadline" in [[4.00 Duty]].
- **The Guilds' clock.** The council massacre kills the guild leaders. The [[Kalaman Guilds]] stop paying per downtime and pay one final dividend before the Wastes (see [[Faction Rules#Dividends]]).

### Held, Not Hanged

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. This is how the party is framed for the massacre, and why they are held instead of killed on the spot.

**What the Watch finds.** The party climbs out into the council chamber and the gate sergeant's men, who were fetched by the noise, find them standing among the dead: the governor and five guild leaders, killed by sword-thrusts, and **nobody else alive**. To a frightened Watch captain, it adds up:
- **Only survivors.** Nobody else is in the room, and nobody saw anyone else leave.
- **The castle was sealed from inside.** The council barred the doors to speak with the "knights" in private, so there was no forced entry. Whoever killed them was inside, or was let in. The esquires have the authority to open any door in the castle.
- **The killers left nothing.** Soth left by teleportation circle and was never seen, and the wights crumbled to empty armour and violet ash when he left, and the party's story (a nameless dread knight, a ghost, a hollow dead man, a spirit who warned them) sounds like madness. Leedara and Jandin are dead and can't testify, Soth is a nursery tale, and Caradoc left the host he wore.
- **Blood and armour.** They are covered in it, and they carry Solamnic pieces and a flame-wrapped lance. A knight PC is a Knight of Solamnia's squire, and the council had just been told the Order wanted Kalaman back.
- **Bakaris's file.** The charges of desertion, theft and conspiracy against Vogler are already on Vendri's desk, and he has been repeating them in every tavern ([[3.02 Downtime 1]]).
- **The witnesses.** Sergeant Holt let six "knights" in at noon, with a tall hooded one at the back whose face he never saw. Mistress Oddny, hiding behind a tapestry, heard boots right behind them.

**Why they are not hanged.** The Watch captain wants the cells and a rope, and the new acting head of the council, **Fuline Thren**, wants an open inquiry to calm a city on the edge of rioting. **Vendri** steps in: the party are her esquires, sworn under military law, so their case is hers until the council says otherwise. She gets them **confined to the castle ballroom** under guard from Darrett's soldiers, not the cells, and gives the city its **inquiry in two days**. If it goes badly there, the cells are waiting ([[4.03 The Trial - Verdicts#If They Are Arrested]]).

**What they can do in the two days.** Collect their evidence, talk to Vendri, Darrett and Wyhan, and read the room. This is also where hidden marks from Storm Clouds start to show ([[Faction Rules#Hidden Consequences]]).
