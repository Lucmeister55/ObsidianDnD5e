## Briefing 9, War Room, Near Home

*Before Mission 4.*

*Vendri, gently. This one's close to the refugees.*

**The summons:** The refugees' Spokesperson comes to fetch them, and Vendri is already waiting at a cookhouse table in the trade camps, out of uniform. This time the war room comes to them.

**The numbers** (expected): **30 scouts** have gone missing in a month. Kalaman: about 2,260. The enemy: about 3,100.

**The news:**
- Two scouts watching Vogler from six miles east have gone silent for a week. (Module, companion.)
- Smoke over Vogler. They're cutting the old bridge's timber for something.
- **Your doing:** the refugees' Spokesperson asks to send a guide with the party, someone who knows the woods. Take them, and it's a mission perk (Refugees).

**The question:** none, unless the Spokesperson wants revenge more than scouts. Then: let refugee volunteers come along? **+1 Smallfolk**, but every one of them who dies costs **-1 Smallfolk** later.

## Mission 4: Missing Scouts

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Missing Scouts|ch. 4, "Missing Scouts"]]), with a **Companion** encounter. A ranger PC's golden stag is **Homebrew** ([[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]]).

### Encounter: Missing Scouts

- **Map:** [[1878331-Rocky_Wilderness_40x40_sq_-_res_4096x4096.jpg|Rocky wilderness]] (companion map, probably)
- **Terrain:** a stand of grey birches (6-8 trees), the scout tied to the central tree, a lookout platform in the branches, and dense undergrowth as difficult terrain.

```encounter
name: Missing Scouts
creatures:
  - "1": Blue Dragon Wyrmling (XMM)
  - "1": Black Dragon Wyrmling (XMM)
  - "1": Green Dragon Wyrmling (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Each wyrmling (Survival DC 12, 1 hour): **1d2 claws** (dagger, 150 gp, 9 days), **1 fang** (shortsword, 150 gp, 9 days), **1d4 teeth** (arrow tips, +1d6 of its breath type), **1d4 scales**, **1 hide**, and **1d6 rations**. Three colours, three damage types.

> [!check] Full fight. **Objective:** keep the captured scout alive. He's tied to a tree in the middle of the clearing, and the wyrmlings use him as bait. If he lives, Kalaman gets his report (module), and the party gets the 100 gp each. If he dies, the report dies with him.

### Rewards

The characters level up to level 5. This is also a ranger PC's golden stag moment (see [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]]).

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** (Part 3, "Mission 4: Missing Scouts").

The scouts were posted six miles east of Vogler. One lies unconscious in the clearing as **bait**. After the fight, the trap springs: a whole draconian force, with an **aurak** at its head, comes out of the trees. The party can't fight it. **Run**, straight into Troops Return below.

## Mission 4: Missing Scouts (Factions)

The surviving scout brings bad news: the Dragon Army is isolating Kalaman from the west.
- **Report to Vendri quietly:** **+1 Military**, plus the module's 100 gp each.
- **Tell the city.** Stand up in the market and warn people what's coming, so they can prepare: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Military**. Vendri wanted it kept secret to avoid panic.
- **Sell the scout's maps** of the enemy's routes to Alexandra Nightvane, who's very interested: **+1 Guilds, -1 Military**. It's a terrible idea, as anyone who's played Downtime 4 knows.

## Troops Return

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Troops Return|ch. 4, "Troops Return"]]).

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ("A Friend's Return").

Running from the Missing Scouts ambush, the party charges straight into ten armoured strangers: **Cudgel** and the last of the Ironclad Regiment. The sudden reinforcements make the draconians pull back. Cudgel admits they were lucky, because her people are dead on their feet. She asks the party to escort them to Kalaman. If Becklin survived the Battle of Vogler ([[2.00 Broken Silence]]), don't bring her back here: she turns up at Wheelwatch instead.
