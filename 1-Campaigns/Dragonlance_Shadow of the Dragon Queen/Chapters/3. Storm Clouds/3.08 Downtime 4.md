## Briefing 8, Council, The Second Army

*Before Downtime 4.*

*The full council, if the party brings them the Maelgoth scouts' scroll. A war room with Vendri alone, if they don't.*

**The summons:** If they have the scroll, the summons is theirs: they demand the council, and the governor's steward tries to make them wait three hours. If not, a sealed note from Vendri: "War room. Now. Tell no one."

**The numbers** (expected; check the [[Campaign Tracker]]): The scroll counts the Blue Army in the **tens of thousands**. Kalaman's whole army, about 2,160, would fit in one corner of its camp. The Red force on Kalaman's doorstep is about 2,900.

**The news:**
- The scroll says there's a **second Dragon Army**, the Blue, already at war with Maelgoth. (Companion.)
- Silvanesti fell last year. Kendermore is under the Black Army. Nobody in Kalaman had put it together until now: it isn't a raid, it's a war across all Ansalon. (Homebrew, from [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]] and [[Milo Geartickle (DM)]].)
- Lord Bakaris and councillor **Sigmund Pross** want an all-out attack, "to end this before Maelgoth's war comes here". (Companion.)
- **Your doing:** whether Vendri's messenger got through the road they cleared.

**The question: tell the city?** The scroll is in the party's hands.
- **Read it in the market:** **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds** (the guilds wanted calm for trade). Kalaman starts to take the war seriously.
- **Council only:** **+1 Guilds, -1 Military**. The council shrugs it off, as the companion has it, and the city stays calm.
- **Vendri only:** **+1 Military, -1 Guilds**, when the council learns it was kept from them.
- **Wyhan first:** let the Mages scry the Blue Army before anyone else hears of it. **+1 Mages, -1 Military**. Vendri learns it second-hand.

## Downtime 4 (4 weeks, the longest)

High summer. The city is crowded, frightened and hot. This is the downtime for big personal projects. Pick two or three:

- **The spy accusation.** A soldier who lost his brother at the farmstead loudly accuses a dragonborn PC of being a Dragon Army spy, in the middle of the Steady Beacon. A crowd gathers.
  - Defend him with words (DC 15 Persuasion): **+1 Refugees**, and the soldier backs down.
  - Defend him with fists: **-1 Military, -1 Smallfolk**.
  - Stay quiet: **-1 Refugees**, and a dragonborn PC notices who stayed quiet.
  - **Later, in the alley.** If the party stayed quiet or lost the argument, and Vendri's protection is gone (Military Wary or worse) or the docks have turned (Smallfolk Wary or worse), the soldier and his friends corner the dragonborn PC alone on an errand. He won't fight back. He never has. The party hears it, or finds him after.
    - Get there in time and talk them down: **+1 Refugees**, and he remembers it.
    - Get there in time and beat them bloody: **-1 Military** or **-1 Smallfolk** (whoever the attackers were), but nobody tries it again.
    - Don't get there in time: the spirit takes him halfway through the beating, and someone dies. **-2 Military, -2 Smallfolk**, and the spy rumours become a manhunt.
- **Fever in the trade camps.** A summer fever spreads through the crowded camps outside the walls. The healers can't keep up. *Why them:* One of them is in the camps when the first children fall sick, and the healers are already overwhelmed. Nobody else is coming.
  - Spend a week nursing the sick (Medicine, or a knight PC's healer's training): **+1 Refugees**, and the healers learn the party's names.
  - Convince the guilds to pay for physicians: **+1 Smallfolk**, but **-1 Guilds** if they're pushed too hard.
- **Nightvane's maps.** Military scouts catch a Cartographers' Guild runner carrying maps of Kalaman's defences out of the city. Alexandra Nightvane claims the maps were "for a client". *Why them:* The scouts bring the runner to the esquires instead of the council, because Nightvane sits on the council.
  - Turn her over to Vendri: **+2 Military, -2 Guilds**.
  - Let Nightvane explain privately: she was selling to both sides, and she'll owe the party forever. **+1 Guilds, -1 Military** (the scouts know who let her go), and a powerful secret.
- **The friendly face.** Run this one in every game. Someone the party has come to know, like the recruits' sergeant, a barmaid at the Steady Beacon, a guild clerk or one of the Vogler refugees, has been dead for a week. A **sivak draconian** killed them and took their shape, and it has been close to the party ever since. Tonight it strikes, with two more sivaks, at the party's Bastion or lodgings while they sleep.
  - Afterwards, **expose it publicly** and burn the bodies in the market so the city understands the threat: **+1 Military**, but **-1 Smallfolk** as panic spreads ("anyone could be one").
  - **Keep it quiet**, and tell only Vendri: no Standing change, and no panic. The party now knows the Dragon Army can be anyone, and they're the only ones who know.
  - Whoever the sivak was impersonating, their faction loses its next dividend in mourning.
- **The Bakaris contract.** If the party is working on freeing a dragonborn PC, this is the downtime to do it (see "The contract" in [[Wurm (DM)]]). Bribing, stealing or politicking can move Guilds, Military or Knights. Freeing him also kills the theft charge in the Vogler file, and if the file is left with nothing true in it, Vendri burns it.
- **Split orders.** Vendri hands the company an order nobody in it will like: the army needs a warehouse by the docks for powder and pitch, and the one it wants is full of refugee families. Clear it by nightfall. It's legal, it's necessary, and the company has to decide together who does it and how. Let the players argue in character first.
  - Do it as ordered: **+1 Military, -1 Refugees**.
  - Find the families somewhere else first, and be late doing it (DC 15 Persuasion with the Spokesperson): **+1 Refugees, -1 Military**, and Vendri notes the delay.
  - Refuse, as a company: **+1 Refugees, -2 Military**, and Vendri reminds them, in private, what's in the file.
  - Let one member refuse while the others obey: no Standing change, but play out what it does to the company.
