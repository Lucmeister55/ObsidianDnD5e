## Briefing 3, Council, Prices

*Before Downtime 2.*

*The full council. The bazaar is packed and prices are up by half (companion). The smallfolk are angry, and the guilds are rich.*

**The summons:** A guild herald in Cartographers' green reads the summons aloud in the castle courtyard, five seals on one ribbon, getting every name slightly wrong. Attendance is "requested, and expected".

**The numbers** (expected): Two thousand soldiers cost the city nearly **3,000 gp a week** in pay and bread, and the guilds want to know what for. The enemy is about 2,700, and the guilds don't believe it.

**The news:**
- Tatina has been appointed chief of tech development, and refuses to build weapons. (Companion.)
- Councillor **Uri Stonegrey** has been seen "welcoming" refugees at the trade camps. (Companion.)
- Two more villages to the north have been emptied. The roads west are thick with carts.
- **Your doing:** where Tatina ended up (the castle, the docks or the party's Bastion), and who's grumbling about it.

**The question: price controls on grain and fish?**
- **Cap the prices:** **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds**.
- **Let the market be**, and the guilds promise "voluntary charity": **+1 Guilds, -1 Smallfolk**.
- **Buy grain for the city** out of the treasury (it's what Vendri wants, for the siege): **+1 Military, -1 Smallfolk**, as prices rise for everyone else, and the treasury will be short later.

## Downtime 2 (3 weeks)

> [!info] New systems: the Bastion and Working a Faction
> The party has just been given the Bastion. Both are new choices on the same downtime list ([[Faction Rules#Working a Faction (downtime)]], [[Playtest Ideas#Introduction Schedule]]).

The party has a Bastion now, and the war feels real. Pick two or three:

- **The masons' offer.** Uri Stonegrey's Masons' Guild wants to shore up the walls using refugee labour at a fraction of the usual wage, and asks the party to "encourage" the refugees' Spokesperson. *Why them:* The Masons know the refugees will do whatever the company tells them.
  - Agree: **+1 Guilds, -1 Smallfolk**.
  - Refuse: **-1 Guilds**.
  - Negotiate a fair wage (DC 15 Persuasion): **+1 Smallfolk**, and Guilds unchanged.
- **The recruits.** A dozen Kalaman recruits have never held a spear. Their sergeant asks for help. *Why them:* Darrett is stretched thin, and esquires outrank sergeants.
  - Spend a week drilling them: **+1 Military**. This is free progress for a knight PC's "Raising a Company".
- **A knight comes calling.** The knight-commander of the Kalaman chapterhouse invites a knight PC to dinner and wants to know about the "rabble" he travels with: a sorcerer, a kender and, later, "that draconian".
  - Defend his companions honestly: **+1 Knights** (they respect honesty, if not the company).
  - Distance himself from them: **+1 Knights, -1 Smallfolk** (word gets around that he disowned them), and a quiet word in the party about loyalty.
  - Tell the knight where to go: **-1 Knights, +1 Smallfolk** if it gets around (it will).
- **The looted book.** A refugee boy is selling a spellbook he found in the ruins of Vogler at the City Market. Wyhan wants it. So does a Cartographers' Guild agent, for Alexandra Nightvane's collection. *Why them:* The boy won't sell to anyone until the people who got him out of Vogler say it's all right, so both buyers come to the company.
  - Give it to Wyhan: **+1 Mages, -1 Guilds**.
  - Let the guild buy it: **+1 Guilds, -1 Mages**.
  - Make sure the boy's family is paid either way, out of the party's own purse (20 gp): **+1 Smallfolk**.
- **The missing kender.** Meulara of Meulara's Oddities comes to the party in a state. A young kender called **Pip Thistlewhistle** went to "look at a boat" at the docks three days ago and hasn't come back. Every kender in town is out looking for him, which is making everything worse. He's in the Firefishers' warehouse: he "borrowed" something of theirs, and they've kept him until someone pays. What he took was a cigar, from a box the Firefishers keep for a man nobody has ever seen. It's the first the party hears of [[Peppino Fettuccini#The Man Upstairs|the man upstairs]]. *Why them:* The watch laughed at her. She comes to the company because they're the only people in town who've been kind to a kender lately (or because one of them is one).
  - Ask nicely at Hetch Warehouse and pay for what he took (30 gp): **+1 Smallfolk, +1 Firefishers**, and the Firefishers know the party's faces.
  - Get him out quietly: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Firefishers**.
  - Tell the watch: Pip is home in a day and the Firefishers lose the warehouse for a week. **+1 Military, -2 Firefishers**, and councillor Vinhollow remembers who did it.
- **The thing in the cisterns.** Dogs have been going missing in the lower city, then a rat-catcher. The smallfolk say something lives in the old Istarian cisterns under the streets. They're right: a **giant crocodile**, grown fat in the dark. It's called Bruno, and the Firefishers have been feeding it for years, on someone's old orders, because nobody dared stop. If the party took the Cistern House as their Bastion, it's under their floor. *Why them:* They're in the lower city when the rat-catcher's wife comes screaming up out of a drain, and nobody else is running towards it.
  - Hunt it down: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Firefishers**, and a good hide for the forge ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]). It's a one-turn, or a short fight in the dark if they want one.
  - Seal the sluices and starve it out (a week of work, with the Masons' Guild's help): **+1 Guilds, -1 Military**, for the soldiers lent to dig drains instead of walls. The lower city goes on losing dogs for a while.
  - Let the Firefishers run it as sport, with betting: **+1 Firefishers, -1 Smallfolk**, and someone gets eaten anyway.
  - **Map:** a blank board as a cistern: walkways along black water, pillars, a broken sluice gate. **Terrain:** water (blue felt), pillars, and a few floating crates.
