## Briefing 4, War Room, The Marshal's Gambit

*Before Scenario 2.*

*Vendri, Darrett and the map. Tokens everywhere.*

**The summons:** The castle bell rings three times, the call for officers. Every soldier in the yard turns to look at the esquires, and someone says "That's you, then."

**The numbers** (expected; check the [[Campaign Tracker]]): Vendri wants **600** in the field against a column of about **800**: more than a quarter of her 2,200. The enemy as a whole is about 2,900.

**The news:**
- A Dragon Army column is moving south along the Vingaard. Vendri means to hit it before it reaches the farmlands.
- Dragonnels have been seen over the Estwilde border. (Module.)

**Vendri, staring at the map:** "They outnumber us. They have dragons. Our council is arguing about the price of bread. If I were her, I'd be at the gates by now. So why isn't she?"

**The question: where do the party ride?** With the main attack, or with the small force guarding the farms behind it?
- **With the attack:** **+1 Military**.
- **With the farms:** **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Military** (Vendri wanted every blade in the attack). If the attack goes badly, the farms are still safe.
- **Ask Wyhan to scry the column first**, and wait a day for her answer: **+1 Mages, -1 Military** (Vendri loses the element of surprise).

## Scenario 2: Marshal's Gambit (Factions)

- **Win by the plan:** **+1 Military**.
- **Break from the plan** to cover a column of fleeing farmers in the army's path, and the battle is harder or lost: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Military**. Vendri respects it privately, but she can't say so.

**The numbers** (expected; check the [[Campaign Tracker]]): Kalaman: about 2,120. The enemy: about 2,780, fewer if the Gambit went well.

### Encounter: Retaking the Farmstead

- **Map:** [[1878331-Farmstead_-_Shadows_30x30_sq_-_Res_4096x4096.jpg|Farmstead (shadows)]] (companion map)
- **Terrain:** farmhouse, barn and fences. For the opened barrow, add a grass mound with a stone door and a black rose on it.

```encounter
name: Retaking the Farmstead
creatures:
  - "1": Wight (XMM)
  - "4": Ghoul (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): The wight's **heart** (Religion DC 13): a component for necrotic spells. The rest is gear.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Yes, unless you run it as the opened barrow ([[Soth's Host]]). Then it's a full fight with an **objective:** roll the barrow stone back into place (DC 15 Athletics, 2 rounds of work) before more dead climb out. Each round it stays open, add a Zombie.

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** (Part 3, "Mission 2: Ambushing the Enemy"). It ties straight into [[Soth's Host]] (homebrew).

- **The setup.** The farm, eighteen miles east near the Estwilde border, wasn't attacked by the Dragon Army. Its family describes **undead soldiers** in ruined plate, out of the dark. A visiting knight, **Durstan Rial**, stayed behind so they could run.
- **The undead** wear ruined plate (AC 14) and burn with violet **Cataclysm fire**: Soth's work, and a foreshadowing of the catacombs. DC 11 group Stealth to approach (with advantage: they're facing the barn).
- **Durstan** turns up mid-fight, at 20 hit points, fighting hard. He's **possessed by Caradoc**, Soth's seneschal, and wants to be "rescued" and recruited into Kalaman's ranks. Contested Insight against Caradoc's Deception spots that something's off. He offers to be locked up if they're suspicious. *Dispel evil and good* forces Caradoc out.
- **Reward:** 25 gp each from Vendri, and a *driftglobe* from the farm's owner, the hill dwarf Quince Eshfell.
- **Afterwards:** Darrett greets "Durstan" as an old friend, and Caradoc fakes the memory. Councillor **Reynold Costwyn**, drunk in a tavern, toasts Vogler and sneers at the gods. Soldiers start saluting the party.

## Mission 2: Ambushing the Enemy (Factions)

The farmstead is held by the undead (a wight and ghouls). The farm's family is among the dead.
- **Report back with proof:** **+1 Military**.
- **Bring the wight's remains, and whatever raised it, to Wyhan:** **+1 Mages, -1 Military**. The military never sees the proof.
- **Lay the family to rest properly** and burn the farm clean, and bring their surviving kin the news yourselves: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Mages** (Wyhan wanted the remains, and burning is a waste).

Each of these is a different use of the same evening, so pick one.
