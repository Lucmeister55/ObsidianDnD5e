## Briefing 11, War Room, Wheelwatch

*Before Mission 5.*

*Darrett and the commander of the Wheelwatch force (Cudgel or Jeyev), while Vendri is away west, after her "raiders" ([[3.10 Downtime 5]]).* (Companion.)

**The summons:** A rider comes through the Trade Gate at a gallop with an arrow in his shoulder. Within the hour, Darrett's runner finds the party: "Wheelwatch."

**The numbers** (expected): Wheelwatch held **120**. About **300** of the enemy hold it now. With Vendri away, Darrett can spare **10** soldiers and whoever the party brings. Kalaman: about 2,340. The enemy: about 3,300.

**The news:**
- **Wheelwatch Outpost** has fallen, twenty-four miles southeast. (Module.)
- The Marshal has left the city with most of the garrison. Lord Bakaris is still in it.

**The question:** none. It's the orders: open the gate, free the prisoners, take the letters.

## Mission 5: Wheelwatch Outpost

> [!info] Forces
> **Engaged:** the party, **10** Kalaman soldiers under Cudgel or Jeyev, against about **300** in the fort. Kalaman lost its **120** defenders when it fell. Retaking it frees about **40** prisoners and costs the enemy the garrison.

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Wheelwatch Outpost|ch. 4, "Wheelwatch Outpost"]]). Becklin's return is **Homebrew** (see [[Becklin Uth Viharin]]).

Run the module's infiltration of Wheelwatch (alarm horns, impostors and the dragonnel rider), twenty-four miles southeast of the city.

> [!info] New system: Infiltrations
> Run the break-in as an [[Infiltrations|infiltration]]: one-sentence plan, one engagement roll, Stress and flashbacks, and the Alarm clock as the horns. The setup for Wheelwatch is in [[Infiltrations#Example: Wheelwatch]]. When the gate opens, it becomes the module's battle.

- **Map:** [[map-4.02-wheelhouse-outpost-player.png|Map 4.2: Wheelwatch Outpost]] (module).
- **Terrain:** the palisade with 4 corner towers, the gate and its control, 2 alarm horns (mark them), the stable and the prison.

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]], Part 4, "Wheelwatch Outpost"), with **Homebrew** ties.

- **The survivor.** *(Homebrew: in the module, Raven briefs them, and Raven is dead.)* The rider who came through the Trade Gate with an arrow in his shoulder is the garrison's one survivor, a young outrider called **Hal Brennick**. From his bed he draws the fort for them (map 4.2) and tells them about the horns.
- **The mission.** Vendri is still away west, bringing in the Maelgoth column ([[3.10 Downtime 5]]). Darrett brings the orders and a commander for the ten Kalaman soldiers coming along: **Cudgel**, or [[Jeyev Veldrews|Jeyev]] if Cudgel isn't available. The party's orders: open a gate for the soldiers, free any prisoners, and take any letters. Darrett hands out Dragon Army scale mail and Takhisis shields for anyone who wants to bluff their way in.
- **The garrison** is far larger than the party. Make most of it **Vanse's Khurish mercenaries** (the Dragon Army Soldier stat block, as desert raiders) and a few hobgoblins, with only the officers and the dragonnel rider in red and black ([[Dragon Armies#Faces of the Army]]). Point out the **alarm horns** early, so someone thinks to guard or break them.
- **The commanders.** **Ardlic Vanse**, a former Khurish bandit whom Kansaldi frightened into service, knows an aurak leads the forces around Kalaman, that the main army is somewhere in the Northern Wastes, and that Kansaldi hasn't been seen since Vogler. **Stella Rosscali**, a Silvanesti dragonnel rider who despises Red Ruin, knows Red Ruin leads about fifty riders, and that the Blue Army's war on Maelgoth is going to plan. Stella flies in with a report an hour into the party's watch.
- **The prisoners** (W6):
  - **Lanal Brint**, a drunk who locked himself in a cell and played prisoner. A coward who knows the fort well (DC 15 to get his help).
  - **Elgo Duckditcher**, a kender "famous adventurer" who demanded to explore the fort the day after it fell. She wants her hoopak back. *Homebrew:* **Elgo is a kender PC's Elgo** ([[Milo Geartickle (DM)]]). Whatever the kender PC promised her, this is where it comes up.
  - **Becklin**, if she survived Vogler: the prisoners say a man in Kalaman armour took "Bek-something" away this morning. He's a **sivak** (W3), questioning her so he can steal her shape. She answers every question with her oath, *Est Sularus oth Mithas*. Save her, and she insists on fighting, and wants her armour back (W4).
- **Opening the gate** takes a full minute (a key from an officer, the W5 trunks or the stables, or DC 14 thieves' tools). Then the fort comes for them, a group at a time, over ten rounds: the courtyard guards, then the tower archers and the alarm, then the Kalaman soldiers charging, then the barracks, the baaz, the sivak, and finally the officer on her dragonnel. On round 10, the gate is open.
- **The letters** in the commander's reports reveal there are **five Dragon Armies**, not one, and a plan to lure Kalaman's army into an ambush at **Steel Springs** with Red Ruin's riders.

## Mission 5: Wheelwatch Outpost (Factions)

The outpost holds prisoners taken from the farms and villages around Kalaman.
- **Retake the outpost and hold it for the army:** **+1 Military**.
- **Abandon the outpost to get every prisoner home safely:** **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Military**, because the army wanted Wheelwatch held.
