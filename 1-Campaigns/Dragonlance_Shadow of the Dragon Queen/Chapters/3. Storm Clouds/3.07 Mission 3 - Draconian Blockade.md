## Briefing 7, War Room, The Road to Maelgoth

*Before Mission 3.*

*Vendri, with the door shut, and Darrett guarding it.*

**The summons:** No summons at all. Darrett finds them, says nothing, and leads them down a servants' stair to a small room with one candle. He locks the door behind them.

**The numbers** (expected): Kalaman: about 2,220. The enemy: about 2,980. Maelgoth is 150 miles west, and could spare a few hundred knights, if it weren't fighting its own war.

**The news:**
- Vendri means to send to **Maelgoth** for help, without asking the council. (Companion.) Every road west is blockaded except one.
- She's risking her command, and she knows it.

**Vendri, with the door shut:** "She isn't coming for us. She's boxing us in. Cutting roads, burning farms, taking forts. A siege is expensive, so whatever she's waiting for, she thinks it'll make this cheap. I'd give a great deal to know what it is."

**The question: do the party back her?**
- **Back her, and keep it quiet:** **+1 Military**, and **-1 Guilds** when the council finds out, which it will.
- **Tell the council:** **+1 Guilds, -2 Military**. Vendri never quite trusts them again.

## Mission 3: Draconian Blockade

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Draconian Blockade|ch. 4, "Draconian Blockade"]]), with a **Companion** encounter.

> [!info] New idea: Supply

### Encounter: Draconian Blockade

- **Map:** [[1878331-Road_Ambush_Grid(140ppi-40x30).jpg|Road ambush]] (companion map, probably)
- **Terrain:** the road with 2 toppled wagons (the stolen food), trees on both sides, the ogre's ballista, and the Maelgoth scouts at one edge.

```encounter
name: Draconian Blockade
creatures:
  - "1": Ogre (XMM)
  - "4": Baaz Draconian (DSotDQ)
  - "1": Kapak Draconian (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): The ogre's **giant's toes** (1d10, 4 gp each). The baaz's petrifying gas, the kapak's acid. The stolen food is **60 Supply**.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Yes. **Side objective:** the stolen food (60 rations). Draconians fire the wagons when they start losing, so ask in the one-turn who's saving the food. If someone is, it's the party's first stash of Supply for the Wastes.

The draconians' stolen food (60 days of rations in the module) is the party's first real **Supply** for the Wastes, if they keep it instead of returning it (see the Blockade choice in the timeline).

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** (Part 3, "Mission 3: Draconian Blockade").

- **The point of it.** Vendri wants a road to **Maelgoth** clear, so a messenger can ask for help without the council's say-so. The blockade is about twenty miles southwest.
- **The fight.** Near the site, the party hears battle: **scouts from Maelgoth** losing to draconians led by an **ogre with a ballista**. Save the scouts and they hand over a scroll for Kalaman's governor.
- **The scroll** reveals a **second Dragon Army**, the Blue, already at war with Maelgoth. Vendri is shaken (DC 18 Insight to see it). The council shrugs it off.
- **Afterwards:** Lord Bakaris and councillor **Sigmund Pross** are seen pushing soldiers for an all-out attack (the road to Steel Springs). "Durstan" spends more and more time with Darrett, and asks the party, oh so innocently, why they're really fighting.

## Mission 3: Draconian Blockade (Factions)

The draconians stole two wagons of food and a *driftglobe* from a guild caravan.
- **Return it all to the guild:** **+1 Guilds, -1 Smallfolk**, who watched the food go back into a warehouse.
- **Hand it to the army's quartermaster** for the war effort: **+1 Military, -1 Guilds**.
- **Drive it straight to the refugee cookhouse:** **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds**.
- **Sell it on the quiet to the Firefishers:** 100 gp, **+1 Firefishers, -1 Smallfolk**.

If they take a draconian alive:
- **Hand it to the military for interrogation:** **+1 Military, -1 Mages** (Wyhan asked for it first).
- **Give it to Wyhan for study:** **+1 Mages, -1 Military**. Vendri hears about it.

## Scenario 3: Engines of War (Factions)

- **Burn the siege engines:** **+1 Military, -1 Guilds** (the Masons wanted one to copy).
- **Capture one intact** and haul it home for Uri Stonegrey's Masons to copy: **+1 Guilds**. The military calls it a reckless risk, so **-1 Military** if anyone was lost doing it.
