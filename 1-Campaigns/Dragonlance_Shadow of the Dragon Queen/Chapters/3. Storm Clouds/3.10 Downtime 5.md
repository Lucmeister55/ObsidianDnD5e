## Briefing 10, Council, The Great Victory

*Before Downtime 5.*

*The full council. Cudgel's back, half her company dead. Lord Bakaris has the floor.*

This is the timeline's **"Bakaris's war council"**. Run it here.

**The summons:** Trumpets. Lord Bakaris's heralds bring an invitation on gilt paper to "a council of victory", and there's wine. Vendri's chair is empty.

**Where Vendri is.** *(Homebrew.)* Maelgoth has answered the letter she sent in secret ([[3.07 Mission 3 - Draconian Blockade]]). It can't spare much while it fights the Blue Army, but a small relief column, a few dozen knights and wagons of arrows, is coming down the western road, and it's being hunted. She can't send anyone else, because the council doesn't know she asked, and ordering out a detachment would mean explaining why. So she rode west herself three days ago, with her own guard, telling the castle she was dealing with "raiders on the western road". Only Darrett knows the truth. It's the one secret only she can keep, and her absence is the gap Bakaris has been waiting for.

**The numbers** (expected): Cudgel brought back **10**. Bakaris wants **1,400**, everything that can march, for one blow at Steel Springs, where the scouts count only **900**. Kansaldi will have **2,500** there by the time he arrives. Kalaman: about 2,240. The enemy: about 3,100.

**The news:**
- The Ironclad Regiment came back from the wilds with ten men. (Module.)
- **Your doing:** with Vendri away, a page slips the company a note in Wyhan's hand, after what they saw leading the trap near Vogler ([[3.09 Mission 4 - Missing Scouts]]): "*Gold. Code name: aurak. That is the last of them. Do not fight one.*" Nobody in the council chamber is talking about it. *(Homebrew.)*
- A Dragon Army force has split off from the main army, small and exposed. Bakaris calls it the chance of the war. Vendri, away west on her "raiders", can't argue. (Module, "Battle at Steel Springs".)
- **Why everyone believes it.** *(Homebrew.)* It isn't only Bakaris's word:
  - **The outriders.** Vendri's own outriders, not Bakaris's men, watched the camp at Steel Springs for three nights. They counted tents, cookfires and horse lines, the way Kalaman has always counted Estwilde raiders, and got about 900.
  - **The deserter.** A Khurish mercenary walked into the Trade Gate wanting to sell what he knows. His story matches the outriders' count, he gives names, and he believes every word of it.
  - **The map.** Steel Springs is the only good water for that camp, 35 miles out and a long way from the main army near Vogler. It really does look exposed.

  Anyone who wants to check can read the outriders' reports and question the deserter, and both hold up. Let the players try. The only clues: the deserter "escaped" a little too easily (DC 16 Insight), and a ranger PC notices that 900 men should have left more of a mess at the springs (DC 15 Survival, from the reports).
- **Your doing:** if the party has questioned the deserter or read the outriders' reports closely, they may already suspect a trap (see "Why everyone believes it"). Nobody else does.

**The question: the attack.** It goes ahead whatever they say (it's Steel Springs), but how they argue decides who remembers it:
- **Argue against it** (DC 15 Persuasion): **+1 Military, -1 Guilds**.
- **Stay silent:** no change, and later Vendri asks them why.
- **Back Bakaris:** **+1 Guilds, -2 Military**, and Steel Springs is on them too.

## Downtime 5 (1 week)

**Bakaris's war council** always happens. Pick two others from the rest. Not every week in Kalaman is about Bakaris.

Short and tense. Everyone knows something is coming. Pick one or two:

- **Bakaris's war council.** Lord Bakaris is lobbying the council to ride out and win a "great victory" while Vendri is away west, and the guild leaders love the idea. Darrett is worried. *Why them:* With Vendri away, the esquires are the only voice of hers left in the council chamber.
  - Argue against it in council (DC 15 Persuasion): **+1 Military, -1 Guilds**. It isn't enough to stop it, which sets up Steel Springs.
  - Stay out of it: no change, but Darrett is hurt.
- **The Younger's move.** If the party is at Neutral or worse with the Guilds, Bakaris the Younger makes his big play now. He lodges a formal complaint with the council that the party has been "harbouring a draconian" (a dragonborn PC), and demands the dragonborn PC be returned to his family's custody and chained. The guilds back him.
  - Fight it in council with proof and witnesses: **+1 Smallfolk**. Whether the guilds lose face (-1) or the party does depends on how it goes.
  - Buy him off: it costs whatever he asks, and **+1 Guilds, -1 Smallfolk**. A dragonborn PC notices he was bargained for.
  - If the party is Trusted or better with the Military, Vendri steps in by letter from the road, through Darrett: a dragonborn PC is one of her esquires' company, and she won't release him. The complaint dies, but **-1 Guilds** for the embarrassment.
  - Refuse to hand a dragonborn PC over: the law is on the Younger's side. **-1 Guilds, -1 Military**, but **+1 Smallfolk** for standing by him.
- **The run on the granaries.** Rumours of a siege start a panic-buying rush at the city granaries, and the Fishers' and Dockhands' guilds raise prices again. *Why them:* They're in the crowd at the granary doors when it turns ugly, and the guards on the doors are two frightened boys.
  - Calm the crowd (a knight PC's speeches are made for this): **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Firefishers**, who were doing well out of the panic.
  - Pressure the guilds to open the stores: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds**.
- **Letters home.** A sorcerer PC gets a letter from Relgoth, and his family is frightened. A dragonborn PC wants to write his first letter but doesn't know who to. A knight PC writes to the chapterhouse. Keep it purely roleplay, with no Standing change, just a quiet scene before the storm.
- **Wyhan's crate.** Wyhan needs a crate taken off a Nordmaar trader before the harbourmaster inspects it. She won't say what's in it ("components"). It's very heavy, and something inside it is breathing. *Why them:* An esquire's badge gets you onto any ship in the harbour without a search.
  - Do it, no questions asked: **+1 Mages**, and **-1 Guilds** if the harbourmaster's men see.
  - Open it first: a young basilisk in a cage, for study. A spellcaster PC knows the type, from the Barb. **-1 Mages** (Wyhan hates prying), but now they know what she keeps in her back room.
  - Report it to the harbourmaster: **+1 Guilds, -2 Mages**.
- **The beacon goes dark.** On a foggy night, the northern harbour beacon goes out for the first time in living memory, and a grain ship runs onto the harbour wall. By morning the city says it's an omen, the guilds say it's sabotage, and the beacon-keeper says the lenses are just old. If the party took the Old Beacon House as their Bastion, they're the first to see it go out. *Why them:* They're on the harbour that night when the light goes out, close enough to hear the grain ship hit the wall.
  - Find out the truth: the keeper's right, the lenses are cracked, and Tatina can fix them in a week. **+1 Smallfolk**, and the guilds lose their sabotage story.
  - Salvage the wreck's cargo, timber and pitch for the walls, and get it to the army before the guild claims it: **+1 Military, -1 Guilds**.
  - Let the Firefishers strip the wreck, for a cut (50 gp): **+1 Firefishers, -1 Guilds**.
  - Let the city keep its omen: no change, and the preacher has a new sermon.
- **Cudgel's company.** Cudgel Ironsmile and the remains of her Ironclad Regiment have come back (the module's "Troops Return"). They want pay, and the council won't give it. *Why them:* The company escorted Cudgel home. The council won't hear a mercenary, but it has to hear an esquire, and one of them has a seat at its table.
  - Lobby the council for them: **+1 Military, -1 Guilds**.
  - Find them work with the Firefishers: **+1 Firefishers, -1 Military**, and Cudgel feels like a hired sword again.
