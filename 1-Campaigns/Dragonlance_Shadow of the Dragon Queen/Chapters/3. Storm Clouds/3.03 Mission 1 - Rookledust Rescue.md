## Briefing 2, War Room, Orders

*Before Mission 1.*

*Darrett, alone, on the castle steps at dawn, nervous about his first real command duty.*

**The summons:** Before dawn, Darrett hammers on the door himself, half his armour buckled, holding a folded order he's clearly read a dozen times.

**The numbers** (expected; check the [[Campaign Tracker]]): Darrett has been handed **100 recruits** to make into soldiers. Kalaman: about 2,100. The enemy: about 2,700.

**The news:**
- The boilerdrak that burned Vogler was a **gnome's invention**, and the gnome lives south of the city: **Tatina Rookledust**. (Companion.)
- Someone asked about her in the market last week: a red-haired woman, a long way from home. (Companion: Red Ruin.)

**The question:** none. It's a mission briefing. Let Darrett be earnest, and let the party give him advice he'll remember.

### Rewards

Upon completion of this mission, the party are awarded a small fortress of their own (Bastion). Tatina Rookledust is a kender PC's old teacher (see [[Milo Geartickle (DM)]]). She gives the party the paired **fargabs** (module appendix A).

### Choosing the Bastion

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. The Bastion itself uses the normal 2024 Bastion rules. This only decides **where** it is.

**Why Vendri gives them a base.** The official reason is the module's: a reward for bringing Tatina in. What she tells them is the useful half: "A company that sleeps in my ballroom is a company the whole castle watches. I want you somewhere the council's clerks don't walk past every hour, with room for whatever you drag back." What she leaves out: a dragonborn PC out of the castle means fewer incidents in her corridors, and if something the party brings home explodes, it won't be in her castle.

The city has a handful of empty properties it can spare. Darrett walks the party round them with a clerk's ring of keys, and they pick one. It's mostly flavour. Facilities, hirelings and Bastion turns all work as normal, wherever it is. Each choice adds one small touch later on.

| Bastion | Where | What it's like | The small difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Old Beacon House** | Out on the harbour wall, beside one of the pre-Cataclysm beacon towers | Salt, wind and gulls, and a view of every ship in the bay. | **Rookledust's Annex** | Next to wherever Tatina sets up, in the artisans' quarter | Half workshop, half house, and something always ticking. | Tatina pops round, uninvited. A tinkerer PC's drone tinkering gets a quiet bonus (advantage once per downtime), and occasionally something explodes. |
| **The Cistern House** | The lower city, over an old Istarian water cistern | Damp, cool and very private, with steps down to black water. | It has its own water in the siege. The cistern's tunnels run under half the lower city, and the Smallfolk know them. |

**The tour.** Darrett's pitch at each door, read straight off the clerk's notes and his own opinions:
- **The Old Beacon House:** "The keeper died in the winter, and nobody wanted the stairs. Two hundred and twelve of them. You'll see every sail in the bay before the harbourmaster does."
- **Hetch's Second Warehouse:** "The Shipwrights seized it for unpaid dues. Big, and dry enough. The neighbours keep odd hours. Don't ask what they store."
- **The Fallen Chapterhouse:** "The city shut it after the Cataclysm and never found a use for it. People still cross the street rather than walk past the door." He's quiet in here.
- **Brookside Farm:** "The family left for Palanthas at the first rumour. It's the nicest of the lot. It's also outside the walls, so it's the first thing an army would burn."
- **Rookledust's Annex:** "The rooms beside Tatina's new workshop. The last tenant moved out after the third fire. The rent's very reasonable."
- **The Cistern House:** "Built over an old Istarian cistern. Cool in summer, private all year, and the water's still sweet. The smallfolk say the tunnels go everywhere. They're probably exaggerating."

If they can't decide, the Beacon House is the dramatic one, and the Farm is the tragic one.

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** (Part 3, "Mission 1: Rookledust Rescue").

- **The clockwork servant.** At the end of the first round, Tatina's door bursts open and her **clockwork servant** charges out (a gargoyle, as a construct, with two bludgeoning claws). Roll a d20 each turn: odd, it attacks a goblin, and even, it attacks a character it mistakes for a threat. Tatina shuts it down with "Lunch break!"
- **Who commissioned the boilerdrak.** Tatina built it as a "weed-clearing device" for a tough-looking woman with red hair. It was **Red Ruin**. The same woman later ordered a long-distance talking device, and the goblins came for it instead: the fargabs.
- **The mission.** Vendri's orders are to learn what Tatina knows about the Dragon Army's machines, and to recruit her as an advisor. She agrees at once, as long as she never has to build another weapon. Vendri makes her chief of tech development.
- **Afterwards:** councillor **Uri Stonegrey** turns up at the trade camps, "introducing himself" to the refugees and really recruiting spies. The bazaar is packed, and everything costs 50% more (DC 10 Investigation to find what you want).

## Mission 1: Rookledust Rescue (Factions)

Tatina Rookledust and her inventions are suddenly the most valuable things in Kalaman. Where they end up is the party's choice:
- **Bring her to Vendri, as ordered.** Her inventions go to the war effort: **+1 Military**.
- **Let the Shipwrights' Guild set her up in a new workshop** at the docks. They'll pay very well: **+1 Guilds, -1 Military**, since Vendri wanted her under her own roof.
- **Move her into the party's own new Bastion** to work for them: no change, but she becomes a Bastion special hireling, and both the military and the guilds want her back.
