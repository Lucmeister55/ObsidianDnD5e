## Scenario 4: Catastrophe

> [!info] Forces
> **Engaged:** Bakaris's **1,400**, most of Kalaman's field army, against Kansaldi's **2,500** and Ignia. **Losses:** Kalaman 450 / 600 / 800, enemy 200 / 150 / 100. "Half the army" is the hold.

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Battle at Steel Springs|ch. 4, "Battle at Steel Springs"]]) and **WoK** scenario 4, the retreat from the losing battle.

With WoK, play scenario 4. Without it, run the module's "Retreat from Steel Springs" and its battlefield events table. Faction standings feed in through the mission check-ins ([[Faction Rules#Mission Check-ins]]).

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]], Part 5, "Battle at Steel Springs").

- **The warning.** The morning after Wheelwatch, the fargab crackles: Tatina, in a panic. Lord Bakaris has taken the army out to Steel Springs. (Without a fargab, Wyhan reaches them with *sending*. If they try to warn Bakaris, he refuses to listen.) It's about 35 miles, so it's a forced march: Constitution saves against exhaustion all round.
- **Why the count was wrong.** *(Homebrew.)* The outriders counted what an army of men looks like. Most of Kansaldi's force is draconians, who need no tents, light no fires and slept in the open and in the old diggings around the springs ([[Dragon Armies#What a Force Looks Like]]). And she knew the attack was coming, because Takhisis hears what the dragonborn PC hears, so she marched the rest of the field army in on the last night, with Ignia overhead. The deserter was hers. She let him go.
- **Arrival.** The trap has already sprung. **Kansaldi** is here, on her red dragon **Ignia**, and the party gets a good look at her before a **Dragon Queen abomination** (a human remade by eating draconian flesh) leads a charge at them.
- **The aurak.** Darrett rides back from the front with Lord Bakaris behind him and the army's **aurak** commander on his heels. Killing it shakes the whole enemy line.
- **Red Ruin comes.** As Darrett rides clear, Red Ruin swoops in on her dragonnel: hit and run, out of reach, and at least one shot from her explosive hand crossbow. After two rounds, or at half her hit points, Ignia breathes a plume of fire straight up, the signal to fall back, and Red Ruin breaks off.
- **Afterwards.** Darrett leads what's left six miles east. Lord Bakaris is in shock: his son is missing, believed dead. Darrett and Becklin won't let anyone touch him. He's finished in Kalaman's politics anyway.

> [!info] New system: Skirmish Battles
> The first skirmish of the campaign, and the only new thing this session ([[Skirmish Battles]], [[Playtest Ideas#Introduction Schedule]]).

> [!example] Skirmish: the Inkwater Crossing
> *Source: **Homebrew** ([[Skirmish Battles]]). Run it right after WoK scenario 4, in place of "Commanders' Retreat".*
>
> The army is running, and the last thing between the Dragon Army and the wounded is the ford over the Inkwater.
> - **Objective:** hold the crossing for **5 rounds**, until Darrett and the commanders' riders are across. Then fall back.
> - **Allies:** 2 **Infantry**, starting **Bloodied** (the wounded); 1 **Knights** unit at half HP (Darrett's riders, arriving on round 3 from the north). Use any faction units the party has earned.
> - **Enemies:** 2 **Dragon Army Soldiers** (mounted, speed 50), 1 **Dragon Army Crossbows** in the trees, then 1 **Baaz** from the Fray on round 2.
> - **Round 4: Kansaldi.** Ignia lands on the far bank, and Kansaldi stops the fighting around her with a raised hand to preach. Run her as a **monster** ([[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]). She can't die here: when Bloodied, she withdraws, laughing and delighted ("Yes! Test me again, children!"). This is the party's second look at her after Vogler, and the first time they can hurt her.
> - **The twist.** Across the stream, the party sees **Bakaris the Younger** drop his sword and kneel to her. This is how he becomes "unaccounted for, believed dead" (module), and it's the start of his defection ([[Bakaris the Younger]]). Lord Bakaris, in shock on Darrett's saddle, sees it too, and says nothing to anyone.
> - **Steel Springs, the stream:** units in the water are on **bad footing** ([[Weapon Triangle#Where Each Weapon Shines]]).
> - **Map:** [[map-4.03-retreat-from-steel-springs-player.png|Map 4.3: Retreat from Steel Springs]] (module), at the stream.
> - **Terrain:** the stream as a band across the board (bad footing) with the ford marked, trees on the far bank for the crossbows, and an open patch where Ignia lands.

## Scenario 4: Catastrophe (Factions)

Whatever the outcome, Lord Bakaris led Kalaman's troops to disaster.
- **Save Darrett and organise the retreat:** **+2 Military, -1 Smallfolk** (Vogler's militia was left in the worst position).
- **Pull Vogler's militia out first,** since they were put in the worst position: **+2 Smallfolk, -1 Military**.
- **Arrive too late:** **-1 Military**, and a lot of empty bunks.

Afterwards, if the party publicly blames Bakaris *and the council that approved his attack*: **+1 Military, -1 Guilds**.

## Scenario 5: Valley of Barbed Tails

> [!info] Forces
> **Engaged:** the rearguard, about **300**, against about **400** pursuers. **Losses:** Kalaman 30 / 60 / 150, enemy 100 / 60 / 20. Expect about **1,600** regulars left in Kalaman afterwards.

> [!wok] Source: **WoK** scenario 5. The module doesn't reference it, so where it goes and what it changes is **Homebrew**.

Run it after Steel Springs, as the commanders retreat ("Commanders' Retreat" in the module). If you give it a custom outcome, tie it to the [[Kalaman Military]] track: a **win** is +1, and a **loss** means Vendri's losses show in the city, -1 Kalaman Smallfolk.
