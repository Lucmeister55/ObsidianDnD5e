## Briefing 6, Council, The Knights

*Before Downtime 3.*

*The full council, and it's angry. Word of the undead has spread, and Kalaman blames the knights, as it always has.*

**The summons:** A crowd is shouting at the castle gate about "the knights' dead". A clerk hurries the party through a side door: the council has been called early, and it's in a temper.

**The numbers** (expected): The chapterhouse has **8 knights and 15 men-at-arms**. Someone asks, loudly, what eight knights are worth against nearly 3,000 of the enemy.

**The news:**
- "Durstan" has asked to be enrolled in Kalaman's army. (Companion.) Darrett is delighted.
- Rumours say the undead serve "the Dread Knight". Nobody on the council believes it, or admits to.
- The Knights' chapterhouse wants to recruit openly in the city for the first time in years.
- **Your doing:** the farm's owner, the dwarf Quince Eshfell, has sent a cart of food and a *driftglobe*. (Companion.)

**The question: may the Knights recruit openly in Kalaman?**
- **Yes:** **+1 Knights, -1 Smallfolk**.
- **No:** **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Knights**.
- **Only under Vendri's command**, as Kalaman's soldiers first (DC 15 Persuasion to sell it): **+1 Military, -1 Smallfolk**, and both sides grumble.

## Downtime 3 (3 weeks)

The first refugees from other burned villages start arriving. Pick two or three:

- **The preacher.** A wild-eyed preacher in the market square is telling crowds the "Queen of Many Colours" is coming, and that she'll be kind to those who kneel early. People are listening. *Why them:* They're in the market when the crowd turns on a heckler, a refugee girl from Vogler, and starts to push.
  - Break it up: **+1 Military**. Whether it's -1 or +1 Smallfolk depends on how roughly they do it.
  - Follow him home: he's a real Dragon Army agent, and it's a lead for the next mission.
- **Kneeling to a dragonborn PC.** A handful of refugees who saw the dragonborn PC rise at High Hill start leaving offerings at his door (see [[Wurm (DM)]]).
  - If the party discourages it gently: no change.
  - If they let it grow: **-1 Military**, as soldiers mutter about a "dragon cult".
- **The shipwright's list.** Sigmund Pross is quietly selling places on his ships to wealthy families, "in case the walls fall". The Smallfolk find out. *Why them:* The smallfolk bring it to the company, because they're the only officers in the castle who aren't guild men, and one of them sits on the council.
  - Expose him: **+2 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds**.
  - Keep his secret in exchange for two places for the party's own people: **+1 Guilds**, and a secret that can come out later. When it does, **-2 Smallfolk**.
- **A ranger PC's grove.** Woodcutters from the trade camps start cutting the grove the ranger PC has been tending (see [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]]).
  - Drive them off: **-1 Smallfolk**.
  - Find them other wood, or pay them to stop: **+1 Smallfolk**, and the grove is safe.
- **The Hammerstrike feud.** The army has one contract to give, for a thousand spearheads, and both Hammerstrike cousins want it. Kadmos says Tiria's steel is brittle. Tiria says Kadmos has never forged a blade in his life. They've stopped speaking across the street, and the order is late. The quartermaster asks the party to sort it out. *Why them:* The esquires are always at the forges anyway, and both cousins will talk to them, if not to each other.
  - Back Tiria, who's right about the steel: **+1 Military, -1 Smallfolk** (Kadmos's apprentices lose the work).
  - Make them work together (DC 14 Persuasion, or a good story about family): **+1 Military, +1 Smallfolk**. On a failure, both of them sulk and the spears are a week late: **-1 Military**.
  - Let the Masons' Guild buy the contract out from under them: **+1 Guilds, -1 Smallfolk**.
  - Whichever cousin they help gives the party a tenth off at the forge, for good.
- **A wedding in the camps.** Two Vogler refugees want to marry before the war takes one of them. There's no priest in Kalaman who isn't a charlatan. The preacher has offered, for a fee. The couple ask the party. *Why them:* The company got them out of Vogler, and a knight PC is the only knight they know.
  - A knight PC marries them by old Solamnic custom, which allows it where there are no clergy: **+1 Knights**, and the refugees will remember who stood up for them.
  - Pay for a feast (25 gp) and let the camp do it its own way: **+1 Smallfolk**.
  - Let the preacher do it: no change, but the preacher's crowd grows (see "The preacher").
- **Bats over Kalaman.** *(Homebrew, from [[Soth's Host]].)* For three nights, **fell bats** roost on the castle's spires: bloated, hairless, with corpse-light eyes. They don't attack. They watch. The smallfolk say it's a curse on the knights, and the council's anger at the Order has an easy target. *Why them:* Vendri sends the esquires: the knights won't go, the guard can't climb, and the company has a kender.
  - Drive them off, on the rooftops at night: **+1 Smallfolk**. It's a one-turn, and the bats come back once, later, when it matters.
  - Make sure the knights are seen doing it: **+1 Knights, -1 Military**, because Vendri's soldiers look useless next to them. A knight PC can lead it.
  - Leave them be: they leave on the fourth night, and **-1 Smallfolk** if the Knights did nothing.
  - **Map:** a castle rooftop on a blank board. **Terrain:** spires, a steep slate roof (difficult terrain), and a parapet.
- **The camp's protection.** Firefishers have started charging the stalls in the trade camps for "insurance". Marta Dace's people are polite about it. *Why them:* the Spokesperson asks them first, because the watch doesn't come out to the camps.
  - Run them off: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Firefishers**.
  - Tell Vendri, who sends soldiers: **+1 Military, -1 Firefishers**, and the camps are full of soldiers for a week.
  - Talk to Dace and get the fee cut in half: **+1 Firefishers, -1 Smallfolk**, and you've agreed there's a fee.
