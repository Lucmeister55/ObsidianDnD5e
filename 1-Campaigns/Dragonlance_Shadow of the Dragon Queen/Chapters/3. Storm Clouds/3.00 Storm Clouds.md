## Summary

> [!homebrew] **The campaign proper starts here.** From here on it's a **high-level plan**, to revise once the prologue has been played: characters may have been swapped, and Vogler may have gone differently ([[DM Guide#Chapters]]). Hence "a knight PC" or "a tinkerer PC" instead of names ([[DM Guide#Roles, Not Names]]).

From Kalaman to the catacombs: months of factions, missions and downtime, ending with Soth (until then only the Dread Knight, his name rediscovered in the catacombs, [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]]) and the news that the party must go to the Northern Wastes. Follow the [[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]] companion except where noted. It's the longest act, with downtime of weeks to months between beats for Bastion turns, crafting, research and factions ([[Faction Rules]]).

**Sources:** [[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]], [[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war|Chapter 4: Shadow of War]].

> [!info] No bookkeeping in Kalaman
> Don't track food, water or carried weight in Storm Clouds. The military and the factions keep the party supplied, and spare gear goes in the Bastion.

## Running Threads

**Vary the downtimes.** At most **one** event per downtime about Bakaris or a dragonborn PC (Downtime 5's war council excepted). The rest is the city: a dispute, a dilemma inside the company, a beast or the dead, a favour. Bring each thread below back once or twice.

**Bakaris the Younger.** The guilds give the Bakarises their council voice, so humiliating them costs a little Guilds Standing. How much trouble the Younger makes depends on **Guilds** Standing:
- **Trusted or better:** he sulks, spreads rumours and waits.
- **Neutral:** once per downtime, one purchase costs double, or one guild favour is "unavailable".
- **Wary or worse:** a **boycott**. The guilds' Hostile effects start a band early, he calls the party Dragon Army sympathisers, and he tries to buy a dragonborn PC's contract out from under them.

**A dragonborn PC and the city.** Spy rumours grow all act ([[Wurm (DM)]]). [[Nestra Vendri|Vendri]]'s protection holds **while Military is Neutral or better**. At Wary Military, she quietly withdraws it. At Wary **Smallfolk**, the docks turn on him regardless. Either makes the rumours violent ("the alley", [[3.08 Downtime 4]]).

**The Dragon Army's knives.** Takhisis hears what a dragonborn PC hears, so by mid-act the army sends **sivaks**, who take the shape of whoever they kill ("The friendly face", [[3.08 Downtime 4]]). Their eyes in the castle are [[Jeyev Veldrews|Jeyev]], Becklin's murderer and now Darrett's right hand. Drop one wrong note about him per downtime, and have him try for Darrett just before the Lord's Arrival.

## How a Briefing Works

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. News items are tagged where they come from the **Module** or **Companion**.

How the players see the war move, and usually get asked what Kalaman should do. Two kinds:
- **The war room:** Vendri, Darrett, maybe Cudgel (or her sergeant) or Tatina, at the map table. Military news and a decision. Short.
- **The council:** Governor Miat, the five guild leaders, Lord Bakaris, Vendri, sometimes the refugees' Spokesperson. Politics, money, blame, a vote. Longer and louder.

Open with **the summons** (a page at midnight, a bell, a herald, Darrett at the door), varied to set the mood. Then four beats:
1. **The map.** NPCs move tokens on a big hand-drawn map ([[map-4.01-kalaman-player.png|Map 4.1]] and the module's region map) as they talk.
2. **The news.** Three or four one-sentence items.
3. **Your doing.** Always one item caused by the party, good or bad.
4. **The question.** Two or three options, each moving Standing ([[Faction Rules]]), with the NPCs pushing loudly for their own.

**The Mages' seat.** Once a spellcaster PC is the Order's observer ([[3.01 Arrival in Kalaman#Message for a Mage]]), once per council he can put the Mages' position as one of the options: **+1 Mages** if he serves the Order, **-1** if he ignores Wyhan's instructions. She always sends them.

**Fifteen minutes at most.** They leave with a choice made and the next move clear.

**"Your doing" ideas:**
- The refugees housed inside the walls are working the docks, or the ones left outside are sick.
- The Shipwrights' new invention (if Tatina went to them) is on the guild ships, not the walls.
- Bakaris the Younger has been calling the party spies, and someone believes it.
- The farm they saved sends a cart of food, with a letter.
- A village they didn't reach in time is gone.
