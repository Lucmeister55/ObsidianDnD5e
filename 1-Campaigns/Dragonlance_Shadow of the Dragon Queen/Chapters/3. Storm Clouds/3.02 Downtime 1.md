## Briefing 1, War Room, After the Council

*Before Downtime 1.*

*Vendri and Darrett, the night after the arrival, a few hours after the party left the cells.*

**The summons:** Past midnight, a page with a candle knocks on the ballroom door. "The Marshal's still up. She says so are you."

**The numbers** (expected): Vendri's ledger: about **2,000** regulars (900 foot, 400 spears, 250 bows, 150 outriders, 80 lancers and 24 ballistae), 120 of them at Wheelwatch and the border forts. Scouts put the enemy around Vogler at about **2,500**, and growing.

**The news:**
- Vogler is occupied. Scouts see red-and-black banners on Thornwall Keep. (Module.)
- Bandits and mercenaries are flocking north to join the Dragon Army, because it pays well. (Module, "Clarifying the Threat".)
- The enemy's commander has a name now: **Highmaster Kansaldi Fire-Eyes**. Her own soldiers say she can see a lie. (Module.)
- The trade camps outside the Trade Gate have doubled in a week. Not all of the newcomers are from Vogler.
- **Your doing:** the guards who arrested the party this morning are on the door tonight, and they salute. Badly.

**Why they're in the war room.** If anyone asks (someone will), Vendri gives them the half of the truth that suits her: "Every officer in this castle has fought Estwilders and ogres. You're the only people in it who've fought them and lived, and we know next to nothing about them. Becklin and Cudgel are dead or gone. You're what I have. And you're the only ones who can't run to the council with what they hear in here." She leaves out the dragonborn PC, and that nobody will ask questions if they don't come back.

**The terms,** laid out once ([[DM Guide#Why They Stay Together]]):
- They go where she sends them, and they answer to her, not the council.
- The file stays in her desk. It moves if they do.
- The dragonborn PC is in the company's custody. If anyone harms him, they answer to her. If he walks off alone, she can't help him.
- "Do well, and one day I'll have nothing in that folder worth keeping."

**Vendri, thinking aloud:** "Vogler. Four hundred fishermen and a bridge to nowhere. Why bring dragons for a fishing village?" She paces the map. "And a Highmaster, on a red dragon, in person. I'll grant the ferry: it's the only crossing between us and the Wastes, and whoever holds it cuts us off from the north and eats the village's fish and timber. But then she had every boat in that harbour in range, and she let them sail. Four hundred mouths. Down the river to me." She taps the trade camps. "If I were her, I'd have sunk them. So either she's a fool, or she wants them here, and she doesn't strike me as a fool. She wanted us to hear about the dragon." No answer yet, for her or the players. *(DM: Vogler is the crossing to the road north into the Wastes, and Ispin's shield was there. See [[6.02 Return to Kalaman#Return to Kalaman|the answer]].)*

**The codewords.** *(Homebrew, [[Draconians]].)* A sheet in Wyhan's hand: the Mages' code names for the scaled soldiers, **baaz** (brass), **kapak** (copper) and **bozak** (bronze). Vendri reads them with open distaste:

> "Baaz. Kapak. Bozak. It sounds like a man clearing his throat. Try shouting that from a wall at night."

Her soldiers will say brass, copper, bronze. Then she stops at the last line: "*Given the pattern, expect silver and gold as well.*" She looks up. "What pattern?" The note doesn't say, and Wyhan won't.

*DM: soldiers say the colour, mages the names. The metals are the first clue to where draconians come from. The last two names arrive after the first sightings: sivak in [[3.09 Mission 4 - Missing Scouts#Briefing 9, War Room, Near Home|Briefing 9]] and aurak in [[3.10 Downtime 5#Briefing 10, Council, The Great Victory|Briefing 10]].*

**The question: what to do with the refugees' fighters?** A few dozen Vogler men and women can hold a spear.
- **Draft them into the garrison:** **+1 Military, -1 Smallfolk** (they wanted to stay together).
- **Let them form their own militia**, under their Spokesperson: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Military** (Vendri doesn't like soldiers she can't command).
- **Put them to work on the walls** for pay, which the guilds have to find: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds**.
- **Send the youngest to the Knights' chapterhouse** to train as squires: **+1 Knights, -1 Smallfolk** (their families want them close).

## Downtime 1 (2 weeks)

> [!info] New system: personal downtime activities
> Each player's own activity, from their character note. No Bastion yet ([[Playtest Ideas#Introduction Schedule]]).

Run "A new voice", then two or three others:

- **A new voice.** With Raven dead, the refugees argue every night about who should speak for them. The candidates: the militia captain (hard, angry, wants to retake Vogler), an old ferryman everyone trusts (gentle, cautious), or no one until the Ironclads return. They ask the party what Raven would have wanted. *Why them:* they got the refugees out, and are the only people with rank they trust.
  - Back the militia captain: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Military** (Vendri doesn't want an angry man drilling a militia she can't command), but the refugees get harder to keep out of trouble, and a later event may cost them dearly.
  - Back the ferryman: **+1 Smallfolk**, and the community stays steady, but quiet when it should be loud.
  - Refuse to choose ("it's not our place"): no change. The refugees settle it themselves, slowly and bitterly. *Hidden mark:* the militia captain wins, and at the siege the refugee wall answers to him, not to Vendri.
  - Whoever they back becomes the **Spokesperson**, the camps' voice from now on ([[Kalaman Smallfolk]]).

- **Bread at the Trade Gate.** Rations are short, and a fight breaks out at the gate between Kalaman's poor and Vogler's refugees over the day's bread cart. *Why them:* they're there, with swords, and both sides know them.
  - Split it evenly, and make the guild bakers send more: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds**.
  - Favour the locals: no change, because what the city gains the camps lose. *Hidden mark:* the Spokesperson doesn't forget it, and at the Trial the refugees' voice won't speak for the party ([[Faction Rules#Hidden Consequences]]).
  - Pay out of their own pockets for a second cart (50 gp): **+1 Smallfolk**.
- **Bakaris holds court.** Lord Bakaris is in the Steady Beacon telling anyone who'll listen that *he* saved Vogler. The soldiers buy him drinks while he tells them about the charges, and the deserters in the Marshal's ballroom. *Why them:* it's their names.
  - Expose him publicly: **+1 Smallfolk, +1 Military, -1 Guilds**, and Bakaris the Younger makes it personal (see "Running Threads"). The soldiers stop buying him drinks, and a refugee who saw him take the first boat becomes a witness for the Trial.
  - Flatter him and let him keep the story: **+1 Guilds, -1 Smallfolk**.
  - Let it go: no change, but the refugees notice. *Hidden mark:* the soldiers keep believing him, and at the Trial the hall opens already convinced, so Thren's patience starts one lower.
- **A new horse.** *(Homebrew, Edric's own, and it doesn't count toward the two or three events.)* The mare was left on the wharf at Vogler ([[2.03 Evacuation]]), and a knight PC needs a mount. Let him choose where to look:
  - **The castle stables** (Military Neutral or better): a sturdy cavalry gelding on loan, free. It's Kalaman's horse, and it goes back to the stables if Military drops to Wary.
  - **The Knights' chapterhouse** (Knights Trusted or better): a proper Solamnic warhorse, trained to stand under fire. The finest one comes with the Knights' signature reward ([[Knights of Solamnia]]).
  - **A refugee's plough horse:** a Vogler farmer who swam his horse out to the flotilla offers it for nothing. Slow, gentle and unflappable. Taking it gratefully is **+1 Smallfolk**.
  - **The horse market** (50 gp at wartime prices): roll a d6 for what he ends up with.

  | d6 | The horse |
  |---|---|
  | 1 | Bites. Everyone, Edric included. |
  | 2 | Fast and beautiful, and won't go near water. |
  | 3 | An old cavalry horse who knows every command, and is steady under fire. |
  | 4 | Young and skittish, but bolts *toward* trouble, not away. |
  | 5 | Placid to the point of sleep. Nothing frightens it, including orders. |
  | 6 | A steppe horse, the same breed as the mare. He can't stop looking at it. |

  The new horse stays in Kalaman when the party sails for the Wastes, since there's one pack per passenger and no room for horses ([[4.04 The Deadline]]).
- **A message for a mage.** Run the module's "Message for a Mage" with a spellcaster PC and Wyhan. Delivering the scroll unopened and treating Wyhan with respect: **+1 Mages**. If the spellcaster PC opened it (see his level 4 moment), Wyhan knows: **-1 Mages**.
- **The fishers' licence.** Vogler's fishermen have boats, nets and nothing to do. Rey Costwyn's Fishers' Guild says nobody fishes Kalaman's waters without a Guild licence, and the licences cost more than a boat. *Why them:* refugees can't petition a guild, and esquires can.
  - Lean on him: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds**.
  - Broker a deal, with refugee fishermen working Guild boats for a share (DC 15 Persuasion): **+1 Smallfolk**. On a failure, Costwyn feels cornered: **-1 Guilds**.
  - Have a word with the Firefishers, who can make Costwyn's objections go away: **+1 Firefishers, -1 Guilds**. *Hidden mark:* the fishermen now owe Peppino, and in the Wastes or the siege someone calls the debt in ([[Peppino Fettuccini#In Kalaman, Late]]).
