## Briefing 1, War Room, After the Council

*Before Downtime 1.*

*Vendri and Darrett, the night after the arrival, a few hours after the party left the cells.*

**The summons:** Past midnight, a page with a candle knocks on the ballroom door. "The Marshal's still up. She says so are you."

**The numbers** (expected): Vendri's ledger: about **2,000** regulars (900 foot, 400 spears, 250 bows, 150 outriders, 80 lancers and 24 ballistae), 120 of them at Wheelwatch and the border forts. Scouts put the enemy around Vogler at about **2,500**, and growing.

**The news:**
- Vogler is occupied. Scouts see red-and-black banners on Thornwall Keep. (Module.)
- Bandits and mercenaries are flocking north to join the Dragon Army, because it pays well. (Module, "Clarifying the Threat".)
- The enemy's commander has a name now: **Highmaster Kansaldi Fire-Eyes**. Her own soldiers say she can see a lie. (Module.)
- The trade camps outside the Trade Gate have doubled in a week. Not all of the newcomers are from Vogler.
- **Your doing:** the guards who arrested the party this morning are on the door tonight, and they salute. Badly.

**Why they're in the war room.** If anyone asks (someone will), Vendri gives them the half of the truth that suits her: "Every officer in this castle has fought Estwilders and ogres. You're the only people in it who've seen what's coming. And you're the only ones who can't run to the council with what they hear in here." All true. She leaves out the rest: that she wants the dragonborn PC where she can watch him, and that nobody will ask questions if they don't come back.

**The terms.** Before anything else, Vendri lays them out, plainly, once ([[DM Guide#Why They Stay Together]]):
- They go where she sends them, and they answer to her, not the council.
- The file stays in her desk. It moves if they do.
- The dragonborn PC is in the company's custody. If anyone harms him, they answer to her. If he walks off alone, she can't help him.
- "Do well, and one day I'll have nothing in that folder worth keeping."

**Vendri, thinking aloud:** "Vogler. Four hundred fishermen and a bridge to nowhere. Why bring dragons for a fishing village?" She has no answer, and nor should the players yet. *(DM: Vogler is the crossing to the road north into the Wastes, and Ispin's shield was there. See [[6.02 Return to Kalaman#Return to Kalaman|the answer]].)*

**The question: what to do with the refugees' fighters?** A few dozen Vogler men and women can hold a spear.
- **Draft them into the garrison:** **+1 Military, -1 Smallfolk** (they wanted to stay together).
- **Let them form their own militia**, under their Spokesperson: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Military** (Vendri doesn't like soldiers she can't command).
- **Put them to work on the walls** for pay, which the guilds have to find: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds**.
- **Send the youngest to the Knights' chapterhouse** to train as squires: **+1 Knights, -1 Smallfolk** (their families want them close).

## Downtime 1 (2 weeks)

> [!info] New system: personal downtime activities
> Each player's own activity, from their character note. No Bastion yet ([[Playtest Ideas#Introduction Schedule]]).

The party finds its feet in Kalaman. Run "A new voice", then pick two or three others:

- **A new voice.** With Raven dead, the refugees argue every night about who should speak for them. The candidates are Vogler's militia captain (hard and angry, and wants to march back and retake the village), an old ferryman everyone trusts (gentle and cautious, and wants to keep everyone alive), or no one at all, until Cudgel returns. The refugees ask the party what Raven would have wanted. *Why them:* They brought the refugees out of Vogler, and they're the only people with rank in Kalaman whom the refugees trust.
  - Back the militia captain: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Military** (Vendri doesn't want an angry man drilling a militia she can't command), but the refugees get harder to keep out of trouble, and a later event may cost them dearly.
  - Back the ferryman: **+1 Smallfolk**, and the community stays steady, but quiet when it should be loud.
  - Refuse to choose ("it's not our place"): no change. The refugees settle it themselves, slowly and bitterly.
  - Whoever they back becomes the **Spokesperson**, the camps' voice from now on ([[Kalaman Smallfolk]]).

- **Bread at the Trade Gate.** Rations are short, and a fight breaks out at the gate between Kalaman's poor and Vogler's refugees over the day's bread cart. *Why them:* They're at the gate when it starts, on their way back from the camps, and they're the nearest people with swords who both sides recognise.
  - Split it evenly, and make the guild bakers send more: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds**.
  - Favour the locals: no change, because what the city gains the camps lose, and the Spokesperson doesn't forget it.
  - Pay out of their own pockets for a second cart (50 gp): **+1 Smallfolk**.
- **Bakaris holds court.** Lord Bakaris is in the Steady Beacon telling anyone who'll listen that *he* saved Vogler. The soldiers are buying him drinks. He tells them about the charges too, and that the Marshal is hiding deserters in her ballroom. *Why them:* It's their names he's dragging through the tavern.
  - Expose him publicly: **+1 Smallfolk, +1 Military, -1 Guilds**, and Bakaris the Younger makes it personal (see "Running Threads"). It doesn't touch the file, which is Vendri's now, but the soldiers stop buying him drinks. A refugee who saw him take the first boat is a witness worth remembering for the Trial.
  - Flatter him and let him keep the story: **+1 Guilds, -1 Smallfolk**.
  - Let it go: no change, but the refugees notice.
- **A message for a mage.** Run the module's "Message for a Mage" with a spellcaster PC and Wyhan. Delivering the scroll unopened and treating Wyhan with respect: **+1 Mages**. If the spellcaster PC opened it (see his level 4 moment), Wyhan knows: **-1 Mages**.
- **The fishers' licence.** Vogler's fishermen have boats, nets and nothing to do. Rey Costwyn's Fishers' Guild says nobody fishes Kalaman's waters without a Guild licence, and the licences cost more than a boat. *Why them:* The Spokesperson brings it to the company. Refugees can't petition a guild, and esquires can.
  - Lean on him: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds**.
  - Broker a deal, with refugee fishermen working Guild boats for a share (DC 15 Persuasion): **+1 Smallfolk**. On a failure, Costwyn feels cornered: **-1 Guilds**.
  - Have a word with the Firefishers, who can make Costwyn's objections go away: **+1 Firefishers, -1 Guilds**, and the fishermen now owe the wrong people.
