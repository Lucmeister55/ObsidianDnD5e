### Arriving in Kalaman

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Arriving in Kalaman|ch. 4, "Arriving in Kalaman"]] to "Reporting for Duty").

- **Refugees at the shore.** The flotilla lands outside the city, and the Vogler survivors are sent to the ramshackle **trade camps** outside the Trade Gate. They aren't let in.
- **Darrett's armour.** He opens Becklin's box: a full suit of Solamnic plate, made for him. Let him have the moment.
- **Bakaris goes ahead.** *(Homebrew.)* While the party is still on the beach with the refugees, Lord Bakaris takes the first cart up to the castle with his retinue. He gets to the council first, with his version of Vogler and his charges ([[DM Guide#Why They Stay Together]]).
- **The arrest.** *(Homebrew.)* At the Trade Gate, a sergeant and a dozen soldiers are waiting with Bakaris's description of them. The charges: **desertion** of the rearguard, **theft** of the Bakaris family's property (the dragonborn PC), and **conspiracy against Vogler**, as agents of the army that burned it. Let them talk, but fighting their way out of Kalaman on their first day is how deserters get hanged. Darrett protests, and is told to go and find someone who outranks a lord. He does.
- **The cells.** *(Homebrew.)* A few hours in the castle basement, behind the long-sealed doors to the old dungeons. Then **Marshal [[Nestra Vendri|Vendri]]** comes down the stairs, with Darrett behind her, and makes her offer (see "Vendri's deal" below).
- **The council.** Sworn in, the party walks into the council chamber as **esquires** of the Kalaman military, special operatives with a lieutenant's rank and **5 gp a week** ([[Kalaman Military]]). Bakaris goes white. Governor Calof Miat and the guild leaders hear the report. The council doubts the threat and doesn't want a war, but Vendri believes it, and now she has people to send.
- **Reporting for duty.** The party get rooms in the castle, a ballroom on the fourth floor, three floors above the cell they woke up in. Darrett becomes their liaison with Vendri.

### Before the First Mission

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]], Part 3, "Before the First Mission").

Use these as the first downtime's colour, alongside the timeline's events:
- **Darrett's assignment.** Vendri puts Darrett in charge of training recruits, and he's still the party's go-between.
- **Dinner with a councillor.** Guild councillor **Alexandra Nightvane** invites the party to dinner and offers to be their patron, if they share everything Vendri tells them. ([[Kalaman Guilds]])
- **The Steady Beacon.** Its owner, Jesen Thold, Vendri's old friend, thanks the party. Soldiers grumble that the council won't back the marshal.
- **Wanderer's Rest.** Four thugs from a gang called the **Firefishers** shake down the owner, Zol Windswell. If challenged, they back off and promise to come back.

### Kalaman at a Glance

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** (Appendix B, "Kalaman Quick Notes") and **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Kalaman Gazetteer|ch. 4, "Kalaman Gazetteer"]], map 4.1).

The full gazetteer, with the gates, walls and castle floors, is in [[Kalaman]].

Kalaman is the capital of Nightlund: a port that survived a war with Istar and came through the Cataclysm almost untouched. A governor rules it, appointed by the five greatest guilds. Being Nightlund's capital, and given its history with the Dread Knight, it doesn't trust the Knights of Solamnia.

## 16 Brookgreen: Arrival in Kalaman

Run the module's arrival, council meeting and "Reporting Back", with the arrest in between (above). It's the first faction test.
- **Helping settle the refugees** on the beach, with Medicine or tool checks, before the arrest: **+1 Refugees**.
- **Vendri's deal**, in the cells ([[DM Guide#Why They Stay Together]]). She can't make the charges go away, but she can take them out of the council's hands: swear in as **one esquire company** under military law, and the charges go into a file on her desk. The players name the company. Hand out the oath.
  - **Swear in:** **+1 Military**. The file exists, and they're bound to each other by it.
  - **Hold out for better terms first,** demanding the refugees be housed *inside* the walls (DC 15 Persuasion): **+1 Refugees, -1 Guilds**, because the guilds own the empty houses. Vendri respects it.
  - **Refuse:** they stay in the cells, and the council meets without them. Bakaris tells his story unopposed (**-1 Refugees**). In the morning, Vendri comes back with the same oath and a list of the council's witnesses. **-1 Military**, and Bakaris tells everyone they had to be dragged.
- **Silencing Lord Bakaris** in front of the council and telling the truth about Vogler: **+1 Refugees, -1 Guilds**, because the guild leaders backed him. The charges stay in Vendri's file either way: under military law, they're hers now.

## Message for a Mage

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Message for a Mage|ch. 4, "Message for a Mage"]]).

Run as written whenever a character wants to join the Mages of High Sorcery. This is the natural first meeting for [[Davos Bloodbane|a spellcaster PC]] and Wyhan, and the start of the [[Mages of High Sorcery]] track.

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]], Part 2, "Expanding Wyhan's Test").

The companion turns Wyhan's errand into a real test: delivering the scroll is only the first part, and the true test is whether the candidate holds to their conviction when it's questioned. When it's over, Wyhan tells each character which Order she thinks suits them, says the choice is still theirs, and gives back their scroll as a **3-use spell scroll** matching that Order. She then writes to the Tower at Wayreth about their Test.

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.

**The last page.** For a spellcaster PC carrying Antimodes's scroll, Wyhan reads one more page aloud, with distaste: the old diviner asks that the bearer be seated as the Order's **observer on Kalaman's council**. "I won't sit in a room with fishmongers," she says. "You will." From now on he has a chair at every council briefing, and he speaks for the Mages there ([[3.00 Storm Clouds#How a Briefing Works]]). The council doesn't like it, and has no grounds to refuse: the Order has had the right since before the Cataclysm. If anyone asks Wyhan why him, she says Antimodes wants him to learn how wars are really run. If anyone asks Antimodes, he says the tides are strong in Kalaman.
