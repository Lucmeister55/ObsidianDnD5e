## Scenario 1: Battle of Vogler

### Setup

The scenario setup can more or less be read directly from the board game introduction, with Becklin explaining that the defenders consist of roughly 150 mercenaries under Cudgel’s command, hastily assembled and poorly equipped compared to the incoming threat. Their plan is to hold the dragon army at a narrow coastal pass between the sea and the eastern cliffs, where the terrain forces the enemy into a bottleneck and partially offsets their numerical disadvantage. The objective is not to win a decisive battle, but to delay the advance long enough for the villagers of Vogler to evacuate safely inland and, if possible, regroup with the defenders later once a defensible position can be re-established.

### Resolution

**Win:** If the battle is won, the draconian army breaks and begins to retreat, and the mercenaries break into cheering at what seems like a hard-fought victory. But Becklin’s relief vanishes when she spots smoke and banners on the horizon, followed by a warhorn and a much larger force cresting the hills—an overwhelming host, many times the size of the original army, advancing toward the village. Cudgel successfully withdraws with the remaining mercenaries, and Becklin has a chance to survive and later reappear at Wheelwatch Outpost.

**Hold:** If the battle is still ongoing, the frontline remains locked in brutal combat when Becklin sees the same smoke, banners, and warhorn in the distance. The reinforcements are coming, and she knows the fight is about to become impossible to sustain, so she orders the players and any others near her to pull back toward Vogler and prepare for full evacuation while she, Cudgel, and the mercenaries hold the line as long as they can. Offscreen, in the chaos of the continuing battle, Becklin is too focused on the enemy advance to notice Jeyev drawing a hidden blade and stabbing her in the back, killing her before the retreat is fully completed, while Cudgel manages to survive and withdraw with the remaining forces.

**Loss:** If the battle is lost, the mercenaries are already retreating when Becklin spots the approaching reinforcements. The advancing force rolls over the field as the retreat turns into a rout, and the enemy begins overrunning the fleeing defenders. Becklin calls for an immediate withdrawal to Vogler, tells them with tears in her eyes to pass on her pride to Darrett, and sends them to retrieve something from the wooden box in Thornwall Keep. Offscreen, as the rout collapses, Becklin is stabbed in the back by Jeyev and killed in the confusion, while Cudgel also falls in battle during the final overrun.

In all cases, Becklin makes one final, unchanging request before sending the characters away: she tells them exactly what to say to Darrett—that she is proud of him, no matter what comes next—and insists they take the wooden box from Thornwall Keep and deliver it into his care, without opening it or questioning its contents.
