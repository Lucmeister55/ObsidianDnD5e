## Scenario 1: Battle of Vogler

*Source: **WoK** scenario 1 (placed by the module in [[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Invasion of Vogler|ch. 3]]), with **Homebrew** outcomes for Becklin's fate per result (see [[Becklin Uth Viharin]]). Jeyev's betrayal is the **Module**'s "Assassination" option ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Lost in Battle|ch. 3, "Lost in Battle"]]; see [[Jeyev Veldrews]]).*

### Setup

The scenario setup can more or less be read directly from the board game introduction, with Becklin explaining that the defenders consist of roughly 150 mercenaries under Cudgel's command, hastily assembled and poorly equipped compared to the incoming threat. Their plan is to hold the dragon army at a narrow coastal pass between the sea and the eastern cliffs, where the terrain forces the enemy into a bottleneck and partially offsets their numerical disadvantage. The objective is not to win a decisive battle, but to delay the advance long enough for the villagers of Vogler to evacuate safely inland and, if possible, regroup with the defenders later once a defensible position can be re-established.

### Resolution

**Win:** If the battle is won, the draconian army breaks and begins to retreat, and the mercenaries break into cheering at what seems like a hard-fought victory. But Becklin's relief vanishes when she spots smoke and banners on the horizon, followed by a warhorn and a much larger force cresting the hills—an overwhelming host, many times the size of the original army, advancing toward the village. Cudgel successfully withdraws with the remaining mercenaries, and Becklin has a chance to survive and later reappear at Wheelwatch Outpost.

**Hold:** If the battle is still ongoing, the frontline remains locked in brutal combat when Becklin sees the same smoke, banners, and warhorn in the distance. The reinforcements are coming, and she knows the fight is about to become impossible to sustain, so she orders the players and any others near her to pull back toward Vogler and prepare for full evacuation while she, Cudgel, and the mercenaries hold the line as long as they can. Offscreen, in the chaos of the continuing battle, Becklin is too focused on the enemy advance to notice Jeyev drawing a hidden blade and stabbing her in the back, killing her before the retreat is fully completed, while Cudgel manages to survive and withdraw with the remaining forces.

**Loss:** If the battle is lost, the mercenaries are already retreating when Becklin spots the approaching reinforcements. The advancing force rolls over the field as the retreat turns into a rout, and the enemy begins overrunning the fleeing defenders. Becklin calls for an immediate withdrawal to Vogler, tells them with tears in her eyes to pass on her pride to Darrett, and sends them to retrieve something from the wooden box in Thornwall Keep. Offscreen, as the rout collapses, Becklin is stabbed in the back by Jeyev and killed in the confusion, while Cudgel also falls in battle during the final overrun.

In all cases, Becklin makes one final, unchanging request before sending the characters away: she tells them exactly what to say to Darrett—that she is proud of him, no matter what comes next—and insists they take the wooden box from Thornwall Keep and deliver it into his care, without opening it or questioning its contents.

## Evacuation

*Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Invasion of Vogler|ch. 3, "Invasion of Vogler"]]: the invasion encounters, Fewmaster Gholcag and the final boat).*

> [!example] Skirmish option: the last boats
> *Source: **Homebrew** ([[Skirmish Battles]]). A good first skirmish, right after WoK scenario 1, so the table can compare the two.*
>
> Instead of running the invasion encounters one by one, run the wharf as a skirmish.
> - **Objective:** get the boats away by **round 6**. Every round a Militia unit spends standing on the wharf, instead of fighting, loads one boat.
> - **Allies:** 2 **Militia** (Vogler's villagers, under Raven and the party), plus Darrett as a champion.
> - **Enemies:** 2 **Baaz**, 1 **Hired Blades** (hobgoblins), and the boilerdrak and Fewmaster Gholcag as monsters.
> - **Round 5:** Kansaldi arrives (see "Raven's Last Stand" below). Stop the skirmish there, and play the last scene as written.
> - **Faction:** each Militia unit that's still standing at the end counts as "getting everyone onto the boats" for the faction intro below.

### Déjà Vu

*Source: **Homebrew**, the payoff of [[0. Premonition]].*

**When:** during the boilerdrak fight (above), the moment it fires its blast or blows up. The blast is the one from the dream. Edric is thrown down, whether the blast hits him or not, and comes to in the rubble with his ears ringing, just as he did in the dream. The other characters are still there this time, picking themselves up nearby.

> The ringing fades, and the screaming comes in under it. Smoke goes up the street the wrong way, the way it did in the dream. Up on the cliffs, the old keep stands black against the fire. To your left there is a beam, and a doorway, and a hand reaching out from under it. Even the boy's face is the same.

Run it as a real scene, with real rolls, and the same three choices. Check which one Edric's player chose in the Premonition:
- **If he makes the same choice,** it goes the way the dream did, unless the party does something the dream didn't have (another hero joins in, a spell, a combo). The dream only showed him alone.
- **If he chooses differently,** it can work: a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check to lift or drag, or holding the line for 2 rounds against a **Baaz** to stand guard.

If the boy lives, he's one of Vogler's refugees from then on, and he never forgets the knight's face. **+1 Vogler Refugees.** So the dream can be wrong, and Edric knows it. If the boy dies, he knows something worse.

Either way, the rest of the dream is still out there: the companions dead in the rubble, and the thing in the sky. It's just a little less certain now.

### Raven's Last Stand

*Source: **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Raven's death), with **Homebrew** staging (see [[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]).*

As the last boats push off, [[Raven Uth Vogler|Raven]] stays on the docks with a handful of militia to cut the mooring lines, and [[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]] arrives with her guard. The party should be close enough to see it, but too far to stop it, ideally from a boat already pulling away. Kansaldi offers Raven forgiveness, Raven spits at her, and Kansaldi burns her alive (see Kansaldi's note for how to play it). Raven's whistle lands on the deck at someone's feet, or sinks into the river.

Don't make this a fight the party can win. Kansaldi is far beyond them, and this is the moment the players learn her name.
