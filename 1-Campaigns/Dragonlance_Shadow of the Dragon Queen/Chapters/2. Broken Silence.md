## Back in Vogler

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Back in Vogler|ch. 3, "Back in Vogler"]] to "Voice of War") and **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Part 5, "The Spy" to "Resistance"). 

**Music:** [1-14 - Something is very wrong.](ttrpg-sound:Soundbar/FE-Awakening/1-14 - Something is very wrong..mp3)

The festival is over. Raven, Becklin and Cudgel gather the party at the Brass Crab while Darrett tends the wounded. The night runs like this:

- **The spy.** The captured mercenary is **Svilnt Sunderlit**, a recent recruit and a fanatic. Before the questioning, he slips his bonds and, with a burst of Takhisis's magic, **burns out his own right eye** as tribute to Kansaldi Fire-Eyes. With DC 16 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) he talks:
  - He serves Takhisis, the Dragon Queen, in an army far bigger than anyone here imagines, under **Highmaster Kansaldi Fire-Eyes**.
  - He doesn't know why they want Vogler, and he doesn't ask.
  - Gragonis was paid to turn on Cudgel.
  - Ispin was targeted for asking too many questions about the Dragon Queen and "draconic soldiers".
- **Blame.** The village wants someone to blame, and Cudgel is right there, with the rest of her regiment camped two miles away. Raven can calm the crowd with the party's help (DC 15 Charisma). Either way, Cudgel rides out to check her company's loyalty, and the party may have to get her out of town quietly.
- **Sighting the enemy.** Raven asks the party to scout the woods west of town, where Gragonis met his paymaster. About three hours out, they find a **huge army camp** in red and black. DC 10 group Stealth to get away clean. On a failure, the sentries wave torches, and a single draconian hunts them back toward Vogler (the companion's "pursuing draconian").
- **Cudgel returns** at dawn with her loyal mercenaries and her scout lieutenant, [[Jeyev Veldrews|Jeyev]], who confirms the army. The alarm bell rings when they arrive, and the village panics for nothing.
- **War council.** Becklin, Cudgel and Raven agree they can't fight it. Then **Lord Bakaris** barges in and demands a seat, citing all the money he has "given" Raven, and asks why these suspicious strangers are allowed in the room. Raven gives in, on the condition that he leaves the heroes of High Hill alone.
- **Voice of War.** A Dragon Army messenger rides up to the River Gate, her right eye burnt out like Svilnt's. She carries a letter from **Kansaldi**: Vogler will open its homes to the Red Dragon Army by nightfall, without resistance, and **no one leaves before then**. On her signal, Dragon Army soldiers stand up on the 30-foot cliff above the village.
- **Resistance.** Raven wants to comply. Becklin and Cudgel don't. If nobody suggests taking out the soldiers on the cliff, Cudgel does. Raven is against it (it's a declaration of war) but DC 16 Persuasion or Intimidation brings her round, and then she comes herself with two sharpshooters (scouts).

## Preparing for the Worst

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Preparing for the Worst|ch. 3, "Preparing for the Worst"]] and "Planning to Evacuate") and **Companion** (Part 6, "Increasing Tension" and "Goodbyes").

Whatever happened on the cliff, the army is coming. Becklin pulls the party and Darrett aside if nobody else says it: Vogler can't be defended, and the only hope is to **evacuate** downriver to Kalaman. With her and the party pushing, Raven agrees.

**Not enough boats.** An hour inspecting the wharf shows the boats can carry only **200 of the village's 400 people**. Let the players solve it. There are three good answers, and each one is worth a **+1 villagers** if the party leads it:
- **Boat made of boats:** DC 16 Wisdom (Survival) to lash rings of small boats around floating log rafts (200 more people).
- **Personal fleet:** DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) to get fishers to bring out every leaky skiff and rowboat they own.
- **Repurposed ferry:** DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) to see that the ferry's rafts and pulleys can be broken down in two hours (100 more).

Raven addresses the village at the circle bell. The villagers pack what they can carry.

**Increasing tension** (companion d4, as they work):
1. A child brings the party water and asks, crying, why the soldiers want to hurt everyone.
2. Two men brawl over a place on the same boat for their families.
3. A raft falls apart at the wharf, and people call it a bad omen.
4. A roar from the far north, enormous, silences the whole village.

**Battle plans.** Cudgel returns in the afternoon. The Ironclad Regiment, Becklin with it, will meet the army at the narrow pass between the river and the eastern cliffs to buy time. The party, Darrett and Raven stay to get the village out.

**Becklin's request.** In private (passive Insight to notice she's holding something back), Becklin thanks the party and asks them to fetch a **wooden box from Thornwall Keep** and give it to Darrett, unopened, if she doesn't come back. It's his armour.

**Goodbyes.** Darrett asks to ride with her. She orders him to stay. Then she drops the soldier for a moment, and the two of them say goodbye properly. [If Ispin's shield already speaks to Edric, Ispin asks to say goodbye to Becklin and Cudgel through him.]

## Scenario 1: Battle of Vogler

> [!wok] Source: **WoK** scenario 1 (placed by the module in [[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Invasion of Vogler|ch. 3]]), with **Homebrew** outcomes for Becklin's fate per result (see [[Becklin Uth Viharin]]). Jeyev's betrayal is the **Module**'s "Assassination" option ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Lost in Battle|ch. 3, "Lost in Battle"]]; see [[Jeyev Veldrews]]).

> [!info] New system: Warriors of Krynn
> The first mass battle. Give it its own session if you can, and nothing else new that night ([[Playtest Ideas#Introduction Schedule]]).

### Setup

The scenario setup can more or less be read directly from the board game introduction, with Becklin explaining that the defenders consist of roughly 150 mercenaries under Cudgel's command, hastily assembled and poorly equipped compared to the incoming threat. Their plan is to hold the dragon army at a narrow coastal pass between the sea and the eastern cliffs, where the terrain forces the enemy into a bottleneck and partially offsets their numerical disadvantage. The objective is not to win a decisive battle, but to delay the advance long enough for the villagers of Vogler to evacuate safely inland and, if possible, regroup with the defenders later once a defensible position can be re-established.

### Resolution

**Win:** If the battle is won, the draconian army breaks and begins to retreat, and the mercenaries break into cheering at what seems like a hard-fought victory. But Becklin's relief vanishes when she spots smoke and banners on the horizon, followed by a warhorn and a much larger force cresting the hills—an overwhelming host, many times the size of the original army, advancing toward the village. Cudgel successfully withdraws with the remaining mercenaries, and Becklin has a chance to survive and later reappear at Wheelwatch Outpost.

**Hold:** If the battle is still ongoing, the frontline remains locked in brutal combat when Becklin sees the same smoke, banners, and warhorn in the distance. The reinforcements are coming, and she knows the fight is about to become impossible to sustain, so she orders the players and any others near her to pull back toward Vogler and prepare for full evacuation while she, Cudgel, and the mercenaries hold the line as long as they can. Offscreen, in the chaos of the continuing battle, Becklin is too focused on the enemy advance to notice Jeyev drawing a hidden blade and stabbing her in the back, killing her before the retreat is fully completed, while Cudgel manages to survive and withdraw with the remaining forces.

**Loss:** If the battle is lost, the mercenaries are already retreating when Becklin spots the approaching reinforcements. The advancing force rolls over the field as the retreat turns into a rout, and the enemy begins overrunning the fleeing defenders. Becklin calls for an immediate withdrawal to Vogler, tells them with tears in her eyes to pass on her pride to Darrett, and sends them to retrieve something from the wooden box in Thornwall Keep. Offscreen, as the rout collapses, Becklin is stabbed in the back by Jeyev and killed in the confusion, while Cudgel also falls in battle during the final overrun.

In all cases, Becklin makes one final, unchanging request before sending the characters away: she tells them exactly what to say to Darrett—that she is proud of him, no matter what comes next—and insists they take the wooden box from Thornwall Keep and deliver it into his care, without opening it or questioning its contents.

## Evacuation

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Invasion of Vogler|ch. 3, "Invasion of Vogler"]]: the invasion encounters, Fewmaster Gholcag and the final boat).

### The Invasion Begins

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Part 6, "The Invasion Begins") and **Module** ("News from the Front", "Invasion Encounters").

An Ironclad scout gallops in through the River Gate shouting that the line has broken, and a baaz drops off the cliff onto him. Minutes later, the whole village sees the Red Dragon Army come over the cliffs to the north, with **Fewmaster Gholcag**, a hulking ogre, at its head. Only half the villagers are on the boats. Darrett draws his sword and asks the party if they're with him, and they take up position at the start of map 3.3.

**Invasion encounters** (module). Run up to three of these on the way to the wharf, or one-turn them:
- **Airborne assassin:** a kapak glides down from the cliffs onto a character (passive Perception 14 to notice).
- **Direct attack:** five draconians with torches burst out between the houses.
- **Vogler militia:** two militia struggle against a pair of baaz, who are toying with them.
- **Ominous riders:** whoever has the highest passive Perception glimpses dark riders on the ridge, watching. [These are Soth's black rose riders, see [[Soth's Host]] (homebrew).]

**Invasion chaos** (companion, d8 on initiative 10 and 0, no repeats):
1. A villager the party knows (Elthar, Froswin, Nesau) meets a character's eyes just before a draconian blade comes through their back.
2. A house a block away goes up in flames: the boilerdrak is on the other side of it.
3. Arrows sail overhead into the villagers on the wharf.
4. The great roar from earlier, close now, shakes the dock and knocks villagers into the water.
5. A makeshift boat falls apart as it pushes off, and the others haul its people aboard.
6. A tug on a character's sleeve: the child who gave them a flower crown, terrified, until Raven scoops them up.
7. Leedara stands alone in the flames. When the character blinks, she's gone.
8. Three villagers kiss their families and jump off a boat to hold the draconians back. Unless the party steps in, they're dead by the end of the third encounter.

> [!check] Objective: the last boats
> *Source: **Homebrew**.*
>
> The sack of Vogler is chaos, not a battle line, so every villager on the wharf is a single person on the map. It's normal combat, with an objective running through all of it, from the invasion encounters to Gholcag.
> - **The villagers.** Put **12 villager tokens** between the village and the wharf (use the [[commoner-xmm|commoner]] stat block, but they only move and Dash). On each villager's turn, they run for the boats, unless something is in the way or they're too frightened to move.
> - **Getting them aboard.** A villager who reaches the end of the pier is safe. A character can spend their move escorting one (the villager moves with them), or an action to talk a frozen one into running (DC 10 Persuasion or Intimidation).
> - **The draconians** go for whoever's nearest, characters and villagers alike. A villager hit once is down, and a character can get them up with an action (DC 10 Medicine), or carry them.
> - **The clock:** the last boat leaves after **round 6** of the Gholcag fight, whoever is aboard.
> - **Faction:** count the villagers who made it. **8 or more** counts as "getting everyone onto the boats" (**+1 Vogler Refugees**). **4 or fewer** counts as "villagers left behind" (**-1**).

### Déjà Vu

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, the payoff of [[0. Premonition]].

**When:** during the boilerdrak fight (above), the moment it fires its blast or blows up. The blast is the one from the dream. Edric is thrown down, whether the blast hits him or not, and comes to in the rubble with his ears ringing, just as he did in the dream. The other characters are still there this time, picking themselves up nearby.

> The ringing fades, and the screaming comes in under it. Smoke goes up the street the wrong way, the way it did in the dream. Up on the cliffs, the old keep stands black against the fire. To your left there is a beam, and a doorway, and a hand reaching out from under it. Even the boy's face is the same.

Run it as a real scene, with real rolls, and the same three choices. Check which one Edric's player chose in the Premonition:
- **If he makes the same choice,** it goes the way the dream did, unless the party does something the dream didn't have (another hero joins in, a spell, a combo). The dream only showed him alone.
- **If he chooses differently,** it can work: a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check to lift or drag, or holding the line for 2 rounds against a **Baaz** to stand guard.

If the boy lives, he's one of Vogler's refugees from then on, and he never forgets the knight's face. **+1 Vogler Refugees.** So the dream can be wrong, and Edric knows it. If the boy dies, he knows something worse.

Either way, the rest of the dream is still out there: the companions dead in the rubble, and the thing in the sky. It's just a little less certain now.

### Kansaldi Arrives: Raven's Death

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Part 6, "Kansaldi Arrives" and Part 2, "A Tragic Loss"), with **Homebrew** dialogue for the fanatic version of Kansaldi ([[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]).

Once Gholcag falls and the party scrambles onto the last boat with Raven, Darrett, Jeyev and the last villagers, the companion's grand entrance happens. Run it like this:

- **The dive.** Out of the smoke over the river comes a red dragon, **Ignia**, with Kansaldi on her back. She goes for the boat. If you like, she aims for one of the characters and Raven shoves them aside, and is caught instead.
- **The bridge.** Ignia carries Raven to the end of **Vogler's Crossing**, the unfinished bridge, and lands on the last arch, so the whole flotilla can see. Kansaldi stands in the saddle with Raven in her fist and preaches, laughing and crying at once: "Look, children! Look how she loved you! Kneel, and it can all stop!" Raven spits at her.
- **The fire.** Ignia burns her on the arch. Raven's whistle falls into the river, or lands on the deck at someone's feet.
- **Why not the boats?** Kansaldi wants the refugees to reach Kalaman and tell everyone what they saw (companion). She flies off laughing.

Don't make this a fight. The party can't reach the bridge in time, and Ignia is far beyond them. This is the moment the players learn Kansaldi's name, and get a reason to want her dead.
