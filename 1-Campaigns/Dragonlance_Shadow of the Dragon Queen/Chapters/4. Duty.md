## Overview

The party follows the Dragon Army into the Northern Wastes, the Cataclysm's open wound, to learn what Lord Soth, Lohezet and Belephaion want with the City of Lost Names. The fleet is ambushed in the fog on the crossing, the supply ship goes down, and the army limps into Wrecker's Edge with a few days' food. Darrett stays to mend the ships. The party goes on alone, with only what they carried. From there it's a race: the army starves while they hunt for the city, and every location below is a danger, a clue and a chance to eat and drink for another week.

## Introduction

### The Deadline

*Source: **Homebrew**. The module's Wastes have no timer, and this one exists to keep the party from waiting out the ship repairs.*

The Dragon Army needs more than the Cataclysm's fire to raise the dead dragons under Onyari. It needs the right night: when **Nuitari and Lunitari stand full together**, the black moon and the red, the Dragon Queen's colours. That's the night the ritual can be done, and the dead will rise and fly.

**How the party learns it.** In the catacombs, as Soth takes the fire, he tells them, out of contempt:

> "Run home, little knights. Count your moons. When the black and the red stand together over the Wastes, the dead of the old world will rise and fly, and then you may come and kneel to them."

Afterwards, the date can be pinned down:
- **[[Davos Bloodbane|Davos]]** can work it out with a DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) check, or it comes to him in a dream: Nuitari and Lunitari full over a city of bones. If he's working on Moon Charms, give him advantage.
- **Wyhan** or **the Mages** can confirm it as a favour, or for free if Mages Standing is Trusted or better.
- **Lohezet's notes**, a captured officer, or Dalamar's research in the Wastes all point to the same night.

**How long.** Set the night **about 45 days** after the party lands at Wrecker's Edge. A party that does three or four locations and doesn't dawdle should get there with a week or two to spare. Tell the players the date. The point isn't to catch them out, it's to make the Wastes the only road.

**Why they can't wait it out.** The ships need weeks of work, and the army's food runs out long before that. Sailing home to resupply and coming back would take longer than they have. Whatever happens, someone has to cross the Wastes now.

**If they're late.** It isn't game over. The ritual has begun when they arrive: the plateau shakes, bone dragons stir in the canyon walls, and dead dragons claw out of the ground during Scenario 7 (add a dracolich-touched **young red dragon zombie**, or reflavour an extra dragonnel as a bone dragon, each round from round 3). Chapter 6 opens with the city already trembling. They can still stop it, but it'll cost more.

> [!tip] The countdown
> Keep a running count of **days left** next to the Supply and Water. Mention it every morning in one line: "Thirty-one days to the black and the red." Davos can always tell them how many nights are left.

### Leaving Kalaman

*Source: **Module** ([[06-chapter-5-the-northern-wastes#Desperation in Kalaman|ch. 5, "Desperation in Kalaman"]]). The faction bullets are **Homebrew** ([[Faction Rules]]).*

Run the module's "Desperation in Kalaman" (the debriefing with Vendri, Lord Bakaris blaming the party, the library and Wyhan's message), then settle the factions before the ships leave:

- **Dividends.** The Guilds pay their **one final dividend** under their new leaders, and the Mages pay theirs for the Wastes (see [[Faction Rules#Dividends]]). Add everything up as **Supply** (food), **Water** and gear.
- **Last favours.** Any faction at Trusted or better can be asked for one last favour. Point the players at the "Ways the Party Can Fight Back" table in [[4. Duty - Survival]]. This is their last chance to buy a still, a map or a mule.
- **Who's on board.** Cudgel and Tatina volunteer as in the module. Tatina gives the party one pair of fargabs and keeps the other with Darrett.
- **Wurm hears the plan.** Darrett briefs the party on the route and the landing at Wrecker's Edge. Make sure Wurm is in the room. Takhisis is listening (see [[Wurm (DM)]]).

## Scenario 6: Dark Moon Rises

*Source: **WoK** scenario 6 (the module doesn't reference it). The ambush on the crossing and the sinking of the supply ship are **Homebrew** ([[4. Duty - Survival#Cut Off]]). The module's crossing is uneventful.*

The night crossing, run as described in "Cut Off" in [[4. Duty - Survival]]. Nuitari is full and invisible, Solinari and Lunitari are both dark, and the fleet sails into a fog bank in the middle of the bay. If you're using *Warriors of Krynn*, this is where its mass-combat scenario belongs. Otherwise, run it as a set piece aboard the party's ship.

> The lanterns go out one by one as the fog takes them, until all that is left of the fleet is the creak of other hulls somewhere in the grey, and a sailor singing softly to keep his nerve. Then the singing stops. Out of the fog, close enough to touch, comes a black sail, and then another, and the air fills with the hiss of fire arrows.

**The target.** The black ships go for the **supply ship**. The party's ship is alongside it, and they can see it being boarded and set alight. Let them try to save it: cross on grappling lines, fight on its burning deck, and fail. It goes down whatever they do. What they can save is a few crates: each round they spend fighting on its deck after the second wave, they haul back **1d6 Supply** or **1d6 Water**, but they lose the 1d4 × 5 roll from [[4. Duty - Survival]] twice over if anyone is still aboard when it sinks.

**Round by round.** Run three waves of boarders. Between waves, roll a d6: 1–2 the fire spreads (put it out within 2 rounds, or the supply ship's hold goes up), 3–4 a dragonnel strafes the deck (DC 14 Dex save, 2d10 fire), 5–6 a troopship nearby takes a fire arrow in the rigging, close enough to hear the screaming.

Where the supply ship was, there is nothing but barrels and spars and one green banner of Kalaman turning slowly on the swell. Ahead, red cliffs rise out of the water.

The fleet limps into the cove at **Wrecker's Edge**.

## The Starving Camp

*Source: **Homebrew**. It replaces the module's "Kalaman Troops" section until the army marches.*

On the beach, Darrett does the sums out loud, and they're bad: a few days' rations for a few hundred soldiers, ships that need weeks of work, and black sails patrolling the bay. He stays at Wrecker's Edge with the soldiers and the engineers to mend the fleet, with Cudgel rationing the water with an axe. He can't spare the party a single ration, and he tells them so. What he needs is for them to find what the Dragon Army is doing out here, and to do it fast.

> [!warning] The clock
> The army runs out of food **10 days** after landing. After that, it loses soldiers every day, and the camp gets uglier: desertions, fights over the water, a soldier hanged for theft. Mark the days. Tatina's fargabs let Darrett report how it's going, and he's honest.

**Sending food back.** Anything the party finds and doesn't need can go back to the coast: carried there, sent with Heart's Hollow scouts, or brought by Clystran. The camp has a brackish spring, so it's short of food, not water.
- For every **20 Supply** sent, the army holds out **5 more days**, and **+1 Kalaman Military** (at most +2 in total).
- The richest sources are Camp Carrionclay's storehouse and Heart's Hollow's goodwill. A shipment from Carrionclay is the moment the camp cheers the party's name.
- If the party sends **nothing** and the clock runs out, the army fights Scenario 7 half-starved: every Kalaman soldier stat block has 1 level of exhaustion, and Darrett asks the party to their faces where the food went.

**The march.** When the party finds the City of Lost Names, they bring Darrett word (or call him on the fargabs), and the army marches to meet them, leaving the engineers to finish the ships. Heart's Hollow scouts guiding the army (module) let it move at normal pace. From then on, use the module's army camp rules, but every camp is a hungry one.

> [!example] Skirmish option: raid on the beached fleet
> *Source: **Homebrew** ([[Skirmish Battles]]).*
>
> Once in the chapter, ideally when the party comes back to the coast with food, a Dragon Army force finds the beached ships (thanks to the Leak, below).
> - **Objective:** keep the ships from burning for **5 rounds**. Each enemy unit that ends its turn touching a ship sets it alight, and every burning ship adds a week to the repairs.
> - **Allies:** 2 **Infantry** (hungry, so -2 Morale if the camp has run out of food), 1 **Siege Crew** (the engineers have turned a ship's ballista round), and Cudgel's **Mercenaries**.
> - **Enemies:** 2 **Baaz**, 1 **Kapaks** gliding down the cliffs, and 1 **Dragonnel Riders** with fire pots.
> - **The beach:** units in the surf are on **bad footing**.

## The Leak

*Source: **Homebrew** ([[Wurm (DM)]]).*

Takhisis hears through Wurm until Wurm's story says otherwise. Whenever the party makes a plan in front of him, she can use it **once per location**: the Dragon Army patrol is waiting where they planned to camp, the draconians at Bluemaw have been warned, or Akhviri arrives at Carrionclay early. Don't overdo it. Two or three times in the chapter is enough for the players to start looking at each other.

## Locations

*Source: **Module** ([[06-chapter-5-the-northern-wastes#Exploring the Wastes|ch. 5, "Exploring the Wastes"]]) for every site and encounter. The water and food, ties and extra payoffs in each list are **Homebrew** ([[4. Duty - Survival]] and the origin characters' DM notes).*

Every location has three things worth marking: **water** (or food), **a reason to go there**, and **a tie** to one of the characters. Locations are lettered as on map 5.1.

### A: Wrecker's Edge

Run as in the module: the party scouts the coast for a landing and finds the smugglers' cove, the Silvanesti ship and its two air elementals, and Dalamar. The cove becomes **Darrett's camp** for the rest of the chapter.

- **Water.** The smugglers' huts hide a stone cistern of stale rainwater: **20 Water**, once. Barrels from the supply ship wash ashore over the first day: **1d4 × 5 Supply**, salt-soaked but edible, and Darrett's quartermaster claims all of it for the army unless someone argues.
- **The elven ship** is the only seaworthy, unburned vessel on this coast, and the players will want it, to fetch food from Kalaman. Dalamar won't give it up, the air elementals guard it, and stealing it strands the elves. Even if they take it, the black sails still patrol the bay. Let them be tempted.
- **Dalamar** is fed by his own magic, and he trades: a casting of *create food and water* (15 Supply and 30 Water) for news of Kalaman, or for an hour alone with Davos.
- **Tie: [[Davos Bloodbane|Davos]].** Dalamar sees what Davos is at once, a sorcerer with raw, unschooled power, and he's fascinated. He offers to teach Davos "the things Antimodes won't". It's real knowledge, and it's a black robe's offer. If Davos accepts, give him one free moon charm and 1 point of *Heat*.

### B: Elven Encampment

Zhelsuel's exiles from House Mystic, cataloguing every rock in the Wastes. Run as in the module. Dalamar's arrangement (sending the party to C, D and E) is the chapter's spine.

- **Water and food.** The elves make what they need by magic and share nothing with outsiders. If Zhelsuel is won over (Elvish, DC 16 Persuasion, or Thorn), they fill every waterskin the party has and give them **quith-pa**, elven travel bread: **10 Supply** that weighs only half a pound a ration.
- **Tie: [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn)|Thorn]].** Zhelsuel was his teacher, as near to an uncle as a prince could have. Zhelsuel doesn't recognise the scarred, white-haired thing in front of him, at least not at first. Give Thorn the scene and let him choose (see [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]]):
  - **Reveal himself.** Zhelsuel breaks. The camp's doors open: free water and quith-pa every visit, the elves' maps (advantage on navigation within 2 hexes of the camp), and a dozen elven mages for Scenario 7, if Zhelsuel can be persuaded the war is Silvanesti's war too. And now the exiles know a Caladon prince is alive, and so will Alhana, eventually.
  - **Stay nameless.** Zhelsuel says, kindly, that Thorn reminds him of a boy he once taught. That's all.
- **Tie: Davos.** Zhelsuel is contemptuous of a human with untrained magic, and says so.

### C: Blue Phoenix Shrine

Habbakuk's flooded shrine and the Dimernesti sea elves. Run as in the module.

- **Water.** The brass basin in C3 is fresh water, and it refills every dawn: **1d4 + 2 Water** a day, taken with a prayer. Anyone who takes it without one wakes the water weirds in C2. The party can camp in the gorge for as long as the Wash stays low.
- **The sea elves** know which ravines carry fresh springs at low Wash. If the party helps Ishvern, he marks them on the map: **advantage on foraging checks** for the rest of the chapter.
- **Habbakuk's Blessing** (module) for everyone who honours the shrine.
- **Tie: Thorn.** Habbakuk is one of Thorn's gods. When he washes in the basin, a sea bird lands on the phoenix statue and watches him. Ishvern asks him, puzzled, why the shrine has never been so bright.

### D: Sunward Fortress

Sirrion's toppled lighthouse, the Spawning Shard, Virruza and the slaadi. Run as in the module.

- **Food.** The abandoned camp holds dried meat: **10 Supply**. There's no water. The D3 crates hold rope, oil and lanterns.
- **Shelter.** The D1 altar's flame, once lit with a prayer, warms the chamber all night: a long rest there counts as **sheltered** on a bitter cold night, if anyone dares sleep with the slaadi below.
- **Rone and Yearkal.** Healing Rone and walking him home earns his guidance to Heart's Hollow. Yearkal reunites with the sea elves at C.
- **Tie: [[Wurm]].** Virruza's notes aren't only about slaadi. Belephaion's orders mention "the egg-work": draconians are made from **the eggs of good dragons**, corrupted. One sloppy line near the end reads "The Queen's first is still out there. The silver one." Whoever reads the notes aloud should be in the room with Wurm.
- **Tie: [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge|Edric]].** Rone is dying, a stranger who's no use to anyone. Carrying him home is the most knightly thing in this whole chapter.

### E: Wakenreth

The House of Silence, the tower that fell off Onyari, half sunk in the Shadowfell. Run as in the module, then meet Dalamar here as he directs.

- **Shelter.** Once the portal is closed, Wakenreth is the safest place in the Wastes: cool by day, warm by night, and lit. Long rests here count as sheltered, and the restored tower grants advantage on the Constitution save against exhaustion. There's no water.
- **Tie: Davos.** The second crypt in E2 holds a dead elf in the **white robes of a Mage of High Sorcery**, the second set Davos has found by stealing. Whether he takes them is up to him.
- **Tie: [[Milo Geartickle|Milo]].** Wakenreth came from Onyari. The drone goes mad with lights the moment it sees the tower, and calms only at the top, pointing north. This is the first sign that home is close.
- **Tie: Thorn.** Silvanesti tradition demands a silver coin on the guardian's pedestal. Thorn knows it without being told, and he's the only one who does.

### F: Bluemaw Cave

The old blue dragon's lair, the Migan clan, the gricks and the draconians digging out the hoard. Run as in the module.

- **Food and shelter.** Once the draconians are gone, the dwarves feed and shelter the party for as long as they like. Treat the cave like a settlement: rest, top up to **15 Supply**, refill every waterskin from the dwarves' seep in the rock, and no daily rolls while they're here.
- **Grasha's still.** Grasha is a gnomish-engineering enthusiast, and the draconians' gear is full of good brass. Give her two days and she builds a **gnomish still** (see [[4. Duty - Survival]]), if the party doesn't already have one. Her warning: "It works. Mostly. Don't stand behind it."
- **Bluemaw salt.** The blue crystals preserve meat for months. Butchered beasts give **double Supply** if packed in Bluemaw salt.
- **Tie: Milo.** Grasha and Milo get on terrifyingly well. A day working on the drone with her gives Milo advantage on their next drone tinkering check.
- **Tie: Wurm.** The draconians here are his kind, digging out a dragon's bones. If any of them is spared, it's the first draconian Wurm has spoken to in his own tongue since he left the army. Use his level 7 moment.

### G: Heart's Hollow

The hidden crater village, Clystran and Ness. It's the one real town in the Wastes. Run as in the module, and start with Clystran and the giant spiders as the Wash rises.

- **Resupply.** Everything is scarce. End's Odds sells Supply and Water at **5 gp each** (a cutthroat price, and End knows it), and the town will gift up to **10 Supply** and all the Water they can carry to anyone who has earned a stone in the Mosaic. The crater's deep well is the only sweet water for fifty miles. The Fort can spare a mule for a favour.
- **Lavender's** tinctures: a draught that lets someone ignore one level of exhaustion for a day (25 gp, and it costs 1 extra Water that night).
- **Clystran** as a guide doubles travel distance and skips navigation (see [[4. Duty - Survival]]), once the party has fed his dragonnels at H.
- **Tie: Milo.** This is where Milo spent those lost months. Let the memories come back in a rush:
  - Halfway along the **Mosaic** there's a stone painted with a crooked little drone. Milo put it there.
  - **End Pike** has very large, patient hands. He taught Milo forgework, and he greets them by name: "Took you long enough, you little thief."
  - **Ness** found Milo half dead in the canyons years ago, and it was her who took the memories, gently, to keep the Hollow's secret. Milo saw her true shape once. She is the bronze dragon. Whether she admits it depends on the egg from Carrionclay.
  - This is also the moment to reveal who Milo promised never to return alone (see [[Milo Geartickle (DM)]]).

### H: Dragonnel Spire

Twelve wasteland dragonnels on a rock shaped like a dragon. Run as in the module, with Clystran.

- **Water.** A rain pool in the dragon rock's "eye", far above the canyon floor, filled by the rare storms: **2d6 Water**, once, if someone climbs for it (DC 15 Athletics, and the dragonnels watch).
- **Payoff.** Clystran becomes the party's guide.
- **Tie: Wurm.** The dragonnels won't go near anyone but Clystran, except Wurm. They come right up to him and sniff, then back away, unsettled, as if they can't decide what he is.

### I: Camp Carrionclay

The Dragon Army's waypoint on the muddy lake, Fewmaster Zirrok, the prisoners and Akhviri. Run as in the module. If the party is beaten by a patrol, this is where they wake up.

- **The storehouse.** It's a supply camp, the best-stocked place in the Wastes: **40 Supply** in crates, and **40 Water** in barrels along the walls. Stealing it is a heist, and carrying it out is a problem.
- **The lake** is salt, and full of quippers when the Wash is high.
- **Prisoners.** The three sea elves (for Ishvern), Kennah (who takes the party home to Heart's Hollow), and a bulette nobody should free.
- **The bronze dragon egg**, meant for Belephaion.
- **Tie: Wurm.** After Virruza's notes, Wurm knows exactly what Belephaion wants with the egg: another one like him. Saving it should feel like saving himself.
- **Tie: Milo.** Use Milo's level 7 moment: they carry the egg, it's warm, and it ticks in time with the drone. Give it to Ness for the bronze dragon scale mail, and Ness's secret.

## Scenario 7: Break the Walls

*Source: **Module** ([[06-chapter-5-the-northern-wastes#K: City of Lost Names|ch. 5, "K: City of Lost Names"]]) and **WoK** scenario 7. The faction payoffs are **Homebrew**.*

The assault on the canyon wall, while the party slips through Wind's End. This needs **Darrett's army** to march from Wrecker's Edge (see "The Starving Camp"). If the party never sends word, there's no distraction, and they'll have to find another way in.

Run the module's "The Allied Army" and "Assaulting the Dragon Army". With *Warriors of Krynn*, play its scenario 7. Without it, run "Attack on Wind's End" and its battlefield events table.

**Faction payoffs here:**
- **Kalaman Military** at Trusted or better: Darrett holds the line long enough. At Wary or worse, the attack starts to break after 3 rounds: roll on the battlefield events table twice each round.
- **Thorn's exiles**, if Zhelsuel joined: elven mages strike the dragonnels overhead, so ignore battlefield event results of 1–4.
- **Fed or starving** (see "The Starving Camp").

```encounter
name: Wind's End
creatures:
  - "3": Dragon Army Officer (DSotDQ)
  - "1": Dragon Army Dragonnel (DSotDQ)
```

The characters reach **8th level** when they find the passage behind the loose boards. Chapter 6 begins in the dark beyond it.
