## The City Rises

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#The City Rises|ch. 6, "The City Rises"]]). The level 10 moments are **Homebrew**.

Run the evacuation and Soth's ascent as written. The Bastion of Takhisis rises on its dragon bones as the first flying citadel. Then run the escape through the breaking city: escaped dragonnels, floating rocks, the ill-fated ambush. Use **bad footing** for weapons.

Use the escape for the **level 10 moments**:
- **A kender PC** walks the maze out from memory, every turn, out loud.
- **A ranger PC** follows birdsong where no birds should be.
- **A knight PC** carries the slowest of them out, and his mare is waiting outside, somewhere no horse could have reached.
- **A moon-touched PC**: if it's the Night of the Eye, he's the most powerful mage on Krynn for one night ([[Davos Bloodbane (DM)#The Night of the Eye]]). If not, he gets them out on skill alone.
- **A dragonborn PC** faces Lohezet, or carries the wounded out while the voice tells him they owe him.

- **Map:** a broken street in the rising city, on a blank board
- **Terrain:** floating rocks at different heights, cracks and drops, and a boulder token for the falling-rock event.

```encounter
name: Ill-Fated Ambush
creatures:
  - "3": Kapak Draconian (DSotDQ)
  - "1": Sivak Draconian (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Gear only, if anything doesn't float away.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Yes, mid-escape. The floating rocks and falling boulders are the fun part.

Once they're clear of the city, go straight to **Scenario 8** in [[6.00 Conquest]].
