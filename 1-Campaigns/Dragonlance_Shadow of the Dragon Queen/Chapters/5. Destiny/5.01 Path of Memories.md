## Path of Memories

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Path of Memories|ch. 6, "Path of Memories"]], areas M1–M9). The level 8 moments are **Homebrew** (each character's DM notes).

Run the buried district as written. It's a tight, wet, haunted place, so use the **tight spaces** and **underwater** situations for weapons.

Use the passage for the **level 8 moments**:
- **A knight PC** writes the letter to Ispin on night watch and burns it.
- **A moon-touched PC** finds a moon calendar carved in the walls and works out the next Night of the Eye.
- **A tinkerer PC** rings an ancient anvil in the **Wizard's Workshop (M8)**. It rings far too long, and the sparks come off grey. This is where the ring was forged (see below).
- **A Silvanesti PC** sings in the dark to keep the party moving, and the echo comes back in more voices than his.
- **A dragonborn PC** touches a metallic dragon carved in the wall, and his black scales reflect for a heartbeat.

### Demelin

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Demelin|ch. 6, "Demelin"]]), with **Homebrew** ties.

The red-robed high wizard of Onyari tests the party with an air elemental, then talks. Run her as written, and add:

- **A kender PC's ring.** She recognises it at a glance: one of the Kingpriest's death-cheating rings, forged in her own city. It can only be unmade at the forge in **M8**, and unmaking it **releases every life it ever took**. That debt has to land somewhere. Either the tinkerer PC pays it, and every borrowed death catches up with them at once, or the party finds another way (a *wish*, a god's mercy, a sacrifice). See [[Milo Geartickle (DM)]]. Let the tinkerer PC decide here or carry the ring on. Either answer is a real ending for their arc.
- **A Silvanesti PC.** Demelin is Silvanesti, old enough to remember Silvanost before the Cataclysm. She knows a Caladon face under the scars, and says nothing unless he speaks first. If he asks, she can tell him the Orbs of Dragonkind were Istar's work too, and what they do to the people who use them.
- **A spellcaster PC's Test.** See below.

- **Map:** [[map-6.01-path-of-memories-player.png|Map 6.1: Path of Memories]] (module), area M9
- **Terrain:** Demelin's apartment: old lamps and stuffed chairs, a door to the east.

```encounter
name: Demelin's Assessment
creatures:
  - "1": Air Elemental (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): The air elemental leaves **1d4+1 vials of air essence** (double jumps and +10 ft speed for a minute) and arcane dust.

> [!check] **Survive and impress.** Demelin calls it off after 3 rounds, or sooner if someone does something clever. Nobody needs to win.

### A spellcaster PC's Test of High Sorcery

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Test of High Sorcery|ch. 6, "Test of High Sorcery"]]). The personalised test is **Homebrew** ([[Davos Bloodbane (DM)]]).

Demelin offers the Test to a spellcaster PC, and a kender PC can sit the same crossroads if they want robes. Use the module's structure: a vision of Onyari's last day, a magical challenge and a moral choice. For the spellcaster PC, build it from his story:

- **The vision.** He wakes in Onyari as it falls, wearing the white robes he stole from Antimodes. Below the falling city, in the burning streets, is the Relgoth bathhouse guard who died for those robes.
- **The challenge.** A green dragon circles the city, and every Bloodbane who ever served it stands on the rooftops, cheering it on. To save the city's people, he has to fight the thing his family served.
- **The choice.** He can save his mother, brother and sister, who are somehow in the vision too. Or he can save a crowd of strangers. Or he can take the dragon's offer, made in his family's name: power, and the old Bloodbane fortune back.
- **The robes.** White, red or black, as the module describes. If the "sliver of white" in his hair has grown over the campaign, the vision leans toward white, but the choice is his.

**Passing** gives **+2 Mages of High Sorcery** ([[Faction Rules]]) and full membership. If he refuses the Test or forsakes magic in it, he dies, as the module says. Make sure the player knows that before he starts.

## View of the City

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#View of the City|ch. 6, "View of the City"]] and "Shadow of Soth").

Run as written: the crater city, the floating rocks, the Threshold's spire, and Soth climbing onto a skeletal dragon with the Cataclysm's fire in his hand.

- **A knight PC's shield** goes cold the moment Soth appears, even at that distance.
- **A tinkerer PC's drone** stops dead in the air, turns to face the Threshold of the Heavens, and won't look away. It's home.
