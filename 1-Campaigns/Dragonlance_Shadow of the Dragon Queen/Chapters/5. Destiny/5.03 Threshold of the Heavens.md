## Threshold of the Heavens

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Threshold of the Heavens|ch. 6, "Threshold of the Heavens"]], T1–T10), with **Homebrew** ties.

Run the tower as written. Add:

- **T5: Drone Monitoring.** This is where a tinkerer PC's drone was made. It flies to the charging columns, docks, and lights up in a pattern no one has seen before. Tinkering here with no check lets the tinkerer PC do one of these:
  - **Command the tower's drones** (no Arcana check needed for the console).
  - **Repair their drone completely.**
  - **Read its last orders:** "Retrieve the rings. Return them to the forge." It was never a pet, and now a tinkerer PC knows.
- **T9: Lohezet.** Run his lies and bargains as written. If you've made him the Draevenfall cult's patron (see [[Lohezet]] and [[Wurm (DM)]]), this is where a dragonborn PC meets the man who took **Vrella**, the elf lady. Lohezet knows exactly what the dragonborn PC is, and says so: "The Queen's first. I wondered what became of you." He'll trade Vrella's whereabouts for his life. That's the dragonborn PC's level 10 moment, if you want it.
- **T10: Belephaion.** He's a blue dragon in a priest's crown, and he touches the helm the moment he sees them. Kill him and the tower still falls. The city tears itself apart around them.

- **Map:** [[map-6.05-threshold-of-the-heavens-player.png|Map 6.5: Threshold of the Heavens]] (module), area T5
- **Terrain:** the crystal-banded columns with lightning arcing between them, the 2 docked drones and the rune console.

```encounter
name: Drone Monitoring
creatures:
  - "2": Istarian Drone (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Each drone (Arcana DC 13): **1 Istarian drone eye** (an *eye of enhanced seeing*, or homebrew: a free upgrade for a tinkerer PC's drone), **1d2 claws** (lightning dagger, 150 gp, 9 days), **arcane dust** and **1d6 vials of crystalline spit** (restraining crystal grenade).

> [!tip] **Avoidable.** The drones only wake if someone damages a column, a drone or the console, and a tinkerer PC can command them.

- **Map:** Map 6.5 (module), area T9
- **Terrain:** the crystal scrying panels (one showing Kalaman), the stone desks and the raised platform.

```encounter
name: Lohezet
creatures:
  - "1": Lohezet (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): **2d4 ounces of arcane dust** and his **spellbook** (*arcane eye*, *detect magic*, *dimension door*, *dominate person*, *wall of force*). A spellcaster PC will want it.

> [!tip] **A conversation first.** He fights only if he must, and runs if he's losing.

- **Map:** [[map-6.05-threshold-of-the-heavens-player.png|Map 6.5: Threshold of the Heavens]] (module), area T10
- **Terrain:** the sunburst throne on its dais, the sunken walkways on three sides, and the great windows (the city rising outside them).

```encounter
name: The Bridge
creatures:
  - "1": Belephaion (DSotDQ)
  - "2": Bozak Draconian (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Belephaion (young blue dragon, Survival DC 19, 4 hours: the tower won't wait): **claws**, **fang**, **hide**, **2d4 scales**, **teeth** (+1d6 lightning), **1d2 wings** (a lightning-resistant cloak, 200 gp, 14 days) and **2d6 Supply**. Bozaks: arcane dust.

> [!check] Full fight, a big one. **Objective:** Belephaion dies or yields, and the party gets out of the tower before it tilts. Every round after the city starts rising, roll a d6: on a 1, the floor drops 10 feet.
