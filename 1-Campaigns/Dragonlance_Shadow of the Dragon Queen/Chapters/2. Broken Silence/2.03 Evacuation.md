## Evacuation

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Invasion of Vogler|ch. 3, "Invasion of Vogler"]]: the invasion encounters, Fewmaster Gholcag and the final boat).

### The Invasion Begins

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Part 6, "The Invasion Begins") and **Module** ("News from the Front", "Invasion Encounters").

An Ironclad scout gallops in through the River Gate shouting that the line has broken, and a baaz drops off the cliff onto him. Minutes later, the whole village sees the Red Dragon Army come over the cliffs to the north, with **Fewmaster Gholcag**, a hulking ogre, at its head. Only half the villagers are on the boats. Darrett draws his sword and asks the party if they're with him, and they take up position at the start of map 3.3.

**Invasion encounters** (module). Run up to three of these on the way to the wharf, or one-turn them:
- **Airborne assassin:** a kapak glides down from the cliffs onto a character (passive Perception 14 to notice).
- **Direct attack:** five draconians with torches burst out between the houses.
- **Vogler militia:** two militia struggle against a pair of baaz, who are toying with them.
- **Ominous riders:** whoever has the highest passive Perception glimpses dark riders on the ridge, watching. [These are Soth's black rose riders, see [[Soth's Host]] (homebrew).]

**Invasion chaos** (companion, d8 on initiative 10 and 0, no repeats):
1. A villager the party knows (Elthar, Froswin, Nesau) meets a character's eyes just before a draconian blade comes through their back.
2. A house a block away goes up in flames: the boilerdrak is on the other side of it.
3. Arrows sail overhead into the villagers on the wharf.
4. The great roar from earlier, close now, shakes the dock and knocks villagers into the water.
5. A makeshift boat falls apart as it pushes off, and the others haul its people aboard.
6. A tug on a character's sleeve: the child who gave them a flower crown, terrified, until Raven scoops them up.
7. Leedara stands alone in the flames. When the character blinks, she's gone.
8. Three villagers kiss their families and jump off a boat to hold the draconians back. Unless the party steps in, they're dead by the end of the third encounter.

> [!check] Objective: the last boats
> *Source: **Homebrew**.*
>
> The sack of Vogler is chaos, not a battle line, so every villager on the wharf is a single person on the map. It's normal combat, with an objective running through all of it, from the invasion encounters to Gholcag.
> - **The villagers.** Put **12 villager tokens** between the village and the wharf (use the [[commoner-xmm|commoner]] stat block, but they only move and Dash). On each villager's turn, they run for the boats, unless something is in the way or they're too frightened to move.
> - **Getting them aboard.** A villager who reaches the end of the pier is safe. A character can spend their move escorting one (the villager moves with them), or an action to talk a frozen one into running (DC 10 Persuasion or Intimidation).
> - **The draconians** go for whoever's nearest, characters and villagers alike. A villager hit once is down, and a character can get them up with an action (DC 10 Medicine), or carry them.
> - **The clock:** the last boat leaves after **round 6** of the Gholcag fight, whoever is aboard.
> - **Faction:** count the villagers who made it. **8 or more** counts as "getting everyone onto the boats" (**+1 Vogler Refugees**). **4 or fewer** counts as "villagers left behind" (**-1**).

### Encounter: The Boilerdrak

- **Map:** [[map-3.03-escape-from-vogler-player.png|Map 3.3: Escape from Vogler]] (module)
- **Terrain:** the wharf with 3-4 boats, burning houses (fire markers), barrels and crates for cover, and one collapsed doorway with a beam (for the Déjà Vu scene). Mark a 20-foot track for the boilerdrak toward the wharf.

```encounter
name: The Boilerdrak
creatures:
  - "1": Bozak Draconian (DSotDQ)
  - "4": Baaz Draconian (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Each baaz leaves a **vial of petrifying gas** (throw 30 ft, 5-ft radius, DC 11 Con or restrained, then petrified). The boilerdrak itself is worth a fortune to Tatina.

> [!check] Full fight. **Objective:** stop the boilerdrak before it reaches the wharf (it moves 20 feet toward the boats each round). Its blast triggers **Déjà Vu** below. Every round it spends at the wharf, one boat burns, and that's one family that doesn't make it to Kalaman (**-1 Vogler Refugees** if two or more burn).

**Running the boilerdrak** (companion): it takes the crew **three full actions** to light, aim and fire it, and its flames are a **60-foot cone for 5d10 fire**, which can drop several characters at once. DC 14 Investigation shows the crew are the weak point, not the machine. A character can turn it on the Dragon Army with DC 15 Intelligence (tinker's tools), and then Gholcag goes straight for them.

### Déjà Vu

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, the payoff of [[0.00 Premonition]].

**When:** during the boilerdrak fight (above), the moment it fires its blast or blows up. The blast is the one from the dream. Edric is thrown down, whether the blast hits him or not, and comes to in the rubble with his ears ringing, just as he did in the dream. The other characters are still there this time, picking themselves up nearby.

> The ringing fades, and the screaming comes in under it. Smoke goes up the street the wrong way, the way it did in the dream. Up on the cliffs, the old keep stands black against the fire. To your left there is a beam, and a doorway, and a hand reaching out from under it. Even the boy's face is the same.

Run it as a real scene, with real rolls, and the same three choices. Check which one Edric's player chose in the Premonition:
- **If he makes the same choice,** it goes the way the dream did, unless the party does something the dream didn't have (another hero joins in, a spell, a combo). The dream only showed him alone.
- **If he chooses differently,** it can work: a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check to lift or drag, or holding the line for 2 rounds against a **Baaz** to stand guard.

If the boy lives, he's one of Vogler's refugees from then on, and he never forgets the knight's face. **+1 Vogler Refugees.** So the dream can be wrong, and Edric knows it. If the boy dies, he knows something worse.

Either way, the rest of the dream is still out there: the companions dead in the rubble, and the thing in the sky. It's just a little less certain now.

### Encounter: Fewmaster Gholcag

- **Map:** [[map-3.03-escape-from-vogler-player.png|Map 3.3: Escape from Vogler]] (module), the same board as the boilerdrak
- **Terrain:** the wharf, whatever's left burning after the boilerdrak, the last boat at the end of the pier, and the Fray at the edges.

```encounter
name: Fewmaster Gholcag
creatures:
  - "1": Bozak Draconian (DSotDQ)
  - "3": Baaz Draconian (DSotDQ)
  - "1": Ogre (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Gholcag (ogre): a broken greatclub, broken hide armour, **1d10 giant's toes** (4 gp each) and 1d4 javelins.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Yes, if you ran the boilerdrak in full. Either way, the objective is the boats and the villagers on the wharf, not the Fewmaster.

**Jeyev** (companion): if the party is being overwhelmed, [[Jeyev Veldrews|Jeyev]] staggers in covered in dirt, at half hit points, carrying a Knight of Solamnia's helmet in his off hand. After the fight he tells them the Ironclads were beaten and Cudgel sent him back to make sure no one waited for them. Asked about Becklin, he only hands Darrett the helmet. Its lining is stiff with dried blood.

### Kansaldi Arrives: Raven's Death

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Part 6, "Kansaldi Arrives" and Part 2, "A Tragic Loss"), with **Homebrew** dialogue for the fanatic version of Kansaldi ([[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]).

Once Gholcag falls and the party scrambles onto the last boat with Raven, Darrett, Jeyev and the last villagers, the companion's grand entrance happens. Run it like this:

- **The dive.** Out of the smoke over the river comes a red dragon, **Ignia**, with Kansaldi on her back. She goes for the boat. If you like, she aims for one of the characters and Raven shoves them aside, and is caught instead.
- **The bridge.** Ignia carries Raven to the end of **Vogler's Crossing**, the unfinished bridge, and lands on the last arch, so the whole flotilla can see. Kansaldi stands in the saddle with Raven in her fist and preaches, laughing and crying at once: "Look, children! Look how she loved you! Kneel, and it can all stop!" Raven spits at her.
- **The fire.** Ignia burns her on the arch. Raven's whistle falls into the river, or lands on the deck at someone's feet.
- **Why not the boats?** Kansaldi wants the refugees to reach Kalaman and tell everyone what they saw (companion). She flies off laughing.

Don't make this a fight. The party can't reach the bridge in time, and Ignia is far beyond them. This is the moment the players learn Kansaldi's name, and get a reason to want her dead.
