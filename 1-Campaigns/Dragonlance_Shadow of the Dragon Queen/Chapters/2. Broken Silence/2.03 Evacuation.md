## Evacuation

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Invasion of Vogler|ch. 3, "Invasion of Vogler"]]: the invasion encounters, Fewmaster Gholcag and the final boat).

### The Invasion Begins

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Part 6, "The Invasion Begins") and **Module** ("News from the Front", "Invasion Encounters").

An Ironclad scout gallops in through the River Gate shouting that the line has broken, and a baaz drops off the cliff onto him. Minutes later, the whole village sees the Red Dragon Army come over the cliffs to the north, with **Fewmaster Gholcag**, a hulking ogre, at its head. Only half the villagers are on the boats (a third, if the cliff ambush wasn't clean). Darrett draws his sword and asks the party if they're with him, and they take up position at the start of map 3.3.

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Invasion of Vogler|ch. 3, "News from the Front", "Perilous Evacuation"]]). The rider is a **scout** Becklin sent to warn Vogler to evacuate at once: her horse is dead and she's locked in a fight with a **baaz** who will kill her unless the party steps in. If saved, she says the attack is imminent: the Dragon Army has split in two, one prong engaging the mercenaries and the second heading for the village. Raven orders the evacuation to begin. The villagers are eerily quiet as they climb into the boats, until cloaked figures appear at the clifftop, linger a moment, then step off, and **wings** open from beneath their cloaks to slow their fall. Invaders sweep into Vogler. Keep the party busy with the evacuation (finding missing villagers, corralling boats) for the hour before the news arrives.

**Invasion encounters** (module). Run up to three of these on the way to the wharf, or one-turn them:
- **Airborne assassin:** a kapak glides down from the cliffs onto a character (passive Perception 14 to notice).
- **Direct attack:** five draconians with torches burst out between the houses.
- **Vogler militia:** two militia struggle against a pair of baaz, who are toying with them.
- **Ominous riders:** whoever has the highest passive Perception glimpses dark riders on the ridge, watching. [These are Soth's black rose riders, see [[Soth's Host]] (homebrew).]

**The full table** (module, d10, run at least three, anywhere in the village). After the second, the party hears loud **clanking** from the north: something large and metal, the boilerdrak. A third encounter distracts them before they can investigate.

| d10 | Encounter |

| 3 | An abandoned cat leaps onto a character, terrified, and won't leave them for the whole invasion. |

**Invasion chaos** (companion, d8 on initiative 10 and 0, no repeats):
1. A villager the party knows (Elthar, Froswin, Nesau) meets a character's eyes just before a draconian blade comes through their back.
2. A house a block away goes up in flames: the boilerdrak is on the other side of it.
3. Arrows sail overhead into the villagers on the wharf.
4. The great roar from earlier, close now, shakes the dock and knocks villagers into the water.
5. A makeshift boat falls apart as it pushes off, and the others haul its people aboard.
6. A tug on a character's sleeve: the child who gave them a flower crown, terrified, until Raven scoops them up.
7. Leedara stands alone in the flames. When the character blinks, she's gone.
8. Three villagers kiss their families and jump off a boat to hold the draconians back. Unless the party steps in, they're dead by the end of the third encounter.

> [!check] Objective: the last boats
> *Source: **Homebrew**.*
>
> The sack of Vogler is chaos, not a battle line, so every villager on the wharf is a single person on the map. It's normal combat, with an objective running through all of it, from the invasion encounters to Gholcag.
> - **The villagers.** Put **12 villager tokens** between the village and the wharf (use the [[commoner-xmm|commoner]] stat block, but they only move and Dash). On each villager's turn, they run for the boats, unless something is in the way or they're too frightened to move.
> - **Getting them aboard.** A villager who reaches the end of the pier is safe. A character can spend their move escorting one (the villager moves with them), or an action to talk a frozen one into running (DC 10 Persuasion or Intimidation).
> - **The draconians** go for whoever's nearest, characters and villagers alike. A villager hit once is down, and a character can get them up with an action (DC 10 Medicine), or carry them.
> - **The clock:** the last boat leaves after **round 6** of the Gholcag fight, whoever is aboard.
> - **Faction:** count the villagers who made it. **8 or more** counts as "getting everyone onto the boats" (**+1 Vogler Refugees**). **4 or fewer** counts as "villagers left behind" (**-1**).

### Encounter: The Boilerdrak

- **Map:** [[map-3.03-escape-from-vogler-player.png|Map 3.3: Escape from Vogler]] (module)
- **Terrain:** the wharf with 3-4 boats, burning houses (fire markers), barrels and crates for cover, and one collapsed doorway with a beam (for the Déjà Vu scene). Mark a 20-foot track for the boilerdrak toward the wharf.

```encounter
name: The Boilerdrak
creatures:
  - "1": Bozak Draconian (DSotDQ)
  - "4": Baaz Draconian (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Each baaz leaves a **vial of petrifying gas** (throw 30 ft, 5-ft radius, DC 11 Con or restrained, then petrified). The boilerdrak itself is worth a fortune to Tatina.

> [!check] Full fight. **Objective:** stop the boilerdrak before it reaches the wharf (it moves 20 feet toward the boats each round). Its blast triggers **Déjà Vu** below. Every round it spends at the wharf, one boat burns, and that's one family that doesn't make it to Kalaman (**-1 Vogler Refugees** if two or more burn).

**Running the boilerdrak** (companion): it takes the crew **three full actions** to light, aim and fire it, and its flames are a **60-foot cone for 5d10 fire**, which can drop several characters at once. DC 14 Investigation shows the crew are the weak point, not the machine. A character can turn it on the Dragon Army with DC 15 Intelligence (tinker's tools), and then Gholcag goes straight for them.

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Escape from Vogler|ch. 3, "Escape from Vogler"]]) and **Companion** (Part 6). Screams ring from the wharf wherever the party is. Through the smoke comes a black beast pitching from side to side, scales clanking like steel plates, with flames in its maw, and someone shouts "Dragon!" It is the **boilerdrak**, a gnomish siege engine crewed by baaz. The party starts anywhere on the wharf, and the boilerdrak is on the scorched ground. **A DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** check, or coming within 5 feet, shows it's a machine.
> - **Round 1.** Three draconians use their actions to load, aim and fire it at the **Wharfinger's Office** (north of the map), which catches fire. A creature that enters the burning building for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there makes a **DC 16 Constitution save** or takes **1d10 fire**.
> - **The Fray.** The 15-foot band at the map's edge is draconians fighting Vogler's last defenders. It and the village beyond the map are **difficult terrain**. A creature that enters for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there makes a **DC 16 Dexterity save** or takes **2d6 slashing**. It can't be damaged.
> - **Battlefield events** (module): at initiative **0** each round, roll a d6 (and also if a character enters the Fray or tries to leave).
>
> | d6 | Event |
> |---|---|
> | 1 | Two Vogler fishers (guards) come to help, adjacent to the Fray nearest a random character. |
> | 2 | A **blinded bozak** with 1 hit point staggers from the Fray. It doesn't attack, but **explodes** (Death Throes) if killed. |
> | 3-4 | A gliding draconian throws a fiery flask at a random player character, who makes a **DC 10 Dexterity save** or takes **1d6 fire**. |
> | 5-6 | Villagers on the docks cheer. A random player character has advantage on their next attack roll. |
>
> **Scaling (five players, with Darrett):** the boilerdrak, 1 bozak and 4 baaz. If the boilerdrak's blast drops several characters, have Raven or another NPC rush in and, with a sudden unexpected burst of divine power, cast *cure wounds* on one of them.

### Déjà Vu

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, the payoff of [[0.00 Premonition]].

**When:** during the boilerdrak fight (above), the moment it fires its blast or blows up. The blast is the one from the dream. Edric is thrown down, whether the blast hits him or not, and comes to in the rubble with his ears ringing, just as he did in the dream. The other characters are still there this time, picking themselves up nearby.

> The ringing fades, and the screaming comes in under it. Smoke goes up the street the wrong way, the way it did in the dream. Up on the cliffs, the old keep stands black against the fire. To your left there is a beam, and a doorway, and a hand reaching out from under it. Even the boy's face is the same.

Run it as a real scene, with real rolls, and the same three choices. Check which one Edric's player chose in the Premonition:
- **If he makes the same choice,** it goes the way the dream did, unless the party does something the dream didn't have (another hero joins in, a spell, a combo). The dream only showed him alone.
- **If he chooses differently,** it can work: a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check to lift or drag, or holding the line for 2 rounds against a **Baaz** to stand guard.

If the boy lives, he's one of Vogler's refugees from then on, and he never forgets the knight's face. **+1 Vogler Refugees.** So the dream can be wrong, and Edric knows it. If the boy dies, he knows something worse.

Either way, the rest of the dream is still out there: the companions dead in the rubble, and the thing in the sky. It's just a little less certain now.

### Encounter: Fewmaster Gholcag

- **Map:** [[map-3.03-escape-from-vogler-player.png|Map 3.3: Escape from Vogler]] (module), the same board as the boilerdrak
- **Terrain:** the wharf, whatever's left burning after the boilerdrak, the last boat at the end of the pier, and the Fray at the edges.

```encounter
name: Fewmaster Gholcag
creatures:
  - "1": Bozak Draconian (DSotDQ)
  - "3": Baaz Draconian (DSotDQ)
  - "1": Ogre (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Gholcag (ogre): a broken greatclub, broken hide armour, **1d10 giant's toes** (4 gp each) and 1d4 javelins.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Yes, if you ran the boilerdrak in full. Either way, the objective is the boats and the villagers on the wharf, not the Fewmaster.

**Jeyev** (companion): if the party is being overwhelmed, [[Jeyev Veldrews|Jeyev]] staggers in covered in dirt, at half hit points, carrying a Knight of Solamnia's helmet in his off hand. After the fight he tells them the Ironclads were beaten and Cudgel sent him back to make sure no one waited for them. Asked about Becklin, he only hands Darrett the helmet. Its lining is stiff with dried blood.

> [!module] Source: **Module** ("Fewmaster Gholcag", "The Final Boat" and "Lost in Battle") and **Companion**.
>
> **Gholcag.** When the baaz are down, an exterior wall of the Brass Crab bursts outward, and a ten-foot-tall brute in black scale armour steps out with a barrel of fish under one arm, stuffing a fistful of wriggling fish in her mouth. Behind her come two reptilian soldiers. "Them next," she says, and they advance. **Fewmaster Gholcag** is a low-ranking Red Dragon Army commander and leads the raid. She uses the **ogre** stat block in scale mail (**AC 14**), arriving with her escort adjacent to the Fray on the northernmost road. They fight to the death. **Scaling (five players, with Darrett):** Gholcag, 1 bozak and 3 baaz.
>
> **The last boat.** Most of the draconians left in Vogler are looting, which gives the villagers time to leave. Only Raven, Darrett and a few others are still at the end of the pier, and the last boats have room for them and the party. Before they board:
>
> > "Hold the boat!" a voice rasps from the smoke-shrouded street. A man runs through the haze toward the wharf, clad in the armor of an Ironclad Regiment mercenary.
>
> It's **Jeyev Veldrews**. What he says depends on how the battle for the pass ended ([[2.02 Preparing for the Worst#Resolution]]). More Dragon Army forces are right behind him, so they must go now:
>
> - **Win.** He has nothing in his hands. The second host came over the hills; Becklin and Cudgel pulled the line back toward the road north, and Becklin sent him to help everyone evacuate and to tell them not to wait for her. (Becklin lives and turns up again later; Jeyev, still the Dragon Army's spy, says whatever keeps him close to the party.)
> - **Hold.** He carries a horned helmet. He says Becklin fell holding the line while the Regiment fell back, and Cudgel got away with a handful. Anyone who looks at the helmet sees it's a Knight of Solamnia's. (He stabbed her in the back, and uses her helmet to get close to Darrett.)
> - **Loss.** He carries a horned helmet. He says the retreat turned into a rout, that Becklin was lost covering it, and that Cudgel fell in the final overrun. (He stabbed Becklin in the confusion, as above.)
>
> He says no more until they're aboard.
>
> **Last glimpse.** The last boat is a small fishing vessel, barely big enough for the party, Darrett, Raven, Jeyev and a few locals:
>
> > Vogler is in flames. Amid the columns of smoke rising from the village, invaders loot what remains. The villagers traveling with you don't look away, watching until their home is nothing more than a red-black smear along the river's edge. The evacuation boats carry all that remains of Vogler down the Vingaard, toward an uncertain sanctuary in Kalaman.
>
> **What really happened to Becklin and Cudgel (module, "Lost in Battle").** Ours follows the Scenario 1 result above, which is the module's **Assassination** option on a hold or loss (Jeyev is a bandit captain bought off by the Dragon Army like Gragonis, hired to kill the Solamnic knights in Vogler, see [[Jeyev Veldrews]]). The module's other options are alternatives if you want them: **Capture** (Becklin was taken, and sent her helmet as proof, and is held in a Dragon Army camp in a later chapter); **Scattered** (Becklin and Cudgel got away and may turn up in Kalaman later); **Witness** (the party already knows Becklin lost, and Jeyev, separated from the others, knows details and has her helmet). On a hold or loss, **Darrett takes Becklin's helmet and promises to keep it safe**. On a win, Becklin returns later as a valued adviser to Kalaman's military leaders.

### Kansaldi Arrives: Raven's Death

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Part 6, "Kansaldi Arrives" and Part 2, "A Tragic Loss"), with **Homebrew** dialogue for the fanatic version of Kansaldi ([[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]).

Once Gholcag falls and the party scrambles onto the last boat with Raven, Darrett, Jeyev and the last villagers, the companion's grand entrance happens. Run it like this:

- **The dive.** Out of the smoke over the river comes a red dragon, **Ignia**, with Kansaldi on her back. She goes for the boat. If you like, she aims for one of the characters and Raven shoves them aside, and is caught instead.
- **The bridge.** Ignia carries Raven to the end of **Vogler's Crossing**, the unfinished bridge, and lands on the last arch, so the whole flotilla can see. Kansaldi stands in the saddle with Raven in her fist and preaches, laughing and crying at once: "Look, children! Look how she loved you! Kneel, and it can all stop!" Raven spits at her.
- **The fire.** Ignia burns her on the arch. Raven's whistle falls into the river, or lands on the deck at someone's feet.
- **Why not the boats?** Kansaldi wants the refugees to reach Kalaman and tell everyone what they saw (companion). She flies off laughing.

Don't make this a fight. The party can't reach the bridge in time, and Ignia is far beyond them. This is the moment the players learn Kansaldi's name, and get a reason to want her dead.

## Conclusion

> [!homebrew] **End of the prologue.** The last boat leaves Vogler, and the prologue is over. Next comes **Session 1.5**: hand the players [[Session 1.5]], the first time they see the world and the house rules on paper, and let each player keep, customise, swap or replace their origin character. The campaign proper starts in Kalaman ([[3.00 Storm Clouds]]).

The characters level up to level 4. The flotilla takes most of the night to carry the survivors down the Vingaard to Kalaman, and after the horrors of the day the journey is uneventful. The next chapter begins with the refugees' arrival ([[3.01 Arrival in Kalaman]]).

> [!companion] **If Raven survives (companion).** The boxed text if you want her to live: *As you sail away from the burning town, a giant form suddenly appears from out of the wall of smoke. Its crimson scales reflect the flames that engulf Vogler behind it. Impossibly, a dragon now stands upon Vogler's Crossing, its yellow eyes watching you closely. The dragon turns to its side, revealing a woman with short, white hair and clad in black armor mounted on its back. Even from this distance, you can see the flame that engulfs her left eye. "Tell Kalaman that the Dragon Queen and her greatest servant, Kansaldi Fire-Eyes, sends their regards! Her flames will find them soon!" she shouts to you and the rest of the fleeing villagers. Then with one last terrible roar, the dragon takes off into the air once more and disappears behind a wall of smoke.* In this campaign Raven dies: the companion's text for that is the scene above, where Ignia carries her to the bridge and burns her in front of the flotilla.
