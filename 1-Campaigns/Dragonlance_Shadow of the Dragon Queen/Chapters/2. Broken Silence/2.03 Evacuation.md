## Evacuation

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Invasion of Vogler|ch. 3, "Invasion of Vogler"]]) and **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Part 6).

### The Invasion Begins

An Ironclad scout gallops in through the River Gate shouting that the line has broken, and a baaz drops off the cliff onto her. Minutes later the whole village sees the Red Dragon Army come over the northern cliffs, with **Fewmaster Gholcag**, a hulking ogre, at its head. Only half the villagers are on the boats (a third, if the cliff ambush wasn't clean). Darrett draws his sword and asks the party if they're with him.

- **The scout** is Becklin's rider, locked in a fight with the baaz. If saved, she says the army has split: one prong on the mercenaries, one on the village. Raven orders the evacuation. Cloaked figures appear on the clifftop and step off, **wings** opening to slow their fall.
- **Invasion encounters.** Run at least three on the way to the wharf, or one-turn them. After the second, a loud **clanking** comes from the north (the boilerdrak).

| d10 | Encounter |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | A family of four is separated from the evacuees. Unless escorted to the wharf, they hide. |
| 3 | An abandoned cat leaps onto a character and won't leave them. |
| 4-5 | **Airborne assassin.** A kapak glides onto a character. Passive Perception **14+** notices it; anyone else is **surprised**. |
| 6-7 | **Direct attack.** Five baaz with torches burst from between the houses to burn the village and kill villagers at the wharf. They fight to the death. |
| 8 | **Ominous riders.** The highest passive Perception glimpses three figures on motionless horses in the trees atop the north cliffs, plate glinting, who vanish. **DC 16 Intelligence (History)** recognises antique Solamnic armour ([[Soth's Host]]). |
| 9-10 | **Vogler militia.** Two guards are outmatched by five baaz who are baiting them. If one lives, they give the party a **potion of healing**. |

**Invasion chaos** (companion, d8 on initiative 10 and 0, no repeats):
1. A villager the party knows (Elthar, Froswin, Nesau) meets a character's eyes just before a blade comes through their back.
2. A house goes up in flames a block away.
3. Arrows sail overhead into the villagers on the wharf.
4. The great roar from earlier, close now, shakes the dock and knocks villagers into the water.
5. A makeshift boat falls apart as it pushes off, and the others haul its people aboard.
6. The child who gave them a flower crown tugs a sleeve, terrified, until Raven scoops them up.
7. Leedara stands alone in the flames. When the character blinks, she's gone.
8. Three villagers jump off a boat to hold the draconians back. Unless the party steps in, they're dead by the end of the third encounter.

> [!check] Objective: the last boats *(Homebrew)*
> Every villager on the wharf is a single token.
> - **12 villager tokens** between the village and the wharf ([[commoner-xmm|commoner]], they only move and Dash). They run for the boats unless blocked or frightened.
> - **Safe** at the end of the pier. A character can escort one with their move, or talk a frozen one into running (action, DC 10 Persuasion or Intimidation).
> - **Draconians** go for whoever's nearest. A villager hit once is down; get them up with an action (DC 10 Medicine) or carry them.
> - **The last boat leaves after round 6** of the Gholcag fight.
> - **Faction:** **8 or more** saved is "everyone onto the boats" (**+1 Vogler Refugees**). **4 or fewer** is "villagers left behind" (**-1**).

### Encounter: The Boilerdrak

- **Music:** [[Battles#^boilerdrak|The Witcher 3 - Wild Hunt: The Hunt Is Coming]]
- **Map:** [[map-3.03-escape-from-vogler-player.png|Map 3.3: Escape from Vogler]] (module)
- **Terrain:** the wharf with 3-4 boats, burning houses, cover, and a collapsed doorway with a beam (for Déjà Vu). Mark a 20-foot track for the boilerdrak toward the wharf.

```encounter
name: The Boilerdrak
creatures:
  - "1": Bozak Draconian (DSotDQ)
  - "4": Baaz Draconian (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): each baaz leaves a **vial of petrifying gas** (throw 30 ft, 5-ft radius, DC 11 Con or restrained, then petrified). The boilerdrak is worth a fortune to Tatina.

> [!check] Full fight. **Objective:** stop the boilerdrak before it reaches the wharf (20 feet toward the boats each round). Every round it spends there, one boat burns (**-1 Vogler Refugees** if two or more burn). Its blast triggers **Déjà Vu**.

Black and pitching, scales clanking like plates, flames in its maw. A **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** check, or coming within 5 feet, shows it's a machine.
- **Firing:** three crew actions to light, aim and fire. **60-foot cone, 5d10 fire.** DC 14 Investigation shows the crew are the weak point. A character can turn it on the Dragon Army with **DC 15 Intelligence (tinker's tools)**; then Gholcag goes straight for them.
- **Round 1:** it fires at the **Wharfinger's Office**, which catches fire. Entering or starting a turn there: **DC 16 Constitution save** or **1d10 fire**.
- **The Fray** (15-foot band at the map edge, and the village beyond): difficult terrain. First entry or start of turn: **DC 16 Dexterity save** or **2d6 slashing**. Can't be damaged.
- **Scaling (five players, with Darrett):** as the block above. If the blast drops several characters, Raven rushes in and casts *cure wounds* on one.
- **Battlefield events,** d6 at initiative 0 (and when a character enters the Fray or tries to leave):

| d6 | Event |
|---|---|
| 1 | Two Vogler fishers (guards) arrive next to the Fray. |
| 2 | A **blinded bozak** (1 HP) staggers out. It doesn't attack, but **explodes** (Death Throes) if killed. |
| 3-4 | A gliding draconian throws a fiery flask at a random PC: **DC 10 Dexterity save** or **1d6 fire**. |
| 5-6 | Villagers cheer. A random PC has advantage on their next attack. |

### Déjà Vu

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, the payoff of [[0.00 Premonition]].

When the boilerdrak fires or blows up, Edric is thrown down (hit or not) and comes to in the rubble, ears ringing, as in the dream. The others are still there this time.

> The ringing fades, and the screaming comes in under it. Smoke goes up the street the wrong way, the way it did in the dream. Up on the cliffs, the old keep stands black against the fire. To your left there is a beam, and a doorway, and a hand reaching out from under it. Even the boy's face is the same.

Run it with real rolls and the same three choices as the Premonition:
- **Same choice:** it goes as in the dream, unless the party does something the dream didn't show (another hero joins, a spell, a combo).
- **Different choice:** it can work. DC 15 Strength (Athletics) to lift or drag, or hold the line 2 rounds against a **baaz**.

If the boy lives, he joins Vogler's refugees and never forgets the knight's face (**+1 Vogler Refugees**). If he dies, Edric knows something worse. Either way the rest of the dream (the companions dead, the thing in the sky) is a little less certain.

### Encounter: Fewmaster Gholcag

- **Music:** [[Battles#^gholcag|The Witcher 3 - Wild Hunt: Like A Wounded Animal]]
- **Map:** same board. **Terrain:** the wharf, what's still burning, the last boat at the end of the pier.

```encounter
name: Fewmaster Gholcag
creatures:
  - "1": Bozak Draconian (DSotDQ)
  - "3": Baaz Draconian (DSotDQ)
  - "1": Ogre (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Gholcag: a broken greatclub, broken hide armour, **1d10 giant's toes** (4 gp each), 1d4 javelins.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Yes, if you ran the boilerdrak in full. The objective is the boats, not the Fewmaster.

When the baaz are down, a wall of the Brass Crab bursts outward and **Gholcag** steps out with a barrel of fish under one arm, stuffing wriggling fish in her mouth. Ten feet tall, black scale armour (use the **ogre** stat block, **AC 14**). "Them next." She fights to the death.

**The last boat.** The draconians left in Vogler are looting, buying time. A man runs out of the smoke in an Ironclad's armour: "Hold the boat!" It's **Jeyev Veldrews**, with more Dragon Army forces on his heels. What he says depends on [[2.02 Preparing for the Worst#Resolution|the Scenario 1 result]]:

- **Win:** nothing in his hands. The second host came over the hills; Becklin and Cudgel pulled the line back north, and Becklin sent him to tell them not to wait. (Becklin lives; Jeyev, still a spy, says whatever keeps him close.)
- **Hold:** he carries a horned helmet (a Knight of Solamnia's). Becklin fell holding the line; Cudgel got away with a handful. (He stabbed her, and uses the helmet to get close to Darrett.)
- **Loss:** the same helmet. The retreat became a rout; Becklin was lost covering it, and Cudgel fell in the final overrun.

On a hold or loss, **Darrett takes Becklin's helmet and promises to keep it safe** (its lining is stiff with dried blood). On a win, Becklin returns later as an adviser to Kalaman's leaders. The module's other options for Becklin's fate (Capture, Scattered, Witness) work as alternatives.

> The last boat is a small fishing vessel, barely big enough for the party, Darrett, Raven, Jeyev and a few locals. Vogler is in flames. Amid the columns of smoke, invaders loot what remains. The villagers don't look away, watching until their home is nothing more than a red-black smear along the river's edge. The boats carry all that remains of Vogler down the Vingaard, toward an uncertain sanctuary in Kalaman.

### Kansaldi Arrives: Raven's Death

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** (Part 6, "Kansaldi Arrives"; Part 2, "A Tragic Loss"), with **Homebrew** dialogue ([[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]).

**Music:** [[Locations#^prologue-end|Fire Emblem Echoes - Shadows of Valentia: Parting]]

Once the party is on the last boat with Raven, Darrett, Jeyev and the last villagers:

- **The dive.** A red dragon, **Ignia**, comes out of the smoke with Kansaldi on her back and goes for the boat. If you like, she aims for a character and Raven shoves them aside and is caught instead.
- **The bridge.** Ignia carries Raven to the last arch of **Vogler's Crossing**, in view of the flotilla. Kansaldi stands in the saddle, laughing and crying: "Look, children! Look how she loved you! Kneel, and it can all stop!" Raven spits at her.
- **The fire.** Ignia burns her. Raven's whistle falls into the river, or lands at someone's feet.
- Kansaldi lets the boats go, so the refugees carry the story to Kalaman, and flies off laughing.

Not a fight: the party can't reach the bridge, and Ignia is far beyond them. The players learn Kansaldi's name and get a reason to want her dead.
