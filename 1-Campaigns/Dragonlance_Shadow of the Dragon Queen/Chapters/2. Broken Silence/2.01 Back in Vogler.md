## Back in Vogler

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Back in Vogler|ch. 3, "Back in Vogler" to "Voice of War"]]) and **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Part 5, "The Spy" to "Resistance").

**Music:** [[Locations#^high-hill-aftermath|Fire Emblem Awakening: Something is very wrong.]]

### The Night

The festival is over. Raven, Becklin and Cudgel gather the party at the Brass Crab while Darrett tends the wounded. Raven's priorities: the wounded and the dead of High Hill, why the Regiment attacked, and any divine healing the party can offer. Cudgel wants to ride back to her camp and check her troops, alone.

**The interrogation.** Raven refuses to let anyone harm the captive. Cudgel is too angry to lead and watches from a distance. The prisoner is **Svilnt Sunderlit**, a recent recruit and fanatic, bound in a fishing net. He slips free and, with Takhisis's magic, **burns out his own right eye** in tribute to Kansaldi Fire-Eyes. A **DC 16 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)** check makes him talk, but only in babbling, manic riddles ([[Svilnt Sunderlit#Voice]]). What he knows:
- He serves Takhisis in an army far bigger than anyone here imagines, under **Highmaster Kansaldi Fire-Eyes**. He doesn't know why they want Vogler.
- Gragonis planned to kill Cudgel and take over the Regiment. Two nights ago he went into the woods west of camp and came back with gold, then paid his most loyal mercenaries to attack the militia.
- Ispin was targeted for asking questions about the Dragon Queen and "draconic soldiers".
- Svilnt brokered the gold: he introduced Gragonis to a black-robed mage, **Lohezet**.
- Jeyev and a few others stayed in camp.

**He does not confess that Lohezet wore Cudgel's face.** Clearing her isn't in his interest, so he hints, grinning: "ask the dwarf where she walked in the dusk, ask the ogre what he carried home." The truth comes out later, from the footprints at the scene ([[1.02 Arrival in Vogler]]) and from Lohezet himself ([[5.03 Threshold of the Heavens]]).

**Blame.** The village wants someone to blame, and Cudgel is right there. Raven calms the crowd with the party's help (**DC 15 Charisma**), or the party gets Cudgel out of town quietly. Cudgel leaves to check her camp, promising to be back by dawn.

**Scouting.** Raven would send hunters at first light, but welcomes the party going tonight, with **Darrett** as guide. An hour of searching finds boot prints and **clawed reptilian tracks** winding north. Three hours in, from a ledge:

> The woods give way to a ledge overlooking a shallow valley. In the distance, dozens of low fires illuminate hundreds of red tents. Among them move patrols of shadowy, humanlike figures. Over a thousand soldiers occupy the camp: a whole army.

A **DC 10 group Stealth** check slips them away. On a failure, two kapaks follow them home ([[#Encounter: The Stalkers in the Trees]]). They learn that a large, well-organised army is camped several miles northwest, of humans and hooded figures who, up close, have **wings and reptilian features**.

### Encounter: The Stalkers in the Trees

> [!homebrew] **Homebrew**, replacing the companion's pursuing draconian. **Tie: Thorn.**

- **Music:** [[Battles#^stalkers|Fire Emblem Echoes - Shadows of Valentia: Treason]]
- **Map:** [[1878331-Mountain_Side_-_Night_20x20_sq_-_Res_4096x4096.jpg|Mountain side, night]] (companion map, probably), as the woods west of Vogler. Trees and rocks.

```encounter
name: The Stalkers in the Trees
creatures:
  - "2": Kapak Draconian (DSotDQ)
```

Two kapaks stalk the party through the treetops, clawing up trunks and gliding between trees (they can't fly). Build dread first (silence, claw marks, clicking), then one drops on the weakest.

- **Hit and run.** Counter: ready actions or use ranged attacks. Chasing up the trees fails.
- **Second kapak** only watches. It joins if the party still looks healthy, otherwise it flees laughing.
- **Thorn:** **DC 14 Wisdom save** when the first kapak shows itself, or he panics (frightened of them, flees). Repeats each turn or when calmed.

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): **1d4 daggers** and **1d6 vials of acid** per kapak.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** No, play it out. The cliffs fight is the first one-turn offer.

### The Morning

When the party reports, Raven is shocked and knows Vogler can't hold against more than a few raiders. She leans towards writing to the camp's commander to declare neutrality, but **Cudgel returns** at dawn with her loyal mercenaries and **Jeyev Veldrews**, to a false alarm from the gate guards. Cudgel trusts Jeyev and her remaining troops (Jeyev's scouts have already seen the army, and they have restrained the Gragonis loyalists who weren't at the reenactment).

**War council.** Becklin is deeply worried but hides it. Cudgel gives the village the Regiment's full support and favours a peaceful answer backed by spears. Raven wants more information. **Lord Bakaris** barges in and demands a seat, citing all the money he has "given" Raven (the wharf loan and the Regiment's fee), and asks why these strangers are in the room. Raven lets him stay if he leaves the heroes of High Hill alone. His ideas make him look important at no cost to himself.

**Voice of War.** Villagers spot a messenger at the River Gate. Under the carved fish on the open gate, a woman in black-and-red scale mail sits on an armoured warhorse, her right eye burnt out like Svilnt's, holding out a rolled parchment: "Who among you speaks for this village?" If no character speaks first, Raven takes the letter. (The module has it from Belephaion; the companion suggests Kansaldi, and we use her.)

> People of Vogler,
>
> By the orders of Kansaldi Fire-Eyes, Highmaster of the Red Dragon Army, you will quarter the soldiers of the invincible Red Dragon Army this night. Refuse and die.
>
> This is the Dragon Queen's will.

A **DC 12 Intelligence (Religion)** check, or a cleric of Takhisis, recognises the **spiral** on her armour as the Dragon Queen's symbol, worn by all Dragon Army armour. She demands Vogler be ready that evening and that **nobody leaves** until then. **Four Dragon Army soldiers** stand openly atop the 30-foot cliff east of the gate with javelins, ready to kill anyone who threatens her or tries to leave. Raven wants to avoid bloodshed.

**Resistance.** Raven sees no option but to obey. Becklin and Cudgel refuse. If nobody suggests using the Regiment, Cudgel does, but bringing the mercenaries (camped north of the village) in unseen means removing the soldiers at the gate. Raven calls that a declaration of war, but **DC 16 Persuasion or Intimidation** brings her round, and then she joins with two sharpshooters (scouts).

### Encounter: Soldiers on the Cliffs

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#On the Cliffs|ch. 3, "On the Cliffs"]], "The Gnomeflinger") and **Companion** ("Resistance"; cliff map, Appendix D).

- **Music:** [[Battles#^cliffs|Fire Emblem Echoes - Shadows of Valentia: Courage in Numbers]]
- **Map:** [[1878331-edited_cliff.jpg|Dragon Army soldiers cliff]]. **Terrain:** the clifftop with scrub and rocks, a 30-foot drop on the village side, the messenger's horse, the keep across the gap.
- **Two ways up:** climb the 30-foot northeastern cliffs (or land from a boat downriver and sneak back; the soldiers notice anyone they aren't distracted from), or **Than's gnomeflinger** on Thornwall Keep's roof, directly across. Becklin or Darrett suggests asking Than first. He is delighted and forgets the **narycrashes** (one per character) unless someone asks about landing. The launch is quiet: the soldiers are **surprised**.
- **High ground:** the soldiers get the [[Combat Extras#Slopes and High Ground|slope bonus]] (+2 to hit with ranged attacks).

```encounter
name: Soldiers on the Cliffs
creatures:
  - "4": Dragon Army Soldier (DSotDQ)
```

**Scaling (five players, level 3):** 4 soldiers, and Raven's two sharpshooters if she came.

**A clean ambush.** The messenger mounts up and bolts at the first blow (**DC 14 Dexterity (Acrobatics) or Athletics** to catch her, or a ranged attack). The ambush is **clean** only if all four soldiers and the messenger are stopped before a signal or rider gets away. **If not,** Kansaldi attacks **this afternoon**, not at night:
- The three-hour boat-lashing option in [[2.02 Preparing for the Worst]] no longer fits, and only a third of the villagers will be aboard when the army arrives.
- Raven is furious in front of the village. If the Regiment isn't in position yet, Cudgel and Becklin are caught on the road.
- Dragon Army scouts are on the hills before noon.

**After the fight,** Cudgel rides out to bring her mercenaries back to a defensible position north of the village. If the cliff was never attacked, go straight to [[2.02 Preparing for the Worst]].

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): javelins, broken longswords, scale mail and shields.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Only if the session is running long. Otherwise play it out, especially if anyone uses the gnomeflinger.

> [!info] New system: One-Turn Resolution
> The first one-turn offer of the campaign, on this fight. Explain it in two sentences, offer it, and let them say no ([[One-Turn Resolution]], [[Playtest Ideas#Introduction Schedule]]).
