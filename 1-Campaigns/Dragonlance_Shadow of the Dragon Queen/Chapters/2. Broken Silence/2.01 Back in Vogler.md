## Back in Vogler

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Back in Vogler|ch. 3, "Back in Vogler"]] to "Voice of War") and **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Part 5, "The Spy" to "Resistance"). 

**Music:** [[Locations#^high-hill-aftermath|Fire Emblem Awakening: Something is very wrong.]]

The festival is over. Raven, Becklin and Cudgel gather the party at the Brass Crab while Darrett tends the wounded. The night runs like this:

- **The spy.** The captured mercenary is **Svilnt Sunderlit**, a recent recruit and a fanatic. Before the questioning, he slips his bonds and, with a burst of Takhisis's magic, **burns out his own right eye** as tribute to Kansaldi Fire-Eyes. With DC 16 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) he talks:
  - He serves Takhisis, the Dragon Queen, in an army far bigger than anyone here imagines, under **Highmaster Kansaldi Fire-Eyes**.
  - He doesn't know why they want Vogler, and he doesn't ask.
  - Gragonis was paid to turn on Cudgel.
  - Ispin was targeted for asking too many questions about the Dragon Queen and "draconic soldiers".
- **Blame.** The village wants someone to blame, and Cudgel is right there, with the rest of her regiment camped two miles away. Raven can calm the crowd with the party's help (DC 15 Charisma). Either way, Cudgel rides out to check her company's loyalty, and the party may have to get her out of town quietly.
- **Sighting the enemy.** Raven asks the party to scout the woods west of town, where Gragonis met his paymaster. About three hours out, they find a **huge army camp** in red and black. DC 10 group Stealth to get away clean. On a failure, the sentries wave torches, and a single draconian hunts them back toward Vogler (the companion's "pursuing draconian").
- **Cudgel returns** at dawn with her loyal mercenaries and her scout lieutenant, [[Jeyev Veldrews|Jeyev]], who confirms the army. The alarm bell rings when they arrive, and the village panics for nothing.
- **War council.** Becklin, Cudgel and Raven agree they can't fight it. Then **Lord Bakaris** barges in and demands a seat, citing all the money he has "given" Raven (the wharf loan and the Regiment's fee), and asks why these suspicious strangers are allowed in the room. Raven gives in, on the condition that he leaves the heroes of High Hill alone.
- **Voice of War.** A Dragon Army messenger rides up to the River Gate, her right eye burnt out like Svilnt's. She carries a letter from **Kansaldi**: Vogler will open its homes to the Red Dragon Army by nightfall, without resistance, and **no one leaves before then**. On her signal, Dragon Army soldiers stand up on the 30-foot cliff above the village.
- **Resistance.** Raven wants to comply. Becklin and Cudgel don't. If nobody suggests taking out the soldiers on the cliff, Cudgel does. Raven is against it (it's a declaration of war) but DC 16 Persuasion or Intimidation brings her round, and then she comes herself with two sharpshooters (scouts).

- **Map:** [[1878331-Mountain_Side_-_Night_20x20_sq_-_Res_4096x4096.jpg|Mountain side, night]] (companion map, probably), as the woods west of Vogler
- **Terrain:** trees and rocks for hiding, a slope, and the distant glow of the army's campfires at one edge.

```encounter
name: Pursuing Draconian
creatures:
  - "1": Kapak Draconian (DSotDQ)
```

**Scaling (five players, level 3).** The pursuer is a medium fight: 1 sivak. The draconian rolls **Dexterity (Stealth)** against each character's passive or active Perception. On a failure it gets a surprise round and goes for whoever it thinks is weakest. When the party returns they pass the news to Raven, and the companion lets them take a **long rest** before Cudgel arrives.

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Gear only: **1d4 daggers** and **1d6 vials of acid** (kapak).

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Yes, or avoid it entirely with the Stealth check. If it catches them, it goes for whoever looks weakest.

> The first one-turn offer of the campaign. Explain it in two sentences, offer it, and let them say no ([[One-Turn Resolution]], [[Playtest Ideas#Introduction Schedule]]).

### The Night and the Morning in Detail

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Back in Vogler|ch. 3, "Back in Vogler" to "Voice of War"]]) and **Companion** (Part 5), filling in the bullets above.

**At the Brass Crab.** Raven opens the meeting with her priorities: she has already sent trusted villagers to tend the wounded and recover the dead from High Hill; she wants to know why the Regiment attacked and whether other dangerous mercenaries are about; and if a character used healing or other divine magic at High Hill, she asks them to help her people. Cudgel has one concern, the fate of the rest of her troops. She plans to ride back to her camp once the prisoner has been questioned, and won't let anyone go with her: if her mercenaries have betrayed her, she doesn't want anyone else walking into danger. Raven asks everyone but Cudgel to stay in the village tonight.

**The interrogation.** Raven lets the party and Cudgel handle it. She doesn't object to coercive magic, but refuses to let anyone harm the captive. Cudgel tries to lead but can't contain her anger, and retreats to watch from a distance, breaking in at news that makes her angriest. The captive is **Svilnt**, brought in with his chest and arms bound in a fishing net. He mocks the party and gives only his name until coerced, and he burns out his own eye as in the bullet above (**DC 16** to make him talk). If the party spared a different mercenary, such as Gragonis, they question him instead, but Svilnt is the one who knows the rest. What Svilnt knows (module and companion):
- Gragonis planned to kill Cudgel and take over the Ironclad Regiment.
- Two nights ago Gragonis went into the woods west of the camp and returned with a lot of gold.
- He then hired his most loyal mercenaries to attack the militia at the reenactment, planning to raid Vogler afterwards.
- He knows who paid the gold, because he brokered it (see the last bullet). Of the army behind it, he says only that the benefactor's people wear black and red.
- A few mercenaries stayed at camp, Jeyev among them. He thinks the others there are loyal to Cudgel: the ones Gragonis paid went with him to the reenactment.
- **Svilnt does not confess the disguise.** It is not in his interest to clear Cudgel. **He talks in babbling, cryptic, manic riddles** ([[Svilnt Sunderlit#Voice]]), so everything the DC 16 earns arrives wrapped, and the players unpick it. Then he **hints**, grinning: "ask the dwarf where she walked in the dusk, ask the ogre what he carried home." He wants Vogler to turn on her, and a divided Regiment helps the army. The truth about the disguise comes out later: the footprints at the scene ([[1.02 Arrival in Vogler]]), and Lohezet himself in the Wastes ([[5.03 Threshold of the Heavens]]).
- (Companion) Svilnt introduced Gragonis to a black-robed mage called **Lohezet**, who offered gold for helping kill Ispin and betray Cudgel (he'd disguised himself as Cudgel to lure Ispin into the trap), and paid it two nights ago.

**The tense evening.** The prisoner is locked in a shed under guard. Cudgel leaves soon after to check her camp and promises to return by dawn. Raven wants to know about Gragonis's meeting in the woods, and plans to send hunters at first light, but is grateful if the party goes tonight instead, and offers **Darrett** as a guide, which he eagerly accepts. (Companion: after the fight the villagers may blame Cudgel and want to see her held responsible, some even calling her the mastermind, especially since the rest of her regiment is camped a couple of miles away. Raven calms the crowd only with the party's help and a **DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)** check. If they can't, the party may have to get Cudgel out of town quietly.)

**Sighting the enemy.** That night the party searches the woods west of the village where Gragonis met his sponsor, with **Darrett** as their guide. An hour of searching turns up tracks: some boot prints, some **clawed reptilian feet** nobody can identify, winding north. Following them takes about three hours in all and leads into rugged hills, to a ledge over a shallow valley:

> The woods give way to a ledge overlooking a shallow valley. In the distance, dozens of low fires illuminate hundreds of red tents. Among them move patrols of shadowy, humanlike figures. Over a thousand soldiers occupy the camp: a whole army.

The group makes a **DC 10 Stealth** check to slip away unseen. On a failure the sentries wave torches, and a single draconian hunts them back toward Vogler (the pursuing draconian above). Even if the camp is alerted, no more follow them to Vogler. By the time they're back, the party has learned that:
- a significant military force is camped several miles northwest of the village;
- it looks well organised, with vigilant patrols;
- the soldiers are a mix of humans and hooded figures, and anyone who fought them up close knows the hooded ones have **wings and reptilian features**.

**The morning.** Raven spends the night and morning in the Brass Crab's common room with Becklin and other worried villagers. When the party reports, she is shocked, and knows Vogler cannot defend itself against more than a few raiders. She is inclined to write to the camp's commander, declaring Vogler's neutrality, asking its intentions and inviting a meeting with the mayor. Before she gets further, **Cudgel returns** with her scout lieutenant **Jeyev Veldrews** (companion: with the rest of her loyal Regiment, to a false alarm from the gate guards). Cudgel reports:
- She believes Jeyev and her remaining troops are loyal: they could easily have overpowered her if they weren't.
- On her return she found Jeyev and other loyal mercenaries had already heard of High Hill and had restrained several soldiers loyal to Gragonis who hadn't been ordered to the reenactment.
- In the night, Jeyev's scouts found a large, organised military force northwest of the village, too big to be a mercenary band: the same army the party saw, and Jeyev's scouts confirm everything.

**Vogler's response.** The party can steer the leaders, who support any plan that avoids a preemptive strike or bringing a battle into the village:
- **Becklin** is deeply worried. She's heard of strange armies in the east and expects the worst, as the most experienced military leader in the room, but tries not to panic anyone.
- **Cudgel** feels responsible for everything at High Hill and gives the village the Regiment's full support. She favours a peaceful answer backed by her mercenaries' spears.
- **Raven** will do anything to protect her people, but wants more information before she acts.
- **Lord Bakaris** barges in and demands a seat, citing all the money he has "given" Raven (the wharf loan and the Regiment's fee), and asks why these suspicious strangers are allowed in the room. Raven gives in, on the condition that he leaves the heroes of High Hill alone. He cares nothing for the village's safety, but offers ideas that make him look important without any effort on his part.

**Voice of War.** Villagers spot a messenger at the River Gate and alert Raven and the party. Under the carved fish on the open gate, a woman in black-and-red scale mail, her right eye burnt out like Svilnt's, sits on an armoured warhorse, holding out a rolled parchment: "Who among you speaks for this village?" If no character speaks first, Raven steps up and takes the letter (in the module it is from Belephaion, the Voice of Takhisis, but the companion suggests, and we use, Kansaldi, since Belephaion doesn't appear again for a long time):

> People of Vogler,
>
> By the orders of Kansaldi Fire-Eyes, Highmaster of the Red Dragon Army, you will quarter the soldiers of the invincible Red Dragon Army this night. Refuse and die.
>
> This is the Dragon Queen's will.

A character proficient in Religion recognises the name Takhisis as one of the gods of Krynn. A cleric of Takhisis, or a **DC 12 Intelligence (Religion)** check, recognises the **spiral** on the messenger's armour as the symbol of Takhisis, the Dragon Queen, which appears on all Dragon Army armour. The messenger demands that the village be ready to be occupied that evening and that nobody leaves until then. She gives no more information. **Four Dragon Army soldiers** stand openly atop the 30-foot cliff east of the gate, javelins ready, and attack anyone who threatens her or tries to leave. Raven wants to avoid bloodshed and urges the party to withdraw.

**Resistance.** Raven sees no option but to obey. Becklin and Cudgel, who have seen what occupation looks like, refuse. Let the party propose plans. If nobody suggests using the Regiment, Cudgel does, but bringing the mercenaries (camped north of the village) to Vogler without alerting the army means removing the soldiers at the gate, who hold the high ground. There are two ways to reach them.

### Encounter: Soldiers on the Cliffs

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#On the Cliffs|ch. 3, "On the Cliffs"]] and "The Gnomeflinger") and **Companion** ("Resistance", and the cliff battle map).

The cliff can be reached by climbing the northeastern cliffs, by boat downriver, or, best of all, by **Than's gnomeflinger** on Thornwall Keep's roof, directly across from the soldiers. Being launched onto the cliff gives a **surprise round**. When the fight starts, the messenger mounts up and rides back to her camp. If she gets away, the ambush is not clean (see below).

- **By the cliffs.** Climb the 30-foot northeastern cliffs (simple enough), or take a boat downriver, land and sneak back. The top is wooded, with an open field below and behind the soldiers' position. Unless the soldiers are distracted, they notice anyone climbing or approaching from another direction.
- **By the gnomeflinger.** Thornwall Keep sits directly across from the soldiers' cliff. If the party raises it, Becklin or Darrett suggests asking **Than** first. Than is overjoyed to let them use it, and is so delighted they forget the safety devices, **narycrashes** (one for every character), and mentions them only if someone raises concerns about landing. The launch is surprisingly quiet and effective: the four soldiers are **surprised**.
- **The high ground.** The soldiers shoot from a 30-foot cliff: use [[Combat Extras#Slopes and High Ground|the slope rules]] (+2 to hit with ranged weapon attacks from 10 feet higher).

- **Map:** [[1878331-edited_cliff.jpg|Dragon Army soldiers cliff]] (companion map, Appendix D)
- **Terrain:** the clifftop with scrub and rocks, a 30-foot drop on the village side, the messenger's horse, and the keep across the gap for the gnomeflinger.

```encounter
name: Soldiers on the Cliffs
creatures:
  - "4": Dragon Army Soldier (DSotDQ)
```

**Scaling (five players, level 3).** Use **4 Dragon Army soldiers**. If Raven is talked round (**DC 16 Persuasion or Intimidation**), she comes herself with **two sharpshooters** (scout stat block) for long-range support.

**A clean ambush (homebrew).** The ambush is **clean** only if all four soldiers **and** the messenger are stopped (dead, captured or silenced) before any of them can get a signal or a rider away. Make the stakes plain to the table: if the party wants this to stay quiet, nobody can leave alive. That includes the messenger, who bolts at the first blow (a DC 14 Dexterity (Acrobatics) or Athletics check to catch her, or a ranged attack), and any soldier who tries to blow a horn or run.

**If the ambush is not clean,** the Dragon Army learns that Vogler resisted. Kansaldi attacks **early**, this afternoon instead of at night:
- **The evacuation window shrinks.** The boat-lashing option in [[2.02 Preparing for the Worst]] (three hours) no longer fits, and only the two-hour solutions work. A third of the villagers, not half, will be on the boats when the army arrives.
- **Raven's reaction.** She is furious that the party started a war on her doorstep, and says so in front of the village. If the Regiment is not yet in position, Cudgel and Becklin are caught on the road.
- **The first Dragon Army scouts** are visible on the hills before noon.

**After the fight.** If the party beats the soldiers, Cudgel is keen to ride out and bring her mercenaries back to a defensible position just north of the village. If they never attacked the cliff, go straight to [[2.02 Preparing for the Worst]].

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Gear only: javelins, broken longswords, scale mail and shields.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Only if the session is running long. It's the party's first fight against Dragon Army soldiers, and if anyone uses the gnomeflinger it's a scene worth playing out. The objective is clearing the cliff before the messenger gets back to her camp with word of who did it.
