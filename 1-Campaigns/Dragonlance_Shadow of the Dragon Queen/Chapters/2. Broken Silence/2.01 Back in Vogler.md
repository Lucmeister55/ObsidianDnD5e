## Back in Vogler

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Back in Vogler|ch. 3, "Back in Vogler"]] to "Voice of War") and **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Part 5, "The Spy" to "Resistance"). 

**Music:** [1-14 - Something is very wrong.](ttrpg-sound:Soundbar/FE-Awakening/1-14 - Something is very wrong..mp3)

The festival is over. Raven, Becklin and Cudgel gather the party at the Brass Crab while Darrett tends the wounded. The night runs like this:

- **The spy.** The captured mercenary is **Svilnt Sunderlit**, a recent recruit and a fanatic. Before the questioning, he slips his bonds and, with a burst of Takhisis's magic, **burns out his own right eye** as tribute to Kansaldi Fire-Eyes. With DC 16 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) he talks:
  - He serves Takhisis, the Dragon Queen, in an army far bigger than anyone here imagines, under **Highmaster Kansaldi Fire-Eyes**.
  - He doesn't know why they want Vogler, and he doesn't ask.
  - Gragonis was paid to turn on Cudgel.
  - Ispin was targeted for asking too many questions about the Dragon Queen and "draconic soldiers".
- **Blame.** The village wants someone to blame, and Cudgel is right there, with the rest of her regiment camped two miles away. Raven can calm the crowd with the party's help (DC 15 Charisma). Either way, Cudgel rides out to check her company's loyalty, and the party may have to get her out of town quietly.
- **Sighting the enemy.** Raven asks the party to scout the woods west of town, where Gragonis met his paymaster. About three hours out, they find a **huge army camp** in red and black. DC 10 group Stealth to get away clean. On a failure, the sentries wave torches, and a single draconian hunts them back toward Vogler (the companion's "pursuing draconian").
- **Cudgel returns** at dawn with her loyal mercenaries and her scout lieutenant, [[Jeyev Veldrews|Jeyev]], who confirms the army. The alarm bell rings when they arrive, and the village panics for nothing.
- **War council.** Becklin, Cudgel and Raven agree they can't fight it. Then **Lord Bakaris** barges in and demands a seat, citing all the money he has "given" Raven, and asks why these suspicious strangers are allowed in the room. Raven gives in, on the condition that he leaves the heroes of High Hill alone.
- **Voice of War.** A Dragon Army messenger rides up to the River Gate, her right eye burnt out like Svilnt's. She carries a letter from **Kansaldi**: Vogler will open its homes to the Red Dragon Army by nightfall, without resistance, and **no one leaves before then**. On her signal, Dragon Army soldiers stand up on the 30-foot cliff above the village.
- **Resistance.** Raven wants to comply. Becklin and Cudgel don't. If nobody suggests taking out the soldiers on the cliff, Cudgel does. Raven is against it (it's a declaration of war) but DC 16 Persuasion or Intimidation brings her round, and then she comes herself with two sharpshooters (scouts).

- **Map:** [[1878331-Mountain_Side_-_Night_20x20_sq_-_Res_4096x4096.jpg|Mountain side, night]] (companion map, probably), as the woods west of Vogler
- **Terrain:** trees and rocks for hiding, a slope, and the distant glow of the army's campfires at one edge.

```encounter
name: Pursuing Draconian
creatures:
  - "1": Kapak Draconian (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Gear only: **1d4 daggers** and **1d6 vials of acid** (kapak).

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Yes, or avoid it entirely with the Stealth check. If it catches them, it goes for whoever looks weakest.

### Encounter: Soldiers on the Cliffs

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#On the Cliffs|ch. 3, "On the Cliffs"]] and "The Gnomeflinger") and **Companion** ("Resistance", and the cliff battle map).

The cliff can be reached by climbing the northeastern cliffs, by boat downriver, or, best of all, by **Than's gnomeflinger** on Thornwall Keep's roof, directly across from the soldiers. Being launched onto the cliff gives a **surprise round**. When the fight starts, the messenger mounts up and rides back to her camp. Let her go, or don't.

- **Map:** [[1878331-edited_cliff.jpg|Dragon Army soldiers cliff]] (companion map, Appendix D)
- **Terrain:** the clifftop with scrub and rocks, a 30-foot drop on the village side, the messenger's horse, and the keep across the gap for the gnomeflinger.

```encounter
name: Soldiers on the Cliffs
creatures:
  - "4": Dragon Army Soldier (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Gear only: javelins, broken longswords, scale mail and shields.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Good candidate, and a great moment for a gnomeflinger combo. The objective is clearing the cliff before the messenger gets back to her camp with word of who did it.
