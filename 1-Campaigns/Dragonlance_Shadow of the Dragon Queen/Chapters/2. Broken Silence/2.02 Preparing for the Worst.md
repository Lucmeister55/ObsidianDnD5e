## Preparing for the Worst

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Preparing for the Worst|ch. 3, "Preparing for the Worst"]] and "Planning to Evacuate") and **Companion** (Part 6, "Increasing Tension" and "Goodbyes").

Whatever happened on the cliff, the army is coming. Becklin pulls the party and Darrett aside if nobody else says it: Vogler can't be defended, and the only hope is to **evacuate** downriver to Kalaman. With her and the party pushing, Raven agrees.

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Preparing for the Worst|ch. 3, "Preparing for the Worst" and "Planning to Evacuate"]]).
>
> **Where everyone stands.** If the party attacked the soldiers on the cliff, Raven is grappling with the fact that they just started a war. If not, she's frustrated and asks how to protect her people by nightfall. She refuses any plan that fights inside the village. **Cudgel** only wants to fetch her troops and bring them back, and thinks fighting is the only choice even outnumbered. If nobody stops her, she and Jeyev ride or sneak out to rally the mercenaries. **Becklin** has grown quiet and wants a private word with the party and Darrett.
>
> **If the party proposes evacuating,** Raven first dismisses it, but a **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** check convinces her. If they don't, Becklin takes the party and Darrett aside ("Becklin's Fear"):
> - She has heard of war and unstoppable armies in the east, and fears these soldiers are part of that army.
> - She knows Vogler's people aren't soldiers: they'd need weeks of training and a hundred more professional soldiers to stand a chance.
> - She suggests getting the villagers ready to flee at a moment's notice in case other plans fail, following the river to **Kalaman**.
> - She asks the party to help convince Raven. If both the party and Becklin advocate it, Raven grudgingly agrees.
>
> A character who succeeds on a **DC 12 Intelligence (History)** check, or who asks a villager about Kalaman, learns what the module's "Knowledge of Kalaman" gives ([[Kalaman]]).

**Not enough boats.** An hour inspecting the wharf shows the boats can carry only **200 of the village's 400 people**. Let the players solve it. There are three good answers, and each one is worth a **+1 villagers** if the party leads it:
- **Boat made of boats:** DC 16 Wisdom (Survival) to lash rings of small boats around floating log rafts (200 more people). With the villagers' help it takes **three hours**.
- **Personal fleet:** DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) to get fishers to lend every boat they own (boats needing simple repairs, boats gathering dust in sheds, even decorative boats in homes). Given **two hours**, they find enough for **100 more** people.
- **Repurposed ferry:** DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) to see that the ferry's rafts and pulleys can be broken down in two hours (100 more).

A character proficient with water vehicles makes these checks with advantage. Festival prizes help here: the **river chart** (+2 to the first check), and **Nesau's winch-hut secret** (advantage on the third) ([[1.03 The Kingfisher Festival]]).

**Planning the evacuation (module).** Becklin concentrates on learning about the Red Dragon Army and shoring up local defences. Raven asks the party and Darrett to: gather everyone in the village circle and tell them; count the ships and how many villagers they can carry; tell the villagers how to reach safety; and keep them calm. She and Darrett go along with any plan that clearly helps the escape without risking villagers. She doesn't want the evacuation to start until it's necessary, so people have time to prepare and leave together.

**Informing the villagers.** The party can easily collect most of the village by going door to door or ringing the bell on the village circle. The festival decorations are still up. Unlike yesterday the crowd is somber and muttering as Raven takes the stage: "A dangerous army is headed for Vogler. The Ironclad Regiment will do what it can to defend Vogler, but we must be ready to flee." Stunned silence, then a wave of questions, shouting, anger and fear. Speaking in support, or explaining the evacuation plan, with a **DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)** check regains control. A character who learned **the festival song** at the talent show can start it, and the crowd joins in: **advantage** on the check. Otherwise it takes Raven over an hour to placate them. At your discretion, **Lord Bakaris or his son** speaks out to undermine Raven and the party: the villagers refuse to evacuate until a character counters him with roleplay or a **DC 14 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)** check.

The villagers then pack what they can carry and help the mayor, Darrett and the party, knowing time is short.

**Increasing tension** (companion d4, as they work):
1. A child brings the party water and asks, crying, why the soldiers want to hurt everyone.
2. Two men brawl over a place on the same boat for their families.
3. A raft falls apart at the wharf, and people call it a bad omen.
4. A roar from the far north, enormous, silences the whole village.

**Battle plans.** Cudgel returns in the afternoon. The Ironclad Regiment, Becklin with it, will meet the army at the narrow pass between the river and the eastern cliffs to buy time. Raven and Darrett stay to load the boats, and the party goes to the line with Becklin and Cudgel, to fall back when it breaks.

> **Cudgel's plan (module).** She has ordered the Regiment to hold a position north of the village, and the mercenaries think they can defend against a larger force for a while. She hopes a show of force will convince the army that Vogler isn't an easy target, and she doesn't want to fight. She has set up a small rear command tent near the River Gate with a messenger, so the village can send word to her troops. She asks Becklin to join her at the front, hoping that a Knight of Solamnia on the field will make the enemy hesitate to provoke the knighthood. Raven and Becklin agree. This leaves the village and its escape defended only by a handful of militia and a few tough fishers, so the party goes to the line as the Regiment's only real reinforcement, and Darrett stays to guard the evacuation with Raven.

**Becklin's request.** In private (passive Insight to notice she's holding something back), Becklin thanks the party and asks them to fetch a **wooden box from Thornwall Keep** and give it to Darrett, unopened, if she doesn't come back. It's his armour.

> **The box (module).** The character with the highest passive Insight notices Becklin is keeping something back, but she won't speak in front of Cudgel, Raven or Darrett. The box is under her bed in Thornwall Keep, and the party can fetch it at any point before the invasion encounters. It's a sturdy wooden box of about **70 pounds**, with nothing on it but a simple **kingfisher emblem** on the lid. Inside is a suit of **Solamnic plate armour**. If they follow her instructions they give it to Darrett after the evacuation. If they hand it over before leaving town, see [[Darrett Highwater]].

**Goodbyes.** Darrett asks to ride with her. She orders him to stay. Then she drops the soldier for a moment, and the two of them say goodbye properly. [If Ispin's shield already speaks to Edric, Ispin asks to say goodbye to Becklin and Cudgel through him.]

## Scenario 1: Battle of Vogler

> [!wok] Source: **WoK** scenario 1 (placed by the module in [[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Invasion of Vogler|ch. 3]]), with **Homebrew** outcomes for Becklin's fate per result (see [[Becklin Uth Viharin]]). Jeyev's betrayal is the **Module**'s "Assassination" option ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Lost in Battle|ch. 3, "Lost in Battle"]]; see [[Jeyev Veldrews]]).

> [!info] New system: Warriors of Krynn
> **The first WoK game, always, and the only WoK battle the story requires.** We skip the book's Scenario 0 (Training Ground), so teach the rules as you play, with the [[Rulebook]] open. Give it its own session if you can, and nothing else new that night ([[Playtest Ideas#Introduction Schedule]]). How it goes decides whether any later WoK battle is used at all ([[Warriors of Krynn#How We Use It]]).
> **Book:** [[03 - Scenario 1 - Battle of Vogler|transcript]] · **Narration:** [[Battle Lines#Scenario 1: Battle of Vogler|narration lines]]

### Setup

The battle begins when a farmer rides into the village circle and reports hundreds of soldiers on the road, heading for Vogler. Becklin summons the party to Cudgel's command tent at the village's edge, and the party stands with the line while Raven and Darrett load the boats behind them in waves. They read the book's introduction to Scenario 1 and play it ([[03 - Scenario 1 - Battle of Vogler|the book's pages]]).

The scenario setup can more or less be read directly from the board game introduction, with Becklin explaining that the defenders consist of the loyal remainder of Cudgel's regiment (a few dozen, after High Hill) and a few Vogler volunteers, hastily assembled and poorly equipped compared to the incoming threat. Their plan is to hold the dragon army at a narrow pass between the river and the eastern cliffs, where the terrain forces the enemy into a bottleneck and partially offsets their numerical disadvantage. The objective is not to win a decisive battle, but to delay the advance long enough for the villagers of Vogler to evacuate safely downriver and, if possible, regroup with the defenders later once a defensible position can be re-established.

### Resolution

**Win:** If the battle is won, the draconian army breaks and begins to retreat, and the mercenaries break into cheering at what seems like a hard-fought victory. But Becklin's relief vanishes when she spots smoke and banners on the horizon, followed by a warhorn and a much larger force cresting the hills—an overwhelming host, many times the size of the original army, advancing toward the village. Cudgel successfully withdraws with the remaining mercenaries, and Becklin has a chance to survive and later reappear at Wheelwatch Outpost.

**Hold:** If the battle is still ongoing, the frontline remains locked in brutal combat when Becklin sees the same smoke, banners, and warhorn in the distance. The reinforcements are coming, and she knows the fight is about to become impossible to sustain, so she orders the players and any others near her to pull back toward Vogler and prepare for full evacuation while she, Cudgel, and the mercenaries hold the line as long as they can. Offscreen, in the chaos of the continuing battle, Becklin is too focused on the enemy advance to notice Jeyev drawing a hidden blade and stabbing her in the back, killing her before the retreat is fully completed, while Cudgel manages to survive and withdraw with the remaining forces.

**Loss:** If the battle is lost, the mercenaries are already retreating when Becklin spots the approaching reinforcements. The advancing force rolls over the field as the retreat turns into a rout, and the enemy begins overrunning the fleeing defenders. Becklin calls for an immediate withdrawal to Vogler, tells them with tears in her eyes to pass on her pride to Darrett, and sends them to retrieve something from the wooden box in Thornwall Keep. Offscreen, as the rout collapses, Becklin is stabbed in the back by Jeyev and killed in the confusion, while Cudgel also falls in battle during the final overrun.

**Rewards (module).** On a **win or a hold**, each character gains **inspiration**, and once the people of Vogler are safe, they are named honorary members of the village. The mayor gives them one of the community's few treasures: a folded paper kingfisher that works as a **Quaal's feather token (bird)**. (Raven won't live to give it later, so let her press it into their hands at the wharf.) On a **loss**, there is no reward. However it ends, the party falls back to Vogler to find about half the villagers aboard, with Raven and Darrett still loading the rest, and Darrett takes the news of Becklin hard. Run any of the invasion encounters on the way out, or skip to the last boat ([[2.03 Evacuation]]).

In all cases, Becklin makes one final, unchanging request before sending the characters away: she tells them exactly what to say to Darrett—that she is proud of him, no matter what comes next—and insists they take the wooden box from Thornwall Keep and deliver it into his care, without opening it or questioning its contents.
