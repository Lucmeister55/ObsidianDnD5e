## Preparing for the Worst

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Preparing for the Worst|ch. 3, "Preparing for the Worst", "Planning to Evacuate"]]) and **Companion** (Part 6, "Increasing Tension", "Goodbyes").

**Music:** [[Locations#^vogler-after|Fire Emblem Awakening: We'd best prepare for combat, just to be safe.]]

Whatever happened on the cliff, the army is coming. Raven first dismisses evacuating, but a **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** check convinces her. If the party doesn't push it, Becklin takes them and Darrett aside:
- She has heard of unstoppable armies in the east and fears these are part of them.
- Vogler's people aren't soldiers. They'd need weeks of training and a hundred more professionals.
- Get the villagers ready to flee downriver to **Kalaman**, and help her convince Raven.

If the cliff ambush was attacked, Raven is also grappling with having just started a war. She refuses any plan that fights inside the village. **Cudgel** wants to fetch her troops and fight, and if nobody stops her she and Jeyev ride out to rally them. A **DC 12 Intelligence (History)** check, or asking a villager, tells the party about [[Kalaman]].

> [!warning] If the cliff ambush was not clean ([[2.01 Back in Vogler#Encounter: Soldiers on the Cliffs]]), the army comes **this afternoon**. Drop the three-hour boat-lashing option, and have only a third of the villagers aboard when the battle starts.

**Not enough boats.** An hour at the wharf shows the boats carry only **200 of the village's 400 people**. Each solution the party leads is worth **+1 villagers**:
- **Boat made of boats:** DC 16 Wisdom (Survival) to lash small boats around log rafts (+200). Three hours.
- **Personal fleet:** DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) to get fishers to lend every boat they own, even decorative ones (+100). Two hours.
- **Repurposed ferry:** DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) to break down the ferry's rafts and pulleys (+100). Two hours.

Proficiency with water vehicles gives advantage. Festival prizes help: the **river chart** (+2 to the first check) and **Nesau's winch-hut secret** (advantage on the third) ([[1.03 The Kingfisher Festival]]).

**Informing the villagers.** The festival decorations are still up, and the crowd is somber. Raven takes the stage: "A dangerous army is headed for Vogler. The Ironclad Regiment will do what it can to defend Vogler, but we must be ready to flee." Then questions, shouting, fear. A **DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)** check regains control (**advantage** if a character learned **the festival song**, and starts it). Otherwise Raven needs over an hour. At your discretion, **Lord Bakaris or his son** undermines the plan, and the villagers refuse to go until a character counters him with roleplay or a **DC 14** check.

**Increasing tension** (companion d4, as they work):
1. A child brings water and asks, crying, why the soldiers want to hurt everyone.
2. Two men brawl over a place on the same boat.
3. A raft falls apart at the wharf, and people call it a bad omen.
4. A roar from the far north silences the village.

**Battle plans.** Cudgel returns in the afternoon. The Ironclad Regiment, with Becklin, will meet the army at the narrow pass between the river and the eastern cliffs to buy time. She hopes a show of force convinces the army that Vogler isn't an easy target, and that a Knight of Solamnia on the field makes the enemy hesitate. Raven and Darrett stay to load the boats, and the party goes to the line as the Regiment's only reinforcement, to fall back when it breaks.

**Becklin's request.** In private (passive Insight to notice she's holding something back), Becklin asks the party to fetch a **wooden box from under her bed in Thornwall Keep**, about 70 pounds, with a **kingfisher emblem** on the lid, and give it to Darrett unopened if she doesn't come back. Inside is **Solamnic plate armour made to fit him** ([[Darrett Highwater#Why He's Still a Squire]]).

**Goodbyes.** Darrett asks to ride with her. She orders him to stay, then drops the soldier for a moment and they say goodbye properly. [If Ispin's shield speaks to Edric, Ispin asks to say goodbye to Becklin and Cudgel through him.]

## Scenario 1: Battle of Vogler

> [!wok] Source: **WoK** scenario 1 (placed by the module in [[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Invasion of Vogler|ch. 3]]), with **Homebrew** outcomes per result ([[Becklin Uth Viharin]], [[Jeyev Veldrews]]; module "Lost in Battle", Assassination option).

> [!info] New system: Warriors of Krynn
> **The first WoK game, always, and the only one the story requires.** We skip Scenario 0 (Training Ground), so teach the rules as you play, with the [[Rulebook]] open. Give it its own session if you can, and nothing else new that night ([[Playtest Ideas#Introduction Schedule]]). How it goes decides whether any later WoK battle is used ([[Warriors of Krynn#How We Use It]]).
> **Music:** [[Battles#^scenario-1|Fire Emblem Echoes - Shadows of Valentia: 1.14 March to Deliverance (Prologue)]]
> **Book:** [[03 - Scenario 1 - Battle of Vogler|transcript]] · **Narration:** [[Battle Lines#Scenario 1: Battle of Vogler|narration lines]]

### Setup

A farmer rides into the village circle and reports hundreds of soldiers on the road. Becklin summons the party to Cudgel's command tent, and they stand with the line while Raven and Darrett load the boats behind them in waves. Read the book's introduction. The defenders are the loyal remainder of Cudgel's regiment (a few dozen) and some Vogler volunteers. The plan is to hold the narrow pass between the river and the eastern cliffs, not to win, but to delay the advance long enough for the villagers to get away downriver.

> [!homebrew] **Who comes first.** The first force is only the army's **vanguard**: mostly inexperienced, non-draconian bandits and ruffians, with a handful of real soldiers to drive them. That is how a thin line can fight them off. The **real warhost**, draconians and elites, arrives afterwards. Narrate the book's enemy units as ruffians, and its "vile champions" as the few real soldiers worth fearing, even if the board pieces are draconian ([[Battle Lines#Scenario 1: Battle of Vogler]]).

### Resolution

**Win.** The vanguard breaks and the mercenaries cheer. Then Becklin spots smoke and banners on the horizon and hears a warhorn: the **real warhost**, draconians and elites, many times the vanguard's size. Cudgel withdraws with the survivors. Becklin lives, and later reappears at Wheelwatch.

**Hold.** The line is still locked when the same warhorn sounds. Becklin orders the party back toward Vogler to evacuate while she, Cudgel and the mercenaries hold. Offscreen, **Jeyev stabs Becklin in the back** and kills her. Cudgel survives and withdraws.

**Loss.** The mercenaries are already retreating when the warhost rolls over the field and the retreat becomes a rout. Becklin calls the party back to Vogler and tells them, in tears, to pass on her pride to Darrett and to fetch the box. Offscreen, **Jeyev stabs her** in the confusion, and **Cudgel falls** in the final overrun.

**Rewards.** On a **win or hold**, each character gains **inspiration** and honorary membership of the village. The mayor gives them a folded paper kingfisher that works as a **Quaal's feather token (bird)** (Raven won't live to give it later, so she presses it into their hands at the wharf). No reward on a loss.

However it ends, the party falls back to Vogler to find about half the villagers aboard, Raven and Darrett still loading. On a hold or loss, Darrett takes the news of Becklin hard. Run the invasion encounters on the way out, or skip to the last boat ([[2.03 Evacuation]]).

On a hold or loss, Becklin's last request is the same: tell Darrett she is proud of him, and deliver the box unopened.
