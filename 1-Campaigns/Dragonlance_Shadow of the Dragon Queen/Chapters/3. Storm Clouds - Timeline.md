# Storm Clouds Timeline

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. The major events come from the **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war|ch. 4]]) and **WoK**, as tagged in [[3. Storm Clouds]].

The running order for [[3. Storm Clouds]]: every major event, every downtime, and the events that move faction Standing in between. Factions run on [[Faction Rules]]. Most of the combat stays in the major events. The downtime events are almost all social choices, and that's where Standing really moves.

**How to run a downtime.** Each downtime, every character takes their personal downtime activity, a bastion turn, crafting or whatever else they like, once per week. Alongside that, run **two or three of the events listed for that downtime**, whichever fit the table best. Each event is a short scene with a choice, and each choice moves Standing. Events you don't use can roll over into the next downtime.

**Briefings.** Before every downtime and every major event, the party is called to a war room or council meeting: news from the front, the consequences of their choices, and a strategic dilemma. They are all in [[3. Storm Clouds - Briefings]], numbered in the order they come.

**Starting Standing:** 0 for every faction, except the Vogler Refugees, who carry over whatever the party earned in Vogler.

## Running Threads

Three threads run through the whole act, on top of the downtime events. Bring each one back once or twice.

**Bakaris the Younger.** The guilds are the only reason the Bakarises have a voice in Kalaman. They admire old noble blood, and the Elder's money and Solamnic name buy him a seat beside them on the council. So anything that humiliates the Bakarises costs a little with the Guilds. The Younger is a thorn in the party's side all act, and how badly depends on the party's **Guilds** Standing:
- **Guilds Trusted or better:** he can't touch them openly. He sulks, spreads rumours and waits.
- **Guilds Neutral:** he works the merchants against them. Once per downtime, one purchase costs double, or one guild favour is "unavailable".
- **Guilds Wary or worse:** he gets them **boycotted**. The guilds' Hostile effects kick in a band early, he spreads stories that the party are Dragon Army sympathisers, and he tries to buy a dragonborn PC's contract out from under any deal they're making.

**A dragonborn PC and the city.** Rumours that the black-scaled creature is a draconian spy grow all act (see [[Wurm (DM)]]). At first the dragonborn PC is protected. [[Nestra Vendri|Marshal Vendri]] makes it known that he serves under her esquires, and that anyone who lays a hand on him answers to her. That protection holds **as long as Military Standing is Neutral or better**. If the party slips to Wary with the Military, Vendri quietly withdraws her protection, and soldiers stop looking the other way. If the party slips to Wary with the **Smallfolk**, the docks turn on him regardless. Either one turns the rumours violent (see "the dragonborn PC in the alley" in Downtime 3).

**The Dragon Army's knives.** The Dragon Army knows exactly who has been wrecking its plans, partly because Takhisis hears everything a dragonborn PC hears. By the middle of the act it sends **sivak draconians** after the party. Sivaks take the shape of whoever they kill, which makes them perfect assassins (see "The friendly face" in Downtime 4). Their eyes inside the castle are [[Jeyev Veldrews|Jeyev]], Becklin's murderer, now Darrett's trusted right hand. Drop one wrong note about him per downtime (see his note), and have him make his own attempt on Darrett just before the Lord's Arrival.

## 16 Brookgreen: Arrival in Kalaman

Run the module's arrival, council meeting and "Reporting Back" as written. It's the first faction test.
- **Silencing Lord Bakaris** in front of the council and telling the truth about Vogler: **+1 Refugees, -1 Guilds**, because the guild leaders backed him.
- **The governor's offer.** The party has a choice here:
  - Swear in as esquires: **+1 Military**.
  - Refuse, and serve only as Darrett's aides, staying with the refugees: **+1 Refugees, -1 Military**.
  - Hold out for better terms, demanding the refugees be housed *inside* the walls (DC 15 Persuasion): **+1 Refugees, -1 Guilds**, because the guilds own the empty houses.
- **Helping settle the refugees** outside the Trade Gate with Medicine or tool checks: **+1 Refugees**.

## Downtime 1 (2 weeks)

> [!info] Briefing first
> Start with **[[3. Storm Clouds - Briefings#Briefing 1, War Room, After the Council|Briefing 1: War room: after the council]]**.

> [!info] New system: personal downtime activities
> Each player's own activity, from their character note. No Bastion yet ([[Playtest Ideas#Introduction Schedule]]).

The party finds its feet in Kalaman. Run "A new voice", then pick two or three others:

- **A new voice.** With Raven dead, the refugees argue every night about who should speak for them. The candidates are Vogler's militia captain (hard and angry, and wants to march back and retake the village), an old ferryman everyone trusts (gentle and cautious, and wants to keep everyone alive), or no one at all, until Cudgel returns. The refugees ask the party what Raven would have wanted.
  - Back the militia captain: **+1 Refugees**, but the refugees get harder to keep out of trouble, and a later event may cost them dearly.
  - Back the ferryman: **+1 Refugees**, and the community stays steady, but quiet when it should be loud.
  - Refuse to choose ("it's not our place"): no change. The refugees settle it themselves, slowly and bitterly.
  - Whoever they back becomes the **Spokesperson** in [[Vogler Refugees]].

- **Bread at the Trade Gate.** Rations are short, and a fight breaks out at the gate between Kalaman's poor and Vogler's refugees over the day's bread cart.
  - Split it evenly: **+1 Refugees, -1 Smallfolk**.
  - Favour the locals: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Refugees**.
  - Pay out of their own pockets for a second cart (50 gp): **+1 to both**.
- **Bakaris holds court.** Lord Bakaris is in the Steady Beacon telling anyone who'll listen that *he* saved Vogler. The soldiers are buying him drinks.
  - Expose him publicly: **+1 Refugees, +1 Military, -1 Guilds**, and Bakaris the Younger makes it personal (see "Running Threads").
  - Flatter him and let him keep the story: **+1 Guilds, -1 Refugees**.
  - Let it go: no change, but the refugees notice.
- **A message for a mage.** Run the module's "Message for a Mage" with a spellcaster PC and Wyhan. Delivering the scroll unopened and treating Wyhan with respect: **+1 Mages**. If the spellcaster PC opened it (see his level 4 moment), Wyhan knows: **-1 Mages**.
- **The fishers' prices.** Rey Costwyn's Fishers' Guild has doubled the price of fish since the refugees arrived.
  - Lean on him: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds**.
  - Broker a deal, with refugee fishermen working Guild boats for a share (DC 15 Persuasion): **+1 Guilds, +1 Refugees**.

## Mission 1: Rookledust Rescue

> [!info] Briefing first
> Start with **[[3. Storm Clouds - Briefings#Briefing 2, War Room, Orders|Briefing 2: War room: orders]]**.

Tatina Rookledust and her inventions are suddenly the most valuable things in Kalaman. Where they end up is the party's choice:
- **Bring her to Vendri, as ordered.** Her inventions go to the war effort: **+1 Military**.
- **Let the Shipwrights' Guild set her up in a new workshop** at the docks. They'll pay very well: **+1 Guilds, -1 Military**, since Vendri wanted her under her own roof.
- **Move her into the party's own new Bastion** to work for them: no change, but she becomes a Bastion special hireling, and both the military and the guilds want her back.

## Downtime 2 (3 weeks)

> [!info] Briefing first
> Start with **[[3. Storm Clouds - Briefings#Briefing 3, Council, Prices|Briefing 3: Council: prices]]**.

> [!info] New systems: the Bastion and Working a Faction
> The party has just been given the Bastion. Both are new choices on the same downtime list ([[Faction Rules#Working a Faction (downtime)]], [[Playtest Ideas#Introduction Schedule]]).

The party has a Bastion now, and the war feels real. Pick two or three:

- **The masons' offer.** Uri Stonegrey's Masons' Guild wants to shore up the walls using refugee labour at a fraction of the usual wage, and asks the party to "encourage" the refugees' Spokesperson.
  - Agree: **+1 Guilds, -1 Refugees**.
  - Refuse: **-1 Guilds**.
  - Negotiate a fair wage (DC 15 Persuasion): **+1 Refugees**, and Guilds unchanged.
- **The recruits.** A dozen Kalaman recruits have never held a spear. Their sergeant asks for help.
  - Spend a week drilling them: **+1 Military**. This is free progress for a knight PC's "Raising a Company".
- **A knight comes calling.** The knight-commander of the Kalaman chapterhouse invites a knight PC to dinner and wants to know about the "rabble" he travels with: a sorcerer, a kender and, later, "that draconian".
  - Defend his companions honestly: **+1 Knights** (they respect honesty, if not the company).
  - Distance himself from them: **+1 Knights**, and a quiet word in the party about loyalty.
  - Tell the knight where to go: **-1 Knights, +1 Smallfolk** if it gets around (it will).
- **The looted book.** A refugee boy is selling a spellbook he found in the ruins of Vogler at the City Market. Wyhan wants it. So does a Cartographers' Guild agent, for Alexandra Nightvane's collection.
  - Give it to Wyhan: **+1 Mages, -1 Guilds**.
  - Let the guild buy it: **+1 Guilds, -1 Mages**.
  - Make sure the boy's family is paid either way: **+1 Refugees**.

## Mission 2: Ambushing the Enemy

> [!info] Briefing first
> Start with **[[3. Storm Clouds - Briefings#Briefing 5, War Room, Something Else Out There|Briefing 5: War room: something else out there]]**.

The farmstead is held by the undead (a wight and ghouls). The farm's family is among the dead.
- **Report back with proof:** **+1 Military**.
- **Bring the wight's remains, and whatever raised it, to Wyhan:** **+1 Mages**. The military never sees the proof.
- **Lay the family to rest properly** and burn the farm clean, and bring their surviving kin the news yourselves: **+1 Smallfolk**.

Each of these is a different use of the same evening, so pick one.

## Downtime 3 (3 weeks)

> [!info] Briefing first
> Start with **[[3. Storm Clouds - Briefings#Briefing 6, Council, The Knights|Briefing 6: Council: the knights]]**.

The first refugees from other burned villages start arriving. Pick two or three:

- **The preacher.** A wild-eyed preacher in the market square is telling crowds the "Queen of Many Colours" is coming, and that she'll be kind to those who kneel early. People are listening.
  - Break it up: **+1 Military**. Whether it's -1 or +1 Smallfolk depends on how roughly they do it.
  - Follow him home: he's a real Dragon Army agent, and it's a lead for the next mission.
- **Kneeling to a dragonborn PC.** A handful of refugees who saw the dragonborn PC rise at High Hill start leaving offerings at his door (see [[Wurm (DM)]]).
  - If the party discourages it gently: no change.
  - If they let it grow: **-1 Military**, as soldiers mutter about a "dragon cult".
- **The shipwright's list.** Sigmund Pross is quietly selling places on his ships to wealthy families, "in case the walls fall". The Smallfolk find out.
  - Expose him: **+2 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds**.
  - Keep his secret in exchange for two places for the party's own people: **+1 Guilds**, and a secret that can come out later.
- **A dragonborn PC in the alley.** Use this once Vendri's protection is gone (Military Wary or worse), or the docks have turned (Smallfolk Wary or worse). The dragonborn PC, alone on an errand, is cornered in an alley by half a dozen dockhands or off-duty soldiers who've decided he's a spy. He won't fight back. He never has. The party hears it, or finds him after.
  - Get there in time and talk them down: **+1 Refugees**, and a dragonborn PC remembers it.
  - Get there in time and beat them bloody: **-1 Military** or **-1 Smallfolk** (whoever the attackers were), but no one tries it again.
  - Don't get there in time: the spirit takes a dragonborn PC halfway through the beating, and someone dies. **-2 Military, -2 Smallfolk**, and the spy rumours become a manhunt.
- **A ranger PC's grove.** Woodcutters from the trade camps start cutting the grove the ranger PC has been tending (see [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]]).
  - Drive them off: **-1 Smallfolk**.
  - Find them other wood, or pay them to stop: **+1 Smallfolk**, and the grove is safe.

## Downtime 4 (4 weeks, the longest)

> [!info] Briefing first
> Start with **[[3. Storm Clouds - Briefings#Briefing 8, Council, The Second Army|Briefing 8: Council: the second army]]**.

High summer. The city is crowded, frightened and hot. This is the downtime for big personal projects. Pick two or three:

- **The spy accusation.** A soldier who lost his brother at the farmstead loudly accuses a dragonborn PC of being a Dragon Army spy, in the middle of the Steady Beacon. A crowd gathers.
  - Defend him with words (DC 15 Persuasion): **+1 Refugees**, and the soldier backs down.
  - Defend him with fists: **-1 Military, -1 Smallfolk**.
  - Stay quiet: **-1 Refugees**, and a dragonborn PC notices who stayed quiet.
- **Fever in the trade camps.** A summer fever spreads through the crowded camps outside the walls. The healers can't keep up.
  - Spend a week nursing the sick (Medicine, or a knight PC's healer's training): **+1 Smallfolk, +1 Refugees**.
  - Convince the guilds to pay for physicians: **+1 Smallfolk**, but **-1 Guilds** if they're pushed too hard.
- **Cudgel's company.** Cudgel Ironsmile and the remains of her Ironclad Regiment have come back (the module's "Troops Return"). They want pay, and the council won't give it.
  - Lobby the council for them: **+1 Military, -1 Guilds**.
  - Find them private work: **+1 Guilds**, and Cudgel feels like a hired sword again.
- **Nightvane's maps.** Military scouts catch a Cartographers' Guild runner carrying maps of Kalaman's defences out of the city. Alexandra Nightvane claims the maps were "for a client".
  - Turn her over to Vendri: **+2 Military, -2 Guilds**.
  - Let Nightvane explain privately: she was selling to both sides, and she'll owe the party forever. **+1 Guilds**, and a powerful secret.
- **The friendly face.** Run this one in every game. Someone the party has come to know, like the recruits' sergeant, a barmaid at the Steady Beacon, a guild clerk or one of the Vogler refugees, has been dead for a week. A **sivak draconian** killed them and took their shape, and it has been close to the party ever since. Tonight it strikes, with two more sivaks, at the party's Bastion or lodgings while they sleep.
  - Afterwards, **expose it publicly** and burn the bodies in the market so the city understands the threat: **+1 Military**, but **-1 Smallfolk** as panic spreads ("anyone could be one").
  - **Keep it quiet**, and tell only Vendri: **+1 Military**, and no panic. The party now knows the Dragon Army can be anyone, and they're the only ones who know.
  - Whoever the sivak was impersonating, their faction loses its next dividend in mourning.
- **The Bakaris contract.** If the party is working on freeing a dragonborn PC, this is the downtime to do it (see "The contract" in [[Wurm (DM)]]). Bribing, stealing or politicking can move Guilds, Military or Knights.

## Mission 4: Missing Scouts

> [!info] Briefing first
> Start with **[[3. Storm Clouds - Briefings#Briefing 9, War Room, Near Home|Briefing 9: War room: near home]]**.

The surviving scout brings bad news: the Dragon Army is isolating Kalaman from the west.
- **Report to Vendri quietly:** **+1 Military**, plus the module's 100 gp each.
- **Tell the city.** Stand up in the market and warn people what's coming, so they can prepare: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Military**. Vendri wanted it kept secret to avoid panic.
- **Sell the scout's maps** of the enemy's routes to Alexandra Nightvane, who's very interested: **+1 Guilds**. It's a terrible idea, as anyone who's played Downtime 4 knows.

## Downtime 5 (1 week)

> [!info] Briefing first
> Start with **[[3. Storm Clouds - Briefings#Briefing 10, Council, The Great Victory|Briefing 10: Council: the great victory]]**.

Short and tense. Everyone knows something is coming. Pick one or two:

- **Bakaris's war council.** Lord Bakaris is lobbying the council to ride out and win a "great victory" while Vendri is away west, and the guild leaders love the idea. Darrett is worried.
  - Argue against it in council (DC 15 Persuasion): **+1 Military, -1 Guilds**. It isn't enough to stop it, which sets up Steel Springs.
  - Stay out of it: no change, but Darrett is hurt.
- **The Younger's move.** If the party is at Neutral or worse with the Guilds, Bakaris the Younger makes his big play now. He lodges a formal complaint with the council that the party has been "harbouring a draconian" (a dragonborn PC), and demands the dragonborn PC be returned to his family's custody and chained. The guilds back him.
  - Fight it in council with proof and witnesses: **+1 Refugees**. Whether the guilds lose face (-1) or the party does depends on how it goes.
  - Buy him off: it costs whatever he asks, and **+1 Guilds**. A dragonborn PC notices he was bargained for.
  - If the party is Trusted or better with the Military, Vendri steps in: a dragonborn PC is one of her esquires' company, and she won't release him. The complaint dies, but **-1 Guilds** for the embarrassment.
  - Refuse to hand a dragonborn PC over: the law is on the Younger's side. **-1 Guilds, -1 Military**, but **+1 Refugees** for standing by him.
- **The run on the granaries.** Rumours of a siege start a panic-buying rush at the city granaries, and the Fishers' and Dockhands' guilds raise prices again.
  - Calm the crowd (a knight PC's speeches are made for this): **+1 Smallfolk**.
  - Pressure the guilds to open the stores: **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds**.
- **Letters home.** A sorcerer PC gets a letter from Relgoth, and his family is frightened. A dragonborn PC wants to write his first letter but doesn't know who to. A knight PC writes to the chapterhouse. Keep it purely roleplay, with no Standing change, just a quiet scene before the storm.

## Mission 5: Wheelwatch Outpost

The outpost holds prisoners taken from the farms and villages around Kalaman.
- **Retake the outpost and hold it for the army:** **+1 Military**.

## Scenario 4: Catastrophe (Steel Springs)

Whatever the outcome, Lord Bakaris led Kalaman's troops to disaster.
- **Save Darrett and organise the retreat:** **+2 Military**.
- **Pull Vogler's militia out first,** since they were put in the worst position: **+2 Refugees, -1 Military**.
- **Arrive too late:** **-1 Military**, and a lot of empty bunks.

Afterwards, if the party publicly blames Bakaris *and the council that approved his attack*: **+1 Military, -1 Guilds**.

## The Lord's Arrival and the Raided Catacombs

> [!info] Briefing first
> Start with **[[3. Storm Clouds - Briefings#Briefing 12, The Grim Room|Briefing 12: The grim room]]**.

- **All five guild leaders and the governor die.** See the warning in [[Kalaman Guilds]]. Their successors judge the party afresh.
- **The false knights.** Caradoc and Soth's undead came disguised as Knights of Solamnia. See the warning in [[Knights of Solamnia]].
- Stopping Caradoc and driving Soth out of the catacombs: **+1 to every faction that's still at Neutral or better**. The city knows who saved it.
- **What they bring up out of the catacombs** matters too. The old Solamnic relics and records down there can go to the Military (**+1 Military**), to the Knights' chapterhouse (**+1 Knights**, and it's evidence at the Trial, [[4. Duty#The Trial]]), or to Wyhan (**+1 Mages**).
