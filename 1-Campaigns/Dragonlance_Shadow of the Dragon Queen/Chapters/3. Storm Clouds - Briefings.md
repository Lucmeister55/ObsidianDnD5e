# Storm Clouds Briefings

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. The news items are tagged where they come from the **Module** or **Companion**.

Before every downtime and before every major event in [[3. Storm Clouds - Timeline]], the party is called to a meeting. It's how the players see the war move: what the Dragon Army has taken, what's happening abroad, what their own choices did to the world, and what they could do next. And most of the time, someone asks them what Kalaman should do.

## How a Briefing Works

There are two kinds:
- **The war room.** Vendri, Darrett, maybe Cudgel or Tatina, around the big map table in Castle Kalaman. Military news, plain talk, and a decision about the next move. Short.
- **The council.** Governor Miat, the five guild leaders, Lord Bakaris, Vendri, and sometimes the refugees' Spokesperson, in the great council chamber. Politics, money, blame, and a vote. Longer, and louder.

Every briefing has the same four beats:
1. **The map.** Move the pieces. Put a big hand-drawn map of the region on the table ([[map-4.01-kalaman-player.png|Map 4.1]] and the region map from the module), and let the NPCs move tokens on it as they talk: a red marker on Vogler, another on Wheelwatch. The players see the war get closer.
2. **The news.** Three or four items, read out by whoever would know. Keep each one to a sentence.
3. **Your doing.** One item that's the direct result of something the party did, good or bad. Always include one. It's how they learn that the world remembers.
4. **The question.** A dilemma with two or three options, put to the players. Let them argue, then decide. Each option moves Standing ([[Faction Rules]]). The NPCs push for what they'd want, loudly.

**Keep them quick.** Fifteen minutes at the table, at most. The players should leave with a choice made and the next move clear.

**"Your doing" ideas** to drop in wherever they fit:
- The refugees they housed inside the walls are working the docks. Or the ones left outside are sick.
- The Shipwrights' new invention (if Tatina went to them) is already on the guild ships, not the walls.
- Bakaris the Younger has been telling the council the party are Dragon Army spies, and someone believes it.
- The farm they saved has sent a cart of food to the castle, with a letter.
- A village they didn't reach in time is gone.

## The Briefings

### Briefing 1, War Room, After the Council

*Before Downtime 1.*

*Vendri and Darrett, the night after the arrival.*

**The news:**
- Vogler is occupied. Scouts see red-and-black banners on Thornwall Keep. (Module.)
- Bandits and mercenaries are flocking north to join the Dragon Army, because it pays well. (Module, "Clarifying the Threat".)
- The enemy's commander has a name now: **Highmaster Kansaldi Fire-Eyes**. Her own soldiers say she can see a lie. (Module.)
- The trade camps outside the Trade Gate have doubled in a week. Not all of the newcomers are from Vogler.

**The question: what to do with the refugees' fighters?** A few dozen Vogler men and women can hold a spear.
- **Draft them into the garrison:** **+1 Military, -1 Refugees** (they wanted to stay together).
- **Let them form their own militia**, under their Spokesperson: **+1 Refugees, -1 Military** (Vendri doesn't like soldiers she can't command).
- **Put them to work on the walls** for pay, which the guilds have to find: **+1 Refugees, -1 Guilds**.
- **Send the youngest to the Knights' chapterhouse** to train as squires: **+1 Knights, -1 Refugees** (their families want them close).

### Briefing 2, War Room, Orders

*Before Mission 1.*

*Darrett, alone, on the castle steps at dawn, nervous about his first real command duty.*

**The news:**
- The boilerdrak that burned Vogler was a **gnome's invention**, and the gnome lives south of the city: **Tatina Rookledust**. (Companion.)
- Someone asked about her in the market last week: a red-haired woman, a long way from home. (Companion: Red Ruin.)

**The question:** none. It's a mission briefing. Let Darrett be earnest, and let the party give him advice he'll remember.

### Briefing 3, Council, Prices

*Before Downtime 2.*

*The full council. The bazaar is packed and prices are up by half (companion). The smallfolk are angry, and the guilds are rich.*

**The news:**
- Tatina has been appointed chief of tech development, and refuses to build weapons. (Companion.)
- Councillor **Uri Stonegrey** has been seen "welcoming" refugees at the trade camps. (Companion.)
- Two more villages to the north have been emptied. The roads west are thick with carts.
- **Your doing:** where Tatina ended up (the castle, the docks or the party's Bastion), and who's grumbling about it.

**The question: price controls on grain and fish?**
- **Cap the prices:** **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds**.
- **Let the market be**, and the guilds promise "voluntary charity": **+1 Guilds, -1 Smallfolk**.
- **Buy grain for the city** out of the treasury (it's what Vendri wants, for the siege): **+1 Military**, and the treasury will be short later.

### Briefing 4, War Room, The Marshal's Gambit

*Before Scenario 2.*

*Vendri, Darrett and the map. Tokens everywhere.*

**The news:**
- A Dragon Army column is moving south along the Vingaard. Vendri means to hit it before it reaches the farmlands.
- Dragonnels have been seen over the Estwilde border. (Module.)

**The question: where do the party ride?** With the main attack, or with the small force guarding the farms behind it?
- **With the attack:** **+1 Military**.
- **With the farms:** **+1 Smallfolk**. If the attack goes badly, the farms are still safe.
- **Ask Wyhan to scry the column first**, and wait a day for her answer: **+1 Mages, -1 Military** (Vendri loses the element of surprise).

### Briefing 5, War Room, Something Else Out There

*Before Mission 2.*

*Vendri, puzzled, with a farmer's report in her hand.*

**The news:**
- A farmstead east of the city was attacked in the night, and not by the Dragon Army. The family says it was **dead men in armour, burning with violet fire**. (Companion.)
- A knight named **Durstan Rial** stayed behind so they could run. (Companion: it's Caradoc, inside him.)
- The old soldiers say the dead of Nightlund wander closer every year, but never like this ([[Soth's Host]], homebrew).

**The question:** none. Let the party feel the chill.

### Briefing 6, Council, The Knights

*Before Downtime 3.*

*The full council, and it's angry. Word of the undead has spread, and Kalaman blames the knights, as it always has.*

**The news:**
- "Durstan" has asked to be enrolled in Kalaman's army. (Companion.) Darrett is delighted.
- Rumours say the undead serve "the Dread Knight". Nobody on the council believes it, or admits to.
- The Knights' chapterhouse wants to recruit openly in the city for the first time in years.
- **Your doing:** the farm's owner, the dwarf Quince Eshfell, has sent a cart of food and a *driftglobe*. (Companion.)

**The question: may the Knights recruit openly in Kalaman?**
- **Yes:** **+1 Knights, -1 Smallfolk**.
- **No:** **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Knights**.
- **Only under Vendri's command**, as Kalaman's soldiers first (DC 15 Persuasion to sell it): **+1 Military**, and both sides grumble.

### Briefing 7, War Room, The Road to Maelgoth

*Before Mission 3.*

*Vendri, with the door shut, and Darrett guarding it.*

**The news:**
- Vendri means to send to **Maelgoth** for help, without asking the council. (Companion.) Every road west is blockaded except one.
- She's risking her command, and she knows it.

**The question: do the party back her?**
- **Back her, and keep it quiet:** **+1 Military**.
- **Tell the council:** **+1 Guilds, -2 Military**. Vendri never quite trusts them again.

### Briefing 8, Council, The Second Army

*Before Downtime 4.*

*The full council, if the party brings them the Maelgoth scouts' scroll. A war room with Vendri alone, if they don't.*

**The news:**
- The scroll says there's a **second Dragon Army**, the Blue, already at war with Maelgoth. (Companion.)
- Silvanesti fell last year. Kendermore is under the Black Army. Nobody in Kalaman had put it together until now: it isn't a raid, it's a war across all Ansalon. (Homebrew, from [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]] and [[Milo Geartickle (DM)]].)
- Lord Bakaris and councillor **Sigmund Pross** want an all-out attack, "to end this before Maelgoth's war comes here". (Companion.)
- **Your doing:** whether Vendri's messenger got through the road they cleared.

**The question: tell the city?** The scroll is in the party's hands.
- **Read it in the market:** **+1 Smallfolk, -1 Guilds** (the guilds wanted calm for trade). Kalaman starts to take the war seriously.
- **Council only:** **+1 Guilds**. The council shrugs it off, as the companion has it, and the city stays calm.
- **Vendri only:** **+1 Military**.
- **Wyhan first:** let the Mages scry the Blue Army before anyone else hears of it. **+1 Mages, -1 Military**. Vendri learns it second-hand.

### Briefing 9, War Room, Near Home

*Before Mission 4.*

*Vendri, gently. This one's close to the refugees.*

**The news:**
- Two scouts watching Vogler from six miles east have gone silent for a week. (Module, companion.)
- Smoke over Vogler. They're cutting the old bridge's timber for something.
- **Your doing:** the refugees' Spokesperson asks to send a guide with the party, someone who knows the woods. Take them, and it's a mission perk (Refugees).

**The question:** none, unless the Spokesperson wants revenge more than scouts. Then: let refugee volunteers come along? **+1 Refugees**, but every one of them who dies costs **-1 Refugees** later.

### Briefing 10, Council, The Great Victory

*Before Downtime 5.*

*The full council. Cudgel's back, half her company dead. Lord Bakaris has the floor.*

This is the timeline's **"Bakaris's war council"**. Run it here.

**The news:**
- The Ironclad Regiment came back from the wilds with ten men. (Module.)
- A Dragon Army force has split off from the main army, small and exposed. Bakaris calls it the chance of the war. Vendri, away west, can't argue. (Module, "Battle at Steel Springs".)
- **Your doing:** if the party has the Wheelwatch letters already, they know it's a trap. If not, they only suspect.

**The question: the attack.** It goes ahead whatever they say (it's Steel Springs), but how they argue decides who remembers it:
- **Argue against it** (DC 15 Persuasion): **+1 Military, -1 Guilds**.
- **Stay silent:** no change, and later Vendri asks them why.
- **Back Bakaris:** **+1 Guilds, -2 Military**, and Steel Springs is on them too.

### Briefing 11, War Room, Wheelwatch

*Before Mission 5.*

*Darrett and the commander of the Wheelwatch force (Cudgel or Jeyev), while Vendri is away south.* (Companion.)

**The news:**
- **Wheelwatch Outpost** has fallen, twenty-four miles southeast. (Module.)
- The Marshal has left the city with most of the garrison. Lord Bakaris is still in it.

**The question:** none. It's the orders: open the gate, free the prisoners, take the letters.

### Briefing 12, The Grim Room

*After Steel Springs, before the Lord's Arrival.*

*Darrett, filthy, exhausted, in command of what's left. Lord Bakaris sits in the corner and doesn't speak.* (Companion, "Battle Conclusion".)

**The news:**
- Half the army is dead or missing. Bakaris the Younger is "believed dead". (The party may know better.)
- Kansaldi was there, on a red dragon. The soldiers can't stop talking about it.
- There are **five** Dragon Armies, if the party read the Wheelwatch letters. (Companion.)
- **Your doing:** the units and allies they saved at the Inkwater are alive. Name them.

**The question: Lord Bakaris.** The army wants him hanged.
- **Hand him to the soldiers' justice:** **+1 Military, +1 Refugees, -2 Guilds**. Ugly.
- **Protect him**, as Darrett and Becklin want: **+1 Knights**. He'll remember who saved him, and it won't help (he still opens the gate in the siege).
- **Send him to the council to be judged:** **+1 Guilds**. They let him off.
