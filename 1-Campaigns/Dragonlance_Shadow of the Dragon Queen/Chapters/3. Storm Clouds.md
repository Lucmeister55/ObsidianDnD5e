## Summary

This chapter handles everything between the players' flight from Vogler to Kalaman, culminating in their confrontation with Lord Soth in the catacombs (until then known only as the Dread Knight, a whispered legend of a shadow cursing Dargaard; his name is rediscovered in the catacombs, see [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]]), and learning that they must travel to the Northern Wastes to foil Takhisis' plans. For the most part, the [[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]] companion guide may be followed, with deviations being mentioned here in this note.

This act will chronologically be the longest, giving the players enough time to gain plenty of reputation with a number of competing factions in the city, and to learn various hints and clues about the world and the looming threat of the Dragon Queen. Between every major story beat and mission, copious amounts of downtime will be provided, allowing for taking bastion turns, crafting, research, sucking up to factions, and so forth. These periods will vary from weeks to months.

> [!example] Skirmish option: the alarm goes up
> *Source: **Homebrew** ([[Skirmish Battles]]).*
>
> If the party's infiltration fails and the horns sound, don't run the whole garrison as individual stat blocks. Run the courtyard (W1) as a skirmish.

> - **Allies:** the freed prisoners as 1 **Militia**, plus Becklin as a champion if she's there.

## Scenario 4: Catastrophe

*Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Battle at Steel Springs|ch. 4, "Battle at Steel Springs"]]) and **WoK** scenario 4, the retreat from the losing battle.*

With WoK, play scenario 4. Without it, run the module's "Retreat from Steel Springs" and its battlefield events table. Faction standings feed in through the mission check-ins ([[Faction Rules#Mission Check-ins]]).

> [!example] Skirmish: the Inkwater Crossing
> *Source: **Homebrew** ([[Skirmish Battles]]). Run it right after WoK scenario 4, in place of "Commanders' Retreat".*
>
> The army is running, and the last thing between the Dragon Army and the wounded is the ford over the Inkwater.
> - **Objective:** hold the crossing for **5 rounds**, until Darrett and the commanders' riders are across. Then fall back.
> - **Allies:** 2 **Infantry**, starting **Bloodied** (the wounded); 1 **Knights** unit at half HP (Darrett's riders, arriving on round 3 from the north). Use any faction units the party has earned.
> - **Enemies:** 2 **Dragon Army Soldiers** (mounted, speed 50), 1 **Dragon Army Crossbows** in the trees, then 1 **Baaz** from the Fray on round 2.
> - **Round 4: Kansaldi.** She rides out of the smoke on the far bank with her guard, and stops the fighting around her with a raised hand to preach. Run her as a **monster** ([[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]). She can't die here: when Bloodied, she withdraws, laughing and delighted ("Yes! Test me again, children!"). This is the party's second look at her after Vogler, and the first time they can hurt her.
> - **The twist.** Across the stream, the party sees **Bakaris the Younger** drop his sword and kneel to her. This is how he becomes "unaccounted for, believed dead" (module), and it's the start of his defection ([[Bakaris the Younger]]). Lord Bakaris, in shock on Darrett's saddle, sees it too, and says nothing to anyone.
> - **Steel Springs, the stream:** units in the water are on **bad footing** ([[Weapon Triangle#Where Each Weapon Shines]]).

## Scenario 5: Valley of Barbed Tails

*Source: **WoK** scenario 5. The module doesn't reference it, so where it goes and what it changes is **Homebrew**.*

Run it after Steel Springs, as the commanders retreat ("Commanders' Retreat" in the module). If you give it a custom outcome, tie it to the [[Kalaman Military]] track: a **win** is +1, and a **loss** means Vendri's losses show in the city, -1 Kalaman Smallfolk.

## The Lord's Arrival and the Raided Catacombs

*Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#The Lord's Arrival|ch. 4, "The Lord's Arrival"]] and "Raided Catacombs"). The bullets below are **Homebrew**.*

The council massacre and the catacombs. The characters reach level 6 after defeating Sarlamir.
- **The name.** Soth's name is rediscovered here (see [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]] and [[Lord Soth]]).
- **The hollow Ispin.** See [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]].
- **The deadline.** When Soth takes the Cataclysm's fire, he lets the date slip, out of contempt for the people he's talking to. See "The Deadline" in [[4. Duty]].
- **The Guilds' clock.** The council massacre kills the guild leaders. The [[Kalaman Guilds]] stop paying per downtime and pay one final dividend before the Wastes (see [[Faction Rules#Dividends]]).
