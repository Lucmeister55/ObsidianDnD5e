## Summary

> [!homebrew] **The campaign proper starts here.** Chapters 0 to 2 were the prologue, planned in full. From here on it is a **high-level plan**, to be revised once the prologue has been played: characters may have been customised, swapped or replaced, and Vogler may have gone differently ([[DM Guide#Chapters]]). That is why the chapters say "a knight PC" or "a tinkerer PC" instead of a name: each label is the kind of character a thread suits best ([[DM Guide#Roles, Not Names]]).

This chapter handles everything between the players' flight from Vogler to Kalaman, culminating in their confrontation with Lord Soth in the catacombs (until then known only as the Dread Knight, a whispered legend of a shadow cursing Dargaard; his name is rediscovered in the catacombs, see [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]]), and learning that they must travel to the Northern Wastes to foil Takhisis' plans. For the most part, the [[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]] companion guide may be followed, with deviations being mentioned here in this note.

This act will chronologically be the longest, giving the players enough time to gain plenty of reputation with a number of competing factions in the city, and to learn various hints and clues about the world and the looming threat of the Dragon Queen. Between every major story beat and mission, copious amounts of downtime will be provided, allowing for taking bastion turns, crafting, research, sucking up to factions, and so forth. These periods will vary from weeks to months.

### Arriving in Kalaman

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#Arriving in Kalaman|ch. 4, "Arriving in Kalaman"]] to "Reporting for Duty").

- **Refugees at the shore.** The flotilla lands outside the city, and the Vogler survivors are sent to the ramshackle **trade camps** outside the Trade Gate. They aren't let in.
- **Darrett's armour.** He opens Becklin's box: a full suit of Solamnic plate, made for him. Let him have the moment.
- **The delegation.** The party, Darrett and Lord Bakaris are taken up to **Castle Kalaman**, where Bakaris loudly claims the credit for leading Vogler's people to safety.
- **The council.** Governor Calof Miat, the guild leaders and **Marshal [[Nestra Vendri|Vendri]]** hear the report. The council doubts the threat and doesn't want a war. Vendri does believe it, and she gets her way in the end: the party are taken on as **esquires** of the Kalaman military, special operatives with a lieutenant's rank and **5 gp a week** ([[Kalaman Military]]).
- **Reporting for duty.** The party get rooms in the castle, and Darrett becomes their liaison with Vendri.

### Choosing the Bastion

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. The Bastion itself uses the normal 2024 Bastion rules. This only decides **where** it is.

Vendri has a handful of empty properties the city can spare. Let the party walk round them and pick one. It's mostly flavour. Facilities, hirelings and Bastion turns all work as normal, wherever it is. Each choice adds one small touch later on.

| Bastion | Where | What it's like | The small difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Old Beacon House** | Out on the harbour wall, beside one of the pre-Cataclysm beacon towers | Salt, wind and gulls, and a view of every ship in the bay. Cold in winter. | You always hear harbour news first. In the siege, it overlooks the harbour, and whoever's posted there spots the enemy boats coming. |
| **Hetch's Second Warehouse** | The docks, two streets from the Firefishers' warehouse | A big, draughty, echoing shed, easy to fill with anything. | Plenty of storage space. The Firefishers are neighbours, which is useful, or a problem ([[Kalaman Guilds]]). |
| **The Fallen Chapterhouse** | A shuttered Solamnic hall in the old quarter, closed since the Cataclysm | Dusty oak, cracked stained glass, and a crest someone chiselled off long ago. | The Knights notice (+1 Knights the first time they visit), and so does the city. | **Brookside Farm** | Just outside the Trade Gate, near the refugee camps | Stone farmhouse, barn, orchard and a stream. Room to grow things. | Close to the refugees and a ranger PC's grove. In the siege it's **outside the walls**: it will burn unless someone defends it. |
| **Rookledust's Annex** | Next to wherever Tatina sets up, in the artisans' quarter | Half workshop, half house, and something always ticking. | Tatina pops round, uninvited. A tinkerer PC's drone tinkering gets a quiet bonus (advantage once per downtime), and occasionally something explodes. |

- **Afterwards:** councillor **Uri Stonegrey** turns up at the trade camps, "introducing himself" to the refugees and really recruiting spies. The bazaar is packed, and everything costs 50% more (DC 10 Investigation to find what you want).

On round 10, the gate is open.

## Scenario 4: Catastrophe

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]], Part 5, "Battle at Steel Springs").

- **The warning.** The morning after Wheelwatch, the fargab crackles: Tatina, in a panic. Lord Bakaris has taken the army out to Steel Springs. (Without a fargab, Wyhan reaches them with *sending*. If they try to warn Bakaris, he refuses to listen.) It's about 35 miles, so it's a forced march: Constitution saves against exhaustion all round.
- **Arrival.** The trap has already sprung. **Kansaldi** is here, on her red dragon **Ignia**, and the party gets a good look at her before a **Dragon Queen abomination** (a human remade by eating draconian flesh) leads a charge at them.

- **Red Ruin comes.** As Darrett rides clear, Red Ruin swoops in on her dragonnel: hit and run, out of reach, and at least one shot from her explosive hand crossbow. After two rounds, or at half her hit points, Ignia breathes a plume of fire straight up, the signal to fall back, and Red Ruin breaks off.

## The Lord's Arrival and the Raided Catacombs

> [!info] Briefing first
> Start with **[[3. Storm Clouds - Briefings#Briefing 12, The Grim Room|Briefing 12: The grim room]]**.

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[05-chapter-4-shadow-of-war#The Lord's Arrival|ch. 4, "The Lord's Arrival"]] and "Raided Catacombs"). The bullets below are **Homebrew**.

The council massacre and the catacombs. The characters reach level 6 after the catacombs.

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]], Part 6, "Raided Catacombs"), with **Homebrew** changes marked.

- **Something's wrong.** Outside the castle, Tatina is arguing with the guards: she went out for supplies and they won't let her back in. DC 12 Charisma: a unit of Knights of Solamnia arrived this morning, and the council has sealed the castle until it can speak to them.
- **The knights at the door** are **wights** in Solamnic armour (passive Perception 15 at 10 feet: a soft violet glow under their visors). These are the false knights ([[Knights of Solamnia]]).
- **Leedara** appears beside a servant's body on the stairs, killed by a single sword-thrust, and urges the party on. DC 20 Charisma to bring her along.
- **The council massacre.** The council is dead, and **Caradoc** is waiting, still in Durstan's body if that thread ran. He has a sneer ready for each character's hypocrisy. If Leedara is there, his sarcasm cracks: "Your master still has much to answer for. The same can be said for you." In the fight, he jumps to a new host whenever his current one drops below half hit points, and prefers melee fighters with weak Charisma saves.
- **The catacombs.** A tomb for fallen knights, built so the living would remember that no oath makes a knight incapable of evil. The Cataclysm fire found its way here because of what the dead had done. The spirit of **Knight Jandin** (R6, "Shrine of the Redeemed") is a knight who once fled and left her comrades to die, then spent her life helping others find redemption. She warns the party what's in the last room, and that it's beyond them.
- **Soth is there.** When the party opens R7, **Soth** is finishing. He holds the rusted, flame-wrapped dragonlance. Attacks barely touch him, and he doesn't bother to fight. He drives the lance into a corpse, which rises as a **skeletal knight**, and the fire spreads to the coffins: in **10 turns**, the whole tomb rises and walks on Kalaman. The ritual ends when the lance is pulled out (DC 25 Strength while the knight stands, an action once it's down). Then Soth punches a hole in the wall, and steps off the ledge into the air without looking back.
  - *Homebrew:* the **hollow Ispin** stands at his side ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]]), and leaves with him. Soth's name and the deadline come out here too (below).
  - *Homebrew:* **Sarlamir's** story stays: the lance is his, and Karavarix remembers him in [[6. Conquest]].
- **Leedara's goodbye.** She tells them the knight's name (in this campaign, the name everyone forgot: **Soth**), that she's bound to him and can feel he's gone north into the Wastes, where he's been for months, and that the fire is for something worse. They should take the lance. If asked, she admits what she is: one of Isolde's elven attendants, whose lie drove Soth to murder, cursed to haunt him.

> [!check] Full fight. **Objective:** pull the lance out of the risen knight before 10 turns pass, or the catacombs empty into Kalaman. Killing the knight makes it easy.

The homebrew beats for this scene:
- **The name.** Soth's name is rediscovered here (see [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]] and [[Lord Soth]]).
- **The hollow Ispin.** See [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]].
- **The deadline.** When Soth takes the Cataclysm's fire, he lets the date slip, out of contempt for the people he's talking to. See "The Deadline" in [[4. Duty]].
- **The Guilds' clock.** The council massacre kills the guild leaders. The [[Kalaman Guilds]] stop paying per downtime and pay one final dividend before the Wastes (see [[Faction Rules#Dividends]]).
