## Overview

This act loosely follows the plot of the first half of [[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns|Chapter 3: When Home Burns]]. In this act, all the characters will slowly enter the story and be allocated to a player. The relatively quiet and positive narrative will culminate with the battle at High Hill, where the final character will be revealed. This storyline will mostly adopt the adaptions made in the companion guide: [[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]].

## Running This Chapter

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Running This Chapter|ch. 3, "Running This Chapter"]]) and **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Parts 1 to 3), adjusted for this campaign.

The module's chapter has two halves. This act is the first: a day and a half in Vogler, meeting the village and each other at the funeral and the Kingfisher Festival, ending with the betrayal at High Hill. The second half, when war comes, is [[2.00 Broken Silence]].

- **Levels.** The module has the characters start at 2nd level, reach 3rd after the Battle of High Hill and 4th after the evacuation. The origin characters start here at **3rd level** ([[1.04 The Battle of High Hill]]) and reach **4th** at the end of the evacuation ([[2.03 Evacuation]]).
- **Familiarise yourself with the gazetteer** in [[1.02 Arrival in Vogler#Vogler at a Glance|Vogler at a Glance]]: you'll improvise a lot of villagers.

> [!tip] Deliberate welcome (module)
> Vogler welcomes good-natured visitors of all walks. As the characters explore, play up its quaint charms and hospitable locals. It should begin to feel like a home away from home, and the villagers like family, so that when Vogler suffers disaster, the characters feel the loss and want to save the villagers and help their friends rebuild new lives. (Companion: don't make it so welcoming that it feels unnatural or unnerving.)

### Behind the Scenes: Ispin and Vogler

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Part 1). Soth's part in it is **Homebrew** ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]]).

- **How Ispin died.** Two nights before the party arrives, while patrolling alone on the outskirts, Ispin met his old friend **Cudgel** and her half-ogre lieutenant **Gragonis**. This Cudgel was really the black-robed mage **Lohezet** under a *disguise self* spell, sent to lure him into a trap. They told Ispin they had found direwolf tracks deeper in the forest and asked him to look them over. Once he was far enough from town, [[Red Ruin]] dived through the trees on her dragonnel and skewered him in the back, killing him at once (the companion has Kansaldi do this on her young red dragon, but here Kansaldi only passed on Soth's order). Red Ruin flew off and Lohezet teleported away. Gragonis crept back to the Ironclad camp alone, with Ispin's shield.
- **How he was found.** Two Vogler hunters found the body the next morning and carried it to Becklin. They reported a monster attack, because of the large tracks. Becklin doesn't believe it (see [[1.02 Arrival in Vogler#Parting Gift|Parting Gift]]).
- **Why Ispin.** The Red Dragon Army learned that a Knight of Solamnia had been asking questions for a year about the Dragon Queen and rumours of strange draconic soldiers.
- **Why Kansaldi is in Vogler at all.** The village and its ferry connect Kalaman directly to the Northern Wastes, and so to the City of Lost Names, which is key to the army's plan to conquer Kalaman. Holding Vogler cuts Kalaman off from the Wastes, and gives the army the village's resources. Appearing on her dragon at the end of the invasion also slows Kalaman's decision to counterattack, out of fear of how many other dragons the army might have.
- **Why she lets the refugees go.** Her strategy is to overcrowd Kalaman with refugees while cutting off the city's resources, and she wants the survivors to spread word of how fearsome she and her dragon are (see [[2.03 Evacuation#Kansaldi Arrives: Raven's Death]]).
