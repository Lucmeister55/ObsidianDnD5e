## Arrival in Vogler

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Arriving in Vogler|ch. 3, "Arriving in Vogler"]]), with **Homebrew** character introductions.

As soon as the entourage unmounts in the village square, have Becklin steer Edric towards the Fishbowl tavern to meet up with Darrett and Cudgel.

> The Fishbowl is one low room with a fire at the far end, and every table in it is taken. The air is thick with pipe smoke and frying fish. In a corner an old man with a banjo is losing a long argument with his own strings, and nobody minds. Behind the bar, a glass jar hangs from a beam with one fat goldfish turning slowly in it.
>
> By the fire, a short, broad dwarf with copper braids bound in iron rings is singing something unfit for company. An iron-shod club leans against her chair, and she holds a tankard the size of her head. You have ridden with her on Ispin's road, and the stories were not exaggerated. Across the table, a young man in a polished tabard has risen halfway to his feet, a hand of cards still fanned in his fist. It is Darrett. He is grinning, and he has knocked over his ale.

> The man sitting opposite him does not get up. He is young and lean and sun-browned from days on the river road, and he deals the next hand without looking at his fingers. His hair falls to his shoulders, silver as a new coin in the firelight. He wears a sky-blue shirt under a dark vest sewn with small stars, and his right sleeve is rolled to the elbow, over a line of moons inked down the forearm, from full to thin to gone. A neat pile of Darrett's coins sits at his elbow. He looks at the knight's tabard on your chest, and smiles as if you were exactly the person he'd been waiting for.

*Prompt the players to decide who will claim this origin character, then give them the character sheet.*

**Cudgel** is drunk by mid-afternoon. She hauls Edric onto the bench beside her, and the singing stops being cheerful: loud, quiet in the wrong places, grinning without meaning it. A few of these, one at a time:

> "Lad. LAD. You look just like him when he's disappointed in me. Don't. I've had a day."
>
> "Fourteen years he had you on the road. Thirty he had me. And you know what he sent me, when he went home to retire? A letter. Two lines. 'Come for the festival, Cud, and bring coin for the tavern.' That's it. Two lines."
>
> "They're gonna put him in the ground tomorrow, and I'm gonna stand there, and I'm gonna say something loud and clever, and I'll feel nothing. Because I'll be sober by then. That's the problem with sober."
>
> "A beast, they said. A BEAST. That man once stared down a fire giant over a card game. A beast. Pah." *(She drinks. She doesn't finish the thought.)*

**Becklin** strides in, says "That's enough of that, Cud" quietly, takes her under one arm and walks her back to camp. Cudgel goes, mumbling "I'm fine, I'm fine, I'm *fine*", and is gone until the funeral.

**Davos** came for a ferry to Kalaman, delayed by the festival, and sat down with Darrett because he heard "knight". He has been cheating him at cards for an hour, with a little magic. Once claimed, he tags along through the festival.

### Optional Arrival Vignettes

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** (Part 3, "First Day in Vogler").

If characters arrive separately, give each a villager they'll meet again:
- **By ferry.** Beside **Froswin**, the kender stallholder, who takes an interest in their gear. If they're kind, she offers to help them find any vendor at the festival.
- **At the Fishbowl.** **Elthar**, the owner, offers a free sample of a new pastry for an honest opinion. It's delicious, though heavy on the butter.
- **Through the River Gate.** A craft table where children make flower crowns. One child silently offers a crown and scurries back to their parents. (They come back during the evacuation, [[2.03 Evacuation]].)

### Running Order

1. **Day 1, afternoon.** Arrival, the Fishbowl, and the **missing child** search with Darrett (Ross, who "saw a dragon in the woods"). The wounded dragonnel crashes in here.
2. **Day 1, dusk.** **Ispin's funeral** and reception: tales of Ispin, the **heckler**, **Cudgel**, **Leedara**, and Milo.
3. **Day 2, morning.** **Thornwall Keep**: breakfast, Darrett, Ispin's parting gift.
4. **Day 2, midday.** The **Kingfisher Festival**, at most 4 activities.
5. **Day 2, afternoon.** **High Hill**, the reenactment, the betrayal.

### Vogler at a Glance

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Vogler Gazetteer|ch. 3, "Vogler Gazetteer"]], [[map-3.01-vogler-player.png|map 3.1]]) and **Companion** (Part 3, Appendix B).

About **four hundred** people at the foot of a pre-Cataclysm bridge that stops halfway across the Vingaard, nominally under Maelgoth, troubled only by Wastes bandits and beasts ([[Vogler]]). The festival honours **Habbakuk**, the Fisher King, and Lord Decater Vogler's victory over Istar. Mention the Knights and the room goes quiet.

**Westide House.** Lord Bakaris claims raiders burned his home in Estwilde. In truth he and his son were driven out, and have spent a year in Vogler throwing money around.
- **Why Vogler puts up with him (homebrew):** money. After the hard winter and the sahuagin that killed Raven's father, the village couldn't pay for the wharf repairs or the Regiment's contract to train the militia. **Bakaris paid for both, as a loan against the wharf**, and won't call it in while he's treated like a lord. His title also keeps the Maelgoth tax collector away. Raven knows he didn't come for the fish, but needs the loan and keeps quiet. Bakaris knows she knows. Everyone else grumbles and drinks his free ale.

### The Missing Child and the Stolen Shield

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** (Part 3, "Finding Darrett" and "Finding the Lost Child"), with **Homebrew** changes.

- **The call for help.** **Ross** says he saw a dragon in the woods yesterday and ran away when no one believed him. Raven organises a search, Darrett volunteers, and she offers **25 gp** to anyone who helps. (The companion has Darrett missing from the Fishbowl: the party waits two hours, then a **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check leads them to Raven.)
- **Finding the boy.** An hour in the woods and **DC 15 Wisdom (Survival)** finds Ross's or Darrett's tracks. On a failure they wander an hour and take **1d4 - 1 piercing** from a thorn bush or root, and the DC drops by 1 after each failure.
- **The dragonnel.** Once they find the child or Darrett, **passive Perception 15** hears loud flapping. A [[dragon-army-dragonnel-dsotdq|Dragon Army dragonnel]] drops out of the trees (a **surprise round** if nobody passed). It was a scout's mount whose rider died in an accident; it has shed its rider and barding, so **AC 12**. Darrett helps.
- **Homebrew.** The boy blubbers that a dragon is chasing him, and the dragonnel crashes through the boughs, dazed, wounded and feral. It flees at the first hostile action. Near it they find a **belt clasp** (Red Ruin's). Villagers say it's just a dragonnel from the Wastes (false).
- **Fighting it (five players, level 3):** 2 dragonnels that retreat at half HP (the companion is written for level 2: let them fight on, or add a third). After a kill, **DC 15 Intelligence (History)** identifies the species and **DC 18 Intelligence (Investigation)** finds saddle marks.
- **After the search,** a **long rest** before the festival.

**Ispin's shield** was gone before the villagers found him: Gragonis took it off the corpse that night (companion). It was never at the funeral, and nobody knows it was stolen. The wounds look bestial, but Becklin thinks them too long and precise. It was Red Ruin's dragonnel.

### The Funeral and Reception

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Final Farewell|ch. 3, "Final Farewell"]]), with **Companion** and **Homebrew** where marked.

At dusk, on the dock behind the Brass Crab, Ispin's body lies shrouded in a small boat, locals in boats around it. (*Homebrew:* Soth's servants take it downstream, [[Soth's Host]].)

#### Meeting Milo (homebrew)

*Before Becklin speaks, introduce the kender origin character.*

> The dock behind the Brass Crab is crowded with lanterns and low voices. Out at the far end a little boat rests on the water, with a shape in a grey shroud laid along it, and a string of paper kingfishers hung over it.
>
> A small figure is kneeling at the edge of the planks, leaning out over that boat as far as a person can lean without going in. They are round-faced and freckled, with a topknot stuck full of pencils and twigs, and a mustard-yellow scarf wound up to their ears. A brass thing the size of a fist hums at their shoulder, turning to watch each mourner in turn.
>
> The figure sees you and waves, with a hand that is holding somebody else's candle. "Hello! Sorry. Is it rude to ask where they've put his shield? I only want to look."

*Prompt the players to decide who will claim this origin character, then give them the character sheet ([[Milo Geartickle]]).*

**Milo's attempt.** Milo tries to "borrow" the shield from the body before launch, and it isn't there: a plain shroud, his sword, nothing on his arm. Milo can hush it up or tell Edric, which plants the question. Becklin hasn't told Edric ([[#Parting Gift]]).

#### Becklin and the Eulogy (module)

In full plate and horned helm, Becklin introduces herself to the others as Ispin's friend. The service is a **celebration** of over-the-top stories. At dusk she takes the stage by the boat:

> "You all knew Ispin as a friend, and there were none like him. Before he died, he told me this: 'There should be tales, not tears, for an old scoundrel like me. Make sure everyone knows my stories when I head off on the greatest adventure of them all.'"
>
> Villagers carry the boat to the dock's edge, where she and Cudgel lower it into the water. "So, let us do just that. On the eve of the Kingfisher Festival, we celebrate you, Ispin. Good travels, friend. Until we meet again."

The boat drifts out of sight. After a moment of silence, Becklin invites everyone into the Brass Crab.

#### Tales of Ispin (module)

Becklin opens with Ispin's favourite, the Mount Nevermind artillerist who launched her pet weasel to Sancrist and hired Ispin to fetch it, then invites everyone to share. **Edric's player starts** ([[Ispin Greenshield#Stories to Tell About Him]]), a **+1 villagers** moment. **Inspiration** for a creative or touching story:

#### Guests at the reception

- **The heckler.** **Bakaris the Younger** yawns and blurts "We've heard this a thousand times!" or "You're telling it worse than he did!" He's there for the free ale. Roleplay or **DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)** silences him, and he leaves. No blows: it makes him a cad.
- **Cudgel,** sobered up at camp, introduces herself to the rest of the party as Ispin's old friend. She's still abrasive and apologises later. (Module: jovial, asks how they knew him, talks about the reenactment, leaves early.)
- **Leedara,** a blue-skinned Silvanesti, says she was a performer who knew Ispin (companion: a seer). Polite and evasive at the edges; passive Perception 14+ notices her watching the party. **DC 16 Charisma (Persuasion)** gets her to sing a heartbreakingly beautiful Silvanesti song, then she retires. Reveal nothing of her agenda. **Stat block:** a **ghost** ([[Leedara]]) with **Change Shape** (her appearance in life, type Humanoid, ending if she drops to 0 hit points or ends it as an action).

After the funeral, Becklin invites the party to Thornwall Keep in the morning: Ispin left something for Edric.

### Parting Gift

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Parting Gift|ch. 3, "Parting Gift"]]), with **Homebrew** changes.

**Meeting Darrett.** Stalls going up, the keep ghostly in the mist. Darrett steps out of a shop with a sword at his hip and a sack under his arm: "Good morning, everyone. Glad I caught you. I hope you haven't had breakfast already." Becklin sent him for hand pies, and he brings the party along, full of questions, and mentions that Cudgel's mercenaries are joining this afternoon's reenactment.

**His running joke:** still a squire ("Becklin says next year. She's said next year for three years"), which stops being funny after Vogler ([[Darrett Highwater#Why He's Still a Squire]]). Establish him as reliable and always on the party's side.

**Thornwall Keep** ([[Vogler#The Edges]]): bronze ivy on the tallest tower in Vogler, excavation pits around it, the gnomeflinger on the roof. Becklin, in shirt and breeches, makes small talk over the hand pies while **Than** tunes the gnomeflinger noisily overhead.

**The shield (homebrew).** In the module, Becklin hands over Ispin's **+1 shield** here (carved with a scarred broadleaf tree, painted mossy green; his story was that a unicorn gave it to him in Darken Wood). It's missing, and Becklin, ashamed, says nothing until Edric asks or Milo has tried for it. Then she admits it: no shield on the body, only a bare scabbard and a torn strap. **Gragonis** took it off the corpse (companion), and the party gets it back from him at High Hill.

The module's condition for the shield becomes a plain request: **take Ispin's place in the reenactment** (he loved dying more ludicrously each year). Then she has to prepare, and Darrett suggests they all go together.

**Becklin's suspicions** (companion, "Investigating Ispin's Murder"): he was found far off his patrol route, his sword still sheathed, a single wound through the back of his armour into his heart, too long and precise for a beast, and his shield gone. If the party goes to the spot:

#### The Scene of the Murder (companion)

Ispin's blood stains the ground, surrounded by large clawed footprints.
- **DC 14 Wisdom (Survival):** the long claw marks aren't a direwolf's, and the tracks appear from nowhere with no sign of arriving or leaving. If the party fought the dragonnel, the marks are also too wide to be its.
- **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception):** broken branches on the ground and a large gap in the branches overhead, where Red Ruin burst from the trees on her dragonnel.
- **DC 16 Intelligence (Investigation):** three sets of humanoid prints besides the hunters': sharp-angled armoured greaves (Ispin), large leather boots (Gragonis), and slim, shallow shoes ill-made for the woods (Lohezet, wearing Cudgel's shape; her own boots are heavy and hobnailed, so the prints don't match hers).
- **The hunters' story.** One of the hunters who found the body saw Ispin the evening before heading into the woods with **a dwarf and an ogre**. He knew Ispin, but not the mercenaries, and never saw their faces (dusk, hoods, distance). That points at the **Regiment** (Kayolin dwarves, and one half-ogre, **Gragonis**), and a suspicious party may settle on Cudgel. It was Lohezet in her face (*disguise self*). Her alibi is Jeyev, a traitor. Play her hurt, not angry ([[Cudgel Ironsmile#Under Suspicion]]). The prints clear her: *disguise self* changes the face, not the weight.
