## The Kingfisher Festival

**Music:** [[Locations#^kingfisher-festival|Zelda - Ocarina of Time 3D: Market]]

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#The Kingfisher Festival|ch. 3, "The Kingfisher Festival"]]) and **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Part 4). The prizes and puzzles are **Homebrew**.

Max **4 activities**, then the battle of High Hill ([[1.04 The Battle of High Hill]]).

The festival is in full swing: banners and paper kingfishers, stalls selling fish pastries, sweet drinks and kingfisher crafts at **3 cp** each, an ancient tree shading the picnickers. Raven takes the stage:

> "Welcome, friends! Welcome to the Kingfisher Festival! Today is a day of not only revelry but also reflection. We are all here thanks to the courage of heroes who came before us. Let us honor our founders, our family, and all those who can't be here to celebrate today. Enjoy your festival, Vogler, with good spirits, good times, and good friends! And look to the kingfishers for good luck!"

Any villager from [[1.02 Arrival in Vogler#The People of Vogler|The People of Vogler]] might talk to the party.

### The Contests

Ispin used to donate a common magic item to each winner. This year the prizes are **useful things that pay off in the next two days**, plus a small purse and a coloured sash. Winning also makes you a Vogler "general" in the reenactment. Generic rivals add +2 to their checks. Milo will want to enter everything.

| Contest | Where | Rival | Prize | Pays off |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fish catch** | Wharf | Raven (7 ft total) | 10 gp, gold sash, **river chart** | +2 to the boat-lashing check; advantage steering downriver ([[2.02 Preparing for the Worst]]) |
| **Chess** | Market | Becklin, +4 | 5 gp, yellow sash, **Solamnic lesson** | Swap two defending units at the start of Scenario 1 |
| **Gnome flinging** | Thornwall Keep | Froswin, +3 | 5 gp, red sash, **Than's tinker's tools** | Advantage turning the boilerdrak ([[2.03 Evacuation]]) |
| **Horseback race** | River Gate | Bakaris the Younger, +3 | 5 gp, silver sash, **courier horse** (3 days) | The night scouting trip ([[2.01 Back in Vogler]]) |
| **Log roll** | Vogler's Crossing | Nesau Lanth, +5 | 5 gp, purple sash, **cleated boots**, a **secret** | Advantage on the ferry check |
| **Pie eating** | The Fishbowl | Cudgel, +4 | 5 gp, green sash, **Elthar's travel pies** | Rations |

| **Wrestling** | Outside the Brass Crab | **Gragonis**, +5 | 5 gp, blue sash, a **clue** | Where Ispin's shield went |

Gragonis goes for whoever he wrestled at High Hill.

**Fish catch.** Three 10-minute rounds. Each round, roll a **d20** on the table (add Perception if you like) for the **Wisdom (Survival) DC** to land the fish. **Advantage** with your own gear; fail by 10 or more and you're in the river. Longest total wins: Raven makes **7 ft**, the best villager 5. Everyone gets a tiny carved kingfisher; the winner also picks a blue knit cap or a fly of blue feathers.

| d20 | DC | Fish |
|---|---|---|
| 1-3 | none | No fish |
| 4-7 | 8 | Tiny river eel (1 ft) |
| 8-11 | 10 | Tiny cuirassed carp (1 ft) |
| 12-15 | 11 | Mature river eel (1.5 ft) |
| 16-18 | 12 | Mature cuirassed carp (3 ft) |
| 19 | 15 | Greater cuirassed carp (5 ft) |
| 20 | 18 | Benebog the Line Breaker (8 ft) |

- **Benebog** can be caught once. The catcher gets a fine kingfisher pole worth **50 gp** and free drinks all day. A later 20 gives a greater carp.
- **Lure riddle.** Before round one, Raven's father's old partner chants: *"Silver in the morning, black in the heat, red when the water runs brown, and never the same one twice."* Solve it (silver spinner, black fly, red lure; don't reuse the last one) and get **advantage** that round. DC 12 Intelligence (Nature) hints.
- **The river chart** is Raven's father's waxed chart of every shallow, snag and hidden landing from the bridge to the Kalaman road.
- **Bad sport.** A drunk Lord Bakaris stands beside the most competent-looking participant, mocking. **Disadvantage** on their Survival checks until sent away (roleplay or **DC 14 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)**, or Raven shoos him off).

**Chess.** Three rounds, two contested rolls each: your **Intelligence** (plus chess tools) against their **Wisdom (Insight)**, and the reverse. A win is a point. Most points wins; tie, one more Intelligence check.
- **Becklin's pass problem (puzzle).** After the game she sets up "the Kingfisher Problem": a river on the left, a cliff on the right, a narrow neck of ground and a village behind. **Defenders:** three pikemen, two archers, a knight. **Attackers:** six soldiers and a siege engine, who win if one piece reaches the village. "Where does the defender put the knight?" Answer: **pikemen across the neck, archers on the heights, the knight in reserve to plug the line where it bends, and never let the line touch the river (it gets turned).** **DC 14 Intelligence (History)** or a good answer solves it. The solver gets the lesson. It's the real pass at Vogler, and Becklin says nothing about that unless asked.

**Gnome flinging.** Three rounds: an **Intelligence** check to aim at the 30-foot pole (success gives **advantage**) then **Dexterity (Acrobatics)** to grab the ribbon mid-flight, at the same DC. **60 ft DC 13 (1 pt), 150 ft DC 15 (2), 300 ft DC 17 (3).** Everyone has a narycrash.
- **Than's counterweights (puzzle).** Than hands over weights of **3, 4, 7, 9 and 11 lb**, and the arm needs exactly **three**: **14 lb** (60 ft), **20 lb** (150 ft), **27 lb** (300 ft). Answers: 3+4+7, 4+7+9, 7+9+11. Solve it for **advantage** on the aim check. Get it wrong and you fall 10 ft short.

**Horseback race.** Horse rental 3 gp. Four obstacles, each a **Wisdom (Animal Handling)** check: **DC 10, 12, 14, 15**. Fail and a **Dexterity** check at the same DC keeps you on. Avoid: 2 points; fail then pass Dexterity: 1; fail both: 0. If the party beats Bakaris (who disdains the mock battle and won't be a general), he sulks in public and has a barbed remark later, and **+1 villagers** if done with grace. The courier horse is fast and trained to run through noise, so the scouting trip takes a third of the time.

**Log roll.** Two contestants on a rotating log, no touching or magic. Each throw is **Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics)**; best two of three wins. Nesau (+5 Acrobatics) has won every year. The boots give **advantage** against slipping or being knocked prone on wet, icy or slick ground. The **secret**: the winch hut holds the bridge's emergency ropes and pulleys, and he shows how they come apart.

**Pie eating.** One minute per pie, **Constitution DC 8, rising by 2** each round; failing knocks you out. If everyone fails the last round, highest roll wins. Cudgel is +4. Elthar hints which pie to avoid: "Never trust the pie that smells of nothing." The prize is six fish-oil pies that keep a week, each **a day's rations** and healing **1d4** once on a short rest.
- **The trap.** If Cudgel wins, she offers her general's position to the party member who got furthest. Whoever takes the title leads the Vogler side, and Gragonis's bought mercenaries single out the "general" first ([[1.04 The Battle of High Hill]]).

**Talent show.** A fitting skill check (Performance, Acrobatics...). A **20+** earns a **d6 inspiration die** until the next long rest. If anyone rolls a 20, Elthar (+6, banjo) proposes best two of three contested checks, and beating him gives the **whole party a d8** (replacing the d6). Either way Elthar teaches the winner **the festival song**, a call-and-response about the kingfisher.

**Wrestling.** Yalme referees and ties a crab-shaped cravat on each wrestler. Contested **Strength (Athletics)** to start a pin; the pinned escape with **Athletics and Acrobatics** against the pinner's roll. **Two pins** wins. Gragonis wrestles gently: he apologises for the bruises, helps his opponent up and offers to carry them to the Brass Crab. This is the nicest he will ever be. If he wins he can't be a general (he's committed to the Istarian side).
- **The clue.** A character who beats him, or who has the best Perception or Insight (**DC 14**), sees under his left sleeve a fresh welt in the shape of a shield strap, and a patch of beard fur pulled out. It points at [[1.02 Arrival in Vogler#The Missing Child and the Stolen Shield]].
