## The Kingfisher Festival

**Music:** [Market - The Legend of Zelda_ Ocarina of Time OST Remastered](ttrpg-sound:Soundbar/Zelda - Ocarina of Time 3D/Market - The Legend of Zelda_ Ocarina of Time OST Remastered.mp3)

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#The Kingfisher Festival|ch. 3, "The Kingfisher Festival"]]) and **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]]). The faction intro is **Homebrew** ([[Faction Rules]]).

Have the players do max 4 activities, at which point the battle of High Hill commences.

When the party reaches the village circle, the festival is in full swing: colourful banners and paper kingfishers, stalls selling fish pastries, sweet drinks and kingfisher crafts at **3 cp** each, and an ancient tree shading the picnickers. A band of local musicians finishes a song and Raven takes the stage (module):

> "Welcome, friends! Welcome to the Kingfisher Festival! Today is a day of not only revelry but also reflection. We are all here thanks to the courage of heroes who came before us. Let us honor our founders, our family, and all those who can't be here to celebrate today. Enjoy your festival, Vogler, with good spirits, good times, and good friends! And look to the kingfishers for good luck!"

Cheers and applause, and the festival is underway. Local musicians take turns on the stage and urge the crowd to dance. Any villager from [[1.02 Arrival in Vogler#The People of Vogler|The People of Vogler]] might talk to the party. Whenever you like, go to [[1.04 The Battle of High Hill]].

**The contests** (*Source: **Companion**, Part 4; the fish catch is from the **Module**, "Festivities"*). Ispin used to donate a common magic item to each winner, and the village keeps the tradition in his honour, but this year the prizes are **useful things that pay off in the next two days** (a poor village by a ruined bridge has more use for a boat chart than a pole of angling), plus a small purse and a coloured winner's sash. Winning also makes you a Vogler "general" in the reenactment. Two contests have a puzzle (**chess** and **gnome flinging**) and the fish catch has a lure riddle.

| Contest | Where | How it works | Rival | Prize |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fish catch** | Wharf | Three 10-minute rounds on the module's Fish to Catch table (Wisdom (Survival)), plus a lure riddle. Fail by 10 and you're in the river. Benebog the Line Breaker can be caught once. | Raven (7 feet total). Lord Bakaris heckles, drunk. | 10 gp, gold sash, the **Vogler river chart** |
| **Chess** | Market | Three rounds of Intelligence against Wisdom (Insight), 2 points a round, then **Becklin's pass problem** (puzzle). | Becklin, +4 | 5 gp, yellow sash, a **Solamnic lesson** |
| **Gnome flinging** | Thornwall Keep | Balance **Than's counterweights** (puzzle), aim (Intelligence), then grab the ribbon in midair (Dexterity (Acrobatics)): 60, 150 and 300 feet, DC 13, 15 and 17. | Froswin, +3, overjoyed | 5 gp, red sash, **Than's tinker's tools** |
| **Horseback race** | River Gate | Four obstacles, DC 10-15 Wisdom (Animal Handling), Dexterity to stay on. | Bakaris the Younger, +3 (Edric's mare says hello) | 5 gp, silver sash, a **courier horse** for three days |
| **Log roll** | Vogler's Crossing | Best two of three Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) to knock the other off. | Nesau Lanth, +5, unbeaten | 5 gp, purple sash, **cleated boots** and a **secret** |

Wrestling Gragonis matters: at High Hill he goes for whoever he wrestled. Milo will want to enter everything.

### How Each Contest Runs

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Part 4), with the fish catch from the **Module**. For any contest you can add generic rivals who add **+2** to all their checks. Ispin has donated a common magic item (from *Xanathar's Guide*) to the winners for several years, with a small bag of gold and a coloured sash. We replace the items with the prizes below, which all pay off later ([[#What the Prizes Are For]]). The winner also acts as a Vogler "general" in the reenactment.

**Fish catch (wharf).** Everyone gets fishing poles and bait, and the contest lasts three 10-minute rounds with 30 minutes in all.
- **Each round,** roll a **d20** on the table below (the companion lets you add the character's Wisdom (Perception) modifier to the roll) to see what bites and the **Wisdom (Survival) DC** to land it. A character has **advantage** if they brought their own gear, and is pulled into the water, out of the contest, if they fail by **10 or more**.

| d20 | Catch DC | Fish |
|---|---|---|
| 1-3 | none | No fish |
| 4-7 | 8 | Tiny river eel (1 ft) |
| 8-11 | 10 | Tiny cuirassed carp (1 ft) |
| 12-15 | 11 | Mature river eel (1.5 ft) |
| 16-18 | 12 | Mature cuirassed carp (3 ft) |
| 19 | 15 | Greater cuirassed carp (5 ft) |
| 20 | 18 | Benebog the Line Breaker (8 ft) |

- **The winner** has the longest total length. Raven totals **7 feet** (two mature cuirassed carp and a tiny eel), the best villager 5. Everyone gets a tiny carved kingfisher. The winner picks a blue knit cap or a fishing fly made of blue feathers (module), plus 10 gp, the gold sash and the **Vogler river chart** (below).
- **Benebog the Line Breaker** is a Vogler legend: sighting it is luck, and catching it makes you a town legend. It can be caught only once, and a later 20 gives a greater carp. The catcher gets an exceptionally crafted kingfisher fishing pole worth **50 gp**, and congratulations and free drinks all day.
- **Repeat contests:** the best villager totals **1d4 + 3** feet. Raven doesn't enter again.
- **Bad sport.** Lord Bakaris drinks heavily at the festival, watches from the wharf and stands next to the most competent-looking participant, mocking the pastime (and, in the companion, anyone who insulted his son at the funeral). For as long as he's there that character has **disadvantage** on their Survival checks. Roleplaying or a **DC 14 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)** check sends him away, or Raven shoos him off.

- **The lure riddle.** Before the first round, Raven's father's old fishing partner chants the village rule, and whoever works it out picks the right lure and gets **advantage** on every Survival check that round: *"Silver in the morning, black in the heat, red when the water runs brown, and never the same one twice."* (Morning: a silver spinner. Midday: a black fly. After rain, or in brown water: a red lure. And don't reuse the previous round's lure. Any sensible answer works, and a DC 12 Intelligence (Nature) check hints at it.)
- **The river chart.** The winner gets a waxed chart of the Vingaard that Raven's father drew: every shallow, snag and hidden landing from the bridge to the Kalaman road. It gives **+2** to the *boat made of boats* check in [[2.02 Preparing for the Worst]], and **advantage** to anyone steering a boat downriver that night.

**Chess (market).** Three rounds. Each round has two contested rolls: the player's **Intelligence** (plus proficiency with chess tools) against the opponent's **Wisdom (Insight)**, and the reverse. Each win is a point, so 2 a round. Most points after three rounds wins, and a tie is one last Intelligence check. Becklin is +4.

**Becklin's pass problem (puzzle).** If the winner or anyone at the table wants more, Becklin clears the board and sets out a war-game she calls "the Kingfisher Problem". A river runs down the left of the board, a cliff down the right, and between them is a narrow neck of ground with a village behind it. The **defenders** are three pikemen, two archers and a knight. The **attackers** are six soldiers and a siege engine, and they win if one piece reaches the village. "Where does the defender put the knight?" she asks. A good answer: **the pikemen in a line across the neck, the archers on the heights, and the knight held back as the reserve to plug wherever the line bends. Never let the line touch the river, because that is where it gets turned.** A **DC 14 Intelligence (History)** check, or a good answer, solves it. The solver gets a **Solamnic lesson**: in [[2.02 Preparing for the Worst#Scenario 1: Battle of Vogler|Scenario 1]] they may swap the starting positions of two defending units before the first round. The problem is the real pass at Vogler, and Becklin says nothing about that, unless they ask.

**Gnome flinging (Thornwall Keep).** Than has persuaded Raven to include the gnomeflinger. Each participant has a narycrash to land safely. Three rounds: first an **Intelligence** check to aim at the 30-foot pole, and on a success **advantage** on the **Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check to grab the ribbon at the top mid-flight. The DC is the same for both: **60 ft, DC 13 (1 point); 150 ft, DC 15 (2 points); 300 ft, DC 17 (3 points).** Froswin is +3 and thrilled.

**Than's counterweights (puzzle).** Before each throw Than hands the contestant a tray of lead weights (**3, 4, 7, 9 and 11 pounds**) and mutters that the arm needs the right counterweight, made of exactly **three** of them: **14 pounds** for the 60 ft pole, **20 pounds** for the 150 ft pole, **27 pounds** for the 300 ft pole. (Answers: 3+4+7, 4+7+9, 7+9+11.) Solving it gives **advantage** on that throw's aim check. Getting it wrong sends you short, and the pole is 10 ft farther than you meant. The prize is **Than's own set of tinker's tools**: a gnome's fine kit, scratched and oily, with a spare spring in the lid. Anyone proficient gets **advantage** on the DC 15 check to turn the boilerdrak on the Dragon Army ([[2.03 Evacuation]]), and it works as a Milo-friendly gift.

**Horseback race (River Gate).** Rent a horse for 3 gp. Four obstacles, each a **Wisdom (Animal Handling)** check to avoid it: **Uneven Ground DC 10, Tight Turn DC 12, Log in Road DC 14, Final Sprint DC 15.** Fail and you need a **Dexterity** check at the same DC or you lose control. Avoid it: 2 points. Fail, then pass the Dexterity check: 1 point. Fail both: 0. Bakaris the Younger is +3, and even if he wins he disdains the mock battle and won't be a general. The winner gets one of Raven's **courier horses** for three days, fast and trained to run through noise: the scouting trip in [[2.01 Back in Vogler]] takes a third of the time, and the ride to the pass is easy. If the party beats Bakaris, he sulks in public. That is worth a barbed remark from him later, and **+1 villagers** if it is done with grace.

**Log roll (Vogler's Crossing).** Nesau started it five years ago. Two contestants stay on a rotating log in the river and try to knock the other off, without touching or using magic. Each throw is a **Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics)** roll, and the best two of three wins. Nesau, who uses Acrobatics at +5, has won every year. The prize is Nesau's own **cleated boots** (advantage on checks and saves against slipping or being knocked prone on wet, icy or slick ground) and a **secret**: if you beat him, he whispers that the crossing's winch hut holds the bridge's emergency ropes and pulleys, and shows you how they come apart. That gives **advantage** on the *repurposed ferry* check in [[2.02 Preparing for the Worst]].

**Pie eating (the Fishbowl).** The festival's most popular event after the fish catch and the mock battle. One minute to eat the pie each round: a **Constitution** check, **DC 8, rising by 2** each round, and failing knocks you out. If everyone fails in the last round, whoever rolled highest wins. Cudgel is +4. If she wins, she offers her general's position to the party member who got farthest, and spectates the mock battle to see how her troops do without her. The prize is a sealed crock of **Elthar's travel pies**: six pies baked hard in fish oil that keep for a week, each counting as **a day's rations** for one character and healing **1d4** hit points once if eaten on a short rest. Elthar also hints the pie to avoid in the final round: "Never trust the pie that smells of nothing." **Homebrew trap:** the character who takes the title leads the Vogler side at High Hill, and Gragonis's bought mercenaries single out the "general" first in the betrayal ([[1.04 The Battle of High Hill]]).

**Talent show (village circle).** Not an official contest. Make a skill check that fits the act (Charisma (Performance) for song or dance, Dexterity (Acrobatics) for tumbling). A **20 or higher** earns a **d6 inspiration die** until the end of your next long rest. Elthar, with his banjo, is **+6**, and if anyone rolls a 20 he proposes a friendly competition: best two of three contested checks wins. Beating him gives the **whole party a d8 inspiration die** (which replaces the d6). Either way, Elthar teaches the winner **the festival song**, a call-and-response about the kingfisher. Anyone who knows it can start it in the crowd, and the whole village sings it back ([[2.02 Preparing for the Worst]]).

**Wrestling (outside the Brass Crab).** Yalme referees and ties a crab-shaped cravat (Fancy or Gorgeous George) on each wrestler. Roll a contested **Strength (Athletics)** check, and the winner begins to pin the opponent. A pinned wrestler escapes with a **Strength (Athletics) and Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check against the opponent's initial Athletics roll, then it repeats. **Two pins** wins. Gragonis is **+5**, and if he wins he can't be a general for Vogler because he's committed to the Istarian side. **The prize is a clue.** At close quarters a character who beats him or has the best Wisdom (Perception or Insight) result can make a **DC 14** check and notices, under his left sleeve, a fresh welt in the shape of a shield strap, and a missing patch of fur in his beard where something pulled it. He didn't have that last week. Gragonis cheats on his second pin with a trip (**DC 15 Perception** to spot it). If you call him on it in public, he laughs and says the loser always complains. The clue points at [[1.02 Arrival in Vogler#The Missing Child and the Stolen Shield]].

### What the Prizes Are For

> [!tip] Faction intro: the villagers of Vogler
> This is where the [[Faction Rules]] quietly begin, with just one track, the [[Vogler Refugees]] (still just "the villagers" for now). Show the players the number the first time it moves.
> - **+1** for joining in the festival with real warmth: helping with a stall, entering a contest in good spirits, or finding the missing child.
> - **+1** for honouring Ispin at the funeral with a good story.
> - **-1** for being rude to the villagers, or making a scene at the funeral (Milo's attempt on the shield, if it goes badly and publicly).
