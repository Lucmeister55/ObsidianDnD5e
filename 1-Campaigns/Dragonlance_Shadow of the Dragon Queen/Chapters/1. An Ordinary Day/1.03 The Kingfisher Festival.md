## The Kingfisher Festival

**Music:** [Market - The Legend of Zelda_ Ocarina of Time OST Remastered](ttrpg-sound:Soundbar/OoT/Market - The Legend of Zelda_ Ocarina of Time OST Remastered.mp3)

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#The Kingfisher Festival|ch. 3, "The Kingfisher Festival"]]) and **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]]). The faction intro is **Homebrew** ([[Faction Rules]]).

Have the players do max 4 activities, at which point the battle of High Hill commences.

**The contests** (*Source: **Companion**, Part 4; the fish catch is from the **Module**, "Festivities"*). Ispin used to donate a common magic item to each winner, and the village keeps the tradition in his honour, plus a small purse and a coloured winner's sash. Winning also makes you a Vogler "general" in the reenactment.

| Contest | Where | How it works | Rival | Prize |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fish catch** | Wharf | Three 10-minute rounds on the module's Fish to Catch table (Wisdom (Survival)). Fail by 10 and you're in the river. Benebog the Line Breaker can be caught once. | Raven (7 feet total). Lord Bakaris heckles, drunk. | 10 gp, gold sash, pole of angling |
| **Chess** | Market | Three rounds of Intelligence against Wisdom (Insight), 2 points a round. | Becklin, +4 | 5 gp, yellow sash, pipe of smoking monsters |
| **Gnome flinging** | Thornwall Keep | Aim (Intelligence), then grab the ribbon in midair (Dexterity (Acrobatics)): 60, 150 and 300 feet, DC 13, 15 and 17. | Froswin, +3, overjoyed | 5 gp, red sash, clockwork amulet |
| **Horseback race** | River Gate | Four obstacles, DC 10–15 Wisdom (Animal Handling), Dexterity to stay on. | Bakaris the Younger, +3 (Edric's mare says hello) | 5 gp, silver sash, cloak of billowing |
| **Log roll** | Vogler's Crossing | Best two of three Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) to knock the other off. | Nesau Lanth, +5, unbeaten | 5 gp, purple sash, rope of mending |
| **Pie eating** | The Fishbowl | Constitution check, DC 8, rising by 2 a round. | Cudgel, +4 | 5 gp, green sash, Heward's handy spice pouch |
| **Talent show** | Village circle | Any fitting check. A 20+ gives a d6 inspiration die. Beat Elthar (+6) for a d8 for the whole party. | Elthar on the banjo | Inspiration |
| **Wrestling** | Outside the Brass Crab | Contested Athletics to pin, Athletics and Acrobatics to escape. Two pins wins. | **Gragonis**, +5 | 5 gp, blue sash, dread helm |

Wrestling Gragonis matters: at High Hill he goes for whoever he wrestled. Milo will want to enter everything.

> [!tip] Faction intro: the villagers of Vogler
> This is where the [[Faction Rules]] quietly begin, with just one track, the [[Vogler Refugees]] (still just "the villagers" for now). Show the players the number the first time it moves.
> - **+1** for joining in the festival with real warmth: helping with a stall, entering a contest in good spirits, or finding the missing child.
> - **+1** for honouring Ispin at the funeral with a good story.
> - **-1** for being rude to the villagers, or making a scene at the funeral (Milo's attempt on the shield, if it goes badly and publicly).
