## The Battle of High Hill

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Betrayal at High Hill|ch. 3, "Betrayal at High Hill"]]). Wurm's death and rebirth and Thorn's entrance are **Homebrew** (see [[Wurm (DM)]] and [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]]).

### Setting Up the Betrayal

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** (Part 5, "Setting Up the Betrayal" and "The Battle of High Hill") and **Module** ("Behind the Battle", "Betrayal").

- **The walk out.** Raven leads twenty Vogler reenactors and twice as many spectators to the hill, with Becklin and Darrett. She points out the wagon of padded spears. DC 12 Insight: she's taking it seriously. She's heard rumours of war in the east and hopes this doubles as a drill.
- **No real weapons.** Cudgel and Raven make everyone leave real weapons at the bottom of the hill. The mercenaries comply, but Gragonis hid real spears under the brush there the night before. **The characters fight with padded spears, improvised weapons or whatever they take from a mercenary.** That makes Edric's shield-less, sword-less first fight a lot more desperate.
- **The signal.** Raven, Becklin and Cudgel leave for a better vantage point. Raven blows the whistle to start.
  - **Where.** The **Knights' Cairn** on the hill's east shoulder, about **600 feet** from the slope, behind a stand of birches. It is where Vogler has always watched the reenactment from, and where the town honours the Solamnic dead of the old battle. It has a clear view of the whole slope, but the trees cut off close sight and sound.
  - **What they are doing.** Watching. The wreath is already laid at the cairn, and as Raven raises the whistle, Cudgel lifts a cup to the dead and Becklin stands beside her. They are looking straight at the slope.
  - **They see it at once.** When the first villager is run through, they hear the screams and see the real spears. Raven keeps blowing the whistle to stop the fight, and nobody on the slope can hear it over the noise.
  - **They can't reach the fight in time,** and they know it. The cairn is **600 feet** away behind a stone wall and a tree line, and the mercenaries on the slope outnumber them. So they run the other way for **reinforcements**: Cudgel sounds the Regiment's brass recall horn, which carries for a mile, and the **loyal mercenaries still in camp with Jeyev** answer it and ride down.
  - **Svilnt** is with them as Cudgel's banner-bearer. He strikes at the whistle, while her attention is on the slope. Becklin stops him and takes him alive, which costs them the first rounds.
  - **The timing.** Raven, Becklin, Cudgel and the loyal mercenaries (**Jeyev** among them) reach the hill **roughly as the party beats its attackers and Gragonis**. Let the arrival read as the cavalry coming over the rise, in time to break the mercenaries who are still standing. (Jeyev leading the rescue gives him Cudgel's trust, which is his cover.)
- **The turn.** Anyone with passive Perception 15 sees the mercenaries' spear tips glint, unpadded, and it's already too late: a villager the party has come to like (Nesau, Froswin) is run through. Gragonis pulls the cloth off the shield on his arm. It's **Ispin's shield**. He orders the slaughter.
- **The general.** If a character won a contest and took the title of Vogler "general" ([[1.03 The Kingfisher Festival]]), Gragonis's bought mercenaries go for them first and hardest, and Gragonis himself goes after them over whoever he wrestled.
- **Taking command.** DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) gets the panicking villagers to at least defend themselves.
- **Darrett** is cut off by two mercenaries early. He breaks through later, if the party is close to going down, and takes the Dodge action to cover them.
- **Cudgel betrayed.** Meanwhile, a mercenary named **Svilnt** tries to kill Cudgel, and Becklin stops him. He's taken alive (see [[2.00 Broken Silence]]).

#### Before the Charge (module and companion)

- **Behind the battle.** This year's reenactment is different. Raven asked her friend Cudgel to take part, and the Ironclad Regiment plays the invading Istarians. Raven calls it a happy coincidence that Cudgel's band was in the area, but some villagers think she hired them because of vague rumours of war in the east. With trained mercenaries on the other side, this year's reenactment is more of a challenge for the militia than usual.
- **The parade.** An hour before the battle, Raven rallies the militia in the village circle and a haphazard parade leads from the village to High Hill, a **30-minute walk north**, followed by dozens of spectators and a wagon of drinks. A feast at the Brass Crab follows the reenactment. Darrett, Becklin or a villager finds the party as the parade begins.
- **The betrayal behind it.** Gragonis has paid off many of the mercenaries under his command, and with the exception of Cudgel and a few who aren't attending, the whole company plans to attack in earnest. Red Dragon Army agents hatched the plan when they heard the mercenaries would join the mock battle, and bought Gragonis's loyalty as part of a plan to take Vogler quickly. A contingent of the army crossed the Vingaard in recent days (the draconians the party may have met before were part of it). If the traitors can take Vogler, all the better. If not, the army brings its own force to bear.
- **Preparing.** The grassy slope has trees and crumbled stone fencing, and several dozen mercenaries in matching armour stand in formation near the base, a sharp contrast with the militia's mismatched armour and crooked helmets. Raven and Cudgel discuss final plans at the top. Raven announces that the mercenaries will **charge up the west side** of the hill while the militia and the other participants charge **down**, clashing midway, and the Istarians will eventually retreat. Everyone uses their own armour but wooden spears with padded tips. If a character refuses to take part, Raven offers them **5 gp** to be a judge, making sure nobody uses real weapons and calling for aid if someone is hurt. (The companion offers 5 gp to each character who takes part.) Both sides use the **guard** stat block, but the militia's spears are padded and deal no damage.
- **The slope matters.** The militia starts on high ground and the mercenaries charge uphill, so use [[Combat Extras#Slopes and High Ground|Slopes and High Ground]]: whoever runs downhill covers double the distance and gets a charge die, and an archer on the hill has the better shot.
- **The charge.** As they charge, each character makes a **Wisdom (Perception)** check (module), and the highest sees sun glint on the mercenaries' spear tips and their half-ogre leader waving a steel axe. (Companion: **passive Perception 15** is enough to notice the tips are unpadded.) It's too late to stop it.

### The Battle

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, on the **Module**'s map 3.2.

Make it so that it is described that there are about 50 villagers and mercenaries each, with two main hills on which the villagers start. Darrett is described as starting on the other hill and will thus stay away from the party's fray, unless needed to swing the battle in their favor if the party start struggling. Right before Raven blows the whistle, Edric notices a ragged figure stumble out of the brush some distance behind the mercenaries. The two forces start charging and clash, but upon impact, Wurm is immediately felled, and effectively dies. **Wurm misses their first turn of combat** because of it.

**Milo loses their head.** On impact in round 1, before Milo's first turn, a mercenary's axe takes **Milo's head clean off**. Describe it plainly and let the table go quiet. **Milo misses their first turn of combat.** At the start of their second turn, the head **grows back**: skin knitting over bone, the grin coming back first, while Milo blinks and asks what they missed. They're back at full hit points. It's the ring ([[Milo Geartickle (DM)]]). A death costs a death: as the head grows back, a **kingfisher** drops dead out of the tree above them, the festival's own bird. Nobody connects the two. Log it in the death tracker. This is for shock, so don't explain it, and let the other players stare.

Also roll initiative for Thorn, and have him enter from the fray on the edge of the map. On his turn, give his character sheet to his respective player.

*Only two players are left without a character: let them decide who claims this origin character (Thorn, [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)|Aeltharion Caladon]]), then give them the character sheet on Thorn's first turn. The other one gets Wurm.*

At the end of the first round of combat, Wurm has a vision of a vast grey dragon, like silver seen through smoke (a hint that it isn't truly Paladine), and is brought back to life with full hit points. Upon opening his eyes, they burn with a divine light, and the last thing he hears is "eradicate them all". Upon hearing this, he tears apart the mercenary bearing down upon his prone body. Give them their full character sheet at this moment, as well as make them roll initiative.

*There are five players, so Wurm goes to whoever is left, with no choice. Wurm seems to die on impact in the first round, so that player has no sheet until he resurrects: hand it over the moment he opens his eyes, at the end of that round ([[Wurm (DM)]]).*

### Encounter: The Battle of High Hill

- **Map:** [[map-3.02-battle-of-high-hill-player.png|Map 3.2: Battle of High Hill]] (module), printed on paper
- **Terrain:** the hill, 1 large tree, a cart (the padded spears), a low stone wall, and the brush at the foot of the hill where Gragonis hid the real spears.

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Battle of High Hill|ch. 3, "Battle of High Hill"]]), map 3.2. The characters start adjacent to the log at the north of the map.
> - **The Fray.** The 15-foot-wide band at the map's edge is dozens of clashing combatants. It and the battlefield beyond the map are **difficult terrain**. A creature that enters the Fray for the first time on a turn, or starts its turn there, makes a **DC 16 Dexterity save** or takes **2d6 slashing** damage from opportunistic foes. The Fray can't be damaged and stays until the battle ends.
> - **Wall and cart.** Both are difficult terrain and give **half cover** to creatures behind them.
> - **Battlefield events.** At initiative **0** each round, roll a **d4** (and also if a character enters the Fray or tries to leave the battlefield):
>
> | d4 | Event |
> |---|---|
> | 1 | A volley of arrows falls on a random player character's position. That character and each creature within 10 feet make a **DC 12 Dexterity save** or take **1d6 piercing**. |
> | 2 | Two riderless, terrified warhorses bolt from the Fray. One random player character makes a **DC 14 Dexterity save** or is knocked **prone**. |
> | 3 | An injured militia member (a guard with **1 hit point**) crawls on and begs for help, adjacent to the Fray nearest a random character. |
> | 4 | A hostile mercenary guard with **6 hit points** appears adjacent to the Fray nearest a random mercenary, and acts on initiative **10**. |
>
> - **The opening wave.** In the module, three hostile mercenary guards, each mounted on a **warhorse**, appear on the road at the southwest edge and go for the characters. They are hard to reach, so the characters have little chance to help villagers. Darrett or another well-liked NPC may be wounded but survives. Raven and Cudgel are too far away to arrive before the fight ends.
> - **Gragonis arrives** when the characters beat the mounted guards (or whenever it feels right): a hulking figure with a formidable battleaxe leads four guards through the chaos from the west edge, adjacent to the Fray, cutting through the last of the militia. He points at the most formidable-looking character and laughs, "Look here! We got one who still thinks this is all a game!"
> - **Down to 0.** When a character drops Gragonis or another mercenary to 0, ask them to describe the final blow, and give the choice to leave them alive. If **all** the characters are defeated, Darrett or surviving militia drag them off and revive them at **1 hit point**, just as Cudgel, Becklin and Raven arrive.
> - **Battlefield encounters (module note).** This is the first of several battlefield encounters: the wider battle is abstracted so that the characters can focus on tide-turning fights. Beyond the map's edge is "the Fray", the dangers of combat, and the source of extra threats noted for each encounter.

**Scaling (for five players, level 3).** The module's 3 guards are a trivial fight, so use:
- **First wave:** 1 regiment skirmisher and 5 guards.
- **Gragonis wave:** Gragonis, 2 regiment skirmishers and 1 guard.

(Use the **scout** stat block for a regiment skirmisher: it's what the encounter blocks above use.) Gragonis uses the half-ogre (ogrillon) stat block. A character can **take command** of the panicking villagers with a **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation)** check, so that they at least defend themselves with their padded spears. Darrett is cut off by two mercenaries early, and if the party looks to be going under he breaks through and takes the Dodge action to cover them, though he can't deal damage. Emphasise the contrast between the cold, organised Ironclad Regiment and the villagers' inexperienced panic. If you want Gragonis to foreshadow the shield's voice, have him shout at nobody in particular ("You dare call me that?!" "I will force you to watch them all die!") while Ispin chatters at him. Gragonis goes after whoever he wrestled at the festival.

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Gear only: broken spears, shields and chain shirts from the mercenaries. Gragonis carries **Ispin's shield**.

> [!check] Full fight. **Objective:** get the villagers off the hill alive. Two characters are revealed here, so play it out. Every villager still standing at the end is worth the healing potion and the faction point below. If half of them fall, the village remembers who was there.

If at least 1 Vogler commoner survives, one of the survivors gives a healing potion to the party as a thanks for saving them.

**Faction:** **+1 Vogler Refugees** if the party protected the villagers in the fight. **-1** if they left villagers to die to save themselves.

## Aftermath

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Aftermath|ch. 3, "Aftermath" and "Response"]]) and **Companion** (Part 5, "The Spy").

**Music (once they start discussing what happened):** [[Locations#^high-hill-aftermath|Fire Emblem Awakening: Something is very wrong.]] *(track 14; it carries on into [[2.01 Back in Vogler]].)*

- **The arrival.** Raven, Becklin, Cudgel and the loyal mercenaries ride over the rise into a hillside of dead and dying villagers. Their first question is the obvious one: **"Who attacked you?"** They assume **raiders**, bandits out of the Wastes, and Raven starts giving orders on that basis. Then someone points at the dead mercenaries and the Ironclad colours, or Gragonis's body with the real spears around it, and they **realise it was the mercenaries themselves**. Let the silence land.
  - **Cudgel's entourage** are dismayed. These are their comrades, men and women they've fought beside for years. A loyal sergeant asks Cudgel the hard question: **"Do we pursue them?"** The fleeing mercenaries are running down the far slope, in range.
  - **Cudgel's choice.** She can order the pursuit (and take prisoners, or kill), hold the loyal ones to tend the wounded, or freeze for a moment. **Jeyev**, quietly, argues for letting them go: they have no food, no pay and nowhere to run, so "the Wastes will have them in a week anyway. Chasing them wastes men we need here." He adds the practical points: the loyal ones are **too few** to hunt a larger band across open ground, with **few horses** between them, and they could be **ambushed** out there, in the dark, by the fleeing mercenaries or whatever the Wastes holds. It sounds sensible, and it also keeps his fellow traitors alive and gives him cover.
- **The rest scatter.** When Gragonis falls, the remaining mercenaries run. Raven, Becklin and Cudgel arrive with the loyal mercenaries under Jeyev. Ask the players to describe the killing blow on Gragonis, and whether they leave him alive. Edric gets **Ispin's shield** back here ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]]).
- **If Gragonis is taken alive,** he is nothing like the man at the festival. He answers quietly. He did it for **money** (Cudgel is too honorable to take the contracts that pay) and because he is **afraid**: the Dragon Armies are coming, and he wants to be on the side that wins. He does not ask for mercy.
- **Raven** thanks the party and promises a reward, but she's busy with the wounded.
- **Cudgel** is livid: one of her own tried to kill her. She wants answers, and she won't get them on the hill.
- **The wounded.** Most of Vogler's militia died. A DC 12 Wisdom (Medicine) check brings a villager back from the brink. Each one saved is a face the party will see again in Kalaman. A character can also save a villager with healing magic (restoring 1 or more hit points). A saved villager thanks them with a piece of jewellery or a lucky charm worth **20 gp**. A character who openly uses a god's power is seen as having worked a miracle, and the villager they save wants to know more about their deity.
- **Back to Vogler.** Raven and Becklin ask the party to escort the survivors home. The festival is over.

> [!companion] **The Greenshield** (companion Appendix C), the item Edric recovers. *Armor (shield), rare, requires attunement.* It has the properties of a **+1 shield**. While attuned you gain the **Protection** fighting style (or **Dueling**, if you already have Protection). It is a **sentient neutral good** item (Intelligence 11, Wisdom 11, Charisma 15) with hearing and ordinary sight to 120 feet, and attuning lets you hear **Ispin's** voice, who gives his advice and wisdom to any **good-aligned** attuned wielder. His great wish is to keep protecting Solamnia and its people. How it speaks in this campaign, and what it really is, are in [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]].

No level-up here: the origin characters start at 3rd level. This is the moment the last of them, Wurm and Thorn, have joined.
