## The Battle of High Hill

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Betrayal at High Hill|ch. 3, "Betrayal at High Hill"]]) and **Companion** (Part 5). Wurm's death and rebirth and Thorn's entrance are **Homebrew** ([[Wurm (DM)]], [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]]).

### Setting Up the Betrayal

- **Behind the battle.** Raven asked Cudgel's Ironclad Regiment to play the invading Istarians, "a happy coincidence", though some villagers think it's because of the rumours of war.
- **The walk out.** An hour before, a haphazard parade leads from the village circle to High Hill, a **30-minute walk north**, with twenty reenactors, twice as many spectators and a wagon of drinks. Raven, Becklin and Darrett lead it. DC 12 Insight: Raven is taking it seriously, and hopes it doubles as a drill.
- **No real weapons.** Cudgel and Raven make everyone leave real weapons at the bottom of the hill, and the mercenaries comply, but Gragonis hid real spears under the brush the night before. **The characters fight with padded spears, improvised weapons or whatever they take from a mercenary.**
- **The plan.** The mercenaries (several dozen, in matching armour) charge **up the west side**, the militia and participants charge **down**, and the Istarians eventually retreat. Both sides use the **guard** stat block, but the militia's padded spears do no damage. Anyone who won't take part gets **5 gp** to judge. The militia holds the high ground ([[Combat Extras#Slopes and High Ground|slopes]]).
- **The betrayal.** Gragonis has paid off most of the mercenaries here. Red Dragon Army agents hatched it when they heard about the mock battle: if the traitors take Vogler, good, and if not, the army comes in force.
- **The signal.** Raven, Becklin and Cudgel go to watch and blow the whistle.
  - **Where:** the **Knights' Cairn** on the east shoulder, **600 feet** away behind birches, where Cudgel lifts a cup to the dead as Raven raises the whistle.
  - **They see it at once,** and Raven keeps blowing the whistle that nobody on the slope hears.
  - **Too far to help** (past a wall and a tree line, against more of them), so they run for **reinforcements**. Cudgel sounds the Regiment's brass recall horn (it carries a mile) and the **loyal mercenaries in camp with Jeyev** ride down. They arrive **as the party beats Gragonis**, like cavalry over the rise, which earns Jeyev Cudgel's trust.
  - **Svilnt**, Cudgel's banner-bearer, strikes at the whistle. Becklin stops him and takes him alive, which costs them the first rounds.
- **The turn.** The charge begins. Passive Perception 15 (or a **Wisdom (Perception)** check, highest wins) sees sun glint on unpadded spear tips and their half-ogre leader waving a steel axe. Too late: a villager the party likes (Nesau, Froswin) is run through. Gragonis pulls the cloth off the shield on his arm. It's **Ispin's shield**. He orders the slaughter.
- **The general.** If a character took the title of Vogler "general" ([[1.03 The Kingfisher Festival]]), the bought mercenaries go for them first and hardest, and Gragonis goes after them over whoever he wrestled.
- **Taking command.** **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation)** gets the panicking villagers to at least defend themselves.
- **Darrett** is cut off by two mercenaries early. If the party is close to going down, he breaks through and takes the Dodge action to cover them (he can't deal damage).

### The Battle

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, on the **Module**'s map 3.2.

About 50 villagers and 50 mercenaries, the villagers on two hills, Darrett on the other one. Just before the whistle, Edric notices a ragged figure stumble out of the brush behind the mercenaries. The two forces clash, and on impact **Wurm** is felled and effectively dies. **He misses his first turn of combat.**

**Milo loses their head.** On impact in round 1, a mercenary's axe takes **Milo's head clean off**. Describe it plainly and let the table go quiet. **Milo misses their first turn.** On their second, the head **grows back**, grin first, at full hit points, and they ask what they missed. It's the ring ([[Milo Geartickle (DM)]]): as it grows back, a **kingfisher** drops dead out of the tree above. Nobody connects the two. Log it in the death tracker, and don't explain.

Roll initiative for **Thorn**, who enters from the Fray at the map edge. *The two players left decide who claims Thorn; give the sheet on his first turn. The other gets Wurm.*

At the end of round 1, **Wurm** has a vision of a vast grey dragon, like silver seen through smoke (a hint that it isn't truly Paladine), and is brought back at full hit points. His eyes burn with divine light, the last thing he hears is "eradicate them all", and he tears apart the mercenary bearing down on him. *Hand the last player Wurm's sheet the moment his eyes open, and roll initiative.*

### Encounter: The Battle of High Hill

- **Map:** [[map-3.02-battle-of-high-hill-player.png|Map 3.2: Battle of High Hill]] (module), printed on paper. The characters start next to the log at the north.
- **Terrain:** the hill, 1 large tree, a cart (the padded spears), a low stone wall (cart and wall are difficult terrain and give **half cover**), and the brush where Gragonis hid the real spears.

> [!module] Source: **Module**, map 3.2.
> - **The Fray.** The 15-foot band at the map's edge, and the battlefield beyond, is **difficult terrain**. Entering for the first time on a turn or starting a turn there: **DC 16 Dexterity save** or **2d6 slashing**. It can't be damaged.
> - **Battlefield events.** d4 at initiative **0** each round (and when a character enters the Fray or tries to leave):
>
> | d4 | Event |
> |---|---|
> | 1 | Arrows fall on a random PC. That character and creatures within 10 feet: **DC 12 Dexterity save** or **1d6 piercing**. |
> | 2 | Two terrified warhorses bolt from the Fray. One random PC: **DC 14 Dexterity save** or **prone**. |
> | 3 | An injured militia member (1 HP guard) crawls on and begs for help, next to the Fray. |
> | 4 | A hostile mercenary guard (6 HP) appears next to the Fray, acting on initiative **10**. |
>
> - **The opening wave.** Three mounted mercenary guards appear at the southwest edge and go for the characters. Hard to reach, so little chance to help villagers. Darrett or another liked NPC may be wounded, but survives.
> - **Gragonis arrives** when the characters beat the mounted guards (or whenever it feels right): a hulking figure with a battleaxe leads four guards from the west edge, cutting down the last of the militia. He points at the most formidable-looking character and laughs, "Look here! We got one who still thinks this is all a game!"
> - **Down to 0.** When a character drops Gragonis or another mercenary, ask them to describe the final blow, and let them spare them. If **all** the characters fall, Darrett or the militia drag them off and revive them at **1 hit point** as Cudgel, Becklin and Raven arrive.

**Scaling (five players, level 3):** first wave 1 skirmisher (**scout**) and 5 guards; Gragonis wave Gragonis (**ogrillon**), 2 skirmishers and 1 guard. Play the Regiment cold and organised against the villagers' panic. For a foreshadow of the shield's voice, have Gragonis shout at nobody ("You dare call me that?!" "I will force you to watch them all die!") while Ispin chatters at him.

If at least 1 commoner survives, a survivor gives the party a healing potion. **Faction:** **+1 Vogler Refugees** if the party protected the villagers, **-1** if they left them to die.

## Aftermath

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Aftermath|ch. 3, "Aftermath", "Response"]]) and **Companion** (Part 5, "The Spy").

**Music (once they discuss what happened):** [[Locations#^high-hill-aftermath|Fire Emblem Awakening: Something is very wrong.]] It carries into [[2.01 Back in Vogler]].

- **The arrival.** Raven, Becklin, Cudgel and the loyal mercenaries ride over the rise into dead and dying villagers. **"Who attacked you?"** They assume Wastes raiders, until someone points at the dead in Ironclad colours. Let the silence land.
  - Cudgel's entourage are dismayed: these are comrades. A loyal sergeant asks, **"Do we pursue them?"** The fleeing mercenaries are running down the far slope, in range.
  - **Cudgel's choice:** pursue (take prisoners or kill), hold the loyal ones to tend the wounded, or freeze. **Jeyev** quietly argues against the chase ("the Wastes will have them in a week anyway", and they're too few to risk an ambush in the dark). It sounds sensible, and it keeps his fellow traitors alive.
- **The rest scatter** when Gragonis falls. Ask the players to describe the killing blow and whether they leave him alive. Edric gets **Ispin's shield** back here.
- **If Gragonis is taken alive,** he answers quietly: **money**, and **fear** of being on the losing side ([[Gragonis]]). He doesn't ask for mercy.
- **Raven** thanks the party and promises a reward, but she's busy with the wounded. **Cudgel** is livid and wants answers she won't get on the hill.
- **The wounded.** Most of the militia died. **DC 12 Wisdom (Medicine)** brings a villager back from the brink, or healing magic of any amount saves one. Each one saved thanks them with a **20 gp** keepsake, and turns up again in Kalaman. Openly using a god's power looks like a miracle.
- **Back to Vogler.** Raven and Becklin ask the party to escort the survivors home. The festival is over.

> [!companion] **The Greenshield** (companion Appendix C). *Armor (shield), rare, requires attunement.* A **+1 shield**; while attuned you gain the **Protection** fighting style (or **Dueling**, if you have Protection). **Sentient neutral good** (Int 11, Wis 11, Cha 15), hearing and sight to 120 feet. Attuning lets you hear **Ispin's** voice, who advises any **good-aligned** wielder. How it speaks here, and what it really is: [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]].

No level-up here: the origin characters start at 3rd level. The last of them, Wurm and Thorn, have joined.
