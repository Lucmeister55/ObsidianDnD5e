## The Battle of High Hill

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Betrayal at High Hill|ch. 3, "Betrayal at High Hill"]]) and **Companion** (Part 5). Wurm's death and rebirth and Thorn's entrance are **Homebrew** ([[Wurm (DM)]], [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]]).

### Setting Up the Betrayal

- **Behind the battle.** Raven asked her friend Cudgel to bring the Ironclad Regiment to play the invading Istarians. She calls it a happy coincidence, though some villagers think she hired them because of rumours of war. Trained mercenaries make it a real challenge for the militia.
- **The walk out.** An hour before, a haphazard parade leads from the village circle to High Hill, a **30-minute walk north**, with twenty reenactors, twice as many spectators and a wagon of drinks. Raven, Becklin and Darrett lead it. DC 12 Insight: Raven is taking it seriously, and hopes it doubles as a drill.
- **No real weapons.** Cudgel and Raven make everyone leave real weapons at the bottom of the hill, and the mercenaries comply, but Gragonis hid real spears under the brush the night before. **The characters fight with padded spears, improvised weapons or whatever they take from a mercenary.** That makes Edric's shield-less, sword-less first fight more desperate.
- **The plan.** The mercenaries (several dozen, in matching armour) charge **up the west side**, the militia and participants charge **down**, and the Istarians eventually retreat. Both sides use the **guard** stat block, but the militia's padded spears do no damage. A character who won't take part gets **5 gp** to be a judge. Use [[Combat Extras#Slopes and High Ground|Slopes and High Ground]]: the militia holds the high ground, so downhill runners get the charge die.
- **The betrayal.** Gragonis has paid off most of the mercenaries (all but Cudgel and a few who aren't attending). Red Dragon Army agents hatched it when they heard the mercenaries would join the mock battle. If the traitors take Vogler, good. If not, the army comes in force.
- **The signal.** Raven, Becklin and Cudgel go to watch and blow the whistle.
  - **Where.** The **Knights' Cairn** on the hill's east shoulder, **600 feet** from the slope behind a stand of birches. Vogler has always watched from there. The wreath is already laid, and Cudgel lifts a cup to the dead as Raven raises the whistle.
  - **They see it at once.** When the first villager is run through, Raven keeps blowing the whistle, and nobody on the slope hears it.
  - **They can't reach the fight in time:** 600 feet, past a stone wall and a tree line, against a larger force. So they run for **reinforcements**. Cudgel sounds the Regiment's brass recall horn (it carries a mile) and the **loyal mercenaries in camp with Jeyev** ride down. They arrive **roughly as the party beats its attackers and Gragonis**, like cavalry over the rise. (Jeyev leading the rescue earns him Cudgel's trust.)
  - **Svilnt**, Cudgel's banner-bearer, strikes at the whistle. Becklin stops him and takes him alive, which costs them the first rounds.
- **The turn.** The charge begins. Passive Perception 15 (or a **Wisdom (Perception)** check, highest wins) sees sun glint on unpadded spear tips and their half-ogre leader waving a steel axe. Too late: a villager the party likes (Nesau, Froswin) is run through. Gragonis pulls the cloth off the shield on his arm. It's **Ispin's shield**. He orders the slaughter.
- **The general.** If a character took the title of Vogler "general" ([[1.03 The Kingfisher Festival]]), the bought mercenaries go for them first and hardest, and Gragonis goes after them over whoever he wrestled.
- **Taking command.** **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation)** gets the panicking villagers to at least defend themselves.
- **Darrett** is cut off by two mercenaries early. If the party is close to going down, he breaks through and takes the Dodge action to cover them (he can't deal damage).

### The Battle

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, on the **Module**'s map 3.2.

About 50 villagers and 50 mercenaries, with two main hills where the villagers start. Darrett starts on the other hill. Right before the whistle, Edric notices a ragged figure stumble out of the brush behind the mercenaries. The two forces clash, and on impact **Wurm** is felled and effectively dies. **He misses his first turn of combat.**

**Milo loses their head.** On impact in round 1, a mercenary's axe takes **Milo's head clean off**. Describe it plainly and let the table go quiet. **Milo misses their first turn.** At the start of their second turn the head **grows back**, the grin first, and they ask what they missed. Full hit points. It's the ring ([[Milo Geartickle (DM)]]). A death costs a death: a **kingfisher** drops dead out of the tree above them as it grows back. Nobody connects the two. Log it in the death tracker. Don't explain it.

Roll initiative for **Thorn**, who enters from the Fray at the map edge. *Only two players are left without a character: they decide who claims Thorn ([[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)|Aeltharion Caladon]]), and give them his sheet on his first turn. The other gets Wurm.*

At the end of round 1, **Wurm** has a vision of a vast grey dragon, like silver seen through smoke (a hint that it isn't truly Paladine), and is brought back at full hit points. His eyes burn with divine light, the last thing he hears is "eradicate them all", and he tears apart the mercenary bearing down on him. *There are five players, so Wurm goes to whoever is left. They have no sheet until now: hand it over the moment his eyes open, and have them roll initiative.*

### Encounter: The Battle of High Hill

- **Map:** [[map-3.02-battle-of-high-hill-player.png|Map 3.2: Battle of High Hill]] (module), printed on paper. The characters start next to the log at the north.
- **Terrain:** the hill, 1 large tree, a cart (the padded spears), a low stone wall (cart and wall are difficult terrain and give **half cover**), and the brush where Gragonis hid the real spears.

> [!module] Source: **Module**, map 3.2.
> - **The Fray.** The 15-foot band at the map's edge, and the battlefield beyond, is **difficult terrain**. Entering for the first time on a turn or starting a turn there: **DC 16 Dexterity save** or **2d6 slashing**. It can't be damaged.
> - **Battlefield events.** d4 at initiative **0** each round (and when a character enters the Fray or tries to leave):
>
> | d4 | Event |
> |---|---|
> | 1 | Arrows fall on a random PC. That character and creatures within 10 feet: **DC 12 Dexterity save** or **1d6 piercing**. |
> | 2 | Two terrified warhorses bolt from the Fray. One random PC: **DC 14 Dexterity save** or **prone**. |
> | 3 | An injured militia member (1 HP guard) crawls on and begs for help, next to the Fray. |
> | 4 | A hostile mercenary guard (6 HP) appears next to the Fray, acting on initiative **10**. |
>
> - **The opening wave.** Three mounted mercenary guards appear at the southwest edge and go for the characters. They are hard to reach, so there's little chance to help villagers. Darrett or another liked NPC may be wounded but survives.
> - **Gragonis arrives** when the characters beat the mounted guards (or whenever it feels right): a hulking figure with a battleaxe leads four guards from the west edge, cutting down the last of the militia. He points at the most formidable-looking character and laughs, "Look here! We got one who still thinks this is all a game!"
> - **Down to 0.** When a character drops Gragonis or another mercenary, ask them to describe the final blow, and let them spare them. If **all** the characters fall, Darrett or the militia drag them off and revive them at **1 hit point** as Cudgel, Becklin and Raven arrive.

**Scaling (five players, level 3).** The module's 3 guards are trivial, so: **first wave** 1 regiment skirmisher and 5 guards; **Gragonis wave** Gragonis, 2 skirmishers and 1 guard. Skirmishers use the **scout** stat block, Gragonis the **ogrillon**. Contrast the cold, organised Regiment with the villagers' panic. For a foreshadow of the shield's voice, have Gragonis shout at nobody ("You dare call me that?!" "I will force you to watch them all die!") while Ispin chatters at him.

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): broken spears, shields and chain shirts. Gragonis carries **Ispin's shield**.

> [!check] Full fight. **Objective:** get the villagers off the hill alive. Two characters are revealed here, so play it out. Every villager still standing is worth the healing potion and the faction point below.

If at least 1 commoner survives, a survivor gives the party a healing potion. **Faction:** **+1 Vogler Refugees** if the party protected the villagers, **-1** if they left them to die.

## Aftermath

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Aftermath|ch. 3, "Aftermath", "Response"]]) and **Companion** (Part 5, "The Spy").

**Music (once they discuss what happened):** [[Locations#^high-hill-aftermath|Fire Emblem Awakening: Something is very wrong.]] It carries into [[2.01 Back in Vogler]].

- **The arrival.** Raven, Becklin, Cudgel and the loyal mercenaries ride over the rise into dead and dying villagers. Their first question: **"Who attacked you?"** They assume **raiders** from the Wastes, until someone points at the dead mercenaries in Ironclad colours, or Gragonis with the real spears around him, and they realise it was the mercenaries themselves. Let the silence land.
  - Cudgel's entourage are dismayed: these are comrades. A loyal sergeant asks, **"Do we pursue them?"** The fleeing mercenaries are running down the far slope, in range.
  - **Cudgel's choice:** pursue (take prisoners or kill), hold the loyal ones to tend the wounded, or freeze. **Jeyev**, quietly, argues against the chase: they have no food, pay or place to run, "the Wastes will have them in a week anyway", and the loyal few are too few with too few horses, and could be ambushed in the dark. It sounds sensible, and it keeps his fellow traitors alive.
- **The rest scatter** when Gragonis falls. Ask the players to describe the killing blow and whether they leave him alive. Edric gets **Ispin's shield** back here.
- **If Gragonis is taken alive,** he is nothing like the man at the festival. He answers quietly: he did it for **money** (Cudgel is too honorable to take the contracts that pay) and because he is **afraid**, and wants to be on the side that wins. He does not ask for mercy.
- **Raven** thanks the party and promises a reward, but she's busy with the wounded. **Cudgel** is livid and wants answers she won't get on the hill.
- **The wounded.** Most of the militia died. **DC 12 Wisdom (Medicine)** brings a villager back from the brink, or healing magic of any amount saves one. Each saved villager is a face the party will see again in Kalaman, and thanks them with a keepsake worth **20 gp**. A character who openly uses a god's power is seen as having worked a miracle, and the villager wants to know about their deity.
- **Back to Vogler.** Raven and Becklin ask the party to escort the survivors home. The festival is over.

> [!companion] **The Greenshield** (companion Appendix C). *Armor (shield), rare, requires attunement.* A **+1 shield**; while attuned you gain the **Protection** fighting style (or **Dueling**, if you have Protection). **Sentient neutral good** (Int 11, Wis 11, Cha 15), hearing and sight to 120 feet. Attuning lets you hear **Ispin's** voice, who advises any **good-aligned** wielder. How it speaks here, and what it really is: [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]].

No level-up here: the origin characters start at 3rd level. The last of them, Wurm and Thorn, have joined.
