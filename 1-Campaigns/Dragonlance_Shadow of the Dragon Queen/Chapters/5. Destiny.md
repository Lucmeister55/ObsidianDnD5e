## Overview

The party slips through the passage at Wind's End into the City of Lost Names: Onyari, the Kingpriest's flying City Without Sin, built on a graveyard of dragons and brought down by them. The Dragon Army is here to raise it again. This is the chapter where almost every origin character's story comes home: a kender PC's ring and drone were made here, a spellcaster PC can take his Test, a knight PC's shield is restored, and a dragonborn PC meets the man who may have stolen his only friend.

**Sources:**
- [[5-City_of_Lost_Names.pdf]]
- [[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names|Chapter 6: City of Lost Names]]

> [!abstract] Running this chapter
> - **Characters:** "a knight PC", "a Silvanesti PC" and so on name the kind of character a thread suits best, not a person. See [[DM Guide#Roles, Not Names]] for who carries each one right now.
> - **Levels:** the party arrives at **8th level** (gained at the passage in Wind's End). They reach **9th** after reforging the dragonlance and raiding the mansion, and **10th** after escaping the Threshold. Each character's level 8, 9 and 10 moments in their DM notes are written for exactly these three places.
> - **The army** is fighting at the canyon wall (Scenario 7). Tatina's fargab call (module) tells the party it's holding, so they don't need to rush.
> - **Weapon situations** ([[Weapon Triangle#Where Each Weapon Shines]]): tight spaces in the Path of Memories, underwater in the flooded passages, bone and stone everywhere.

## The Deadline Lands

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew** ([[4. Duty#The Deadline]]).

Check how many days were left when the party found the passage.
- **On time.** The Night of the Eye (14 Reapember) hasn't come yet. Soth's ritual at the Bastion is unfinished, so the city's dead are still mostly asleep. In [[6. Conquest]], **remove one lesser death dragon** from every encounter that has more than one, and skip the "Death Dragon Attack" on the walls.
- **Late.** The ritual has begun. The city shakes every hour, bone dragons stir in the walls, and the "Shadow of Soth" scene below has **two** skeletal dragons, not one. In [[6. Conquest]], the death dragons are at full strength.

Either way, the Threshold still rises when Belephaion touches the helm. That's the module's story, and the deadline only changes how much is waiting on the other side.

## Path of Memories

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Path of Memories|ch. 6, "Path of Memories"]], areas M1–M9). The level 8 moments are **Homebrew** (each character's DM notes).

Run the buried district as written. It's a tight, wet, haunted place, so use the **tight spaces** and **underwater** situations for weapons.

Use the passage for the **level 8 moments**:
- **A knight PC** writes the letter to Ispin on night watch and burns it.
- **A moon-touched PC** finds a moon calendar carved in the walls and works out the next Night of the Eye.
- **A tinkerer PC** rings an ancient anvil in the **Wizard's Workshop (M8)**. It rings far too long, and the sparks come off grey. This is where the ring was forged (see below).
- **A Silvanesti PC** sings in the dark to keep the party moving, and the echo comes back in more voices than his.
- **A dragonborn PC** touches a metallic dragon carved in the wall, and his black scales reflect for a heartbeat.

### Demelin

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Demelin|ch. 6, "Demelin"]]), with **Homebrew** ties.

The red-robed high wizard of Onyari tests the party with an air elemental, then talks. Run her as written, and add:

- **A kender PC's ring.** She recognises it at a glance: one of the Kingpriest's death-cheating rings, forged in her own city. It can only be unmade at the forge in **M8**, and unmaking it **releases every life it ever took**. That debt has to land somewhere. Either the tinkerer PC pays it, and every borrowed death catches up with them at once, or the party finds another way (a *wish*, a god's mercy, a sacrifice). See [[Milo Geartickle (DM)]]. Let the tinkerer PC decide here or carry the ring on. Either answer is a real ending for their arc.
- **A Silvanesti PC.** Demelin is Silvanesti, old enough to remember Silvanost before the Cataclysm. She knows a Caladon face under the scars, and says nothing unless he speaks first. If he asks, she can tell him the Orbs of Dragonkind were Istar's work too, and what they do to the people who use them.
- **A spellcaster PC's Test.** See below.

- **Map:** [[map-6.01-path-of-memories-player.png|Map 6.1: Path of Memories]] (module), area M9
- **Terrain:** Demelin's apartment: old lamps and stuffed chairs, a door to the east.

```encounter
name: Demelin's Assessment
creatures:
  - "1": Air Elemental (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): The air elemental leaves **1d4+1 vials of air essence** (double jumps and +10 ft speed for a minute) and arcane dust.

> [!check] **Survive and impress.** Demelin calls it off after 3 rounds, or sooner if someone does something clever. Nobody needs to win.

### A spellcaster PC's Test of High Sorcery

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Test of High Sorcery|ch. 6, "Test of High Sorcery"]]). The personalised test is **Homebrew** ([[Davos Bloodbane (DM)]]).

Demelin offers the Test to a spellcaster PC, and a kender PC can sit the same crossroads if they want robes. Use the module's structure: a vision of Onyari's last day, a magical challenge and a moral choice. For the spellcaster PC, build it from his story:

- **The vision.** He wakes in Onyari as it falls, wearing the white robes he stole from Antimodes. Below the falling city, in the burning streets, is the Relgoth bathhouse guard who died for those robes.
- **The challenge.** A green dragon circles the city, and every Bloodbane who ever served it stands on the rooftops, cheering it on. To save the city's people, he has to fight the thing his family served.
- **The choice.** He can save his mother, brother and sister, who are somehow in the vision too. Or he can save a crowd of strangers. Or he can take the dragon's offer, made in his family's name: power, and the old Bloodbane fortune back.
- **The robes.** White, red or black, as the module describes. If the "sliver of white" in his hair has grown over the campaign, the vision leans toward white, but the choice is his.

**Passing** gives **+2 Mages of High Sorcery** ([[Faction Rules]]) and full membership. If he refuses the Test or forsakes magic in it, he dies, as the module says. Make sure the player knows that before he starts.

Run as written, including the captive wasteland dragonnel (save it: it comes back in [[6. Conquest]]). Add to the command intelligence in the master bedroom (O10):

### Temple of Paladine

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Temple of Paladine|ch. 6, "Temple of Paladine"]]). The shield and the level 9 moments are **Homebrew** ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]], "Stage 2: Restored").

Run as written: the causeway, Duskwalker the treant on the berm, the lake, and the chancel where Sarlamir's lance is reforged.

- **A ranger PC and Duskwalker.** The ranger PC counts as a servant of nature. Duskwalker knows it at once, and talks instead of attacking. If the ranger PC has tended the grove outside Kalaman, the treant gives him a seed from Onyari's gardens to plant there. That's the treant that stands with him at the Siege (his grove at progress 10).
- **The shield.** When the lance is reforged, the old blessing comes back to Ispin's shield too. It becomes a **Shield of the Cavalier** with **Through the Flames**. Light runs through the carved tree, and every name on the rim shines, Ispin's too if a knight PC has carved it.
- **Level 9 moments:**
  - A knight PC's palm scar is gone.
  - For one breath, nobody can say what colour a sorcerer PC's hair is.
  - A kender PC holds something wonderful and doesn't want to keep it.
  - A Silvanesti PC calls the reforging beautiful, or doesn't.
  - A dragonborn PC's axe won't come when he calls it, and the voice is quiet. Ask him what he prays to here.

- **Map:** [[map-6.04-temple-of-paladine-player.png|Map 6.4: Temple of Paladine]] (module), area P2
- **Terrain:** violet-leaved trees in a ring (some animated), dead soldiers in the branches, and the stairs down to the lake.

```encounter
name: Duskwalker
creatures:
  - "1": Duskwalker (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): If the treant dies (Survival DC 19): **bark plates** (plate armour), **trunks** (maul or greatclub), **branches** (fire components) and **sap** (*potion of healing*). A ranger PC will never forgive whoever does it.

> [!tip] **Avoidable.** A ranger PC can talk the treant down (see above). If it comes to a fight, the objective is to get through to the temple doors, not to fell the treant.

## Threshold of the Heavens

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Threshold of the Heavens|ch. 6, "Threshold of the Heavens"]], T1–T10), with **Homebrew** ties.

Run the tower as written. Add:

- **T5: Drone Monitoring.** This is where a tinkerer PC's drone was made. It flies to the charging columns, docks, and lights up in a pattern no one has seen before. Tinkering here with no check lets the tinkerer PC do one of these:
  - **Command the tower's drones** (no Arcana check needed for the console).
  - **Repair their drone completely.**
  - **Read its last orders:** "Retrieve the rings. Return them to the forge." It was never a pet, and now a tinkerer PC knows.
- **T9: Lohezet.** Run his lies and bargains as written. If you've made him the Draevenfall cult's patron (see [[Lohezet]] and [[Wurm (DM)]]), this is where a dragonborn PC meets the man who took **Vrella**, the elf lady. Lohezet knows exactly what the dragonborn PC is, and says so: "The Queen's first. I wondered what became of you." He'll trade Vrella's whereabouts for his life. That's the dragonborn PC's level 10 moment, if you want it.
- **T10: Belephaion.** He's a blue dragon in a priest's crown, and he touches the helm the moment he sees them. Kill him and the tower still falls. The city tears itself apart around them.

- **Map:** [[map-6.05-threshold-of-the-heavens-player.png|Map 6.5: Threshold of the Heavens]] (module), area T5
- **Terrain:** the crystal-banded columns with lightning arcing between them, the 2 docked drones and the rune console.

```encounter
name: Drone Monitoring
creatures:
  - "2": Istarian Drone (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Each drone (Arcana DC 13): **1 Istarian drone eye** (an *eye of enhanced seeing*, or homebrew: a free upgrade for a tinkerer PC's drone), **1d2 claws** (lightning dagger, 150 gp, 9 days), **arcane dust** and **1d6 vials of crystalline spit** (restraining crystal grenade).

> [!tip] **Avoidable.** The drones only wake if someone damages a column, a drone or the console, and a tinkerer PC can command them.

- **Map:** Map 6.5 (module), area T9
- **Terrain:** the crystal scrying panels (one showing Kalaman), the stone desks and the raised platform.

```encounter
name: Lohezet
creatures:
  - "1": Lohezet (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): **2d4 ounces of arcane dust** and his **spellbook** (*arcane eye*, *detect magic*, *dimension door*, *dominate person*, *wall of force*). A spellcaster PC will want it.

> [!tip] **A conversation first.** He fights only if he must, and runs if he's losing.

- **Map:** [[map-6.05-threshold-of-the-heavens-player.png|Map 6.5: Threshold of the Heavens]] (module), area T10
- **Terrain:** the sunburst throne on its dais, the sunken walkways on three sides, and the great windows (the city rising outside them).

```encounter
name: The Bridge
creatures:
  - "1": Belephaion (DSotDQ)
  - "2": Bozak Draconian (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Belephaion (young blue dragon, Survival DC 19, 4 hours: the tower won't wait): **claws**, **fang**, **hide**, **2d4 scales**, **teeth** (+1d6 lightning), **1d2 wings** (a lightning-resistant cloak, 200 gp, 14 days) and **2d6 Supply**. Bozaks: arcane dust.

> [!check] Full fight, a big one. **Objective:** Belephaion dies or yields, and the party gets out of the tower before it tilts. Every round after the city starts rising, roll a d6: on a 1, the floor drops 10 feet.

## The City Rises

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#The City Rises|ch. 6, "The City Rises"]]). The level 10 moments are **Homebrew**.

Run the evacuation and Soth's ascent as written. The Bastion of Takhisis rises on its dragon bones as the first flying citadel. Then run the escape through the breaking city: escaped dragonnels, floating rocks, the ill-fated ambush. Use **bad footing** for weapons.

Use the escape for the **level 10 moments**:
- **A kender PC** walks the maze out from memory, every turn, out loud.
- **A ranger PC** follows birdsong where no birds should be.
- **A knight PC** carries the slowest of them out, and his mare is waiting outside, somewhere no horse could have reached.
- **A moon-touched PC**: if it's the Night of the Eye, he's the most powerful mage on Krynn for one night ([[Davos Bloodbane (DM)#The Night of the Eye]]). If not, he gets them out on skill alone.
- **A dragonborn PC** faces Lohezet, or carries the wounded out while the voice tells him they owe him.

- **Map:** a broken street in the rising city, on a blank board
- **Terrain:** floating rocks at different heights, cracks and drops, and a boulder token for the falling-rock event.

```encounter
name: Ill-Fated Ambush
creatures:
  - "3": Kapak Draconian (DSotDQ)
  - "1": Sivak Draconian (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Gear only, if anything doesn't float away.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Yes, mid-escape. The floating rocks and falling boulders are the fun part.

Once they're clear of the city, go straight to **Scenario 8** in [[6. Conquest]].
