## Overview

The party slips through the passage at Wind's End into the City of Lost Names: Onyari, the Kingpriest's flying City Without Sin, built on a graveyard of dragons and brought down by them. The Dragon Army is here to raise it again. This is the chapter where almost every origin character's story comes home: Milo's ring and drone were made here, Davos can take his Test, Edric's shield is restored, and Wurm meets the man who may have stolen his only friend.

## The Deadline Lands

*Source: **Homebrew** ([[4. Duty#The Deadline]]).*

Check how many days were left when the party found the passage.
- **On time.** The black and the red moons haven't risen together yet. Soth's ritual at the Bastion is unfinished, so the city's dead are still mostly asleep. In [[6. Conquest]], **remove one lesser death dragon** from every encounter that has more than one, and skip the "Death Dragon Attack" on the walls.
- **Late.** The ritual has begun. The city shakes every hour, bone dragons stir in the walls, and the "Shadow of Soth" scene below has **two** skeletal dragons, not one. In [[6. Conquest]], the death dragons are at full strength.

Either way, the Threshold still rises when Belephaion touches the helm. That's the module's story, and the deadline only changes how much is waiting on the other side.

## Path of Memories

*Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Path of Memories|ch. 6, "Path of Memories"]], areas M1–M9). The level 8 moments are **Homebrew** (each character's DM notes).*

Run the buried district as written. It's a tight, wet, haunted place, so use the **tight spaces** and **underwater** situations for weapons.

Use the passage for the **level 8 moments**:
- **Edric** writes the letter to Ispin on night watch and burns it.
- **Davos** finds a moon calendar carved in the walls and works out the next Night of the Eye.
- **Milo** rings an ancient anvil in the **Wizard's Workshop (M8)**. It rings far too long, and the sparks come off grey. This is where the ring was forged (see below).
- **Thorn** sings in the dark to keep the party moving, and the echo comes back in more voices than his.
- **Wurm** touches a metallic dragon carved in the wall, and his black scales reflect for a heartbeat.

### Demelin

*Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Demelin|ch. 6, "Demelin"]]), with **Homebrew** ties.*

The red-robed high wizard of Onyari tests the party with an air elemental, then talks. Run her as written, and add:

- **Milo's ring.** She recognises it at a glance: one of the Kingpriest's death-cheating rings, forged in her own city. It can only be unmade at the forge in **M8**, and unmaking it **releases every life it ever took**. That debt has to land somewhere. Either Milo pays it, and every borrowed death catches up with them at once, or the party finds another way (a *wish*, a god's mercy, a sacrifice). See [[Milo Geartickle (DM)]]. Let Milo decide here or carry the ring on. Either answer is a real ending for their arc.
- **Thorn.** Demelin is Silvanesti, old enough to remember Silvanost before the Cataclysm. She knows a Caladon face under the scars, and says nothing unless he speaks first. If he asks, she can tell him the Orbs of Dragonkind were Istar's work too, and what they do to the people who use them.
- **Davos's Test.** See below.

### Davos's Test of High Sorcery

*Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Test of High Sorcery|ch. 6, "Test of High Sorcery"]]). The personalised test is **Homebrew** ([[Davos Bloodbane (DM)]]).*

Demelin offers the Test to Davos, and Milo can sit the same crossroads if they want robes. Use the module's structure: a vision of Onyari's last day, a magical challenge and a moral choice. For Davos, build it from his story:

- **The vision.** He wakes in Onyari as it falls, wearing the white robes he stole from Antimodes. Below the falling city, in the burning streets, is the Relgoth bathhouse guard who died for those robes.
- **The challenge.** A green dragon circles the city, and every Bloodbane who ever served it stands on the rooftops, cheering it on. To save the city's people, he has to fight the thing his family served.
- **The choice.** He can save his mother, brother and sister, who are somehow in the vision too. Or he can save a crowd of strangers. Or he can take the dragon's offer, made in his family's name: power, and the old Bloodbane fortune back.
- **The robes.** White, red or black, as the module describes. If the "sliver of white" in his hair has grown over the campaign, the vision leans toward white, but the choice is his.

**Passing** gives **+2 Mages of High Sorcery** ([[Faction Rules]]) and full membership. If he refuses the Test or forsakes magic in it, he dies, as the module says. Make sure the player knows that before he starts.

## Exploring the City

*Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Exploring the City|ch. 6, "Exploring the City"]]).*

Let the party choose their route between the Occupied Mansion, the Temple of Paladine and the Threshold. Use the module's city sights and encounters. Weapon situations: **bad footing** on the tilted streets and floating rocks, and **bone and stone** near the dragon bones in the walls.

### Occupied Mansion

*Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Occupied Mansion|ch. 6, "Occupied Mansion"]]). The letters are **Homebrew**.*

Run as written, including the captive wasteland dragonnel (save it: it comes back in [[6. Conquest]]). Add to the command intelligence in the master bedroom (O10):
- **A letter from Bakaris the Younger** to Kansaldi, offering "my father, and a gate". This is the first hint of the Warrior's Gate betrayal in [[6. Conquest]].
- **If Jeyev is still loose**, a report in his hand on Darrett's movements, signed with a scout's mark Cudgel would recognise (see [[Jeyev Veldrews]]).

### Temple of Paladine

*Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Temple of Paladine|ch. 6, "Temple of Paladine"]]). The shield and the level 9 moments are **Homebrew** ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]], "Stage 2: Restored").*

Run as written: the causeway, Duskwalker the treant on the berm, the lake, and the chancel where Sarlamir's lance is reforged.

- **Thorn and Duskwalker.** Thorn counts as a servant of nature. Duskwalker knows it at once, and talks instead of attacking. If Thorn has tended the grove outside Kalaman, the treant gives him a seed from Onyari's gardens to plant there. That's the treant that stands with him at the Siege (his grove at progress 10).
- **The shield.** When the lance is reforged, the old blessing comes back to Ispin's shield too. It becomes a **Shield of the Cavalier** with **Through the Flames**. Light runs through the carved tree, and every name on the rim shines, Ispin's too if Edric has carved it.
- **Level 9 moments:**
  - Edric's palm scar is gone.
  - For one breath, nobody can say what colour Davos's hair is.
  - Milo holds something wonderful and doesn't want to keep it.
  - Thorn calls the reforging beautiful, or doesn't.
  - Wurm's axe won't come when he calls it, and the voice is quiet. Ask him what he prays to here.

name: Duskwalker
creatures:
  - "1": Duskwalker (DSotDQ)

## Threshold of the Heavens

*Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Threshold of the Heavens|ch. 6, "Threshold of the Heavens"]], T1–T10), with **Homebrew** ties.*

Run the tower as written. Add:

- **T5: Drone Monitoring.** This is where Milo's drone was made. It flies to the charging columns, docks, and lights up in a pattern no one has seen before. Tinkering here with no check lets Milo do one of these:
  - **Command the tower's drones** (no Arcana check needed for the console).
  - **Repair their drone completely.**
  - **Read its last orders:** "Retrieve the rings. Return them to the forge." It was never a pet, and now Milo knows.
- **T9: Lohezet.** Run his lies and bargains as written. If you've made him the Draevenfall cult's patron (see [[Lohezet]] and [[Wurm (DM)]]), this is where Wurm meets the man who took **Vrella**, the elf lady. Lohezet knows exactly what Wurm is, and says so: "The Queen's first. I wondered what became of you." He'll trade Vrella's whereabouts for his life. That's Wurm's level 10 moment, if you want it.
- **T10: Belephaion.** He's a blue dragon in a priest's crown, and he touches the helm the moment he sees them. Kill him and the tower still falls. The city tears itself apart around them.

## The City Rises

*Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#The City Rises|ch. 6, "The City Rises"]]). The level 10 moments are **Homebrew**.*

Run the evacuation and Soth's ascent as written. The Bastion of Takhisis rises on its dragon bones as the first flying citadel. Then run the escape through the breaking city: escaped dragonnels, floating rocks, the ill-fated ambush. Use **bad footing** for weapons.

Use the escape for the **level 10 moments**:
- **Milo** walks the maze out from memory, every turn, out loud.
- **Thorn** follows birdsong where no birds should be.
- **Edric** carries the slowest of them out, and his mare is waiting outside, somewhere no horse could have reached.
- **Davos**: if it's the Night of the Eye, he's at the height of his power. If not, he gets them out on skill alone.
- **Wurm** faces Lohezet, or carries the wounded out while the voice tells him they owe him.

```encounter
name: Ill-Fated Ambush
creatures:
  - "3": Kapak Draconian (DSotDQ)
  - "1": Sivak Draconian (DSotDQ)
```

Once they're clear of the city, go straight to **Scenario 8** in [[6. Conquest]].
