## Scenario 12: The Field of Kalaman

> [!info] Forces
> **Engaged:** a sortie of about **800** against an army whose citadel has fallen. **Losses:** Kalaman 50 / 100 / 200. The enemy loses 2,000 and routs on a win, loses 1,200 on a hold, or loses 500 and withdraws in good order on a loss. Expect about **1,000** of Kalaman's 2,000 to be left standing. Update the [[Campaign Tracker]] ([[The War in Numbers|war in numbers]]).

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Aftermath of the Siege|ch. 7, "Aftermath of the Siege"]]) and **WoK** scenario 12.

The defenders sortie to sweep the broken Dragon Army from the field. With WoK, play scenario 12. On a win or a hold, Darrett brings the party something from the rubble. In this campaign, for a knight PC, it's whatever's left of Soth's banner, with the black rose. Without WoK, narrate the rout, or run one last one-turn ([[One-Turn Resolution]]).

## Kalaman Victorious

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Kalaman Victorious|ch. 7, "Kalaman Victorious"]]), with **Homebrew** endings.

Run the celebration, the medals and the mysterious letter in blue wax as written. Change the allies' plans for this campaign:
- **Vogler's people** go home under the **Spokesperson**, not Raven ([[Raven Uth Vogler]]). If the Refugees reached +10, the whole militia and fishing fleet sail to retake Vogler, and they ask the party to lead them. Raven's whistle starts the first Kingfisher Festival after the war, blown by whoever kept it.
- **Darrett**, if he lived, goes to Maelgoth to become a true knight, as the module says. If he fell, the city buries him at the gate he held. If Becklin lived, she goes with him.
- **Cudgel** buries Jeyev's name, whatever happened to him.

**The origin characters' endings.** Give each one a closing scene drawn from their DM notes:
- **A knight PC:** knighted, or not, his rank in Kalaman's army (Grand Marshal, if Vendri fell), and the shield's names. Does he carve his own?
- **A sorcerer PC:** his robes, his family upriver, and the Night of the Eye.
- **A kender PC:** the ring, unmade or not, and who paid for it. The chronicle's first volume is finished.
- **A Silvanesti PC:** the grove, Zhelsuel, and whether Alhana hears that a Caladon prince is alive.
- **A dragonborn PC:** his own name, if he chose one, and whose god he is now.
