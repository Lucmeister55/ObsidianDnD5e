## Scenario 12: The Field of Kalaman

> [!info] Forces
> **Engaged:** a sortie of about **800** against an army whose citadel has fallen. **Losses:** Kalaman 50 / 100 / 200. The enemy loses 2,000 and routs on a win, loses 1,200 on a hold, or loses 500 and withdraws in good order on a loss. Expect about **1,000** of Kalaman's 2,000 to be left standing.

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Aftermath of the Siege|ch. 7, "Aftermath of the Siege"]]).

The defenders sortie to sweep the broken Dragon Army from the field. Narrate the rout, or run one last one-turn ([[One-Turn Resolution]]). Either way, Darrett brings the party something from the rubble: for a knight PC, it's whatever's left of Soth's banner, with the black rose.

> [!wok] **Bonus battle (optional).** WoK scenario 12 (the book prints it as "Retreat from the Field"), which the module offers for the aftermath ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Aftermath of the Siege|ch. 7]]). **Slot:** the sortie, in place of the one-turn. **Book:** [[14 - Scenario 12 - Retreat from the Field|transcript]] · **Narration:** [[Battle Lines#Scenario 12: The Field of Kalaman|narration lines]]. Skipping it changes nothing ([[Warriors of Krynn#How We Use It]]).
>
> **The book's setup:** the flying citadel is crumbling and Kalaman's forces are still clashing with the Dragon Army. Darrett and Vendri ask the heroes to help rally the troops beyond the gates and drive the enemy from the field. (The book's list of commanders a hero can place includes Raven Uth Vogler. She is dead, so leave her off it: [[Warriors of Krynn#House Rules]].) **Win** (5 or fewer Dragon Army units left): the title *Saviors of Kalaman*. Hold or loss: nothing, and the rout is narrated.

## Kalaman Victorious

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Kalaman Victorious|ch. 7, "Kalaman Victorious"]]), with **Homebrew** endings.

Run the celebration, the medals and the mysterious letter in blue wax as written. Change the allies' plans for this campaign:
- **Vogler's people** go home under the **Spokesperson**, not Raven ([[Raven Uth Vogler]]). If the Smallfolk reached Admired, the whole militia and fishing fleet sail to retake Vogler, and they ask the party to lead them. Raven's whistle starts the first Kingfisher Festival after the war, blown by whoever kept it.
- **Darrett**, if he lived, goes to Maelgoth to become a true knight, as the module says. If he fell, the city buries him at the gate he held. If Becklin lived, she goes with him.
- **The Vogler file.** If the party helped save the city, Vendri burns it in front of them: "You were never deserters. I needed you." Then the honourable discharge. If they didn't, it stays in her desk ([[DM Guide#Why They Stay Together]]).
- **Cudgel** buries Jeyev's name, whatever happened to him. If she fell at Vogler, her sergeant does.

**The origin characters' endings.** Give each one a closing scene drawn from their DM notes:
- **A knight PC:** knighted, or not, his rank in Kalaman's army (Grand Marshal, if Vendri fell), and the shield's names. Does he carve his own?
- **A sorcerer PC:** his robes, his family upriver, and the Night of the Eye.
- **A kender PC:** the ring, unmade or not, and who paid for it. The chronicle's first volume is finished.
- **A Silvanesti PC:** the grove, Zhelsuel, and whether Alhana hears that a Caladon prince is alive.
- **A dragonborn PC:** his own name, if he chose one, and whose god he is now.
