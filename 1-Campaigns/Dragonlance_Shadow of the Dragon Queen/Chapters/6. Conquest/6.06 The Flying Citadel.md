## The Flying Citadel

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#The Flying Citadel|ch. 7, "The Flying Citadel"]], S1–S25), with **Homebrew** ties.

Run the citadel as written: the tunnels, the crypts of Chemosh's cult, the priests' quarters and the Bastion above. There are three ways to win: unseat Soth, find the mirror of reflected pasts, and put out the Cataclysm's fire. **Tight spaces** apply in the foundations, and **bone and stone** everywhere.

- **Tenadria's Tomb (S9).** Tenadria was Jentida's sister, the ghost in Wakenreth who asked after her ([[4.00 Duty]], location E). Telling her spirit that Jentida is at peace, if the party closed Wakenreth's portal, is worth a moment.
- **The mirror of reflected pasts.** It paralyses Soth: he whispers "Isolde" and stares. If the old man gave **A dragonborn PC** a small silver mirror "for when you want to see who's really looking", it works exactly the same way. It always was this.
- **Caradoc (S22)**, Soth's cowardly seneschal, sells out his master for his own unlife. He's the one who murdered the council, so the Knights would want him alive.

### The Mourning Sanctum

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#The Flying Citadel|ch. 7, "S23: Mourning Sanctum"]]). A knight PC and the fire are **Homebrew** ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]]).

Wersten Kern, Soth's standard-bearer, demands single combat on the scaffold above the brazier of Cataclysm fire. Soth arrives in 3 rounds unless he's paralysed.

**Putting out the fire.** Bathing a holy relic of a good god in the flames puts them out (module): the dragonlance, or a second relic (homebrew): **Ispin's shield**. It held back the Cataclysm's fire at Dargaard's gate once, and **Through the Flames** means it can't be harmed by it now. If a knight PC climbs into the brazier with the shield raised, the same way his ancestor stood in the burning gateway, the fire goes out around him, and he walks out unburnt.

**Soth and a knight PC.** Soth speaks little, but he speaks to "Greenshield's boy". He tells the lie about the first Greenshield, that he was a coward who opened the gates in a panic. If the knight PC knows the truth by now, this is where he says it to Soth's face. Then the ground opens under Soth, as the module describes, and he falls into the dark. He'll be back, at Dargaard, in some other story.

- **Map:** [[map-7.03-bastion-of-takhisis-player.png|Map 7.3: Bastion of Takhisis]] (module), area S23
- **Terrain:** the great brazier of violet fire, the iron scaffold and its stairs, the 4 floating supports, the Dragon Queen's statue, and Soth's ledge 40 feet up.

```encounter
name: Mourning Sanctum
creatures:
  - "1": Wersten Kern (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Wersten Kern's ***banner pike*** (attunement, 1d10 piercing + 1d6 necrotic, charges), **1d8 revenant bones** (crush one for *augury*, three for *commune*) and **1d10 cloak strips** (necrotic components).

> [!check] **Objective:** put out the fire. Wersten Kern and Soth are in the way, but the brazier is the target.

- **Map:** the same board as the Mourning Sanctum
- **Terrain:** Soth arrives from his throne ledge, 40 feet up.

```encounter
name: Lord Soth (don't fight him straight)
creatures:
  - "1": Lord Soth (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): He falls into the dark. Nothing to take.

### The Citadel's Destruction

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#The Citadel's Destruction|ch. 7, "The Citadel's Destruction"]]).

The citadel breaks up. Run the escape routes and the scenes of destruction. Then **Karavarix**, the gold dragon Sarlamir murdered and now the greatest of the death dragons, bursts out of the ground screaming for Sarlamir. Whoever carries the dragonlance is Sarlamir to her.

- **Map:** [[map-7.04-flying-citadel-surface-player.png|Map 7.4: Flying Citadel Surface]] (module)
- **Terrain:** cracks opening across the board (mark one new crack each round), falling rubble, and the escape routes at the edges.

```encounter
name: Karavarix's Revenge
creatures:
  - "1": Greater Death Dragon (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): If there's time while the citadel falls (there isn't): death dragon claws, fangs, wings, **2d8 teeth** and **2d6 vials of Cataclysmic flame essence**.

> [!warning] **Escape.** The citadel is falling. Fight her on the way out, but the objective is the ground.

## Clash of Fallen Flames

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Clash of Fallen Flames|ch. 7, "Clash of Fallen Flames"]]). Kansaldi's demand and a dragonborn PC's answer are **Homebrew** ([[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]] and [[Wurm (DM)]]).

On the battlefield under the falling citadel, Kansaldi comes down on her red dragon, Ignia. She's beside herself with joy that the party lived, because it means the Queen saved them for her.

**Her demand,** from the module, rewritten for her: one of them must kneel and burn, and the others go free. She knows which one she wants. She turns to **A dragonborn PC**, weeping, and calls him "brother". She knows what he is. The Queen told her, last night, in the dark.

**A dragonborn PC's moment.** This is where his arc lands (see [[Wurm (DM)#Arc]]). The voice in him rises to meet her, and offers him everything.
- **If he rejects Takhisis,** helped by everything the old man taught him, the grey in his eyes turns silver. Paladine wakes in him, and his weapon becomes a radiant greatsword. If the old man is anywhere near, this is the moment someone finally learns his name: Fizban.
- **If he gives in,** Kansaldi wins her true believer, and the party has to fight their friend as well as her.

Then Kansaldi and Ignia attack, and fight to the death. As she dies, Kansaldi is laughing: her death is the sacrifice the Queen wanted, and victory is assured. Her eye-gem is a **sentient gem of seeing**, and it whispers to whoever picks it up in Kansaldi's voice.

- **Map:** [[map-7.05-clash-of-fallen-flames-player.png|Map 7.5: Clash of Fallen Flames]] (module)
- **Terrain:** the fallen stone spire (30 feet high) with Kansaldi and Ignia on top, citadel rubble across the south, and dragon bones.

```encounter
name: Clash of Fallen Flames
creatures:
  - "1": Kansaldi Fire-Eyes (DSotDQ)
  - "1": Young Red Dragon (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Kansaldi: the **gem of seeing** in her eye socket (module), a broken pike and broken plate. Ignia (young red dragon, Survival DC 20): **claws**, **fangs**, **hide**, **2d4 scales**, **teeth** (+1d6 fire), **wings** (fire-resistant cloak) and **2d6 Supply**. A dragonborn PC may not want to watch.

> [!check] The finale, played out in full. No one-turn.
