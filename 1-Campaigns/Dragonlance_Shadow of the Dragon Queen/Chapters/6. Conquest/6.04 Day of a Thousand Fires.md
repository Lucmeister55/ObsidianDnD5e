## Day of Dread

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Day of Dread|ch. 7, "Day of Dread"]]), with **Homebrew** faction effects.

The citadel is sighted, and the city waits. Run the "Dread in the Streets" table, then:
- **Trouble at the Gates.** Captain Narnhelm has shut the Trade Gate on the refugees from the trade camps. Opening it gives **+2 Smallfolk** and **-1 Military**. Leaving it shut gives **+1 Military** and **-1 Smallfolk**. This is the last Standing change before the siege, and it lands straight in the ledger.
- **Strange Riders.** Clystran and Darrett arrive on the coppery wasteland dragonnel: the one from the mansion, if the party saved it. Plan the counterattack: the tunnels in the citadel's base, and Clystran's dragonnels.

## Scenario 10: Day of a Thousand Fires

> [!wok] **Bonus battle (optional).** WoK scenario 10. The module doesn't reference it, so the slot is **Homebrew**, taken from the book: it follows the ambush in the mist and comes right before the siege. **Slot:** the Day of Dread, after "Strange Riders" and before the night's attack. **Book:** [[12 - Scenario 10 - Day of a Thousand Fires|transcript]] · **Narration:** [[Battle Lines#Scenario 10: Day of a Thousand Fires|narration lines]]. Skipping it changes nothing ([[Warriors of Krynn#How We Use It]]).

**The book's setup:** Darrett says the Dragon Army has taken the fight to Kalaman on all sides, but Tatina Rookledust's device can carry the heroes beyond the blockade to five local leaders: Bransad Wheathair (a vile beast troubles his land), Ghaldric Longfang, the Bandit King (he wants supplies, and is fickle), Mayor Martha of the crossroads, Mayor Victoria of the mountains and Vulkak Warhoof of the steppes. The heroes win their support by completing each one's quest. **Win** (all five won): the title *Heralds of the West*. For **every leader won**, on a win or a hold, add **one allied unit** to the [[6.02 Return to Kalaman#The Siege Ledger|Siege Ledger]] for [[6.05 Siege of Kalaman]] *(Homebrew bonus.)*. A loss gives nothing.

## Night of Terror

> [!info] Forces
> **Engaged:** the whole wall, under fire all night. **Losses:** Kalaman 100 / 200 / 350, plus Bakaris's 6 knights, enemy 50 / 30 / 10. The militia loses twice as many.

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Night of Terror|ch. 7, "Night of Terror"]]). It's a D&D night, not a WoK battle.

The Dragon Army camps outside the walls and spends the night setting the city on fire. Run the module's night encounters:

- **Kapaks on the walls**, and **sivaks in Kalaman uniforms.** If Jeyev is still loose, he's the one who let them in (see [[Jeyev Veldrews]]).
- **Testing the Defenses.** The ballistae against dragonnels carrying bozaks. **Against fliers** applies, so hand out the javelins.
- **Highmaster's Message.** A lesser death dragon crashes into the street with a note pinned to its ribs. Kansaldi is a fanatic now ([[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]), so replace the module's note with hers:

  > *Children! I can see you! She lets me see you, little lights, all of you, running about on your walls. I prayed for you tonight. I prayed she lets me be the one. Sleep well. Tomorrow I bring the fire, and you will thank me for it. It is the Dragon Queen's will!!*

- **Bakaris Defects.** Lord Bakaris opens the Warrior's Gate to six Dragon Army officers, with six Kalaman knights he talked into it. His son's letter is on him. In this campaign, also:
  - **A dragonborn PC's contract** is in his coat if it wasn't taken earlier. If the party takes it, it's the dragonborn PC's to burn.
  - The six knights who follow Bakaris are **Kalaman knights**, not the Order's. If the Knights were cleared at the Trial, the city sees the difference, and the real Order hunts the traitors down (**+1 Knights**). If they weren't, it's more proof that anyone in knightly armour is a traitor (**-1 Knights**).

- **Map:** a section of Kalaman's wall (see [[map-4.01-kalaman-player.png|Map 4.1: Kalaman]] for where)
- **Terrain:** the rampart walkway with crenellations (half cover), 3 ballistae, a stair down, and flying stands for the dragonnels.

```encounter
name: Testing the Defenses
creatures:
  - "4": Dragon Army Dragonnel (DSotDQ)
  - "8": Bozak Draconian (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Dragonnels that fall inside the walls: **hide**, **teeth** and **2d6 Supply** each, which the Smallfolk will be glad of. Bozaks: arcane dust.

> [!check] **Objective:** the ballistae. Every dragonnel hit by a bolt panics and flees with its riders (module). Stop them before round 3, and the bozaks never reach the wall.

- **Map:** a city square in Kalaman
- **Terrain:** a crater where the death dragon lands, market stalls, 2-3 cellar doors (for herding people to safety), and crowd tokens.

```encounter
name: Highmaster's Message
creatures:
  - "1": Lesser Death Dragon (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): The lesser death dragon (Religion DC 18): **claws**, **fangs** (necrotic weapons), **2d4 teeth**, **wings** (necrotic-resistant cloak), and **2d4 vials of Cataclysmic flame essence** (a violet fire grenade that raises the dead it kills). Kansaldi's note is pinned to its ribs.

> [!check] **Objective:** protect the street. The death dragon crashes into a crowded square, and every round, someone in the crowd dies. Herding the people into a cellar (an action for anyone) is worth as much as hitting the dragon.

- **Map:** the Warrior's Gate
- **Terrain:** the gate passage with the portcullis (track its height over 3 rounds), the guardroom with the winch, and the Dragon Army officers outside the bars.

```encounter
name: Bakaris Defects
creatures:
  - "1": Noble (XMM)
  - "6": Knight (XMM)
  - "6": Dragon Army Officer (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): The officers' ***vicious lances***, the knights' gear, and Lord Bakaris's **1d4 gems** (10 gp) and a dragonborn PC's contract.

> [!check] **Objective:** stop the portcullis within 3 rounds (module). Taking Lord Bakaris alive is the side objective, with his son's letter and a dragonborn PC's contract.
