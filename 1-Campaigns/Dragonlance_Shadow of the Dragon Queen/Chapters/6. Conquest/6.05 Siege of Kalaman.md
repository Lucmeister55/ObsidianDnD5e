## Scenario 11: Siege of Kalaman

> [!info] Forces
> **Engaged:** everyone, plus the Siege Ledger allies, against an assault of about **6,000**. **Losses:** Kalaman 150 / 300 / 600 (the militia twice that), enemy 1,200 / 800 / 400.

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Battle of Kalaman|ch. 7, "Battle of Kalaman"]]) and **WoK** scenario 11.

The assault on the walls, and the defenders' sortie that gives the party their chance to reach the citadel. With WoK, play scenario 11 using the **Siege Ledger** for bonuses. Without it, run the module's "Defending the Wall" encounters, and zoom in on the gate:

> [!example] Skirmish: the Trade Gate
> *Source: **Homebrew** ([[Skirmish Battles]]).*
> - **Objective:** hold the gate for **5 rounds**, until Clystran's dragonnels arrive to carry the party to the citadel.
> - **Allies:** everything in the **Siege Ledger**, and every hero's own unit. This is the battle all of that was for.
> - **Enemies:** 2 **Dragon Army Soldiers** with a ram, 1 **Kapaks** on the walls, 1 **War Engine** out of reach, 1 **Ogres** on round 2, and 1 **Dragonnel Riders** on round 3.
> - **On the walls** applies: polearms shove climbers off ladders. Units on the walls are **behind cover**.
> - **If the Smallfolk are Hostile,** the Trade Gate's section of the wall is empty, and the enemy starts on top of it.
> - **Map:** the Trade Gate and a stretch of wall on a blank board (see [[map-4.01-kalaman-player.png|Map 4.1: Kalaman]] for where).
> - **Terrain:** the wall with crenellations, the gatehouse and portcullis, 2-3 siege ladders, the ram, a ballista on the tower, and the trade camps burning outside.

### Flight of the Dragonnels

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#Flight of the Dragonnels|ch. 7, "Flight of the Dragonnels"]]). Red Ruin's fate is **Homebrew** ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]], [[Wurm (DM)]] and [[Red Ruin]]).

Clystran's dragonnels carry the party up through the battle to the citadel, where **Red Ruin** and **Bakaris the Younger** come for them. This is the moment a knight PC's and a dragonborn PC's stories have been heading for.

- **Red Ruin** fights to knock riders off their mounts, as the module says. She's the one who killed Ispin, and she's a child the Dragon Army raised into a weapon, the same as a dragonborn PC.
- **The choice.** A knight PC wants her dead. A dragonborn PC wants to save her. If someone shouts that Soth gave the order and she only carried it out, she hesitates for one round. Nobody has ever told her she could stop.
  - **Kill her:** the module's version. A knight PC has his revenge, and it's hollow, because Ispin's voice told him who really did it.
  - **Spare her:** she has nowhere to go. If a dragonborn PC or a knight PC offers her a place, she breaks off and flies away. Whether she ever comes back is a sequel.
- **Bakaris the Younger** takes his helmet off to taunt them, and flees at 20 hit points. If he falls, he vanishes into the battle below. If the party never got a dragonborn PC's contract, it's in his saddlebag.

- **Map:** [[1878331-Eye_in_the_Sky_Map.jpg|Eye in the Sky]] (companion map, probably), or an open sky board with clouds
- **Terrain:** flying stands at different heights, the citadel's underside at one edge, and the battle far below.

```encounter
name: Red Ruin
creatures:
  - "1": Red Ruin (DSotDQ)
  - "1": Bakaris the Younger (DSotDQ)
  - "2": Dragon Army Dragonnel (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Red Ruin's ***explosive hand crossbow*** (attunement, a once-a-day explosive bolt), if anyone catches her or her body. Bakaris the Younger's officer gear, if he falls.

> [!check] Full fight, in the air. **Objective:** stay mounted and reach the citadel. Red Ruin tries to knock riders off rather than kill them (module), so the fight is about falling, and the choice about her.

The characters reach **11th level** when they land on the citadel.
