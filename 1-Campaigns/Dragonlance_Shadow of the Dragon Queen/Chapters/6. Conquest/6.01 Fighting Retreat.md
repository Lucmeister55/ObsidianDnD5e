## Scenario 8: Fighting Retreat

> [!info] Forces
> **Engaged:** about **330** left of the expedition against the pursuit. **Losses:** 30 / 70 / 150. About **260** make it home. Update the [[Campaign Tracker]] ([[The War in Numbers|war in numbers]]).

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Escaping the Enemy|ch. 6, "Escaping the Enemy"]]) and **WoK** scenario 8.

The party rejoins Darrett as the Dragon Army regroups beneath its new citadel, and the army runs for the coast. With WoK, play scenario 8 (on a hold, the module's potion reward applies). Without it, or straight after it, zoom in on the rearguard:

> [!example] Skirmish: the canyon mouth
> *Source: **Homebrew** ([[Skirmish Battles]]).*
> - **Objective:** get the army clear of the canyon in **6 rounds**. After that, every allied unit still in the canyon is cut off.
> - **Allies:** 2 **Infantry** and Cudgel's **Mercenaries**, the last of Darrett's army, plus any heroes' own units that came to the Wastes.
> - **Enemies:** 1 **Baaz** per round from the canyon floor, and 1 **Dragonnel Riders** from round 3.
> - **Round 4:** a lesser death dragon flies over, out of the citadel's shadow (as a monster). Terrifying, so every allied unit makes a Morale check.
> - **Map:** [[1878331-edited_cliff.jpg|Cliff]] (companion map), or a blank board as the canyon floor.
> - **Terrain:** two canyon walls of rock pieces, scree as difficult terrain, and the exit line at one edge.

**Fed or starving** ([[4.06 Into the Wastes#The Starving Camp]]) still applies. A starving army starts every unit Bloodied.

## A Hasty Retreat

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#A Hasty Retreat|ch. 7, "A Hasty Retreat"]]). The ships are **Homebrew** ([[4.06 Into the Wastes#The Starving Camp]]).

Darrett sends the party ahead to warn Kalaman and stays to bring the troops home himself. In this campaign, the ships are where the party left them: the engineers at **Wrecker's Edge** have finished the repairs, and they're waiting. If the camp ran out of food, the engineers are fewer, and the ships are short-crewed but sailing.

- **Dragonnel Down** (module) on the way to the coast. If they save it, it's another friendly wasteland dragonnel for Clystran later.
- **Clystran** guides them, if he's with them, then says goodbye at the coast and goes home to Heart's Hollow. He promises to send help if he finds any.
