## Scenario 8: Fighting Retreat

> [!info] Forces
> **Engaged:** about **330** left of the expedition against the pursuit. **Losses:** 30 / 70 / 150. About **260** make it home.

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Escaping the Enemy|ch. 6, "Escaping the Enemy"]]).

The party rejoins Darrett as the Dragon Army regroups beneath its new citadel, and the army runs for the coast. Zoom in on the rearguard with the skirmish below, or play the bonus battle first.

> [!wok] **Bonus battle (optional).** WoK scenario 8, which the module offers for "Escaping the Enemy" ([[07-chapter-6-city-of-lost-names#Escaping the Enemy|ch. 6]]). **Slot:** the flight from the canyon, right after the citadel rises. **Book:** [[10 - Scenario 8 - Fighting Retreat|transcript]] · **Narration:** [[Battle Lines#Scenario 8: Fighting Retreat|narration lines]]. Skipping it changes nothing ([[Warriors of Krynn#How We Use It]]).
>
> **The book's setup:** boulders fall from the fortress rising overhead, and Commander Willem signals a full retreat to the river, because the enemy is trying to cut the army off; the heroes command scouts sent ahead. **Win** (the Alliance is the attacker on all three lines at the River Crossing): the title *Life Guards*. On a hold, the module's potion reward applies. Loss: nothing.

> [!example] Skirmish: the canyon mouth
> *Source: **Homebrew** ([[Skirmish Battles]]).*
> - **Objective:** get the army clear of the canyon in **6 rounds**. After that, every allied unit still in the canyon is cut off.
> - **Allies:** 2 **Infantry** and the Regiment's **Mercenaries** (Cudgel's, or her sergeant's if she fell at Vogler), the last of Darrett's army, plus any heroes' own units that came to the Wastes.
> - **Enemies:** 1 **Baaz** per round from the canyon floor, and 1 **Dragonnel Riders** from round 3.
> - **Round 4:** a lesser death dragon flies over, out of the citadel's shadow (as a monster). Terrifying: every allied battalion makes a **DC 15 Wisdom save** or is **frightened** of it (Group Morale gives advantage above half HP).
> - **Map:** [[1878331-edited_cliff.jpg|Cliff]] (companion map), or a blank board as the canyon floor.
> - **Terrain:** two canyon walls of rock pieces, scree as difficult terrain, and the exit line at one edge.

**Fed or starving** ([[4.06 Into the Wastes#The Starving Camp]]) still applies. A starving army starts every battalion at half HP.

## A Hasty Retreat

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman#A Hasty Retreat|ch. 7, "A Hasty Retreat"]]). The ships are **Homebrew** ([[4.06 Into the Wastes#The Starving Camp]]).

Darrett sends the party ahead to warn Kalaman and stays to bring the troops home himself. In this campaign, the ships are where the party left them: the engineers at **Wrecker's Edge** have finished the repairs, and they're waiting. If the camp ran out of food, the engineers are fewer, and the ships are short-crewed but sailing.

- **Dragonnel Down** (module) on the way to the coast. If they save it, it's another friendly wasteland dragonnel for Clystran later.
- **Clystran** guides them, if he's with them, then says goodbye at the coast and goes home to Heart's Hollow. He promises to send help if he finds any.
