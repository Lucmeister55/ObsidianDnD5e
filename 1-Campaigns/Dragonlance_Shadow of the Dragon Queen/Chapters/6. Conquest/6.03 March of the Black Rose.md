## Scenario 9: March of the Black Rose

> [!info] Forces
> **Engaged:** about **300** against Soth's **30** skeletal knights and **200** marching behind them. **Losses:** Kalaman 40 / 80 / 150. Soth loses 10 knights on a win, 5 on a hold.

> [!wok] **Bonus battle (optional).** WoK scenario 9. The module doesn't reference it, so the slot is **Homebrew**: Soth's skeletal knights and the Blood Knight captain are ours ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]]). **Slot:** the night the vanguard arrives outside Kalaman, before the Day of Dread; the book puts it after the retreat and before the rallying of the five lands. **Book:** [[11 - Scenario 9 - March of the Black Rose|transcript]] · **Narration:** [[Battle Lines#Scenario 9: March of the Black Rose|narration lines]]. Skipping it changes nothing ([[Warriors of Krynn#How We Use It]]).
>
> **The book's setup:** the heroes are woken by a message from Thea: Lord Soth's forces have ambushed their contingent, and an unnatural mist kills the weak and raises them as undead. The heroes search the mist-shrouded field for friendly mystic forces (a witch, barrow spirits, a fairy pool, a tomb). Entries 20 and 91 name Raven Uth Vogler's friendly voice. She is dead, so read Darrett Highwater's instead ([[Warriors of Krynn#House Rules]]). **Win** (the Alliance holds three battle tiles and the Dragon Army none): the title *Survivors*, and Soth loses 10 knights. Hold or loss: nothing.

Run it on the road outside Kalaman as the Dragon Army's vanguard arrives, before the Day of Dread. Ahead of the army march Soth's own knights, skeletal riders under the black rose. At their head rides a **Blood Knight**, a vampire noble of Nightlund in black plate, the captain of Soth's Host.

- If you play the bonus battle, then zoom in for **one scene**: a knight PC and the Blood Knight, who salutes them before the charge.
- Otherwise, run it as a skirmish:

> [!example] Skirmish: the black rose on the road
> *Source: **Homebrew** ([[Skirmish Battles]]).*
> - **Objective:** break the vanguard before it reaches the trade camps outside the walls (**6 rounds**), or the camps burn (-1 Smallfolk).
> - **Allies:** 2 **Infantry**, 1 **Knights**, a knight PC's Company if he has it, and anything from the Siege Ledger.
> - **Enemies:** 2 **Undead Knights**, 1 **Baaz** following behind, and the Blood Knight captain as a monster at their head.
> - **The black rose standard** *(Homebrew)*: planted on the road at the head of the column, black silk on a lance. While it stands, every enemy unit within 60 feet of it **regains 10 HP** at the start of each enemy turn, and the first enemy unit destroyed within 60 feet **rises again** at half HP. A hero can tear it down with an action next to it (DC 15 Strength), or destroy it (AC 15, 30 HP, vulnerable to radiant). When it falls, the regeneration stops, and the Blood Knight turns on whoever did it.

- **The Blood Knight captain.** Use a [[vampire-nightbringer-xmm|vampire nightbringer]] on a [[warhorse-skeleton-xmm|warhorse skeleton]], or a weakened version if the party is struggling. He is proud, courteous and terrifying, and talks before he kills. Ispin's shield is restored now, and its **Through the Flames** protects a knight PC from fire. (The hollow Ispin is already gone: the party laid him to rest in the catacombs, [[3.13 The Raided Catacombs]].)

- **Map:** [[1878331-Road_Ambush_Grid(140ppi-40x30).jpg|Road ambush]] (companion map, probably), as the road outside Kalaman
- **Terrain:** the road, the tents of the trade camps at one edge, a low wall, and the black-rose banner at the head of the column.

> [!danger] Bloodied: skeletal knight
> *At half HP. Tell the players. Source: **Companion** ([[Bloodied and Bruised]]).*
> - **Cornered:** when first Bloodied, a melee attack as a reaction.
> - **Battle Presence:** two reactions a round.
> - **Boneshard** (roll a d4 when it dies: on a 1): it bursts. 10-foot radius, DC 16 Dexterity, 20 (8d4) piercing, half on a success. Undead take none.

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Nothing. A knight PC won't let anyone touch him. His black-rose armour goes into the river, or onto a pyre.

> [!check] Full fight, personal. **Objective:** lay Ispin to rest. A knight PC has to land the final blow, or the shield does.
