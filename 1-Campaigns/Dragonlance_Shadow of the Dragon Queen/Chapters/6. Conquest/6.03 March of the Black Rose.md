## Scenario 9: March of the Black Rose

> [!info] Forces
> **Engaged:** about **300** against Soth's **30** skeletal knights and **200** marching behind them. **Losses:** Kalaman 40 / 80 / 150. Soth loses 10 knights on a win, 5 on a hold.

> [!wok] Source: **WoK** scenario 9. The module doesn't reference it, so where it goes is **Homebrew**: Soth's skeletal knights, and the hollow Ispin ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]]).

Run it on the road outside Kalaman as the Dragon Army's vanguard arrives, before the Day of Dread. Ahead of the army march Soth's own knights, skeletal riders under the black rose. At their head rides the **hollow Ispin**, wearing Ispin's face, the death knight Soth raised from his stolen body.

- With WoK, play scenario 9. Then zoom in for **one scene**: a knight PC and the hollow Ispin.
- Without WoK, run it as a skirmish:

> [!example] Skirmish: the black rose on the road
> *Source: **Homebrew** ([[Skirmish Battles]]).*
> - **Objective:** break the vanguard before it reaches the trade camps outside the walls (**6 rounds**), or the camps burn (-1 Smallfolk).
> - **Allies:** 2 **Infantry**, 1 **Knights**, a knight PC's Company if he has it, and anything from the Siege Ledger.
> - **Enemies:** 2 **Undead Knights**, 1 **Baaz** following behind, and the hollow Ispin as a monster at their head.
> - **Bone and stone** applies, so maces and clubs shine.

- **The hollow Ispin.** Use the full [[death-knight-xmm|death knight]] at this level, or a weakened version if the party is struggling. Ispin's shield is restored now, and its **Through the Flames** makes a knight PC immune to the death knight's hellfire. When the thing falls, the shield is warm again, and the knight PC hears Ispin one last time: "There. Now let me go, lad." This is Ispin's real funeral.

- **Map:** [[1878331-Road_Ambush_Grid(140ppi-40x30).jpg|Road ambush]] (companion map, probably), as the road outside Kalaman
- **Terrain:** the road, the tents of the trade camps at one edge, a low wall, and the black-rose banner at the head of the column.

```encounter
name: The Hollow Ispin
creatures:
  - "1": Death Knight (XMM)
  - "4": Skeletal Knight (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Nothing. A knight PC won't let anyone touch him. His black-rose armour goes into the river, or onto a pyre.

> [!check] Full fight, personal. **Objective:** lay Ispin to rest. A knight PC has to land the final blow, or the shield does.
