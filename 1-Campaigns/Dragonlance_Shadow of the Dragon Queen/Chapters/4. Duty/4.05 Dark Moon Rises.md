## Scenario 6: Dark Moon Rises

> [!info] Forces
> **Engaged:** about **510** in the fleet against 6 minotaur corsair galleys (about 300 minotaurs and crew). **Losses:** 20 / 50 / 120 (a burning troopship on a hold). The supply ship goes down whatever happens.

> [!wok] Source: **WoK** scenario 6 (the module doesn't reference it). The ambush on the crossing, the minotaur corsairs and the sinking of the supply ship are **Homebrew** ([[4.99 Survival Rules#Cut Off]]). The module's crossing is uneventful.

The night crossing, run as described in "Cut Off" in [[4.99 Survival Rules]]. It's the night of **22 Fierswelth**: Nuitari is full (invisible to all but its servants, though a moon-touched PC feels it), Lunitari is waning and Solinari is close to new, and the fleet sails into a fog bank in the middle of the bay. If you're using *Warriors of Krynn*, this is where its mass-combat scenario belongs. Otherwise, run it as a set piece aboard the party's ship.

> The lanterns go out one by one as the fog takes them, until all that is left of the fleet is the creak of other hulls somewhere in the grey, and a sailor singing softly to keep his nerve. Then the singing stops. Out of the fog, close enough to touch, comes a black sail, and then another, and the air fills with the hiss of fire arrows.

**Who they are.** The black sails are **minotaur corsairs** out of Mithas, hired with Dragon Army steel. Huge, horned and fearless, they come over the rail with boarding axes, roaring in their own tongue. They're paid to sink the supply ship, and they don't stay to die for anyone else's war. A **Dragon Army officer** sails with them as paymaster, to make sure the job gets done. (How did they know the route? The Leak: see [[4.06 Into the Wastes#The Leak]].) A knight PC may remember Ispin's story about the minotaur pirates who chased him across the Blood Sea for three days. It's starting to sound less like a tall tale.

**The target.** The black ships go for the **supply ship**. The party's ship is alongside it, and they can see it being boarded and set alight. Let them try to save it: cross on grappling lines, fight on its burning deck, and fail. It goes down whatever they do. What they can save is a few crates: each round they spend fighting on its deck after the second wave, they haul back **1d6 Supply** or **1d6 Water**, but they lose the 1d4 × 5 roll from [[4.99 Survival Rules]] twice over if anyone is still aboard when it sinks.

**Round by round.** Run three waves of boarders. Between waves, roll a d6: 1–2 the fire spreads (put it out within 2 rounds, or the supply ship's hold goes up), 3–4 a dragonnel strafes the deck (DC 14 Dex save, 2d10 fire), 5–6 a troopship nearby takes a fire arrow in the rigging, close enough to hear the screaming.

- **Map:** [[1878331-242_Sail_Ship-grid.jpg|Sail ship]] (companion map), the same board as wave 2
- **Terrain:** fog at the edges, grappling hooks over the rail, and lanterns going out.

```encounter
name: Boarders (wave 1)
creatures:
  - "4": Minotaur of Baphomet (XMM)
```

**Minotaurs of Krynn.** Use the *Minotaur of Baphomet*, but they serve no demon lord: they're proud sailors who live by honour and strength. Their axes are the same.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Wave 1, yes. Save the table time for waves 2 and 3, where the **objective** is the supply ship's crates (see above).

- **Map:** [[1878331-242_Sail_Ship-grid.jpg|Sail ship]] (companion map) twice, side by side: the party's ship and the supply ship
- **Terrain:** grappling lines between the decks, fire markers, crates in the supply ship's hold (each one is Supply or Water), and fog at the map edges.

```encounter
name: Boarders (wave 2)
creatures:
  - "1": Pirate Captain (XMM)
  - "2": Minotaur of Baphomet (XMM)
  - "3": Pirate (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Gear only. Boarding axes (greataxes), the human crew's cutlasses, and the **corsair captain's chart** of the bay's shoals. On its back, in Dragon Army script, is the contract for the job, with a sum that would buy a small ship.

- **Map:** the same two ships as wave 2
- **Terrain:** add fire markers on the supply ship, a flying stand for the dragonnel, and a sinking marker (the supply ship goes down when this wave breaks).

```encounter
name: Boarders (wave 3)
creatures:
  - "1": Sivak Draconian (DSotDQ)
  - "1": Dragon Army Officer (DSotDQ)
  - "1": Dragon Army Dragonnel (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): The sivak's **broken serrated sword** (repairs into a 2d8 two-hander, 150 gp) and **2d4 ounces of arcane dust**. If the dragonnel falls on deck: **1 hide** and **2d6 Supply** of meat.

When the third wave breaks, the black ships simply leave. They came for one thing, and it's on the bottom of the bay.

> When the fog lifts, the fleet is still there, but only just: hulls scorched and listing, sails in rags, men bailing with their helmets. Where the supply ship was, there is nothing but barrels and spars and one green banner of Kalaman turning slowly on the swell. Ahead, red cliffs rise out of the water.

The fleet limps into the cove at **Wrecker's Edge**.
