## Testimony

These are the defaults. Rewrite the lines around whatever actually happened in your Storm Clouds.

**Nobody is just wrong.** Every witness either **lies for a reason** or **makes an honest mistake**, and working out which is half the game. A liar's testimony cracks when the party finds the motive and asks the right question. An honest witness can be won over: once evidence or another witness clears up the mistake, they often admit it themselves.

**Charge 1: The Order sent the killers.**

*Witness: Sergeant Holt of the castle gate.*

*Why:* **an honest mistake, and his neck.** Holt let the "knights" in. If they were monsters, it's his fault, so he needs them to have been real knights. He believes every word, and he's terrified. Shown the remains, he goes grey, and says quietly that he wondered why they never lifted their visors.

1. "Six knights came up the hill at noon, in the Order's armour, with the Crown on their shields."
2. "They came straight from the chapterhouse. I saw them leave it." → **Point out with** Tatina**: she was at the gate, and they came up from the **harbour**, not the chapterhouse.
3. "I saw their faces. Solamnic men, every one." → ★ **Break it with** the wights' remains**: there were no faces under those visors.
4. "They said the Order's oath at the gate, so I let them in." → Press: they said it wrong. Holt didn't know the words himself.

*Witness: Lord Bakaris, speaking for himself.*

*Why:* **lying, with a motive.** Bakaris led the disaster at Steel Springs, and his son is "missing". As long as the city is angry at the Order, nobody asks about that. And he's hated the Knights for every snub since Vogler. Press him on Steel Springs, and he loses his temper.

1. "The Order has wanted Kalaman back since before the Cataclysm."
2. "This 'Dread Knight' is a nursery tale the knights tell to frighten nobles." → ★ **Break it with** the catacomb records**, or with **Svilnt's boasting**: the Dread Knight is real, he was in the catacombs, and the Dragon Army calls him an ally.
3. "No knight of Solamnia has worn that armour in Kalaman for a century, except to make trouble." → **Point out with** Durstan Rial**: the armour came out of the crypts, on the dead.

**Charge 2: The esquires let it happen.**

*Witness: Mistress Oddny, a councillor's clerk who hid behind the tapestries.*

*Why:* **an honest mistake, and a small kindness.** She heard boots right behind the knights, and assumed. From behind the tapestry, she couldn't see a thing. DC 15 Insight: someone has been kind to her since the massacre. Press her, and she admits Lord Bakaris paid for her husband's funeral.

1. "The esquires walked into the castle right behind the knights."
2. "They went straight up to the council chamber with them." → ★ **Break it with** the wights' remains**: those wights died at the **castle doors**. The party was fighting them outside.
3. "When it was over, they were the only ones standing."
4. "They didn't even look surprised." → Press: she was behind a tapestry. She didn't see their faces.

*Witness: Lord Bakaris.*

*Why:* **lying, with a motive.** Same as before: blame the party for Kalaman's dead, and nobody blames him for Steel Springs.

1. "These so-called heroes knew the Dragon Army's plans before anyone else."
2. "If they'd cared about Kalaman, they'd have warned us." → ★ **Break it with** the war council minutes** (they argued against Steel Springs) or **the Wheelwatch letters** (they did warn the city).
3. "Instead, they let my son ride to his death." → Press: Bakaris led that attack himself.

**Charge 3: The draconian is one of them** (only if the rumours spread).

Nobody in Kalaman has a word for what a dragonborn PC is, so everyone calls him a draconian, his friends included. The charge isn't really what he is. It's whether he's **the enemy's**.

*Witness: Teague, a dockworker.*

*Why:* **an honest mistake, and grief.** His brother died at Steel Springs, cut down by draconians. He really did see a dragonborn PC's eyes burn, and he isn't lying. He just doesn't know what it means. If the High Hill villager speaks, Teague listens, and by the end he might be the one who apologises.

1. "I've seen the lizard-men up close. Scales, claws, the lot. That one's the same."
2. "And at night, his eyes burn. Just like theirs." → **Point out with** the High Hill villager**: his eyes burned when he fought **for** Vogler. Draconians' eyes don't burn at all (passive Perception or any character who's fought them).
3. "He came out of nowhere, and nobody knows what he is."

*Witness: Lord Bakaris.*

*Why:* **lying, with a motive.** If a dragonborn PC is a draconian, he's a monster, and Bakaris keeps his property or gets paid for its head. If he's anything else, the contract was a slave sale, and Kalaman might set him free. Nobody in the hall knows *what* the dragonborn PC is, Bakaris included. But Bakaris knows how long he's owned him, and his own seal is on the contract.

1. "The creature has been my family's property for years. I know what it is."
2. "It's one of the Dragon Army's draconians, the same as the rest, and it always has been." → ★ **Break it with** a petrified baaz**: the Dragon Army's draconians turn to stone, acid or fire when they die. A dragonborn PC **died** at High Hill, and got back up. Whatever he is, he isn't one of theirs. Or ★ **break it with the contract**: Bakaris has owned him for decades, since he was a hatchling. Draconians appeared out of nowhere a few years ago, with the Dragon Armies.
3. "It should be chained, or killed." → Press: because Bakaris would lose his property otherwise.

## The Hidden Witness: Caradoc

Caradoc, Soth's seneschal, can't be caught or killed here: he's a ghost bound to Soth, and when he's in danger he simply leaves the body he's wearing. But he can **testify**, wearing someone else, and he's the one who got the party accused in the first place.

- **If Durstan Rial was never freed** in Storm Clouds, Caradoc is still wearing him. "Ser Durstan" is the prosecution's star witness: a real knight of the Order who survived the massacre and swears he saw the esquires **open the castle doors** to the killers. He's the reason the party is in the dock at all.
- **If Durstan was freed,** Caradoc takes someone else for the day: a guard, a clerk, a juror. Pick whoever would hurt most to lose.

*Why:* **lying, for his master.** Turning Kalaman against the Order and its champions weakens the city before the siege, and it amuses him.

*Witness: "Ser Durstan Rial".*

1. "I was in the hall when the gates were opened. I saw the esquires at the doors."
2. "They let the killers in. I watched them do it." → Press: ask him something only the real Durstan would know. **Darrett** met him once, in Vogler, years ago, and Caradoc has been faking that memory all along (Storm Clouds).
3. "I would know a true knight anywhere. Those were not the Order's." → **Point out**, with the **wights' remains**,: he's right that they weren't knights, which he could only know if he knew what they were.
4. "Search my memory, if you like. I have nothing to hide." → ★ **Break it** with anything that exposes the possession: Darrett's question, a *detect evil and good* scroll from Wyhan, or a spellcaster PC feeling the cold of the Shadowfell coming off him (DC 18 Arcana).

**When he's exposed,** Caradoc doesn't fight. He laughs, and leaves the body in front of the whole hall: a cold shape that drifts up through the rafters and is gone. Durstan, or whoever he wore, collapses, with no memory of the last weeks. Every charge he testified to falls with him, and it's the moment the hall understands what really walked into the castle.

**If he isn't exposed,** his testimony is what gets the party arrested ([[4.03 The Trial - Verdicts#If They Are Arrested]]).

## Defence Witnesses

Each faction at **Trusted or better** sends someone to speak for a charge it believes in: a voice the hall already trusts, which can back up the party's argument or fill in a piece of evidence they're missing. At **Honoured**, their word alone can carry a charge. Each faction at **Hostile** sends a witness for the prosecution instead, with one more testimony to break.

- **The Knights' charge:** the Knights, the Military (Vendri knows better) and the Mages (Wyhan can read Soth's fire). The Guilds testify against it unless they're Trusted or better.
- **The party's charge:** any faction at Trusted or better.
- **A dragonborn PC's charge:** the Military (Vendri's protection), the Vogler Refugees (they saw him at High Hill) and the Knights, if a knight PC speaks for him. The Smallfolk testify against it unless they're Trusted or better.
