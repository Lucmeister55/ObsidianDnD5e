## C: Blue Phoenix Shrine

Habbakuk's flooded shrine and the Dimernesti sea elves. Run as in the module.

- **Water.** The brass basin in C3 is fresh water, and it refills every dawn: **1d4 + 2 Water** a day, taken with a prayer. Anyone who takes it without one wakes the water weirds in C2. The party can camp in the gorge for as long as the Wash stays low.
- **The sea elves** know which ravines carry fresh springs at low Wash. If the party helps Ishvern, he marks them on the map: **advantage on foraging checks** for the rest of the chapter.
- **Habbakuk's Blessing** (module) for everyone who honours the shrine.
- **Tie: a ranger PC.** Habbakuk is one of the ranger PC's gods. When he washes in the basin, a sea bird lands on the phoenix statue and watches him. Ishvern asks him, puzzled, why the shrine has never been so bright.

- **Map:** [[map-5.02-blue-phoenix-shrine-player.png|Map 5.2: Blue Phoenix Shrine]] (module), area C2
- **Terrain:** 2 feet of standing water everywhere (difficult terrain), twin pillars, the coral altar and the phoenix statue.

```encounter
name: Altar Guardians
creatures:
  - "3": Water Weird (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Water weirds leave **1d2 vials of water essence** each (drink it: it cleanses the body, homebrew: and counts as **1 Water**) and arcane dust. Taking them angers the shrine.

> [!tip] **Avoidable.** The water weirds only rise if someone skips the cleansing rite. If they do, one-turn it, and let the price be Habbakuk's Blessing for whoever angered the shrine.

- **Map:** Map 5.2 (module), area C6
- **Terrain:** black algae on every wall (the puddings come out of it), and the well in the middle.

```encounter
name: Shrine Guardians
creatures:
  - "3": Black Pudding (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Each black pudding: **2d6 vials of ooze goop** (Arcana DC 14). Thrown, it sticks and burns.

> [!tip] **Avoidable**, as above.
