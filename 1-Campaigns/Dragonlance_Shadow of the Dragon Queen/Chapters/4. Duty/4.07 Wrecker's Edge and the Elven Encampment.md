## A: Wrecker's Edge

Run as in the module: the party scouts the coast for a landing and finds the smugglers' cove, the Silvanesti ship and its two air elementals, and Dalamar. The cove becomes **Darrett's camp** for the rest of the chapter.

- **Water.** The smugglers' huts hide a stone cistern of stale rainwater: **20 Water**, once. Barrels from the supply ship wash ashore over the first day: **1d4 × 5 Supply**, salt-soaked but edible, and Darrett's quartermaster claims all of it for the army unless someone argues.
- **The elven ship** is the only seaworthy, unburned vessel on this coast, and the players will want it, to fetch food from Kalaman. Dalamar won't give it up, the air elementals guard it, and stealing it strands the elves. Even if they take it, the black sails still patrol the bay. Let them be tempted.
- **Dalamar** is fed by his own magic, and he trades: a casting of *create food and water* (15 Supply and 30 Water) for news of Kalaman, or for an hour alone with a sorcerer PC.
- **Tie: [[Davos Bloodbane|a sorcerer PC]].** Dalamar sees what the sorcerer PC is at once, a sorcerer with raw, unschooled power, and he's fascinated. He offers to teach the moon-touched PC "the things Antimodes won't". It's real knowledge, and it's a black robe's offer. If he accepts, Dalamar teaches him one spell from a Black Robe's book: swap one sorcerer spell he knows for a necromancy or enchantment spell of the same level from the wizard list. The Mages find out eventually: **-1 Mages** when they do.

- **Map:** the same sail ship (companion map), as the elven ship in the hidden cove
- **Terrain:** a small deck, the cove's rocky beach and the rotten smugglers' huts at one edge.

```encounter
name: Elven Ship Guardians
creatures:
  - "2": Air Elemental (XMM)
```

> [!tip] **Avoidable.** This fight only happens if the party boards the elves' ship. If it does, one-turn it, and make sure Dalamar hears about it.

## B: Elven Encampment

Zhelsuel's exiles from House Mystic, cataloguing every rock in the Wastes. Run as in the module. Dalamar's arrangement (sending the party to C, D and E) is the chapter's spine.

- **Water and food.** The elves make what they need by magic and share nothing with outsiders. If Zhelsuel is won over (Elvish, DC 16 Persuasion, or a Silvanesti PC), they fill every waterskin the party has and give them **quith-pa**, elven travel bread: **10 Supply** that weighs only half a pound a ration.
- **Tie: [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn)|a Silvanesti PC]].** Zhelsuel was his teacher, as near to an uncle as a prince could have. Zhelsuel doesn't recognise the scarred, white-haired thing in front of him, at least not at first. Give the Silvanesti PC the scene and let him choose (see [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]]):
  - **Reveal himself.** Zhelsuel breaks. The camp's doors open: free water and quith-pa every visit, the elves' maps (advantage on navigation within 2 hexes of the camp), and a dozen elven mages for Scenario 7, if Zhelsuel can be persuaded the war is Silvanesti's war too. And now the exiles know a Caladon prince is alive, and so will Alhana, eventually.
  - **Stay nameless.** Zhelsuel says, kindly, that a Silvanesti PC reminds him of a boy he once taught. That's all.
- **Tie: a sorcerer PC.** Zhelsuel is contemptuous of a human with untrained magic, and says so.
