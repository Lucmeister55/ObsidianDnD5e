# The Northern Wastes: Survival

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, built on the **Module**'s travel rules, the Wash and the encounter tables ([[06-chapter-5-the-northern-wastes#Into the Wastes|ch. 5, "Into the Wastes"]]).

For [[4.00 Duty]]: endless canyons, blistering days, freezing nights, storms out of a clear sky, and the **Wash**, a moon-driven salt flood that roars through the canyons and leaves them dry a week later. No fresh water, almost no food, nobody coming. Three things to track: each character's **Supply** (food), **Water** and **exhaustion**.

## Cut Off

**The crossing** ([[4.05 Dark Moon Rises]]): black sails wait in the fog **exactly where the fleet would be**, and go straight for the **supply ship**, because Takhisis heard Darrett's plan through [[Wurm|a dragonborn PC]] ([[Wurm (DM)]]). Let the players ask *how did they know?*, and don't answer.

**What survives:**
- **What the party carries**, minus **1d4 × 5** Supply and the same of Water lost in the chaos (more if they fought on the burning supply ship).
- **Darrett and the army**, with a few days' rations, staying at Wrecker's Edge to mend the ships ([[4.06 Into the Wastes#The Starving Camp]]).
- **No way back.** The black sails patrol the bay, repairs take weeks, and the Night of the Eye is coming ([[4.04 The Deadline]]). The only way is forward.

## Supply and Water

- **Supply** is food: **1 ration** (a day for one character) weighs **2 lb**. Dried fish, hardtack, quith-pa and butchered meat all count. Each character carries their own share.
- **Water:** **1 Water** is a gallon, a day for one character, **8 lb**. A waterskin holds 4, a cask 20.
- **Pack animals** (a mule or pack sled from the Guilds) carry **300 pounds**, but a mule drinks **2 Water a day** itself.
- Sources: the factions' dividends ([[Faction Rules#Dividends]]) and anything bought in Kalaman.

### Encumbrance

**Variant encumbrance**, all campaign:
- Carrying more than **5 × Strength** in pounds: your speed drops by 10 feet.
- Carrying more than **10 × Strength**: your speed drops by 20 feet, and you have disadvantage on ability checks, attack rolls and saving throws that use Strength, Dexterity or Constitution.

### Travel Light

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.

Before the ships leave, everyone should **stash what they don't need in the Bastion**, since every pound of gear is a pound of water they can't carry. **Have an NPC say so** without giving anything away: the quartermaster at the military docks says the ships are small, the holds are for the troops' food and water, and each passenger gets one pack. "What you can carry, you can bring. The rest stays ashore." Cudgel (or her sergeant, if she fell at Vogler) adds their own advice: "Leave the silverware, lad."

A little spare gear can ride in **Darrett**'s baggage, to the camp at Wrecker's Edge.

**The numbers** (average Strength 13, so 65 lb before Encumbered and 130 lb before Heavily Encumbered, with about 30 lb of gear each):

| | Unencumbered | Encumbered (−10 ft) |
|---|---|---|
| Party carrying capacity for provisions | about 175 lb: **3 days** | about 500 lb: **10 days** |
| If a knight PC keeps his plate (about 80 lb of gear) | about 140 lb: **under 3 days** | about 450 lb: **9 days** |

The party uses **50 lb a day** (five characters, 1 Supply and 1 Water each). The plan: **everyone Encumbered, nobody Heavily Encumbered**, since Heavily Encumbered means disadvantage on the Constitution saves against heat and cold.

> [!tip] What to expect
> Water sources are fairly common. What hurts is **carrying capacity across the long dry stretches**: packs hold 8–10 days, and a 6–7 day stretch without water (say, the shrine to Heart's Hollow, via Sunward and Wakenreth) turns into 9–11 days once you add getting lost, sandstorms, heat and detours round encounters. A party with no help runs dry for **2–4 days** on a short route. What changes that is **capacity and magic** (mules, the still, the map, *create food and water* scrolls, the decanter), not extra rations. Spare rations can't be carried anyway, so they're best sent back to Darrett's camp.

A **mule** adds about 4 days (it drinks 2 Water a day), and a **pack sled** adds about 6. **Tents** count as shelter but weigh 20 lb per two people, about a day of provisions for the party.

## Exhaustion

Use the 2024 exhaustion rules: each level gives -2 to all d20 Tests and -5 feet of speed, and a character dies at level 6.

In the Wastes, exhaustion is **sticky**:
- A long rest removes **only 1 level of exhaustion**, and only if the character ate and drank that day and rested in shelter.
- Without all three, a long rest removes **no** exhaustion.

## The Daily Routine

Run every day in the Wastes in this order. It takes a minute once everyone knows it.

> [!info] Introduce it in stages
> Day 1: Supply, Water and exhaustion only (steps 5 to 7). Day 2: add navigation and the Wastes Day table. Day 3: add the Wash and foraging. By day 4 it's routine ([[Playtest Ideas#Introduction Schedule]]).

1. **The sky.** Count down one day to the Deadline (see [[4.00 Duty]]). Roll on the **Wastes Day** table below, and roll the module's **Nature of the Wash** table (d10: 1–3 low, 4–6 high, 7–10 unchanged).
2. **Pace and route.** The party picks a pace: fast (15 miles), normal (12) or slow (9, and they can use Stealth). The Wastes are difficult terrain, so these are already halved.
3. **Navigation.** One character makes a **DC 15 Wisdom (Survival)** check. On a failure, the party is lost. They cover only half the distance, but still eat and drink a full day's worth. Having Clystran as a guide doubles distance and skips this check.
4. **Encounter.** Roll the module's Northern Wastes Encounters table (d20) once a day, but treat **11–12 as no encounter** too: patrols on 13–14, the gate on 15, predators on 16–20. That's an encounter about 2 days in 5, or 10–12 over the whole trek. Most encounters are a **choice**, not a fight (see "Fight or Avoid" below).
5. **Drink.** Each character drinks **1 Water**. A character who can't **gains 1 level of exhaustion** automatically.
6. **Eat.** Each character eats **1 Supply**. A character who can't must succeed on a **DC 10 Constitution** saving throw, or gain 1 level of exhaustion. The DC rises by 2 for each day in a row without food.
7. **Make camp.** A long rest needs **shelter**: a tent, a cave, a ruin or a settlement. Resting in the open, a character must succeed on a **DC 12 Constitution** saving throw or gain 1 level of exhaustion. The DC is 15 during a sandstorm or a high Wash.

## Wastes Day (d8)

| d8 | The day | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Killing heat** | Each character must drink an **extra 1 Water** or make a **DC 13 Constitution** saving throw. On a failure, they gain 1 level of exhaustion. Heavy armour gives disadvantage. Travelling only by night avoids it, but halves the day's distance. |
| 2 | **Bitter cold night** | Each character without a tent or fire must succeed on a **DC 12 Constitution** saving throw or gain 1 level of exhaustion. |
| 3 | **Sandstorm** | Heavily obscured all day. Navigation DC rises to 20. Travel at half distance, or shelter in place and lose the day (still eating and drinking). |
| 4 | **Salt rain** | Stinging brine falls from a clear sky. It can't be drunk, and it fouls **1 Water** per character in any open or leaking container, unless someone thought to cover them. |
| 5–6 | **Merciless sun** | Nothing extra, just a long, hard day. |
| 7 | **Still and grey** | A rare kind day. Navigation and Survival checks are made with advantage. |
| 8 | **Strange light** | The moons are visible in daylight. The Wash will change tomorrow, so roll it twice and take the worse result. [[Davos Bloodbane\|A moon-touched PC]] can feel it coming (see below). |

### The Wash

Use the module's rules. When the Wash rises, the party makes a **DC 12 group Wisdom (Survival)** check, or they're caught in a **flash flood** (module chapter 5). In this campaign, the Wash is also **salt water**. Anyone who drinks it must succeed on a **DC 13 Constitution** saving throw or be poisoned for the day, and it doesn't count as Water. Fresh water exists only where noted in the locations of [[4.00 Duty]].

## Fight or Avoid

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, on the **Module**'s encounter tables.

Out here, a fight is a trade. It costs **hit points**, which cost **rest**, which costs **days** and **provisions**. But a dead predator is **food**. So when an encounter comes up, tell the players what they see, and let them choose:

| Encounter | Avoid it | Fight it |
|---|---|---|
| **Wastes predators** | DC 13 group Stealth or Survival to slip past. On a success, it costs half a day's travel to go round. On a failure, it's a fight anyway. | A [[One-Turn Resolution\|one-turn]] (or a full fight, if the table wants). The reward is meat: **2d6 Supply** from most predators, **4d6** from a giant shark, **10d6** from a purple worm, plus boiled **Water** from land beasts, a **feast** that night, and hides and teeth ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]). Butchering takes an hour or more. |
| **Dragon Army patrol** | DC 13 group Stealth. On a failure, they're spotted, and the patrol attacks or signals. | Their packs carry **1d4 Supply and 1d4 Water** per soldier. A survivor can be questioned. If the party loses, Camp Carrionclay (module). |
| **Malfunctioning gate** | Just walk on. | A clay golem fight, and a teleportation circle afterwards (module). Worth it if the party is far from water. |
| **Bones in the sand** | | Nothing to fight. Sometimes a dead traveller's pack: 1d4 Supply. |

**The economy in one line:** avoiding costs **time**, fighting costs **HP** and pays in **food**. With the deadline ticking and the water running low, both are real choices.

**Keep the combat budget.** Offer almost every one of these as a [[One-Turn Resolution|one-turn]]. Save full fights for the ones the players pick, or the ones that matter (a patrol that knows the party's route, thanks to the Leak).

### One-Turn Complications

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, on the **Module**'s encounter tables ([[06-chapter-5-the-northern-wastes#Wastes Predators|ch. 5]]).

Out here a one-turn costs more than hit points. **If any character's turn is Basic, the complication lands on them**, on top of the damage. Mention the danger when you set the scene, and a Stylish turn that answers it stops it outright. When the encounters pile up, use [[One-Turn Resolution#Quick Resolve|Quick Resolve]] instead: no descriptions, one hit each, and the complication on a 1 on a d6.

| Encounter | Complication |
|---|---|
| **Giant scorpions** (low Wash) | **Stung.** Poisoned until their next long rest, and the fever costs 1 extra Water that night. |
| **Giant octopuses** (high Wash) | **Grabbed by the pack.** A tentacle tears it open in the brine: lose **4 Water** and **1d4 Supply**. |
| **Giant spiders** (low) | **Webbed in.** Poisoned for the day, and their pack is left in the web unless someone spends an hour cutting it free (half a day's travel). |
| **Merrow** (high) | **Harpooned and dragged under.** They swallow the Wash: DC 13 Constitution save or poisoned for the day. |
| **Manticores** (low) | **They come back.** The survivors stalk the party and rain spikes on the camp at night, so the next long rest isn't sheltered. |
| **Plesiosauruses** (high) | **Swamped.** A wave takes a pack: lose **1d6 Supply** and **4 Water**. |
| **Wasteland dragonnels** (low) | **Snatched.** One flies off with a pack (**2d6 Supply or Water**, the party's choice). If dragonnels die and [[Clystran]] hears of it, he's slow to forgive. |
| **Giant sharks** (high) | **Mauled.** 1 level of exhaustion, and the blood draws the sharks back to the carcass: no Supply from this kill. |
| **Purple worm** | Don't one-turn it unless the table insists. If you do: **swallowed**. They're cut out in time, but the acid ruins their pack and everything in it. |
| **Baaz patrol** | **Stuck.** A dying baaz turns to stone around a weapon, which stays stuck for a minute, and the rest run. |
| **Sivak patrol** | **A face in the dark.** The sivak dies in the shape of whoever killed it. If that's a PC, the corpse is left behind for someone else to find, and their face is now known to the Dragon Army. |
| **Kapak patrol** | **Acid.** A kapak bursts against their pack: lose **4 Water**, from a split skin. |
| **Officers on dragonnels** | **One gets away.** A rider escapes to Camp Carrionclay. The next patrol knows roughly where the party is, and Akhviri arrives at Carrionclay early. |
| **Malfunctioning gate** | **Thrown.** The gate surges as the golem falls, and flings them a mile away. Finding them costs half a day. |

## Finding Food and Water

- **Foraging.** Instead of travelling for a day, the party can forage. Each character makes a **Wisdom (Survival)** check. On a 15 or higher they gain 1 Supply, and on a 20 or higher, 2. When the Wash is high, the DC drops to 13: the canyons fill with fish, crabs and stranded sea creatures, but the water still can't be drunk without a still or magic. Foraging finds food, not Water, and foraging days still cost a day's food and water.
- **Hunting predators.** A slain beast from the encounter table yields rations, which count as **Supply**: 2d6 from a giant scorpion, manticore, spider, octopus, plesiosaurus or dragonnel, 4d6 from a giant shark, and 10d6 from a purple worm. Each also yields hides, teeth and spikes worth crafting ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]). It takes a harvesting check (Survival, DC 10 + CR) and an hour or more. A ranger PC harvests with advantage, and his rite applies.
- **Blood and gut-water.** The beasts that live out here hold water the way a cactus does. A land predator (scorpion, manticore, spider, dragonnel, purple worm) also yields **Water**: 1d6 from most, 4d6 from a purple worm. It has to be boiled first, which takes a fire and an hour, or it counts as Wash water. Sea beasts from the Wash are too salty to give any.
- **The feast.** Fresh meat, cooked that night, is the best thing that can happen to anyone in the Wastes. A character who eats fresh meat before a sheltered long rest removes **2 levels of exhaustion** instead of 1. The meat keeps for a day, unless it's packed in Bluemaw salt or smoked.
- **The hunt.** Instead of travelling, the party can spend the day **hunting**. One character makes a **DC 15 Wisdom (Survival)** check. On a success, they find tracks and choose a predator from the encounter table to go after (the DM rolls if they fail by 5 or more: something found *them*). It's a fight, so run it as a one-turn or a full fight. It costs a day and hit points, and pays in food, water and the feast.

> [!tip] Pack light, hunt heavy
> Every pound of food the party doesn't carry is a pound of water it can. A party that plans to hunt can leave most of its rations behind and carry 2–3 more days of water, as long as the hunting goes well. That's the gamble the Wastes should tempt them into. If the players are clever about it, let it work. If they're greedy, let a purple worm teach them.
- ***Goodberry*** heals, but gives no nourishment in this campaign.
- **Fresh water** is the real prize. Every reliable source the party finds should feel like gold. They're marked in the location notes of [[4.00 Duty]].

## Ways the Party Can Fight Back

Everything here should feel earned in Kalaman or in the Wastes.

| Source | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Gnomish still** (Guilds) | Turns Wash water into **2 Water a day** while the Wash is high. |
| **Cartographers' map** (Guilds) | Advantage on navigation checks. |
| **Mule or pack sled** (Guilds) | Carries 300 pounds, and a mule drinks 2 Water a day. |
| ***Create food and water*** (Mages, or a caster) | Each casting is **15 Supply** and **30 Water**. |
| ***Decanter of endless water*** (Mages) | Water is solved. Only food matters now. |
| **Clystran** (Heart's Hollow) | Doubles travel distance and skips navigation checks. |
| **Tatina's fargabs** (module) | One pair stays with Darrett at Wrecker's Edge. The party can report in, warn the camp, or arrange a rendezvous from anywhere in the Wastes. |
| **A moon-touched PC and the moons** | The Wash follows the three moons. With a **DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana)** check each morning, the moon-touched PC can predict tomorrow's Wash, which gives advantage on the group check to avoid flash floods. |
| **A ranger PC's woodcraft** | The ranger PC gives advantage on foraging checks to anyone who forages with him. |
| **A knight PC's mare** | Once she finds him again in the Wastes ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)#Level-Up Moments\|level 7]]): she smells fresh water within a mile, once per day. |
| **A tinkerer PC's drone** | Its lantern and scouting make one long rest per week count as sheltered, wherever the party is. |

## Tone

Describe the toll. Cracked lips, salt crusted on armour, the sound of the last waterskin sloshing, someone quietly handing their share to someone else. Let the players make the hard choices: who gets the last drink, whether to turn back, whether to drink the Wash water.
