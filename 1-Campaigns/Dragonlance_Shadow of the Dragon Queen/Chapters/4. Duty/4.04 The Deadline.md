## The Deadline

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. The module's Wastes have no timer, and this one exists to keep the party from waiting out the ship repairs.

Soth let the date slip in the catacombs. Between the Trial and the ships, a spellcaster PC, Wyhan or the Mages can pin it down.

The Dragon Army needs more than the Cataclysm's fire to raise the dead dragons under Onyari. It needs the right night: the **Night of the Eye**, when all three moons stand full together, once every 504 days. The next one is **14 Reapember 351 AC** ([[Calendar]]). That's the night the ritual can be done, and the dead will rise and fly.

**How the party learns it.** In the catacombs, as Soth takes the fire, he tells them, out of contempt:

> "Run home, little knights. Count your moons. When the Eye opens over the Wastes, the dead of the old world will rise and fly, and then you may come and kneel to them."

Afterwards, the date can be pinned down:
- **[[Davos Bloodbane|a moon-touched PC]]** knows at once. He's a lunar sorcerer, and his own trial on the Barb fell on the last Night of the Eye, 504 days before. "The Eye" can only mean one night. He can tell them the date without a roll.
- **Wyhan** or **the Mages** can confirm it as a favour, or for free if Mages Standing is Liked or better.
- **Lohezet's notes**, a captured officer, or Dalamar's research in the Wastes all point to the same night.

**How long.** The party lands at Wrecker's Edge on **23 Fierswelth** (see the Timeline), so the Night of the Eye is **47 days** after the landing. A party that does three or four locations and doesn't dawdle should get there with a week or two to spare. Tell the players the date. The point isn't to catch them out, it's to make the Wastes the only road.

**Why they can't wait it out.** The ships need weeks of work, and the army's food runs out long before that. Sailing home to resupply and coming back would take longer than they have. Whatever happens, someone has to cross the Wastes now.

**If they're late.** It isn't game over. The ritual has begun when they arrive: the plateau shakes, bone dragons stir in the canyon walls, and dead dragons claw out of the ground during Scenario 7 (add a dracolich-touched **young red dragon zombie**, or reflavour an extra dragonnel as a bone dragon, each round from round 3). Chapter 6 opens with the city already trembling. They can still stop it, but it'll cost more.

> [!tip] The countdown
> Keep a running count of **days left** next to the Supply and Water. Mention it every morning in one line: "Thirty-one days to the Eye." Track the moons day by day from the [[Calendar]], and let a moon-touched PC read them out.

## Leaving Kalaman

> [!info] Forces
> **The expedition:** Darrett takes **500** regulars, the Regiment's 10, and the chapterhouse knights at Liked or better, on **8 troopships and 1 supply ship**. Vendri keeps about **1,100** in the city.

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[06-chapter-5-the-northern-wastes#Desperation in Kalaman|ch. 5, "Desperation in Kalaman"]]). The faction bullets are **Homebrew** ([[Faction Rules]]).

After the Trial, run the rest of the module's "Desperation in Kalaman" (the castle library, Darrett's negotiations and Wyhan's message), then settle the factions before the ships leave:

- **The Trial's verdicts** decide how the city sends them off, and adjust the dividends below ([[4.03 The Trial - Verdicts#Every Road Leads to the Wastes]]).
- **Dividends.** The Guilds' new leaders hand over their **send-off** (heirlooms and expedition gear, [[Kalaman Guilds#By Tier]]), and the Mages pay theirs for the Wastes ([[Faction Rules#Dividends]]). Add everything up as **Supply** (food), **Water** and gear.
- **Last favours.** Any faction at Liked or better can be asked for one last favour. Point the players at the "Ways the Party Can Fight Back" table in [[4.99 Survival Rules]]. This is their last chance to buy a still, a map or a mule.
- **Travel light.** The quartermaster at the military docks tells them: the ships are small, the holds are for the troops' food and water, and each passenger brings one pack. Everything else goes into the Bastion, and a little spare gear can ride in Darrett's baggage. Don't hint at the ambush. Let a knight PC decide about his plate ([[4.99 Survival Rules#Travel Light]]).
- **Darrett is knighted (homebrew).** The night before the ships leave, he takes the oath that Becklin kept saying he wasn't ready for ([[Darrett Highwater#Why He's Still a Squire]]). It fits here because the Trial is over, he is about to command **500 soldiers** and needs the rank, and the Knights need something to be proud of after the false knights. He wears Becklin's armour, made to fit him.
  - **If Becklin survived Vogler** (a win), she knights him herself, in the chapterhouse, in her own plate, with her gruff, proud, motherly speech. Her rescue at Wheelwatch ([[3.11 Mission 5 - Wheelwatch Outpost]]) is the reason this happens now.
  - **If she is dead** (a hold or a loss), a **knight PC** dubs him with Becklin's helmet on the altar beside them. This is the knight PC's first act as a senior knight, and Darrett's face when he is told what the armour was for is the scene. If the chapterhouse is Hated after the Trial, it happens quietly at the docks, with no knight-commander to witness it.
  - Either way, he is **Ser Darrett** from now on.
- **Who's on board.** Cudgel (or her sergeant, if she fell at Vogler) and Tatina volunteer as in the module. Tatina gives the party one pair of fargabs and keeps the other with Darrett.
- **A dragonborn PC hears the plan.** Darrett briefs the party on the route and the landing at Wrecker's Edge. Make sure the dragonborn PC is in the room. Takhisis is listening (see [[Wurm (DM)]]).
