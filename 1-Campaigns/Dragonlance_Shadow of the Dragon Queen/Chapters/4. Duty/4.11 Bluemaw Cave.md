## F: Bluemaw Cave

The old blue dragon's lair, the Migan clan, the gricks and the draconians digging out the hoard. Run as in the module.

- **Food and shelter.** Once the draconians are gone, the dwarves feed and shelter the party for as long as they like. Treat the cave like a settlement: rest, top up to **15 Supply**, refill every waterskin from the dwarves' seep in the rock, and no daily rolls while they're here.
- **Grasha's still.** Grasha is a gnomish-engineering enthusiast, and the draconians' gear is full of good brass. Give her two days and she builds a **gnomish still** (see [[4.99 Survival Rules]]), if the party doesn't already have one. Her warning: "It works. Mostly. Don't stand behind it."
- **Bluemaw salt.** The blue crystals preserve meat for months. Butchered beasts give **double Supply** if packed in Bluemaw salt.
- **Tie: a tinkerer PC.** Grasha and the tinkerer PC get on terrifyingly well. A day working on the drone with her gives the tinkerer PC advantage on their next drone tinkering check.
- **Tie: a dragonborn PC.** The draconians here are his kind, digging out a dragon's bones. If any of them is spared, it's the first draconian the dragonborn PC has spoken to in his own tongue since he left the army. Use his level 7 moment.

- **Map:** [[map-5.05-bluemaw-cave-player.png|Map 5.5: Bluemaw Cave]] (module), area F1
- **Terrain:** blue salt crystals (they explode with lightning), cracks in the walls for the gricks, and dim light.

```encounter
name: Grick Pack
creatures:
  - "7": Grick (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Each grick (Survival DC 12): **1 hide** (two make a cloak for hiding in rock), **1d4 tentacles** (dagger, 2 gp, 1 day), **1d6 Supply**. Seven gricks is a week of food.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Yes.

- **Map:** [[map-5.05-bluemaw-cave-player.png|Map 5.5: Bluemaw Cave]] (module), area F3
- **Terrain:** quicksand zones (mark them on the map), treasure sacks (one token each), the open roof above, and the 50-foot cliff the party comes down.

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): The grick alpha's **beak** (greataxe, 30 gp, 3 days: a dragonborn PC's?) and **hide** (cloak, 75 gp, 6 days), and **2d6 Supply**. The sivak's serrated sword and arcane dust. The hoard is in the module.

> [!check] Full fight. **Objective:** the hoard. Each round, the sivak flies one sack of treasure out through the open roof. Stop it early, and the party keeps the boots of levitation and the chalice.
