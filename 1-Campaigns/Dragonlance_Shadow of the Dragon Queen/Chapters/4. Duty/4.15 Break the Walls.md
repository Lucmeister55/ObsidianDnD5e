## Scenario 7: Break the Walls

> [!info] Forces
> **Engaged:** Darrett's **450** against about **1,500** holding the canyon wall. **Losses:** Kalaman 60 / 120 / 220, enemy 400 / 250 / 100 (from the Wastes army, not the Kalaman front).

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[06-chapter-5-the-northern-wastes#K: City of Lost Names|ch. 5, "K: City of Lost Names"]]) and **WoK** scenario 7. The faction payoffs are **Homebrew**.

The assault on the canyon wall, while the party slips through Wind's End. This needs **Darrett's army** to march from Wrecker's Edge (see "The Starving Camp"). If the party never sends word, there's no distraction, and they'll have to find another way in.

Run the module's "The Allied Army" and "Assaulting the Dragon Army". Then run "Attack on Wind's End" and its battlefield events table, or, if the table kept WoK, the bonus battle instead:

> [!wok] **Bonus battle (optional).** WoK scenario 7, which the module offers instead of "Attack on Wind's End" ([[06-chapter-5-the-northern-wastes#Assaulting the Dragon Army|ch. 5]]). **Slot:** the assault on the canyon wall, while the party slips through Wind's End. **Book:** [[09 - Scenario 7 - Break the Walls|transcript]] · **Narration:** [[Battle Lines#Scenario 7: Break the Walls|narration lines]]. Skipping it changes nothing ([[Warriors of Krynn#How We Use It]]).
>
> **The book's setup:** the siege of the City of Lost Names went well until Dragon Army reinforcements arrived. Commander Willem sends the heroes to gather intelligence around the battlefield and find the secret passage into the city (the book's Willem commands the army; at our table it is Darrett). **Win** (the passage is found before the fourth key moment): the title *Infiltrators*. Hold or loss: nothing, and the party finds the loose boards anyway.

**Faction payoffs here:**
- **Kalaman Military** at Liked or better: Darrett holds the line long enough. At Disliked or worse, the attack starts to break after 3 rounds: roll on the battlefield events table twice each round.
- **A Silvanesti PC's exiles**, if Zhelsuel joined: elven mages strike the dragonnels overhead, so ignore battlefield event results of 1–4.
- **Fed or starving** (see "The Starving Camp").

- **Map:** [[map-5.08-attack-on-winds-end-player.png|Map 5.8: Attack on Wind's End]] (module)
- **Terrain:** the dragonnel pens and cage, crates of water and meat, the Fray at the edges, and the loose boards hiding the passage.

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): The officers' ***vicious lances*** (+1d4 fire, ready to use) and broken gear. The dragonnel: **1 hide**, **1d10 teeth** (arrow tips, +1), **2d6 Supply**. The crates are water and meat.

> [!check] Full fight. **Objective:** find the passage behind the loose boards (module), while the battle above rains dragonnels.

The characters reach **8th level** when they find the passage behind the loose boards. Chapter 6 begins in the dark beyond it.
