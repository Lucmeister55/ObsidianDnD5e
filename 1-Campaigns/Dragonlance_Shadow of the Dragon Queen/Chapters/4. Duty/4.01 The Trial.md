## The Trial

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. It's an expansion of the **Module**'s debriefing ([[06-chapter-5-the-northern-wastes#Group Debriefing|ch. 5, "Group Debriefing"]]), where Lord Bakaris is already blaming the party and the city wants someone to blame.

The governor and five guild leaders are dead, the city wants someone to blame, and Lord Bakaris is happy to give it names. The party, found alone among the dead, is held in the ballroom in Vendri's custody ([[3.13 The Raided Catacombs#Held, Not Hanged]]). Two days later comes the **inquiry** in the great hall: what's left of the council, the guilds' new leaders, Vendri, and as many smallfolk as can push in. Its verdicts follow the party to the siege.

### The Charges

Up to three, each brought loudly by Bakaris, who is finished after Steel Springs unless someone else takes the blame. The guilds let him, since it isn't them in the dock.

1. **The Order sent the killers.** The "knights" at the door wore Solamnic armour. The Knights of Solamnia have always wanted Kalaman back. *(Against the Knights.)*
2. **The esquires let it happen.** They were in the castle, they're the Order's friends, they came back alive, and the council didn't. *(Against the party.)* Bakaris brings the **Vogler file** as proof that "they ran before".
3. **The draconian is one of them.** Use this one only if the rumours about a dragonborn PC have spread ([[3.00 Storm Clouds]], "Running Threads"). *(Against the dragonborn PC.)* If it stands, the whole company is accused of harbouring him.

### How the Trial Plays

**Music:** the usual Phoenix Wright cues, per segment, in [[Locations#The Trial]].

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**: a courtroom framework of testimony, cross-examination and evidence.

The inquiry is chaired by **Fuline Thren**, the oldest surviving councillor (the module's future governor). Fair, frightened, and running out of patience. Bakaris and the new guild leaders accuse, but the case is argued by a polished **advocate** nobody remembers hiring: **Caradoc**, in a guild lawyer's body ([[4.02 The Trial - Testimony#The Prosecutor: Caradoc in Disguise]]). The party **represents itself**, rotating who takes each charge.

Each charge runs the same way:
1. **Testimony.** A few lines, read one at a time or handed out on cards.
2. **Cross-examination.** **Ask** about a line (a good question gets detail and often a hint, or an Insight check if stuck), or **point out** what doesn't fit: evidence, a witness, or something a character knows for certain.
3. **Closing.** If the charge is still undecided, one character closes against the advocate (see "The Closing" below).

**No magic settles the truth.** No clerics, no *zone of truth* or *detect thoughts*, and nobody in Kalaman trusts a spell. The only caster in the hall is **Davos** (Wyhan testifies but never casts). A spell spotted in the hall (DC 15 Perception for the guards and gallery, DC 18 for a subtle one) is **tampering**, and **an immediate failure**:
- **The charge on the table stands at once.** No closing, no ★ break.
- The Mages take **-1**, and the advocate seizes on it: "Sorcery in the hall of justice."
- If it happens on the esquires' charge, or happens twice, the prosecution paints Davos as **one of the culprits** and that charge stands as well. Without magic, Davos still has Insight, the card tables and his council seat.

**What decides it.** There's no score. A charge falls when the party has **broken the lie at its heart**, marked ★ in each testimony, or has shown the hall enough smaller cracks that nobody believes it any more. Faction witnesses help, since the hall already trusts them. Let a clever argument win even if it isn't the one written here.

**Three clocks,** as props on the table, not in the vault:
- **Thren's patience** (6 segments). One per accusation that falls flat, insult or shout. When full, Thren stops the hearing: the charge stands, and if it's the party's, they're **arrested** (below).
- **The gallery** (a dial: *Hostile, Wary, Torn, Warm, Behind you*). It starts at **Torn**. A good question or a well-timed witness moves it one step towards the party, a clumsy accusation or a slip left alone moves it away. At **Warm or better**, Thren's patience doesn't advance on the first miss in each charge. At **Hostile**, every charge starts with one patience segment already filled. Hidden marks move the starting position.
- **The slips** (3 marks, below). Each time the advocate lets something slip and the party catches it, mark one.

**The court record.** Print each evidence item below as a card. To point something out, a player **presents a card** to Thren and says what it shows. A card can be presented once per charge (unless it breaks the ★ lie, which it can always do), and the advocate can challenge it. A card the party never got isn't on the table, and a hidden mark can take one away.

> [!tip] Run it as a scene, not a procedure
> A packed, loud hall: let the crowd heckle and Bakaris play to the gallery. Run the Knights' charge first, so the party warms up, with corridor recesses between charges for Vendri, Darrett or a nervous witness. Save the advocate for last, and when Caradoc is exposed, describe the hall going silent and let it land.

**Hidden marks** from Storm Clouds ([[Faction Rules#Hidden Consequences]]): a snubbed Spokesperson won't speak, a story Bakaris kept makes the hall hostile, an owed favour brings a witness. Don't announce them.

### The Closing

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, borrowing from the idea of Burning Wheel's *Duel of Wits*: both sides commit to a move in secret, then reveal together.

Use this when a charge is still undecided after the testimony. The advocate and the character taking the charge close against each other in up to **three exchanges**, each with a secret move on a card:
- **Evidence** (point to the record). Beats Procedure.
- **Plea** (to the gallery's heart). Beats Evidence.
- **Procedure** (to Thren's authority and the rules of the hall). Beats Plea.

Reveal together. The winner of an exchange moves the gallery **one step** their way, and **two** if they won with a card on the record (Evidence) or a faction witness (Plea). A tie changes nothing. **Win two exchanges** to carry the closing. A broken ★ lie wins the first exchange automatically. The advocate plays to the gallery; Caradoc knows what the hall wants to hear and plays Plea more often than he should.

**What they can bring.** Remind the players what they came out of Storm Clouds with. Only what they actually have counts.
