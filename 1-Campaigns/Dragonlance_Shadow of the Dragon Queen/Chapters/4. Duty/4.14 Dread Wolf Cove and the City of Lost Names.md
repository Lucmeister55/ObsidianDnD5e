## J: Dread Wolf Cove

The fog-bound cove, the wolf-shaped anhkolox, and the Shard of Darkness. Dalamar asks the party to escort him here after Wakenreth.

- **Food.** The cove's rocks are thick with mussels: foraging here **automatically succeeds**.
- **Tie: a Silvanesti PC.** The shard is a piece of a destroyed Orb of Dragonkind, perhaps even the one the Silvanesti PC carried out of Silvanost. When he comes near it, he hears the voice again: patient, loving, his father's voice. He knows exactly what it is before anyone casts *detect magic*. Dalamar wants it. The Silvanesti PC knows better than anyone what it does to the people who keep it. Who walks away with it is one of the best scenes in the chapter.

- **Map:** a sea cave. [[1878331-Underground_Ambush-Grid(70).jpg|Underground ambush]] (companion map, probably) works.
- **Terrain:** fog over the whole map (lightly obscured), tide pools, and the shard at the back of the cave.

```encounter
name: Dread Wolf
creatures:
  - "1": Anhkolox (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): The anhkolox: **bone spikes**, **claws** and **flesh strips** (as Wakenreth).

> [!check] Full fight, but the real **objective** is the shard: whoever reaches the back of the cave first decides whether Dalamar ever gets his hands on it.

## K: City of Lost Names

The plateau of the Giant's Spine, the Dragon Army camp in the canyon, and Wind's End. Run "The Occupied City" as in the module.
- **So this is why.** The size of the dig is the first real answer to Vendri's question from Storm Clouds: the Dragon Army was never waiting for more soldiers, it was waiting for this. Let Darrett be the one to say it, when he sees it.

- **Water and food.** Wind's End is stacked with crates of water and dried meat for the dragonnels: once taken, Supply and Water stop mattering for the rest of the chapter.
- **The army's rations.** Whatever the party takes from the Dragon Army camp in the canyon after Scenario 7 feeds Darrett's soldiers for the trip home.
- **Tie: a tinkerer PC.** The drone's guidance snaps into focus once the party finds the passage (see [[Milo Geartickle (DM)]]).
- **Tie: a knight PC.** Somewhere in the city ahead is the Dread Knight. The knight PC's shield grows cold to the touch the closer he gets.
