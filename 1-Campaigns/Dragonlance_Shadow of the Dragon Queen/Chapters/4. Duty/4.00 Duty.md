## Overview

The party follows the Dragon Army into the Northern Wastes, the Cataclysm's open wound, to learn what Lord Soth, Lohezet and Belephaion want with the City of Lost Names. The fleet is ambushed in the fog on the crossing, the supply ship goes down, and the army limps into Wrecker's Edge with a few days' food. Darrett stays to mend the ships. The party goes on alone, with only what they carried. From there it's a race: the army starves while they hunt for the city, and every location below is a danger, a clue and a chance to eat and drink for another week.

> [!abstract] Running this chapter
> - **Characters:** "a knight PC", "a Silvanesti PC" and so on name the kind of character a thread suits best, not a person. See [[DM Guide#Roles, Not Names]] for who carries each one right now.
> - **Survival rules:** [[4.99 Survival Rules]]. Run the daily routine every day, and keep the Supply tight.
> - **Levels:** the party arrives at **6th level**. They reach **7th** after three locations (use each character's level 7 moment from their DM notes), and **8th** when they find the passage at Wind's End.
> - **Module:** chapter 5, "The Northern Wastes". Map 5.1 shows every location, and the player map is in appendix E.
> - **The army stays behind.** Darrett's troops are stuck at Wrecker's Edge mending the ships until "The Starving Camp" below. Ignore the module's "Army in Tow" section until they march.
