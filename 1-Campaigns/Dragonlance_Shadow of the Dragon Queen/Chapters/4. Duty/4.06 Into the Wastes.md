## The Starving Camp

> [!info] Forces
> **Attrition:** a fed camp loses about **10** (sickness, accidents). A starving one loses about **60**, to sickness and desertion. Expect about **450** at Wrecker's Edge.

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. It replaces the module's "Kalaman Troops" section until the army marches.

On the beach, Darrett does the sums out loud, and they're bad: a few days' rations for a few hundred soldiers, ships that need weeks of work, and black sails patrolling the bay. He stays at Wrecker's Edge with the soldiers and the engineers to mend the fleet, with Cudgel rationing the water with an axe. He can't spare the party a single ration, and he tells them so. What he needs is for them to find what the Dragon Army is doing out here, and to do it fast.

> [!warning] The clock
> The army runs out of food **10 days** after landing. After that, it loses soldiers every day, and the camp gets uglier: desertions, fights over the water, a soldier hanged for theft. Mark the days. Tatina's fargabs let Darrett report how it's going, and he's honest.

**Sending food back.** Anything the party finds and doesn't need can go back to the coast: carried there, sent with Heart's Hollow scouts, or brought by Clystran. The camp has a brackish spring, so it's short of food, not water.
- For every **20 Supply** sent, the army holds out **5 more days**, and **+1 Kalaman Military** (at most +2 in total).
- The richest sources are Camp Carrionclay's storehouse and Heart's Hollow's goodwill. A shipment from Carrionclay is the moment the camp cheers the party's name.
- If the party sends **nothing** and the clock runs out, the army fights Scenario 7 half-starved: every Kalaman soldier stat block has 1 level of exhaustion, and Darrett asks the party to their faces where the food went.

**The march.** When the party finds the City of Lost Names, they bring Darrett word (or call him on the fargabs), and the army marches to meet them, leaving the engineers to finish the ships. Heart's Hollow scouts guiding the army (module) let it move at normal pace. From then on, use the module's army camp rules, but every camp is a hungry one.

> [!example] Skirmish option: raid on the beached fleet
> *Source: **Homebrew** ([[Skirmish Battles]]).*
>
> Once in the chapter, ideally when the party comes back to the coast with food, a Dragon Army force finds the beached ships (thanks to the Leak, below).
> - **Objective:** keep the ships from burning for **5 rounds**. Each enemy unit that ends its turn touching a ship sets it alight, and every burning ship adds a week to the repairs.
> - **Allies:** 2 **Infantry** (hungry, so -2 Morale if the camp has run out of food), 1 **Siege Crew** (the engineers have turned a ship's ballista round), and Cudgel's **Mercenaries**.
> - **Enemies:** 2 **Baaz**, 1 **Kapaks** gliding down the cliffs, and 1 **Dragonnel Riders** with fire pots.
> - **The beach:** units in the surf are on **bad footing**.
> - **Map:** a blank board, or the cove from Wrecker's Edge.
> - **Terrain:** 3 beached hulls (the sail ship map cut up, or long card hulls), surf along one edge, the cliff along the other, a ship's ballista, and fire markers.

## The Leak

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew** ([[Wurm (DM)]]).

Takhisis hears through a dragonborn PC until the dragonborn PC's story says otherwise. Whenever the party makes a plan in front of him, she can use it **once per location**: the Dragon Army patrol is waiting where they planned to camp, the draconians at Bluemaw have been warned, or Akhviri arrives at Carrionclay early. Don't overdo it. Two or three times in the chapter is enough for the players to start looking at each other.
