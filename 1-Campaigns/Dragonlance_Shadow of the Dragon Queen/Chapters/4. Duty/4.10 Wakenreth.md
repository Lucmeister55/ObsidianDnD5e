## E: Wakenreth

The House of Silence, the tower that fell off Onyari, half sunk in the Shadowfell. Run as in the module, then meet Dalamar here as he directs.

- **Shelter.** Once the portal is closed, Wakenreth is the safest place in the Wastes: cool by day, warm by night, and lit. Long rests here count as sheltered, and the restored tower grants advantage on the Constitution save against exhaustion. There's no water.
- **Tie: a spellcaster PC.** The second crypt in E2 holds a dead elf in the **white robes of a Mage of High Sorcery**, the second set the spellcaster PC has found by stealing. Whether he takes them is up to him.
- **Tie: [[Milo Geartickle|a tinkerer PC]].** Wakenreth came from Onyari. The drone goes mad with lights the moment it sees the tower, and calms only at the top, pointing north. This is the first sign that home is close.
- **Tie: a Silvanesti PC.** Silvanesti tradition demands a silver coin on the guardian's pedestal. The Silvanesti PC knows it without being told, and he's the only one who does.

- **Map:** [[map-5.04-wakenreth-player.png|Map 5.4: Wakenreth]] (module), area E2
- **Terrain:** rubble from the broken ceiling, 2 stone crypts, empty burial niches, and the hole up to E3.

```encounter
name: Crypt Wraiths
creatures:
  - "2": Wraith (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Each wraith (Religion DC 15): **1 spirit cloth** (a **bag of holding**, 200 gp, 14 days, but only once they're back in Kalaman: nobody in the Wastes can make it), **phantom dust** (frighten one creature), **ectoplasm** (see the Ethereal Plane) and **shadow flux** (a 15-ft cloud of darkness). Wakenreth's dead might object.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Yes. The point of this floor is the spirit and her sister, not the wraiths.

- **Map:** [[map-5.04-wakenreth-player.png|Map 5.4: Wakenreth]] (module), area E4, and a second, empty board for E5 (a black tower top over a colourless waste)
- **Terrain:** the portal arch with rotating runes, floating wall fragments, and the identical arch on the E5 side.

```encounter
name: The Shadowfell
creatures:
  - "1": Anhkolox (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): The anhkolox (Religion DC 15): **2d10 bone spikes** (arrow tips, +1d6 necrotic: a ranger PC's), **1d4 claws** (necrotic dagger, 150 gp, 9 days), and **2d4 flesh strips** (advantage against charm, fear and poison for an hour).

> [!check] Full fight. **Objective:** turn the runes in E4 while the anhkolox hunts the rest of the party in E5. The rune-turner needs 3 rounds of uninterrupted actions (Veriel calls out the order). Close the portal, and the anhkolox is cut off and dragged back into the Shadowfell.
