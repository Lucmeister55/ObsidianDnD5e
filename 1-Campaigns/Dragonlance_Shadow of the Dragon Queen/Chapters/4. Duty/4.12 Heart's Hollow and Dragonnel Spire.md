## G: Heart's Hollow

The hidden crater village, Clystran and Ness. It's the one real town in the Wastes. Run as in the module, and start with Clystran and the giant spiders as the Wash rises.

- **Resupply.** Everything is scarce. End's Odds sells Supply and Water at **5 gp each** (a cutthroat price, and End knows it), and the town will gift up to **10 Supply** and all the Water they can carry to anyone who has earned a stone in the Mosaic. The crater's deep well is the only sweet water for fifty miles. The Fort can spare a mule for a favour.
- **Lavender's** tinctures: a draught that lets someone ignore one level of exhaustion for a day (25 gp, and it costs 1 extra Water that night).
- **Clystran** as a guide doubles travel distance and skips navigation (see [[4.99 Survival Rules]]), once the party has fed his dragonnels at H.
- **Tie: a kender PC.** This is where the kender PC spent those lost months. Let the memories come back in a rush:
  - Halfway along the **Mosaic** there's a stone painted with a crooked little drone. The kender PC put it there.
  - **End Pike** has very large, patient hands. He taught the kender PC forgework, and he greets them by name: "Took you long enough, you little thief."
  - **Ness** found the kender PC half dead in the canyons years ago, and it was her who took the memories, gently, to keep the Hollow's secret. The kender PC saw her true shape once. She is the bronze dragon. Whether she admits it depends on the egg from Carrionclay.
  - This is also the moment to reveal who the kender PC promised never to return alone (see [[Milo Geartickle (DM)]]).

- **Map:** a canyon. [[1878331-edited_cliff.jpg|Cliff]] (companion map, probably) works.
- **Terrain:** the canyon walls, Clystran's rope hanging from the rim, and water-level markers (the Wash rises 10 feet on initiative 10 and 0).

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Each giant spider (Survival DC 11): **1 hide** (leather armour, 50 gp, 2 days) and **2d6 Supply**.

> [!check] Short full fight. **Objective:** save Clystran. He's on a rope above the rising Wash, and the spiders are climbing after him. If he falls, he's swept away and turns up a day later, half-drowned and indignant. Saving him on the spot makes him a friend for life.

## H: Dragonnel Spire

Twelve wasteland dragonnels on a rock shaped like a dragon. Run as in the module, with Clystran.

- **Water.** A rain pool in the dragon rock's "eye", far above the canyon floor, filled by the rare storms: **2d6 Water**, once, if someone climbs for it (DC 15 Athletics, and the dragonnels watch).
- **Payoff.** Clystran becomes the party's guide.
- **Tie: a dragonborn PC.** The dragonnels won't go near anyone but Clystran, except the dragonborn PC. They come right up to him and sniff, then back away, unsettled, as if they can't decide what he is.
