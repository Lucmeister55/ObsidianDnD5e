## D: Sunward Fortress

Sirrion's toppled lighthouse, the Spawning Shard, Virruza and the slaadi. Run as in the module.

- **Food.** The abandoned camp holds dried meat: **10 Supply**. There's no water. The D3 crates hold rope, oil and lanterns.
- **Shelter.** The D1 altar's flame, once lit with a prayer, warms the chamber all night: a long rest there counts as **sheltered** on a bitter cold night, if anyone dares sleep with the slaadi below.
- **Rone and Yearkal.** Healing Rone and walking him home earns his guidance to Heart's Hollow. Yearkal reunites with the sea elves at C.
- **Tie: [[Wurm|a dragonborn PC]].** Virruza's notes aren't only about slaadi. Belephaion's orders mention "the egg-work": draconians are made from **the eggs of good dragons**, corrupted. One sloppy line near the end reads "The Queen's first is still out there. The silver one." Whoever reads the notes aloud should be in the room with the dragonborn PC.
- **Tie: [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge|a knight PC]].** Rone is dying, a stranger who's no use to anyone. Carrying him home is the most knightly thing in this whole chapter.

- **Map:** [[map-5.03-sunward-fortress-player.png|Map 5.3: Sunward Fortress]] (module), area D3
- **Terrain:** cramped halls (tight spaces), digging crates and the barricaded door to D4.

```encounter
name: Dracophage Subjects
creatures:
  - "4": Kapak Draconian (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Gear only (kapak acid and daggers). Don't touch them without gloves.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Yes. But anyone who takes a hit in the description is infected with a slaad egg (module). Let that be the cost of a careless description.

- **Map:** [[map-5.03-sunward-fortress-player.png|Map 5.3: Sunward Fortress]] (module), area D5
- **Terrain:** the 30-foot chasm with 2 wooden footbridges (fire markers for when they burn), the orange Spawning Shard on the far side, and crates.

```encounter
name: Spire Depths
creatures:
  - "1": Red Slaad (XMM)
  - "5": Slaad Tadpole (XMM)
  - "1": Green Slaad (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): The red slaad: **slaad claws** (dagger, 2 gp, 1 day), **1d6 vials of slaad mesh** (advantage on saves against magic for 10 minutes, once treated). Tadpoles: 1 vial each. **Virruza** (green slaad): **shapeshifter blood** as well. All Arcana DC 15+.

> [!check] Full fight. **Objective:** get across the burning bridges to the Spawning Shard and bury it, or get out with Rone alive. Killing everything is the hard way to do either.

- **Map:** the abandoned Dragon Army camp around the Sunward Fortress (no map needed; one-turn it)
- **Terrain:** a dozen ragged tents, crates, the earthen ramp and the toppled spire.

```encounter

  - "2": Red Slaad (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Two red slaadi: claws and **slaad mesh**.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Yes, if it happens at all.
