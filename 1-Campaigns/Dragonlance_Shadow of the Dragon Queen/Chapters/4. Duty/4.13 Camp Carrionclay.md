## I: Camp Carrionclay

The Dragon Army's waypoint on the muddy lake, Fewmaster Zirrok, the prisoners and Akhviri. Run as in the module. If the party is beaten by a patrol, this is where they wake up.

- **The storehouse.** It's a supply camp, the best-stocked place in the Wastes: **40 Supply** in crates, and **40 Water** in barrels along the walls. Stealing it is a heist, and carrying it out is a problem.
- **As a heist.** The storehouse and the cages both work as an [[Infiltrations|infiltration]]: the camp is ready for raiders (-1d) unless they come in disguise, and the watchtower is the Alarm.
- **The lake** is salt, and full of quippers when the Wash is high.
- **Prisoners.** The three sea elves (for Ishvern), Kennah (who takes the party home to Heart's Hollow), and a bulette nobody should free.
- **The bronze dragon egg**, meant for Belephaion.
- **Tie: a dragonborn PC.** After Virruza's notes, the dragonborn PC knows exactly what Belephaion wants with the egg: another one like him. Saving it should feel like saving himself.
- **Tie: a tinkerer PC.** Use the tinkerer PC's level 7 moment: they carry the egg, it's warm, and it ticks in time with the drone. Give it to Ness for the bronze dragon scale mail, and Ness's secret.

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Gear only. If the bulette dies: **1d3 + 1 bulette feet** (two make *boots of striding and springing*, 200 gp, 14 days), the **plated hide** makes plate (1,500 gp, 5 days), and **2d6 Supply**.

> [!check] Full fight. **Objective:** get the cages open before the horn sounds. Each round of noise risks the watchtower spotting them.

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.
> **The idol.** The army raised it when it built the camp: a five-headed dragon of lashed timber, mud and black lake-stone, daubed red, white, blue, green and black, ten feet tall, in the middle of the yard. Every Dragon Army camp of any size builds one. The camp prays to it at dusk, and the bronze egg sits on the altar at its feet, waiting for Belephaion.
> - **Draconic Devotion.** The draconians and Dragon Army soldiers treat the idol as a Dragon. Any of them who can see it **have advantage on attack rolls**. Put it centre stage, so that almost everyone can.
> - **Bringing it down:** AC 17, 40 HP, immune to poison and psychic. Or two characters next to it can topple it with a DC 18 Strength (Athletics) check, one action each, together.
> - **The catch.** It falls with a crash the watchtower can't miss: the horn sounds at once. And anyone who topples it towards the altar smashes the egg, unless someone has already taken it (DC 12 Dexterity to snatch it as it goes).
> - **When it falls,** every draconian in sight takes a moment to see it, and loses its reaction until its next turn.
>
> So the party has three things to do and not much time: open the cages quietly, get the egg, and decide whether the idol is worth the noise.

> [!danger] Bloodied: Akhviri
> *At half HP. Tell the players. Source: **Companion** ([[Bloodied and Bruised]]).*
> - **Recharge:** when first Bloodied, Acid Breath recharges and she regains all legendary actions.
> - **Cornered:** when first Bloodied, a Claw attack as a reaction.
> - **Acidic Hide:** touching her or hitting her unarmed costs 4 (1d8) acid.
> - **Fling:** after a bite hits a Medium or smaller creature, swap a Claw to throw it 30 feet, prone. 1d6 bludgeoning per 10 feet if it hits something; a creature it hits makes DC 17 Dexterity or takes the same and falls prone.

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): If Akhviri somehow dies: an **acid gland** (grenade), claws, fangs, hide, **2d8 scales**, teeth, wings, **black dragon lung tissue** (amphibious for 8 hours), and **4d6 Supply**.

> [!warning] **Survive.** Don't let this become a fight. The objective is to get out of the camp with the egg and the prisoners while Akhviri breathes once and roars.
