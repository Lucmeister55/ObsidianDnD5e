## I: Camp Carrionclay

The Dragon Army's waypoint on the muddy lake, Fewmaster Zirrok, the prisoners and Akhviri. Run as in the module. If the party is beaten by a patrol, this is where they wake up.

- **The storehouse.** It's a supply camp, the best-stocked place in the Wastes: **40 Supply** in crates, and **40 Water** in barrels along the walls. Stealing it is a heist, and carrying it out is a problem.
- **As a heist.** The storehouse and the cages both work as an [[Infiltrations|infiltration]]: security Guarded (DC 15), and the watchtower is the Alarm.
- **The lake** is salt, and full of quippers when the Wash is high.
- **Prisoners.** The three sea elves (for Ishvern), Kennah (who takes the party home to Heart's Hollow), and a bulette nobody should free.
- **The bronze dragon egg**, meant for Belephaion.
- **Tie: a dragonborn PC.** After Virruza's notes, the dragonborn PC knows exactly what Belephaion wants with the egg: another one like him. Saving it should feel like saving himself.
- **Tie: a tinkerer PC.** Use the tinkerer PC's level 7 moment: they carry the egg, it's warm, and it ticks in time with the drone. Give it to Ness for the bronze dragon scale mail, and Ness's secret.

- **Map:** [[map-5.07-camp-carrionclay-player.png|Map 5.7: Camp Carrionclay]] (module), areas I1 and I2
- **Terrain:** the wooden bridge over the marsh, the gate in the driftwood palisade, and the watchtower in view.

```encounter

creatures:
  - "1": Sivak Draconian (DSotDQ)
  - "2": Baaz Draconian (DSotDQ)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): Gear only.

> [!tip] **One-turn?** Yes, or avoid it entirely with disguises (module). The real objectives here are the **prisoners** and the **egg**, before Akhviri arrives.

- **Map:** [[map-5.07-camp-carrionclay-player.png|Map 5.7: Camp Carrionclay]] (module)
- **Terrain:** the bridge over the lake, the gate, 4 cages (one with the bulette), the watchtower, Zirrok's hut, and bone spurs along the palisade.

> [!check] **Objective:** get the cages open before the horn sounds. Each round of noise risks the watchtower spotting them.

- **Terrain:** the watchtower Akhviri lands on, the escape routes (the bridge, the lake), and the acid breath's line marked out before she uses it.

```encounter

  - "1": Adult Black Dragon (XMM)
```

**Harvest** ([[Harvesting and Crafting]]): If Akhviri somehow dies: an **acid gland** (grenade), claws, fangs, hide, **2d8 scales**, teeth, wings, **black dragon lung tissue** (amphibious for 8 hours), and **4d6 Supply**.

> [!warning] **Survive.** Don't let this become a fight. The objective is to get out of the camp with the egg and the prisoners while Akhviri breathes once and roars.
