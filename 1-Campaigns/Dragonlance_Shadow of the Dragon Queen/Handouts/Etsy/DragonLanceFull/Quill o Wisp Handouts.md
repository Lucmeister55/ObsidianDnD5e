# Quill o Wisp Handouts

> [!info] Source: **Dragonlance Handouts** by Quill o Wisp (Etsy). 40 flyers in `digital/` (one PNG each, for the screen), plus print sheets: [[DragonLanceFull-compressed.pdf]] (colour), [[DragonLance_BW.pdf]] (printer-friendly) and [[DragonLanceBlackBoard.pdf]] (white-on-black versions of the Heart's Hollow board). [[DragonLance_README.pdf]] is the seller's guide. The 166 MB `DragonLanceFull.pdf` is git-ignored: it has the same pages as the compressed one.

Hand them over at the moment listed, and let the players keep them. **Rumour flyers** also make good "one useful rumour" payouts from the Smallfolk ([[Kalaman Smallfolk#By Band]]).
