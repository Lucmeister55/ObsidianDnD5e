# Marshal Nestra Vendri

Commander of Kalaman's military and the most practical person in the city. She has spent her career fighting Estwilde raiders and Taman Busuk ogres, and she is very good at it, but she has never seen anything like the Dragon Army. She's lawful, stern and allergic to glory, and she cares about one thing above all, which is bringing her soldiers home alive. She's the party's commanding officer for all of [[3. Storm Clouds]], and she'll treat them exactly as they deserve.

## Voice

Clipped, dry and patient, never a word wasted. She asks one question at a time and waits for the answer. *(Suggestion: Gwendoline Christie as Brienne, but older and wearier.)*

## Look

Tall and weathered, close-cropped grey hair, a scar through one eyebrow, and plain armour in Kalaman's blue and gold. Her office is all maps, reports and cold tea.

## Wants

Kalaman held, good intelligence, and soldiers who don't die for noblemen's glory. She also wants Lord Bakaris kept as far from her army as possible.

## Secret

She knows Kalaman can't hold against a real Dragon Army assault, not without help it doesn't have. She hasn't told the council. She's counting on the party more than she lets them see.

## Ties

- **The party:** her esquires, special operatives on a lieutenant's rank and 5 gp a week (module, chapter 4).
- **[[Darrett Highwater]]:** her liaison with the party, and a young officer she trusts.
- **[[Wurm]]:** she makes it known that Wurm serves under her esquires, and that anyone who lays a hand on him answers to her. That protection lasts as long as the party's Military Standing stays Neutral or better (see [[3. Storm Clouds - Timeline]]).
- **[[Bakaris Uth Estide|Lord Bakaris]]:** she despises him, and he goes over her head at Steel Springs.
- **Jesen Thold:** her old friend who runs the Steady Beacon.
- **[[Kalaman Military]]:** she *is* the faction's voice.

## Under Pressure

She gets quieter and colder, and makes the hard call. She'll sacrifice the outpost to save the army, and the army to save the city. She respects anyone who disobeys her for a good reason, and never says so out loud.

## Stats

Knight (lawful neutral, human), per the module.
