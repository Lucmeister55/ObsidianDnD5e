# Darrett Highwater

Becklin's squire, and Edric's friend from their squire days. His parents abandoned him in Vogler as a boy, the village raised him, and Becklin took him under her wing. He's fastidious, well-read and painfully earnest. His mind works faster than his mouth, and he apologises every time he catches himself saying something he thinks isn't knightly enough. He's the party's link to Kalaman's military, and he wants more than anything to deserve the Order.

## Voice

Quick, eager and polite, tripping over himself, apologising, then starting again. *(Suggestion: Tom Holland.)*

## Look

Young, tidy, armour polished more often than it needs to be, always carrying a book or a map. Later he carries Becklin's gift: Solamnic plate made to fit him exactly.

## Wants

To become a Knight of Solamnia worthy of Becklin, to keep Vogler's people safe, and to never let anyone down.

## Secret

None, really. He hides how afraid he is that he isn't good enough. If Becklin dies at Vogler, he hides how much he's grieving too.

## Ties

- **[[Becklin Uth Viharin]]:** his mentor.
- **[[Ser Edric of Brackenridge|Edric]]:** his oldest friend, and his rival for the Order's notice. They're the same age, and they want the same thing.
- **[[Wurm]]:** the natural person to teach him to fight instead of beating him (see Wurm's level 5 moment).
- **Lord Bakaris:** he's forced to serve under him at Steel Springs, and it nearly kills him.

## Under Pressure

He follows orders, even bad ones, and hates himself for it. He'll send a desperate note to the party before he'll openly disobey (module, Steel Springs).
