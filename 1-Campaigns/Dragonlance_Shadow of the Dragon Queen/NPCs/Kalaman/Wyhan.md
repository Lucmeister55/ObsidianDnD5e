# Wyhan

A black-robed Mage of High Sorcery who has been the Order's representative in Kalaman for over a decade, in her late 30s. Officially she's retired and runs a little apothecary selling good-luck charms. In the back room she keeps esoteric components, and at least one thing that breathes. She despises ignorance above all else, and she has failed dozens of candidates for lacking drive, and dozens more for not fearing magic enough.

## Voice

Dry, unhurried and faintly bored, and she never repeats herself. Every question is a test. *(Suggestion: Tilda Swinton.)*

## Look

Raven hair, and a dark dress with feathered, winglike sleeves. She stands behind a counter heaped with open books, in a shop that smells of liquorice and pepper.

## Wants

- To serve the Order, and to judge who's fit to join it.
- Privately, for the Mages to rule Kalaman one day. She thinks its people are sheep who need a shepherd, and she hopes to bring enough mages round to a quiet coup against the council (companion).

## Secret

- **The coup.** Nobody knows, though Antimodes may suspect.
- **The crate.** See [[3.10 Downtime 5]].
- **She knows about the prophecy** around a spellcaster PC, and reports on him to Wayreth ([[Davos Bloodbane (DM)]]).

## Ties

- **[[Davos Bloodbane|A spellcaster PC]]:** her charge, and the Order's observer on the council, because she refuses to sit in a room with fishmongers. She sends him instructions, and he doesn't always follow them.
- **[[Antimodes]] and Rovina:** the White and Red Robes who send her their strays.
- **[[Nestra Vendri]]:** a cool working respect.
- **[[Mages of High Sorcery]]:** she *is* the faction, in Kalaman.

## Under Pressure

She doesn't raise her voice. She closes the shop, and whoever crossed her finds that the Mages have stopped answering letters. If she has to fight, she does it once, and very thoroughly.
