# Governor Calof Miat

The governor of Kalaman, appointed by the five great guilds, and a decent, tired, careful man who wants the war to be somebody else's problem. He speaks for the city's people in council, and he's the one who signs off on Bakaris's attack at Steel Springs while Vendri is away. He dies in the council massacre, with Caradoc's sword in him.

## Voice

Polite, soothing and slightly too quiet, always trying to find the sentence that will calm the room. *(Suggestion: Jim Broadbent.)*

## Look

Good robes worn a little thin, ink on his fingers, and the look of a man who hasn't slept properly since Vogler.

## Wants

Calm, trade, and for the guilds to stop shouting. He'd rather believe Bakaris than believe in dragons.

## Secret

He knows he isn't equal to this, and he's relieved every time Vendri overrules him.

## Ties

- **[[Nestra Vendri]]:** he trusts her more than he dares say in front of the guilds.
- **[[The Guild Council]]:** they appointed him, and they can remove him.
- **[[Bakaris Uth Estide|Lord Bakaris]]:** he takes his word, and it costs the city half its army.
- **[[Fuline Thren]]:** his successor.

## Under Pressure

He adjourns.

## Stats

Noble (module).
