# The Guild Council

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[3-Shadow_of_War.pdf]], Part 1). Voices are **Homebrew**.

The five guild leaders who sit on Kalaman's council beside the governor ([[Kalaman Guilds]]). The party will see them at every council briefing. Soth's massacre in the council chamber kills most of them, so whoever you've made the players hate may not live to pay for it.

## Alexandra Nightvane (Cartographers)

Human, mid-50s, lawful neutral. She inherited the guild from her mother, and she's a shrewd merchant and Kalaman's unofficial ambassador.
- **Voice:** gracious and exact, like someone pricing you as she speaks.
- **Wants:** to know everything. She offers to be the party's patron if they tell her all Vendri says.
- **Secret:** she sells maps to whoever pays, and that may include the enemy ([[3.08 Downtime 4]]).

## Reynold "Rey" Costwyn (Fishers)

Human, late 30s, neutral. A carefree playboy to the council, and popular with the working class.
- **Voice:** always a little drunk, always the funniest person in the room.
- **Wants:** a good time, and for nobody to mention the gods. He's scornful of anyone who prays: if they've come back, why should anyone worship them, after what they did?
- **Secret:** he's terrified of water. His family is said to be cursed by Zeboim.

## Uri Stonegrey (Masons)

Dwarf, looks in his late 80s, neutral. He claims to have seen the Cataclysm himself, and nobody alive can say otherwise.
- **Voice:** slow, patient and pleasant, like a very old cat.
- **Wants:** his schemes, some of them decades long, to pay off.
- **Secret:** he runs a private spy network, built over two centuries of favours and blackmail. He knows about the Vogler file.

## Sigmund Pross (Shipwrights)

Human, 60s, lawful evil. Nicknamed "the Barge" for his size.
- **Voice:** loud and certain, and he interrupts.
- **Wants:** Vendri gone. He brings up removing her at least once a meeting. He adopts Lord Bakaris as an ally the moment Vogler's people arrive.
- **Secret:** he's selling places on his ships to wealthy families, in case the walls fall ([[3.06 Downtime 3]]).
- **Backup:** he takes over Bakaris's plot if Bakaris dies early ([[Bakaris Uth Estide#If He Dies Early]]).

## Oflyn Vinhollow (Dockhands)

Dwarf, looks in his 50s, neutral evil. He oversees all the trade through the harbour.
- **Voice:** tired and reasonable, a man worn out by committees.
- **Wants:** things done. He's given up on the council, and on Kalaman.
- **Secret:** he runs the **Firefishers** ([[Kalaman Guilds]]). He has decided the city can't be saved, he works against Vendri, and he means to help surrender it for a reward. It doesn't save him when Soth comes.
