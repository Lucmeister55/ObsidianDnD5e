# Fuline Thren

The oldest surviving councillor after Soth's massacre. She chairs the inquiry at the Trial, and she's the module's future governor. She's fair, frightened and exhausted, and she's holding a city together with her bare hands.

## Voice

Dry, old and precise, with long pauses. She doesn't repeat a warning. *(Suggestion: Maggie Smith.)*

## Look

Small and upright, in mourning black, with a walking stick she taps once for silence.

## Wants

The truth about the massacre, if the city can survive hearing it. Failing that, a verdict the city can live with.

## Secret

She doesn't know whom to trust, and she's afraid the next person she looks at might be wearing someone else's body.

## Ties

- **[[Nestra Vendri]]:** they need each other, and neither says so.
- **[[Calof Miat]]:** the man whose chair she's in.
- **[[Bakaris Uth Estide|Lord Bakaris]]:** she lets him bring his charges, and she doesn't like him.
- **The party:** she judges them at [[4.01 The Trial]]. Her patience is the mood of the hall.

## Under Pressure

She stops the hearing. Once she's decided, she doesn't change her mind in public.

## Stats

Noble (module).
