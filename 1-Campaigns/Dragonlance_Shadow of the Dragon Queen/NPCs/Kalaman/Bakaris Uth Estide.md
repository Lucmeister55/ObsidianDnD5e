# Lord Bakaris Uth Estide (the Elder)

A minor Solamnic lord who has lived his whole life on stories of his family's honour, none of which are his own. His ancestors were Knights of Solamnia. He is a coward full of bluster, and he has decided the war is his chance at glory. In this campaign he was also a lord of Draevenfall in Estwilde until his son's duel got the family exiled. He owns [[Wurm]], by contract, and treats him like an ox.

## Voice

Booming, red-faced and pompous, every sentence a speech. *(Brian Blessed: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w9Kaxd0vhrg)*

## Look

A heavy man gone soft in the saddle, red in the face, wrapped in furs too warm for the season, with a ring on every finger that will take one.

## Wants

Glory, credit for things other people did, a place on Kalaman's council, and his family's name made great again.

## Secret

- **Money.** The family is running short since the exile, so he'd sell Wurm's contract for the right price, if his son didn't know.
- **Courage.** He's terrified of actual battle.

## Ties

- **[[Bakaris the Younger]]:** his son and heir, the only person he's afraid to disappoint.
- **[[Wurm]]:** his property.
- **[[Ser Edric of Brackenridge|Edric]]** and **[[Becklin Uth Viharin|Becklin]]:** the knights he needled all the way to Vogler.
- **Kalaman's council:** whom he lies to about leading Vogler's survivors (module, chapter 4). When the party contradicts him, he charges them with desertion, theft and conspiracy against Vogler, and the charges become the file that binds them to Vendri ([[DM Guide#Why They Stay Together]]).

## Under Pressure

He blusters and then folds, or backs down the moment someone stands up to him (DC 14 Intimidation in the module). He leads Kalaman's troops to disaster at Steel Springs, and blames everyone else for it.

## If He Dies Early

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. A loose plan, in case the party decides the simplest way to deal with the file is to deal with the man who started it.

**They're suspected, whatever happens.** The motive is in Vendri's desk. However clean the job, the whole city's first thought is the esquires. If they plan it, run it as an [[Infiltrations|infiltration]]: the Bakaris house by night is Lax (DC 10). A clean job (the Alarm never passes 3) leaves suspicion but no proof.

| Faction | If there's proof | If there's only suspicion |
|---|---|---|
| [[Kalaman Guilds]] | -3. They backed him. | -1 |
| [[Kalaman Military]] | -2. Vendri adds a murder charge to the file, and won't burn it this act. | -1. Vendri asks them once, to their face, and remembers the answer. |
| [[Knights of Solamnia]] | -2. A knight PC's friends murder lords in their beds. | No change |
| [[Kalaman Smallfolk]] | +1, quietly | +1, quietly |

**With proof, they're arrested.** Use [[4.03 The Trial - Verdicts#If They Are Arrested]] early: a faction gets them out, or the old man does. After that, they're fugitives in their own city until they earn their way back.

**Who takes over his parts:**

| His part | Who takes it |
|---|---|
| **The charges and the file** | [[Bakaris the Younger]], now Lord Bakaris, presses them harder, and adds his father's murder. |
| **A dragonborn PC's contract** | Passes to the son. If the son is gone too, the guilds auction the estate, and **Oflyn Vinhollow** buys the contract. |
| **The war council and Steel Springs** ([[3.10 Downtime 5]], [[3.12 Steel Springs]]) | **Sigmund Pross** of the Shipwrights, who already wanted the all-out attack. He leads the army out for the glory, and Steel Springs happens anyway. |
| **The Grim Room** ([[3.13 The Raided Catacombs]]) | Pross sits in the corner instead, and the army wants him hanged. |
| **The Trial** ([[4.01 The Trial]]) | Pross brings the charges, since the son is with the enemy by then. Add the murder if they're suspected. |
| **The Warrior's Gate** ([[6.04 Day of a Thousand Fires]]) | His six household knights open it, for their dead lord's son, carrying the son's letter. |
| **Bakaris the Younger's arc** | Sharper. Kalaman killed his father, so when he kneels to Kansaldi at the Inkwater, he means it. |

**If they kill him at the Warrior's Gate** in the siege, that's the module's fight, and nobody minds.
