# Tatina Rookledust

A rock gnome inventor who left Mount Nevermind decades ago and lives alone, twelve miles south of Kalaman down Esker Brook, so her experiments don't bother the neighbours. She talks at great speed, thinks faster, and is always halfway through designing something overcomplicated. She's also the reason the Dragon Army has a boilerdrak, and she'd like very much to make up for that. After the party rescues her, she becomes Vendri's chief of tech development.

## Voice

Very fast and breathless, with no gaps between ideas, and sentences that turn into other sentences halfway through. She forgets names constantly. *(Suggestion: Christopher Lloyd's Doc Brown, a little higher.)*

## Look

Tiny, wiry, grey hair escaping in all directions, singed eyebrows, and a leather apron whose pockets clink. Her spells come out of tools and tiny devices she carries everywhere.

## Wants

- To make things better, faster and more interesting.
- To never build another weapon, which lasts until the walls are under attack.
- To teach. She's a kender PC's old teacher ([[Milo Geartickle (DM)]]).

## Secret

- **She built the boilerdrak.** It was a "weed-clearing device", modified for a tough-looking woman with red hair. That was **[[Red Ruin]]**, who later ordered a long-distance talking device too. Tatina doesn't dwell on the past, but it bothers her more than she shows.
- **Her clockwork servant** goes wrong about half the time.

## Ties

- **[[Milo Geartickle|A kender PC]]:** her old student, and she's delighted and appalled about it in equal measure.
- **[[Nestra Vendri]]:** her employer, if she goes to the castle. The Shipwrights or the party's Bastion may get her instead ([[3.03 Mission 1 - Rookledust Rescue]]).
- **Than:** her assistant, from Vogler (module).
- **[[Red Ruin]]:** her worst customer.

## In the Campaign

- **Mission 1:** the workshop, the goblins and the fargabs.
- **Steel Springs:** she's the panicked voice on the fargab ([[3.12 Steel Springs]]).
- **The beacon** goes dark in Downtime 5, and she can fix the lenses ([[3.10 Downtime 5]]).
- **The siege:** she and Than turn a dead catapult into a **gnomeflinger** on the southwest wall ([[Kalaman]]).

## Under Pressure

She talks faster. She'll fix anything put in front of her, even in the middle of a battle, and she never notices the danger until it's explained to her twice.
