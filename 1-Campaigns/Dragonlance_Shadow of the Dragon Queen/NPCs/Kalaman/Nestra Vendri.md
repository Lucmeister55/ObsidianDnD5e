# Marshal Nestra Vendri

Commander of Kalaman's military and the most practical person in the city. She has spent her career fighting Estwilde raiders and Taman Busuk ogres, and she is very good at it, but she has never seen anything like the Dragon Army. She's lawful, stern and allergic to glory, and she cares about one thing above all, which is bringing her soldiers home alive. She's the party's commanding officer for all of [[3.00 Storm Clouds]], and she'll treat them exactly as they deserve.

## Voice

Clipped, dry and patient, never a word wasted. She asks one question at a time and waits for the answer. *(Suggestion: Gwendoline Christie as Brienne, but older and wearier.)*

## Look

Tall and weathered, close-cropped grey hair, a scar through one eyebrow, and plain armour in Kalaman's blue and gold. Her office is all maps, reports and cold tea.

## Wants

Kalaman held, good intelligence, and soldiers who don't die for noblemen's glory. She also wants Lord Bakaris kept as far from her army as possible.

## Family

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. The module gives her no family.

The Vendris were Knights of Solamnia. In the Time of Darkness her great-grandparents hung up their spurs and stayed in Nightlund as plain soldiers. **It's a secret:** in this city, knightly blood would cost her the council and the smallfolk within a week.
- **Jesen Thold** knows, and would never say.
- **A barracks rumour** that her family "used to wear the Crown" comes and goes. She lets it die.
- **Darrett** or a knight PC may work it out from how she talks about the Measure without naming it.

She keeps a small carved platinum dragon, Paladine's old sign, in a locked drawer. She was raised on the stories and outgrew them.

If it comes out (Bakaris digging, a sivak in her office), she loses the council's trust, and Bakaris uses it at [[4.01 The Trial|the Trial]] to tie her to the Order's "killers".

## Secret

She knows Kalaman can't hold against a real Dragon Army assault, not without help it doesn't have. She hasn't told the council. She's counting on the party more than she lets them see.

**Why she risks Wurm.** She's heard about the "draconian" who died at High Hill and stood up to fight for the villagers. In his vision the dragon was grey, like silver through smoke, and in her family's stories Paladine came as a **platinum dragon**. A thin thread, and she knows it. But there hasn't been a true cleric since the Cataclysm. She doesn't dare hope, and keeps him close.

> [!warning] DM only: she's reading it backwards
> A god who raises the dead, speaks in a mortal's head and moves his hand in battle is a god **acting directly in the world**, and only one god can do that: Takhisis, who came back first while the others are barred ([[Wurm (DM)]], [[The Cataclysm and the Gods]]). The real Paladine leaves only small signs, in true silver. The stronger the "proof" it's Paladine, the stronger the proof it isn't. Let the party work it out late, and let it hurt Vendri most.

## Ties

- **The party:** her esquires, special operatives on a lieutenant's rank and 5 gp a week (module, chapter 4). In truth they're her suicide squad: accused of desertion, theft and conspiracy against Vogler by Lord Bakaris, and sworn to her as one company to escape the noose. She keeps the charges in a file on her desk ([[DM Guide#Why They Stay Together]]). Her reasons: she needs fighters she can trust, and she has a feeling she can't explain that the dragonborn is the first sign of the gods coming back.
- **[[Darrett Highwater]]:** her liaison with the party, trusted faster than anyone. He's what the Vendris were before they hung up their spurs, and on his first day he told her to her face that a lord was lying. Within weeks he has a hundred recruits to train. She tells herself it's because he's useful.
- **[[Wurm]]:** she makes it known that Wurm serves under her esquires, and that anyone who lays a hand on him answers to her. That protection lasts as long as the party's Military Standing stays Neutral or better (see [[3.00 Storm Clouds]]).
- **[[Bakaris Uth Estide|Lord Bakaris]]:** she despises him, and he goes over her head at Steel Springs.
- **Jesen Thold:** her old friend who runs the Steady Beacon.
- **[[Kalaman Military]]:** she *is* the faction's voice.

## Under Pressure

She gets quieter and colder, and makes the hard call. She'll sacrifice the outpost to save the army, and the army to save the city. She respects anyone who disobeys her for a good reason, and never says so out loud.

## Her Fate

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.

Vendri may fall on the walls in the siege. If she does, and the city holds, Kalaman needs a new Grand Marshal, which could be Edric. See "Who Falls in the Siege" in [[6.00 Conquest]].

## Stats

Knight (lawful neutral, human), per the module.
