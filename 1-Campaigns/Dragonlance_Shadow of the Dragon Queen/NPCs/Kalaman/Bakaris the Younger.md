# Bakaris the Younger

Lord Bakaris's son, and much more dangerous than his father. He's lean, dark and handsome, and knows it. Where his father blusters, he smiles. He killed another lord's son in a duel in Draevenfall and got his family exiled, and he has never once been sorry. He treats [[Wurm]] as entertainment, and carries the contract that owns him in his coat so he can wave it at people. In the module he goes over to the Dragon Army in the end, as one of its officers in the Siege of Kalaman.

## Voice

Silky, sneering and amused by everyone. *(Draco Malfoy, from the films.)*

## Look

Lean, dark-haired and handsome, in a riding coat better cut than anything the party owns, with a fine ebony crossbow across his back, oiled and never scratched. A crossbow does the aiming for you, and he has never had the patience for a bow.

## Wants

Power, admiration, to be feared, and to be on the winning side, whichever side that turns out to be.

## Secret

- **The duel.** He provoked it on purpose.
- **His father.** He despises his father, and is waiting for the old man to die or fail.
- **Contact.** He's already been approached by the Dragon Army, which pays well and doesn't care about his reputation.

## In Kalaman

He's the party's running nuisance all through Storm Clouds, and he uses the guilds, who admire his family's name, as his weapon. How far he gets depends on the party's Guilds Standing. At worst he gets them boycotted, spreads rumours they're Dragon Army sympathisers, and makes a formal claim on Wurm before the council. See "Running Threads" in [[3.00 Storm Clouds]].

## Ties

- **[[Bakaris Uth Estide]]:** his father.
- **[[Wurm]]:** his property and his favourite victim, and he keeps the contract on him.
- **The Dragon Army:** his eventual employer.

## Under Pressure

He smiles and backs away, then strikes later, from behind. He's a coward who knows how to hide it.
