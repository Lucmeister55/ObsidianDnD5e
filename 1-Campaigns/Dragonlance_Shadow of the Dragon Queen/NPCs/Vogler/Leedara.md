# Leedara

A blue-skinned, sombre Silvanesti woman at the edge of Ispin's funeral reception. She says she was a performer who knew him. She's politely evasive, and nobody in Vogler knows anything else about her. In truth she's a ghost: once an elven priest and one of Isolde's attendants, who helped turn Soth back from his quest and has been cursed for it ever since. For three and a half centuries she has made sure he never finds peace. Now the Queen has called him away, and she has followed him.

## Voice

Soft and sad, with an elven formality, and she stops mid-sentence as if listening to something else. *(Suggestion: Cate Blanchett's Galadriel, tired.)*

## Look

Pale blue skin, a mourning veil, and clothes a few centuries out of fashion. Nobody quite remembers seeing her eat. In her true form, she's a ghost in a burned priest's robe.

## Wants

To end Soth, and through him, her own curse.

## Secret

- **She's dead.** She reveals it only late ([[08-chapter-7-siege-of-kalaman|module, ch. 7]]).
- **She lied to Soth once,** about Isolde, and it helped bring on the Cataclysm.
- **She knows what the violet fire can't do.** The fire only remembers, so it can never give Soth back his wife ([[Lord Soth#Why He Serves the Queen]]).

## Ties

- **[[Lord Soth]]:** her torment, and she's his.
- **[[Ispin Greenshield]]:** she came to his funeral because he was the last Greenshield.
- **[[Dargaard Keep]]:** where she has spent the centuries.

## Under Pressure

If she's attacked or disbelieved, she vanishes, and reappears later to help anyway.
