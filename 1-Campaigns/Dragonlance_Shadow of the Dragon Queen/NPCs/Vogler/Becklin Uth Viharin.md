# Becklin Uth Viharin

A Knight of the Crown in her later years, and castellan of the ruined Thornwall Keep above Vogler for the last decade, a posting that is half duty and half quiet retirement. She adventured with Ispin and Cudgel when they were young. She's warm and patient, fiercely protective of Vogler and of her squire Darrett, and happiest of all with a trowel in her hand, digging through the ruins of Thornwall and telling anyone who'll listen that Vogler was an island before the Cataclysm.

## Voice

Calm, kind, a little dry, with a teacher's habit of turning everything into a history lesson. *(Suggestion: Olivia Colman.)*

## Look

Silver hair in a plain braid that's always coming loose, a lined face quick to smile, and well-kept old plate covered in Solamnic knotwork. Her horned helm usually hangs from her saddle. She has a round shield that has been dented and beaten straight a hundred times.

## Wants

To give Ispin the send-off he deserved, to find out what really killed him, and to see Darrett knighted.

## Secret

- **The Greenshields.** She is the only person Ispin told the truth about his family to, so she knows the story of the Dread Knight is real ("You know the story. Not the truth").
- **Her suspicions.** She suspected Ispin's wounds were too long and targeted for a beast, and wrote to Edric about it.
- **The box in Thornwall Keep** holds a suit of armour made for Darrett (module, chapter 4).

## Ties

- **[[Ispin Greenshield]]:** old friend, and the reason she's here.
- **[[Darrett Highwater]]:** her squire, like a son.
- **[[Ser Edric of Brackenridge|Edric]]:** her ally on the road to Vogler, and Ispin's squire.
- **[[Cudgel Ironsmile]]:** old friend, and commander of the mercenaries at the Battle of Vogler.

## Under Pressure

Steady as a wall. She'll hold the line so others can get away, and her last thought is always for Darrett. She may die at the Battle of Vogler, depending on how it goes (see [[2.00 Broken Silence]]).
