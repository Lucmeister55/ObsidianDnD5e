# Cudgel Ironsmile

**Ridomir "Cudgel" Ironsmile**, a hill dwarf from Kayolin, south of Solamnia, and captain of the **Ironclad Regiment**, a small mercenary company with a reputation for beating the odds. She spent most of her life on the road with Ispin and Becklin, which is where she earned the name. She's loud, warm and practical, she loves a good plan and a better tavern, and she grins at everything, especially trouble. Her people would follow her anywhere, and at Vogler most of them do.

## Voice

Big, booming and cheerful, always the loudest voice in the room, because "the loudest voice usually wins the argument". She calls everyone "lad" or "lass" regardless. *(Suggestion: Brian Blessed, as a dwarf woman.)*

## Look

Short, broad and solid as a door, with copper braids bound in iron rings and the grin Ispin's stories made famous. She carries the iron-shod club that earned her the name, and she's never far from a tankard.

## Wants

- **In Vogler:** to give Ispin a proper send-off, to win tomorrow's reenactment of High Hill, and to be paid by Raven for training the militia.
- **In Kalaman:** pay for the ten of her people who are left, and a fight worth the ones who aren't.

## Secret

- **She was betrayed twice, and only knows about once.** Her lieutenant **Gragonis** sold the regiment out at High Hill, and she blames herself for not seeing it. Her other lieutenant, [[Jeyev Veldrews|Jeyev]], is a Dragon Army spy too, and she has no idea. When the truth comes out, it will break her.
- **The Ironclad Regiment is nearly gone.** She led it to the pass at Vogler with Becklin, and came back with ten.

## Ties

- **[[Ispin Greenshield]]:** her old friend, and half the stories about him are really about her.
- **[[Becklin Uth Viharin]]:** her oldest friend. They held the pass at Vogler together.
- **[[Raven Uth Vogler]]:** hired her to train Vogler's militia.
- **[[Jeyev Veldrews]]:** her lieutenant, whom she trusts completely.
- **Gragonis:** her other lieutenant, who betrayed her at High Hill.
- **[[Nestra Vendri]]:** they don't get on. Vendri hates mercenaries, and Cudgel hates being told what to do. Vendri still knows she can't afford to lose her.
- **The party:** she met them at the funeral, and a knight PC knows her from Ispin's road. After the Missing Scouts mission she owes them her life.

## In the Campaign

- **Vogler** ([[1.00 An Ordinary Day]], [[2.00 Broken Silence]]): the funeral, the reenactment, Gragonis's betrayal at High Hill, and the stand at the pass with Becklin.
- **Back from the wilds** ([[3.09 Mission 4 - Missing Scouts]]): she and the last ten of the regiment run into the party, dead on their feet. Then there's the fight over their pay ([[3.10 Downtime 5]]).
- **Wheelwatch** ([[3.11 Mission 5 - Wheelwatch Outpost]]): she commands the ten soldiers who go with the party.
- **The Wastes** ([[4.04 The Deadline]]): her ten sail with Darrett. In skirmishes, they're the **Mercenaries** unit ([[Skirmish Battles]]).
- **The end** ([[6.07 Kalaman Victorious]]): she buries Jeyev's name, whatever happened to him.

## Under Pressure

She gets louder, and then she makes a plan, and the plan is usually good. She never leaves her people behind, and she takes every loss personally. Her weakness is comfort: she can be slow to leave a warm inn, and slow to believe the worst of a friend.
