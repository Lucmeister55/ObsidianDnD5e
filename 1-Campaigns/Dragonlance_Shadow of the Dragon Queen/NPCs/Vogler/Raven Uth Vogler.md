# Raven Uth Vogler

Vogler's mayor: a practical, warm, river-born woman in her thirties who runs the village the way she'd run a boat, by doing whatever needs doing herself, first. She lives in a two-storey house on the village circle and keeps two guest rooms, lavishly decorated with mounted fish, for any villager going through a hard time. She's the one who blows the whistle to start the Battle of High Hill reenactment every year. She **dies at the end of the Vogler arc**, killed by [[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]] while holding the line so her people can get onto the boats (see the companion PDF and [[2.00 Broken Silence]]).

## Voice

Brisk, friendly and blunt, with a fisherwoman's turn of phrase. She laughs easily and swears freely. *(Suggestion: Jodie Whittaker.)*

## Look

Weathered, freckled, sleeves always rolled up, hair tied back with a length of net cord, and a whistle on a string round her neck.

## Wants

A good festival, Ispin's funeral done properly, and her people safe. After the betrayal at High Hill, that last one is all she wants.

## Ties

- **The villagers of Vogler** ([[Vogler Refugees]]): her people, every one of them.
- **[[Becklin Uth Viharin|Becklin]]** and **[[Cudgel Ironsmile]]:** old friends and defenders of the village.
- **[[Bakaris Uth Estide|The Bakarises]]:** she can't stand them, and they undermine her at every turn.
- **[[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]:** her killer.

## Her Death

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], "A Tragic Loss").

Make it count. Play her warm and capable all through Vogler, so the players like her. On the last boat, Kansaldi's dragon snatches her (perhaps after she shoves a character out of its path) and burns her on the end of Vogler's Crossing, in sight of everyone. Her whistle ends up in the party's hands, or in the river. Once she's gone, Vogler's people have **no leader** in Kalaman. Who rises to speak for them becomes one of the party's first choices in [[3.00 Storm Clouds]].

The companion adds that "Uth Vogler" is a title, not a family name, inherited by the village's appointed leader. Her father was mayor before her, killed by a sahuagin while fishing, and the village let her take over at sixteen "for now". Nobody ever got round to replacing her.
