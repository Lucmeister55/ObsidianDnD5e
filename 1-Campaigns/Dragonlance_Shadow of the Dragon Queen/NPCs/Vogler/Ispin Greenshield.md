# Ispin Greenshield

The man whose funeral starts the campaign. To everyone else Ispin was a cheerful, eccentric hedge knight who told outrageous stories, called every stranger "old friend" and couldn't cook to save his life. The Order thought he was a joke in armour. [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge|Edric]], who spent fourteen years at his side, knew he was the most dangerous man in any fight, and that he played the fool so people would forget to be afraid of him.

He was also the last of the Greenshields, a family descended from the one knight who refused Lord Soth on the night of the Cataclysm, and he carried that family's shield all his life.

## Voice

Warm, rambling and always halfway into a story, laughing at his own jokes before he reaches them. He sings badly. *(Suggestion: Brendan Gleeson.)*

## Look

Weathered and grinning, with laugh lines everywhere, and travelling clothes that have seen too many roads. Always carrying **Old Moss**, a round, mossy-green shield carved with a scarred broadleaf tree, which he talks to.

## Wants (in life)

To be a good knight his own way, to see his old friends again, and to keep Edric well away from his family's legend.

## Secret

- **His family.** He knew the truth about the Greenshields and Soth, and told only Becklin.
- **The black rose.** After the black rose riders ambushed him and Edric, he went to Vogler to consult Becklin, and Soth had him killed.
- **The shield.** His spirit is in Old Moss now.
- **His body.** Soth raised it as a death knight.
- Full detail is in [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]].

## Ties

- **[[Ser Edric of Brackenridge|Edric]]:** his squire, and the closest thing he had to a son.
- **[[Becklin Uth Viharin]]** and **[[Cudgel Ironsmile]]:** his old adventuring companions.
- **[[Lord Soth]]:** his family's ancient enemy.
- **[[Fizban|The befuddled old man]]:** Ispin once shared a fire with an old man who vanished before dawn (one of the module's funeral stories). That's the same old man who mentors [[Wurm]].

## Stories to Tell About Him

The module's funeral reception has players share Ispin's tall tales. Have Edric's player start with one from his backstory:
- the minotaur pirates on the Blood Sea;
- the gnome's weasel launched from Mount Nevermind;
- the Lord of Palanthas's poodles;
- the unicorn who gave him his shield.
