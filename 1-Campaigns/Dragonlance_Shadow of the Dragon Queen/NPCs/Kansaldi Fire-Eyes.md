# Kansaldi Fire-Eyes

Dragon Highmaster of the Red Dragon Army, the woman Highlord Verminaard left in charge of the invasion of the Kalaman region, and a zealot even by the Dragon Army's standards. Verminaard brought her into the Dragon Queen's faith, and the faith ate everything else. To prove her devotion she tore out her own left eye with her fingers and pushed a red gem into the socket, and she still talks about it as the happiest day of her life. Her own soldiers are more afraid of her than of the enemy. She **kills [[Raven Uth Vogler|Raven]]** on the docks at the end of the Vogler arc, and she makes a sermon of it.

## Voice

Unhinged and joyful. She laughs in the middle of sentences, sings hymns while she fights, and swings from a whisper to a scream without warning. She calls everyone "child", even her enemies, and especially the ones she's about to kill. She talks to the Dragon Queen out loud, mid-conversation, as if Takhisis were standing behind her, and sometimes she stops to listen to the answer. *(Suggestion: Bellatrix Lestrange preaching a revival.)*

## Look

A cleric in scorched black-and-red Dragon Army plate. One wild human eye, and one red gem that blazes like a coal when she prays. Her armour is scratched all over with prayers, and her hands are always blistered, because she holds them in her own holy fire to stay pure.

## Wants

Kalaman burned clean in the Dragon Queen's name, the City of Lost Names claimed as a weapon (module), and the whole world kneeling. She doesn't want to conquer people. She wants to save them, and she believes fire is the only way.

## Secret

- **Red Ruin.** She raised [[Red Ruin]] from childhood and shaped her into the weapon she is. She loves the girl in her own way, and would sacrifice her in an instant if the Queen asked.
- **The deal.** She passed on [[Lord Soth]]'s order to kill [[Ispin Greenshield|Ispin]] without asking why. The Queen allows the Dread Knight, so she allows him too.
- **She hears things.** Something answers when she talks to the Dragon Queen. Takhisis really does whisper to her, and it's slowly driving her mad.

## Raven's Death

At the docks, as the last boats leave, Kansaldi finds Raven holding the line. Have her stop the fighting around her with a raised hand, and offer Raven forgiveness, tenderly, in front of everyone: "Kneel, child, and it can all stop." When Raven spits at her, Kansaldi weeps, kisses her forehead, and burns her alive. Then she turns to the party, still crying, and thanks them for watching.

## Ties

- **[[Red Ruin]]:** her ward, her weapon and her proudest work.
- **[[Lohezet]]:** an ally she thinks is a coward and an unbeliever.
- **[[Lord Soth]]:** the Queen's servant, and the one thing in the world she's quietly afraid of.
- **The Dragon Queen:** her god, her mother and her voice in the dark.

## Under Pressure

She never retreats from fear, only on the Queen's word. Wounding her makes her ecstatic ("Yes! Test me!"). If something shakes her faith, she doesn't break. She doubles down, and becomes far more dangerous.
