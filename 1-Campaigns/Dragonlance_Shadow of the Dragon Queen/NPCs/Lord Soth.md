# Lord Soth

To the world he isn't a person at all, just a legend: **the Dread Knight**, a shadow over the black ruin of Dargaard Keep, the curse of some ancient evil that some say was once a Knight of Solamnia. In truth he's Lord Loren Soth, once the perfect Knight of the Rose. The gods gave him the chance to stop the Cataclysm, and his pride let it happen. He burned with his keep and rose as the most powerful death knight on Krynn. For three and a half centuries he brooded in Dargaard. Now the Dragon Queen has called, and he has ridden out again.

## Voice

Very slow, very quiet, and utterly certain, like someone who has had three hundred years to choose every word. He never raises his voice. *(Suggestion: Charles Dance as Tywin Lannister, but colder.)*

## Look

A huge knight in ancient, fire-blackened Solamnic plate, a rose withered black on his breastplate, and two points of orange flame where his eyes should be. He smells of old smoke. Wherever he rides, the air grows cold and the shadows lean toward him.

## Wants

- **In the module:** to reach the City of Lost Names, for reasons of his own, using the Dragon Army as long as it's useful to him.
- **For this campaign:** to end the Greenshield line for good, and to destroy Ispin's shield, the last symbol of the one knight who defied him.

## Secret

- **Ispin's murder.** He gave the order, and Red Ruin carried it out. He blames her openly, to toy with Edric.
- **The hollow Ispin.** He raised Ispin's stolen body as a death knight of his retinue, as a mockery of the man.
- **His lies about the Greenshields.** He tells Edric the first Greenshield was a coward who opened the gates in a panic, and that Ispin was a fool cut from the same cloth.
- Full detail is in [[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]].

## Ties

- **[[Ser Edric of Brackenridge|Edric]]:** "Greenshield's boy". Soth sees himself in the young knight who clings to the Measure, and wants to watch him fall.
- **[[Ispin Greenshield]]:** the last of the line he swore to end.
- **[[Red Ruin]]:** his dagger, through his alliance with [[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]].
- **Caradoc:** his charming, cowardly undead seneschal, who does his talking (module, chapter 4).
- **[[Soth's Host]]:** the undead of Nightlund who rise for him: fell bats, barrow guard, barrow knights, vargheists and blood knights.

## Under Pressure

He doesn't get angry, and he doesn't hurry. When he's losing, he simply leaves, and makes it clear that he'll be back when it suits him. He's most dangerous when he's being courteous.
