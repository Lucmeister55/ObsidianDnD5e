# Ness

The unofficial leader of Heart's Hollow, the one real town in the Northern Wastes, a hidden crater village of outcasts and survivors. She holds court at a table in the Salt Lick that everyone jokingly calls "the Mayor's Office". She's kind, unhurried and very hard to surprise. Her true name is **Nezrah**, and she is a **bronze dragon**.

## Voice

Warm and low, with a storyteller's timing, and long patient silences. *(Suggestion: Helen Mirren.)*

## Look

A middle-aged human woman, as far as anyone knows, broad and weathered, with salt in her hair, who sits very still and always seems to be enjoying a private joke.

## Wants

To keep the Hollow safe and hidden. She came to the Wastes over a hundred years ago looking for clutches of good dragon eggs, she never found any, and she has never stopped hoping.

## Secret

- **She's a bronze dragon**, and the metallic dragons have sworn to stay out of the war for their eggs' sake.
- **She took a kender PC's memories.** She found them half dead in the canyons years ago and gently took the memories, to keep the Hollow's secret. They saw her true shape once ([[Milo Geartickle (DM)]]).
- **The egg at Camp Carrionclay** is bronze. Whether she admits what she is depends on whether the party brings it to her ([[4.13 Camp Carrionclay]]).

## Ties

- **[[Milo Geartickle|A kender PC]]:** a child of the Hollow, in a way. She has been waiting for them to come back.
- **[[Clystran]]:** the boy she watches over.
- **[[Belephaion]]:** the egg was meant for him.

## Under Pressure

She gets calmer. She won't show her true shape to save herself, but she might to save the Hollow, and that breaks an oath with consequences.

## Stats

[[adult-bronze-dragon-xmm|Adult Bronze Dragon]], magically disguised. She's **Nezrah** in the module, and no one knows what she is.
