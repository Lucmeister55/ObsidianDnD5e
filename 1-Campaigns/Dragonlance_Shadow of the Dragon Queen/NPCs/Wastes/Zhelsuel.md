# Zhelsuel

A once-respected elder of House Mystic, venerable even by elven standards, who leads a dozen Silvanesti mages camped on a plateau in the Northern Wastes. He knows the expedition is a fool's errand and a polite exile, and he believes his homeland is lost to him. So he has decided to spend his remaining days cataloguing every rock in the Wastes, as exactly as possible. He was also the teacher of a young prince of the Caladon line.

## Voice

Precise, courteous and very tired, with the habits of a teacher who still corrects your grammar. *(Suggestion: Derek Jacobi.)*

## Look

Very old for an elf, white-haired and thin, in once-fine robes neatly mended. He's always holding a measuring cord and a notebook.

## Wants

To finish his catalogue, and to die with his dignity. Underneath that, to be told he didn't fail.

## Secret

He sat up through long nights of study with a boy he thought of as a nephew, and he believes that boy is dead. He doesn't recognise the scarred thing standing in front of him, at least not at first.

## Ties

- **[[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn)|A Silvanesti PC]]:** his old student. If he's revealed, Zhelsuel breaks, the camp's doors open, and a dozen mages may join Scenario 7 ([[4.07 Wrecker's Edge and the Elven Encampment]]).
- **[[Dalamar]]:** his brilliant, insubordinate junior.
- **[[Davos Bloodbane|A sorcerer PC]]:** a human with untrained magic. He says what he thinks of that.
- **Darrett's army:** the exiles fight for Kalaman in the siege, if they're won over ([[6.02 Return to Kalaman]]).

## Under Pressure

He goes very still and very polite. He won't risk his people for a human war unless someone persuades him it's Silvanesti's war too.
