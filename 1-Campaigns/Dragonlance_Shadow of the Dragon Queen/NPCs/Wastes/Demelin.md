# Demelin

A red-robed high wizard of Onyari, a Silvanesti elf hundreds of years old, and one of a handful old enough to have lived through the Cataclysm. She has stayed in the ruins of the City of Lost Names ever since, in an apartment of old lamps and stuffed chairs, wearing a bone mask shaped like a skull. She's the last person who remembers what the city was for.

## Voice

Dry, ancient and unsentimental. She talks like a lecturer who has given up on the class but won't leave. "Here's your opportunity to learn from my mistakes." *(Suggestion: Judi Dench.)*

## Look

A skull-like bone mask and heavy red robes, which are a *cape of the mountebank*. Without the mask, she's very old, and very tired.

## Wants

For Onyari to stay buried. "Onyari is my tomb. We must both stay buried." She won't lead the fight. Her time is over, and it's up to the people of the new world to avoid the sins of the past.

## Secret

- **The rings.** She recognises the kender PC's ring at a glance, as one of the Kingpriest's death-cheating rings forged in her own city. It can only be unmade at the forge in **M8**, and unmaking it releases every life it ever took.
- **The Orbs.** She knows they were Istar's work too, and what they do to the people who use them.
- **She knows a Caladon face** under the scars, and says nothing unless he speaks first.

## Ties

- **[[Milo Geartickle|A kender PC]]:** the ring.
- **[[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn)|A Silvanesti PC]]:** the last of a house she remembers.
- **[[Davos Bloodbane|A spellcaster PC]]:** she offers him the Test ([[5.01 Path of Memories]]), and she's the one who administers it.

## Under Pressure

She tests the party with an air elemental before she speaks to them, and she calls it off when someone does something clever. She has outlived everything, and very little frightens her.
