# Clystran

A young scout of Heart's Hollow who has spent his whole life in the Northern Wastes and considers himself the leading expert on them. He knows every hollow and rock formation around High Hunt, and the local names for everything: Wormgut, the Giant's Spine. He's also the one who comes back in the siege with the dragonnels.

## Voice

Eager, talkative and a bit too confident, full of local knowledge nobody asked for. "I know all there is to know about the Northern Wastes, except where the dragons live." *(Suggestion: Tom Holland.)*

## Look

Sunburnt and lanky, in patched leathers with too many ropes on him, and a coppery wasteland dragonnel who treats him like a slightly stupid sibling.

## Wants

To be the best explorer in the Wastes. Both the Wastes and Heart's Hollow are his home, and he's loyal to both.

## Secret

He has no idea what **Ness** really is. Nobody in the Hollow does.

## Ties

- **[[Ness]]:** the Hollow's "mayor".
- **[[Milo Geartickle|A kender PC]]:** he remembers a kender who spent months in the Hollow.
- **[[Wurm|A dragonborn PC]]:** the dragonnels won't go near anyone but Clystran, except him. They sniff him and back away, unsettled ([[4.12 Heart's Hollow and Dragonnel Spire]]).

## In the Campaign

- **The first meeting:** the party finds him on a rope above the rising Wash, with giant spiders coming for him.
- **The siege:** he brings a flight of wasteland dragonnels to Hawker's Grove, which is how the party reaches the citadel ([[6.05 Siege of Kalaman]]).

## Under Pressure

He keeps talking, and he takes one risk too many. He's brave, and he hasn't yet learned the difference between that and lucky.
