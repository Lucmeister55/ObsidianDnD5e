# Dalamar

An ambitious young Silvanesti mage who fought in the doomed defence of his homeland. What he did there was desperate enough to get him sent to the Northern Wastes with Zhelsuel's exiles. He has an insatiable thirst for knowledge, and no patience at all for conservative elves. Dragonlance fans know where he's heading. Here, he's the party's way into the chapter, and possibly the most dangerous friend they make.

## Voice

Smooth, amused and always negotiating. "Everybody wants something. I'm certain we can help one another." *(Suggestion: Timothée Chalamet, colder.)*

## Look

Slim and sharp-featured, in practical dark travelling clothes, with ink on his hands and an expression of polite calculation.

## Wants

To recover Silvanesti, whatever that costs. Magic is meant to be used. He's after the Shard of Darkness at Dread Wolf Cove, and he uses the party to get it.

## Secret

- **He knows far more about the City of Lost Names** than he admits (DC 14 Insight).
- **The arrangement.** He sends the party to locations C, D and E, for his own reasons ([[4.07 Wrecker's Edge and the Elven Encampment]]).
- **He'd teach a human.** He offers a sorcerer PC "the things Antimodes won't", and it's a Black Robe's offer.

## Ties

- **[[Zhelsuel]]:** his leader, whose caution drives him mad.
- **[[Davos Bloodbane|A sorcerer PC]]:** raw, unschooled power, and he's fascinated.
- **[[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn)|A Silvanesti PC]]:** the shard may be a piece of what destroyed their home.

## Under Pressure

He trades. Everything has a price, including his loyalty, and including his life.
