# Fizban

> [!danger] Don't say the name at the table
> To everyone in the game he's just **the old man**. Dragonlance fans will recognise "Fizban" at once, so save the name for a big moment, ideally the Temple of Paladine ([[5.02 Exploring the City]]). When anyone asks his name, he can't remember it, or offers a different ridiculous one each time ("Oswald? No. Barnaby, perhaps").

A muddled old man in a battered hat who forgets everyone's name, sets things on fire by accident, and is never quite where you left him. He's **Paladine**, in his favourite disguise, walking the world while the other gods think he's gone. He's barred from acting directly, so he never does anything important. He only happens to be sitting where someone needs him, and he asks them a question.

## Voice

Rambling, delighted and easily distracted, always losing his thread and finding someone else's. About once a visit, for a single sentence, he's perfectly clear, and then he asks what he was saying. *(Suggestion: Ian McKellen playing a much sillier Gandalf, or Michael Gough.)*

## Look

Stooped and thin, with a straggling white beard, a shapeless hat that keeps slipping over one eye, and robes with burn marks in them. Keep the hat and habits a little vaguer than the famous version, so he doesn't give himself away. He loses his staff and blames other people.

## Wants

For mortals to choose for themselves. That's the whole of it. He can't win this war for them, and he wouldn't if he could. He wants one creature made by the Dragon Queen to find out it can say no to her.

## Secret

- **He's Paladine.** Never confirm it until the moment you've chosen.
- **He knows about the leak.** He can't stop Takhisis hearing through a dragonborn PC, but he can hint: "someone's been reading over your shoulder, my boy, terribly rude."
- **He knows what the dragonborn PC is.** He calls him "Silverscale" or "Brightwing" by mistake, and each wrong name hints at the metallic egg.
- **He's the same old man** who once shared a fire with Ispin and vanished before dawn (a module funeral story). If Edric or Becklin recognises the description, that's a lovely thread.

## Ties

- **[[Wurm|A dragonborn PC]]:** the visits grow into the first real teaching he's had since the elf lady. The old man never gives orders, and that's the lesson. Where the voice commands, he asks: "And what do *you* think you should do?" He teaches silly, small things: how to skip a stone, how to haggle, and how to say no, which he makes Wurm practise on him. He refuses to teach fighting.
- **[[Ispin Greenshield]]:** a night by a fire, years ago.
- **Takhisis:** the voice in the dragonborn PC's head. The old man is the opposite of that voice.

## In the Campaign

Spread these out, a handful of visits at most, never for long, and never explained. He's gone before dawn every time, and nobody else in camp remembers him arriving. See [[Wurm (DM)]] for the full list.

| When | What he does |
|---|---|
| **A night on the road** | Shares a fire with the dragonborn PC, rambles for an hour, and gets his name wrong. |
| **A dark room** | Fumbles a spell to light a candle, and blows out every light except the one directly behind the dragonborn PC. |
| **Any time** | Gives him a small silver mirror, "for when you want to see who's really looking". It pays off against Soth ([[6.06 The Flying Citadel]]). |
| **If the party is arrested** | He's the one fallback if no faction will help. He's in the next cell, nobody knows how, complaining ([[4.03 The Trial - Verdicts#If They Are Arrested]]). |
| **The Temple of Paladine** | The best place for the name ([[5.02 Exploring the City]]). |
| **The Day of Dread** | On a bench, telling children the story of Huma: the module's old man in a pointed green hat ([[6.02 Return to Kalaman]]). |
| **The citadel** | If the dragonborn PC rejects Takhisis, helped by everything the old man taught him, the grey in his eyes turns silver ([[6.06 The Flying Citadel]]). |

## Under Pressure

He wanders off, or sets his own sleeve on fire. If anyone tries to pin him down, he's suddenly very interested in a beetle. He bails the party out once, and it should look like an accident.

## Stats

None. Don't fight him. If the party tries, the spell goes wrong in a way that embarrasses everyone but him.
