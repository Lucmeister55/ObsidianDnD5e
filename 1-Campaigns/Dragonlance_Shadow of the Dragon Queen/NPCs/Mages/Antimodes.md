# Antimodes

A White Robe archmage and diviner who wanders the roads of Ansalon, with a famous habit of finding gifted strays. In canon he's best known as Raistlin's sponsor. Here he's the man who saved a young sorcerer from the gallows, and who arranged a good deal more than that young man knows.

## Voice

Gentle, amused and maddeningly vague, like a favourite uncle who won't say what's in the box. *(Suggestion: Ian McKellen, on a good day.)*

## Look

A lean old man in travel-stained white, with a walking staff and very sharp eyes. He's always just arrived, or just leaving.

## Wants

To see the prophecy of the three moons fulfilled: a moon-touched mage who can "bind the three lights into one balance and still the quarrel of robes".

## Secret

- **The robes were bait.** He left them on the bathhouse bench on purpose. He never expected the guard to die, and he feels responsible.
- **He chose Kalaman**, where the three moons pull the tides hardest and his divinations keep returning. He asked for Davos's seat on the council in the sealed scroll ([[Davos Bloodbane (DM)]]).

## Ties

- **[[Davos Bloodbane]]:** his stray, his hope and his guilt.
- **[[Wyhan]]:** a Black Robe he trusts completely as an enemy.
- **Rovina:** the Red Robe who ran the Trial of the Barb.

## Under Pressure

He answers a different question from the one you asked, and then he's gone.

## Stats

Archmage, if it ever comes to that. It shouldn't.
