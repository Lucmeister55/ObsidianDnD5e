# The Bloodbanes

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, from [[Davos Bloodbane]] and [[Davos Bloodbane (DM)]]. The first names are new.

[[Davos Bloodbane|Davos]]'s family, in the lower districts of [[Relgoth]], outside the old walls. They live on what he sends home, and they're the one thing in the world he takes completely seriously. They never appear in Kalaman unless you bring them there. They reach the table as letters.

## Maren Bloodbane, his mother

Mid-40s. She has held the household together by stubbornness alone since her husband died, on whatever honest work she can find. She's half proud and half bitter about the name.
- **Voice:** short sentences, no complaints, and she notices if you haven't eaten.
- **Wants:** her children fed, and her eldest to stop sending so much and eat something himself.
- **Secret:** she knows why her husband was in prison, and she won't say.
- **Her letters** are dictated to a neighbour, since she can't write much. They're all about the price of bread, and they never say she's afraid.

## Tobin Bloodbane, his brother

17. He has been the man of the house since Davos left, and he's angry about it. He thinks Davos got out, and that the money is an apology.
- **Voice:** sarcastic, like his brother, with less practice.
- **Wants:** to matter. He's been talking to a recruiting sergeant, which Davos would hate.
- **Hook:** if the war reaches the Vingaard, he's the one who picks up a spear.

## Lissa Bloodbane, his sister

12. She's the one who writes the letters, in careful round handwriting with little drawings in the margins. She thinks her big brother is a great wizard.
- **Voice:** many questions, and she doesn't wait for answers.
- **Wants:** to know whether it's true about the dragons.
- **Hook, if you want it:** a line in one of her letters, in passing, that her hair went a funny colour for a day. Davos will read that line many times. The Mages would very much like to know.

## Joren Bloodbane, his father

Dead, in the city prison, when Davos was still a boy. **Why he was there is left open.** It could tie into the Bloodbane name, or into the same city guard, the Ferryman, who later confronted Davos.

## The Name

The family has it that an ancestor committed some terrible sin in the old wars, and the Knights stripped them of everything. The truth is older and worse ([[Cyan Bloodbane]]). Maren doesn't know it.

## In the Campaign

- **Letters home** ([[3.10 Downtime 5]]): the first frightened letter.
- **The stakes:** Relgoth lies upriver on the Vingaard. If Kalaman falls, nothing stands between the Dragon Army and Maren's door.
- **The pay:** his wage goes home, so the company matters to them, though they don't know it.
- **The ending:** his family upriver is one of his questions at the end ([[6.07 Kalaman Victorious]]).

## Under Pressure

Maren won't leave the house. Tobin will do something stupid and brave. Lissa will write to Davos about it.
