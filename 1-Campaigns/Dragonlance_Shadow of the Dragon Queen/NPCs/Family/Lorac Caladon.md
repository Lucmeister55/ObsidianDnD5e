# Lorac Caladon

Speaker of the Stars, ruler of Silvanesti, and father of Alhana Starbreeze and of the prince who is now called [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn)|Thorn]]. For most of his long life he was a wise Speaker and a gentle father, patient with his son's wildness and quick to laugh at his verses. Then a stranger brought him back the Orb he had lost in the Cataclysm. Everyone knows the end of that story, or thinks they do: Lorac used an Orb of Dragonkind to save his homeland, and it turned Silvanesti into a land of nightmares.

He's offstage for the whole campaign. He matters as a memory, a voice and a question.

## Voice

As Thorn remembers him: warm, unhurried, a little amused. By the end: a cracked whisper that loses its way, then a shout, then a whisper again. *(Suggestion: Christopher Lee.)*

## Look

Tall and silver-haired, in the white and gold of the Speaker. At the end he's grey and shaking, holding the Orb against his chest like a child.

## Wants

- **Before the Orb:** to keep his people safe, and to make up for losing it in the first place.
- **At the end:** to keep the Orb. Nothing else was left.

## Secret

- **He was a good man.** What ruined him was a plan. Thorn doesn't know that.
- **The stranger was [[Cyan Bloodbane]],** acting for Takhisis. Lorac's fortune paid for the invasion, and Cyan's week-long tour of Silvanost showed the Dragon Army the hidden way in.
- **Takhisis was in the Orb.** She worked on his hunger for power and his duty to his people, and convinced him that the Orb's presence alone protected the city.
- **Whether he's alive is left open.** Thorn found no sign of him in the ruins.

## Ties

- **[[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn)|His son]]:** the last time they met, they fought for the Orb at the top of the Tower of the Stars. Thorn hates everything he stood for, and misses him ([[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn)#^bond-lorac|the bond]]).
- **Alhana Starbreeze:** he cursed her by name at the end, for leaving.
- **[[Zhelsuel]]:** he sent House Mystic into exile, and the court called it a mission of hope.
- **[[Cyan Bloodbane]]:** the stranger he paid.

## In the Campaign

- **The shard** at Dread Wolf Cove speaks to Thorn in his father's voice, patient and loving ([[4.14 Dread Wolf Cove and the City of Lost Names]]).
- **[[Demelin]]** can tell him what the Orbs do to the people who use them ([[5.01 Path of Memories]]).
- **The truth,** if Thorn learns there was a dragon behind the Orb, may finally let him forgive his father ([[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]]).
