# Alhana Starbreeze

Daughter of [[Lorac Caladon]], born on the night of the Cataclysm, and older sister of the prince now called [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn)|Thorn]]. When her father couldn't decide and the Dragon Armies closed on Silvanost, she did what he wouldn't. She took most of the Silvanesti to ship and led them to Southern [[Ergoth]]. She's the last of the royal house, as far as anyone knows, and she's now a queen with no country.

She stays offstage in this campaign. She's far away, and the question is whether she ever hears that her brother is alive.

## Voice

Cool, exact and very tired, and everything she says sounds like a decision. Only with her brother did she ever sound young. *(Suggestion: Rosamund Pike.)*

## Look

Silver hair and pale, grave beauty, in travel-stained court white. She carries herself as if a crown were still there. She kept every verse her brother ever wrote for her.

## Wants

To keep what's left of her people alive, among Kagonesti cousins who are helping them but won't be overwhelmed by them. Then, to go home, whatever home has become.

## Secret

- **She thinks he's dead.** She said goodbye to him at the edge of the royal grounds, and neither of them said what they were both thinking.
- **She doesn't know what happened at the end:** the fight in the tower, the white fire, or that her father cursed her by name.

## Ties

- **[[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn)|Her brother]]:** he thinks she left him behind ([[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn)#^bond-alhana|the bond]]). She thinks he stayed to die.
- **[[Lorac Caladon]]:** the father she had to defy to save anyone.
- **[[Zhelsuel]]:** the old teacher of both her and her brother.
- **The Silvanesti in Ergoth:** see [[Ergoth]].

## In the Campaign

- **If a Silvanesti PC reveals himself to Zhelsuel,** the exiles know a Caladon prince is alive, and a letter may start the long way west ([[4.07 Wrecker's Edge and the Elven Encampment]]).
- **At the end,** one of his questions is whether she ever hears ([[6.07 Kalaman Victorious]]).
- **For later:** a sealed reply reaching Kalaman after the siege would land harder than any reward.
