# Svilnt Sunderlit

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], Parts 1 and 5, "The Spy"). The eye is from the same scene.

A neutral evil spy for the Red Dragon Army, planted in the Ironclad Regiment as a recent recruit. Quiet, helpful and unremarkable until he isn't. He is a fanatic. He **organised the meeting between Gragonis and [[Lohezet]]** that led to Ispin's murder and the betrayal of Cudgel, and he is the mercenary who tries to kill [[Cudgel Ironsmile|Cudgel]] at High Hill. [[Becklin Uth Viharin|Becklin]] stops him and takes him alive.

## Scenes

- **High Hill** ([[1.04 The Battle of High Hill]]): goes for Cudgel in the confusion. Becklin stops him.
- **The interrogation** ([[2.01 Back in Vogler]]): bound in a fishing net, he slips his bonds and burns out his own right eye with a burst of Takhisis's magic, as tribute to [[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]. With a DC 16 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check he talks: the army's size, Highmaster Kansaldi, Gragonis's payment, Ispin's murder. The Dragon Army's messenger at the River Gate has a burnt-out eye like his.
- **Later:** he matters at the Trial ([[Gragonis]]).

## Voice

Calm and eager to please. Under questioning he turns serene, then smiles. *(Suggestion: a mild clerk who has just found God.)*
