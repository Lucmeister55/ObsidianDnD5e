# Caradoc

Soth's seneschal, in life and after it. A cowardly, charming noble who bought his way into the Knights of Solamnia and made himself useful with courtly advice. When Soth rose, Caradoc rose too, as an incorporeal spirit bound to Dargaard Keep, who can only leave inside a body he has possessed. He delights in going from body to body, and in throwing them away. **He's never caught.**

## Voice

Silky, delighted and terribly polite, an old courtier enjoying himself enormously. *(Suggestion: Tom Hiddleston's Loki, with less sincerity.)*

## Look

Whoever he's wearing. The tell is a smile that's slightly too pleased, and a cold that comes off the body. As himself, he's a faint, handsome ghost.

## Wants

To amuse himself, and to get Soth back to what matters: revenge on the Knights. He thinks the alliance with the Dragon Army is folly.

## Secret

- **He's Durstan Rial.** He possesses the knight **Durstan Rial** for over a week before the massacre, and arrives in Kalaman in his body. Darrett remembers Durstan, and Caradoc has to fake it.
- **He kills Governor Miat** himself ([[Calof Miat]]).
- **At the Trial** he's the hidden witness ([[4.02 The Trial - Testimony]]). When the possession is exposed, Durstan collapses with no memory, and Caradoc is already gone. Don't let him be caught, and don't let him be fought to a finish.

## Ties

- **[[Lord Soth]]:** his master and his only friend, whom he also finds faintly embarrassing.
- **[[Dargaard Keep]]:** the only place he can exist without a host.
- **Durstan Rial:** the man he wore.

## Under Pressure

He leaves the body and laughs on the way out. He's a coward to the end, and never where the danger is.
