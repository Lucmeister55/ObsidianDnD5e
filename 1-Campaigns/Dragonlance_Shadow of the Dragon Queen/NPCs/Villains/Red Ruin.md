# Red Ruin

The Red Dragon Army's dragonnel ace, who leads its airborne forces. No one knows her real name. Her soldiers call her "commander" or Red Ruin, for the towns she has burned from the air. She rarely removes her dragonnel helm and commands by hand signal. Here, she's a child taken young and shaped into a weapon by [[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]].

## Who She Was

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.

She was a girl who studied dragonnels like other children study birds, and read their moods, calls and fears. She was the **best rider in the region because they trusted her**, which is why Kansaldi wanted her.

**Kansaldi broke her herself:** the name taken, the friends made to look like traitors, the love of dragonnels turned into an order to burn. She calls it a mercy. The understanding of dragonnels is the part she couldn't break, and the part someone can reach.

## Voice

She doesn't speak, or almost never. When she does, it's short and flat, and younger than anyone expects.

## Look

Plate armour and a helm shaped like a dragonnel's head, a shield, and a red dragonnel mount whose claws leave long, deep wounds. Under the helm, if anyone ever sees it, she's barely more than a girl.

## Wants

To obey, to please Kansaldi, and to never fail. If you dig down far enough, she wants something she has no word for, like a choice of her own.

## Secret

- **Ispin.** She killed [[Ispin Greenshield]] on her dragonnel, on an order that came from [[Lord Soth]] through Kansaldi. Soth openly blames her, and she doesn't deny it, because no one ever taught her she could.
- **The clasp.** The belt clasp the party finds by the dragonnel in the woods near Vogler is hers (see [[1.02 Arrival in Vogler#The Missing Child and the Stolen Shield]]).

## Ties

- **[[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]:** her mistress and the only parent she knows.
- **[[Ser Edric of Brackenridge|Edric]]:** he'll want her dead for a long time.
- **[[Wurm]]:** a mirror of her. He was raised into obedience too, and he may want to save her.

## Under Pressure

She follows her last order to the letter, even to death, unless someone gives her a real choice for the first time. The way in is her old love: a wounded dragonnel, a call she remembers, someone who treats her mount as a friend. Her dragonnel won't harm anyone who speaks to it the way she once did.
