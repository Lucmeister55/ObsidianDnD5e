# Kansaldi Fire-Eyes

Dragon Highmaster of the Red Dragon Army, left by Verminaard in charge of the Kalaman invasion, and a zealot even by Dragon Army standards. She tore out her own left eye and pushed a red gem into the socket, and calls it the happiest day of her life. Her soldiers fear her more than the enemy. She **kills [[Raven Uth Vogler|Raven]]** from the back of her red dragon, Ignia, as a sermon.

## Voice

Unhinged and joyful: laughs mid-sentence, sings hymns in battle, swings from whisper to scream. Calls everyone "child", especially those she's about to kill. Talks to the Dragon Queen aloud, as if she stood behind her, and stops to listen. *(Suggestion: Bellatrix Lestrange preaching a revival.)*

## Look

A cleric in scorched black-and-red Dragon Army plate. One wild human eye, and one red gem that blazes like a coal when she prays. Her armour is scratched all over with prayers, and her hands are always blistered, because she holds them in her own holy fire to stay pure.

## Wants

Kalaman burned clean, the City of Lost Names claimed as a weapon (module), and the world kneeling. She wants to save people, and fire is the only way.

## Secret

- **Red Ruin.** She raised [[Red Ruin]] from childhood and **broke her with her own hands**: a girl who loved dragonnels and was the best rider in the region because they trusted her, remade into the weapon she is ([[Red Ruin#Who She Was]]). She loves the girl in her own way, and would sacrifice her in an instant if the Queen asked.
- **The Draevenfall cult.** She was the cult's distant patron: she paid for it, sent an elf lady (**Vrella Farsky**) to study the platinum hatchling [[Wurm]], then took her away, "given" to an oracle of dragons. The second seal on Wurm's contract is hers ([[Wurm (DM)]]).
- **The deal.** She passed on [[Lord Soth]]'s order to kill [[Ispin Greenshield|Ispin]] without asking why. The Queen allows the Dread Knight, so she allows him too.
- **She hears things.** Something answers when she talks to the Dragon Queen. Takhisis really does whisper to her, and it's slowly driving her mad.

## Why She Is in Vogler

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], "Why Is Kansaldi in Vogler?").

Vogler's ferry links Kalaman to the Northern Wastes and the City of Lost Names, so holding it cuts Kalaman off from the Wastes and takes the village's resources. Showing her dragon also makes Kalaman slow to counterattack. (Homebrew: Ispin's shield was there too, [[6.02 Return to Kalaman]].) [[Nestra Vendri]] wonders about this in [[3.02 Downtime 1]].

## Why She Lets Them Go

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.

She let Vogler's boats go, and sent fire into the sky to let the army run at Steel Springs. With the citadel coming she can't lose, so every survivor she sends to Kalaman works for her:
- **Another mouth** in a city that's already short of bread.
- **Another quarrel**, refugee against local, guild against marshal, while the walls go unmended.
- **Another frightened voice** telling the others what dragons do.
- **And in the end, another offering.** The more people inside Kalaman when it falls, the greater the sacrifice to the Queen.

The companion adds pride: she wants the survivors to spread word of her, her dragon and her army.

If anyone ever asks her why, she'll tell them, happily: "I didn't spare them. I *sent* them."

## Raven's Death

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], "Kansaldi Arrives"), with **Homebrew** dialogue.

On **Ignia**, she snatches Raven off the last boat and carries her to the last arch of Vogler's Crossing, in view of everyone: "Kneel, child, and it can all stop." Raven spits. Kansaldi weeps, kisses her forehead, and lets Ignia burn her ([[2.03 Evacuation]]).

## Ties

- **[[Red Ruin]]:** her ward, her weapon and her proudest work.
- **[[Lohezet]]:** an ally she thinks is a coward and an unbeliever.
- **[[Lord Soth]]:** the Queen's servant, and the one thing in the world she's quietly afraid of.
- **The Dragon Queen:** her god, her mother and her voice in the dark.

## Under Pressure

Retreats only on the Queen's word, never from fear. Wounds make her ecstatic ("Yes! Test me!"). Shaken faith makes her more dangerous, not less.
