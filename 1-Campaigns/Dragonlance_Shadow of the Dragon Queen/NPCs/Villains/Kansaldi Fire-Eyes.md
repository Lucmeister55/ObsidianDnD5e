# Kansaldi Fire-Eyes

Dragon Highmaster of the Red Dragon Army, the woman Highlord Verminaard left in charge of the invasion of the Kalaman region, and a zealot even by the Dragon Army's standards. Verminaard brought her into the Dragon Queen's faith, and the faith ate everything else. To prove her devotion she tore out her own left eye with her fingers and pushed a red gem into the socket, and she still talks about it as the happiest day of her life. Her own soldiers are more afraid of her than of the enemy. She **kills [[Raven Uth Vogler|Raven]]** at the end of the Vogler arc from the back of her red dragon, Ignia, and she makes a sermon of it.

## Voice

Unhinged and joyful. She laughs in the middle of sentences, sings hymns while she fights, and swings from a whisper to a scream without warning. She calls everyone "child", even her enemies, and especially the ones she's about to kill. She talks to the Dragon Queen out loud, mid-conversation, as if Takhisis were standing behind her, and sometimes she stops to listen to the answer. *(Suggestion: Bellatrix Lestrange preaching a revival.)*

## Look

A cleric in scorched black-and-red Dragon Army plate. One wild human eye, and one red gem that blazes like a coal when she prays. Her armour is scratched all over with prayers, and her hands are always blistered, because she holds them in her own holy fire to stay pure.

## Wants

Kalaman burned clean in the Dragon Queen's name, the City of Lost Names claimed as a weapon (module), and the whole world kneeling. She doesn't want to conquer people. She wants to save them, and she believes fire is the only way.

## Secret

- **Red Ruin.** She raised [[Red Ruin]] from childhood and **broke her with her own hands**: a girl who loved dragonnels and was the best rider in the region because they trusted her, remade into the weapon she is ([[Red Ruin#Who She Was]]). She loves the girl in her own way, and would sacrifice her in an instant if the Queen asked.
- **The Draevenfall cult.** She was the cult's distant patron: she paid for it, sent an elf lady (**Vrella Farsky**) to study the platinum hatchling [[Wurm]], then took her away, "given" to an oracle of dragons. The second seal on Wurm's contract is hers ([[Wurm (DM)]]).
- **The deal.** She passed on [[Lord Soth]]'s order to kill [[Ispin Greenshield|Ispin]] without asking why. The Queen allows the Dread Knight, so she allows him too.
- **She hears things.** Something answers when she talks to the Dragon Queen. Takhisis really does whisper to her, and it's slowly driving her mad.

## Why She Is in Vogler

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], "Why Is Kansaldi in Vogler?").

A Dragon Highmaster leading the attack on a small fishing village looks strange, so expect the question. The village and its ferry across the Vingaard connect Kalaman directly to the Northern Wastes, and so to the City of Lost Names, which is now a key part of the army's plan to conquer Kalaman. A foothold in Vogler cuts Kalaman off from the Wastes and takes the village's abundant natural resources. By appearing on her red dragon at the end of the invasion, she also hopes to slow Kalaman's decision to counterattack, out of fear of how many other dragons her army might keep. (Homebrew: Vogler is also where Ispin's shield was, see [[6.02 Return to Kalaman]].) [[Nestra Vendri]] wonders about all of this in [[3.02 Downtime 1]].

## Why She Lets Them Go

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.

She let Vogler's boats go downriver. She let the army run from Steel Springs, and sent fire up into the sky to call her soldiers off. It isn't mercy, and it isn't a mistake. With the citadel coming, she can't lose, so every survivor she sends to Kalaman works for her:
- **Another mouth** in a city that's already short of bread.
- **Another quarrel**, refugee against local, guild against marshal, while the walls go unmended.
- **Another frightened voice** telling the others what dragons do.
- **And in the end, another offering.** The more people inside Kalaman when it falls, the greater the sacrifice to the Queen.

The companion adds her pride. She is a powerful servant of the Dragon Queen and arrogant with it, and she wants Vogler's survivors to see how fearsome she, her dragon and her army are, so that they spread the word in Kalaman. The wider plan is the one above: the army has been attacking the region's smaller communities and forcing their people to relocate to Kalaman as refugees, to overcrowd the city while cutting off its surrounding resources.

If anyone ever asks her why, she'll tell them, happily: "I didn't spare them. I *sent* them."

## Raven's Death

> [!companion] Source: **Companion** ([[2-When_Home_Burns.pdf]], "Kansaldi Arrives"), with **Homebrew** dialogue.

As the last boat leaves Vogler, Kansaldi dives out of the smoke on her red dragon **Ignia**, snatches Raven off the deck, and carries her to the last arch of Vogler's Crossing, where everyone can see. She offers forgiveness, tenderly: "Kneel, child, and it can all stop." When Raven spits at her, Kansaldi weeps, kisses her forehead, and lets Ignia burn her. Then she lets the boats go, so the refugees carry the story to Kalaman.

## Ties

- **[[Red Ruin]]:** her ward, her weapon and her proudest work.
- **[[Lohezet]]:** an ally she thinks is a coward and an unbeliever.
- **[[Lord Soth]]:** the Queen's servant, and the one thing in the world she's quietly afraid of.
- **The Dragon Queen:** her god, her mother and her voice in the dark.

## Under Pressure

She never retreats from fear, only on the Queen's word. Wounding her makes her ecstatic ("Yes! Test me!"). If something shakes her faith, she doesn't break. She doubles down, and becomes far more dangerous.
