# Cyan Bloodbane

A great green dragon of the Dragon Queen, and one of the cruellest minds on Krynn. In canon (*Dragons of Winter Night*, and Fizban's Treasury in the vault), Cyan is the dragon who poisoned Lorac's mind through the Dragon Orb and drowned Silvanesti in nightmare. He serves Takhisis when it suits him, and himself always. He is never seen by the party directly in this module. He is the hand behind two characters' tragedies.

## Voice

Silky, patient and enormously pleased with himself. He loves the sound of his own cleverness. *(Suggestion: Benedict Cumberbatch as Smaug.)*

## Look

In his true shape, a vast green dragon with acid-bright eyes. In disguise, a charming stranger whose face no one can quite remember afterwards.

## Wants

To ruin the elves, to be admired, and to be proven cleverer than everyone, gods included.

## Secret

- **The stranger.** He was the stranger who brought the Orb back to Lorac in Silvanost, took a fortune for it, and asked for a tour of the city, including its hidden way in. Later he whispered to Lorac through the Orb on Takhisis's behalf. Thorn has no idea a dragon was behind it. See [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]].
- **The Bloodbanes.** In the Third Dragon War, a company of humans served Cyan and took his name, and the Knights later stripped them of everything for it. Those are [[Davos Bloodbane|Davos]]'s ancestors. See [[Davos Bloodbane (DM)]].

## Ties

- **[[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn)|Thorn]]:** destroyed his home and his father.
- **[[Davos Bloodbane|Davos]]:** his family once served him.
- **Takhisis:** his queen, when it suits him.

## Using Him

Keep him offstage. He works best as a name that turns up in the wrong place: a Bloodbane record in the Knights' archives, a green scale in a box of Lorac's, a stranger at the edge of a crowd who smiles at Thorn as if they've met before. If Davos and Thorn ever work out what links them, that's the moment to let him speak, once.
