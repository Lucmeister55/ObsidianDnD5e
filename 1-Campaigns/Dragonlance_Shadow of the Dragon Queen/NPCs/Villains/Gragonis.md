# Gragonis

A half-ogre (neutral evil), Cudgel's lieutenant until the Dragon Army buys him. He's the betrayal at High Hill: real spears hidden in the brush, a reenactment turned massacre, and Ispin's shield on his arm. He likely dies on the hill.

## Voice

Slow, heavy and amused, with a laugh that comes half a second too late. *(Suggestion: Ron Perlman.)*

**Before the battle,** the nicest person at the festival: apologising when he bumps things, careful with children, offering to carry the wrestling loser to the Brass Crab, humming. **In the battle,** the same voice and laugh, and the axe.

## Look

Huge even for a mercenary, in the regiment's patched iron, with a broken tusk and small, clever eyes. At High Hill, he carries Ispin's shield on his arm.

## How He Got Here

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**.

People flinched at him young, so he learned to be gentle first: wooden animals carved for camp children, coin sent home to a sister, everyone's name remembered. He followed Cudgel because she treated him like a person. What turned him, over years:
- **The pay.** Cudgel's honour kept the Regiment poor: no dirty contracts, fair rates, everything shared. His sister's letters got thinner.
- **The dead.** Each lost fight took someone he liked. He stopped carving.
- **The fear.** He's seen what the Dragon Armies leave behind, and decided gentleness never saved anyone.

When Svilnt came, the gold was the permission he wanted.

**He led Ispin to his death.** Ispin was one of the few knights kind to him, and he walked him into the woods and left him there. He took the shield because the deal required it and hates himself for it, which is why he carries it wrapped, and why its voice gets under his skin ([[1.04 The Battle of High Hill]]).

**On the hill,** no sympathy: he roars, cleaves through villagers and laughs late. The guilt only shows if he's taken alive: he doesn't bargain, and asks only that someone tell his sister.

## Wants

Pay, no more orders from a dwarf, and to be **on the winning side** when the Dragon Armies sweep the continent.

## Secret

- **Why he did it:** money first, fear second (see above). Questioned, he says so, and doesn't sound proud of it.
- **He sold the regiment.** The Red Dragon Army bought him. The spy **Svilnt** arranged a meeting with [[Lohezet]], and he paid off the mercenaries he trusted most.
- **The shield.** He took it off Ispin's corpse in the woods the night Ispin died, two nights before the party arrives (it was never at the funeral) ([[1.02 Arrival in Vogler#The Missing Child and the Stolen Shield]]).
- **He isn't the only one.** He knows about [[Jeyev Veldrews|Jeyev]], and if he's taken alive, he may say so.

## Ties

- **[[Cudgel Ironsmile]]:** his captain, whom he betrays.
- **[[Jeyev Veldrews]]:** his fellow lieutenant and fellow traitor.
- **Svilnt:** a Red Dragon Army spy in the regiment, who arranged the deal and tries to kill Cudgel at High Hill. Becklin takes him alive, and he matters at the Trial.
- **[[Lohezet]]:** the man who made the deal.

## Under Pressure

He hits things until they stop. When he falls, the rest of the mercenaries run.
