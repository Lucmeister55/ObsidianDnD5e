# Gragonis

A half-ogre, neutral evil, and Cudgel's lieutenant in the Ironclad Regiment, until the Dragon Army buys him. He's the betrayal at High Hill: he hides real spears under the brush, turns the reenactment into a massacre, and steals Ispin's shield. If the party does its job, he dies on the hill. Until the whistle, **play him as a comically nice, gentle giant**, and then let him cut loose.

## Voice

Slow, heavy and amused, with a laugh that comes half a second too late. *(Suggestion: Ron Perlman.)*

**Before the battle** he is the nicest person at the festival: apologetic when he bumps into things, careful with children and stalls, offering to carry the loser of the wrestling match to the Brass Crab, humming while he works. The players should like him. **In the battle** all of that drops at once. The same slow, heavy voice, the same half-second late laugh, and the axe. The contrast is the point.

## Look

Huge even for a mercenary, in the regiment's patched iron, with a broken tusk and small, clever eyes. At High Hill, he carries Ispin's shield on his arm.

## How He Got Here

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. The aim is a villain the players can sympathise with: he used to be kind, and he became this.

He was the nicest soldier in the Ironclad Regiment. A half-ogre who learned young that people flinch when they see him, so he got into the habit of being gentle first: he carved wooden animals for the camp children, sent coin home to a sister, remembered everyone's name. He followed Cudgel because she treated him like a person.

What turned him, over years and not in a day:
- **The pay.** Cudgel's honor kept the Regiment poor. She wouldn't take the dirty contracts, haggled for fair rates, and shared out the little they had. He watched his sister's letters get thinner and his comrades die for coin that never came.
- **The dead.** Each lost fight took someone he liked. He stopped carving.
- **The fear.** He has seen what the Dragon Armies leave behind. He decided he would not be on the wrong side of it, and that being gentle had never saved anyone.

By the time Svilnt came to him, he had already half-decided. The gold was the permission he wanted.

**He led Ispin to his death.** Ispin was kind to him, one of the few knights who ever was, and he walked that man into the woods and left him there. He took the shield off the corpse because the deal required it, and he hates himself for it. It is why he wears it on his arm in the battle, wrapped, and why the shield's voice ([[1.04 The Battle of High Hill]]) gets under his skin.

**Play it** so the players like him at the festival, then on the hill let him show his **ugliest, most ferocious side**. He is still killing innocents, and the sympathy must never soften that. No humming, no regret in the moment: he roars, cleaves through villagers, and laughs half a second late. The guilt only shows afterwards, if he is taken alive, when he doesn't bargain and asks only that someone tell his sister.

## Wants

To be paid, and to stop taking orders from a dwarf. And, under the jokes, to be **on the winning side** when the Dragon Armies sweep the continent.

## Secret

- **Why he did it.** Mostly **money**. Cudgel is too honorable: she turns down dirty contracts, haggles for fair pay and not good pay, and the Regiment has been underpaid for years, which is why loyalty to her was already shaky and why Gragonis found others who would follow him. But he is also **genuinely afraid**. He has seen what the Dragon Armies leave behind, he believes they will win, and he decided he would rather be on the winning side when they sweep over the continent. He says so if questioned, and he doesn't sound proud of it. (If he is questioned at all. He may die on the hill.)
- **He sold the regiment.** The Red Dragon Army bought him. The spy **Svilnt** arranged a meeting with [[Lohezet]], and he paid off the mercenaries he trusted most.
- **The shield.** He took it off Ispin's corpse in the woods the night Ispin died, two nights before the party arrives (it was never at the funeral) ([[1.02 Arrival in Vogler#The Missing Child and the Stolen Shield]]).
- **He isn't the only one.** He knows about [[Jeyev Veldrews|Jeyev]], and if he's taken alive, he may say so.

## Ties

- **[[Cudgel Ironsmile]]:** his captain, whom he betrays.
- **[[Jeyev Veldrews]]:** his fellow lieutenant and fellow traitor.
- **Svilnt:** a Red Dragon Army spy in the regiment, who arranged the deal and tries to kill Cudgel at High Hill. Becklin takes him alive, and he matters at the Trial.
- **[[Lohezet]]:** the man who made the deal.

## Under Pressure

He hits things until they stop. When he falls, the rest of the mercenaries run.
