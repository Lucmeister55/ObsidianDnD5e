# Lohezet

A black-robed Mage of High Sorcery and a scholar obsessed with extinct creatures and fallen empires. He sees the Dragon Army as the means to bring the glories of Ansalon's past back to life. His digging through Istarian ruins during the invasions of Khur and Kendermore revealed where the flying city of Onyari, the City of Lost Names, lies buried, and now he travels with the Red Dragon Army to find it and raise it into the sky again. He fights with the poisons of predators that died out centuries ago.

## Voice

Precise, dry and delighted by his own expertise. He lectures even in battle. *(Suggestion: Mads Mikkelsen as Kaecilius.)*

## Look

Black robes stained at the hems with something purple, fingers yellowed by toxins, and a satchel full of fossil bones and bottled specimens. He always smells faintly of something sweet and wrong.

## Wants

Onyari restored to the skies, ancient knowledge recovered at any cost, and his name in the histories.

## Secret

- **The disguise.** At Vogler he wore Cudgel's face (a *disguise self*) to lure Ispin into the trap, and brokered the gold through [[Svilnt Sunderlit|Svilnt]] ([[1.00 An Ordinary Day]]). He is the Dragon Army's teleporter, and opens the gate for Soth in Kalaman ([[3.13 The Raided Catacombs]]).

## Ties

- **[[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]:** his military protector, and his uneasy ally.
- **[[Wurm]]:** optionally, the creature he once studied.
- **Davos's Order:** he's a renegade black robe, and the Dragon Army's black robes may be the ones who try to recruit [[Davos Bloodbane|Davos]].
- **The Threshold of the Heavens:** where the module puts him in the City of Lost Names.

## Under Pressure

He runs. *Dimension door* first, questions later. His research matters far more to him than his dignity.
