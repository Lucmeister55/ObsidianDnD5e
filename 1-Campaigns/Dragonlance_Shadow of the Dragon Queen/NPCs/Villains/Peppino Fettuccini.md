# Peppino Fettuccini

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, a character from another campaign with a full history in Faerûn. Only what matters here is in this note. The module's "People from Beyond" lets him step through a portal into Krynn.

"Mister Fettuccini": a three-foot **kobold** crime lord in a pinstriped coat and fedora, with a cigar as long as his arm. His family runs the **Dragon's Den** casino in Waterdeep. Years ago he bought Kalaman's dockside rackets as a sideline and has never visited. The Firefishers send a cut upstairs, and he reads the numbers twice a year. Nobody on Krynn knows what a kobold is.

**Through Storm Clouds he's only an urban legend.** The party never meets him or hears from him. Soth's massacre kills the local boss, and he comes to run things himself.

> [!danger] An easter egg, never a reveal
> **He's a warlock of Tiamat**, Takhisis under another name. He never says so, and nobody in the game ever confirms it ([[#The Easter Egg]]). If a player guesses out loud, he tips his hat and changes the subject.

## The Man Upstairs

The local boss is councillor **Oflyn Vinhollow** ([[The Guild Council]]), with **Marta Dace** running the street and the tables. The money goes somewhere above Vinhollow, and nobody has seen who.

**What the Firefishers say (d6):**
1. He's nine feet tall, and he has never been seen to blink.
2. He's a dragon in a man's coat.
3. He died a hundred years ago, and he still takes his cut.
4. He once won a city in a card game, and lost it back on purpose.
5. He has a thunder-stick that can kill a man through a door.
6. He's very small. (Nobody believes this one.)

Only 5 and 6 are true, more or less.

## Doing Business

**Before the massacre:** through Marta Dace at the Cinque Teste. Anything big "goes upstairs", and an unsigned yes or no comes back a week later.

**After it:** he's at a corner table in the Cinque Teste. His letters are short and courteous, on heavy paper, signed *P.F.*, with a stamp of five little heads.

| What | The price | For example |
|---|---|---|
| **Rare goods** | Gold, at a markup | Foreign potions and steel, a cigar that never goes out. Nothing that trivialises the Wastes ([[DM Guide#House Rules\|no easy outs]]). |
| **Buying what others won't** | Fair, no questions | Monster parts, dragon scales, Dragon Army gear, stolen goods. |
| **Information** | Gold or a favour | Who owes whom on the council, what's moving through the harbour. |
| **Papers** | Gold | Forged passes, a clean name, an unrecorded berth. |
| **The dragonborn's contract** | A very large sum or favour | Bought from the Bakarises, quietly ([[3.08 Downtime 4]]). |
| **Getting someone out** | A favour | A sorcerer PC's family from Relgoth ([[The Bloodbanes]]). |

- **Standing:** each deal is **+1 [[The Firefishers|Firefishers]]**, and **-1 Military** if Vendri finds out.
- **Favours** he prefers to gold. Keep a tally. Whatever the party owed the Firefishers, they owe him. He honours every deal to the letter, and always collects.
- **Collateral:** someone who can't pay may be offered "a different kind of collateral", on a very nice piece of paper. A wise person doesn't sign.

## Voice

A gravelly, unhurried old-country mob boss, all "paisan" and "with respect", who has never needed to raise his voice. Disappointing people are "stunad". Murder is a matter of manners. *(Suggestion: Joe Pesci doing Marlon Brando's Don Corleone.)*

## Look

Rust-red scales, a sharp pinstriped coat, a grey fedora with holes for his horns, gold rings on his claws, and a cigar that's never out. A badge of honour from a city nobody's heard of, "given by its ruler" (he won it at poker). He sits on three cushions and smells of tobacco and expensive oil. His hands are steady, except around one person.

## Wants

- **What he says:** respect, a fair cut, a quiet town.
- **What he wants:** keep his head down, go home to his real casino, and one day get back what he lost.

## Secret

- **The pact.** He was a soldier in the crew of a dracolich, Don Telbarino. In the Don's hoard he found a twin-barrelled weapon, and his first shot destroyed the Don's phylactery by accident. Tiamat spoke to him then: he had done her will, and the weapon was his to wield in her name. Nobody says no to her, and he fears her more than anything alive. He calls her "my partner" or "the Lady", rarely.
- **No soul.** His brother **Vinnie** turned informer. Peppino couldn't kill him, so Asmodeus's people (the casino's other backer) did. Vinnie's soul is in the Nine Hells, and Peppino's is held in reservation. He tells people he killed Vinnie himself. On Krynn it's never explained: play it as a tiredness nobody can place.
- **The order.** Late in the war, she sends him to Kalaman in person to spy and to undermine the defence. He doesn't want to go.
- **He feels her attention** the way a dog feels thunder, and it's strongest around the dragonborn PC ([[Wurm (DM)]]).

## Why He Comes

Vinhollow dies in Soth's massacre and the money stops. Days later a door opens in the cisterns, and the man upstairs walks out ("business is business"). Number 6 was true.

**The first sign,** between the Trial and the ships: Dace, grey-faced, says the box of cigars has finally been opened. No time to act on it. By the time the party comes home ([[6.02 Return to Kalaman]]), the gang is quieter, better run, and very frightened of someone.

## In Kalaman, Late

**"Not in front of him."** The first time he's in a room with the dragonborn PC, the colour goes out of his scales and he puts out his cigar: "Your friend waits outside, with respect, or we got nothing to talk about." Pushed: "I got a feeling about him. In my business, you listen to your feelings." He never shares a room with him again.

**The sabotage.** He does what he's told and no more. Before the siege, unless the party stops him, pick one or two:
- **The arsenal:** the Trade Gate's ballista has no bolts.
- **The sluice:** a cistern is left open, and **+1 Kapaks** unit comes up inside the walls at the Trade Gate (under the Cistern House, if that's the Bastion).
- **The boats:** cut loose. No boats for the siege, and no Goodwill boat rides.
- **The favours:** he calls them in. The party loses **2 tokens** of its choice (Commendations, Goodwill or Pledges).

The party can trace it to the Firefishers, but never gets more out of him than "business".

**The last deal.** He's hedging. Caught, or simply made a good offer, he can be bought: the sabotage stops, and the smugglers' tunnels and every Firefisher boat are the party's for the siege. It costs a great deal, or every favour they have left. How early it's offered depends on [[The Firefishers|Standing]].

## Soft Spots

He does terrible things without enjoying them, and tells himself it's for the family, abused by their dragon masters for generations. He can't resist anything innocent:
- **Animals.** Strays follow him, and he feeds them from his plate (see Bruno, below).
- **Children.** He overpays a refugee child for a drawing and hangs it in his office.
- **At the table:** an appeal about the lower city's people gets further than gold. If a sabotage would hurt children, he picks a different one.

He doesn't actually like spaghetti and meatballs.

## The Easter Egg

Never explained. Leave the clues lying around:
- **The Cinque Teste**, "five heads", and five little heads on the stamp and the chips.
- **His lupara** fires five colours of energy, matching the Dragon Queen's five dragons.
- **"My partner,"** a lady he won't name and clearly fears.
- **The dragonborn,** the one creature Takhisis listens through.
- **The old man.** If you like, [[Fizban]] wanders past one day and tips his hat. Peppino goes grey and doesn't finish his cigar.

## The Cinque Teste

A modest private gambling house in the old Istarian cisterns under the lower city, reached through Hetch Warehouse. A poor copy of the Dragon's Den, without the devils, named as a small, very frightened private joke. Marta Dace runs it.

## Ties

- **[[The Guild Council|Oflyn Vinhollow]]** sent him money for years and never met him. Peppino once calls him "Vinnie" by mistake, and goes quiet for the rest of the meeting.
- **[[Davos Bloodbane|A sorcerer PC]]** works the tables for Dace. Peppino inherits every favour he owes ([[Davos Bloodbane (DM)#Downtime: The Underground]]).
- **[[Milo Geartickle|A kender PC]]** can't be kept out of a locked casino. Peppino finds this unforgivable, and a little impressive.
- **Pip Thistlewhistle** ([[3.04 Downtime 2]]) stole a cigar from the box kept for a man who never comes.
- **Bruno** ([[3.04 Downtime 2]]), the crocodile in the cisterns, left years ago with orders to feed him. The Firefishers still do. He's the only thing in Kalaman Peppino cares about. If the party killed him, Peppino asks about it, politely, and that's the most dangerous conversation they'll ever have.

## In the Campaign

- **Storm Clouds:** stories only.
- **After the massacre:** he arrives, and the party hears of it just before they sail.
- **Duty:** out of reach. He rebuilds the gang.
- **Conquest:** business, "not in front of him", the sabotage and the last deal.
- **The siege:** if the last deal was made, the tunnels and boats count in the [[6.02 Return to Kalaman#The Siege Ledger|Siege Ledger]].
- **After Kalaman:** gone before the smoke clears, owed a great deal, with a new hat.

## Under Pressure

He never runs, because someone else does it for him. If it comes to it, a sawn-off shotgun appears in his claws, and everyone in the room learns what that is.

## Stats

[[bandit-crime-lord-xmm|Bandit Crime Lord]], Small, with Pack Tactics from the [[kobold-warrior-xmm|Kobold Warrior]]. Never alone. Add:

**La Lupara (pact weapon).** The Don's twin-barrelled gun, cut down. It appears or vanishes as a bonus action. Nobody on Krynn has seen a firearm, and Tatina would give a finger to look at it.
- **Action:** a 15-foot cone, DC 15 Dexterity save, 3d8 damage on a failure, half on a success.
- **Five colours:** roll a d5 per shot: **acid, cold, fire, lightning or poison**.
- **Reload:** a bonus action, which he enjoys.

**Hex** at will, cast by tipping his hat.
