# Peppino Fettuccini

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**, a character from another campaign, with his own full history in Faerûn. Only the parts that matter here are in this note. The module's note on "People from Beyond" lets him be here: folk from other worlds can step through a portal into Krynn.

"Mister Fettuccini" is a three-foot **kobold** in a pinstriped coat and a fedora, smoking a cigar as long as his arm. He's a crime lord from far away, from a port called Waterdeep, where his family runs a grand casino called the **Dragon's Den**. Kalaman barely matters to him. Years ago he bought up its dockside rackets as a sideline, and he hasn't set foot in the place since. The Firefishers send a cut upstairs, and he reads the numbers about twice a year. Nobody on Krynn knows what a kobold is.

**Through all of Storm Clouds, he's only an urban legend.** The party never meets him, never gets a letter from him, and can't be sure he exists. Then Soth's massacre kills the local boss, and he comes to run things himself. **From that point on, he's in the story:** someone to do business with, a dangerous, very well-supplied man who can get things nobody else can, for a price.

> [!danger] An easter egg, never a reveal
> **He's a warlock, and his patron is Tiamat**, who is Takhisis under another name. **He will never say so**, to anyone, under any circumstances. It's a treat for players who notice the clues ([[#The Easter Egg]]). Nobody in the game ever confirms it. If a player guesses out loud, he tips his hat and changes the subject.

## The Man Upstairs

**The Firefishers have a local boss:** councillor **Oflyn Vinhollow** ([[The Guild Council]]), with **Marta Dace** running the street and the card tables. Even they know the money goes somewhere above Vinhollow. Nobody alive has seen who. All they have is a name, and the stories keep getting bigger.

**What the Firefishers say (d6):**
1. He's nine feet tall, and he has never been seen to blink.
2. He's a dragon in a man's coat.
3. He died a hundred years ago, and he still takes his cut.
4. He once won a city in a card game, and lost it back on purpose.
5. He has a thunder-stick that can kill a man through a door.
6. He's very small. (Nobody believes this one.)

Drop these in whenever the party deals with the Firefishers. Only 5 and 6 are true, more or less.

## Doing Business

**Before the massacre,** the party deals with the Firefishers, through Marta Dace at the Cinque Teste. She can manage the smaller things in the table below. Anything bigger "goes upstairs", and a yes or no comes back a week later, with no name on it.

**After the massacre,** he's at a corner table in the Cinque Teste, with a cigar, and the door is open for business. He writes short, courteous letters on heavy paper, signed *P.F.*, with a stamp of five little heads.

**What he deals in:**

| What | The price | For example |
|---|---|---|
| **Rare goods** | Gold, at a markup | Odd things from far away: unfamiliar potions, foreign steel, a cigar that never goes out. Apply the [[DM Guide#House Rules|no easy outs]] rule: nothing that trivialises the Wastes. |
| **Buying what others won't** | Fair, no questions asked | Monster parts, dragon scales, Dragon Army gear, stolen goods. |
| **Information** | Gold or a favour | Who owes whom on the council, and what's moving through the harbour. |
| **Papers** | Gold | Forged passes, a clean name, a ship's berth with no entry in the book. |
| **The dragonborn's contract** | A very large sum, or a very large favour | He can have it bought from the Bakarises, quietly ([[3.08 Downtime 4]]). |
| **Getting someone out** | A favour | A sorcerer PC's family from Relgoth, for instance ([[The Bloodbanes]]). |

**Standing.** The Firefishers are a faction ([[The Firefishers]]). Each deal is **+1 Firefishers**, and **-1 Military** if Vendri finds out.

**Collateral.** If someone can't pay, he may offer to accept "a different kind of collateral", on a very nice piece of paper. Nobody has ever explained what that means. A wise person doesn't sign.

**Favours.** He prefers them to gold. Keep a tally at the table. Whatever the party owed the Firefishers, they now owe him. He always honours a deal to the letter, and he always collects. Every deal costs the party **-1 Military** if Vendri finds out, and it pushes the file a little further from the fire.

## Voice

A gravelly, unhurried, tiny old-country mob boss, all "paisan" and "with respect", who has never once needed to raise his voice. Anyone who disappoints him is a "stunad". He talks about murder as if it were a matter of good manners. *(Suggestion: Joe Pesci doing Marlon Brando's Don Corleone.)*

## Look

Three feet of rust-red scales under a sharp pinstriped coat, a grey fedora with a hole cut for each horn, gold rings on his claws, and a cigar that's never out. He wears a badge of honour from a city nobody has heard of, which he says was given to him by its ruler (he won it at poker). He sits on three cushions. He smells of tobacco and expensive oil, and his hands are very steady, except for one moment, around one particular person.

## Wants

- **What he says:** respect, a fair cut, and a quiet town.
- **What he wants:** to keep his head down, get his work done, go home to his real casino, and one day get back what he's lost.

## Secret

These are for you. He never tells anyone.
- **The pact.** He grew up a soldier in the crew of a dracolich, Don Telbarino. Poking through the Don's hoard, he found a strange twin-barrelled weapon, and his first shot by accident destroyed the Don's phylactery. Tiamat spoke to him in that moment: he had done her will, the weapon was his, and he'd wield it in her name. He took the deal, because nobody says no to the Queen of Dragons. He's more afraid of her than of anything alive. He calls her "my partner", or "the Lady", on the rare occasions he mentions her at all.
- **He's a man with no soul.** A second partner, Asmodeus, backed the casino. His brother **Vinnie** turned informer, Peppino couldn't bring himself to kill him, and Asmodeus's people did it instead. Vinnie's soul is in the Nine Hells, and Peppino's is held in reservation. He has been running the business without it ever since, and it shows. He tells people he killed his brother himself. He didn't. None of this means anything on Krynn, and he'll never explain it. Play it as a tiredness nobody can quite place.
- **The order.** Late in the war, she tells him to go to Kalaman in person, to spy for her and to undermine the city's defence. He doesn't want to. It isn't a request.
- **The attention.** He can feel her attention, the way a dog feels thunder. When a dragonborn PC walks in, he goes very still and puts out his cigar ([[Wurm (DM)]]).

## Why He Comes

Vinhollow dies in Soth's massacre at the end of Storm Clouds, which leaves the Firefishers with no local boss, and the money stops. Days later, a door opens in the cisterns, and the man upstairs walks out to run things himself. If anyone asks why, he says business is business. The gang is astonished. Number 6 was true.

**The first sign.** The party can catch the news between the Trial and the ships: Dace, grey-faced, says the box of cigars has finally been opened. There's no time to do anything about it before they sail.

By the time they come home ([[6.02 Return to Kalaman]]), the gang has changed. It's quieter, better run, and very frightened of someone.

## In Kalaman, Late

**"Not in front of him."** The first time he's in a room with the dragonborn PC, every bit of colour goes out of his scales, and he puts out his cigar. "Your friend waits outside, with respect, or we got nothing to talk about." He won't say why. If anyone pushes: "I got a feeling about him. In my business, you listen to your feelings." He never explains, and he won't sit in a room with him again.

**The sabotage.** He does what he's told, and no more than he has to. Before the siege, unless the party stops him, pick one or two:
- The Firefishers empty an arsenal, and the walls run short of bolts.
- A sluice in the cisterns is left open, and kapaks come up under the lower city. If the party took the Cistern House, it's under their floor.
- The harbour boats are cut loose on the night they're needed.
- He calls in the favours the party owes him, at the worst possible moment.

The party will find the Firefishers behind it. They'll never get a reason out of him beyond "business".

**The last deal.** He's hedging, and he'd never admit that either. If the party catches him, or simply makes him a good offer, he can be bought: the sabotage stops, and the smugglers' tunnels and every Firefisher boat are theirs for the siege. It costs a great deal, or every favour they have left.

How soon it's on the table depends on the party's [[The Firefishers|Standing]].
## Soft Spots

He does terrible things, and he doesn't enjoy them. He tells himself it's all for the family, who were abused by their dragon masters for generations. And he has a weakness he won't admit to: anything he thinks of as innocent.
- **Animals.** He loves them. Strays follow him, and he feeds them from his plate. See Bruno, below.
- **Children.** He overpays a refugee child for a drawing and hangs it in his office. Nobody mentions it.
- **At the table:** this is the party's real way in. An appeal to him about the people in the lower city will get further than gold, though he'd deny it. If the sabotage would hurt children, he finds a reason to do a different one.

**Small things that are true:** he doesn't actually like spaghetti and meatballs, and his badge was won at poker.

## The Easter Egg

Nothing here is ever explained. Leave the clues lying around, and let the players who spot them feel clever.
- **The name.** His casino is the **Cinque Teste**, "five heads".
- **The stamp and the chips:** five little heads.
- **The shots.** His lupara fires one of five colours of energy, and they happen to match the Dragon Queen's five dragons.
- **"My partner."** A lady he won't name, and whom he clearly fears.
- **The dragonborn.** He won't sit in a room with the one creature Takhisis listens through.
- **The old man.** If you want a treat, have [[Fizban]] wander past one day and tip his hat to him. Peppino goes grey, and doesn't finish his cigar.

## The Cinque Teste

A modest private gambling house in the old Istarian cisterns under the lower city, reached through Hetch Warehouse. It's a poor imitation of the Dragon's Den in Waterdeep, without the devils. He named it himself, as a small, private, very frightened joke. Marta Dace runs it.

## Ties

- **[[The Guild Council|Oflyn Vinhollow]]:** the local boss, who sent him money for years and never met him. His death is what brings Peppino to Kalaman. Peppino once calls him "Vinnie", by mistake, and goes quiet for the rest of the meeting.
- **[[Davos Bloodbane|A sorcerer PC]]:** works the tables for Dace. Peppino inherits every favour he owes ([[Davos Bloodbane (DM)#Downtime: The Underground]]).
- **[[Wurm|A dragonborn PC]]:** he won't be in a room with him, and he won't say why.
- **[[Milo Geartickle|A kender PC]]:** cannot be kept out of a locked casino. Peppino finds this unforgivable, and a little impressive.
- **Pip Thistlewhistle** ([[3.04 Downtime 2]]): stole a cigar from the box the Firefishers keep for a man who never comes.
- **Bruno** ([[3.04 Downtime 2]]): the crocodile in the cisterns, left behind years ago with orders to feed him. The Firefishers still do, because nobody dares stop. He's the only thing in Kalaman Peppino actually cares about. If the party killed him, Peppino asks about it, politely, and that's the most dangerous conversation they'll ever have.

## In the Campaign

- **Storm Clouds:** only stories. The Firefishers shake down Wanderer's Rest, and they talk.
- **After the massacre:** he arrives, and the party hears of it just before they sail.
- **Duty:** he can't reach them in the Wastes, and he spends the time taking the gang apart and putting it back together.
- **Conquest:** in person: business, "not in front of him", the sabotage and the last deal.
- **The siege:** if the last deal was made, the tunnels and boats count in the Siege Ledger ([[6.02 Return to Kalaman#The Siege Ledger]]).
- **After Kalaman:** he's gone before the smoke clears, owed a great deal, and with a new hat.

## Under Pressure

He never runs, because he never has to. He has someone else do it, and it's always "business". If it does come to it, a sawn-off shotgun appears in his claws out of nowhere, and everyone in the room learns what that is.

## Stats

[[bandit-crime-lord-xmm|Bandit Crime Lord]], Small, with Pack Tactics from the [[kobold-warrior-xmm|Kobold Warrior]]. He never fights alone. Add:

**La Lupara (pact weapon).** The twin-barrelled gun from the Don's hoard, cut down. It appears in his claws as a bonus action, and vanishes the same way. Nobody on Krynn has ever seen a firearm. Tatina would give a finger to look at it.
- **Action:** a 15-foot cone. Each creature in it makes a DC 15 Dexterity save, taking 3d8 damage on a failure, or half on a success.
- **Five colours:** roll a d5 for each shot: **acid, cold, fire, lightning or poison**. The blast comes out in that colour.
- **Reload:** it's ready again after he spends a bonus action, and he enjoys doing it.

**Hex.** He can cast *hex* at will. He does it by tipping his hat.
