# Belephaion

"Glorious Belephaion", high priest of Takhisis and joint commander of the Dragon Army's dig in the Northern Wastes. He's a tall, serene old cleric in a jewelled crown who treats everyone below him as a congregation. He is also, secretly, a **young blue dragon** in human shape. Nobody in his own army knows, including his partner [[Lohezet]], who thinks of him as a tiresome priest.

## Voice

Warm, musical and endlessly patient, a preacher's voice, as if every sentence were a blessing. He calls people "child", and he never stops smiling. *(Suggestion: Ian McDiarmid before the reveal.)*

## Look

Long blue-and-black vestments, a tall crown worth a small fortune, and a ruby holy symbol of Takhisis. His skin is very smooth for his apparent age, and the air around him smells faintly of a coming storm.

## Wants

To raise the City of Lost Names into the sky as a flying citadel, in the Dragon Queen's name, and to be the one sitting on its throne when it rises. He collects whatever Onyari has to offer: eggs, slaad research, old Istarian weapons.

## Secret

- **He's a dragon.** He uses the module's young blue dragon stat block, and he changes shape only when threatened.
- **The dracophage.** He ordered the bozak **Virruza** to study the slaadi and the Spawning Shard at the Sunward Fortress, looking for new ways to breed new kinds of draconian ([[4.09 Sunward Fortress]]).
- **The bronze egg.** The egg at Camp Carrionclay is meant for him, and he sent the black dragon **Akhviri** to fetch it ([[4.13 Camp Carrionclay]]).

## Ties

- **[[Lohezet]]:** his peer and rival. They share a command and despise each other.
- **[[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]:** she needs what he's digging up. He thinks she's a useful lunatic.
- **Captain Hask:** his officer in the city, whose reports the party can find ([[5.02 Exploring the City]]).
- **[[Wurm]]:** Virruza's notes speak of "the egg-work". Belephaion wants another creature like him ([[4.09 Sunward Fortress]], [[4.13 Camp Carrionclay]]).

## Under Pressure

He mocks the party and promises their souls to the Queen. The moment they threaten him, he orders the draconians in, changes shape and fights to the death. He touches the helm first, so killing him never stops the city rising ([[5.03 Threshold of the Heavens]]).
