# Jeyev Veldrews

> [!module] Source: **Module** ([[04-chapter-3-when-home-burns#Lost in Battle|ch. 3, "Lost in Battle"]], the "Assassination" option). His life in Kalaman is **Homebrew**.

Cudgel's lieutenant and the Ironclad Regiment's chief scout: easy-going, quick with a joke, the mercenary everyone in Vogler likes. He's also the Dragon Army's second buyout, after Gragonis, and the better one. Gragonis was bought with gold and a grudge. Jeyev was bought with gold and nothing else, and he's patient. His contract is for **the Solamnic knights of Vogler**: Becklin, then Darrett. In the Battle of Vogler, he stabs Becklin in the back (see [[2.00 Broken Silence]]), takes her helmet, and runs for the last boat to get close to Darrett.

## Voice

Warm, wry and helpful. He's the first to offer a hand and the last to take credit, and he calls everyone "friend". He never raises his voice. *(Suggestion: Jason Isaacs being nice.)*

## Look

Lean and sun-browned, in scuffed Ironclad Regiment leathers, with a scout's squint and a ready smile. On the boat he's gripping Becklin's horned helmet like it's a holy relic.

## Wants

To finish the contract and get paid: Darrett dead, and clean enough that no one looks at him. After that, a ship south and a new name.

## Secret

- **He murdered Becklin.** If she survived the battle (a win in Scenario 1), she knows he tried, and he knows she knows. Then he's the one running.
- **He's the Dragon Army's man in Kalaman.** The sivak assassins and "the friendly face" in [[3.00 Storm Clouds]] are his doing. He tells them where Darrett and the party will be.

## Ties

- **[[Darrett Highwater]]:** his target. Jeyev carries Becklin's last words to him ("tell him I'm proud of him") and becomes his loyal right hand.
- **[[Becklin Uth Viharin]]:** his victim.
- **[[Cudgel Ironsmile]]:** his old commander. She trusted him, and when the truth comes out it will break her.
- **[[Kansaldi Fire-Eyes]]:** his paymaster, at a distance. He thinks she's insane, and he's right.

## In Kalaman

He's never alone with Darrett when the party is watching. Give the players small wrong notes, one per downtime at most:
- He knows details of Becklin's death he shouldn't, since he says he fled before the end.
- His story of the battle changes slightly each time he tells it.
- A sivak attack comes exactly where only Darrett, the party and Jeyev knew they'd be.
- The fargab channel crackles at night from his quarters.

**The move.** Late in Storm Clouds, just before the Lord's Arrival, he makes his attempt on Darrett. If the party has caught on, they can be there. If not, Darrett survives wounded, and Jeyev vanishes into the city.

## Under Pressure

He lies smoothly, then blames someone else, then runs. Cornered, he fights dirty and offers to sell out anyone. He's no fanatic. He'll name Kansaldi for his life.
