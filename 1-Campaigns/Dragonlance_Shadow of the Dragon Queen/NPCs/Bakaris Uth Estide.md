# Lord Bakaris Uth Estide (the Elder)

A minor Solamnic lord who has lived his whole life on stories of his family's honour, none of which are his own. His ancestors were Knights of Solamnia. He is a coward full of bluster, and he has decided the war is his chance at glory. In this campaign he was also a lord of Draevenfall in Estwilde until his son's duel got the family exiled. He owns [[Wurm]], by contract, and treats him like an ox.

## Voice

Booming, red-faced and pompous, every sentence a speech. *(Brian Blessed: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w9Kaxd0vhrg)*

## Look

A heavy man gone soft in the saddle, red in the face, wrapped in furs too warm for the season, with a ring on every finger that will take one.

## Wants

Glory, credit for things other people did, a place on Kalaman's council, and his family's name made great again.

## Secret

- **Money.** The family is running short since the exile, so he'd sell Wurm's contract for the right price, if his son didn't know.
- **Courage.** He's terrified of actual battle.

## Ties

- **[[Bakaris the Younger]]:** his son and heir, the only person he's afraid to disappoint.
- **[[Wurm]]:** his property.
- **[[Ser Edric of Brackenridge|Edric]]** and **[[Becklin Uth Viharin|Becklin]]:** the knights he needled all the way to Vogler.
- **Kalaman's council:** whom he lies to about leading Vogler's survivors (module, chapter 4).

## Under Pressure

He blusters and then folds, or backs down the moment someone stands up to him (DC 14 Intimidation in the module). He leads Kalaman's troops to disaster at Steel Springs, and blames everyone else for it.
