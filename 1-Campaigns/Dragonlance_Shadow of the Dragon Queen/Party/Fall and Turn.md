# Fall and Turn

> [!homebrew] Source: **Homebrew**. Late-game paths on which an origin character can turn evil or change sides. They build on each character's own DM note ([[Ser Edric of Brackenridge (DM)]], [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]], [[Wurm (DM)]], [[Milo Geartickle (DM)]], [[Davos Bloodbane (DM)]]) and on [[DM Guide#When a Character Dies]].

Every path has three steps: **Seeds** (early signs the table can see), **The Slide** (choices that cost something), and **The Door** (one scene with a real choice). The Door is the point of no return. Until then, a redemption route stays open.

## Ground Rules

- **Opt in, privately.** Ask each player once, away from the table (Session 1.5 is the place, [[Session 1.5]]): "Do you want a dark turn available for your character? You can say no." A no means the path is skipped and they get the redemption version of the Door. Never spring this on a player.
- **Keep it on a card, not in the vault.** Per [[IRL tracking|the tracking rule]], the count for each path lives on a physical card in your DM folder.
- **Warn three times.** Before any Door, the table should have seen at least three clear signs, so the turn feels earned.
- **After the turn,** the player picks: **play the fallen character for one last scene** and then hand them to you as an NPC (and roll a new origin character per [[DM Guide#When a Character Dies]]), or **keep playing them as a secret traitor** until the reveal (Davos and Wurm only).
- **Takhisis wears the gods they trust.** The true gods are barred from the world and cannot speak directly. Takhisis is the only god on the same plane, so she is the only voice any of them can hear, and she can pass as any god a character trusts. Use it at the Slide and the Door, always deniable: a dream, a sign, a voice in a prayer. The tell is that the "god" asks for something the real one never would, or arrives exactly when the character is weakest. The table should find it suspicious, never certain.

  | Character | Their god | The guise |
  |---|---|---|
  | Edric | **Mishakal** | A healer's voice at the moment he is dying: "Come, child, there is no shame in rest." |
  | Thorn | **Branchala** (and the wild gods) | Song and birdsong in the grove, calling him to what he has lost. |
  | Wurm | **Paladine** | Already so, from High Hill. |
  | Milo | **Reorx** | Grey sparks off the anvil, and a forge-voice that says the ring is a gift. |
  | Davos | **Solinari, Lunitari, Nuitari** | Whichever moon is full: Solinari urging mercy for the family, Nuitari offering a way to protect it. |

- **Takhisis hears through Wurm.** Her link to Wurm ([[Wurm (DM)]], "Wurm is her spy") is how she learns anything the party says in front of him. That is also how she learns about Davos's family.

## Ser Edric: the Hollow Knight

**Idea.** He dies and Soth raises him, as he raised Ispin's body.

- **Seeds.** Soth's offer in the catacombs ("what is worth saving?", [[3.13 The Raided Catacombs]]). Black rose riders appearing wherever Edric is. The shield going cold near them.
- **The Slide.** Each time Edric chooses an order over a person (the Measure over Ispin's way), mark a rose. Soth's lies about the first Greenshield start to land.
- **The Door.** If Edric drops to 0 hit points within Soth's reach (the Night Crossing, [[4.05 Dark Moon Rises]], [[6.03 March of the Black Rose]], or the citadel, [[6.06 The Flying Citadel]]), offer a Last Stand first ([[Last Stands]]). If he dies there anyway, a black rose rider stands over him: "Kneel, Greenshield's boy, and rise." If he refuses and dies, Soth raises him anyway. That is the cruellest version.
- **The turn.** He rises as a **death knight aspirant** wearing the black rose. Unlike Ispin, who was hollow, Edric keeps his memories, bound by oath. He cannot touch the shield, because the blessing burns undead hands, so it stays with the party. Ispin's voice in it is the last thing he hears.
- **Redemption.** The shield's blessing frees him, as it freed the body of Ispin. It needs someone to carry it to him and say his name, and he has to choose to put his sword down. That choice should be his, not the dice's.

## Thorn: the Hunter

**Idea.** His vengeance eats everything else, until the forest he loves is hunting for him.

- **Seeds.** The stag at the Missing Scouts mission (level 5 moment). Killing a surrendering draconian when no one is watching. Refusing to bury a kill.
- **The Slide.** Mark a **pip on his card** each time he kills a helpless or surrendering foe, or lets a refugee die for the chance to hunt. At 3 pips, the grove starts to answer: animals flee him, the shoots wither. At 5, he stops hearing the song.
- **The bait: his father is alive.** Takhisis (or Cyan, in any shape) tells Thorn that **Lorac still lives**, bound to the Orb or held in Silvanost, and that Thorn can reach him if he leaves Kalaman now. It may be true or a lie. Time it for when Kalaman needs him most (the Siege of Kalaman, or just before the march). Leaving means abandoning the city, the refugees and the grove, with no warning to the party. If he goes, he is walking into the dragon's trap, and the party has to decide whether to follow.
- **Triggers that push it.** The truth about Cyan and Takhisis behind the Orb. Silvanesti refugees dying in the Wastes. The grove being cut down or burned. Learning that Red Ruin killed Ispin and the Dragon Army raised her, and him not forgiving that. Alhana being hurt.
- **The Door.** The Siege of Kalaman ([[6.05 Siege of Kalaman]]). The grove comes for the defenders, as his downtime promised, but with his rage in it. The treant hunts anyone who stood by while the forest burned, which includes Kalaman's own soldiers and refugees.
- **The turn.** He leads the grove against the city, or hunts prisoners and the wounded of both sides. The primal fury drives him, and Chislev's gentleness is gone.
- **Redemption.** The dryad he nursed. A child he saved. The song from level 8. Hearing Lorac's name sung back to him by the grove.

## Wurm: Takhisis's Vessel

**Idea.** He kneels to Takhisis, for many reasons, and none of them is stupid.

- **Why he might.** The contract and the Bakarises ([[Bakaris the Younger]]). The suspicion and the smear from Kalaman's soldiers. The worshippers who treat him as a holy sign, which is worse. Vrella, if she is dead or taken. Takhisis as the loving mother who made him, when no one else ever did. The rage that already feels holy.
- **Seeds.** Rages that last longer. Not feeling horror at what the rage-self did (the danger sign in his note). The voice turning from commands to comfort.
- **The Slide.** Each time the party fails him (a refused request, a rumour that sticks, a soldier who spits), mark a **mark** on his card. Fizban's visits, and the party's care, take marks away.
- **The Door.** Kansaldi's demand in the finale ([[DM Guide#When a Character Dies]], "Kansaldi's brother"): one of them must kneel and burn. Takhisis offers Wurm everything at once (Vrella, the Bakarises' end, a world with no more cages), and the voice finally says who it is.
- **The turn.** The meek Wurm goes quiet. Takhisis walks in his body as her avatar (his note's Arc).
- **Redemption.** Fizban's patience, a name he chose, Vrella's paper kingfisher in his hand, the platinum under the black.

## Milo: the Ring's Creature

**Idea.** The ring doesn't only kill what Milo loves. It consumes what binds them, until nothing does.

- **Seeds.** The Death Tracker ([[Milo Geartickle (DM)#The Death Tracker]]): strays, then pets, then people. Level 6, the ring tightens and pleases itself.
- **The corruption.** Each death takes a person bound to Milo, and with them go the bonds that made Milo a kender who cares. By step 3 Milo has stopped grieving. By step 4 they find they can **choose**: if they befriend someone on purpose, the ring will take them next. A cheerful kender who has learned that friendship is a store of lives is more frightening than any villain.
- **The temptations.** Borrowed memories, numbness, the power to give life, and the maker's voice are laid out in [[Milo Geartickle (DM)#The Ring's Temptations]], with the rewards for each road.
- **The Slide.** The drone's recorder (progress 8) shows Milo the cost. Mark a **pip** each time Milo, knowing, lets the ring take someone to save themselves.
- **The Door.** Onyari ([[5.04 The City Rises]]). Demelin says the ring can only be unmade there, and unmaking it releases every life it took. The ring's maker, the Black Robe in the Kingpriest's service, offers the other road: a throne in a City Without Sin, immortal on the lives of the devoted.
- **The turn.** Milo refuses to unmake it and takes the offer. The drone, built to bring the rings home, brings them to the city's door.
- **Redemption.** A bond the ring can't take: a friend who refuses to be Milo's friend any more, or Elgo, or Tatina. Unmaking the ring costs Milo the life they borrowed. They give it.

## Davos: the Black Robe

**Idea.** He goes for power through Nuitari and the Dragon Queen, especially if his family looks threatened.

- **Seeds.** The temptation in his note ("make sure, at least once, that switching sides looks genuinely attractive"). The Cinque Teste favours. The Black Robe column of his Orders table.
- **The Slide.** Letters from Relgoth that stop. Dragon Army columns mapped on the Vingaard. Every time he chooses family over strangers, or power over rules, mark a **robe** on his card.
- **How Takhisis learns.** Through [[Wurm (DM)|Wurm's bond]]: whatever he says near Wurm about his mother, brother and sister in **Relgoth**. Svilnt, Jeyev or any captured spy can carry it too.
- **The offer.** A black-robed envoy ([[Lohezet]]'s people, or Kansaldi herself) comes by moonlight, with a token from his mother's house. The Dragon Queen's word: **join her, or sabotage Kalaman, or the Red and Blue Armies march up the Vingaard and kill his family.** It may be true (a column really does turn toward Relgoth) or a lie. Davos can't check. Antimodes, Wyhan and a letter home are his only ways to know.
- **What she asks.** Small things first, all within his seat on the council ([[3.01 Arrival in Kalaman#Message for a Mage]]): a copy of the war council minutes, a ward report left unread, a warning not passed on. Then the Trade Gate ward, or a spell on the wrong night.
- **The Door.** The Night of the Eye (14 Reapember 351 AC, [[5.00 Destiny]]): all three moons full and Davos at the height of his power. Nuitari's offer is simple: keep it.
- **The turn.** He sabotages Kalaman from inside the council, or walks out of it on the Night of the Eye with the power and doesn't look back.
- **Redemption.** Telling the party before the Door. Sending the Knights or Vendri's riders to Relgoth. Antimodes's prophecy ("bind the three lights into one balance").

## Where They Cross

- **Edric and Wurm** already have a rift over Red Ruin. If both turn, they end up on opposite sides of her.
- **Davos's sabotage** can look like Wurm's leak. Let the party suspect the wrong one.
- **Milo's bonds** are what Edric, Davos and Wurm pull on. A friend of Milo's is a body the ring can take.
- If more than one turns, spread the Doors across chapters 5 and 6, and never have two at the same session.
