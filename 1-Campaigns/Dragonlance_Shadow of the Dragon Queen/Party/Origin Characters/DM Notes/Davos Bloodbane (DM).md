## The Truth

What Davos doesn't know.

- **The prophecy.** The Mages read his shifting hair, and its sudden blackening on the night of the killing, as the sign of an obscure, much-debated prophecy: one day a moon-touched sorcerer will arise whose divided nature will "bind the three lights into one balance and still the quarrel of robes." That is why they claimed him instead of letting him hang. Affects [[Davos Bloodbane#^bond-mages|the Mages]].
- **What they want from him.** They are shaping him as a possible solution to the Orders' internal conflict, whether or not he wants that role. Affects [[Davos Bloodbane#^ideal-autonomy|Autonomy]].
- **Antimodes left the robes on purpose.** He's a diviner, and he had foreseen a moon-touched boy in Relgoth. The robes on the bathhouse bench were bait, and he was watching from the moment Davos picked them up. He never expected the guard to die, and he feels responsible for it. Davos has no idea any of it was arranged. (Antimodes is canon, a White Robe archmage best known as Raistlin's sponsor. His school is our choice.)
- **His father's death.** Why his father was in prison, and how he died there, is left open. It could tie into the Bloodbane name, or into the same city guard who later confronted Davos.
- **Where the name comes from.** In the elder days of the Third Dragon War, the age of Huma, a company of human warriors swore themselves to the green dragon **Cyan Bloodbane** and took his name as their own. They were his hands among mortals, and they were feared. When Huma drove Takhisis and her dragons from the world, the company scattered. Its survivors hid what they had been and, over the centuries, rebuilt themselves into a respectable, wealthy noble house that kept the old name and forgot, or pretended to forget, where it came from. There's no dragon blood in the family. The name is a legacy of service, not descent.
- **The Bloodbane sin.** During the Cataclysm, one of Davos's ancestors went back to the old allegiance. What exactly they did is left open: tried to call Cyan back, hid something for him, or sold out the Knights in the chaos. The Knights of Solamnia found out and stripped the house of everything, and the family has told the story ever since as "a terrible sin" without ever knowing the truth. Davos knows none of this.
- **Cyan today.** Cyan is the stranger who brought the Orb to Lorac and destroyed Silvanesti, Thorn's homeland. See [[Aeltharion Caladon (Thorn) (DM)]]. Whether he remembers the Bloodbanes, and whether he has noticed a moon-touched sorcerer carrying the name, is your call. Either way, Davos's family history is tied to the dragon who ruined Thorn's life.
- **The orb at the Trial of the Barb** turned red with a thread of black and a faint sliver of white through its heart. Red for the trickery and for harming nothing directly. Black for leaving the other initiate to the basilisks without a second look. White for the scream he heard behind him and couldn't forget, the guilt he's never admitted to. All three moons in one orb has never been recorded. Rovina reported it to Wyhan, and the Mages see it as the clearest sign yet of the divided nature the prophecy describes. Whether the other initiate survived is left open, and they could turn up again.

## Hooks

- **Why Davos fights the Dragon Queen.** His reasons are practical, and they could pull him the other way.
  1. **Orders and pay (from the start).** The Mages posted him to Kalaman, and his wage and his protection depend on doing what Wyhan says. If Kalaman falls, so does everything the Order gave him.
  2. **Family (as the war spreads).** The Red and Blue Dragon Armies are massing to invade Solamnia, and Relgoth sits on the Vingaard right in their path. Holding the line at Kalaman keeps the war away from his mother, brother and sister.
  3. **Standing (mid-campaign).** Renown with the Knights and with Kalaman's factions means influence, money and a little freedom from the Order. He's using the war to buy himself a future.
  4. **The temptation (late).** The Dragon Armies have black-robed mages too, like Lohezet, and they offer exactly what Nuitari whispers about: power without anyone's permission. Make sure, at least once, that switching sides looks genuinely attractive.

- **Bound to the Order.** Prophecy, coercion and practical necessity tie him to the Mages. Plays on [[Davos Bloodbane#^bond-mages|the Mages]], [[Davos Bloodbane#^bond-salary|the salary]] and [[Davos Bloodbane#^flaw-trapped|feeling trapped]].
- **The moons.** His temperament and power swing with the lunar phases. Track them in [[Calendar#The Three Moons]]. Plays on [[Davos Bloodbane#^flaw-unstable|unstable magic]].
- **Mistrust of justice.** He's one of the few people with real reasons to mistrust both the law and the myth of noble justice, which will clash with the Knights. Plays on [[Davos Bloodbane#^flaw-authority|distrust of authority]].
- **Family at stake.** His family is alive and depends on him, which gives him an immediate personal stake in the war. Plays on [[Davos Bloodbane#^bond-family|his family]] and [[Davos Bloodbane#^flaw-rationalizing|rationalizing]].
- **Kalaman.** His role in Kalaman and his presence at Ispin's funeral tie him to the campaign's central military and emotional threads.
- **Late training.** He started late, so he's powerful but incomplete. Mastery is a story of growth, not arrival. Plays on [[Davos Bloodbane#^bond-magic|his magic]].

## Level-Up Moments

The gods are almost entirely absent, so any divine touch has to be deniable: a coincidence, the weather, light, something in his own body. Never say a god did it. Davos's gods are the **three moons**, and their sign is his hair. Check the phases in [[Calendar#The Three Moons]] before each one. *Divine* moments are the moons nudging; *training* and *earned* moments are just Davos getting better.

- **Level 4: the flotilla downriver** *(earned)*. A long night on the water, alone with Rovina's sealed scroll for Wyhan. Ask: does he open it? The level comes from the choice, whatever it is. Note it down; Wyhan will be able to tell.
- **Level 5: downtime in Kalaman** *(training)*. Wyhan makes him cast the same cantrip every night for a month, under every phase of every moon, and write down how it feels. It's boring. It works.
- **Level 6: after the catacombs** *(divine)*. Deep under the city there's no moonlight at all, and his magic should be at its weakest. It isn't. Something answered him in the dark, and his hair stays black for a week. Ask: does he tell Wyhan?
- **Level 7: the Northern Wastes** *(divine)*. Solinari is bright on the salt flats after the Wash has drained away. He spends magic he can't spare to save someone who's no use to him. Afterwards, one strand of his hair has gone silver and doesn't change back with the others.
- **Level 8: the passage to the City of Lost Names** *(training)*. A moon calendar is carved into the passage walls, with all three moons aligned. He works out the date of the next Night of the Eye. With this vault's 28-day months, it's 504 days after his trial: **1 Fierswelth, 352 AC**.
- **Level 9: reforging the dragonlance** *(divine)*. In the Temple of Paladine, for one breath, nobody can say what colour his hair is.
- **Level 10: escaping the Threshold** *(divine or training)*. If the timeline reaches 1 Fierswelth 352 AC, it's the Night of the Eye: all three moons are full and he's at the height of his power. If not, he levels from everything Wyhan has drilled into him and gets them out on skill alone.

## Arc: Trials and Orders

Trials test skill, morality, and identity.
- Trial I – The Barb (passed): resisted illusions, survived hostile environments, exploited loopholes.
- Trial II – The Heart: tests loyalty and compassion versus power.
- Trial III – The Veil: confront shadow self, accepting or destroying inner darkness.

| Order                  | Convictions & Beliefs                             | Actions That Lead There                                                                | Temptations / Appeals                                            |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| White Robes (Solinari) | Magic is a sacred gift for justice and protection | Sacrifice wealth, status, safety to aid others; resist shortcuts; protect innocents    | Family struggles tug at compassion; praised by Paladine's allies |
| Red Robes (Lunitari)   | Knowledge and balance; pursuit of understanding   | Clever compromises, exploiting loopholes, moral gray choices                           | Whispers freedom through knowledge; rules are chains             |
| Black Robes (Nuitari)  | Magic is power; morality is weakness              | Pursue magic for security or ambition; betray allies; prioritize family over strangers | Poverty and trauma tempt selfishness; Nuitari offers greatness   |
